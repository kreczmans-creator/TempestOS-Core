# Changelog

## v0.26.0 (in progress)

### Added
- `Tempest.Calc.*` module family: transparent worksheet engine, sheets, library, verification (ADR-0165). Engine: unit-aware expressions over a runtime SI dimension vector, step log (formula → substitution → result), caps, seeded Monte Carlo, tornado sweeps. Sheets: the one §3.4 sheet shape, validation, CSV/JSON exchange. Library: calculation instances with provenance, Evidence-governed Check/Issue in one transaction, Calculation-register link, calc packs, stale marker.
- `IEvidenceService.IssueInOneTransactionAsync`: the issue sheet bytes, attachment, issue record and status commit together; the Workspace issue command uses it.
- Desktop: **Worksheets** node under Modules → Calculations with the shared worksheet form (live recalculation, unit pickers, provenance per input, governing-check bar, Show working, read-only issued instances); calc-sheet PDF renderer; TrueType glyph subsetting for every PDF (issue sheet 222 → 162 KB).
- Mechanical worksheets: M1 `mech.tolerance-stack` v2 (WC, RSS, modified RSS, distributions, C_p/C_pk/DPMO/yield, Pareto, Monte Carlo, GD&T contributors, BS 8888/ASME notation); M2 `mech.iso286-fit` (ISO 286-1 formulae), `mech.press-fit`, `mech.thick-cylinder` (migrated); M3 `mech.bolted-joint` (simplified VDI 2230), `mech.bolt-group` (elastic method). Each with known-answer, property, robustness and golden tests, a spec under `docs/calcs/`, and two worked examples under `docs/worked-examples/`.
- Structural worksheets: S1 `struct.section-properties` (parametric shapes with fillets, polygon with holes, composites; Green's theorem, plastic moduli, torsion, warping; Blue Book UB/SHS within 0.5 %); S2 `struct.frame-2d` (direct stiffness 2D frames: releases, springs, cases and combinations, envelope, caps 2,000 nodes / 4,000 elements / 50 cases) and `struct.beam-formulas` (Roark single-span library); S3 `struct.en1993-member` (EN 1993-1-1 tension, compression, flexural buckling, bending, shear, simplified interaction; clause numbers only, editable imperfection and partial factors). Same DoD as the mechanical set.
- Building-services worksheets: B1 `bs.pipe-pump` (Darcy–Weisbach, Colebrook with Swamee–Jain check, K and equivalent-length fittings, system curve, pump duty point, affinity laws, NPSH; water correlations Kell/Vogel/Hyland–Wexler); B2 `bs.psychrometrics` (state from any second property, sensible/cooling-dehumidification/steam/evaporative/mixing processes, chart series); B3 `bs.duct-sizing` (equal-friction and velocity methods, round/rectangular/flat-oval equivalent diameters, fittings by user coefficients, index-run totals, limit checks). Same DoD.
- Electrical worksheets: E1 `elec.motor-sizing` (linear and rotary loads through screw / belt / rack / gearbox, trapezoidal and S-curve profiles, peak and RMS torque, inertia ratio, candidate-motor checks); E2 `elec.enclosure-heat` (heat-load schedule, effective surface by face exposure, editable heat-transfer coefficient, natural rise, forced-ventilation airflow with altitude correction, fan duty point, solar gain, cooling-unit capacity); E3 `elec.voltage-drop` (DC, single- and three-phase from resistivity, R/X per km or mV/A/m; parallel conductors; loss; conductor temperature estimate; user limits, BS 7671 cited not embedded). Same DoD.

- Addendum C, Track S, C-3 (ADR-0174): **record entry tooling.** A records screen (Engineering → Modules → Calculations → Reference records) enters a record type from one of Track P's CSV templates, typed into a grid with unit-aware columns or imported from a file, with a preview, a column mapping, a unit check per column, row-level errors and an all-or-nothing commit in one transaction. The source, edition and amendment, table or clause and licence holder are required; `licensed_source` is on by default; the signed-in person, the time and the file's SHA-256 are stamped. A worksheet lists the required records it is missing, each with a link to fill it. 71 of the 82 templates import; the export lock is unchanged.
- Addendum C, Track S, C-1: **calculation fixes.** 130 of the 145 findings of the independent re-derivation fixed across 35 templates (each with its version raised, its known answers, golden sheet, worked examples and PDFs regenerated), among them the accumulator's real-gas and load-pressure sizing, the cylinder's buckling length at full stroke, the bolt bearing cap for a single-lap joint and the oversize-hole factor, the motor design torque including braking, the fault-protection loop impedance at the conductor's maximum resistance (`k_R`), and the chain packs' links (room temperatures, supply voltage, bearing speed). Eight items that need the owner's licensed standard are recorded, not changed (DC-03 … DC-09).
- Addendum C, Track S, C-4: **verification dashboard** (Engineering → Modules → Calculations → Verification). One read-only row per calculator and chain: version, known-answer results, Unconfirmed and Secondary counts, the independent re-derivation, records not entered, open defects and the manual-test status; discipline and status filters; markdown and PDF export. The document data is an embedded snapshot with a regenerate script, a staleness test and a CI `--check` (DC-11).
- Addendum C, Track S, C-5 (ADR-0175): **guided test mode** (Engineering → Modules → Calculations → Guided test). Walks the owner test pack (87 worked examples and the runbook's manual steps, generated and embedded), opens each example as a scratch sheet, compares the outputs with a stated half-a-unit tolerance, and records Pass, Fail, Blocked or Skipped with a note in its own collection (`Manual.TestRuns`), resumable and exported as the results sheet. A run never writes to a calculation or its Evidence (DC-12).
- Addendum C, Track S, C-6 (ADR-0176): **seeded demo project** "Demo — Hydraulic Press Cell" (Settings → Demo project). Installs, after a confirmation, 60 draft calculations in 13 packs from invented data through the ordinary services; every sheet and PDF of the flagged project says DEMO; Remove deletes only its calculation, pack and scan-profile rows in one transaction and soft-deletes its objects, and is refused once anything in it is issued. Never installed at start-up (DC-13).

### Changed (Phase 7 — Black/Blue, ADR-0166)
- Desktop worksheet form: *Import inputs (CSV/JSON)* re-seeds the form from the clipboard (a worked example's `inputs.json` or the exported CSV), carrying table and vector inputs the form has no field for; every import notice is shown and a blocking one imports nothing.
- Check and CheckPack refuse a draft whose inputs lack provenance; a Rejected check stays on the instance and leaves the Evidence Draft; empty checker, organisation, issue reference and client are refusals.
- Sheets: table inputs are written row by row (up to 100 rows); utilisation checks read `u = 0.375 vs u ≤ 1`; valid ranges follow the entered unit; a non-optional input that takes its template default raises a warning naming the default.
- JSON import: duplicate symbols and ValueText-only values are refused by name; a malformed document is described in plain words.
- PDFs: the font subsetter keeps only the tables a PDF needs, so Windows's default face embeds at the same size as the brand faces.

### Security (Phase 6 — Red/Blue)
- Imports: duplicate CSV headers, quoted-cell length, sign-prefixed DDE formulas, null JSON entries and null table rows are refused with notices (never exceptions); provenance reference/revision capped at 200/40 characters; validator notices capped per input.
- Engine and solvers: variadic reducers bounded before allocation; calculator Monte Carlo capped at 100,000 samples; frame solver bounded per member (200 loads) and by factor work.
- Library and Core: optimistic concurrency on every instance write; Check and Issue commit with the instance in one transaction with a transaction-time status re-check; issued records compared in full before a Superseded mark is accepted; Revise atomic and recoverable; pack members frozen after the pack check.
- PDF: every drawn string cleaned of control, separator and format characters and capped per field and paragraph.

#### Changed
- `mech.bolted-joint` 1.1.1 (from 1.0.0, the version v0.26.0-rc1 shipped; 1.1.0 existed only on `release/v0.27.0`): once the joint separates the clamp is zero and the bolt carries the whole working load (before, the bolt load was understated: the overloaded example showed yield at 89 % instead of 115 %); fatigue amplitude from the bolt-load range; separation-aware yield factor (D-42); a given-preload method with optional given stiffnesses and stress area; legacy joint-diagram load factors n_0, n_L.
- `mech.bolt-group` 1.2.0: a single bolt takes direct shear but blocks an in-plane torque it cannot resist; a moment about the line of collinear bolts is refused (rule `collinear` of the shared group-geometry kernel, which folds in the legacy-port rule `unresisted-moment`); the unsymmetric-bending form of Addendum A is kept.
- `struct.beam-formulas` 1.0.1 (from 1.0.0): the legacy stress and deflection-limit checks and cases. It gains three optional inputs (`y_c`, `sigma_allow`, `delta_limit`), the checks "Bending stress" and "Deflection" (each runs when its inputs are given; a deep-beam warning), four known-answer cases from the legacy `calc.beam-deflection` examples, and a reference-library fill for `E`. The results with those inputs left blank are unchanged. This is a change of inputs and checks, so it is not covered by the "wording only" patch bumps below, and by the project's rule it would have been a minor version; it keeps 1.0.1 because 1.0.1 was never released with other content.
- `struct.en1993-member` 1.1.0 (was 1.0.1): two lines of development each moved this template from 1.0.0 to 1.0.1 (the N_Ed sign label on the v0.26.0 line; the worded method lines and reference-fill fields on the legacy-replacement line), so "1.0.1" named two different contents. The merged template is one version of its own, 1.1.0; a calculation saved at 1.0.1 shows "newer version" until it is re-run. `tests/Tempest.Calc.Tests.Library/TemplateVersionManifest.json` now records the content hash of every shipped `id@version`, and `TemplateVersionManifestTests` fails if a template's content changes under an unchanged version, so one `id@version` can no longer carry two contents.
- Every worksheet template states its method one step per line, each saying what it calculates (D-46): the 27 templates of the legacy replacement move up one patch version (e.g. `mech.bolted-joint` 1.1.1) and the 22 added by Addenda A and B whose method lines lacked a worded label move up one patch version too. Eight templates that Addenda A and B had also changed merge both sets of changes at the next minor version: `bs.pipe-pump` 1.6.0, `bs.psychrometrics` 1.2.0, `elec.enclosure-heat` 1.5.0, `elec.motor-sizing` 1.5.0, `mech.bolt-group` 1.2.0, `mech.iso286-fit` 1.2.0, `mech.tolerance-stack` 2.2.0, `struct.frame-2d` 1.2.0. Apart from the changes listed above (`struct.beam-formulas` above all, and the `mech.tolerance-stack` precision change below), these version bumps change the method wording and the reference-fill metadata only: no formula, input, check or known answer. A saved calculation shows "newer version" until it is re-run.
- Worksheet form and sheets: record pickers keep their records after an import; the author, checker and header name the same person; Results uses the full width; units print as m², W/(m²·K); symbols the mono face lacks (Σ) print correctly.
- Statistics: the upper tail of the normal distribution keeps its precision deep in the tail (DPMO at 7σ was right to four digits only). This changes a result of `mech.tolerance-stack` 2.2.0 in the fifth significant figure: the worked example `bearing-housing-gap` now reports DPMO 7.7755e-7 (was 7.7759e-7); the new value is the more accurate one.
