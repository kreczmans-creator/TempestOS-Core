## 5. Honest Disclosure of Non-Exhaustive Coverage

This instruction names dozens of individual UI surfaces and states
(hover/selected/focus for every panel, tab overflow, split-document
view, screen-scaling/High-DPI verification, and more). Given no
physical multi-monitor/High-DPI test rig exists in this environment,
and given most named interaction states (hover/selected/focus for
`Button`/`TreeView`/`TabControl`/`ComboBox`) are already provided
automatically by `Avalonia.Themes.Fluent.FluentTheme` for every stock
control this platform already uses (confirmed directly — none of
`RibbonView`/`ProjectExplorerView`/`DocumentAreaView`/`PropertyInspectorView`
draw their own custom hover/selected chrome; all delegate to stock
controls), this Work Package concentrated its own real, hand-written
effort on the surfaces that were genuinely bespoke, hardcoded, or
broken (theme-reactive brushes, icon consistency, the four new feedback
controls, the two closed debt items) rather than re-implementing
behaviour Avalonia's own theme already correctly provides. See the
Implementation Report §8 and the new Future Capability entries for the
complete, honest list of what remains unbuilt.
