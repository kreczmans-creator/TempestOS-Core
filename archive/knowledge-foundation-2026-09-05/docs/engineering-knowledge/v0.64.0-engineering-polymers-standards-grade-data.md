# v0.64.0 — Standards, Exact-Grade Data & Selection Examples

The Engineering Polymers foundation now has the connective tissue required to
turn family-level knowledge into controlled engineering records.

## Standards/test-method mapping

Property domains are mapped to commonly used ISO/ASTM/IEC methods. The map is
a reference aid, not a claim that every method applies to every grade or
application. Method edition, specimen preparation and conditioning remain part
of the evidence.

## Exact-grade schema

An `EngineeringPolymerGrade` record now explicitly carries:
- supplier and grade identity;
- reinforcement;
- process form;
- property records;
- environmental records;
- processing window;
- conditioning state;
- provenance.

Numerical properties require units, test context and traceable source data.

## Selection metadata

The library distinguishes technical material data from commercial metadata:
availability, cost, supply risk and substitution are separate fields and must
not be inferred from family identity.

## Worked examples

Four screening workflows demonstrate:
- gear selection;
- polymer press fits;
- bolted-joint clamp-load retention;
- reinforced structural components.

These are deliberately method-focused and do not invent universal numerical
allowables.

## Evidence hierarchy

Screening → verified grade data → qualified application evidence.

The promotion gate requires exact-grade identity and traceability before
numerical values can become controlled engineering data.
