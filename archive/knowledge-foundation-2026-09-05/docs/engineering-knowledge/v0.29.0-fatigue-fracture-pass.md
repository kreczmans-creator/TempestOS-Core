# Engineering Knowledge Foundation v0.29.0

## Fatigue / fracture / impact evidence pass

This release begins populating the evidence layer defined in v0.28.0.

### 13-8 Mo
A published fretting-fatigue study reports an endurance limit of 419 MPa stress amplitude at R=0.1 for its reported test configuration. The result is literature evidence, not a generic grade allowable.

### 17-4 PH
A high-temperature high-cycle-fatigue study covering A, H900 and H1150 from room temperature to 400 °C is indexed. Its key finding is condition and temperature dependence, not a universal fatigue value.

### 15-5 PH
Fracture-toughness evidence now includes a separate AM/L-PBF H1150M result and a wrought heat-treatment-sensitivity study. AM and wrought evidence remain segregated.

### 440C
Technical literature records the trade-off between high hardness/wear resistance and limitations in toughness/fatigue resistance.

### Test-method registry
ASTM E466, E647, E399, E1820, E23 and ISO 12135 are represented as method references.

## Engineering rule
Fatigue, crack growth, impact and fracture toughness remain separate property domains. They must not be collapsed into a single “strength” field.
