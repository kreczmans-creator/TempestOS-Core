# Engineering Knowledge Foundation v0.26.0

## Steel catalogue expansion

### 15-5 PH
Added condition-specific mechanical data for H900, H1025, H1075, H1100 and H1150, plus condition-dependent density, resistivity and thermal conductivity. Data is retained as supplier/product evidence and is not a generic grade allowable.

### 13-8 Mo
Added H950 source-backed reference data: UTS 1550 MPa, 0.2% yield 1450 MPa, elongation 12%, modulus 197 GPa and density 7.8 g/cm³. Charpy impact is also recorded with its test-temperature context.

### 310S
Added high-temperature stainless identity, product forms, applications and service context. Outokumpu describes 310S/4845 as an austenitic heat-resistant grade with good oxidation/high-temperature corrosion resistance and good elevated-temperature mechanical strength.

### 321
Added EN/ASTM/UNS mapping, titanium-stabilized austenitic classification, elevated-temperature context and product availability. 321H remains a distinct state for high-temperature work.

### 430
Added source-backed room-temperature mechanical values by product form under EN 10088-2:
- cold rolled: Rp0.2 280 MPa, Rm 430–600 MPa, A 20%;
- hot rolled: Rp0.2 260 MPa, Rm 430–600 MPa, A 18%.

### 440C
Added BÖHLER N695 source binding: 440C / 1.4125 / X105CrMo17 / S44004, with ASTM A276/A276M and ASTM F899 links, product/application context and hardness capability up to approximately 60 HRC.

## Library status

The catalogue is now broad enough to support meaningful comparative material-selection work, but evidence verification remains deliberately incomplete. `sourced/reviewed` observations must not be interpreted as design allowables.
