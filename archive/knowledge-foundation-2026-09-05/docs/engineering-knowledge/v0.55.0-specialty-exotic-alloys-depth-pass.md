# v0.55.0 — Specialty Corrosion and High-Performance Exotic Alloys

The Exotics library now includes a specialty family covering corrosion-resistant,
high-strength and functional alloy systems.

## Representative systems

- Zirconium 702 and 705
- Hafnium
- Hastelloy C-276 and C-22
- Alloy 825
- Alloy 59
- MP35N
- Nitinol

## Engineering coverage

- mechanical selection
- thermal properties
- environment-specific corrosion mechanisms
- localized corrosion and stress-corrosion considerations
- hydrogen effects
- fatigue/corrosion-fatigue
- functional fatigue for shape-memory alloys
- manufacturing and processing
- joining
- standards mapping
- provenance
- relative cost and availability
- application selection

## Engineering principle

Corrosion resistance is not a generic material attribute. The relevant
environment, temperature, concentration, impurities, stress state, surface
condition and manufacturing condition must travel with the material record.

## Evidence boundary

Records remain screening-level until quantitative evidence is tied to exact
grade, specification, product form, condition, environment, test method and source.
