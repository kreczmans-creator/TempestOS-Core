## Critical engineering principle

For refractory metals, **melting point is not a service-environment specification**.
Many systems that are mechanically attractive at extreme temperature require
vacuum, inert/reducing atmosphere or an engineered protection system.

## Evidence boundary

The records are screening-level. Quantitative design use requires exact grade,
composition, product form, condition, processing route, temperature, atmosphere,
test method and source.
