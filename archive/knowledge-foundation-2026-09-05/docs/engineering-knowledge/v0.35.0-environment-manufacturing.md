# v0.35.0 — Environmental and Manufacturing Fundamentals

## Environmental coverage

The steel library now records family-level environmental behaviour for all
16 priority grades, including:

- general atmospheric corrosion
- chloride exposure and localized corrosion
- stress-corrosion cracking
- hydrogen-assisted cracking considerations
- galvanic compatibility

The database deliberately does not convert generic environmental descriptions
into universal corrosion rates or design allowables. Corrosion performance is
highly dependent on environment, temperature, concentration, stress, surface
condition and product/heat-treatment state.

## Manufacturing coverage

Family-level process metadata now identifies the principal manufacturing and
joining considerations relevant to each steel family.

## Engineering control

Environmental protection systems — coatings, plating, passivation and
surface treatments — are recorded as separate engineering interventions.
They must not be conflated with the intrinsic base-material property.

No material is approved for design by this release.
