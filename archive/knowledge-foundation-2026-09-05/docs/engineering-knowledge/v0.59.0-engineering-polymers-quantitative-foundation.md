# v0.59.0 — Engineering Polymers Quantitative Mechanical + Thermal Foundation

Representative family-level screening ranges have now been populated for ABS,
PA6, PA66, PA12, POM, PC, PBT, PPS, PEI, PPA, LCP and PEEK.

Covered first-pass quantities:
- density
- elastic modulus
- tensile strength
- elongation
- HDT/service-temperature reference

These are screening values only. Exact grade, formulation, product form,
conditioning, test method, temperature, orientation and authoritative source
remain mandatory before quantitative data can be promoted for design use.

Next: electrical properties, environmental/chemical behaviour, creep/stress
relaxation, fatigue/wear, and exact grade-specific standards/source mapping.
