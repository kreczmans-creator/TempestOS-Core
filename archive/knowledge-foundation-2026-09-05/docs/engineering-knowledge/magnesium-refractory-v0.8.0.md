## Magnesium and refractory-material population

This pass extends the catalogue into the lightweight/high-temperature specialist material space.

### Magnesium
- AZ31B
- AZ61A
- AZ80A
- AZ91D
- WE43

### Tungsten/refractory
- commercially pure tungsten
- lanthanated tungsten electrode material
- 90W tungsten heavy alloy
- commercially pure molybdenum
- commercially pure tantalum

## Why these families matter to TempestOS

These materials expose requirements that a simple "material + strength" database cannot handle:

- extremely low/high density
- strong temperature dependence
- oxidation/environment constraints
- process-specific properties
- directionality and product-form effects
- specialist availability
- very large cost differences

The material model therefore needs to support applicability and provenance as first-class information.

## Status

All numeric entries in this pass remain `candidate_reference`. They are engineering screening values, not released design data.
