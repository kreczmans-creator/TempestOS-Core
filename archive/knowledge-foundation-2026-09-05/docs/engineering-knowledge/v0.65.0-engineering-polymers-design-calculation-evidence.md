# v0.65.0 — Design Calculations, Compatibility & Evidence Control

The Engineering Polymers foundation now includes the principal connective
mechanisms required for repeatable engineering screening.

## Design calculations

Transparent calculation patterns cover:
- thermal expansion;
- creep screening;
- stress relaxation;
- press-fit behaviour;
- bolted-joint bearing checks;
- gear tribology screening.

The equations are deliberately generic and expose their assumptions and
required inputs. They do not create material allowables where the library has
no qualified data.

## Compatibility

A media/environment screening matrix is established for common water,
humidity, chemical, hydrocarbon, solvent, UV and vacuum exposures. Compatibility
must be evaluated against concentration, temperature, duration, stress and
exact grade.

## Manufacturing and inspection

The library links process state to engineering performance and identifies
controls for injection moulding, machining, additive manufacturing and
inspection.

## Evidence control

Source capture, technical review, unit/test-method checks, conflict handling,
promotion levels and release controls are now explicitly defined.

## Fundamentals status

The Engineering Polymers foundation has progressed from material description
through properties, environment, time dependence, manufacturing, applications,
standards, calculation patterns and evidence governance.
