## Fundamental coverage

Mechanical, thermal, electrical, environmental/chemical, fatigue/creep,
manufacturing, joining, standards, provenance, economics and applications.

## Critical engineering distinction

Polymer behaviour is strongly dependent on **temperature, time, conditioning
state and processing history**. Long-term structural design therefore cannot
be based on room-temperature tensile strength alone.

## Evidence boundary

The current records establish the library taxonomy and engineering-selection
framework. They are screening-level until exact grades and authoritative
test data are populated.
