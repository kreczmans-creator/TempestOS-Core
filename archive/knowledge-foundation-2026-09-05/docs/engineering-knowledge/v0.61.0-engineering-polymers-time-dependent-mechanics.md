# v0.61.0 — Engineering Polymers Creep, Stress Relaxation, Fatigue + Wear

The Engineering Polymers library now has a first-pass engineering behaviour
model for long-duration loading, cyclic loading and tribological applications.

## Creep / stress relaxation

Coverage includes:
- creep strain/compliance
- creep modulus
- stress relaxation
- retained clamp load
- creep rupture considerations

The library explicitly treats stress, temperature, time and conditioning as
first-order variables.

## Fatigue

Coverage includes:
- S-N / Wöhler behaviour
- strain-life where appropriate
- crack-growth behaviour where available
- stress ratio, frequency, temperature and environment

## Wear / tribology

Coverage includes:
- friction
- wear rate
- PV limits
- counterface effects
- surface finish
- lubrication
- temperature
- debris

The key architectural rule is that wear is treated as a **contact-system
property**, not a universal material constant.

## Evidence boundary

All records remain screening-level. No long-term allowable stress, fatigue
limit or wear life is inferred from generic family data.
