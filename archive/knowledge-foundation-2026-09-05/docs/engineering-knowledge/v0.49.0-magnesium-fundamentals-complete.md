# v0.49.0 — Magnesium Fundamentals Complete

The Magnesium family now has a complete **fundamentals architecture** covering
material identity, representative alloy families, mechanical behaviour,
fatigue, fracture, thermal and electrical behaviour, corrosion, manufacturing,
machining/fire controls, joining, surface treatment, standards, economics,
availability, applications and provenance.

This is a **library foundation milestone**, not a certification of numerical
design data.
