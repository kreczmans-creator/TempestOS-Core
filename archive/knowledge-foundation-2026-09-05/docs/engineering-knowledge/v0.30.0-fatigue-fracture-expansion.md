# Engineering Knowledge Foundation v0.30.0

## Fatigue / crack-growth expansion

v0.30.0 expands the fatigue/fracture layer into condition-aware evidence records.

### 17-4 PH
The library now distinguishes room-temperature rotating-beam fatigue strength, elevated-temperature fatigue strength, room-temperature crack-growth behaviour, high-temperature crack-growth behaviour, corrosion-fatigue behaviour, and AM crack-growth threshold/cyclic-toughness evidence.

The numerical values remain screening evidence and are tied to the reported specimen/product form and method.

### 15-5 PH
AM fatigue-crack-growth and fracture-toughness research is indexed as a separate evidence population. Orientation and defect effects remain explicit.

### Data-model hardening
Property semantics formally distinguish fatigue strength, fatigue crack growth, fatigue threshold and fracture toughness.

No conversion between these domains is permitted without an explicit engineering transformation/model.

## Release rule
The evidence layer is reference material for engineering decisions. It is not a library of certified design allowables.
