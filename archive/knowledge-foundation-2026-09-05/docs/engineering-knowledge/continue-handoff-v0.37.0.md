## Next family: Aluminium

Reuse the established material-family ontology without weakening its evidence
controls.

Populate aluminium in the same sequence:

1. grade identity and common designations
2. mechanical fundamentals
3. thermal fundamentals
4. electrical fundamentals
5. magnetic behaviour
6. environmental/corrosion behaviour
7. manufacturing and joining
8. common standards and test methods
9. relative cost, availability and uses
10. family coverage audit

Priority aluminium families should include common wrought alloys, cast alloys,
and engineering grades relevant to aerospace, machinery, structures,
enclosures and thermal/electrical applications.

Maintain the distinction between reference data and design-approved data.
