# v0.50.0 — Exotics Taxonomy and Foundation

The Exotics material family has been opened with a controlled taxonomy.

## Initial families

1. Titanium and titanium alloys
2. Nickel-base superalloys
3. Cobalt-base alloys
4. Refractory metals and alloys
5. Zirconium and specialist reactive metals
6. Specialty copper alloys
7. High-hardness/wear-resistant specialty alloys
8. Specialty systems such as shape-memory alloys

The first populated engineering records are commercially pure titanium,
Ti-6Al-4V, Inconel 718, Inconel 625 and Haynes 230.

## Core engineering model

Exotic-material selection must account for temperature, time, environment,
manufacturing route, microstructure, joining and qualification burden. Trade
names are not accepted as sole material identity.

## Evidence boundary

Quantitative values remain screening/reference information until exact
grade/product-form/condition evidence is source-linked and applicability
checked.
