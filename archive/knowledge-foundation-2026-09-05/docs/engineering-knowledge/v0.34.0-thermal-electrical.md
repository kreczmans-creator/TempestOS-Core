# v0.34.0 — Thermal, Electrical and Magnetic Fundamentals

Thermal reference coverage is now present for all 16 priority steel grades:
thermal conductivity, coefficient of thermal expansion and specific heat.

Electrical resistivity reference coverage is present for all 16 grades.
Magnetic behaviour is classified across all 16 grades, with an explicit
condition caveat for austenitic stainless steels.

These are engineering-reference screening data, not certified design
allowables. Temperature dependence, exact material condition, product form,
specification and provenance remain mandatory for design use.
