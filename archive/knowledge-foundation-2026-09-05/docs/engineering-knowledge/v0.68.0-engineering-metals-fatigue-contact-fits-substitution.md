# v0.68.0 — Engineering Metals: Fatigue, Contact, Fits & Substitution

This release deepens the metals foundation into core mechanical design
decision-making.

## Fatigue and fracture

The library now distinguishes:
- stress-life;
- strain-life;
- fracture-mechanics crack-growth approaches.

It explicitly treats mean stress, stress concentration, surface/process state,
corrosion, fretting, defects and inspection intervals as fatigue-design
variables.

## Contact and wear

Contact fundamentals cover Hertzian-style screening and the major wear modes:
adhesive, abrasive, fretting, erosive, surface-fatigue and galling.

Surface engineering is linked to the substrate, load, contact mode, corrosion
environment, temperature and dimensional requirements.

## Fits and tolerances

Fit selection now includes clearance, transition and interference fits,
differential thermal expansion and tolerance-stack methodology.

Worst-case, RSS and Monte Carlo approaches are treated as engineering methods
whose assumptions must be justified.

## Material substitution

Material substitution is explicitly treated as an engineering change. The
workflow compares strength, stiffness, density, toughness, fatigue, corrosion,
wear, temperature, manufacturing, joining, cost, availability and traceability.

## Worked design cases

Four reusable frameworks cover:
- rotating-shaft fatigue;
- contact/bearing assessment;
- steel interference fits;
- controlled material substitution.

## Fundamentals boundary

These are repeatable design frameworks, not uncontrolled design allowables.
Exact grade/condition, geometry, process, environment and verification evidence
remain mandatory for release-quality engineering decisions.
