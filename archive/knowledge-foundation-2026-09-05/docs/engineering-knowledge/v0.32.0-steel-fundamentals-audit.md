# v0.32.0 — Steel Fundamentals Audit

## Purpose

v0.32.0 is the formal **Steel Fundamentals Audit**.

The purpose is not to claim that every numerical property is already populated.
It establishes the complete fundamental property ontology and audits each of
the 16 priority steel grades against that ontology.

## Fundamental property classes

The audit covers identity/designation, chemistry, family, condition, product
form, density, mechanical properties, hardness/ductility/toughness, fatigue
and fracture, thermal properties, electrical/magnetic properties, corrosion
and environmental behaviour, manufacturing/joining, applications,
availability, relative cost, standards, provenance/evidence quality and
engineering-use restrictions.

## Important status distinction

A property can be:

1. **Defined** — the TempestOS model knows the property exists.
2. **Present** — an explicit record currently exists for the grade/property.
3. **Verified** — the value/context has passed independent verification.
4. **Approved for design** — formally authorised for design use.

These states are deliberately not interchangeable.

v0.32.0 therefore closes the architectural question — *what are the
fundamentals?* — while exposing the remaining population gaps as an
actionable ledger.

## Current priority grades

S235JR, S355JR, C45, 42CrMo4, 304, 316L, 17-4 PH, H13, 410, 420, 15-5 PH,
13-8 Mo, 310S, 321, 430 and 440C.
