# v0.47.0 — Magnesium Fundamentals Foundation

Magnesium is now the active material family.

## Initial alloy coverage

The foundation includes commercially pure magnesium plus representative
wrought, cast, rare-earth and high-strength families:

- Commercially pure magnesium
- AZ31B
- AZ91D
- WE43
- ZK60A

Each record captures the established fundamental ontology: composition,
density, elastic properties, tensile properties, thermal/electrical behaviour,
corrosion, manufacturing, machinability, cost, availability and uses.

## Magnesium-specific engineering controls

The model explicitly treats the following as first-class considerations:

- galvanic corrosion
- environment-dependent corrosion
- machining chip/fire risk
- wrought texture and anisotropy
- cast porosity and microstructure
- temperature-dependent strength

## Evidence boundary

All quantitative values are screening/reference estimates pending
grade/product-form/temper-specific authoritative provenance.
