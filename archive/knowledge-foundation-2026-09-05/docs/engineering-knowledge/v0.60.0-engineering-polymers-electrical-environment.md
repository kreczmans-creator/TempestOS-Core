# v0.60.0 — Engineering Polymers Electrical + Environmental Foundation

The Engineering Polymers library now includes first-pass electrical and
environmental/chemical screening records for the principal families.

## Electrical

Coverage:
- volume resistivity
- surface resistivity
- dielectric strength
- relative permittivity
- dissipation factor
- tracking/arc resistance where relevant

The records explicitly retain frequency, temperature and conditioning as
engineering variables.

## Environmental / chemical

Coverage:
- water/moisture
- acids/alkalis
- oils/fuels
- organic solvents
- UV
- hydrolysis
- vacuum/outgassing considerations

Chemical compatibility remains condition-dependent: concentration, temperature,
exposure duration and applied stress must be retained.

## Evidence boundary

This is screening-level evidence. Exact grade, formulation, test context and
authoritative source remain mandatory before design promotion.
