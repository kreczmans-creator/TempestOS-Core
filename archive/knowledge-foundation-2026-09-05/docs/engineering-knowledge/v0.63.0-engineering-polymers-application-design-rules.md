# v0.63.0 — Engineering Polymers Application & Design Rules

The Engineering Polymers library now moves from material-property fundamentals
into application-oriented engineering guidance.

## Applications covered

- gears
- bearings
- bushings
- seals
- snap-fits
- threaded/bolted joints
- press fits
- structural polymer parts

Each application has:
- a selection rule;
- principal failure modes;
- engineering control variables;
- candidate material families;
- an explicit evidence boundary.

## Fundamental engineering principle

Polymer selection is a coupled **material + process + application** decision.

For long-life components, short-term tensile data alone are insufficient.
Creep, stress relaxation, fatigue, wear, thermal behaviour, environmental
compatibility and manufacturing state must be considered together.

## Evidence boundary

These records are design-screening guidance. They are not application-specific
allowables or certification evidence. Exact grade data and application-specific
verification remain mandatory.
