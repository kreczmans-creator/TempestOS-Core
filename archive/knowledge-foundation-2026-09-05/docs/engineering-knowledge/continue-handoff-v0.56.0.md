Exotics consolidation and structural completeness audit complete.

Next major branch: **Engineering Polymers**.

Populate the polymer library using the same fundamental schema:

- material families and grades
- mechanical properties
- thermal properties
- electrical properties
- environmental/chemical resistance
- fatigue/creep
- manufacturing/processability
- joining
- common standards
- provenance
- relative cost
- availability
- general uses
- engineering selection rules
- governance/evidence state

Begin with the major engineering polymer families and do not treat supplier
datasheets as interchangeable with standards-based material identity.
