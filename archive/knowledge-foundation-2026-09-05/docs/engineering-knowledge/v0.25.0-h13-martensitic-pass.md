# Engineering Knowledge Foundation v0.25.0

## H13 numerical wrought evidence

BÖHLER W302 ISOBLOC numerical delivery-condition evidence has been added. The source identifies 1.2344 / X40CrMoV5-1 / T20813 / H13 and EN ISO 4957. Annealed hardness is recorded as max 229 HB. Hardened-and-tempered hardness ranges are retained exactly as published and remain tied to the manufacturer's product context.

## 410 / 420

Outokumpu Dura source records have been added for 410/4006 and 420/4021, including designation, martensitic structure, product forms and typical application context. Supplier wallchart UTS values are retained as typical supplier data, not minimum design properties.

## Evidence discipline

No supplier typical value is promoted to a normative allowable. No value is converted between hardness and strength without a separate explicit correlation record. Verification remains a distinct state.
