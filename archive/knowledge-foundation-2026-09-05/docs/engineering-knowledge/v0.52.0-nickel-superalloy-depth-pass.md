# v0.52.0 — Nickel-Base Superalloy Depth Pass

The Exotics library now has a second deeply structured engineering family:
nickel-base superalloys.

## Representative grades

- Alloy 718 / Inconel 718
- Alloy 625 / Inconel 625
- Haynes 230
- 718Plus
- Waspaloy

## Engineering coverage

The family now explicitly models:

- precipitation and solid-solution strengthening
- temperature-dependent mechanical behaviour
- creep and stress rupture
- HCF/LCF/thermo-mechanical fatigue
- oxidation and hot corrosion
- heat treatment and microstructure
- forging, casting, machining and additive manufacturing
- welding, brazing and bolted joints
- standards and provenance
- relative cost and availability
- application selection

## Evidence boundary

All quantitative information remains screening-level unless the exact grade,
product form, condition, test context and source are linked and reviewed.
