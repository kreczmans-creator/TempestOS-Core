## Critical modelling note for polymers

Polymer properties are strongly affected by temperature, moisture, strain rate, reinforcement, orientation, crystallinity and processing. Therefore the simple room-temperature baseline is intentionally insufficient for final design use.

For glass-filled polymers, reinforcement orientation and moulding direction need to become explicit condition metadata.
