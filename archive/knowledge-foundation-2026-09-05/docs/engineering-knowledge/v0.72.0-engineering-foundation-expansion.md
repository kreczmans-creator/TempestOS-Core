# v0.72.0 — Engineering Foundation Expansion

This release extends the foundation beyond component mechanics into the
material/process, measurement, tribology, thermal/fluid, actuation and
electromechanical interface layers.

## Materials and process selection

Material choice is connected explicitly to grade, condition, product form,
manufacturing process and heat treatment. The library treats the resulting
material/process state as part of the controlled engineering definition.

## Metrology

Measurement-system fundamentals now include accuracy, precision, repeatability,
reproducibility, bias, resolution, traceability and uncertainty. Inspection
strategy is linked to functional characteristics and datum/reference systems.

## Tribology

Friction, lubrication regimes and wear diagnosis are structured around the
complete contact system: material pair, load, speed, temperature, surface and
contamination.

## Fluid and thermal interfaces

First-order pressure-loss and thermal-resistance reasoning is introduced for
mechanical systems. The simplified equations are explicitly bounded as
screening methods.

## Actuation and transmission

Actuator sizing, transmission selection and motion profiles connect mechanical
load, inertia, speed, duty cycle, efficiency, stiffness, backlash and thermal
limits.

## Electromechanical interfaces

Mechanical mounting, shaft/coupling, electrical power, feedback, thermal
management and control interfaces are treated as one end-to-end engineering
chain.

## Systems verification

Verification is extended into interface control, risk-based verification and
evidence-quality criteria.

## Worked cases

Five cross-domain cases provide repeatable engineering workflows:
- material/process selection;
- CMM inspection strategy;
- lubricated contact;
- actuated axis;
- interface verification.

## Boundary

This remains a knowledge and engineering-reasoning foundation. It does not
create universal design allowables, replace standards, or constitute product
certification.
