# v0.36.0 — Standards, Manufacturing and Commercial Fundamentals

The steel library now has common material-standard and test-method mappings for
all 16 priority grades, plus qualitative cost, availability and common-use
metadata.

The standards layer is a **reference mapping**, not a procurement
certification. Current specification edition, product form, condition,
inspection class and certification requirements must be checked against the
actual purchase specification.

Commercial fields intentionally use broad categories:
**cheap / medium / expensive** and **very_high / high / medium availability**.
They are planning metadata, not live quotations.

Manufacturing metadata captures the major selection considerations without
pretending that a family-level rating replaces a qualified process procedure.
