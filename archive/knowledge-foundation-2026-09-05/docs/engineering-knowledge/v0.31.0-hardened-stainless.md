# Engineering Knowledge Foundation v0.31.0

## Hardened stainless / tool-steel condition matrix

v0.31.0 adds condition-aware reference coverage for AISI 410, 420 and 440C.
The records deliberately distinguish annealed and hardened states and retain
the fact that these grades are martensitic/ferromagnetic stainless steels.

### Engineering interpretation

- **410:** useful balance of corrosion resistance, strength and hardenability;
  magnetic and condition-sensitive.
- **420:** higher carbon martensitic stainless with higher achievable hardness;
  magnetic and commonly selected where wear resistance is more important.
- **440C:** very high hardness/wear capability, with the expected trade-off in
  toughness and corrosion performance relative with lower-carbon stainless
  grades.

The values in this layer are **screening/reference data**, not certified
design allowables.

## Fatigue data contract

The package now defines a strict fatigue-curve-point record. Downstream
TempestOS consumers must not accept a fatigue datum that has lost the material
state, stress ratio, temperature/environment, method or provenance.

This is the bridge between the human-readable engineering library and the
future computational material-selection/query layer.
