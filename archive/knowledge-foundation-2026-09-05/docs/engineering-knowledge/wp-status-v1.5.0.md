### Headline

**10/12 complete = 83% complete at workstream level.**

The remaining 2 sections are not empty. They are the two areas where the catalogue needs to become trustworthy and operational:

1. **Standards/provenance/designation governance**
   - attach exact source evidence to individual observations;
   - reconcile standard editions;
   - resolve designation mappings;
   - establish verification/promotion workflow.

2. **Selection/validation/application intelligence**
   - connect the real catalogue to the selection contract;
   - implement deterministic unit/property filtering;
   - deepen temperature, fatigue, fracture, corrosion and process data;
   - build end-to-end golden tests.

### Catalogue breadth

The remembered scope is now represented across:
**steels, aluminium, magnesium, tungsten/refractory, exotic alloys, copper/copper alloys, engineering polymers and general polymers.**

The database therefore has the **breadth phase essentially complete**. The next phase is **depth + evidence + integration**, rather than adding more top-level families.
