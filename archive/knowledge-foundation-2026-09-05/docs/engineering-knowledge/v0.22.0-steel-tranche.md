# Engineering Knowledge Foundation v0.22.0

## Steel evidence tranche

### 304

The Outokumpu Core range table provides EN 10088-2 mechanical values at 20 °C for three product forms of Core 304/4301:
- cold rolled coil/sheet: Rp0.2 230 MPa; Rp1.0 260 MPa; Rm 540–750 MPa; A 45%;
- hot rolled coil/sheet: Rp0.2 210 MPa; Rp1.0 250 MPa; Rm 520–720 MPa; A 45%;
- quarto plate: Rp0.2 210 MPa; Rp1.0 250 MPa; Rm 520–720 MPa; A 45%.

The values remain tied to the source's product-form and standard context.

### 17-4 PH

ATI publishes a condition matrix for 1.4542 / 17-4 PH. The tranche records H900, H1025, H1075, H1150, H1150M and H1150-D, retaining UTS, 0.2% yield strength, elongation and Brinell hardness.

This is particularly important because 17-4 PH cannot be represented by one generic strength value: age condition is a first-class material-state attribute.

### H13

A process-specific H13 dataset from Oerlikon Metco has been added for laser powder bed fusion. The data is explicitly classified as `PROCESS_SPECIFIC_REFERENCE`; it is not mixed with conventional wrought H13.

### Evidence boundary

The new observations are source-backed and reviewed, but remain subject to the existing verification gate. None are approved for design use.
