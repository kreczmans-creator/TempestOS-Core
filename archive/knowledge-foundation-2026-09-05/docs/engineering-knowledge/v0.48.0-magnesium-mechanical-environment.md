# v0.48.0 — Magnesium Mechanical and Environmental Depth

The magnesium foundation has been deepened from basic material cards into
engineering selection behaviour.

## Added

- fatigue/S-N framework
- fracture and crack-growth framework
- elevated-temperature strength and creep framework
- galvanic compatibility model
- joining-process framework
- surface-treatment framework

## Key engineering principle

Magnesium selection is strongly context dependent. Grade alone is insufficient
for many engineering decisions. Product form, temper, orientation, surface
condition, environment, temperature and manufacturing route must remain attached
to the material record.

## Evidence boundary

Screening values and qualitative engineering rules are not design allowables.
Qualification requires authoritative, condition-specific evidence.
