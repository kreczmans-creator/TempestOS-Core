# v0.53.0 — Cobalt-Base Alloy Depth Pass

The Exotics library now has a structured cobalt-base family covering both
high-temperature structural alloys and wear/hardfacing systems.

## Representative systems

- CoCrMo
- Stellite 6
- Haynes 188
- Haynes 25 / L-605

## Engineering coverage

- mechanical selection
- elevated-temperature behaviour
- creep/rupture considerations
- fatigue
- wear and tribology
- aqueous corrosion and high-temperature oxidation
- casting, forging, machining, hardfacing and additive manufacturing
- welding, brazing and bolting
- standards mapping
- provenance
- relative cost and availability
- application selection

## Evidence boundary

Screening records are not design allowables. Exact specification, product form,
condition, processing route and test context must accompany quantitative data.
