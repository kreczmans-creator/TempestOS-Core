# v0.70.0 — Integrated Engineering Mechanics

This release consolidates the metals knowledge foundation into cross-domain
mechanical engineering reasoning.

## Combined loading

The library now provides structured treatment of axial force, bending and
torsion, including load-path tracing and static interaction screening.

## Stability and buckling

Column and local-plate instability are explicitly separated from material
strength. Slenderness, end conditions, imperfections, local buckling and
inelastic behaviour are included in the screening workflow.

## Thermal-mechanical coupling

Thermal expansion, differential CTE, restraint, gradients and thermal cycling
are connected to mechanical response, alignment, joint behaviour and fatigue.

## Dynamics and vibration

First-order natural-frequency, forced-response and modal-analysis workflows
are established. Dynamic adequacy is explicitly treated as distinct from
static strength.

## Verification

The verification layer distinguishes:
- requirements verification;
- analytical checks;
- model verification;
- numerical convergence/sensitivity;
- component/system testing;
- model-to-test correlation.

## Integrated cases

Five reusable cases combine the preceding fundamentals:
- combined-loaded bracket;
- buckling member;
- thermally restrained assembly;
- resonant component;
- integrated bracket verification.

## Boundary

These frameworks are intended to make engineering reasoning repeatable and
auditable. They are not substitutes for applicable design codes, validated
models, qualified material data, or application-specific verification.
