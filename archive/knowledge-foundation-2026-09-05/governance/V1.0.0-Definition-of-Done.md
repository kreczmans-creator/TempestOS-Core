# V1.0.0 Definition of Done

**Status:** Approved by the Product Owner, 2026-09-05.
**Applies to:** TempestOS Engineering Knowledge Foundation.
**Supersedes:** no prior definition — none existed. Seventy-four versions
were produced without one, which is why "complete" has meant something
different in each of them.

---

## 1. The Decision This Document Records

The v0.74.0 baseline inspection (`baseline/BASELINE-v0.74.0.md`) measured
the package rather than trusting its status files, and found:

- 684 content records, of which **447 declare themselves placeholder**,
  151 declare no status at all, 84 require review, 2 are sourced and
  **none is verified**;
- an engineering knowledge layer of **14,380 words** spanning 44+
  disciplines, at a **median of 1 of 15** required depth elements, with
  **zero** records reaching 10;
- no populated numeric property dataset behind the materials catalogue.

Two responses were possible. Either hold v1.0.0 until the content is
authored and source-bound — a programme of months and a materials
licensing budget — or **ship the structure and classify the data
honestly as provisional**.

**The Product Owner chose the second.** The data stays as it is, marked
as placeholder, while the product moves toward a release-ready state
around it.

That decision is legitimate and this document makes it explicit rather
than letting it be inferred later from a version number.

## 2. What V1.0.0 Therefore Is

> **TempestOS Engineering Knowledge Foundation v1.0.0 —
> Structure, Governance and Provenance Baseline.**

V1.0.0 is a release of **the framework the knowledge will live in**: its
taxonomy, its schema, its provenance model, its maturity classification,
its governance registers and its automated validation — with a data
layer that is **complete in extent and provisional in content**, and
which says so in every record and in a generated register anyone can
re-derive.

**V1.0.0 is not a claim that the engineering content is complete,
correct, sourced, or fit for design use.** It is a claim that the
structure is sound and that the content's status is stated truthfully
and checkably everywhere.

## 4. V1.0.0 Explicitly Excludes

These are **not** defects in v1.0.0. They are the release's declared
boundary, and each is registered as debt with a disposition.

| Excluded | Why | Where recorded |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering content at teaching depth (the 15-element standard, §7) | Not authored yet — 14,380 words exist against an estimated 100,000–170,000 required | `TD-K01`, AMBER, deferred to the Content Programme |
| Source-bound numeric material properties | Requires licensed standards and datasheet ingestion; not a content-authoring problem but a licensing and pipeline one | `TD-K02`, AMBER, deferred |
| Design allowables of any kind | Cannot be derived from this package and must never appear to be | `TD-K03`, closed by exclusion |
| Verified or certified status for any record | Nothing in the package has been independently verified | Validator enforces |
| Standards text, tables or reproduced clauses | Copyrighted; the library cites standards by number and title only | Permanent exclusion |
| Advanced/specialist subjects — FEA, CFD, advanced control theory, RF, fracture mechanics beyond fundamentals, specialist reliability mathematics, regulatory compliance, product certification | Outside a fundamentals baseline by definition | Advanced Programme, post-v1.0 |

## 5. The Design-Use Boundary

Stated once, and repeated in the package README:

> **No value, rule, threshold or worked case in this package is approved
> for design use.** The content is screening-level and provisional. Any
> product engineering decision must be taken against the applicable
> controlled standard, a qualified supplier datasheet, or test evidence —
> not against this library.

A record may only lose that boundary by being source-bound and reviewed
under the Content Programme, and the validator is what will prove it.

## 6. Version Identity

The baseline found four competing identities in one package (archive root
`v0.3.0`, `MANIFEST.json` `0.72.0`, suffixed manifest `0.74.0`, status
files `0.74.0`). V1.0.0 resolves this:

- `VERSION` at the package root is **the** authority.
- `MANIFEST.json` is regenerated at every release and always matches it.
- Versioned manifests (`MANIFEST-v0.73.0.json`, `-v0.74.0.json`) are
  retained as historical records and are not updated.
- Version strings inside historical documents in `docs/` are **left
  alone**; they are accurate statements about the release that wrote
  them, and rewriting them would be falsifying a record.

## 7. The Depth Standard (for the Content Programme, not for v1.0.0)

Recorded here so the later programme has a fixed target. A subject
qualifies as covered when its record carries: definition; physical
meaning; governing principles; key variables; units; assumptions;
equations where applicable; engineering interpretation; design
implications; failure implications; verification implications; common
mistakes; limitations; a worked example where justified; and its
relationship to adjacent disciplines.

Fifteen elements. The validator scores every record against them today,
so progress is measurable from the first record authored — the current
median is 1.

## 8. The Completion Gate

V1.0.0 may be declared complete when, and only when, all of the
following are true and evidenced by a validator run:

1. `python3 tools/validate.py --check` exits 0.
2. Zero records claim verification without a source.
3. Every content record is classified, or is listed as an accepted
   `undeclared` exception with a reason.
4. `MANIFEST.json` reconciles exactly against the package.
5. `VERSION` matches the manifest and the release documentation.
6. The Technical Debt Register has no open RED item.
7. Every AMBER item carries a written, dated deferral justification.
8. The design-use boundary appears in the README and in this document.
9. An independent adversarial review has been performed against the
   actual tree, and its findings are dispositioned.
10. The final report states plainly that content depth and source
    binding are **excluded** from this release.

**Gate 6 note.** RED means "blocks this release as defined". The content
deficit is deliberately **not** RED, because this release does not claim
content completeness. Calling it RED would make the gate unpassable by
design; calling it GREEN would be dishonest. It is AMBER with a recorded
justification, which is what it actually is.
