# 18 en1992.factors

Kind: `ec2.national-annex` (EN 1992-1-1 National Annex factors).
Status: existing CalcRecordKind `ec2.national-annex`
Planned name: `en1992.factors`. The built calculator names the kind `ec2.national-annex`. The file name stays.

## Used by
- S6 base plate (built, `struct.base-plate`) reads `alpha_cc` and `gamma_c` for the concrete bearing strength.

## Symbol mapping
- `gamma_c[-]` fills the S6 input `gamma_c`. `alpha_cc[-]` fills the S6 input `alpha_cc`. Both are numbers.
- `situation` identifies the row. The importer stores it but S6 never reads it.
- The importer saves one record per row. Pick the row for your design situation.

## Copy from
- Type gamma_c from the National Annex to BS EN 1992-1-1, the partial factor table (Table 2.1N in the Eurocode).
- Type alpha_cc from the National Annex clause that refers to 3.1.6(1)P.

## Columns
- `situation[text]`: design situation, as the table names it, such as persistent or accidental.
- `gamma_c[-]`: partial factor for concrete.
- `alpha_cc[-]`: coefficient for long-term effects on compressive strength.

## Edition to record
Record the National Annex edition and amendment, and the edition of the Eurocode part it modifies.

## Importer checks
- Reject any row with a blank or non-positive number in a numeric column.
- Reject gamma_c below 1. Reject alpha_cc above 1.
- S6 refuses gamma_c outside 1.2 to 1.8 and alpha_cc outside 0.8 to 1.0.
- Reject a duplicate situation.

## Maps to CalcDataRecord
Kind is `ec2.national-annex`. The importer saves one CalcDataRecord per CSV row.
Each numeric column becomes one CalcDataValue with its Symbol, Number and Unit `-`.
The `situation` text joins the record title. Columns the kind does not list are stored but never applied.
