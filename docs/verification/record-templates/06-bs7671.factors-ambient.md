# 06 bs7671.factors-ambient

Kind: `cable.correction-factors` (ambient, grouping and thermal-insulation factors for one installation method).
Status: existing CalcRecordKind `cable.correction-factors`
Planned name: `bs7671.factors-ambient`. The built calculator names the kind `cable.correction-factors`. The file name stays.

## Used by
- E4 conductor sizing (built, `elec.conductor-sizing`) reads `C_a` into its ambient correction factor input.
- E4 can instead derive C_a from the temperatures. That option is marked as an approximation. Compare it with this table.

## Symbol mapping
- `C_a[-]` is the only applied column. It fills the E4 input `C_a`, a number.
- `medium`, `insulation` and `theta_a` identify the row. The importer stores them but E4 never reads them.
- The E4 input `theta_a` is a separate input. Applying this record never sets it.
- The importer saves one record per row, as for template 12. Pick the row for your ambient temperature.
- Templates 07 and 08 fill the other two symbols of the same kind.

## Copy from
- Type the values from BS 7671:2018 (as amended), Appendix 4.
- Use the ambient air temperature and ambient ground temperature factor tables, such as Tables 4B1 and 4B2.
- Confirm the table numbers against your copy.

## Columns
- `medium[text]`: air or ground, as the table names it.
- `insulation[text]`: insulation type, as the table names it.
- `theta_a[degC]`: ambient temperature.
- `C_a[-]`: ambient temperature correction factor.

## Edition to record
Record the BS 7671 edition and amendment printed on your copy. Use the edition and amendment fields.

## Importer checks
- Reject a blank theta_a or C_a. Reject a non-positive C_a.
- Reject a duplicate medium, insulation and theta_a row.
- Within one medium and insulation, theta_a must rise and C_a must fall.
- E4 refuses C_a outside 0.1 to 1.5. Warn when theta_a is outside -20 to 120 degC.

## Maps to CalcDataRecord
Kind is `cable.correction-factors`. The importer saves one CalcDataRecord per CSV row.
The `C_a` column becomes one CalcDataValue with Symbol `C_a`, a Number and the Unit `-`.
The `medium`, `insulation` and `theta_a` cells join the record title. The kind does not list them, so they are stored but never applied.
