# 07 bs7671.factors-grouping

Kind: `bs7671.factors-grouping` (BS 7671 grouping correction factor Cg).
Status: proposed — no CalcRecordKind yet; Track S confirms symbols when the calculator is built

## Used by
- E4 conductor sizing (planned) multiplies the capacity by C_g.

## Copy from
- Type the values from BS 7671:2018 (as amended), Appendix 4.
- Use the grouping factor tables, such as Tables 4C1 to 4C5.
- Enter one table per file. Confirm the table numbers against your copy.

## Columns
- `arrangement[text]`: arrangement as the table names it, such as bunched or single layer.
- `n_circuits[-]`: number of circuits or multicore cables in the group.
- `C_g[-]`: grouping correction factor.

## Edition to record
Record the BS 7671 edition and amendment printed on your copy. Use the edition and amendment fields.

## Importer checks
- Reject a blank or non-positive C_g. Reject a non-integer n_circuits.
- Reject a duplicate arrangement and n_circuits row.
- Within one arrangement, n_circuits must rise and C_g must not rise.
- Reject a C_g above 1.

## Maps to CalcDataRecord
Kind is `bs7671.factors-grouping`. The importer saves one CalcDataRecord for the file.
One CalcDataValue with Symbol `C_g_table` holds Rows. Each CalcRow has one cell per column, keyed by column symbol.
