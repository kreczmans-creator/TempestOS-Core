# 15 en1990.factors

Kind: `en1990.factors` (EN 1990 partial and psi factors).
Status: existing CalcRecordKind

## Used by
- S5 EN 1990 combinations (built) reads every column. S8, S6 and S7 (planned) take their load combinations from S5.

## Copy from
- Type the partial factors from the National Annex to BS EN 1990, Table NA.A1.2(A) to (C).
- Type the psi factors from the same National Annex, Table NA.A1.1, or from EN 1990 Annex A1.

## Columns
- `gamma_G_sup[-]`: partial factor for unfavourable permanent actions. Fill on the first row only.
- `gamma_G_inf[-]`: partial factor for favourable permanent actions. First row only.
- `gamma_Q[-]`: partial factor for variable actions. First row only.
- `xi[-]`: reduction factor for expression 6.10b. First row only.
- `category[text]`: action category, as the table names it.
- `psi_0[-]`: combination factor.
- `psi_1[-]`: frequent factor.
- `psi_2[-]`: quasi-permanent factor.

## Edition to record
Record the National Annex edition and amendment, and the edition of the Eurocode part it modifies.

## Importer checks
- Reject a blank or negative number in a numeric column. Scalar columns may be blank after the first row.
- Warn when gamma_G_sup is outside 1 to 2 or gamma_G_inf is outside 0.8 to 1.2.
- Warn when gamma_Q is outside 1 to 2 or xi is outside 0.8 to 1.
- Reject a psi row where psi_0 is below psi_1 or psi_1 is below psi_2. Reject a psi above 1 or a duplicate category.

## Maps to CalcDataRecord
Kind is `en1990.factors`. The importer saves one CalcDataRecord for the file.
Scalar columns (`gamma_G_sup`, `gamma_G_inf`, `gamma_Q`, `xi`) become one CalcDataValue each, read from the first data row.
The other columns become Rows under one CalcDataValue with Symbol `psi`.
