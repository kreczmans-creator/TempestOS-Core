# 05 bs7671.voltage-drop

Kind: `bs7671.voltage-drop` (BS 7671 voltage drop per ampere per metre).
Status: proposed — no CalcRecordKind yet; Track S confirms symbols when the calculator is built

## Used by
- E3 voltage drop (built) reads the figure as its mVAm input. E4 conductor sizing (planned) will reuse it.

## Copy from
- Type the values from BS 7671:2018 (as amended), Appendix 4. Use the B-suffix voltage drop tables, such as Table 4D2B.
- Where the table gives one figure, enter it in the z column and leave r and x blank.

## Columns
- `conductor[text]`: copper or aluminium, as the table names it.
- `csa[mm2]`: conductor cross-sectional area.
- `r_dc[mV/A/m]`: two cables, dc.
- `r_1ph[mV/A/m]`: two cables, single-phase ac, resistive component.
- `x_1ph[mV/A/m]`: two cables, single-phase ac, reactive component.
- `z_1ph[mV/A/m]`: two cables, single-phase ac, impedance.
- `r_3ph[mV/A/m]`: three or four cables, three-phase ac, resistive component.
- `x_3ph[mV/A/m]`: three or four cables, three-phase ac, reactive component.
- `z_3ph[mV/A/m]`: three or four cables, three-phase ac, impedance.

## Edition to record
Record the BS 7671 edition and amendment printed on your copy. Use the edition and amendment fields.

## Importer checks
- Reject a blank or non-positive csa, a negative mV/A/m value or a duplicate conductor and csa row.
- Within one conductor, csa must rise and each filled column must fall.
- Warn when z is below r, or differs from the root of r2 plus x2 by over 5 percent.
- Warn when a value is outside 0.1 to 100 mV/A/m, the range of the E3 mVAm input.

## Maps to CalcDataRecord
Kind is `bs7671.voltage-drop`. The importer saves one CalcDataRecord for the file.
One CalcDataValue with Symbol `mVAm_table` holds Rows. Each CalcRow has one cell per column, keyed by column symbol.
