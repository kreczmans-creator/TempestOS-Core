# 13 ec3.partial-factors

Kind: `ec3.partial-factors` (EN 1993-1-1 National Annex partial factors).
Status: proposed — no CalcRecordKind yet; Track S confirms symbols when the calculator is built

## Used by
- S3 member checks (built) can take its resistance factors from this record.
- S8 lifting lugs, S6 base plate and S7 LTB (all planned) need gamma_M0, gamma_M1 and gamma_M2.

## Copy from
- Type the values from the National Annex to BS EN 1993-1-1, the clause on partial factors for resistance.
- The Eurocode places the clause at 6.1(1). Confirm the National Annex clause number against your copy.

## Columns
- `gamma_M0[-]`: partial factor for cross-section resistance.
- `gamma_M1[-]`: partial factor for member instability.
- `gamma_M2[-]`: partial factor for net-section and connection resistance.

## Edition to record
Record the National Annex edition and amendment, and the edition of the Eurocode part it modifies.

## Importer checks
- Reject any row with a blank or non-positive number in a numeric column.
- Reject a gamma below 1.
- Warn when gamma_M0 is outside 1.0 to 1.2 or gamma_M2 is outside 1.1 to 1.5.
- Warn when gamma_M2 is below gamma_M0.

## Maps to CalcDataRecord
Kind is `ec3.partial-factors`. The importer saves one CalcDataRecord for the file.
Each column becomes one CalcDataValue with its Symbol, Number and Unit.
