# 08 bs7671.factors-insulation

Kind: `cable.correction-factors` (ambient, grouping and thermal-insulation factors for one installation method).
Status: existing CalcRecordKind `cable.correction-factors`
Planned name: `bs7671.factors-insulation`. The built calculator names the kind `cable.correction-factors`. The file name stays.

## Used by
- E4 conductor sizing (built, `elec.conductor-sizing`) reads `C_i` into its thermal insulation correction factor input.

## Symbol mapping
- `C_i[-]` is the only applied column. It fills the E4 input `C_i`, a number.
- `length_in_insulation` identifies the row. The importer stores it but E4 never reads it.
- The importer saves one record per row. Pick the row for the length of cable in insulation.
- Templates 06 and 07 fill the other two symbols of the same kind.

## Copy from
- Type the values from BS 7671:2018 (as amended), the table for cables surrounded by thermal insulation.
- Confirm the table number and regulation against your copy, for example regulation 523.7.

## Columns
- `length_in_insulation[mm]`: length of cable surrounded by thermal insulation.
- `C_i[-]`: thermal insulation correction factor.

## Edition to record
Record the BS 7671 edition and amendment printed on your copy. Use the edition and amendment fields.

## Importer checks
- Reject a blank or non-positive C_i or length. Reject a duplicate length.
- Length must rise down the rows. C_i must not rise.
- Reject a C_i above 1. E4 refuses C_i outside 0.1 to 1.5.

## Maps to CalcDataRecord
Kind is `cable.correction-factors`. The importer saves one CalcDataRecord per CSV row.
The `C_i` column becomes one CalcDataValue with Symbol `C_i`, a Number and the Unit `-`.
The `length_in_insulation` cell joins the record title. The kind does not list it, so it is stored but never applied.
