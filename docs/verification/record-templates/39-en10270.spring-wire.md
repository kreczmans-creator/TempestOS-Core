# 39 en10270.spring-wire

Kind: `spring.wire-strength` (wire tensile strength R_m at one diameter).
Status: existing CalcRecordKind `spring.wire-strength`
Planned name: `en10270.spring-wire`. The built calculator names the kind `spring.wire-strength`. The file name stays.
Also covered by the W3 material library: `Rm_A` and `Rm_m` on a material give R_m(d). See Alternative below.

## Used by
- M7 springs (built, `mech.spring`) reads `Rm` and scales every stress limit from it.
- M7 has no default for `Rm`. The sheet blocks until you enter a value.

## Symbol mapping
- `Rm[MPa]` fills the M7 input `Rm`.
- `grade` and `d` identify the row. The importer stores them and M7 never reads them.
- The importer saves one record per row. Pick the row for your wire grade and your wire diameter d.
- Shear modulus and Young's modulus no longer sit here. They come from a W3 material. See template 62.

## Alternative: W3 material curve
- Set the M7 input `rm_source` to the W3 curve. M7 then computes R_m(d) from two material properties.
- Enter `Rm_A` and `Rm_m` on a material. Use template 62.
- Use this route when one fitted curve describes the wire. Use the table route when you hold per-diameter values.

## Copy from
- Type R_m for each diameter from your licensed copy of BS EN 10270, the part for your wire.
- You may instead type it from the wire certificate. Cite the part, the edition and the table number.

## Columns
- `grade[text]`: wire grade, as the standard prints it.
- `d[mm]`: wire diameter the strength applies to.
- `Rm[MPa]`: tensile strength at that diameter.

## Edition to record
Record the part number, edition and amendment of the standard, or the certificate number and date.

## Importer checks
- Reject a blank grade. Reject a duplicate grade and d row.
- Reject a blank or non-positive d or Rm.
- M7 refuses Rm outside 50 to 5000 MPa. Warn outside 300 to 3500 MPa.
- Within one grade, warn when Rm rises as d rises.

## Maps to CalcDataRecord
Kind is `spring.wire-strength`. The importer saves one CalcDataRecord per CSV row.
Each numeric column becomes one CalcDataValue with its Symbol, Number and Unit.
The `grade` and `d` text join the record title. Columns the kind does not list are stored but never applied.
