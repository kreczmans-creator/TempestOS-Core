# 40 en13906.permissible-stress

Kind: `spring.permissible` (permissible and ultimate stress fractions of R_m).
Status: existing CalcRecordKind `spring.permissible`
Planned name: `en13906.permissible-stress`. The built calculator names the kind `spring.permissible`. The file name stays.

## Used by
- M7 springs (built, `mech.spring`) compares its stresses with fractions of R_m.
- M7 ships with default fractions. A record replaces them and cites its source.

## Symbol mapping
- `f_tau[-]`, `f_sigma[-]` and `f_su[-]` fill the M7 inputs of the same symbol.
- `wire_grade`, `condition`, `d_min` and `d_max` identify the row. The importer stores them and M7 never reads them.
- The importer saves one record per row. Pick the row for your wire, loading and diameter.
- Enter fractions of R_m only. If your source gives a stress in MPa, divide by the R_m of the same row by hand.

## Copy from
- Type the fractions from your design reference or from BS EN 13906-1, the permissible stress clauses.
- Cite the edition and the clause or diagram number. Confirm them against your copy.

## Columns
- `wire_grade[text]`: wire grade, as your source prints it.
- `condition[text]`: cold or hot coiled, and static or fatigue loading.
- `d_min[mm]`: lower bound of the wire diameter band.
- `d_max[mm]`: upper bound of the wire diameter band.
- `f_tau[-]`: permissible shear stress as a fraction of R_m.
- `f_sigma[-]`: permissible bending stress as a fraction of R_m. Used for torsion springs and hooks.
- `f_su[-]`: ultimate shear strength as a fraction of R_m. Used by the Goodman check.

## Edition to record
Record the standard or reference edition and amendment.

## Importer checks
- Reject a blank wire_grade or condition. Reject a duplicate wire_grade, condition and d_min row.
- Reject a row where d_max does not exceed d_min. Reject a negative or zero diameter.
- Reject a blank or non-positive f_tau or f_su. Leave f_sigma blank if the source gives none.
- M7 refuses f_tau outside 0.01 to 1, f_sigma outside 0.01 to 1.5, and f_su outside 0.05 to 1.
- Warn when f_tau is outside 0.2 to 0.8, f_sigma outside 0.3 to 1, or f_su outside 0.4 to 0.9.

## Maps to CalcDataRecord
Kind is `spring.permissible`. The importer saves one CalcDataRecord per CSV row.
Each numeric column becomes one CalcDataValue with its Symbol, Number and Unit.
The `wire_grade` and `condition` text join the record title. Columns the kind does not list are stored but never applied.
