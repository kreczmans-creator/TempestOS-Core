# 40 en13906.permissible-stress

Kind: `en13906.permissible-stress` (EN 13906-1 permissible spring stresses).
Status: proposed — no CalcRecordKind yet; Track S confirms symbols when the calculator is built

## Used by
- M7 springs (planned) compares the corrected shear stress with these limits.

## Copy from
- Type the values from BS EN 13906-1, the tables or clauses on permissible stress.
- Cite the edition and the table or clause numbers. Confirm them against your copy.

## Columns
- `wire_grade[text]`: wire grade, as the standard prints it.
- `condition[text]`: cold or hot coiled, static or fatigue loading.
- `d_min[mm]`: lower bound of the wire diameter band.
- `d_max[mm]`: upper bound of the wire diameter band.
- `tau_ratio[-]`: permissible shear stress as a ratio of R_m. Blank if the standard gives a stress.
- `tau_perm[MPa]`: permissible shear stress. Blank if the standard gives a ratio.

## Edition to record
Record the BS EN 13906-1 edition and amendment.

## Importer checks
- Reject a row that fills both tau_ratio and tau_perm, or neither.
- Reject a row where d_max does not exceed d_min. Reject a negative or zero value.
- Warn when tau_ratio is above 1.
- Reject a duplicate wire_grade, condition and d_min row.

## Maps to CalcDataRecord
Kind is `en13906.permissible-stress`. The importer saves one CalcDataRecord for the file.
One CalcDataValue with Symbol `tau_limit_table` holds Rows. Each CalcRow has one cell per column, keyed by column symbol.
