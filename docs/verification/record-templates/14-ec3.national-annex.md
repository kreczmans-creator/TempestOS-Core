# 14 ec3.national-annex

Kind: `ec3.national-annex` (EN 1993-1-8 National Annex factors).
Status: existing CalcRecordKind `ec3.national-annex`

## Used by
- S4 EN 1993-1-8 bolts and welds (built, `struct.connection-ec3`) lists all six columns for this kind.
- S6 base plate (built, `struct.base-plate`) lists `gamma_M2` only. It fills the anchor bolt factor input `gamma_M2`.
- S7 LTB (built, `struct.ltb-interaction`) lists `gamma_M1` only. It fills the member buckling factor input `gamma_M1`.
- S8 lifting lugs (planned) will reuse gamma_M2 and the spacing limits.

## Symbol mapping
- Every header is the symbol of a calculator input. S4 applies the first six. S6 applies `gamma_M2` only. S7 applies `gamma_M1` only.
- S6 does not read `gamma_M0` from this kind. It reads gamma_M0 from template 13, kind `ec3-1-1.national-annex`.
- Enter gamma_M0 here as well for S4. Keep the two values consistent.

## Copy from
- Type gamma_M0 and gamma_M2 from the National Annex to BS EN 1993-1-8.
- Type gamma_M1 from the National Annex to BS EN 1993-1-1.
- Type the spacing multiples from BS EN 1993-1-8, Table 3.3, or from the National Annex if it replaces them.

## Columns
- `gamma_M2[-]`: partial factor for bolts, welds and plates in bearing.
- `gamma_M0[-]`: partial factor for cross-section resistance.
- `k_e1[-]`: minimum end distance e1 as a multiple of d0.
- `k_e2[-]`: minimum edge distance e2 as a multiple of d0.
- `k_p1[-]`: minimum pitch p1 as a multiple of d0.
- `k_p2[-]`: minimum gauge p2 as a multiple of d0.
- `gamma_M1[-]`: partial factor for member buckling resistance. Only S7 reads it.

## Edition to record
Record the National Annex edition and amendment, and the edition of the Eurocode part it modifies.

## Importer checks
- Reject any row with a blank or non-positive number in a numeric column. Only gamma_M1 may be blank.
- Reject gamma_M2, gamma_M0 or gamma_M1 below 1.
- S7 refuses gamma_M1 outside 1 to 1.25.
- Warn when gamma_M2 is outside 1.1 to 1.5 or gamma_M0 is outside 1.0 to 1.2.
- Warn when k_e1 or k_e2 is outside 1 to 3, or k_p1 or k_p2 is outside 2 to 4.

## Maps to CalcDataRecord
Kind is `ec3.national-annex`. The importer saves one CalcDataRecord for the file.
Each column becomes one CalcDataValue with its Symbol, Number and Unit.
Leave gamma_M1 blank on a row that only serves S4 or S6.
