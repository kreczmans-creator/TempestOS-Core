# 11 en60204.factors

Kind: `en60204.factors` (BS EN 60204-1 derating factors).
Status: proposed — no CalcRecordKind yet; Track S confirms symbols when the calculator is built

## Used by
- E4 conductor sizing (planned) applies these factors to the Table 6 capacity.

## Copy from
- Type the values from BS EN 60204-1:2018, Annex D.
- Use the ambient temperature and grouping derating tables. Record the table numbers you use.

## Columns
- `factor[text]`: factor name as the table gives it, such as ambient or grouping.
- `theta_a[degC]`: ambient temperature. Fill on ambient rows only.
- `n_circuits[-]`: number of loaded circuits or cables. Fill on grouping rows only.
- `k_derate[-]`: derating factor.

## Edition to record
Record the BS EN 60204-1 edition and any amendment or corrigendum.

## Importer checks
- Reject a blank or non-positive k_derate. Reject a k_derate above 1.5.
- Reject a row that fills both theta_a and n_circuits, or neither.
- Within one factor, the condition must rise and k_derate must not rise, except below the reference ambient.
- Reject a duplicate factor and condition row.

## Maps to CalcDataRecord
Kind is `en60204.factors`. The importer saves one CalcDataRecord for the file.
One CalcDataValue with Symbol `k_derate_table` holds Rows. Each CalcRow has one cell per column, keyed by column symbol.
