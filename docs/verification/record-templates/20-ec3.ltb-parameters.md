# 20 ec3.ltb-parameters

Kind: `ec3.ltb-parameters` (EN 1993-1-1 National Annex LTB parameters).
Status: proposed — no CalcRecordKind yet; Track S confirms symbols when the calculator is built

## Used by
- S7 lateral-torsional buckling and N+M (planned) uses lambda_LT0 and beta_LT for rolled sections.

## Copy from
- Type lambda_LT0 and beta_LT from the National Annex to BS EN 1993-1-1, the clause on 6.3.2.3(1).
- Type alpha_LT from BS EN 1993-1-1, Table 6.3. Confirm the numbers against your copy.

## Columns
- `lambda_LT0[-]`: plateau length of the LTB curve. Fill on the first row only.
- `beta_LT[-]`: LTB curve shape factor. Fill on the first row only.
- `curve[text]`: buckling curve name, as the table prints it.
- `alpha_LT[-]`: imperfection factor for the curve.

## Edition to record
Record the National Annex edition and amendment, and the edition of the Eurocode part it modifies.

## Importer checks
- Reject any row with a blank or non-positive number in a numeric column. Scalar columns may be blank after the first row.
- Warn when lambda_LT0 is outside 0.2 to 0.5 or beta_LT is outside 0.5 to 1.
- Warn when alpha_LT is outside 0.1 to 1.
- Reject a duplicate curve.

## Maps to CalcDataRecord
Kind is `ec3.ltb-parameters`. The importer saves one CalcDataRecord for the file.
Scalar columns (`lambda_LT0`, `beta_LT`) become one CalcDataValue each, read from the first data row.
The other columns become Rows under one CalcDataValue with Symbol `alpha_LT_table`.
