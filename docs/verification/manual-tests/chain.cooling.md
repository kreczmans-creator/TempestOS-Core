# Manual test — chain.cooling (Enclosure cooling: panel load budget, enclosure heat, cooling-water air coil, pipework and pump)
Liability: High. Time: ~70 min. Pre-requisites: a project open in the desktop app. A pocket calculator for the hand checks. Keep the same project open for the whole chain, because links only reach calculations in the open project.

All steps use the Calculation packs panel and the Saved calculations panel. Both sit in the left column of the Calculation worksheets view, under the catalogue. Open the view with Ctrl+K and `Open worksheet:` and any calculator name. To reopen a calculation, select its row in a list. Use the Saved calculations list or the member list of the pack. Press Open or Open pack member. If the status line says the form has unsaved changes, press Open a second time to discard them. A row ending with STALE has an upstream that moved. The results panel shows five significant figures.

The pack `pack.cooling` is new in this release. It is defined in src/Tempest.Calc/Tempest.Calc.Library/CalcPackTemplates.cs. It has seven links. E6 Phi goes to E2 P_add. E2 P_cool goes to B5 Q_req and E2 T_max goes to B5 T_h_in. B5 Q goes to B1 Phi_load. B5 dT_c goes to B1 dT_load. B5 T_c_m goes to B1 T. B5 cp_c goes to B1 cp_load. The pack starts from the example inputs in CalcPackExamples.cs. They describe the heat of a drive panel, a floor-standing enclosure, a water-cooled air coil and the cooling-water pipework. Every example input carries the source assumption "example input — replace with your own". That source is enough to check and issue. No step needs a JSON set.
