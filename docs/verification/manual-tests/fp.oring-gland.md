## Notes for the tester
- The Monte Carlo rows (p_ and cpk_) print in every run. Step 6 is the only step that checks them.
- Findings -04, -05, -06 and -08 to -12 have no step. They are Low or Info. Finding -08 is the 6 % stretch default: a 5.85 % stretch passes. Note it in the defect log if your handbook says 5 %.
