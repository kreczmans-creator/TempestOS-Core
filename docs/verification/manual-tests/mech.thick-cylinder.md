## Import blocks
Copy the whole line of a block to the clipboard. Then press *Import inputs (CSV/JSON)*. The status line must read "Imported n input(s)".

**Block N** — Inner radius entered in newtons.
```json
[{"Symbol":"a","Number":50,"Unit":"N"}]
```

## Notes for the tester
- Steps 11 and 12 are the finding steps. Step 13 is the stale test.
- The check uses von Mises only. Tresca is shown but not checked (P1 finding 03, Info). Say which criterion your allowable assumes.
