## Import blocks
Copy the whole line of a block to the clipboard. Then press *Import inputs (CSV/JSON)*. The status line must read "Imported n input(s)".

**Block N** — D entered in newtons.
```json
[{"Symbol":"D","Number":25,"Unit":"N"},{"Symbol":"hole_letter","Text":"H"},{"Symbol":"hole_grade","Number":7},{"Symbol":"shaft_letter","Text":"g"},{"Symbol":"shaft_grade","Number":6}]
```

## Notes for the tester
- The formulas differ from the printed tables by up to a few micrometres. Judge each normal step against the formula value. Judge each finding step against your table.
- Step numbers 14 to 20 are the finding steps.
