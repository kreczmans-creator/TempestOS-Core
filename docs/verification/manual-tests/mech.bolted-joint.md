## Import blocks
Copy the whole line of a block to the clipboard. Then press *Import inputs (CSV/JSON)*. The status line must read "Imported n input(s)".

**Block N** — Thread diameter entered in newtons.
```json
[{"Symbol":"d","Number":12,"Unit":"N"}]
```

## Notes for the tester
- Steps 10 to 15 are finding steps. Step 16 is the unit test. The independent script uses the VDI mean diameter d_S for the polar modulus.
- The preload-yield warning shows on the defaults. That is expected.
