# TempestOS v0.26.0 — known open calculation defects

Read this before you test. These defects were found by Addendum C Track P (the parallel verification pack, `docs/verification/`) and triaged on the merged release line (commit b72b0085, before the final merge 10085408, which changed no calculation). Addendum C Track S fixes them; it has not started. Do not use a calculator with an open Critical or High finding for real work until it is fixed. When a test meets one of these, tick it on your results sheet; do not log it again.

**Summary.** 3 Critical, 55 High and 85 Medium are open. The F2 emergency-stroke Critical (`fp.accumulator-R1`) and `mech.bolted-joint-02` are fixed.

**Open Critical:**

- `struct.section-properties-01`: a tube or hollow-rectangle part in a composite section gives a wrong area and inertia with no warning (tube d 200, t 10: A 568.6 cm² against 59.69 cm²).
- `bs.psychrometrics-01`: cooling with dehumidification swaps sensible and latent heat (28 °C 50 % to 12 °C saturated, 1 kg/s: SHR 0.322).
- `chain.drive-train-01` (also `pack.mechanism-review`): the motor's RMS torque reaches the shaft as steady torque, so shaft fatigue and key stress are computed on the wrong torque.

The full triage follows (source: `python3 tools/verification/findings.py --min Medium --open`; each row gives the evidence on the merged code). Finding ids match `docs/verification/rederivation/`.
