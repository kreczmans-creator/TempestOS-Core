# TempestOS v0.26.0 — known calculation defects (after Track S C-1)

Read this before you test. These defects were found by Addendum C Track P (the parallel verification pack, `docs/verification/`) and triaged on the merged release line (commit b72b0085, before the final merge 10085408, which changed no calculation). Addendum C Track S item C-1 has fixed them on the release line and the C-1 verification pass re-derived each fix: all 4 Critical and 50 of the 55 High findings are fixed, and the other 5 High findings wait for an owner decision (DECISIONS DC-03 and DC-05). Do not use a calculator for a result that one of the 5 owner-decision rows below touches until the owner has decided it. When a test meets one of these, tick it on your results sheet; do not log it again.

**Summary (after Track S C-1, including both Medium batches).** Of 145 findings, 134 are Fixed (4 Critical, 52 High, 78 Medium; `struct.connection-ec3-R1`, `struct.ltb-interaction-R1`, `struct.load-combinations-01` and `struct.load-combinations-02` were built on the owner's DC-21 answers). 4 wait for an owner decision: 3 High (`bs.sealed-system-02`, `bs.sealed-system-03` in DECISIONS DC-03; `elec.conductor-sizing-R1` in DC-05) and 1 Medium (`struct.ltb-interaction-R3` in DC-07). 7 Medium are still open, each partly fixed or listed for the owner as larger than about two hours (DC-07, DC-08). 'Fixed' includes fixes that use a default or a warning the owner must confirm against a licensed standard (DC-05, DC-08) and the coupling design torque, which is owner-selectable (DC-04).

**Open Critical:** none. The four Critical findings (`struct.section-properties-01`, `bs.psychrometrics-01`, `chain.drive-train-01`, `fp.accumulator-R1`) are fixed; the Critical table below says how and which test pins each fix.
