# WP 14.1A — Brand Alignment to the Tempest Engineering Design System

## 1. Introduction

`WP 14.1A` replaced the Companion's provisional `WP 14.0A` branding with
the authoritative **Tempest Engineering Design System**, supplied by the
Product Owner immediately after `WP 14.0A` shipped. Every visual
decision the Companion carries — palette, themes, type roles, corner
system, card anatomy, iconography policy, timestamps, the logo itself —
was re-derived from the pack's tokens and rules; nothing of the
provisional identity survives. `src/` changes are confined to
`Tempest.Companion` (plus its tests); the platform, API, and offline
architecture are untouched. 69/69 Companion tests pass (17 added,
including a new brand-conformance suite); the full regression is green
in Debug and Release with 0 warnings under CI's flag.

## 2. Purpose

Make the Companion unmistakably *Tempest* — by the brand's own
definition, not an inference from a text brief.

## 3. Background

`WP 14.0A`'s commissioning brief specified colours ("Royal Blue
#1E2F97…"), fonts, and a "six-blade shutter/iris" logo, asserting they
were established. Reconnaissance proved the repository carried no visual
identity at all, so those values shipped as the first concrete
realisation — correctly flagged as provisional in the retrospective's
assumptions. The Product Owner then supplied the real design system: a
complete pack (colour/type/spacing/radius/motion tokens, content and
iconography rules, logo lockups as PNG and derived SVG, the brand's own
font builds) describing an instrument-dark identity materially different
from the brief on nearly every axis.

## 4. The Problem

Five concrete divergences, each needing a decisive correction: wrong
hues (the triad is indigo `#1c2d97` / cyan `#40a2ce` / violet `#6c29d9`,
sampled from the mark itself); wrong ground (dark-first navy, not
light-first with a royal-blue banner); wrong logo (the mark is eighteen
layered strokes around a hexagonal core — and the pack forbids
rebuilding it in code); wrong idiom (squared 2/3/5px corners, top-edge
status rules, UPPERCASE tracked labels, mono machine data with UTC `Z`,
log levels, and a strict no-emoji/no-hand-drawn-glyph rule the
`WP 14.0A` UI violated with its Unicode nav glyphs); and a governance
question — what happens to `ADR-0113`'s now-wrong brand paragraph.

## 5. The Design

- **Tokens transcribed, not approximated.** `BrandPalette` carries the
  pack's `colors.css`/`semantic.css` values verbatim, as real Avalonia
  theme dictionaries — Dark (instrument) is the default variant and the
  app's default theme; Light maps to the paper theme.
  `CompanionTokens` carries the squared-corner system, label tracking,
  and type roles; `CompanionStatusColors` maps health/freshness/severity
  onto the machine-state hues and the `INFO WARN ERR OK` log levels.
- **The logo is the supplied artwork.** `TempestMarkGeometry` is a
  generated, test-spot-checked transcription of the pack's own SVG
  coordinates (18 strokes, three layers, the paper core, the TEMPEST/OS
  logotype outline paths); `TempestLogoControl`/`TempestLockupControl`
  replay it, and the About surface renders the pack's PNG lockups
  directly. The pack's "never rebuild the mark in code" rule is honoured
  as "never *redraw*": carrying supplied coordinates unchanged is a
  transcription of the asset, recorded as such in the geometry file's
  own documentation.
- **Idiom sweep.** Cards: flat fill, hairline, 5px corners, 2px top
  status rule (cyan act / amber attention / red blocked / violet
  category), UPPERCASE Chakra titles with `·`-separated mono counts.
  Chrome: sunken navy bars; the lockup replaces text branding; `CMD` and
  `SYNC` label buttons replace glyphs; the nav is text-only labels with
  a 2px accent top rule on the selected tab. Buttons implement the
  pack's exact hover/press steps (lighter/darker cyan) via per-button
  Fluent resource overrides. All glyphs are gone; Unicode is limited to
  the pack's `●` `→` `·`. Timestamps became UTC with a trailing `Z`;
  identifiers lowercase in mono. The 64px/5.5%-cyan blueprint grid
  textures every page ground behind opaque cards.
- **Governance.** `ADR-0113` is corrected in place (a "Corrected,
  `WP 14.1A`" Status note plus the corrected Decision paragraph — the
  `ADR-0111`/`WP 13.10B` precedent), because the decision is unchanged
  and only its concrete values were provisional. The pack is condensed
  into `docs/design/Tempest Engineering Design System Reference.md` with
  provenance; the desktop's now-visible divergence is registered as
  `FCR-0092`, deliberately not fixed as a side effect.
- **Conformance is executable.** `BrandConformanceTests` pins the token
  hexes, the mark's stroke counts and a coordinate spot-check, the
  logotype paths' parseability, and the corner system — brand drift now
  fails the build.

## 6. Alternatives Considered

Rendering the pack's SVGs via a runtime SVG package (rejected: a new
dependency for static, known geometry); bitmap-only logo usage
everywhere (rejected: the app-bar lockup would blur at 24px on high-DPI
— PNGs are used where the pack's raster lockups fit naturally, the
About surface); keeping the desktop's `HealthColors` values for
Companion health (rejected: the pack reserves exact machine-state hues;
the shared *vocabulary* stays, the values are per-design-system, and the
desktop's realignment is `FCR-0092`); a new ADR superseding `ADR-0113`
(rejected: nothing about the decision changed — the correction precedent
fits exactly); adopting the pack's Lucide icon substitution (rejected:
that is the pack's own flagged web-kit stopgap; native surfaces use text
labels and the three permitted symbols instead).

## 7. Why This Solution Was Chosen

The pack is the authority the brief only claimed to be; every choice
reduces to transcribing it faithfully and making the transcription
self-defending — verbatim tokens, verbatim geometry, and tests that pin
both.

## 8. Architectural Principles

One source of truth (the pack; the reference doc and code cite it, never
restate it loosely); corrections are disclosed in place, never silently
rewritten history; scope discipline (the desktop's divergence is
registered, not sprawled into); evidence over inference (the `WP 14.0A`
assumption that the brief's brand was authoritative is now recorded as
falsified, with the paper trail intact).

## 9. Benefits

The Companion now reads as the brand's own product — side by side with
the pack's web kits it is recognisably the same system; brand drift is
test-guarded; the supplied fonts/artwork ship with clean provenance and
licences; and the next surface (the heads, the future display client)
inherits a finished visual system.

## 10. Trade-offs

The desktop and Companion now visibly diverge until `FCR-0092` lands —
disclosed, registered, deliberate. The pack's motion rules (mechanical
easing curves) are largely untestable headlessly and only minimally
applied (the UI animates little). The paper-theme lockup uses ink via
the theme's heading brush rather than a second geometry set — exactly
the pack's own ink variant colours.

## 11. Common Mistakes

Treating a text brief's brand claims as authoritative without asking for
the pack (the root of the `WP 14.0A` rework); redrawing a supplied logo
"close enough" instead of carrying its coordinates; approximating token
values from screenshots instead of the token files; assigning
`Foreground = null` in Avalonia object initialisers (a local null brush
that renders nothing — found by `WP 14.0A`'s visual review, avoided
throughout here); and assuming a `ScrollViewer` with horizontal
scrolling disabled constrains its content's width — it lays out at
desired width, so one unbreakable mono line can push a card past the
viewport (found by this Work Package's visual review; fixed structurally
in `CompanionPage`).

## 12. Future Evolution

`FCR-0092` (desktop realignment) is the natural next brand Work Package,
with the Companion as its reference implementation. The pack's notch
motif (the 10px top-right cut, allowed on hero panels at most twice per
screen) is not yet applied anywhere — a candidate flourish for the
health hero card when a polish pass warrants it. When platform heads
ship (`TD-57`), the pack's app icons (`Assets/Brand/tempest-appicon-*`)
become the launcher icons.

## 13. Key Takeaways

A brand is data, not adjectives: the moment the real token files and
artwork exist, "look like the brand" becomes transcription plus
conformance tests, and stops being a matter of taste. And the correction
machinery governance already owns (in-place ADR corrections, registered
follow-ups, disclosed assumptions) is exactly what makes a same-day
identity reversal cheap instead of embarrassing.

## Observations

- The two pre-existing Linux-environment Core test failures reproduce
  unchanged (baseline-identical; green on the Windows CI runners).
- The pack's web component kit (`components/`, React/JSX) and site
  export are web-specific and deliberately not imported into this
  repository; only brand assets, fonts, and rules were taken.
