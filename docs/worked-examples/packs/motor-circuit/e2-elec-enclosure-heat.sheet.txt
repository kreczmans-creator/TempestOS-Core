1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Total heat load inside a control enclosure, the effective surface that can dissipate it for the mounting condition, the internal temperature rise with natural convection and radiation through a stated overall heat-transfer coefficient, the forced-ventilation airflow for a target rise (density corrected for altitude), the fan duty point against a fan curve and the system resistance, optional solar gain, and the cooling-unit capacity needed when the limit cannot be met.
P = Σ P_i n_i d_i (+ solar α I A_sun). A_eff = Σ f_face × A_face. Natural: ΔT = P/(h A_eff) (a plain sum of exposed face areas times one coefficient; the IEC TR 60890 tables are not used). Forced: V = P/(ρ c_p ΔT_target) with ρ = p(z)/(287.055 T) and c_p = 1006 J/kg·K; fan duty where p_fan(V) = p_sys (V/V_ref)² (bisection). Cooling unit: P_cool = P − h A_eff (T_max − T_amb) when positive. Sealed enclosure (T_seal entered, no breather): the pressure difference across the walls when the air warms from the sealing temperature to T_int at constant volume, Δp = p_site·|T_int/T_seal − 1| (absolute temperatures), a rise when T_int > T_seal, a suction when below. A pressure-compensation element (breather or relief) limits it: dp_seal = min(that difference, dp_relief), the cracking pressure. The force of dp_seal on a cover of area A_cover is F_seal = dp_seal·A_cover.
  Ref: IEC TR 60890 — A method of temperature-rise verification of low-voltage switchgear and controlgear assemblies by calculation
  Ref: Manufacturer enclosure climate-control guides (Rittal, nVent Hoffman) — Effective surface, heat-transfer coefficient and fan-sizing practice (cited, not copied)
  Ref: BS EN 61439-1 10.10 — Verification of temperature rise

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Heat leaves the enclosure through its exposed faces at one overall coefficient h (natural convection plus radiation to surroundings at the ambient temperature); covered faces dissipate nothing. (IEC TR 60890-style effective-surface approach; h is a reference value the user may edit.)
  A2. Forced ventilation removes the full heat load with air entering at ambient and leaving at the target rise; natural dissipation is neglected for the airflow (conservative). (Manufacturer fan-sizing practice.)
  A3. A sealed enclosure (T_seal entered) is a closed, rigid, leak-free volume of dry air at the site pressure when closed: the pressure difference follows from the ideal-gas relation at constant volume, from the sealing temperature to the internal temperature with natural dissipation T_int (the temperature without a cooling unit); a breather, a seal that leaks or a flexing wall reduces it, so it is an upper bound (a pressure-compensation element is modelled only as a cap at its cracking pressure, with no flow resistance); the site pressure is the standard atmosphere at the altitude. (Ideal gas at constant volume (derived).)
  A4. The fan curve is linear between the entered points; the system resistance scales with airflow squared. (Duty-point idealisation.)
  A5. I_sun (Solar irradiance on the enclosure (0 = indoors)) is taken as 0 W/m^2. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A6. A_sun (Sunlit area) is taken as 0 m^2. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A7. alpha_sun (Surface absorptivity) is taken as 0.5. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A8. f_top (Top exposure (1 free, 0 covered)) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A9. f_front (Front exposure) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A10. f_rear (Rear exposure (0 = wall-mounted)) is taken as 0. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A11. f_left (Left side exposure) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A12. f_right (Right side exposure) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A13. f_bottom (Bottom exposure (0 = on the floor)) is taken as 0. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A14. h (Overall heat-transfer coefficient of the enclosure surface (editable reference value)) is taken as 5.5 W/(m^2*K). (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A15. altitude (Site altitude (air density)) is taken as 0 m. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A16. p_sys (System resistance (filters, grilles) at the reference airflow) is taken as 20 Pa. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A17. V_ref (Reference airflow for the system resistance) is taken as 100 m^3/h. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)

4. WORKING
  [18] P_loads = Σ P_i × n_i × d_i
      = 2 item(s)
      = 80 W
        Internal heat load
  [19] P_solar = alpha_sun * I_sun * A_sun
      = 0.5 × 0 W/m² × 0 m²
      = 0 W
        Solar gain
  [20] P_total = P_loads + P_add + P_solar
      = 80 W + 140 W + 0 W
      = 220 W
        Total heat to remove
  [21] A_eff = f_top * W * D + f_bottom * W * D + f_front * W * H + f_rear * W * H + f_left * H * D + f_right * H * D    « IEC TR 60890 approach
      = 1 × 1 m × 0.3 m + 0 × 1 m × 0.3 m + 1 × 1 m × 1.2 m + 0 × 1 m × 1.2 m + 1 × 1.2 m × 0.3 m + 1 × 1.2 m × 0.3 m
      = 2.22 m²
        Effective dissipating surface
  [22] dT_nat = P_total / (h * A_eff)
      = 220 W / (5.5 W/(m²·K) × 2.22 m²)
      = 18.018 K
        Temperature rise with natural dissipation
  [23] T_int = T_amb + dT_nat
      = 35 degC + 18.018 K
      = 53.018 degC
        Internal temperature with natural dissipation
  [24] dT_allow = T_max - T_amb
      = 55 degC − 35 degC
      = 20 K
        Allowed rise
  [25] u_nat = dT_nat / dT_allow
      = 18.018 K / 20 K
      = 0.9009
        Natural dissipation utilisation
  [26] P_nat_max = h * A_eff * dT_allow
      = 5.5 W/(m²·K) × 2.22 m² × 20 K
      = 244.2 W
        Heat the enclosure dissipates naturally at the limit
  [27] P_cool = max(0 W, P_total - P_nat_max)
      = max(0 W, 220 W − 244.2 W)
      = 0 W
        Cooling-unit capacity needed if ventilation is not used
  [28] p_site = 101325 (1 − 2.25577e-5 z)^5.2559
      = z = 0 m
      = 101.32 kPa
        Site pressure
  [29] rho_air = p_site / (287.055 J/(kg*K) * T_amb)
      = 101.32 kPa / (287.055 J/(kg*K) × 35 degC)
      = 1.1455 kg/m³
        Air density at ambient and altitude
  [30] V_req = P_total / (rho_air * 1006 J/(kg*K) * dT_allow)
      = 220 W / (1.1455 kg/m³ × 1006 J/(kg*K) × 20 K)
      = 34.364 m³/h
        Airflow for forced ventilation at the allowed rise (natural dissipation neglected)
  [31] V_req_net = max(0 m^3/h, (P_total - P_nat_max) / (rho_air * 1006 J/(kg*K) * dT_allow))
      = max(0 m^3/h, (220 W − 244.2 W) / (1.1455 kg/m³ × 1006 J/(kg*K) × 20 K))
      = 0 m³/h
        Airflow crediting natural dissipation
