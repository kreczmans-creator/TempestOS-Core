1. SCOPE AND METHOD
The demand of a set of loads per supply (three-phase AC, single-phase AC, DC) and the minimum DC power supply, transformer and battery for it, each with a margin; the heat the loads dissipate for the enclosure calculation.
Demand per load: P_i·d_i·k_i; apparent power P_i·d_i·k_i/cosφ_i (arithmetic sum, conservative) Bus currents: I_3φ = S_3φ/(√3·U), I_1φ = S_1φ/U, I_DC = P_DC/U_DC; when a bus is chosen for the DC power supply its input P_DC/η_PSU (power factor 1) is added to that bus Heat dissipated inside the enclosure: Φ = Σ h_i·P_i·d_i·k_i, h_i = the share of the input power released inside (blank: the loss 1 − η_i; electronics 1; a machine outside the enclosure 0) PSU: P_PSU ≥ margin·P_DC/f_derate (f_derate from the manufacturer's curve at θ and altitude) Transformer: S_T ≥ margin·S for the running load; starting peak S_peak = S + max(m_i·P_i/cosφ_i − S_i) ≤ k_T·S_T (the starting load undiversified), so S_T ≥ S_peak/k_T for the start; the rating to buy is the larger of the two Battery: C = I_b·t/(DoD·η_b·k_temp·k_age), I_b = Σ backed P_i·d_i·k_i/(U_b·η_conv); Peukert: I_b·H·(t/H)^(1/k_p) in place of I_b·t
  Ref: Linden's Handbook of Batteries 5th ed., Ch. 3–4 — Battery sizing and the Peukert relation
  Ref: BS EN 60204-1:2018 cl. 4.3 — Electrical supply (supply voltage tolerance; cited by number only)

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Each load's P is its electrical input; DC loads count at power factor 1 in the transformer demand; with a PSU bus chosen they count at P/η_PSU, the PSU's own loss being a load row if its heat is wanted. (Load budget convention.)
  A2. The heat released inside the enclosure is h·P·d·k per load; the default h = 1 − η is the loss of a drive or supply, electronics release all their input (h = 1) and a machine outside the panel releases none (h = 0). (Stated convention for the heat a load releases inside the panel; change h for a load that differs.)
  A3. Apparent powers are added arithmetically, which is conservative when power factors differ. (Upper bound on S.)
  A4. PSU derating, transformer overload and battery factors are the manufacturer's values, entered as records. (Entered as reference records, which carry their source, edition and licence.)
  A5. inrush_add (Starting multiple of the additional load) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A6. U_3ph (Three-phase line voltage) is taken as 400 V. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A7. U_1ph (Single-phase voltage) is taken as 230 V. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
