1. Scope and method
Torque, speed and power a motor needs to move a load through a mechanism on a point-to-point cycle: the load reflected to the motor shaft, the acceleration from the motion profile, peak torque during acceleration, RMS torque over the cycle (thermal duty), peak speed, inertia ratio, and a check of a candidate motor's rated and peak torque and speed with a safety margin. A rotary load that runs continuously (a pump, a fan) is sized instead from its steady torque and speed and a run-up time.
Motor-to-load ratio of a linear mechanism, in metres per motor radian: k = p/(2π i) (screw); D/(2 i) (belt); D_p/(2 i) (rack). Load inertia reflected to the motor through a linear mechanism: J_refl = m k²/η. Load torque at the motor through a linear mechanism: T_load = F k/η, with F = m g (sin θ + μ cos θ) + F_p. Reflected inertia and torque of a rotary load through a gearbox: J_refl = J_L/(i² η); T_load = T_L/(i η). Move profile, trapezoidal 1/3–1/3–1/3 (adjustable): v_pk = s/(t_move − t_acc); a = v_pk/t_acc. Move profile, S-curve (sine acceleration, same peak speed): a_pk = (π/2) a_trap. Torque to accelerate the total inertia, and the peak torque: T_acc = J_total α; T_pk = T_acc + T_load; deceleration T_dec = −T_acc + T_load; the peak check is on the larger magnitude, SF·max(|T_pk|, |T_dec|) ≤ T_peak, and the braking power is P_brake = max(0, −T_dec·ω_pk). Design torque for a coupling or any part downstream that must carry what the drive can deliver, on the basis the owner chooses (T_basis): the load peak T_design = max(|T_pk|, |T_dec|) (default; T_pk alone in continuous duty, where there is no deceleration), the motor peak T_design = T_peak, a service factor on the rated torque T_design = SF_c·T_rated, or a protective limit T_design = T_lim (a torque limiter or a drive setting). Holding torque in the dwell of a linear mechanism that is not held by a brake: T_hold = m g sin θ·k (efficiency not credited: it is a torque to stand still); it is part of the RMS torque for the whole dwell. Alternating torque amplitude of a point-to-point cycle about T_load, for a shaft's fatigue: T_alt = (T_pk − T_dec)/2 (0 in continuous duty). Torque behind the ratio on the output shaft of a rotary mechanism: T_out = T·i·η (mean, alternating and peak), n_out = n_pk/i. Peak axial thrust of a ball screw on its support bearings: F_ax_pk = F_load + m a_pk (0 for other mechanisms). RMS (thermal) torque over the whole cycle, dwell included: T_rms = √(Σ T_i² t_i / t_cycle), the dwell at T_hold. RMS mechanical power over the cycle, the thermal-equivalent continuous power a supply sees (regeneration counts as power; the instantaneous peak P_pk is not a continuous demand), trapezoidal profile: P_rms = ω_pk √{[t_acc·(2/3)(T_load² + T_acc²) + T_load² t_const]/t_cycle}; for the S-curve the acceleration term becomes t_acc·[(3/4) T_load² + (5/16) T_acc²]. Continuous rotary duty (a pump or fan), speed at the motor: ω = 2π n_L i/60; n_pk = n_L i. Continuous rotary duty, load torque and RMS torque: T_load = T_L/(i η); T_rms = T_load. Peak torque during the run-up, with full load throughout (conservative): T_pk = J_total ω/t_start + T_load. Continuous power: P_cont = T_load ω; P_rms = P_cont; continuous duty needs the steady load torque T_L (blank or zero is refused: it would size the motor for no load). Rated shaft power of the candidate motor, from its rated torque and its rated speed n_rated (not the running speed), the shaft power its supply must be sized for; without n_rated there is no P_rated and the sheet warns: P_rated = T_rated·2π n_rated/60; check P_cont ≤ P_rated, u_power = P_cont/P_rated.

3. Assumptions
  A1. Rigid transmission; the mechanism efficiency applies to the driving direction and is also used in the deceleration phase (conservative for peak, slightly pessimistic for RMS). (Servo-sizing practice.)
  A2. The friction force is Coulomb friction on the moved mass; viscous and breakaway friction are not modelled unless included in F_p. (Load model.)
  A3. Continuous duty: the load torque is constant at the stated speed and the motor accelerates the whole inertia linearly to speed in t_start while carrying the full load torque (conservative); the move distance, move time, acceleration fraction, dwell and profile are ignored. (Run-up idealisation for pumps and fans.)
  A4. The S-curve profile has sine-shaped acceleration over the same acceleration time, so its peak acceleration is π/2 times the trapezoidal value. (Jerk-limited profile idealisation.)
  A5. i (Gear ratio i (motor : output, 1 = none)) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A6. eta (Mechanism efficiency (overall)) is taken as 0.9. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A7. J_mech (Transmission inertia referred to the motor shaft (screw, pulleys, gearbox, coupling)) is taken as 0.0001 kg·m². (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A8. theta (Incline from horizontal (+ moving uphill)) is taken as 0 °. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A9. F_p (Process force opposing the move (linear)) is taken as 0 N. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A10. brake (A brake or a self-locking drive holds a linear axis during the dwell (no holding torque); unticked, a vertical or inclined axis is held by the motor) is taken as no. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A11. T_L (Constant load torque at the output (rotary; continuous duty needs it: blank or zero is refused)) is taken as 0 N·m. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A12. duty_mode (Duty (point-to-point move cycle, or continuous rotary running with a run-up)) is taken as point-to-point. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A13. profile (Motion profile) is taken as trapezoidal. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A14. f_acc (Acceleration (and deceleration) fraction of the move time) is taken as 0.333333. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A15. t_dwell (Dwell between moves) is taken as 0.5 s. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)

4. Working
  [22] k = p_screw / (2 * pi * i)
      = 10 mm / (2 × π × 1)
      = 1.5915 mm
        Travel per motor radian
  [23] F_load = m * 9.80665 m/s^2 * (sin(theta) + mu * cos(theta)) + F_p
      = 50 kg × 9.80665 m/s^2 × (sin(0 deg) + 0.1 × cos(0 deg)) + 0 N
      = 49.033 N
        Load force along the motion (gravity, friction, process)
  [25] T_hold = m * 9.80665 m/s^2 * sin(theta) * k
      = 50 kg × 9.80665 m/s^2 × sin(0 deg) × 1.5915 mm
      = 0 N·m
        Holding torque in the dwell: the motor holds the weight of the axis (efficiency not credited)
  [26] T_load = F_load * k / eta
      = 49.033 N × 1.5915 mm / 0.9
      = 0.08671 N·m
        Load torque at the motor shaft
  [27] J_refl = m * k^2 / eta
      = 50 kg × (1.5915 mm)^2 / 0.9
      = 0.00014072 kg·m²
        Load inertia reflected to the motor shaft
  [28] v_pk = s * 1 m / (t_move * (1 - f_acc))
      = 0.5 × 1 m / (0.5 s × (1 − 0.33333))
      = 1.5 m/s
        Peak linear velocity (trapezoid)
  [29] a_trap = v_pk / (f_acc * t_move)
      = 1.5 m/s / (0.33333 × 0.5 s)
      = 9 m/s²
        Trapezoidal acceleration
  [30] omega_pk = v_pk / k
      = 1.5 m/s / 1.5915 mm
      = 942.48 rad/s
        Peak motor speed
  [32] a_pk = a_trap
      = 9 m/s²
      = 9 m/s²
        Peak acceleration
  [33] alpha_pk = a_pk / k
      = 9 m/s² / 1.5915 mm
      = 5654.9 rad/s²
        Peak motor angular acceleration
  [34] J_total = J_motor + J_mech + J_refl
      = 0.0002 kg·m² + 0.0001 kg·m² + 0.00014072 kg·m²
      = 0.00044072 kg·m²
        Total inertia at the motor shaft
  [35] T_acc = J_total * alpha_pk
      = 0.00044072 kg·m² × 5654.9 rad/s²
      = 2.4922 N·m
        Acceleration torque
  [36] T_pk = T_acc + T_load
      = 2.4922 N·m + 0.08671 N·m
      = 2.5789 N·m
        Peak torque (accelerating against the load)
  [37] T_dec = -T_acc + T_load
      = −2.4922 N·m + 0.08671 N·m
      = -2.4055 N·m
        Torque during deceleration
  [38] T_alt = (T_pk - T_dec) / 2
      = (2.5789 N·m − (-2.4055 N·m)) / 2
      = 2.4922 N·m
        Alternating torque amplitude of the cycle about T_load
  [40] T_design = max(abs(T_pk), abs(T_dec))
      = max(abs(2.5789 N·m), abs(-2.4055 N·m))
      = 2.5789 N·m
        Design torque: the load's peak torque, the larger in magnitude of the accelerating T_pk and the braking T_dec (an overhauling drive brakes harder than it accelerates)
  [41] F_ax_pk = F_load + m * a_pk
      = 49.033 N + 50 kg × 9 m/s²
      = 499.03 N
        Peak axial thrust on the screw's support bearings (load force plus the moved mass accelerated)
  [42] n_pk = omega_pk
      = 942.48 rad/s
      = 9000 rpm
        Peak motor speed
  [43] P_pk = T_pk * omega_pk
      = 2.5789 N·m × 942.48 rad/s
      = 2.4306 kW
        Peak mechanical power
  [44] P_brake = max(0 N*m, -T_dec) * omega_pk
      = max(0 N*m, −-2.4055 N·m) × 942.48 rad/s
      = 2.2672 kW
        Peak braking power at the start of the deceleration
  [45] t_acc = f_acc * t_move
      = 0.33333 × 0.5 s
      = 0.16667 s
        Acceleration time
  [46] t_const = t_move - 2 * t_acc
      = 0.5 s − 2 × 0.16667 s
      = 0.16667 s
        Constant-velocity time
  [47] t_cycle = t_move + t_dwell
      = 0.5 s + 0.5 s
      = 1 s
        Cycle time
  [48] n_mean = omega_pk * (t_move - t_acc) / t_cycle
      = 942.48 rad/s × (0.5 s − 0.16667 s) / 1 s
      = 3000 rpm
        Mean motor speed over the cycle, dwell included (the travel's motor turns per cycle time)
  [50] P_rated = T_rated * n_rated
      = 2 N·m × 3000 rpm
      = 628.32 W
        Rated (nameplate) shaft power of the candidate motor: its rated torque times its rated speed
  [51] P_rms = omega_pk * sqrt((t_acc * (2 / 3) * (T_load^2 + T_acc^2) + T_load^2 * t_const) / t_cycle)
      = 942.48 rad/s × sqrt((0.16667 s × 2 / 3 × ((0.08671 N·m)^2 + (2.4922 N·m)^2) + (0.08671 N·m)^2 × 0.16667 s) / 1 s)
      = 0.78414 kW
        RMS mechanical power over the cycle (thermal-equivalent continuous power)
  [52] T_rms = sqrt((T_pk^2 * t_acc + T_load^2 * t_const + T_dec^2 * t_acc + T_hold^2 * t_dwell) / t_cycle)
      = sqrt(((2.5789 N·m)^2 × 0.16667 s + (0.08671 N·m)^2 × 0.16667 s + (-2.4055 N·m)^2 × 0.16667 s + (0 N·m)^2 × 0.5 s) / 1 s)
      = 1.4402 N·m
        RMS torque over the cycle (thermal), the dwell at the holding torque
  [53] J_ratio = (J_refl + J_mech) / J_motor
      = (0.00014072 kg·m² + 0.0001 kg·m²) / 0.0002 kg·m²
      = 1.2036
        Inertia ratio (load : motor)
  [54] u_peak = SF * max(abs(T_pk), abs(T_dec)) / T_peak
      = 1.25 × max(abs(2.5789 N·m), abs(-2.4055 N·m)) / 6 N·m
      = 0.53728
        Peak torque utilisation with safety margin, on the larger of the accelerating and the braking torque
  [55] u_rms = SF * T_rms / T_rated
      = 1.25 × 1.4402 N·m / 2 N·m
      = 0.90012
        Continuous torque utilisation with safety margin
  [56] u_speed = n_pk / n_max
      = 9000 rpm / 10000 rpm
      = 0.9
        Speed utilisation
  [57] duty = T_rms / T_rated
      = 1.4402 N·m / 2 N·m
      = 0.7201
        Thermal duty (without margin)

1. Scope and method
The demand of a set of loads per supply (three-phase AC, single-phase AC, DC) and the minimum DC power supply, transformer and battery for it, each with a margin; the heat the loads dissipate for the enclosure calculation.
Demand per load: P_i·d_i·k_i; apparent power P_i·d_i·k_i/cosφ_i (arithmetic sum, conservative); this duty-weighted demand sizes the transformer and the enclosure heat Running load of each load while it is on: P_i·k_i, apparent P_i·k_i/cosφ_i (the duty d is the share of time on, so it is left out: a cable, a bus and a power supply carry the load while it is on) Bus currents: I_3φ = S_3φ,run/(√3·U), I_1φ = S_1φ,run/U, I_DC = P_DC,run/U_DC, from the running loads; when a bus is chosen for the DC power supply its input P_DC,run/η_PSU (power factor 1) is added to that bus Heat dissipated inside the enclosure: Φ = Σ h_i·P_i·d_i·k_i + P_DC·(1/η_PSU − 1), h_i = the share of the input power released inside (blank: the loss 1 − η_i; electronics 1; a machine outside the enclosure 0); the second term is the power supply's own loss, present when an AC bus is chosen for it Power supply sizing: P_PSU ≥ margin·P_DC,run/f_derate (the running DC load, duty not applied; f_derate from the manufacturer's curve at θ and altitude) Transformer sizing: S_T ≥ margin·S for the running load; starting peak S_peak = S + max(m_i·P_i/cosφ_i − S_i) ≤ k_T·S_T (the starting load undiversified), so S_T ≥ S_peak/k_T for the start; the rating to buy is the larger of the two Battery sizing: C = I_b·t/(DoD·η_b·k_temp·k_age), I_b = Σ backed P_i·d_i·k_i/(U_b·η_conv); Peukert: I_b·H·(t/H)^(1/k_p) in place of I_b·t, never below I_b·t (the exponent is not valid beyond the rated hour rate H)

1. Scope and method
Voltage drop along a circuit in volts and per cent of the nominal voltage, the conductor power loss, and an estimate of the conductor operating temperature, for DC, single-phase and three-phase AC, from resistivity and cross-section (with temperature correction), from tabulated R and X per kilometre, or from a mV/A/m figure; parallel conductors per phase; the drop limit is a user input (the regulatory limits live in BS 7671 Appendix 4 and Regulation 525 and are not embedded).
Conductor resistance at its operating temperature, for n conductors in parallel: R = ρ_T L/(A n), with ρ_T = ρ_20 [1 + α (T_c − 20)]. Resistance and reactance from per-length figures instead: R = R′ L/n; X = X′ L/n. Voltage drop on a DC circuit: ΔV = 2 I R. Voltage drop on a single-phase AC circuit: ΔV = 2 I (R cos φ + X sin φ). 1. Scope and method
Total heat load inside a control enclosure, the effective surface that can dissipate it for the mounting condition, the internal temperature rise with natural convection and radiation through a stated overall heat-transfer coefficient, the forced-ventilation airflow for a target rise (density corrected for altitude), the fan duty point against a fan curve and the system resistance, optional solar gain, and the cooling-unit capacity needed when the limit cannot be met.
Heat dissipated inside the enclosure, plus any solar gain: P = Σ P_i n_i d_i (+ α I A_sun). Effective cooling surface, each face weighted by how freely it sheds heat: A_eff = Σ f_face × A_face. Temperature rise with natural cooling, one coefficient over the exposed faces (the IEC TR 60890 tables are not used): ΔT = P/(h A_eff). Air flow a fan must move to hold the target rise: V = P/(ρ c_p ΔT_target), with ρ = p(z)/(287.055 T) and c_p = 1006 J/(kg·K). Fan duty point, where the fan curve meets the system resistance (found by bisection): p_fan(V) = p_sys (V/V_ref)². Cooling unit capacity, when the surfaces alone cannot reject the heat: P_cool = P − h A_eff (T_max − T_amb); with T_max at or below T_amb the term is a gain, P_cool = P + h A_eff (T_amb − T_max), and no ventilation airflow or fan duty is given. Sealed enclosure (sealing temperature T_seal entered, no breather), pressure difference across the walls when the air warms or cools from the sealing temperature at constant volume, in absolute temperatures (a rise when T_int > T_seal, a suction when below): Δp = p_site·|T_int/T_seal − 1|. A pressure-compensation element (breather or relief) limits it to its cracking pressure: dp_seal = min(Δp, dp_relief). Force of that pressure on a cover of area A_cover: F_seal = dp_seal·A_cover.

  A16. p_sys (System resistance (filters, grilles) at the reference airflow) is taken as 20 Pa. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A17. V_ref (Reference airflow for the system resistance) is taken as 100 m³/h. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A18. The effective surface is the plain exposed-area method of manufacturer practice: each face area weighted by how freely it sheds heat, one overall coefficient h. This is not a verification of temperature rise to IEC TR 60890 or BS EN 61439-1 cl. 10.10 (neither the surface factors nor the standard's temperature-rise correlation is used). (Stated on every run so the result is not read as an assembly verification.)

4. Working
  [18] P_loads = Σ P_i × n_i × d_i
      = 2 item(s)
      = 80 W
        Internal heat load
  [19] P_solar = alpha_sun * I_sun * A_sun
      = 0.5 × 0 W/m² × 0 m²
      = 0 W
        Solar gain
  [20] P_total = P_loads + P_add + P_solar
      = 80 W + 153.33 W + 0 W
      = 233.33 W
        Total heat to remove
  [21] A_eff = f_top * W * D + f_bottom * W * D + f_front * W * H + f_rear * W * H + f_left * H * D + f_right * H * D    « plain exposed-area method (manufacturer practice), not an IEC TR 60890 verification
      = 1 × 1 m × 0.3 m + 0 × 1 m × 0.3 m + 1 × 1 m × 1.2 m + 0 × 1 m × 1.2 m + 1 × 1.2 m × 0.3 m + 1 × 1.2 m × 0.3 m
      = 2.22 m²
        Effective dissipating surface
  [22] dT_nat = P_total / (h * A_eff)
      = 233.33 W / (5.5 W/(m²·K) × 2.22 m²)
      = 19.11 K
        Temperature rise with natural dissipation
  [23] T_int = T_amb + dT_nat
      = 35 degC + 19.11 K
      = 54.11 degC
        Internal temperature with natural dissipation
  [24] dT_allow = T_max - T_amb
      = 55 degC − 35 degC
      = 20 K
        Allowed rise
  [25] u_nat = dT_nat / dT_allow
      = 19.11 K / 20 K
      = 0.9555
        Natural dissipation utilisation
  [26] P_nat_max = h * A_eff * dT_allow
      = 5.5 W/(m²·K) × 2.22 m² × 20 K
      = 244.2 W
        Heat the enclosure dissipates naturally at the limit
  [27] P_cool = max(0 W, P_total - P_nat_max)
      = max(0 W, 233.33 W − 244.2 W)
      = 0 W
        Cooling-unit capacity needed if ventilation is not used
  [28] p_site = 101325 (1 − 2.25577e-5 z)^5.2559
      = z = 0 m
      = 101.32 kPa
        Site pressure
  [29] rho_air = p_site / (287.055 J/(kg*K) * T_amb)
      = 101.32 kPa / (287.055 J/(kg*K) × 35 degC)
      = 1.1455 kg/m³
        Air density at ambient and altitude
  [30] V_req = P_total / (rho_air * 1006 J/(kg*K) * dT_allow)
      = 233.33 W / (1.1455 kg/m³ × 1006 J/(kg*K) × 20 K)
      = 36.447 m³/h
        Airflow for forced ventilation at the allowed rise (natural dissipation neglected)
  [31] V_req_net = max(0 m^3/h, (P_total - P_nat_max) / (rho_air * 1006 J/(kg*K) * dT_allow))
      = max(0 m^3/h, (233.33 W − 244.2 W) / (1.1455 kg/m³ × 1006 J/(kg*K) × 20 K))
      = 0 m³/h
        Airflow crediting natural dissipation
