1. Scope and method
Calculates the design values of one action effect under every EN 1990 Annex A1 combination for buildings, from persistent to accidental. Each variable action leads in turn; the envelopes and governing combinations are reported.
ULS 6.10: Σ γ_G·G + γ_Q·K_FI·Q_1 + Σ γ_Q·K_FI·ψ_0·Q_i (K_FI also on γ_G,sup) ULS 6.10a: Σ γ_G·G + Σ γ_Q·K_FI·ψ_0·Q_i; 6.10b: Σ ξ·γ_G·G + γ_Q·K_FI·Q_1 + Σ γ_Q·K_FI·ψ_0·Q_i Permanent factor: γ_G,sup when the action adds to the effect sought, γ_G,inf when it opposes it Variable actions: included only when they add to the effect sought (otherwise zero) SLS characteristic 6.14b: Σ G + Q_1 + Σ ψ_0·Q_i; frequent 6.15b: Σ G + ψ_1·Q_1 + Σ ψ_2·Q_i; quasi-permanent 6.16b: Σ G + Σ ψ_2·Q_i Accidental 6.11b: Σ G + A_d + ψ_1·Q_1 + Σ ψ_2·Q_i

1. Scope and method
Calculates reactions, member forces and deflections of a linear elastic plane frame or continuous beam by the direct stiffness method. Global x is right and y up; bending is about the section y-y axis, anticlockwise positive.
Member stiffness: three freedoms per node, Euler–Bernoulli members with axial EA/L and bending EI/L³ terms. End releases: pins are taken out by static condensation; supports remove freedoms or add spring stiffness. Span loads: they become equivalent nodal loads, the exact fixed-end forces. Nodal displacements: K u = F, solved by banded LDLᵀ after a reverse Cuthill–McKee ordering; a non-positive pivot is reported as a mechanism. Internal forces: they follow from the member end actions and the Macaulay functions of the span loads. Member extremes: found at stations along each member, plus the zero-shear and zero-slope points found by bisection.

3. Assumptions
  A1. The analysis is linear elastic and first order, with small displacements. (Euler–Bernoulli direct stiffness.)
  A2. Members have no shear deformation, and there are no P–Δ effects or buckling. (Euler–Bernoulli direct stiffness.)
  A3. Members are prismatic between nodes and connect rigidly unless a release is stated. (Static condensation of the released rotation.)
  A4. A released member end transmits no moment. (Static condensation of the released rotation.)
  A5. Distributed loads act perpendicular to the member unless marked as gravity loads. (Load table convention.)
  A6. Gravity loads act in global −y per metre of member length. (Load table convention.)
  A7. n_div (Stations per member) is taken as 20. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)

1. Scope and method
Calculates the resistance of a class 1, 2 or 3 steel member to tension, compression, buckling, bending, shear and their interaction, to EN 1993-1-1. Tension is positive; class 4 sections and lateral-torsional buckling are outside this sheet.
Axial plastic resistance: N_pl,Rd = A f_y/γ_M0. Bending resistance, plastic modulus for class 1–2 and elastic for class 3: M_c,Rd = W f_y/γ_M0. Shear resistance: V_pl,Rd = A_v f_y/(√3 γ_M0). Bending resistance reduced by high shear, when V_Ed > 0.5 V_pl,Rd: ρ = (2V_Ed/V_pl,Rd − 1)². Class 1–2 I-section with h, t_w, t_f entered: M_y,V,Rd = (W_pl,y − ρ A_w²/(4 t_w)) f_y/γ_M0, A_w = (h − 2t_f) t_w, and M_z,c,Rd is not reduced; otherwise the whole resistance × (1 − ρ). Elastic critical load and non-dimensional slenderness: N_cr = π²EI/L_cr²; λ̄ = √(A f_y/N_cr). Buckling reduction factor and buckling resistance: Φ = 0.5[1 + α(λ̄ − 0.2) + λ̄²]; χ = 1/(Φ + √(Φ² − λ̄²)) ≤ 1; N_b,Rd = χ A f_y/γ_M1. Cross-section check, linear interaction: N_Ed/N_Rd + M_y,Ed/M_y,Rd + M_z,Ed/M_z,Rd ≤ 1. Member check, axial force and bending with buckling (6.61, 6.62): N_Ed/N_b,Rd,min + k_yy M_y,Ed/(W_y f_y/γ_M1) + k_zz M_z,Ed/(W_z f_y/γ_M1) ≤ 1, the characteristic bending resistance M_Rk over γ_M1 without the shear reduction.

1. Scope and method
Calculates the shear and tension in every bolt of a planar group under eccentric in-plane forces, torque, axial load and out-of-plane moments, by the elastic method. It also finds the governing bolt and an optional lever-model prying addition.
Centroid of the bolt group, each bolt weighted by its area: x_c = Σ A x / Σ A (y_c likewise). Torsion about the centroid from the eccentric load: T = F_y (e_x − x_c) − F_x (e_y − y_c) + M_z. Polar moment of the bolt areas about the centroid: J = Σ A r². Shear on each bolt, its direct share plus the torsion (vector sum): v = A (F/ΣA) + T·A·r/J. Tension on each bolt, its direct share plus the moments, with the product of inertia so that an unsymmetric pattern is right: t = A N/ΣA + A·[(M_x I_y − M_y I_xy) y' + (M_y I_x − M_x I_xy) x']/(I_x I_y − I_xy²), where I_x = Σ A y'², I_y = Σ A x'², I_xy = Σ A x'y' (this reduces to M_x A y'/Σ A y'² + M_y A x'/Σ A x'² only for a pattern symmetric about a centroidal axis). Tension with prying, when the distances a and b are given: t_p = t (1 + b/a). Combined shear and tension, circular interaction (elastic method, Shigley-style): (v/F_v_Rd)² + (t_p/F_t_Rd)² ≤ 1. Combined shear and tension, EN 1993-1-8 linear form (each is also checked alone; the highest governs): v/F_v_Rd + t_p/(1.4 F_t_Rd) ≤ 1.

1. Scope and method
Calculates the bearing, bolt tension, T-stub and shear utilisations of a base plate under an I/H, RHS or CHS column on grout, and the plate thickness that satisfies them. The anchorage is designed outside this sheet.
Bearing (cl. 6.2.5): f_cd = α_cc·f_ck/γ_c; f_jd = β_j·α·f_cd, β_j = 2/3; c = t_p·√[f_y/(3·f_jd·γ_M0)] Axial (cl. 6.2.8.2): N_j,Rd = f_jd·A_eff, A_eff the bearing strips of width c round the column footprint (full rectangle when they overlap); u_N = N_c,Ed/N_j,Rd Moment (cl. 6.2.8.3, Table 6.7): z_C from the section ((h − t_f)/2, (h − t)/2, half-annulus centroid) or entered, x_b the bolt row from the axis, z = z_C + x_b; C_Ed = (M_Ed + N_Ed·x_b)/z and T_Ed = C_Ed − N_Ed when both are ≥ 0; with N_Ed > 0 and T_Ed < 0 both sides are in compression, C = N_Ed/2 ± M_Ed/(2z_C); with N_Ed < 0 and C_Ed < 0 both rows are in tension, T = −N_Ed/2 ± M_Ed/(2x_b); F_t,Ed = T_Ed/n_t at the more loaded row Plate T-stub (cl. 6.2.6.11, 6.2.4, Table 6.2): M_pl,Rd = 0.25·l_eff·t_p²·f_y/γ_M0; without prying min(2·M_pl,Rd/m, n_t·F_t,Rd); with prying the modes 4·M_pl,Rd/m, (2·M_pl,Rd + n·ΣF_t,Rd)/(m + n) with n = min(e_min, 1.25m), and n_t·F_t,Rd; l_eff is the total for the n_t bolts Compression T-stub (cl. 6.2.6.9, 6.2.6.10): C_Rd = f_jd·A_flange, checked when M_Ed > 0 and the resultant is in compression Shear (cl. 6.2.2): F_2,vb,Rd = α_bc·f_ub·A_s/γ_M2, α_bc = 0.44 − 0.0003·f_yb; F_vb,Rd = min(F_1,vb,Rd, F_2,vb,Rd); V_Rd = C_f,d·N_c,Ed + n_b·F_vb,Rd; V_b,Ed = max(V_Ed − C_f,d·N_c,Ed, 0)/n_b Required thickness: the smallest t_p in 10–100 mm with u_N, u_C and u_T all ≤ 1, z_C, C_Ed and T_Ed re-evaluated at every trial t_p

3. Assumptions
  A1. The axial force is positive in compression, the opposite of the member check sheet. (Sign convention.)
  A2. A link from the frame sheet takes the reaction R1_y, never the member force N_e1. (R1_y is up positive, so compression into the base.)
  A3. Loading is static, with a major-axis moment only. (Scope of this sheet.)
  A4. The plate is unstiffened and rectangular, on grout adequate for β_j = 2/3. (The grout is checked when its strength and thickness are entered.)
  A5. The simplified T-stub model has one compression resultant and one tension resultant per bolt row. (BS EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.8.3.)
  A6. The compression resultant acts at the flange centre, or at the half-annulus centroid of a CHS. (BS EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.8.3.)
  A7. The force regime follows Table 6.7, and the web is not credited in the moment check. (BS EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.8.3.)
  A8. The effective length l_eff is the total for the n_t bolts of a row. (Read from the cl. 6.2.6.5 patterns.)
  A9. One l_eff serves modes 1 and 2, which is conservative for mode 2. (l_eff,1 is not more than l_eff,2.)
  A10. The concrete, pull-out, breakout, edge and interaction failures of the anchors are designed in Hilti PROFIS. (From F_t,Ed and V_b,Ed per anchor; the report is filed as Evidence and referenced.)
  A11. With prying switched on, the prying force Q is not in F_t,Ed and PROFIS must add it. (External anchorage design.)
  A12. The friction resistance counts only the compression, C_f,d·max(N_Ed, 0). (BS EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.2.)
  A13. A column in net tension has no friction resistance. (BS EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.2.)
  A14. Anchor bolts share the shear equally. (BS EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.2.)
  A15. Anchor bolt holes are no larger than the standard clearance. (Oversized or slotted holes are outside this sheet.)
  A16. The bearing outstand c is cut back where it would pass the plate edge. (Conservative against an uncut c.)
  A17. The web and the column interior keep the full outstand c. (Conservative against an uncut c.)
  A18. The CHS compression side is half the annular bearing area, with its centroid as lever arm. (An approximation.)
  A19. The compression T-stub is checked against the flange area alone when M_Ed is above 0. (Conservative limitation.)
  A20. The whole footprint resists at M_Ed = 0, so the compression check jumps just above 0, conservatively. (Limitation.)
  A21. Partial factors and the friction coefficient come from the records in use. (γ_c and α_cc: EN 1992-1-1 NA; γ_M2: EN 1993-1-8 NA; γ_M0: EN 1993-1-1 NA; C_f,d: the grout used.)
