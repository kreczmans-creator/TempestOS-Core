1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Design values of a single action effect (a force, a moment or a load intensity) under every EN 1990 Annex A1 combination for buildings: ULS persistent/transient, SLS characteristic, frequent and quasi-permanent, and accidental. Every variable action is tried as the leading one; the maximum and minimum envelopes and their governing combinations are reported.
ULS 6.10: Σ γ_G·G + γ_Q·K_FI·Q_1 + Σ γ_Q·K_FI·ψ_0·Q_i (K_FI also on γ_G,sup) ULS 6.10a: Σ γ_G·G + Σ γ_Q·K_FI·ψ_0·Q_i; 6.10b: Σ ξ·γ_G·G + γ_Q·K_FI·Q_1 + Σ γ_Q·K_FI·ψ_0·Q_i Permanent factor: γ_G,sup when the action adds to the effect sought, γ_G,inf when it opposes it Variable actions: included only when they add to the effect sought (otherwise zero) SLS characteristic 6.14b: Σ G + Q_1 + Σ ψ_0·Q_i; frequent 6.15b: Σ G + ψ_1·Q_1 + Σ ψ_2·Q_i; quasi-permanent 6.16b: Σ G + Σ ψ_2·Q_i Accidental 6.11b: Σ G + A_d + ψ_1·Q_1 + Σ ψ_2·Q_i
  Ref: EN 1990:2002+A1:2005 cl. 6.4.3.2 — Combinations for persistent or transient design situations (6.10, 6.10a, 6.10b)

Title: Beam and 2D frame analysis
Author: unknown    Authored: 2026-10-25
Checker: J. Reviewer    Checked: 2026-10-25
Issued: 2026-10-25    Issue: STR-SUP-001    Client: Client Co

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Linear elastic analysis of a plane frame or continuous beam by the direct stiffness method: reactions, member end forces, axial force, shear, bending moment and deflection along every member with their extremes and positions, for each load combination and as an envelope. Global x to the right, y up; moments and couples anticlockwise positive; sagging moments positive along a member from node i to node j.
Member stiffness, three freedoms per node, Euler–Bernoulli members: axial EA/L and bending EI/L³ terms. End releases (pins) are taken out by static condensation; supports remove freedoms or add spring stiffness. Span loads become equivalent nodal loads, the exact fixed-end forces. Displacements, by banded LDLᵀ after a reverse Cuthill–McKee ordering (a non-positive pivot is reported as a mechanism): K u = F. Internal forces and deflections follow from the member end actions and Macaulay functions of the span loads. Extremes are found at stations along each member, plus the zero-shear and zero-slope points found by bisection.
  Ref: Hibbeler, Structural Analysis Ch. 16 — Plane frame analysis using the stiffness method
  Ref: Roark's Formulas for Stress and Strain Ch. 8, Table 8.1 — Shear, moment, slope and deflection formulas for elastic straight beams
  Ref: BS EN 1990 6.4.3 — Combinations of actions (user-entered factors)

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Resistance checks of a steel member of class 1, 2 or 3 cross-section: tension (6.2.3), compression (6.2.4) and flexural buckling about y and z (6.3.1), bending about y and z (6.2.5), shear with the high-shear reduction (6.2.6, 6.2.8), the linear cross-section interaction (6.2.1(7)) and a simplified member interaction (6.3.3 with user-entered k factors). Lateral-torsional buckling and class 4 sections are outside this sheet.
Axial plastic resistance: N_pl,Rd = A f_y/γ_M0. Bending resistance, plastic modulus for class 1–2 and elastic for class 3: M_c,Rd = W f_y/γ_M0. Shear resistance: V_pl,Rd = A_v f_y/(√3 γ_M0). Bending resistance reduced by high shear, when V_Ed > 0.5 V_pl,Rd: ρ = (2V_Ed/V_pl,Rd − 1)², resistance × (1 − ρ). Elastic critical load and non-dimensional slenderness: N_cr = π²EI/L_cr²; λ̄ = √(A f_y/N_cr). Buckling reduction factor and buckling resistance: Φ = 0.5[1 + α(λ̄ − 0.2) + λ̄²]; χ = 1/(Φ + √(Φ² − λ̄²)) ≤ 1; N_b,Rd = χ A f_y/γ_M1. Cross-section check, linear interaction: N_Ed/N_Rd + M_y,Ed/M_y,Rd + M_z,Ed/M_z,Rd ≤ 1. Member check, axial force and bending with buckling: N_Ed/N_b,Rd,min + k_yy M_y,Ed/M_y,Rd + k_zz M_z,Ed/M_z,Rd ≤ 1.
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.2.3 — Tension
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.2.4 — Compression
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.2.5 — Bending moment
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.2.6 — Shear
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.2.8 — Bending and shear
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.2.1(7) — Linear summation of utilisations
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.3.1.1, 6.3.1.2 — Uniform members in compression: buckling resistance, buckling curves
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.3.3 — Uniform members in bending and axial compression
  Ref: Gardner & Nethercot, Designers' Guide to Eurocode 3 §6.3.1 — Imperfection factors for the buckling curves

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
A pattern of bolts in a plane under in-plane forces (with eccentricity, so torsion), out-of-plane moments and axial load: the shear and tension in every bolt by the elastic (area-weighted) method, the governing bolt, and an optional lever-model prying addition.
Centroid of the bolt group, each bolt weighted by its area: x_c = Σ A x / Σ A (y_c likewise). Torsion about the centroid from the eccentric load: T = F_y (e_x − x_c) − F_x (e_y − y_c) + T_z. Polar moment of the bolt areas about the centroid: J = Σ A r². Shear on each bolt, its direct share plus the torsion (vector sum): v = A (F/ΣA) + T·A·r/J. Tension on each bolt, its direct share plus the moments, with the product of inertia so that an unsymmetric pattern is right: t = A N/ΣA + A·[(M_x I_y − M_y I_xy) y' + (M_y I_x − M_x I_xy) x']/(I_x I_y − I_xy²), where I_x = Σ A y'², I_y = Σ A x'², I_xy = Σ A x'y' (this reduces to M_x A y'/Σ A y'² + M_y A x'/Σ A x'² only for a pattern symmetric about a centroidal axis). Tension with prying, when the distances a and b are given: t_p = t (1 + b/a). Combined shear and tension, circular interaction (elastic method, Shigley-style): (v/V_R)² + (t_p/T_R)² ≤ 1. Combined shear and tension, EN 1993-1-8 linear form (each is also checked alone; the highest governs): v/V_R + t_p/(1.4 T_R) ≤ 1.
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 §3.6, §3.8 — Design resistance of individual fasteners; elastic distribution in a bolt group
  Ref: AISC Steel Construction Manual Part 7 — Eccentrically loaded bolt groups — elastic method

Bolt strengths: f_ub = class × 100 MPa; A_s = π/4·((d₂ + d₃)/2)², d₂ = d − 0.6495P, d₃ = d − 1.2269P Shear (cl. 3.6, Table 3.4): F_v,Rd = α_v·f_ub·A·n_s/γ_M2; α_v = 0.6 (4.6, 5.6, 8.8 or shank in the plane), 0.5 (4.8, 5.8, 6.8, 10.9 with threads in the plane) Bolt bearing resistance: F_b,Rd = k₁·α_b·f_u·d·t/γ_M2; α_b = min(α_d, f_ub/f_u, 1), α_d = e₁/3d₀ (end) or p₁/3d₀ − 1/4 (inner); k₁ = min(2.8e₂/d₀ − 1.7, 1.4p₂/d₀ − 1.7, 2.5) Bolt tension resistance: F_t,Rd = k₂·f_ub·A_s/γ_M2, k₂ = 0.9; punching B_p,Rd = 0.6π·d_m·t·f_u/γ_M2, d_m = (s + e)/2 from the across-flats s and the across-corners e of the smaller of head and nut, or d_m = s when e is not entered (conservative); combined F_v/F_v,Rd + F_t/(1.4F_t,Rd) ≤ 1 Block tearing (cl. 3.10.2): V_eff,1,Rd = f_u·A_nt/γ_M2 + f_y·A_nv/(√3·γ_M0) Weld, simplified (cl. 4.5.3.3): f_vw,d = f_u/(√3·β_w·γ_M2), F_w,Rd = f_vw,d·a; directional (cl. 4.5.3.2): √(σ⊥² + 3(τ⊥² + τ∥²)) ≤ f_u/(β_w·γ_M2), σ⊥ ≤ 0.9f_u/γ_M2

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
An unstiffened rectangular base plate under an I/H, RHS or CHS column on grouted concrete: bearing resistance, the force regime and holding-down bolt tension for a major-axis moment, the tension T-stub of the plate and the compression T-stub, shear by friction and anchor bolts, and the plate thickness that satisfies them together. The bolt tension and shear per anchor go to Hilti PROFIS for the anchorage, which is done outside this sheet.
Bearing (cl. 6.2.5): f_cd = α_cc·f_ck/γ_c; f_jd = β_j·α·f_cd, β_j = 2/3; c = t_p·√[f_y/(3·f_jd·γ_M0)] Axial (cl. 6.2.8.2): N_j,Rd = f_jd·A_eff, A_eff the bearing strips of width c round the column footprint (full rectangle when they overlap); u_N = N_c,Ed/N_j,Rd Moment (cl. 6.2.8.3, Table 6.7): z_C from the section ((h − t_f)/2, (h − t)/2, half-annulus centroid) or entered, x_b the bolt row from the axis, z = z_C + x_b; C_Ed = (M_Ed + N_Ed·x_b)/z and T_Ed = C_Ed − N_Ed when both are ≥ 0; with N_Ed > 0 and T_Ed < 0 both sides are in compression, C = N_Ed/2 ± M_Ed/(2z_C); with N_Ed < 0 and C_Ed < 0 both rows are in tension, T = −N_Ed/2 ± M_Ed/(2x_b); F_t,Ed = T_Ed/n_t at the more loaded row Plate T-stub (cl. 6.2.6.11, 6.2.4, Table 6.2): M_pl,Rd = 0.25·l_eff·t_p²·f_y/γ_M0; without prying min(2·M_pl,Rd/m, n_t·F_t,Rd); with prying the modes 4·M_pl,Rd/m, (2·M_pl,Rd + n·ΣF_t,Rd)/(m + n) with n = min(e_min, 1.25m), and n_t·F_t,Rd; l_eff is the total for the n_t bolts Compression T-stub (cl. 6.2.6.9, 6.2.6.10): C_Rd = f_jd·A_flange, checked when M_Ed > 0 and the resultant is in compression Shear (cl. 6.2.2): F_2,vb,Rd = α_bc·f_ub·A_s/γ_M2, α_bc = 0.44 − 0.0003·f_yb; F_vb,Rd = min(F_1,vb,Rd, F_2,vb,Rd); V_Rd = C_f,d·N_c,Ed + n_b·F_vb,Rd; V_b,Ed = max(V_Ed − C_f,d·N_c,Ed, 0)/n_b Required thickness: the smallest t_p in 10–100 mm with u_N, u_C and u_T all ≤ 1 (each falls as t_p rises)
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.2 — Design resistance of column bases in shear

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. N_Ed is positive in compression here, the opposite of S3 (tension positive): a link from S2 must take the reaction R1_y (up positive, compression into the base), never the member force N_e1. (Sign convention.)
  A2. Static loading; a major-axis moment only (uniaxial); an unstiffened rectangular plate on grout of adequate strength and thickness for β_j = 2/3 (checked when the grout strength and thickness are entered). (Scope of S6.)
  A3. The simplified T-stub model: one compression resultant at the centre of the compression T-stub (flange centre, or the half-annulus centroid of a CHS) and one tension resultant per bolt row; the force regime follows EN 1993-1-8 Table 6.7. The web is not given credit in the moment check. (EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.8.3.)
  A4. The effective length l_eff is the total for the n_t bolts of a row, read from the cl. 6.2.6.5 patterns; it is used for modes 1 and 2 alike (l_eff,1 ≤ l_eff,2, so this is conservative for mode 2). (One input.)
  A5. Concrete, anchor tension, pull-out, breakout, edge and the tension–shear interaction of the anchors are designed in Hilti PROFIS from F_t,Ed and V_b,Ed per anchor; the report is filed as Evidence and referenced. With prying switched on the prying force Q is not in F_t,Ed and PROFIS must add it. (External anchorage design.)
  A6. The friction resistance counts only the compression, C_f,d·max(N_Ed, 0); a column in net tension has none. Anchor bolts share shear equally, with their holes assumed no larger than the standard clearance (oversized or slotted holes are outside this sheet). (EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.2.)
  A7. The bearing outstand c is cut back where it would pass the plate edge; the web and the column interior keep the full c. The CHS compression side is half of the annular bearing area, with its centroid as lever arm (an approximation). (Conservative against an uncut c.)
  A8. The compression T-stub is checked against the flange area alone as soon as M_Ed > 0, while at M_Ed = 0 the whole footprint (web included) resists, so the compression check jumps at M_Ed = 0⁺ (conservative). (Limitation.)
  A9. Partial factors and the friction coefficient come from the records in use (γ_c, α_cc: EN 1992-1-1 NA; γ_M2: EN 1993-1-8 NA; γ_M0: EN 1993-1-1 NA; C_f,d: the grout used); the values shown default to the EN recommended and UK values. (A small set of factors only; no table from a standard is shipped.)
  A10. t (Wall thickness (RHS and CHS)) is taken as 10 mm. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A11. D (Outside diameter (CHS)) is taken as 220 mm. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A12. f_y (Plate yield strength) is taken as 275 MPa. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A13. f_ck (Concrete characteristic cylinder strength) is taken as 25 MPa. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A14. alpha_cc (α_cc, long-term effects on the concrete strength) is taken as 0.85. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A15. gamma_c (γ_c, partial factor for concrete) is taken as 1.5. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A16. gamma_M0 (γ_M0, partial factor for the plate) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A17. gamma_M2 (γ_M2, partial factor for the anchor bolts) is taken as 1.25. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A18. C_fd (C_f,d, friction coefficient plate to grout) is taken as 0.2. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A19. n_t (Holding-down bolts in each bolt row (one row each side)) is taken as 2. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A20. n_b (Holding-down bolts in total (both sides)) is taken as 4. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A21. prying (Prying forces develop (short bolts, L_b ≤ L_b*); off is conservative for the plate) is taken as no. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A22. The outstand c is cut back to the plate edge where it would pass it, so no area outside the plate is counted. (Conservative against an uncut c.)
  A23. β_j = 2/3 assumes the grout is at least 0.2·f_ck and no thicker than 0.2 × the smaller plate dimension; enter the grout strength and thickness to check it. (EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5.)
