1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Resistance checks of a steel member of class 1, 2 or 3 cross-section: tension (6.2.3), compression (6.2.4) and flexural buckling about y and z (6.3.1), bending about y and z (6.2.5), shear with the high-shear reduction (6.2.6, 6.2.8), the linear cross-section interaction (6.2.1(7)) and a simplified member interaction (6.3.3 with user-entered k factors). Lateral-torsional buckling and class 4 sections are outside this sheet.
N_pl,Rd = A f_y/γ_M0; M_c,Rd = W f_y/γ_M0 with W = W_pl (class 1–2) or W_el (class 3); V_pl,Rd = A_v f_y/(√3 γ_M0); ρ = (2V_Ed/V_pl,Rd − 1)² when V_Ed > 0.5 V_pl,Rd reduces the bending resistance by (1 − ρ); N_cr = π²EI/L_cr², λ̄ = √(A f_y/N_cr), Φ = 0.5[1 + α(λ̄ − 0.2) + λ̄²], χ = 1/(Φ + √(Φ² − λ̄²)) ≤ 1, N_b,Rd = χ A f_y/γ_M1; cross-section: N_Ed/N_Rd + M_y,Ed/M_y,Rd + M_z,Ed/M_z,Rd ≤ 1; member: N_Ed/N_b,Rd,min + k_yy M_y,Ed/M_y,Rd + k_zz M_z,Ed/M_z,Rd ≤ 1.
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.2.3 — Tension
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.2.4 — Compression
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.2.5 — Bending moment
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.2.6 — Shear
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.2.8 — Bending and shear
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.2.1(7) — Linear summation of utilisations
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.3.1.1, 6.3.1.2 — Uniform members in compression: buckling resistance, buckling curves
  Ref: BS EN 1993-1-1 6.3.3 — Uniform members in bending and axial compression
  Ref: Gardner & Nethercot, Designers' Guide to Eurocode 3 §6.3.1 — Imperfection factors for the buckling curves

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. The cross-section class is as stated by the user (1, 2 or 3); class 4 effective properties are not derived here. (6.2.2.5 effective widths are outside this sheet.)
  A2. Lateral-torsional buckling is not checked: the compression flange is assumed restrained, or LTB is checked elsewhere. (6.3.2 not implemented.)
  A3. The member interaction uses the user's k_yy and k_zz (default 1.0) with the smaller flexural-buckling reduction on the axial term; the Annex A/B interaction factors are not derived. (6.3.3 simplified.)
  A4. Imperfection factors per curve are the textbook values and may be overridden; partial factors default to 1.0 (UK National Annex) and may be overridden. (User-editable with provenance.)
