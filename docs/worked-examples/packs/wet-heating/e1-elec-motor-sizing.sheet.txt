1. Scope and method
Torque, speed and power a motor needs to move a load through a mechanism on a point-to-point cycle: the load reflected to the motor shaft, the acceleration from the motion profile, peak torque during acceleration, RMS torque over the cycle (thermal duty), peak speed, inertia ratio, and a check of a candidate motor's rated and peak torque and speed with a safety margin. A rotary load that runs continuously (a pump, a fan) is sized instead from its steady torque and speed and a run-up time.
Motor-to-load ratio of a linear mechanism, in metres per motor radian: k = p/(2π i) (screw); D/(2 i) (belt); D_p/(2 i) (rack). Load inertia reflected to the motor through a linear mechanism: J_refl = m k²/η. Load torque at the motor through a linear mechanism: T_load = F k/η, with F = m g (sin θ + μ cos θ) + F_p. Reflected inertia and torque of a rotary load through a gearbox: J_refl = J_L/(i² η); T_load = T_L/(i η). Move profile, trapezoidal 1/3–1/3–1/3 (adjustable): v_pk = s/(t_move − t_acc); a = v_pk/t_acc. Move profile, S-curve (sine acceleration, same peak speed): a_pk = (π/2) a_trap. Torque to accelerate the total inertia, and the peak torque: T_acc = J_total α; T_pk = T_acc + T_load; deceleration T_dec = −T_acc + T_load; the peak check is on the larger magnitude, SF·max(|T_pk|, |T_dec|) ≤ T_peak, and the braking power is P_brake = max(0, −T_dec·ω_pk). Design torque for a coupling or any part downstream that must carry what the drive can deliver, on the basis the owner chooses (T_basis): the load peak T_design = max(|T_pk|, |T_dec|) (default; T_pk alone in continuous duty, where there is no deceleration), the motor peak T_design = T_peak, a service factor on the rated torque T_design = SF_c·T_rated, or a protective limit T_design = T_lim (a torque limiter or a drive setting). Holding torque in the dwell of a linear mechanism that is not held by a brake: T_hold = m g sin θ·k (efficiency not credited: it is a torque to stand still); it is part of the RMS torque for the whole dwell. Alternating torque amplitude of a point-to-point cycle about T_load, for a shaft's fatigue: T_alt = (T_pk − T_dec)/2 (0 in continuous duty). Torque behind the ratio on the output shaft of a rotary mechanism: T_out = T·i·η (mean, alternating and peak), n_out = n_pk/i. Peak axial thrust of a ball screw on its support bearings: F_ax_pk = F_load + m a_pk (0 for other mechanisms). RMS (thermal) torque over the whole cycle, dwell included: T_rms = √(Σ T_i² t_i / t_cycle), the dwell at T_hold. RMS mechanical power over the cycle, the thermal-equivalent continuous power a supply sees (regeneration counts as power; the instantaneous peak P_pk is not a continuous demand), trapezoidal profile: P_rms = ω_pk √{[t_acc·(2/3)(T_load² + T_acc²) + T_load² t_const]/t_cycle}; for the S-curve the acceleration term becomes t_acc·[(3/4) T_load² + (5/16) T_acc²]. Continuous rotary duty (a pump or fan), speed at the motor: ω = 2π n_L i/60; n_pk = n_L i. Continuous rotary duty, load torque and RMS torque: T_load = T_L/(i η); T_rms = T_load. Peak torque during the run-up, with full load throughout (conservative): T_pk = J_total ω/t_start + T_load. Continuous power: P_cont = T_load ω; P_rms = P_cont; continuous duty needs the steady load torque T_L (blank or zero is refused: it would size the motor for no load). Rated shaft power of the candidate motor, from its rated torque and its rated speed n_rated (not the running speed), the shaft power its supply must be sized for; without n_rated there is no P_rated and the sheet warns: P_rated = T_rated·2π n_rated/60; check P_cont ≤ P_rated, u_power = P_cont/P_rated.

3. Assumptions
  A1. Rigid transmission; the mechanism efficiency applies to the driving direction and is also used in the deceleration phase (conservative for peak, slightly pessimistic for RMS). (Servo-sizing practice.)
  A2. The friction force is Coulomb friction on the moved mass; viscous and breakaway friction are not modelled unless included in F_p. (Load model.)
  A3. Continuous duty: the load torque is constant at the stated speed and the motor accelerates the whole inertia linearly to speed in t_start while carrying the full load torque (conservative); the move distance, move time, acceleration fraction, dwell and profile are ignored. (Run-up idealisation for pumps and fans.)
  A4. The S-curve profile has sine-shaped acceleration over the same acceleration time, so its peak acceleration is π/2 times the trapezoidal value. (Jerk-limited profile idealisation.)
  A5. i (Gear ratio i (motor : output, 1 = none)) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A6. eta (Mechanism efficiency (overall)) is taken as 0.9. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A7. p_screw (Screw lead (per revolution of the screw)) is taken as 10 mm. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A8. m (Moved mass (linear mechanisms)) is taken as 50 kg. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A9. mu (Friction coefficient (guide / bearing)) is taken as 0.1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A10. theta (Incline from horizontal (+ moving uphill)) is taken as 0 °. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A11. F_p (Process force opposing the move (linear)) is taken as 0 N. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A12. brake (A brake or a self-locking drive holds a linear axis during the dwell (no holding torque); unticked, a vertical or inclined axis is held by the motor) is taken as no. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A13. profile (Motion profile) is taken as trapezoidal. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A14. s (Move distance (linear: m; rotary: output revolutions)) is taken as 0.5. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A15. t_move (Move time) is taken as 0.5 s. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A16. f_acc (Acceleration (and deceleration) fraction of the move time) is taken as 0.333333. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A17. t_dwell (Dwell between moves) is taken as 0.5 s. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A18. J_motor (Candidate motor rotor inertia) is taken as 0.0002 kg·m². (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A19. T_rated (Candidate motor continuous (rated) torque) is taken as 2 N·m. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A20. T_peak (Candidate motor peak torque) is taken as 6 N·m. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A21. T_basis (Design torque basis for downstream couplings (T_design): the load's peak (the larger of the accelerating and the braking torque, in magnitude), the motor's typed peak torque, a service factor on the rated torque, or a protective limit such as a torque limiter) is taken as Load peak (T_pk). (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A22. n_max (Candidate motor maximum speed) is taken as 6000 rpm. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A23. SF (Safety margin on torque) is taken as 1.25. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A24. J_ratio_max (Inertia ratio limit (load : motor), warning) is taken as 10. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)

4. Working

      = 1 / 1
      = 1
        Output radians per motor radian
  [20] T_load = T_L / (i * eta)
      = 0.00032339 N·m / (1 × 0.9)
      = 0.00035933 N·m
        Load torque at the motor shaft (constant)
  [21] J_refl = J_L / (i^2 * eta)
      = 0.002 kg·m² / (1^2 × 0.9)
      = 0.0022222 kg·m²
        Load inertia reflected to the motor shaft
  [22] omega_pk = n_L * i
      = 2800 rpm × 1
      = 293.22 rad/s
        Motor speed, n_L · i
  [23] J_total = J_motor + J_mech + J_refl
      = 0.0002 kg·m² + 0 kg·m² + 0.0022222 kg·m²
      = 0.0024222 kg·m²
        Total inertia at the motor shaft
  [24] alpha_pk = omega_pk / t_start
      = 293.22 rad/s / 5 s
      = 58.643 rad/s²
        Run-up angular acceleration (to speed in t_start)
  [25] T_acc = J_total * alpha_pk
      = 0.0024222 kg·m² × 58.643 rad/s²
      = 0.14205 N·m
        Run-up (acceleration) torque
  [26] T_pk = T_acc + T_load
      = 0.14205 N·m + 0.00035933 N·m
      = 0.14241 N·m
        Peak torque: run-up carrying the full load (conservative)
  [27] T_rms = T_load
      = 0.00035933 N·m
      = 0.00035933 N·m

  [28] T_alt = 0 N*m

      = 0.14241 N·m

  [34] n_pk = omega_pk
      = 293.22 rad/s

        Motor speed
  [35] T_out_m = T_load * i * eta
      = 0.00035933 N·m × 1 × 0.9
      = 0.00032339 N·m
        Mean torque on the output shaft (the constant load torque)
  [36] T_out_a = T_alt * i * eta
      = 0 N·m × 1 × 0.9
      = 0 N·m
        Alternating torque amplitude on the output shaft
  [37] T_out_pk = T_pk * i * eta
      = 0.14241 N·m × 1 × 0.9
      = 0.12817 N·m

  [38] n_out = n_pk / i
      = 2800 rpm / 1

        Output shaft speed
  [39] P_cont = T_load * omega_pk
      = 0.00035933 N·m × 293.22 rad/s

      = 0.10536 W / 596.9 W

      = 0.041756 kW
