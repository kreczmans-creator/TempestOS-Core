1. SCOPE AND METHOD
A closed LTHW system below 100 °C: the catalogue output (at ΔT 50 K) each room's emitter needs at the design flow, return and room temperatures, checked against the selected emitters; and the minimum expansion vessel for the system volume, fill and maximum temperatures, static head and pressures.
Emitter temperature difference: ΔT = (t_f + t_r)/2 − t_i, or the log mean (t_f − t_r)/ln((t_f − t_i)/(t_r − t_i)) Output at design: Φ = Φ50·(ΔT/50)^n; required catalogue output Φ50 = Φ_req/(ΔT/50)^n System volume: V_s = Σ V_em + V_pipe + V_plant Water expansion: e_x = ρ(t_fill)/ρ(t_max) − 1 (water density by Kell 1975), or a glycol record Vessel pre-charge: p_i = ρ·g·h_static + margin (≥ static head) Vessel size: V_e = e_x·V_s/[1 − (p_i + 1)/(p_f + 1)] (pressures in bar gauge, + 1 bar for absolute); the expansion volume only, sized for the higher of t_max and t_f; a water reserve and a safety-valve margin are not included
  Ref: BS EN 442-2:2014 cl. 5 — Radiators and convectors — test methods and rating (characteristic equation)
  Ref: CIBSE Guide B1 2016, §1.4 — Heating — sealed systems and expansion

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Closed LTHW system below 100 °C; emitter output follows Φ50·(ΔT/50)^n with the manufacturer's n. (Catalogue ratings at 75/65/20 °C.)
  A2. Water density from the Kell (1975) correlation, within about 0.01 % of IAPWS-95 at atmospheric pressure, 0–100 °C; glycol mixtures take e from a record. (Formula, not a table.)
  A3. Gauge pressures are made absolute by adding 1 bar, as the vessel relation is usually written. (Within 1.3 % of the standard atmosphere.)
  A4. V_e is the expansion volume by CIBSE Guide B1's relation only. It does not include a water reserve for system losses, nor a margin between the final pressure and the safety valve setting beyond p_f below p_prv; add both when the vessel is selected, from the heating-system standard in use. (Stated omission.)

4. WORKING
  [14] dT = (t_f + t_r) / 2 - t_i    « BS EN 442-2 cl. 5
      = (75 degC + 65 degC) / 2 − 20 degC
      = 50 K
        Mean water-to-room temperature difference
  [15] dT_fr = t_f - t_r
      = 75 degC − 65 degC
      = 10 K
        Flow-to-return temperature difference (what the water gives up across the emitters)
  [16] t_m = (t_f + t_r) / 2
      = (75 degC + 65 degC) / 2
      = 70 degC
        Mean water temperature, (flow + return)/2
  [17] f_dT = (dT / 50 K)^n
      = (50 K / 50 K)^1.3
      = 1
        Output correction from ΔT 50 K
  [18] R1 = Φ_req/f_dT
      = 379.44 / 0.97408
      = 389.54 W
        Room A: catalogue output needed at ΔT 50 K at its room temperature 21 °C (ΔT 49 K)
  [19] R2 = Φ_req/f_dT
      = 304.61 / 0.97408
      = 312.71 W
        Room B: catalogue output needed at ΔT 50 K at its room temperature 21 °C (ΔT 49 K)
  [20] Phi_tot = Σ Φ_req
      = 684.05 W
      = 684.05 W
        Total heat required by the rooms
  [21] V_em = Σ V_em
      = 8 L
      = 8 L
        Emitter water content
  [22] V_s = V_em + V_pipe + V_plant
      = 8 L + 3.14 L + 10 L
      = 21.14 L
        System water volume
  [23] rho_fill = ρ(t_fill), Kell 1975
      = t = 10 °C
      = 999.7 kg/m³
        Water density at fill
  [24] rho_max = ρ(t_max), Kell 1975
      = t = 85 °C
      = 968.62 kg/m³
        Water density at maximum temperature
  [25] e_x = rho_fill / rho_max - 1
      = 999.7 kg/m³ / 968.62 kg/m³ − 1
      = 0.032086
        Expansion coefficient of water
  [26] p_st = rho_fill * 9.80665 m/s^2 * h_static
      = 999.7 kg/m³ × 9.80665 m/s^2 × 5 m
      = 0.49019 bar
        Static head at the vessel
  [27] p_i = p_st + p_margin
      = 0.49019 bar + 0.3 bar
      = 0.79019 bar
        Pre-charge (initial) pressure, gauge
  [28] V_e = e_x * V_s / (1 - (p_i + 1 bar) / (p_f + 1 bar))    « CIBSE Guide B1 §1.4
      = 0.032086 × 21.14 L / (1 − (0.79019 bar + 1 bar) / (2.5 bar + 1 bar))
      = 1.3885 L
        Minimum expansion vessel volume
