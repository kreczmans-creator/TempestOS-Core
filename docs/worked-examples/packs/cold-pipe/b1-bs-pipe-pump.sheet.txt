1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Friction and fitting losses through series pipe segments at a given flow, the static head, the system curve, the duty point against a pump curve entered as points, the affinity-law shift for a speed or impeller change, and the NPSH available at the pump suction.
Q = Φ/(ρ c_p ΔT) when a heat load is given; v = Q/(πD²/4); Re = ρvD/μ; f = 64/Re (Re < 2300) else Colebrook–White 1/√f = −2 log10(ε/3.7D + 2.51/(Re√f)) by fixed-point iteration (≤ 100 steps) with the Swamee–Jain explicit value as start and cross-check; h_f = f (L + L_eq)/D · v²/2g + K v²/2g; H_sys(Q) = Σdz + h_out + Σh_f(Q) with h_f ∝ Q² scaled from the design flow (closed circuit: Σdz = 0, the rise in the flow is recovered in the return); the water held in the pipes V_pipe = Σ π/4·D²·L; duty point where H_pump(Q) = H_sys(Q) (bisection); affinity: Q ∝ n D³, H ∝ n² D²; NPSH_a = p_atm/ρg + z_s − h_f,suction − p_v/ρg.
  Ref: Crane Technical Paper 410 — Flow of fluids through valves, fittings and pipe
  Ref: Çengel & Cimbala, Fluid Mechanics Ch. 8 — Internal flow: friction factor, minor losses, pump duty
  Ref: CIBSE Guide C Section 4 — Flow of fluids in pipes and ducts (principles)
  Ref: Kell, J. Chem. Eng. Data 20(1), 1975 — Density of liquid water

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Steady, incompressible, fully developed flow; all segments carry the full design flow in series. (Darcy–Weisbach.)
  A2. Fitting losses scale with velocity head (K method) or add pipe length (equivalent-length method); both may be used on one segment. (Crane TP-410 practice.)
  A3. The system curve scales friction losses with Q² from the design flow (turbulent assumption); the static part is independent of flow. (H_sys = H_static + k Q².)
  A4. Water properties are from published correlations at atmospheric pressure (0–100 °C); any other fluid is entered with its own density and viscosity and their provenance. (Kell 1975; Vogel fit; Hyland & Wexler 1983.)
  A5. h_out (Pressure head required at the outlet) is taken as 0 m. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A6. v_max (Velocity limit (warning)) is taken as 3 m/s. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A7. n_ratio (Speed ratio n₂/n₁ (affinity)) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A8. D_ratio (Impeller diameter ratio D₂/D₁ (affinity)) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A9. eta_pump (Pump efficiency at duty (for shaft power)) is taken as 0.7. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A10. p_atm (Atmospheric pressure (NPSH)) is taken as 101.325 kPa. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A11. z_s (Static head at the pump suction (+ flooded, − lift)) is taken as 0 m. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
