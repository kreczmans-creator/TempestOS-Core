1. Scope and method
Calculates the preload from the tightening torque, the load factor, the residual clamp, separation, yield, thread stripping and fatigue amplitude of one bolt clamping members under an axial working load. It uses a simplified VDI 2230 approach.
Tensile stress area: A_s = (π/4)·((d_2 + d_3)/2)², with d_2 = d − 0.6495 p_thread and d_3 = d − 1.2269 p_thread Assembly preload: F_M = M_A/(K d) by the nut factor; F_M = M_A/(0.16 p_thread + 0.58 d_2 μ_G + 0.5 D_Km μ_K) by thread and head friction; F_M = F_V when measured; F_M,min = F_M/α_A after the scatter Thread torsion under preload: τ_M = M_G/W_p with M_G = F_M (0.16 p_thread + 0.58 d_2 μ_G) and W_p = π/16·d_3³ at the minor diameter, which is conservative against the VDI 2230 mean diameter d_S Bolt resilience, the head, free shank and engaged thread as springs in series: δ_S = (0.4 d + 0.4 d)/(E_S A_N) + l_k/(E_S A_s) + 0.5 d/(E_S A_d3) Member stiffness, the clamped parts as two pressure frusta: k = 0.5774 π E d / ln[((1.155 t + d_w − d_h)(d_w + d_h))/((1.155 t + d_w + d_h)(d_w − d_h))] Load factor and embedding: Φ = n_load k_S/(k_S + k_P); F_Z = f_embed (k_S k_P)/(k_S + k_P) Working state: F_KR = max(F_M,min − (1 − Φ) F_A − F_Z, 0); F_S,max = max(F_M + Φ F_A, F_A); σ_red = √(σ_z² + 3 (0.5 τ)²); σ_a = (F_S,max − F_M)/(2 A_s) Thread engagement needed against stripping: m_min = S F_S,max/(0.5 π d τ_B)

3. Assumptions
  A1. The joint is concentrically clamped and loaded, and the members behave as two 30° frusta in series. (Eccentric clamping and loading (VDI 2230 full procedure) are outside this sheet.)
  A2. Thread torsion is taken at 50 % in the equivalent stress for the working state. (Common VDI 2230 practice (k_τ = 0.5).)
  A3. The thread torsion modulus is taken at the minor diameter d_3, which is conservative. (With the VDI 2230 mean diameter the torsion stress is about 14 % lower and ν_M about 5 % lower at the defaults.)
  A4. F_M is the maximum preload, from the smallest friction and nut factor, and F_M,min is the minimum after scatter. (VDI 2230 R2: the maximum preload comes from the minimum friction coefficients.)
  A5. The working load pulsates from 0 to F_A, so the fatigue amplitude is Φ F_A / (2 A_s). (Compare it with the bolt's endurance limit as entered.)
  A6. Thread stripping uses a shear area of half the pitch per unit length on the nominal diameter. (A conservative simplification of ISO 898-2 / VDI 2230 §5.5.5.)

4. Working
  [24] d_2 = d - 0.6495 * p_thread    « ISO 898-1 §9.1.6
      = 12 mm − 0.6495 × 1.75 mm
      = 10.863 mm
        Pitch diameter
  [25] d_3 = d - 1.2269 * p_thread    « ISO 898-1 §9.1.6
      = 12 mm − 1.2269 × 1.75 mm
      = 9.8529 mm
        Minor diameter
  [26] A_s = pi / 4 * ((d_2 + d_3) / 2)^2    « ISO 898-1 §9.1.6
      = π / 4 × ((10.863 mm + 9.8529 mm) / 2)^2
      = 84.266 mm²
        Tensile stress area
  [27] A_N = pi / 4 * d^2
      = π / 4 × (12 mm)^2
      = 113.1 mm²
        Nominal shank area
  [28] A_d3 = pi / 4 * d_3^2
      = π / 4 × (9.8529 mm)^2
      = 76.247 mm²
        Minor-diameter area
  [29] D_Km = (d_w + d_h) / 2
      = (18 mm + 13 mm) / 2
      = 15.5 mm
        Mean bearing diameter under the head
  [30] F_M = M_A / (K * d)    « Shigley eq. 8-27
      = 93 N·m / (0.2 × 12 mm)
      = 38.75 kN
        Assembly preload (nut factor)
  [31] F_M_min = F_M / alpha_A    « VDI 2230 R2
      = 38.75 kN / 1.6
      = 24.219 kN
        Minimum preload after scatter
  [32] sigma_M = F_M / A_s
      = 38.75 kN / 84.266 mm²
      = 459.85 MPa
        Preload stress
  [33] M_G = F_M * (0.16 * p_thread + 0.58 * d_2 * mu_G)    « VDI 2230 §5.4.3
      = 38.75 kN × (0.16 × 1.75 mm + 0.58 × 10.863 mm × 0.12)
      = 40.149 N·m
        Thread torque under preload
  [34] W_p = pi / 16 * d_3^3
      = π / 16 × (9.8529 mm)^3
      = 187.81 mm³
        Polar section modulus at the minor diameter
  [35] tau_M = M_G / W_p
      = 40.149 N·m / 187.81 mm³
      = 213.77 MPa
        Thread torsional stress under preload
  [36] sigma_red_M = sqrt(sigma_M^2 + 3 * tau_M^2)    « VDI 2230 R8
      = sqrt((459.85 MPa)^2 + 3 × (213.77 MPa)^2)
      = 590.38 MPa
        Equivalent stress at assembly (full torsion)
  [37] nu_M = sigma_red_M / R_p02
      = 590.38 MPa / 640 MPa
      = 0.92248
        Utilisation of proof strength at assembly
  [38] sigma_perm = nu_perm * R_p02    « VDI 2230 R7
      = 1 × 640 MPa
      = 640 MPa
        Permitted equivalent stress at assembly (permitted ν times the proof strength)
  [39] u_assembly = nu_M / nu_perm
      = 0.92248 / 1
      = 0.92248
        Assembly utilisation over the permitted ν
  [40] delta_S = 0.4 * d / (E_S * A_N) + l_k / (E_S * A_s) + 0.5 * d / (E_S * A_d3) + 0.4 * d / (E_S * A_N)    « VDI 2230 §5.1.1
      = 0.4 × 12 mm / (205 GPa × 113.1 mm²) + 40 mm / (205 GPa × 84.266 mm²) + 0.5 × 12 mm / (205 GPa × 76.247 mm²) + 0.4 × 12 mm / (205 GPa × 113.1 mm²)
      = 0.0000031135 m/kN
        Bolt resilience (head, shank, engaged thread, nut)
  [41] k_S = 1 / delta_S
      = 1 / 0.0000031135 m/kN
      = 321190 kN/m
        Bolt stiffness
  [42] t_f = l_k / 2
      = 40 mm / 2
      = 20 mm
        Frustum height (two equal frusta)
  [43] k_f = 0.5774 * pi * E_P * d / ln(((1.155 * t_f + d_w - d_h) * (d_w + d_h)) / ((1.155 * t_f + d_w + d_h) * (d_w - d_h)))    « Shigley eq. 8-20
      = 0.5774 × π × 205 GPa × 12 mm / ln((1.155 × 20 mm + 18 mm − 13 mm) × (18 mm + 13 mm) / ((1.155 × 20 mm + 18 mm + 13 mm) × (18 mm − 13 mm)))
      = 3815600 kN/m
        Stiffness of one frustum
  [44] k_P = k_f / 2    « Shigley eq. 8-21
      = 3815600 kN/m / 2
      = 1907800 kN/m
        Clamped members' stiffness (two frusta in series)
  [45] Phi_K = n_load * k_S / (k_S + k_P)    « VDI 2230 R3
      = 0.5 × 321190 kN/m / (321190 kN/m + 1907800 kN/m)
      = 0.072047
        Load factor
  [46] F_Z = f_embed * (k_S * k_P) / (k_S + k_P)    « VDI 2230 R4
      = 11 µm × 321190 kN/m × 1907800 kN/m / (321190 kN/m + 1907800 kN/m)
      = 3.024 kN
        Preload loss from embedding
  [47] F_SA = Phi_K * F_A
      = 0.072047 × 10 kN
      = 0.72047 kN
        Additional bolt load from the working load
  [48] F_PA = (1 - Phi_K) * F_A
      = (1 − 0.072047) × 10 kN
      = 9.2795 kN
        Load relieved from the members
  [49] F_KR = max(F_M_min - F_PA - F_Z, 0 kN)    « VDI 2230 R7
      = max(24.219 kN − 9.2795 kN − 3.024 kN, 0 kN)
      = 11.915 kN
        Residual clamp force (minimum; zero once the joint opens)
  [50] F_S_max = max(F_M + F_SA, F_A)
      = max(38.75 kN + 0.72047 kN, 10 kN)
      = 39.47 kN
        Maximum bolt load (maximum preload + additional load; the whole working load once the joint opens)
  [51] sigma_z_max = F_S_max / A_s
      = 39.47 kN / 84.266 mm²
      = 468.4 MPa
        Maximum bolt tensile stress
  [52] sigma_red_B = sqrt(sigma_z_max^2 + 3 * (0.5 * tau_M)^2)    « VDI 2230 R8
      = sqrt((468.4 MPa)^2 + 3 × (0.5 × 213.77 MPa)^2)
      = 503.66 MPa
        Equivalent stress in the working state (50 % torsion)
  [53] u_yield = sigma_red_B / R_p02
      = 503.66 MPa / 640 MPa
      = 0.78697
        Yield utilisation in the working state
  [54] sigma_a = (F_S_max - F_M) / (2 * A_s)    « VDI 2230 R9
      = (39.47 kN − 38.75 kN) / (2 × 84.266 mm²)
      = 4.2749 MPa
        Fatigue stress amplitude (pulsating 0…F_A load)
  [55] u_fatigue = sigma_a / sigma_a_limit
      = 4.2749 MPa / 50 MPa
      = 0.085499
        Fatigue utilisation
  [56] m_min = S_strip * F_S_max / (0.5 * pi * d * tau_B)    « VDI 2230 §5.5.5
      = 1.25 × 39.47 kN / (0.5 × π × 12 mm × 400 MPa)
      = 6.5437 mm
        Thread engagement needed against stripping
  [57] u_strip = m_min / m_eff
      = 6.5437 mm / 12 mm
      = 0.54531
        Engagement utilisation
  [58] F_A_sep = (F_M_min - F_Z) / (1 - Phi_K)    « VDI 2230 R7
      = (24.219 kN − 3.024 kN) / (1 − 0.072047)
      = 22.84 kN
        Working load at which the members separate (minimum preload, after embedding)
  [59] F_proof = R_p02 * A_s
      = 640 MPa × 84.266 mm²
      = 53.93 kN
        Bolt load at the proof (yield) strength
  [60] n_0 = F_A_sep / F_A
      = 22.84 kN / 10 kN
      = 2.284
        Load factor against separation (F_A,sep / F_A)
  [61] F_A_yield = min((F_proof - F_M) / Phi_K, F_proof)
      = min((53.93 kN − 38.75 kN) / 0.072047, 53.93 kN)
      = 53.93 kN
        Working load at which the bolt reaches its proof load (separation included)
  [62] n_L = F_A_yield / F_A
      = 53.93 kN / 10 kN
      = 5.393
        Load factor against axial yield of the bolt, torsion excluded
  [63] u_clamp = F_KR_req / F_KR
      = 5 kN / 11.915 kN
      = 0.41963
        Required over available residual clamp
