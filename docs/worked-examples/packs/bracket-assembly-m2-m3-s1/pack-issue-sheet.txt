Title: Press and shrink fit
Author: unknown    Authored: 2026-10-27
Checker: J. Reviewer    Checked: 2026-10-27

1. Scope and method
Calculates the interface pressure, stresses, assembly force and friction torque of a shaft pressed or shrunk into a hub, and the shrink temperature. It uses the Lamé thick-cylinder relations and ignores end effects.
Radial interference: δ_r = δ/2. Interface pressure, the hub and the shaft as thick cylinders: p_int = δ_r / [ (R/E_h)·((r_o² + R²)/(r_o² − R²) + ν_h) + (R/E_s)·((R² + r_i²)/(R² − r_i²) − ν_s) ]. Stresses at the hub bore: σ_θ = p_int·(r_o² + R²)/(r_o² − R²); σ_r = −p_int. Stresses at the shaft surface: σ_θ = −p_int·(R² + r_i²)/(R² − r_i²); σ_r = −p_int. Stress at the bore of a hollow shaft: σ_θ = −2·p_int·R²/(R² − r_i²); σ_r = 0. Surface smoothing on assembly (when Rz is entered): δ_eff = max(δ − 0.8·(Rz_h + Rz_s), 0), p_eff = p_int·δ_eff/δ. Axial force and torque the fit holds by friction, on p_eff: F = μ·p_eff·2πR·L; T = μ·p_eff·2πR²·L. The stresses stay at the nominal p_int (the larger interference of the fit). Temperature difference to assemble by heating the hub, with assembly clearance c_a: ΔT = (δ + c_a)/(α·2R).

3. Assumptions
  A1. Both parts are long cylinders in plane stress, and the interface pressure is uniform along the engagement. (The Lamé thick-cylinder relations apply, and end effects are neglected.)
  A2. The interference is the nominal value entered. (Use the minimum interference of the fit for the torque and the force, and the maximum for the stresses.)
  A3. Surface roughness smooths on assembly and takes about 0.8·(Rz_h + Rz_s) off the interference when Rz is entered. (DIN 7190-1 gives the allowance; enter Rz to apply it to the torque and the force.)
  A4. The friction coefficient is as entered and the same for assembly and for torque transmission. (Enter it with its source, because it dominates the force and torque results.)
  A5. Yield is checked by von Mises stress at the hub bore, the shaft surface and a hollow shaft's bore. (The axial stress is zero, and the larger of the two shaft values governs.)
  A6. No surface smoothing is applied, so the torque needs the minimum interference of the fit and the stresses the maximum. (Roughness takes about 0.8·(Rz_h + Rz_s) off the interference, so enter Rz_h and Rz_s to apply it (DIN 7190-1).)

Title: Bolted joint (simplified VDI 2230)

1. Scope and method
Calculates the preload from the tightening torque, the load factor, the residual clamp, separation, yield, thread stripping and fatigue amplitude of one bolt clamping members under an axial working load. It uses a simplified VDI 2230 approach.
Tensile stress area: A_s = (π/4)·((d_2 + d_3)/2)², with d_2 = d − 0.6495 p_thread and d_3 = d − 1.2269 p_thread Assembly preload: F_M = M_A/(K d) by the nut factor; F_M = M_A/(0.16 p_thread + 0.58 d_2 μ_G + 0.5 D_Km μ_K) by thread and head friction; F_M = F_V when measured; F_M,min = F_M/α_A after the scatter Thread torsion under preload: τ_M = M_G/W_p with M_G = F_M (0.16 p_thread + 0.58 d_2 μ_G) and W_p = π/16·d_3³ at the minor diameter, which is conservative against the VDI 2230 mean diameter d_S Bolt resilience, the head, free shank and engaged thread as springs in series: δ_S = (0.4 d + 0.4 d)/(E_S A_N) + l_k/(E_S A_s) + 0.5 d/(E_S A_d3) Member stiffness, the clamped parts as two pressure frusta: k = 0.5774 π E d / ln[((1.155 t + d_w − d_h)(d_w + d_h))/((1.155 t + d_w + d_h)(d_w − d_h))] Load factor and embedding: Φ = n_load k_S/(k_S + k_P); F_Z = f_embed (k_S k_P)/(k_S + k_P) Working state: F_KR = max(F_M,min − (1 − Φ) F_A − F_Z, 0); F_S,max = max(F_M + Φ F_A, F_A); σ_red = √(σ_z² + 3 (0.5 τ)²); σ_a = (F_S,max − F_M)/(2 A_s) Thread engagement needed against stripping: m_min = S F_S,max/(0.5 π d τ_B)

3. Assumptions
  A1. The joint is concentrically clamped and loaded, and the members behave as two 30° frusta in series. (Eccentric clamping and loading (VDI 2230 full procedure) are outside this sheet.)
  A2. Thread torsion is taken at 50 % in the equivalent stress for the working state. (Common VDI 2230 practice (k_τ = 0.5).)
  A3. The thread torsion modulus is taken at the minor diameter d_3, which is conservative. (With the VDI 2230 mean diameter the torsion stress is about 14 % lower and ν_M about 5 % lower at the defaults.)
  A4. F_M is the maximum preload, from the smallest friction and nut factor, and F_M,min is the minimum after scatter. (VDI 2230 R2: the maximum preload comes from the minimum friction coefficients.)
  A5. The working load pulsates from 0 to F_A, so the fatigue amplitude is Φ F_A / (2 A_s). (Compare it with the bolt's endurance limit as entered.)
  A6. Thread stripping uses a shear area of half the pitch per unit length on the nominal diameter. (A conservative simplification of ISO 898-2 / VDI 2230 §5.5.5.)

1. Scope and method
Calculates area, centroid, second moments, moduli, radii of gyration, and torsion and warping constants of a parametric, polygon or composite section. The outline has x horizontal and y vertical; properties use EN 1993 axes, y-y horizontal and z-z vertical.
Area and centroid: A = ½∮(x dy − y dx); x_c = ∫x dA / A; y_c = ∫y dA / A. Second moment about y-y: I_y = ∫y² dA − A y_c², by Green's theorem and the parallel-axis theorem. Second moment about z-z: I_z = ∫x² dA − A x_c²; product I_yz = ∫xy dA − A x_c y_c. Principal axes: tan 2θ = −2 I_yz / (I_y − I_z). Elastic section modulus: W_el_y = I_y / y_max. Plastic section modulus about the equal-area axis, found by bisection: W_pl_y = |Q_above| + |Q_below|. Torsion constant J: Saint-Venant series (solid rectangle), πd⁴/32 (circle), Bredt (closed thin-walled), Σbt³/3 (open). Warping constant of an I-section: I_w = I_z (h − t_f)²/4.

3. Assumptions
  A1. A polygon outline does not cross itself. (Green's theorem integrates the signed loops as given.)
  A2. Holes lie inside the outline and do not overlap one another. (Green's theorem integrates the signed loops as given.)
  A3. Rolled-section root fillets are quarter circles of radius r and flange tips are square. (Published section tables use the same idealisation.)
  A4. Hollow-section corners are quarter circles of radius 1.5 t_w outside and 1.0 t_w inside by default. (BS EN 10210-2 hot-finished idealisation.)
  A5. Thin-walled torsion constants neglect the junction and tip effects except the fillet term kept for rolled I-sections. (Open sections: Σbt³/3; closed: Bredt.)
