1. SCOPE AND METHOD
A shaft (solid or hollow) pressed or shrunk into a hub: the interface pressure for a given diametral interference, the hoop and radial stresses at the interface in hub and shaft, von Mises utilisation against each yield strength, the axial assembly force and the torque the joint can carry by friction, and the temperature rise to shrink-fit the hub.
Radial interference δ_r = δ/2. p = δ_r / [ (R/E_h)·((r_o² + R²)/(r_o² − R²) + ν_h) + (R/E_s)·((R² + r_i²)/(R² − r_i²) − ν_s) ]. Hub bore: σ_θ = p·(r_o² + R²)/(r_o² − R²), σ_r = −p. Shaft surface: σ_θ = −p·(R² + r_i²)/(R² − r_i²), σ_r = −p. F = μ·p·2πR·L; T = μ·p·2πR²·L; ΔT = (δ + c_a)/(α·2R).
  Ref: Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design §3-16, §3-17 — Press and shrink fits; stresses in pressurised cylinders
  Ref: Roark's Formulas for Stress and Strain Ch. 13, Table 13.5 — Thick-walled cylinders under pressure

2. INPUTS
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  d | Interface (nominal) diameter | 50 | mm | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 1 … 1000 mm
  delta | Diametral interference | 40 | µm | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 1 … 500 µm
  d_o | Hub outer diameter | 100 | mm | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 1 … 2000 mm
  d_i | Shaft bore diameter (0 for a solid shaft) | 0 | mm | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 0 … 1000 mm
  L | Engagement length | 60 | mm | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 1 … 2000 mm
  E_h | Hub Young's modulus | 207 | GPa | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 10 … 450 GPa
  nu_h | Hub Poisson's ratio | 0.292 |  | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 0.1 … 0.45
  E_s | Shaft Young's modulus | 207 | GPa | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 10 … 450 GPa
  nu_s | Shaft Poisson's ratio | 0.292 |  | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 0.1 … 0.45
  mu | Coefficient of friction at the interface | 0.15 |  | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 0.05 … 0.4
  S_y_h | Hub yield strength | 350 | MPa | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 50 … 2000 MPa
  S_y_s | Shaft yield strength | 350 | MPa | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 50 … 2000 MPa
  alpha_h | Hub thermal expansion coefficient | 0.000012 | 1/K | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 0.000005 … 0.00003 1/K
  c_a | Assembly clearance wanted for shrink fitting | 20 | µm | drawing: DRG-BRK-010 rev B | 0 … 500 µm

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Both parts are long cylinders in plane stress; the interface pressure is uniform along the engagement. (Lamé thick-cylinder relations; end effects neglected.)
  A2. The friction coefficient is as entered and the same for assembly and for torque transmission. (Enter it with its source; it dominates the force and torque results.)
  A3. Yield is checked by the von Mises equivalent stress at the hub bore and at the shaft surface. (Axial stress taken as zero.)

4. WORKING

  [16] r_o = d_o / 2
      = 100 mm / 2
      = 50 mm
        Hub outer radius
  [17] r_i = d_i / 2
      = 0 mm / 2

  [18] delta_r = delta / 2
      = 40 µm / 2
      = 20 µm
        Radial interference
  [19] C_h = (R / E_h) * ((r_o^2 + R^2) / (r_o^2 - R^2) + nu_h)    « Shigley §3-16
      = 25 mm / 207 GPa × (((50 mm)^2 + (25 mm)^2) / ((50 mm)^2 − (25 mm)^2) + 0.292)
      = 0.23655 mm/GPa
        Hub compliance
  [20] C_s = (R / E_s) * ((R^2 + r_i^2) / (R^2 - r_i^2) - nu_s)    « Shigley §3-16
      = 25 mm / 207 GPa × (((25 mm)^2 + (0 mm)^2) / ((25 mm)^2 − (0 mm)^2) − 0.292)
      = 0.085507 mm/GPa
        Shaft compliance
  [21] p = delta_r / (C_h + C_s)    « Shigley eq. 3-56
      = 20 µm / (0.23655 mm/GPa + 0.085507 mm/GPa)
      = 62.1 MPa
        Interface pressure
  [22] sigma_t_hub = p * (r_o^2 + R^2) / (r_o^2 - R^2)    « Shigley eq. 3-57
      = 62.1 MPa × ((50 mm)^2 + (25 mm)^2) / ((50 mm)^2 − (25 mm)^2)
      = 103.5 MPa
        Hub hoop stress at the bore
  [23] sigma_r_hub = -p
      = −62.1 MPa
      = -62.1 MPa
        Hub radial stress at the bore
  [24] sigma_t_shaft = -p * (R^2 + r_i^2) / (R^2 - r_i^2)    « Shigley eq. 3-58
      = −62.1 MPa × ((25 mm)^2 + (0 mm)^2) / ((25 mm)^2 − (0 mm)^2)
      = -62.1 MPa
        Shaft hoop stress at the surface
  [25] sigma_r_shaft = -p
      = −62.1 MPa
      = -62.1 MPa
        Shaft radial stress at the surface
  [26] sigma_vm_hub = sqrt(sigma_t_hub^2 - sigma_t_hub * sigma_r_hub + sigma_r_hub^2)
      = sqrt((103.5 MPa)^2 − 103.5 MPa × (-62.1 MPa) + (-62.1 MPa)^2)
      = 144.9 MPa
        Hub von Mises stress at the bore
  [27] sigma_vm_shaft = sqrt(sigma_t_shaft^2 - sigma_t_shaft * sigma_r_shaft + sigma_r_shaft^2)
      = sqrt((-62.1 MPa)^2 − (-62.1 MPa) × (-62.1 MPa) + (-62.1 MPa)^2)
      = 62.1 MPa
        Shaft von Mises stress at the surface
  [28] F_a = mu * p * 2 * pi * R * L
      = 0.15 × 62.1 MPa × 2 × π × 25 mm × 60 mm
      = 87.792 kN
        Axial assembly (press) force
  [29] T = mu * p * 2 * pi * R^2 * L
      = 0.15 × 62.1 MPa × 2 × π × (25 mm)^2 × 60 mm
      = 2194.8 N·m
        Torque capacity by friction
  [30] dT = (delta + c_a) / (alpha_h * d)
      = (40 µm + 20 µm) / (0.000012 1/K × 50 mm)
      = 100 K
        Hub temperature rise for shrink fitting

  p = 62.1 MPa    Interface pressure
  sigma_t_hub = 103.5 MPa    Hub hoop stress at bore
  sigma_t_shaft = -62.1 MPa    Shaft hoop stress at surface
