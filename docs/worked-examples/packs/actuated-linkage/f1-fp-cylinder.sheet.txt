1. Scope and method
Calculates a hydraulic cylinder in its circuit: force, speed, swept volume, pump and motor duty, line velocities, orifice flow and a rod buckling check (screening only; ISO/TS 13725 method not implemented). It is not a design to a standard.
Piston and rod areas: A_b = π·D²/4, A_r = π·d_r²/4, A_a = A_b − A_r, area ratio φ = A_b/A_a Cylinder force: F_ext = (p_sys·A_b − p_back·A_a)·η_m, F_ret = (p_sys·A_a − p_back·A_b)·η_m, regenerative F_ext = p_sys·A_r·η_m Speed and time: v_ext = Q·η_vc/A_b, v_ret = Q·η_vc/A_a, t = stroke/v, t_cycle = t_ext + t_ret + t_dwell, and a required speed gives Q = v_req·A_b/η_vc (regenerative A_r) Swept volume: ΔV_ext = A_b·stroke (regenerative A_r·stroke), ΔV_ret = A_a·stroke, ΔV = the larger Pump and motor: Q_p = V_g·n_p·η_vp, P = p_sys·Q_p/η_tp, T = P/ω, motor T = Δp·V_gm/(2π)·η_hm Line velocities: v = Q_line/A_line with Q_suc = max(Q, V_g·n_p), Q_prs = Q_line·A_b/A_a (regenerative A_b/A_r if larger), Q_rtn = Q_line·A_b/A_a, Q_line = max(Q, Q_p), d_min = √(4Q/(π·v_max)) Orifice flow: Q = C_d·A_o·√(2Δp/ρ), d_o = √(4Q/(π·C_d·√(2Δp/ρ))) Rod buckling: L_eff = K_m·L_b, L_b = L_closed + stroke (blank 2·stroke + L_add), λ = L_eff/(d_r/4), λ_c = π·√(2E/f_y), F_cr by Euler π²·E·I/L_eff² above λ_c or Johnson A_r·f_y·[1 − f_y·λ²/(4π²E)], u = max(F_push, p_sys·A_b)·S_f/F_cr

3. Assumptions
  A1. The supply pressure p_sys is the gauge pressure at the cylinder port and at the pump outlet. (F = p·A·η_m, closed form.)
  A2. Line losses are not subtracted, because the pipe calculation gives the losses of a pipe run. (F = p·A·η_m, closed form.)
  A3. The force is the pressure on the area less the back pressure on the other side, times the mechanical efficiency. (F = p·A·η_m, closed form.)
  A4. The speed is the flow into the cylinder times the volumetric efficiency over the area. (v = Q·η_vc/A.)
  A5. The motion is steady, with no acceleration, cushioning or compressibility. (v = Q·η_vc/A.)
  A6. The cycle time is the two strokes plus the dwell. (v = Q·η_vc/A.)
  A7. The pump power is the supply pressure times the pump delivery over the total efficiency. (P = p·Q_p/η_tp.)
  A8. When the pump delivers more than the circuit takes, the excess goes over the relief valve at the supply pressure. (P = p·Q_p/η_tp.)
  A9. That is the conservative case for the drive. (P = p·Q_p/η_tp.)
  A10. The shaft torque is the power over the angular speed. (P = p·Q_p/η_tp.)
  A11. The line flows use the pump delivery when it is larger than the circuit flow. (Continuity.)
  A12. The suction line carries the geometric delivery V_g·n_p. (Continuity.)
  A13. The pressure and return lines carry the larger of the circuit flow and the pump delivery, with no volumetric efficiency. (Continuity.)
  A14. These flows are the conservative side of the speeds, which use Q·η_vc. (Continuity.)
  A15. The cap-port line is judged at the larger flow of the two strokes. (Continuity.)
  A16. On the retract stroke the cap-port line carries the cap-side discharge Q_line·A_b/A_a. (Continuity.)
  A17. In the regenerative extend it carries Q_line·A_b/A_r when that is larger. (Continuity.)
  A18. The velocity limits are your records, and the sheet defaults are placeholders, not values from a standard. (Continuity.)
  A19. The orifice relation is the sharp-edged turbulent one, Q = C_d·A·√(2Δp/ρ), with your discharge coefficient. (Orifice equation, closed form.)
  A20. It ignores viscosity and cavitation. (Orifice equation, closed form.)
  A21. It screens a flow-control orifice and is not a valve selection. (Orifice equation, closed form.)
  A22. The rod is a solid round column of its own diameter, with the effective-length factor of the mounting. (Euler and Johnson column relations, closed form.)
  A23. The tube, the piston and the rod-end stiffness are not modelled. (Euler and Johnson column relations, closed form.)
  A24. The rod diameter is taken over the whole pin-to-pin length at full stroke, closed length plus stroke. (Euler and Johnson column relations, closed form.)
  A25. A body stiffer than the rod makes this conservative. (Euler and Johnson column relations, closed form.)
  A26. The rod is judged at the larger of the push load you enter and the stall thrust p_sys·A_b. (Euler and Johnson column relations, closed form.)
  A27. A stalled, obstructed or relief-limited cylinder loads the rod with the full pressure force on the bore. (Euler and Johnson column relations, closed form.)
  A28. At stall there is no flow, so there is no back pressure, and seal friction does not relieve the rod. (Euler and Johnson column relations, closed form.)
  A29. No pressure-limiting device is credited. (Euler and Johnson column relations, closed form.)
  A30. The regenerative extend is judged at the same stall thrust, because the standard connection can always be selected. (Euler and Johnson column relations, closed form.)
  A31. The mounting factor K_m defaults to 1, pinned at both ends, and a fixed-free mounting is K_m = 2. (Euler and Johnson column relations, closed form.)
  A32. The mounting factor K_m and the safety factor S_f are your records. (Euler and Johnson column relations, closed form.)
  A33. The rod buckling check is a screening, and the ISO/TS 13725 method is not implemented. (Euler and Johnson column relations, closed form.)
  A34. d_suc (Suction line inside diameter) is taken as 40 mm. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)

4. Working
  [25] A_b = pi * D^2 / 4
      = π × (40 mm)^2 / 4
      = 12.566 cm²
        Bore (cap-side) area
  [26] A_r = pi * d_r^2 / 4
      = π × (22 mm)^2 / 4
      = 3.8013 cm²
        Rod area
  [27] A_a = A_b - A_r
      = 12.566 cm² − 3.8013 cm²
      = 8.765 cm²
        Annulus (rod-side) area
  [28] phi = A_b / A_a
      = 12.566 cm² / 8.765 cm²
      = 1.4337
        Area ratio A_b/A_a
  [30] Q = v_req * A_b / eta_vc
      = 331.32 mm/s × 12.566 cm² / 0.98
      = 25.491 L/min
        Flow the required extend speed needs: v·A_b/η_vc
  [31] F_ext = (p_sys * A_b - p_back * A_a) * eta_m
      = (100 bar g × 12.566 cm² − 0 bar g × 8.765 cm²) × 0.95
      = 11.938 kN
        Extend force
  [32] v_ext = Q * eta_vc / A_b
      = 25.491 L/min × 0.98 / 12.566 cm²
      = 331.32 mm/s
        Extend speed
  [33] dV_ext = A_b * stroke
      = 12.566 cm² × 200 mm
      = 0.25133 L
        Liquid taken from the supply for the extend stroke
  [34] F_ret = (p_sys * A_a - p_back * A_b) * eta_m
      = (100 bar g × 8.765 cm² − 0 bar g × 12.566 cm²) × 0.95
      = 8.3268 kN
        Retract force
  [35] v_ret = Q * eta_vc / A_a
      = 25.491 L/min × 0.98 / 8.765 cm²
      = 475.02 mm/s
        Retract speed
  [36] t_ext = stroke / v_ext
      = 200 mm / 331.32 mm/s
      = 0.60364 s
        Extend stroke time
  [37] t_ret = stroke / v_ret
      = 200 mm / 475.02 mm/s
      = 0.42104 s
        Retract stroke time
  [38] t_cycle = t_ext + t_ret + t_dwell
      = 0.60364 s + 0.42104 s + 0 s
      = 1.0247 s
        Cycle time
  [39] dV_ret = A_a * stroke
      = 8.765 cm² × 200 mm
      = 0.1753 L
        Liquid taken from the supply for the retract stroke
  [40] dV = max(dV_ext, dV_ret)
      = max(0.25133 L, 0.1753 L)
      = 0.25133 L
        Swept volume of the larger stroke
  [41] Q_pump = Q
      = 25.491 L/min
      = 25.491 L/min
        Flow the pump delivers at the supply pressure (the circuit flow; no pump entered)
  [42] P_hyd = p_sys * Q_pump
      = 100 bar g × 25.491 L/min
      = 4.2485 kW
        Hydraulic power at the pump outlet
  [43] P_pump = P_hyd / eta_tp
      = 4.2485 kW / 0.88
      = 4.8279 kW
        Pump shaft power
  [44] Q_line = Q
      = 25.491 L/min
      = 25.491 L/min
        Flow in the pressure and return lines before the area ratio (the circuit flow)
  [45] Q_suc = Q
      = 25.491 L/min
      = 25.491 L/min
        Flow in the suction line (the circuit flow)
  [46] Q_prs = Q_line * A_b / A_a
      = 25.491 L/min × 12.566 cm² / 8.765 cm²
      = 36.546 L/min
        Flow in the line to the cap port, worst stroke (retract: the cap-side discharge Q·A_b/A_a)
  [47] Q_rtn = Q_line * A_b / A_a
      = 25.491 L/min × 12.566 cm² / 8.765 cm²
      = 36.546 L/min
        Flow in the return line on the retract stroke (cap-side discharge)
  [48] v_suc = Q_suc / (pi * d_suc^2 / 4)
      = 25.491 L/min / (π × (40 mm)^2 / 4)
      = 0.33809 m/s
        Velocity in the suction line
  [49] u_suc = v_suc / v_suc_max
      = 0.33809 m/s / 1.2 m/s
      = 0.28174
        Velocity in the suction line over its limit
  [50] d_min_suc = sqrt(4 * Q_suc / (pi * v_suc_max))
      = sqrt(4 × 25.491 L/min / (π × 1.2 m/s))
      = 21.232 mm
        Smallest suction line bore for the velocity limit
  [51] v_prs = Q_prs / (pi * d_prs^2 / 4)
      = 36.546 L/min / (π × (16 mm)^2 / 4)
      = 3.0294 m/s
        Velocity in the pressure line
  [52] u_prs = v_prs / v_prs_max
      = 3.0294 m/s / 5 m/s
      = 0.60589
        Velocity in the pressure line over its limit
  [53] d_min_prs = sqrt(4 * Q_prs / (pi * v_prs_max))
      = sqrt(4 × 36.546 L/min / (π × 5 m/s))
      = 12.454 mm
        Smallest pressure line bore for the velocity limit
  [54] v_rtn = Q_rtn / (pi * d_ret^2 / 4)
      = 36.546 L/min / (π × (25 mm)^2 / 4)
      = 1.2409 m/s
        Velocity in the return line
  [55] u_rtn = v_rtn / v_ret_max
      = 1.2409 m/s / 3 m/s
      = 0.41362
        Velocity in the return line over its limit
  [56] d_min_rtn = sqrt(4 * Q_rtn / (pi * v_ret_max))
      = sqrt(4 × 36.546 L/min / (π × 3 m/s))
      = 16.078 mm
        Smallest return line bore for the velocity limit
  [57] L_b = 2 * stroke + L_add
      = 2 × 200 mm + 100 mm
      = 500 mm
        Buckling length, estimated: the closed length (stroke + L_add) plus the stroke
  [58] L_eff = K_m * L_b
      = 1 × 500 mm
      = 500 mm
        Effective length K·L
  [59] I_r = pi * d_r^4 / 64
      = π × (22 mm)^4 / 64
      = 11499 mm⁴
        Second moment of area of the rod
  [60] r_g = d_r / 4
      = 22 mm / 4
      = 5.5 mm
        Radius of gyration of the solid rod
  [61] lam = L_eff / r_g
      = 500 mm / 5.5 mm
      = 90.909
        Slenderness ratio
  [62] lam_c = pi * sqrt(2 * E / f_y)
      = π × sqrt(2 × 210 GPa / 500 MPa)
      = 91.052
        Transition slenderness between Johnson and Euler
  [63] F_cr = A_r * f_y * (1 - f_y * lam^2 / (4 * pi^2 * E))
      = 3.8013 cm² × 500 MPa × (1 − 500 MPa × 90.909^2 / (4 × π^2 × 210 GPa))
      = 95.331 kN
        Critical load, Johnson parabola (stocky rod)
  [64] F_allow = F_cr / S_f
      = 95.331 kN / 3.5
      = 27.238 kN
        Allowable push load: critical load over the safety factor
  [65] F_stall = p_sys * A_b
      = 100 bar g × 12.566 cm²
      = 12.566 kN
        Stall thrust: the supply pressure on the bore area (no flow, so no back pressure; the friction of the seals does not relieve the rod of a stalled cylinder)
  [66] F_buck = max(F_push, F_stall)
      = max(3.0526 kN, 12.566 kN)
      = 12.566 kN
        Rod load for the buckling check: the larger of the push load entered and the stall thrust p_sys·A_b
  [67] u_buck = F_buck / F_allow
      = 12.566 kN / 27.238 kN
      = 0.46136
        Buckling utilisation
  [68] u_push = F_push / F_ext
      = 3.0526 kN / 11.938 kN
      = 0.25571
        Push load over the extend force
  [69] u_pull = F_pull / F_ret
      = 3.0526 kN / 8.3268 kN
      = 0.3666
        Pull load over the retract force
  [70] p_push = (F_push / eta_m + p_back * A_a) / A_b
      = (3.0526 kN / 0.95 + 0 bar g × 8.765 cm²) / 12.566 cm²
      = 25.571 bar
        Pressure at the cylinder the push load needs: (F_push/η_m + p_back·A_a)/A_b
