1. Scope and method
Calculates the bending moment, slope, deflection, fatigue and yield safety factors, critical speed and keys of a stepped shaft on two bearings. It takes radial loads in two planes and a steady torque with an optional alternating part.
Shaft bending moment: the shaft as an S2 beam (pin at A, roller at B) in each plane; M = √(M_y² + M_z²) Stress at a section: σ_a = K_f·32M_a·d/(π(d⁴ − d_i⁴)), τ_m = K_fs·16T_m·d/(π(d⁴ − d_i⁴)), τ_a = K_fs·16T_a·d/(π(d⁴ − d_i⁴)); K_f = 1 + q(K_t − 1) Endurance limit: S_e = k_a·k_b·k_c·k_d·k_e·k_f·S_e1, S_e1 (the rotating-beam limit S_e′) = 0.5·S_ut (S_ut ≤ 1400 MPa, else 700 MPa); k_a = a·S_ut^b, k_b = 1.24d^−0.107 or 1.51d^−0.157, k_e = 1 − 0.08·z_R Fatigue (DE-Goodman, bending fully reversed, torque T_m ± T_a): 1/n_f = √(σ_a² + 3τ_a²)/S_e + √3·τ_m/S_ut; yield n_y = S_y/√((σ_a)² + 3τ_pk²), τ_pk from the larger of T_m + T_a and the peak torque T_pk when given Critical speed: Rayleigh ω = √(g·Σm·y/Σm·y²), y signed (an overhung mass lifts the span, which lowers the quotient); Dunkerley 1/ω² = Σ m_i·δ_ii (lower bound, used for the ratio to the operating speed) Weight loads (with w_masses): F_y,i = −m_i·g at each node, m_i the carried mass plus the shaft's own mass split to the node; without it the loads table is the whole load Parallel key, on the peak torque T_k = the larger of T_m + T_a and T_pk: τ = 2T_k/(d·w·L), σ = 4T_k/(d·h·L); required length L = max(2T_k·n_target/(d·w·0.577·S_y,key), 4T_k·n_target/(d·h·S_y,min)), S_y,min the lowest of the key, the shaft and (when entered) the hub; a shoulder is checked on both sides and the lower n_f governs

3. Assumptions
  A1. The shaft is linear elastic on two simple bearing supports, a pin at A and a roller at B. (Shigley 10th ed. §7-4.)
  A2. Bending is fully reversed by rotation. (Shigley 10th ed. §7-4.)
  A3. The torque is a steady part T_m plus an alternating part T_a, acting between the torque-in and torque-out positions. (The torque is fully alternating when T_m = 0.)
  A4. The critical speed is the first lateral mode, by Rayleigh with Dunkerley as a lower bound. (Shigley 10th ed. §7-6.)
  A5. K_t and K_ts come from user records or published fit equations. (No stress-concentration chart is shipped, so the factors are entered or derived.)
  A6. At a shoulder both sides are checked, each on its own diameter and bore, and the lower factor governs. (Shigley 10th ed. §6-9, §7-4.)
  A7. The load factor k_c is 1, because the stresses are combined by von Mises. (Shigley 10th ed. §6-9, §7-4.)
  A8. The loads table is the whole load unless w_masses is on, which adds the weights. (With w_masses on, the sheet adds m·g at the nodes in the vertical plane.)
  A9. Loads, sections and steps closer together than 1/200 of the shaft length share one node. (Keeps the beam model well conditioned.)
  A10. T (Torque transmitted, steady or mean part T_m) is taken as 0 kN·m. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A11. w_masses (Add the weight of the carried masses and of the shaft itself as downward vertical loads (optional; blank = refused when a mass is carried; off: the loads table is the whole load, the weights are entered as forces and the masses enter the critical speed only)) is taken as no. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A12. R_rel (Reliability) is taken as 99 %. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A13. k_d (Temperature factor) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A14. k_f (Miscellaneous factor) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A15. n_target (Target safety factor) is taken as 1.5. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A16. r_c_min (Smallest ratio of the first critical speed to the operating speed) is taken as 2. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A17. theta_max (Slope limit at the bearings) is taken as 0.001 rad. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A18. y_max (Deflection limit at the loads) is taken as 0.13 mm. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
