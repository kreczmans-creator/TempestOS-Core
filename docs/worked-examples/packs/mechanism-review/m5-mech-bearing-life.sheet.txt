1. SCOPE AND METHOD
The basic and modified rating life of a rolling bearing under a steady load or a duty cycle, at a chosen reliability, against a required life; and the static safety factor against a required value.
Equivalent load: P = X_1·F_r + Y_1·F_a when F_a/F_r ≤ e_lim (X_1 = 1, Y_1 = 0 for a single-row radial ball bearing), else P = X·F_r + Y·F_a (Y = 0 is refused when an axial load acts above e_lim) Duty cycle: P_eq = (Σ P_i^p·U_i / Σ U_i)^(1/p) Basic rating life: L_10 = (C/P)^p million revolutions, p = 3 ball, 10/3 roller Life in hours: L_10h = 10⁶·L_10 / (60·n) Reliability factor (ISO 281:2007, derived): a_1 = 0.95·[ln(100/R)/ln(100/90)]^(2/3) + 0.05 Modified life: L_nm = a_1·a_ISO·L_10 Static safety: P_0 = max(X_0·F_r + Y_0·F_a, F_r), s_0 = C_0/P_0
  Ref: ISO 281:2007 cl. 6–7 — Dynamic equivalent load and basic rating life
  Ref: ISO 281:2007 cl. 9 — Modified rating life
  Ref: ISO 76:2006 cl. 6–7 — Static equivalent load and static safety factor
  Ref: Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design 11th ed., Ch. 11 — Rolling-contact bearings
