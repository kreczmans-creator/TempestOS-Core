1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Hole and shaft limits for a nominal size and tolerance classes, the resulting fit (clearance, transition or interference) and its extreme values, from the ISO 286-1 formulae.
i = 0.45·D^(1/3) + 0.001·D (µm) at the geometric mean D of the size step; ITn = multiplier × i; fundamental deviations by letter from the published formulae; hole deviations by the general rule (EI = −es for A–H; ES = −ei + Δ for K–ZC). Limits = nominal + deviations; fit extremes from the limits.
  Ref: ISO 286-1:2010 §4, §5 — Bases of tolerances, deviations and fits — tolerance grades and fundamental deviations
  Ref: ISO 286-2:2010 — Tables of standard tolerance classes and limit deviations (not reproduced)

2. INPUTS
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  D | Nominal size | 35 | mm | link: Shaft rev A · d_A | 1 … 500 mm
  hole_letter | Hole fundamental deviation letter | H |  | drawing: DRG-CNV-411 rev B | any
  hole_grade | Hole IT grade | 7 |  | drawing: DRG-CNV-411 rev B | 5 … 11
  shaft_letter | Shaft fundamental deviation letter | k |  | drawing: DRG-CNV-411 rev B | any
  shaft_grade | Shaft IT grade | 6 |  | drawing: DRG-CNV-411 rev B | 4 … 11

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. IT values and fundamental deviations are the unrounded results of the ISO 286-1 formulae at the geometric mean of the size step. (The tabulated values apply the standard's own rounding and may differ by about 1 µm; enter overrides from a released reference record where the tabulated value governs.)
  A2. Hole deviations follow the general rule from the shaft deviations, with Δ = ITn − IT(n−1) where the standard applies it. (Table-specific departures in the standard are not reproduced; use the overrides where they govern.)
