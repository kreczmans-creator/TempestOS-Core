1. SCOPE AND METHOD
A stepped (optionally hollow) shaft on two bearings carrying radial loads in two planes and a steady torque: bending moment, slope and deflection along it, the fatigue and yield safety factors at the sections the user marks, slopes at the bearings, deflection at the loads, the first lateral critical speed and parallel keys.
Bending: the shaft as an S2 beam (pin at A, roller at B) in each plane; M = √(M_y² + M_z²) Stress at a section: σ_a = K_f·32M_a·d/(π(d⁴ − d_i⁴)), τ_m = K_fs·16T_m·d/(π(d⁴ − d_i⁴)); K_f = 1 + q(K_t − 1) Endurance: S_e = k_a·k_b·k_c·k_d·k_e·k_f·S_e′, S_e′ = 0.5·S_ut (S_ut ≤ 1400 MPa, else 700 MPa); k_a = a·S_ut^b, k_b = 1.24d^−0.107 or 1.51d^−0.157, k_e = 1 − 0.08·z_R Fatigue (DE-Goodman): 1/n_f = σ_a/S_e + √3·τ_m/S_ut; yield n_y = S_y/√((σ_a)² + 3τ_m²) Critical speed: Rayleigh ω = √(g·Σm·y/Σm·y²); Dunkerley 1/ω² = Σ m_i·δ_ii Parallel key: τ = 2T/(d·w·L), σ = 4T/(d·h·L)
  Ref: Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design 10th ed., Ch. 6 (§6-9, Table 6-2 Marin surface factors), Ch. 7 (§7-4 to §7-7) — Fatigue, shaft stresses, deflection, critical speeds, keys
  Ref: Mott, Machine Elements in Mechanical Design 6th ed., Ch. 12 — Shaft design

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Linear elastic, two simple bearing supports (pin at A, roller at B); bending fully reversed by rotation, torque steady. (Shigley 10th ed. §7-4.)
  A2. No torsional vibration; only the first lateral mode, by Rayleigh with Dunkerley as a lower bound. (Shigley 10th ed. §7-6.)
  A3. K_t and K_ts come from user records or published fit equations; no stress-concentration chart is reproduced. (§A5.)
  A4. At a shoulder the smaller diameter governs; k_c = 1 because the stresses are combined by von Mises. (Shigley 10th ed. §6-9, §7-4.)
  A5. Loads, sections and steps closer together than 1/200 of the shaft length share one node. (Keeps the beam model well conditioned.)
  A6. rho (Density (shaft self-mass in the critical speed)) is taken as 0 kg/m^3. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A7. R (Reliability) is taken as 99 %. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A8. k_d (Temperature factor k_d) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A9. k_f (Miscellaneous factor k_f) is taken as 1. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A10. n_target (Target safety factor) is taken as 1.5. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A11. theta_max (Slope limit at the bearings) is taken as 0.001 rad. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
  A12. y_max (Deflection limit at the loads) is taken as 0.13 mm. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)
