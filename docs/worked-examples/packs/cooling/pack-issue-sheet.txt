1. SCOPE AND METHOD
The demand of a set of loads per supply (three-phase AC, single-phase AC, DC) and the minimum DC power supply, transformer and battery for it, each with a margin; the heat the loads dissipate for the enclosure calculation.
Demand per load: P_i·d_i·k_i; apparent power P_i·d_i·k_i/cosφ_i (arithmetic sum, conservative) Bus currents: I_3φ = S_3φ/(√3·U), I_1φ = S_1φ/U, I_DC = P_DC/U_DC; when a bus is chosen for the DC power supply its input P_DC/η_PSU (power factor 1) is added to that bus Heat dissipated inside the enclosure: Φ = Σ h_i·P_i·d_i·k_i, h_i = the share of the input power released inside (blank: the loss 1 − η_i; electronics 1; a machine outside the enclosure 0) PSU: P_PSU ≥ margin·P_DC/f_derate (f_derate from the manufacturer's curve at θ and altitude) Transformer: S_T ≥ margin·S for the running load; starting peak S_peak = S + max(m_i·P_i/cosφ_i − S_i) ≤ k_T·S_T (the starting load undiversified), so S_T ≥ S_peak/k_T for the start; the rating to buy is the larger of the two Battery: C = I_b·t/(DoD·η_b·k_temp·k_age), I_b = Σ backed P_i·d_i·k_i/(U_b·η_conv); Peukert: I_b·H·(t/H)^(1/k_p) in place of I_b·t
  Ref: Linden's Handbook of Batteries 5th ed., Ch. 3–4 — Battery sizing and the Peukert relation
  Ref: BS EN 60204-1:2018 cl. 4.3 — Electrical supply (supply voltage tolerance; cited by number only)

Title: Enclosure heat dissipation and fan sizing
Author: unknown    Authored: 2026-10-24

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Total heat load inside a control enclosure, the effective surface that can dissipate it for the mounting condition, the internal temperature rise with natural convection and radiation through a stated overall heat-transfer coefficient, the forced-ventilation airflow for a target rise (density corrected for altitude), the fan duty point against a fan curve and the system resistance, optional solar gain, and the cooling-unit capacity needed when the limit cannot be met.
P = Σ P_i n_i d_i (+ solar α I A_sun). A_eff = Σ f_face × A_face. Natural: ΔT = P/(h A_eff) (a plain sum of exposed face areas times one coefficient; the IEC TR 60890 tables are not used). Forced: V = P/(ρ c_p ΔT_target) with ρ = p(z)/(287.055 T) and c_p = 1006 J/kg·K; fan duty where p_fan(V) = p_sys (V/V_ref)² (bisection). Cooling unit: P_cool = P − h A_eff (T_max − T_amb) when positive.
  Ref: IEC TR 60890 — A method of temperature-rise verification of low-voltage switchgear and controlgear assemblies by calculation
  Ref: Manufacturer enclosure climate-control guides (Rittal, nVent Hoffman) — Effective surface, heat-transfer coefficient and fan-sizing practice (cited, not copied)
  Ref: BS EN 61439-1 10.10 — Verification of temperature rise

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Heat leaves the enclosure through its exposed faces at one overall coefficient h (natural convection plus radiation to surroundings at the ambient temperature); covered faces dissipate nothing. (IEC TR 60890-style effective-surface approach; h is a reference value the user may edit.)
  A2. Forced ventilation removes the full heat load with air entering at ambient and leaving at the target rise; natural dissipation is neglected for the airflow (conservative). (Manufacturer fan-sizing practice.)
  A3. The fan curve is linear between the entered points; the system resistance scales with airflow squared. (Duty-point idealisation.)
  A4. I_sun (Solar irradiance on the enclosure (0 = indoors)) is taken as 0 W/m^2. (Sheet default value; no project figure given.)

Title: Heat exchanger (LMTD and ε-NTU)
Author: unknown    Authored: 2026-10-24
Checker: J. Reviewer    Checked: 2026-10-24

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Single-phase heat exchanger with constant properties: in rating mode the duty and outlet temperatures for a given area; in sizing mode the area for a given duty. Counterflow, parallel flow, 1-shell-pass shell and tube, crossflow with both fluids unmixed (approximate), and one fluid at constant temperature.
Capacity rates: C_h = ṁ_h·c_p,h, C_c = ṁ_c·c_p,c, C_r = C_min/C_max; with one fluid at constant temperature (C_r = 0) that fluid's capacity rate is infinite, C_min is the other stream's and the constant stream leaves at its inlet temperature Overall coefficient with fouling: U_f = 1/(1/U + R_f) Transfer units: NTU = U_f·A/C_min; effectiveness ε(NTU, C_r) for the arrangement Duty: Q = ε·C_min·(T_h,in − T_c,in); outlets from the energy balance Sizing: ε = Q/(C_min·ΔT_max), NTU from the inverse relation, A = NTU·C_min/U_f Cross-check: Q = U_f·A·F·ΔT_lm, F from R and P (1 shell pass) or back-calculated
  Ref: Incropera, Fundamentals of Heat and Mass Transfer 7th ed., §11.3–11.5, Tables 11.3–11.4 — LMTD and effectiveness-NTU methods
  Ref: Çengel & Ghajar, Heat and Mass Transfer 5th ed., Ch. 11 — Heat exchangers

Friction and fitting losses through series pipe segments at a given flow, the static head, the system curve, the duty point against a pump curve entered as points, the affinity-law shift for a speed or impeller change, and the NPSH available at the pump suction.
