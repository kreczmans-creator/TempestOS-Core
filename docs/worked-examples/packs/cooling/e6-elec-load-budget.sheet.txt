1. SCOPE AND METHOD
The demand of a set of loads per supply (three-phase AC, single-phase AC, DC) and the minimum DC power supply, transformer and battery for it, each with a margin; the heat the loads dissipate for the enclosure calculation.
Demand per load: P_i·d_i·k_i; apparent power P_i·d_i·k_i/cosφ_i (arithmetic sum, conservative) Bus currents: I_3φ = S_3φ/(√3·U), I_1φ = S_1φ/U, I_DC = P_DC/U_DC Heat dissipated: Φ = Σ(1 − η_i)·P_i·d_i·k_i PSU: P_PSU ≥ margin·P_DC/f_derate (f_derate from the manufacturer's curve at θ and altitude) Transformer: S_T ≥ margin·S; starting peak S_peak = S + max(m_i·P_i/cosφ_i − S_i) ≤ k_T·S_T (the starting load undiversified) Battery: C = I_b·t/(DoD·η_b·k_temp·k_age), I_b = Σ backed P_i·d_i·k_i/(U_b·η_conv); Peukert: I_b·H·(t/H)^(1/k_p) in place of I_b·t
  Ref: Linden's Handbook of Batteries 5th ed., Ch. 3–4 — Battery sizing and the Peukert relation
  Ref: BS EN 60204-1:2018 cl. 4.3 — Electrical supply (supply voltage tolerance; cited by number only)

  A3. PSU derating, transformer overload and battery factors are the manufacturer's values, entered as records. (§A3 reference records.)
