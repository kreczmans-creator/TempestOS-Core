# Calc pack — Enclosure cooling (`pack.cooling`)

**Chain.** Panel load budget (`elec.load-budget`) → Enclosure heat (`elec.enclosure-heat`) → Cooling-water exchanger (`bs.heat-exchanger`) → Cooling-water pipework and pump (`bs.pipe-pump`).

**Purpose.** Takes the heat a control panel dissipates, finds what the enclosure cannot lose naturally, sizes the water-cooled exchanger that removes it and the pipework and pump that carry the cooling water.

The heat a drive panel dissipates is a linked load of its enclosure; the heat the enclosure cannot lose naturally is the required duty of a water-cooled exchanger; the exchanger's duty, cold-side temperature rise and mean cold-side temperature give the cooling-water pipework and pump their flow and fluid temperature. The enclosure step's own check, natural dissipation holding the limit, FAILS by design: that is the reason the exchanger is needed, and the duty it hands over is exactly the shortfall.

**Issue.** Project “Motor control centre 3”, pack “Drive panel cooling calc pack”, checked once as a bundle and issued under issue reference **`MCC-COOL-001`** to Client Co in one transaction. After issue every step is read-only and carries that reference. Checked against SPEC-MCC-03 and DRG-MCC-210/215 rev B.
