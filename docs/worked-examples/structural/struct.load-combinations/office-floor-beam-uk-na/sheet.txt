1. Scope and method
Calculates the design values of one action effect under every EN 1990 Annex A1 combination for buildings, from persistent to accidental. Each variable action leads in turn; the envelopes and governing combinations are reported.
ULS 6.10: Σ γ_G·G + γ_Q·K_FI·Q_1 + Σ γ_Q·K_FI·ψ_0·Q_i (K_FI also on γ_G,sup) ULS 6.10a: Σ γ_G·G + Σ γ_Q·K_FI·ψ_0·Q_i; 6.10b: Σ ξ·γ_G·G + γ_Q·K_FI·Q_1 + Σ γ_Q·K_FI·ψ_0·Q_i Permanent factor: γ_G,sup when the action adds to the effect sought, γ_G,inf when it opposes it Variable actions: included only when they add to the effect sought (otherwise zero) SLS characteristic 6.14b: Σ G + Q_1 + Σ ψ_0·Q_i; frequent 6.15b: Σ G + ψ_1·Q_1 + Σ ψ_2·Q_i; quasi-permanent 6.16b: Σ G + Σ ψ_2·Q_i Accidental 6.11b: Σ G + A_d + ψ_1·Q_1 + Σ ψ_2·Q_i

3. Assumptions
  A1. Each action's characteristic value is its signed contribution to one scalar effect. (Linear superposition (EN 1990 cl. 6.3.1 representative values).)
  A2. The combinations are those of EN 1990 Annex A1 for buildings. (EN 1990 Annex A1 scope.)
  A3. Bridge, fatigue and seismic combinations are not included. (EN 1990 Annex A1 scope.)
  A4. Without a ψ record the recommended values of EN 1990 Annex A1 are used and labelled on the sheet. (No National Annex table is shipped.)
  A5. UK National Annex values of ψ and γ come from a user record. (No National Annex table is shipped.)
  A6. The factor set says whether the factors are the EN recommended values or come from a National Annex record. (No National Annex value is shipped; a record carries its source, edition and table or clause, and the sheet prints them.)
  A7. Each permanent action takes γ_G,sup when it adds to the effect sought and γ_G,inf when it opposes it. (EN 1990 Table A1.2(B) note on favourable and unfavourable permanent actions.)
  A8. For an equilibrium check with one source, the source's permanent actions are entered as one row. (EN 1990 Table A1.2(B) note on favourable and unfavourable permanent actions.)
  A9. unit (Unit of the effect) is taken as kN/m. (Line load on the beam.)
  A10. factor_set (Factor set: the EN 1990 recommended values as entered, or a National Annex record applied to the partial factors and the ψ table) is taken as EN recommended. (The UK National Annex partial factors are entered by hand above, each cited to its table, so no National Annex record is applied on top of them.)
  A11. K_FI (Consequence-class factor) is taken as 1. (Consequence class CC2.)
  A12. ψ factors: EN 1990 Annex A1 recommended values (Table A1.1). (No ψ record entered.)
