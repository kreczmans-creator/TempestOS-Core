1. Scope and method
Calculates area, centroid, second moments, moduli, radii of gyration, and torsion and warping constants of a parametric, polygon or composite section. The outline has x horizontal and y vertical; properties use EN 1993 axes, y-y horizontal and z-z vertical.
Area and centroid: A = ½∮(x dy − y dx); x_c = ∫x dA / A; y_c = ∫y dA / A. Second moment about y-y: I_y = ∫y² dA − A y_c², by Green's theorem and the parallel-axis theorem. Second moment about z-z: I_z = ∫x² dA − A x_c²; product I_yz = ∫xy dA − A x_c y_c. Principal axes: tan 2θ = −2 I_yz / (I_y − I_z). Elastic section modulus: W_el_y = I_y / y_max. Plastic section modulus about the equal-area axis, found by bisection: W_pl_y = |Q_above| + |Q_below|. Torsion constant J: Saint-Venant series (solid rectangle), πd⁴/32 (circle), Bredt (closed thin-walled), Σbt³/3 (open). Warping constant of an I-section: I_w = I_z (h − t_f)²/4.

3. Assumptions
  A1. A polygon outline does not cross itself. (Green's theorem integrates the signed loops as given.)
  A2. Holes lie inside the outline and do not overlap one another. (Green's theorem integrates the signed loops as given.)
  A3. Rolled-section root fillets are quarter circles of radius r and flange tips are square. (Published section tables use the same idealisation.)
  A4. Hollow-section corners are quarter circles of radius 1.5 t_w outside and 1.0 t_w inside by default. (BS EN 10210-2 hot-finished idealisation.)
  A5. Thin-walled torsion constants neglect the junction and tip effects except the fillet term kept for rolled I-sections. (Open sections: Σbt³/3; closed: Bredt.)

  [14] I_2 = (I_y + I_z)/2 - sqrt(((I_y - I_z)/2)^2 + I_yz^2)

  [15] theta = ½ atan2(−2 I_yz, I_y − I_z)
      = ½ atan2(0, 43381)
      = 0 deg

  [16] y_top = y_max − y_c
      = Green's theorem over 108 vertices in 1 loop(s)
      = 233.6 mm
        Extreme fibre above the centroid
  [17] y_bot = y_c − y_min
      = Green's theorem over 108 vertices in 1 loop(s)
      = 233.6 mm

  [18] x_ext = max(x_max − x_c, x_c − x_min)
      = Green's theorem over 108 vertices in 1 loop(s)
      = 96.4 mm

  [19] W_el_y = I_y / max(y_top, y_bot)
      = 45728 cm⁴ / max(233.6 mm, 233.6 mm)
      = 1957.5 cm³
        Elastic section modulus about the y-y axis, governing fibre
  [20] W_el_z = I_z / x_ext
      = 2347 cm⁴ / 96.4 mm
      = 243.47 cm³

      = 2232.5 cm³

  [22] W_pl_z = |Q_right| + |Q_left| about the equal-area axis
      = equal-area axis at x = 0 mm
      = 378.9 cm³
        Plastic section modulus about the z-z axis
  [23] r_g_y = sqrt(I_y / A)
      = sqrt(45728 cm⁴ / 125.27 cm²)
      = 191.06 mm
        Radius of gyration about the y-y axis
  [24] r_g_z = sqrt(I_z / A)
      = sqrt(2347 cm⁴ / 125.27 cm²)
      = 43.286 mm
        Radius of gyration about the z-z axis
  [25] alpha_pl_y = W_pl_y / W_el_y
      = 2232.5 cm³ / 1957.5 cm³
