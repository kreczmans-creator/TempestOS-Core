1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Area, centroid, second moments of area (Ixx, Iyy, Ixy), principal axes and values, elastic and plastic section moduli, radii of gyration, torsion constant and (I-sections) warping constant for a parametric shape, an arbitrary polygon with holes, or a composite of placed parts. The x axis is horizontal (major axis of an I-section), y vertical.
Outline loops are integrated by Green's theorem: A = ½∮(x dy − y dx), Ixx = ∫y² dA, Iyy = ∫x² dA, Ixy = ∫xy dA, transferred to the centroid by the parallel-axis theorem. Principal: tan 2θ = −2Ixy/(Ixx − Iyy). Elastic modulus Ixx/y_max; plastic modulus from the equal-area axis found by bisection, Wpl = |Q_above| + |Q_below|. Torsion: Saint-Venant series for a solid rectangle, πd⁴/32 for circles, Bredt for closed thin-walled, Σbt³/3 (+ fillet term for rolled I) for open sections. Warping (I): Iw = Iyy (h − t_f)²/4.
  Ref: Roark's Formulas for Stress and Strain Appendix A, Table A.1; Ch. 10, Table 10.1 — Properties of sections; torsion of solid and thin-walled sections
  Ref: AISC Design Guide 9 (Seaburg & Carter) Appendix — Torsional properties of rolled I-shapes
  Ref: BS EN 10365 — Hot rolled steel channels, I and H sections: dimensions and masses
  Ref: BS EN 10210-2 — Hot finished structural hollow sections: tolerances, dimensions and sectional properties

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. A polygon outline is simple (no self-intersection); holes lie inside the outline and do not overlap. (Green's theorem integrates the signed loops as given.)
  A2. Rolled-section root fillets are quarter circles of radius r; flange tips are square; hollow-section corners are quarter circles of outer radius r_o (default 1.5 t) and inner radius r_i (default 1.0 t). (Published section tables use the same idealisation.)
  A3. Thin-walled torsion constants neglect the junction and tip effects except the fillet term kept for rolled I-sections. (Open sections: Σbt³/3; closed: Bredt.)
