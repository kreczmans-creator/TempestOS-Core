1. Scope and method
Calculates reactions, maximum moment and shear, and maximum deflection of a single-span beam for the standard support and load cases. Left end at x = 0; loads downward; sagging moments and downward deflections positive.
Formula source: Roark Table 8.1, in this sheet's own notation, for the chosen support and load case. Simply supported beam, point load at a (b = L − a), reaction and largest moment: R_A = F b / L; M_max = F a b / L. Simply supported beam, point load at a, largest deflection when a ≥ b: δ_max = F b (L² − b²)^(3/2) / (9√3 L EI) at x = √((L² − b²)/3). Propped cantilever, uniform load, reaction and moments: R_B = 3wL/8; M_A = −wL²/8; sagging M_max = 9wL²/128 at x = 5L/8.

2. Inputs
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  case | Case | simply supported, point load at a |  | assumption: Single span, pin–roller. | any
  L | Span | 6 | m | drawing: DRG-FL-201 rev A | 0.1 … 100 m
  I | Second moment of area | 8503 | cm⁴ | reference: UB 305×165×40 (Blue Book) | 1 … 100000000 cm⁴
  E | Young's modulus | 210 | GPa | spec: BS EN 1993-1-1 3.2.6 | 1 … 500 GPa
  F | Point load, downward (optional; blank = not used by the distributed-load and couple cases) | 50 | kN | spec: Load schedule LS-01: plant load | 0 … 100000 kN
  a | Load position from the left end (optional; blank = not used by the distributed-load cases) | 4 | m | drawing: DRG-FL-201 rev A | 0 … 100 m

3. Assumptions
  A1. The beam is prismatic, linear elastic and Euler–Bernoulli, with small deflections. (Roark Table 8.1 conditions.)
  A2. A cantilever is fixed at the left end (x = 0) and free at the right. (Case convention.)
  A3. case (Case) is taken as simply supported, point load at a. (Single span, pin–roller.)

4. Working
  [5] EI = E * I
      = 210 GPa × 8503 cm⁴
      = 17856 kN·m²
        Flexural rigidity
  [8] b = L - a
      = 6 m − 4 m
      = 2 m
        Distance from the load to the right end
  [9] R_A = F * b / L    « Roark 8.1 2a
      = 50 kN × 2 m / 6 m
      = 16.667 kN
        Left reaction
  [10] R_B = F * a / L
      = 50 kN × 4 m / 6 m
      = 33.333 kN
        Right reaction
  [11] M_max = F * a * b / L
      = 50 kN × 4 m × 2 m / 6 m
      = 66.667 kN·m
        Maximum moment, under the load
  [12] x_M = a
      = 4 m
      = 4 m
        Position of the maximum moment
  [13] M_min = 0 kN*m
      = 0 kN·m
        Minimum moment
  [14] V_max = max(R_A, R_B)
      = max(16.667 kN, 33.333 kN)
      = 33.333 kN
        Maximum shear
  [15] c_far = b
      = 2 m
      = 2 m
        Shorter segment (the deflection formula's b)
  [16] x_delta = sqrt((L^2 - c_far^2) / 3)
      = sqrt(((6 m)^2 − (2 m)^2) / 3)
      = 3.266 m
        Position of the maximum deflection
  [17] delta_max = F * c_far * sqrt((L^2 - c_far^2)^3) / (9 * sqrt(3) * L * EI)
      = 50 kN × 2 m × sqrt(((6 m)^2 − (2 m)^2)^3) / (9 × sqrt(3) × 6 m × 17856 kN·m²)
      = 10.839 mm
        Maximum deflection

5. Results
  R_A = 16.67 kN    Left reaction
  R_B = 33.33 kN    Right reaction
  M_max = 66.67 kN·m    Maximum sagging moment
  M_min = 0 kN·m    Minimum (hogging) moment
  V_max = 33.33 kN    Maximum shear
  delta_max = 10.84 mm    Maximum deflection
  x_M = 4 m    Position of M_max
  x_delta = 3.266 m    Position of δ_max
