1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Closed-form reactions, maximum moment and its position, maximum shear and maximum deflection for the standard single-span beam cases, as a quick check. Left end at x = 0; loads downward; sagging moments positive; deflection downward positive.
Formulas from Roark Table 8.1, in this sheet's own notation, for the chosen support and load case. Simply supported beam, point load at a (b = L − a), reaction and largest moment: R_A = Pb/L; M_max = Pab/L. Simply supported beam, point load at a, largest deflection when a ≥ b: δ_max = Pb(L² − b²)^(3/2)/(9√3 L EI) at x = √((L² − b²)/3). Propped cantilever, uniform load, reaction and moments: R_B = 3wL/8; M_A = −wL²/8; sagging M_max = 9wL²/128 at x = 5L/8.
  Ref: Roark's Formulas for Stress and Strain Ch. 8, Table 8.1 — Shear, moment, slope and deflection formulas for elastic straight beams

2. INPUTS
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  case | Case | simply supported, point load at a |  | assumption: Single span, pin–roller. | any
  L | Span | 6 | m | drawing: DRG-FL-201 rev A | 0.1 … 100 m
  E | Modulus of elasticity | 210 | GPa | spec: BS EN 1993-1-1 3.2.6 | 1 … 500 GPa
  I | Second moment of area | 8503 | cm^4 | reference: UB 305×165×40 (Blue Book) | 1 … 100000000 cm^4
  P | Point load (downward) | 50 | kN | spec: Load schedule LS-01: plant load | 0 … 100000 kN
  a | Load position from the left end | 4 | m | drawing: DRG-FL-201 rev A | 0 … 100 m

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Prismatic, linear elastic, Euler–Bernoulli beam; small deflections. (Roark Table 8.1 conditions.)
  A2. A cantilever is fixed at the left end (x = 0) and free at the right. (Case convention.)
  A3. case (Case) is taken as simply supported, point load at a. (Single span, pin–roller.)

4. WORKING
  [5] EI = E * I
      = 210 GPa × 8503 cm⁴
      = 17856 kN·m²
        Flexural rigidity
  [8] b = L - a
      = 6 m − 4 m
      = 2 m
        Distance from the load to the right end
  [9] R_A = P * b / L    « Roark 8.1 2a
      = 50 kN × 2 m / 6 m
      = 16.667 kN
        Left reaction
  [10] R_B = P * a / L
      = 50 kN × 4 m / 6 m
      = 33.333 kN
        Right reaction
  [11] M_max = P * a * b / L
      = 50 kN × 4 m × 2 m / 6 m
      = 66.667 kN·m
        Maximum moment, under the load
  [12] x_M = a
      = 4 m
      = 4 m
        Position of the maximum moment
  [13] M_min = 0 kN*m
      = 0 kN·m
        Minimum moment
  [14] V_max = max(R_A, R_B)
      = max(16.667 kN, 33.333 kN)
      = 33.333 kN
        Maximum shear
  [15] c_far = b
      = 2 m
      = 2 m
        Shorter segment (the deflection formula's b)
  [16] x_delta = sqrt((L^2 - c_far^2) / 3)
      = sqrt(((6 m)^2 − (2 m)^2) / 3)
      = 3.266 m
        Position of the maximum deflection
  [17] delta_max = P * c_far * sqrt((L^2 - c_far^2)^3) / (9 * sqrt(3) * L * EI)
      = 50 kN × 2 m × sqrt(((6 m)^2 − (2 m)^2)^3) / (9 × sqrt(3) × 6 m × 17856 kN·m²)
      = 10.839 mm
        Maximum deflection
