1. SCOPE AND METHOD
This is a design calculation, not a thorough examination. Pin-loaded lifting lugs and the lug loads of spreader or lifting beams, checked by the BTH-1 allowable-stress approach (clause 3-3.3), by the EN 13155 proof route (2× the rated load without permanent set, 3× without release) or both: sling force from the rated load, sling angle and out-of-plane angle; tension beyond the hole, net-section fracture, shear-out on two planes, pin bearing and dishing; capacity and utilisation per mode, the governing mode, the proof load and a marking and certification checklist. Scope notes (LOLER and PUWER): this sheet does not replace the thorough examination and inspection of lifting equipment by a competent person (LOLER reg 9), the marking, test and certification that the regulations and the standards require, the planning of the lifting operation, the weld, bolt and beam checks of the linked sheets, or the pin, shackle and sling ratings. It is available only while the lifting feature is enabled; its issue needs a competent-person attestation and a second independent check.
Sling force: F_v = WLL/n_eff (n_eff = n_lugs, at most 3); θ_d = θ (+6° for a horizontal beam); β_d = β (+6° for a horizontal beam); F_s = F_v/cos θ_d; in-plane F_ip = F_s·cos β_d; out-of-plane F_op = F_s·sin β_d; horizontal N_beam = F_v·tan θ_d; M_op = F_op·h Geometry: b_e,eff = min(b_e, R − D_h/2); a_eff = a (default R − D_h/2); A_t = 2·t·b_e,eff; A_net = t·(2R − D_h); φ = 55·D_p/D_h (degrees); A_v = 2·t·[a_eff + (D_h/2)(1 − cos φ)]; A_b = D_p·t Pin clearance: C_r = 1 − 0.275·√(1 − D_p²/D_h²) BTH-1 (3-3.3): F_y,a = f_y/n_y; F_u,a = f_u/n_u; design load D = N_d·k_svc·F_s; P_t = C_r·F_y,a·A_t; P_sp = C_r·F_u,a·A_net; P_v = (F_y,a/√3)·A_v; P_b = k_b·F_y,a·A_b; u = D/P for each; dishing D_h/t ≤ dish_max EN 13155 (2× elastic, 3× no release): P_EN = min(Y/2, U/3) for each section, Y with f_y and U with f_u (shear with f/√3); u = F_s/P_EN Capacity: WLL_cap = WLL/u_lug for the governing section of each mode (smaller of the modes used); beam: u_beam from N_beam on A_beam; proof load = k_proof·WLL
  Ref: ASME BTH-1 3-3.3 — Pin-connected plates (design of lifting lugs)
  Ref: BS EN 13155 design requirements (clause to be confirmed) — Non-fixed load lifting attachments: 2× elastic, 3× no release
  Ref: BS EN 13001-3-1 5 — Proof of static strength: the calculation route cited, not implemented
  Ref: LOLER 1998 reg 7, reg 9 — Marking of lifting equipment; thorough examination and inspection
  Ref: PUWER 1998 reg 4, reg 5 — Suitability and maintenance of work equipment

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. A design calculation, not a thorough examination: it checks the lug plate at the pin hole against the user's chosen criteria and nothing else. (LOLER reg 9 and PUWER duties are the owner's and the competent person's.)
  A2. Nominal-stress screening of four sections (tension beyond the hole, net section across the hole, shear-out, bearing) on a plate loaded through a pin; no stress-concentration factor, no fatigue (the cycle count is limited to 16,000), no weld, bolt or beam check, which are made on the linked sheets. (The notch-stress route of EN 13001-3-1 is cited, not implemented.)
  A3. The load on the hole is the resultant sling force F_s whatever the out-of-plane angle, which is conservative; the out-of-plane component and its moment are outputs for the weld and base checks. (F_op = F_s·sin β_d.)
  A4. Equal sharing between the lugs, with a fourth lug not relied on (three share). The share of a statically indeterminate set is not computed. (Conservative convention; replace with the actual share if it is known.)
  A5. A horizontal beam adds the automatic tilt case: sling angle and out-of-plane angle each increased by 6° together, which dominates the untilted case. (Addendum B §B6 S8.)
  A6. The design factor, service-class factor, allowable-stress divisors, bearing multiple, dishing limit and proof-load factor are the user's records from their own licensed copy of the code; none is shipped and none has a default. (ASME BTH-1 and EN 13155 text and tables are not shipped.)
  A7. The equations are written out in the spec document in the author's notation; the owner compares them with BTH-1 (2023 or later) and EN 13155+A1 before the feature is ever enabled. (Owner test pack, S8 review checklist.)
  A8. mode (Design mode) is taken as both. (Both modes are run so the sheet shows which governs; the owner's code route decides which counts.)
  A9. beta (Out-of-plane angle of the sling) is taken as 2 deg. (Allowance for the sling not hanging exactly in the plane of the lug.)
  A10. Nd (Design factor N_d of the Design Category (BTH-1 mode; user record)) is taken as 1.8. (Illustrative user-record value for this worked example, not a value from any standard: replace with the design factor of your Design Category from your own copy of the code.)
  A11. n_y (Divisor n_y on f_y for the allowable stress, F_y,a = f_y/n_y (BTH-1 mode; user record)) is taken as 1.6. (Illustrative user-record value, not from a standard: replace with your own record.)
  A12. n_u (Divisor n_u on f_u for the allowable stress, F_u,a = f_u/n_u (BTH-1 mode; user record)) is taken as 2.2. (Illustrative user-record value, not from a standard: replace with your own record.)
  A13. k_b (Bearing multiple k_b of the allowable yield stress on the projected pin area (BTH-1 mode; user record)) is taken as 1.2. (Illustrative user-record value, not from a standard: replace with your own record.)
  A14. dish_max (Dishing-ratio limit on D_h/t (blank: the dishing check is not made; user record)) is taken as 40. (Illustrative user-record value, not from a standard: replace with your own record.)
  A15. k_proof (Proof-load factor on the rated load (blank: no proof load given; user record)) is taken as 1.5. (Illustrative user-record value, not from a standard: replace with the proof-load factor of your procedure.)
