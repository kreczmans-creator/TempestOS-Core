1. Scope and method
Resistance checks of a steel member of class 1, 2 or 3 cross-section: tension (6.2.3), compression (6.2.4) and flexural buckling about y and z (6.3.1), bending about y and z (6.2.5), shear with the high-shear reduction (6.2.6, 6.2.8), the linear cross-section interaction (6.2.1(7)) and a simplified member interaction (6.3.3 with user-entered k factors). Lateral-torsional buckling and class 4 sections are outside this sheet.
Axial plastic resistance: N_pl,Rd = A f_y/γ_M0. Bending resistance, plastic modulus for class 1–2 and elastic for class 3: M_c,Rd = W f_y/γ_M0. Shear resistance: V_pl,Rd = A_v f_y/(√3 γ_M0). Bending resistance reduced by high shear, when V_Ed > 0.5 V_pl,Rd: ρ = (2V_Ed/V_pl,Rd − 1)². Class 1–2 I-section with h, t_w, t_f entered: M_y,V,Rd = (W_pl,y − ρ A_w²/(4 t_w)) f_y/γ_M0, A_w = (h − 2t_f) t_w, and M_z,c,Rd is not reduced; otherwise the whole resistance × (1 − ρ). Elastic critical load and non-dimensional slenderness: N_cr = π²EI/L_cr²; λ̄ = √(A f_y/N_cr). Buckling reduction factor and buckling resistance: Φ = 0.5[1 + α(λ̄ − 0.2) + λ̄²]; χ = 1/(Φ + √(Φ² − λ̄²)) ≤ 1; N_b,Rd = χ A f_y/γ_M1. Cross-section check, linear interaction: N_Ed/N_Rd + M_y,Ed/M_y,Rd + M_z,Ed/M_z,Rd ≤ 1. Member check, axial force and bending with buckling (6.61, 6.62): N_Ed/N_b,Rd,min + k_yy M_y,Ed/(W_y f_y/γ_M1) + k_zz M_z,Ed/(W_z f_y/γ_M1) ≤ 1, the characteristic bending resistance M_Rk over γ_M1 without the shear reduction.

3. Assumptions
  A1. The cross-section class is as stated by the user (1, 2 or 3); class 4 effective properties are not derived here. (6.2.2.5 effective widths are outside this sheet.)
  A2. Lateral-torsional buckling is not checked: the compression flange is assumed restrained, or LTB is checked elsewhere. (6.3.2 not implemented.)
  A3. The member interaction uses the user's k_yy and k_zz (default 1.0) with the smaller flexural-buckling reduction on the axial term; the Annex A/B interaction factors are not derived. (6.3.3 simplified.)
  A4. Imperfection factors per curve are the textbook values and may be overridden; partial factors default to 1.0 (UK National Annex) and may be overridden. (User-editable with provenance.)
  A5. section_class (Cross-section class) is taken as 1. (UC 203×203×46 S355 is class 1 in compression (Blue Book).)
  A6. L_cr_y (Buckling length about y-y) is taken as 4 m. (Pinned both ends: L_cr = L = 4 m.)
  A7. L_cr_z (Buckling length about z-z) is taken as 4 m. (Pinned both ends: L_cr = L = 4 m.)
  A8. k_yy (Interaction factor k_yy (member check)) is taken as 1. (Simplified interaction: k_yy = 1.0.)
  A9. k_zz (Interaction factor k_zz (member check)) is taken as 1. (Simplified interaction: k_zz = 1.0.)
