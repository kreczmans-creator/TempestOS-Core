1. Scope and method
Calculates the resistance of a class 1, 2 or 3 steel member to tension, compression, buckling, bending, shear and their interaction, to EN 1993-1-1. Tension is positive; class 4 sections and lateral-torsional buckling are outside this sheet.
Axial plastic resistance: N_pl,Rd = A f_y/γ_M0. Bending resistance, plastic modulus for class 1–2 and elastic for class 3: M_c,Rd = W f_y/γ_M0. Shear resistance: V_pl,Rd = A_v f_y/(√3 γ_M0). Bending resistance reduced by high shear, when V_Ed > 0.5 V_pl,Rd: ρ = (2V_Ed/V_pl,Rd − 1)². Class 1–2 I-section with h, t_w, t_f entered: M_y,V,Rd = (W_pl,y − ρ A_w²/(4 t_w)) f_y/γ_M0, A_w = (h − 2t_f) t_w, and M_z,c,Rd is not reduced; otherwise the whole resistance × (1 − ρ). Elastic critical load and non-dimensional slenderness: N_cr = π²EI/L_cr²; λ̄ = √(A f_y/N_cr). Buckling reduction factor and buckling resistance: Φ = 0.5[1 + α(λ̄ − 0.2) + λ̄²]; χ = 1/(Φ + √(Φ² − λ̄²)) ≤ 1; N_b,Rd = χ A f_y/γ_M1. Cross-section check, linear interaction: N_Ed/N_Rd + M_y,Ed/M_y,Rd + M_z,Ed/M_z,Rd ≤ 1. Member check, axial force and bending with buckling (6.61, 6.62): N_Ed/N_b,Rd,min + k_yy M_y,Ed/(W_y f_y/γ_M1) + k_zz M_z,Ed/(W_z f_y/γ_M1) ≤ 1, the characteristic bending resistance M_Rk over γ_M1 without the shear reduction.

3. Assumptions
  A1. The cross-section class is the class the user states, 1, 2 or 3. (Class 4 effective properties are not derived here.)
  A2. The compression flange is restrained against lateral-torsional buckling, as the input ltb says. (Lateral-torsional buckling of a member that is not restrained is checked on the LTB interaction sheet.)
  A3. A k left at 1.0 is replaced by the Annex A or B upper bound C_m(1 + 0.8n). (Applies under compression with a moment; the smaller flexural-buckling reduction is used on the axial term and the factors are not derived.)
  A4. Imperfection factors are the textbook values for the buckling curve unless the user overrides them. (Gardner and Nethercot, Designers' Guide to EN 1993-1-1.)
  A5. Partial factors are 1.0 as in the UK National Annex unless the user overrides them. (UK National Annex to BS EN 1993-1-1.)
  A6. section_class (Cross-section class) is taken as 1. (UB 305×165×40 S275 is class 1 in bending (Blue Book).)
  A7. ltb (Compression flange against lateral-torsional buckling (optional; blank = blocks when M_y_Ed is above 0)) is taken as Restrained. (The deck restrains the compression flange (at 1.5 m, as L_cr_z), so lateral-torsional buckling does not govern; 6.3.2 is outside this sheet.)
  A8. L_cr_y (Buckling length about y-y) is taken as 6 m. (Span 6 m.)
  A9. L_cr_z (Buckling length about z-z) is taken as 1.5 m. (Compression flange restrained at 1.5 m by the deck.)
  A10. k_yy (Interaction factor for bending about y-y in the member check) is taken as 1. (No axial force; interaction factors irrelevant.)
  A11. k_zz (Interaction factor for bending about z-z in the member check) is taken as 1. (No axial force; interaction factors irrelevant.)
  A12. The compression flange is restrained against lateral-torsional buckling, as the input ltb says. (Input ltb.)
