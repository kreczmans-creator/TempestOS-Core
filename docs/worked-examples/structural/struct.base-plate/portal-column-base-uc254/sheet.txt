1. SCOPE AND METHOD
An unstiffened rectangular base plate under an I/H, RHS or CHS column on grouted concrete: bearing resistance and the plate thickness it needs, the tension T-stub and holding-down bolts and the compression flange T-stub for a major-axis moment, and shear by friction and bolts. The bolt tension per bolt goes to Hilti PROFIS for the anchorage, which is done outside this sheet.
Bearing (cl. 6.2.5): f_cd = α_cc·f_ck/γ_c; f_jd = β_j·α·f_cd, β_j = 2/3; c = t_p·√[f_y/(3·f_jd·γ_M0)] Axial: N_j,Rd = f_jd·A_eff, A_eff the bearing strips of width c around the column footprint (full rectangle when they overlap); u_N = N_c,Ed/N_j,Rd; t_req the smallest t_p in 10–100 mm with N_j,Rd ≥ N_c,Ed Moment (cl. 6.2.8.3, simplified): C_Ed = (M_Ed + N_Ed·z_T)/z, T_Ed = max(C_Ed − N_Ed, 0) = max((M_Ed − N_Ed·z_C)/z, 0); symmetric z_C = z_T = z/2: M_Ed/z ± N_Ed/2; F_t,Ed = T_Ed/n_t; C_Rd = f_jd·A_flange Plate T-stub (cl. 6.2.6.11, 6.2.4, Table 6.2): M_pl,Rd = 0.25·l_eff·t_p²·f_y/γ_M0; mode 1: 4·M_pl,Rd/m; mode 2: (2·M_pl,Rd + n·ΣF_t,Rd)/(m + n), n = min(e_min, 1.25m); mode 3: ΣF_t,Rd = n_t·F_t,Rd; without prying: mode 1-2 = 2·M_pl,Rd/m Shear (cl. 6.2.2): V_Rd = C_f,d·N_c,Ed + n_b·F_vb,Rd, N_c,Ed = max(N_Ed, 0); u_V = V_Ed/V_Rd
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.2 — Design resistance of column bases in shear
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.4, Table 6.2 — Equivalent T-stub in tension
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.5 — Equivalent T-stub in compression
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.6.11 — Base plate in bending and anchor bolts in tension
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.8.3 — Column bases subject to axial force and bending moment
  Ref: EN 1992-1-1:2004 cl. 3.1.6, 6.7 — Design strength of concrete; partial area loading

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Static loading; a major-axis moment only (uniaxial); an unstiffened rectangular plate on grout of adequate strength and thickness for β_j = 2/3. (Scope of S6.)
  A2. The simplified T-stub model: the compression resultant acts at the centre of the compression flange T-stub and the tension at the bolt row; the plate is checked as a tension T-stub (modes 1, 2 and 3) at the bolts and the compression flange T-stub is checked against f_jd. The web is not given credit in the moment check. (EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.6.11.)
  A3. The effective length l_eff is the one the user reads from the cl. 6.2.6.5 patterns; it is used for modes 1 and 2 alike (l_eff,1 ≤ l_eff,2, so this is conservative for mode 2). (One input.)
  A4. Concrete, anchor tension, pull-out, breakout, edge and shear-on-anchor are designed in Hilti PROFIS from the bolt tension per bolt that this sheet outputs; the report is filed as Evidence and referenced. (External anchorage design.)
  A5. The friction resistance counts only the compression, C_f,d·max(N_Ed, 0); a column in net tension has none. Bolts are assumed to share shear equally. (EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.2.)
  A6. The bearing outstand c is cut back where it would pass the plate edge; the web and the column interior keep the full c. The CHS compression side is taken as half of the annular bearing area (an approximation). (Conservative against an uncut c.)
  A7. Partial factors and the friction coefficient come from the National Annex records in use; the values shown default to the EN recommended and UK values. (§A5 small factor set.)
  A8. alpha (α, concentration factor √(A_c1/A_c0) of the foundation) is taken as 1.5. (Conservative: a 1.2 m square pad under the 0.45 × 0.40 m plate gives A_c1/A_c0 well above 2.25; checked on drawing PF-FN-001.)
  A9. z_C (Compression resultant distance from the column axis (blank: z/2, symmetric)) is taken as 121.5 mm. (Compression resultant at the centre of the compression flange: (h − t_f)/2 = 121.5 mm.)
  A10. m (Bolt to flange-weld toe distance, tension T-stub) is taken as 43.5 mm. (Bolt at 185 mm from the axis, flange face at 130.15 mm, less 0.8·√2 × 10 mm fillet leg.)
  A11. l_eff (Effective length of the tension T-stub (cl. 6.2.6.5 patterns)) is taken as 350 mm. (Two bolts in the row, about 175 mm each from the cl. 6.2.6.5 patterns (engineer's estimate).)
  A12. prying (Prying forces develop (bolts short enough, L_b ≤ L_b*)) is taken as yes. (Short holding-down bolts; prying forces assumed to develop.)

4. WORKING
  [29] f_cd = alpha_cc * f_ck / gamma_c    « EN 1992-1-1 cl. 3.1.6
      = 0.85 × 30 MPa / 1.5
      = 17 MPa
        Concrete design compressive strength
  [30] beta_j = β_j, fixed    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5
      = grouted joint
      = 0.66667
        Joint coefficient β_j = 2/3 (grout conditions assumed met)
  [31] f_jd = beta_j * alpha * f_cd    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5
      = 0.66667 × 1.5 × 17 MPa
      = 17 MPa
        Design bearing strength of the joint
  [32] c = t_p * sqrt(f_y / (3 * f_jd * gamma_M0))    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5
      = 25 mm × sqrt(265 MPa / (3 × 17 MPa × 1))
      = 56.987 mm
        Additional bearing width round the column footprint
  [33] A_eff = 2(b + 2c)(t_f + 2c) + (h − 2t_f − 2c)(t_w + 2c)    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5
      = 2 × 370.27 × 131.27 + 111.73 × 124.27 mm
      = 111100 mm²
        Effective bearing area
  [34] N_j_Rd = f_jd * A_eff    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5
      = 17 MPa × 111100 mm²
      = 1888.7 kN
        Axial bearing resistance of the joint
  [35] N_c_Ed = max(N_Ed, 0)
      = N_Ed = 210 kN
      = 210 kN
        Design compression in the column
  [36] u_N = N_c_Ed / N_j_Rd
      = 210 kN / 1888.7 kN
      = 0.11119
        Axial bearing utilisation
  [37] t_req = smallest t_p in 10–100 mm with f_jd·A_eff(c(t_p)) ≥ N_c,Ed (bisection)    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5
      = N_c,Ed = 210 kN; carried at the 10 mm minimum
      = 10 mm
        Plate thickness required for bearing
  [38] z_T = z − z_C
      = 306.5 − 121.5
      = 185 mm
        Tension bolt row distance from the column axis
  [39] C_Ed = (M_Ed + N_Ed·z_T)/z    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.8.3
      = (95 kN·m + 210 kN × 185 mm)/306.5 mm
      = 436.7 kN
        Compression resultant under the compression flange
  [40] T_Ed = max(C_Ed − N_Ed, 0) = max((M_Ed − N_Ed·z_C)/z, 0)    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.8.3
      = max(436.7 − 210, 0)
      = 226.7 kN
        Tension resultant at the bolt row
  [41] F_t_Ed = T_Ed / n_t    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.6.11
      = 226.7 kN / 2
      = 113.35 kN
        Holding-down bolt tension per bolt (→ PROFIS)
  [42] A_flange = (b + 2c)(t_f + 2c) for the compression flange; RHS (b + 2c)(t + 2c); CHS half of A_eff    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.6.11
      = from the footprint, c = 56.987 mm
      = 48608 mm²
        Bearing area of the compression flange T-stub
  [43] C_Rd = f_jd * A_flange    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.6.11
      = 17 MPa × 48608 mm²
      = 826.33 kN
        Compression flange T-stub bearing resistance
  [44] C_Ed_pos = max(C_Ed, 0)
      = C_Ed = 436.7 kN
      = 436.7 kN
        Compression resultant carried by the flange
  [45] u_C = C_Ed_pos / C_Rd
      = 436.7 kN / 826.33 kN
      = 0.52849
        Compression flange T-stub utilisation
  [46] M_pl_Rd = 0.25 * l_eff * t_p^2 * f_y / gamma_M0    « EN 1993-1-8 Table 6.2
      = 0.25 × 350 mm × (25 mm)^2 × 265 MPa / 1
      = 14.492 kN·m
        Plastic moment resistance of the plate over l_eff
  [47] T_Rd_1 = 4 * M_pl_Rd / m    « EN 1993-1-8 Table 6.2
      = 4 × 14.492 kN·m / 43.5 mm
      = 1332.6 kN
        Mode 1: plate yields, no bolt failure
  [48] n_pry = min(e_min, 1.25·m)    « EN 1993-1-8 Table 6.2
      = min(40, 1.25 × 43.5) mm
      = 40 mm
        Prying length n
  [49] T_Rd_2 = (2 * M_pl_Rd + n_pry * n_t * F_t_Rd) / (m + n_pry)    « EN 1993-1-8 Table 6.2
      = (2 × 14.492 kN·m + 40 mm × 2 × 203 kN) / (43.5 mm + 40 mm)
      = 541.61 kN
        Mode 2: plate yields and bolts fail
  [50] T_Rd_3 = n_t * F_t_Rd    « EN 1993-1-8 Table 6.2
      = 2 × 203 kN
      = 406 kN
        Mode 3: bolt failure
  [51] T_Rd = min(T_Rd_1, T_Rd_2, T_Rd_3)    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.4
      = min(1332.6 kN, 541.61 kN, 406 kN)
      = 406 kN
        Tension T-stub resistance
  [52] u_T = T_Ed / T_Rd
      = 226.7 kN / 406 kN
      = 0.55839
        Plate bending and bolts in tension, T-stub utilisation
  [53] u_bt = F_t_Ed / F_t_Rd
      = 113.35 kN / 203 kN
      = 0.55839
        Holding-down bolt tension utilisation
  [54] F_f_Rd = C_fd * N_c_Ed    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.2
      = 0.2 × 210 kN
      = 42 kN
        Friction resistance, compression only
  [55] V_Rd = F_f_Rd + n_b * F_vb_Rd    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.2
      = 42 kN + 4 × 135.4 kN
      = 583.6 kN
        Shear resistance of the base
  [56] u_V = V_Ed / V_Rd
      = 60 kN / 583.6 kN
      = 0.10281
        Shear utilisation
  [57] u_max = max(u_N, u_C, u_T, u_bt, u_V)
      = u_N = 0.11119, u_C = 0.52849, u_T = 0.55839, u_bt = 0.55839, u_V = 0.10281
      = 0.55839
        Governing utilisation
