1. Scope and method
A fillet weld carrying forces parallel to, transverse to and normal to its axis, by the simplified method: the resultant over the throat area against the design shear strength of the weld, with the required throat and leg.
Resultant force on the weld: F = √(F_∥² + F_⊥² + F_n²). Design shear strength of the weld, simplified method: f_vw,d = f_u/(√3·β_w·γ_M2). Shear stress on the throat: τ_w = F/(a·L). Weld resistance and utilisation: F_w,Rd = f_vw,d·a·L; u = F/F_w,Rd. Throat needed (not less than 3 mm) and the matching leg: a_req = max(F/(f_vw,d·L), 3 mm); leg = √2·a.

2. Inputs
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  F_par | Force parallel to the weld axis | 0 | kN | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | -100000 … 100000 kN
  F_tr | Force transverse to the weld axis, in the plane of the parts | 0 | kN | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | -100000 … 100000 kN
  F_n | Force normal to the plane of the parts | 120 | kN | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | -100000 … 100000 kN
  L | Effective weld length | 100 | mm | drawing: DRG-WD-702 rev A | 30 … 10000 mm
  a | Throat thickness | 4 | mm | drawing: DRG-WD-702 rev A | 3 … 50 mm
  f_u | Ultimate strength of the weaker part joined | 490 | MPa | spec: SPEC-STR-02 design criteria | 300 … 1000 MPa
  beta_w | Correlation factor β_w (0.8 S235, 0.85 S275, 0.9 S355, 1.0 S420/S460) | 0.9 |  | spec: SPEC-STR-02 design criteria | 0.8 … 1
  gamma_M2 | Partial factor γ_M2 | 1.25 |  | spec: SPEC-STR-02 design criteria | 1 … 2

3. Assumptions
  A1. The resultant force is shared uniformly along the effective length. (Simplified method.)
  A2. Equal-leg fillet: leg = √2 × throat. (Geometry.)

4. Working
  [9] F = sqrt(F_par^2 + F_tr^2 + F_n^2)
      = sqrt((0 kN)^2 + (0 kN)^2 + (120 kN)^2)
      = 120 kN
        Resultant force on the weld
  [10] f_vw_d = f_u / (sqrt(3) * beta_w * gamma_M2)    « BS EN 1993-1-8 4.5.3.3
      = 490 MPa / (sqrt(3) × 0.9 × 1.25)
      = 251.47 MPa
        Design shear strength of the weld
  [11] tau_w = F / (a * L)
      = 120 kN / (4 mm × 100 mm)
      = 300 MPa
        Stress on the throat
  [12] F_w_Rd = f_vw_d * a * L    « BS EN 1993-1-8 4.5.3.3
      = 251.47 MPa × 4 mm × 100 mm
      = 100.59 kN
        Design resistance of the weld
  [13] u = F / F_w_Rd
      = 120 kN / 100.59 kN
      = 1.193
        Utilisation
  [14] a_calc = F / (f_vw_d * L)
      = 120 kN / (251.47 MPa × 100 mm)
      = 4.772 mm
        Throat needed for the load
  [15] a_req = max(a_calc, 3 mm)    « BS EN 1993-1-8 4.5.2(2)
      = max(4.772 mm, 3 mm)
      = 4.772 mm
        Required throat (not less than 3 mm)
  [16] s_leg = sqrt(2) * a
      = sqrt(2) × 4 mm
      = 5.6569 mm
        Leg length of the throat provided (equal legs)
