1. Scope and method
Calculates a single-phase heat exchanger with constant properties: the duty and outlet temperatures for a given area, or the area for a given duty. It covers counterflow, parallel flow, shell and tube, crossflow and one fluid at constant temperature.
Capacity rates: C_h = ṁ_h·c_p,h, C_c = ṁ_c·c_p,c, C_r = C_min/C_max; with one fluid at constant temperature (C_r = 0) that fluid's capacity rate is infinite, C_min is the other stream's and the constant stream leaves at its inlet temperature Overall coefficient with fouling: U_f = 1/(1/U_clean + R_f) Transfer units: NTU = U_f·A/C_min; effectiveness ε(NTU, C_r) for the arrangement Heat exchanger duty: Phi = ε·C_min·(T_h,in − T_c,in); outlets from the energy balance Area for a required duty: ε = Q_req/(C_min·ΔT_max), NTU from the inverse relation, A = NTU·C_min/U_f Cross-check with LMTD: Phi = U_f·A·F_corr·ΔT_lm, F_corr from R and P (one shell pass) or back-calculated

3. Assumptions
  A1. Single phase on both sides (except the C_r = 0 arrangement), constant properties, no heat loss to surroundings. (Incropera 7th ed. §11.3–11.4.)
  A2. The crossflow both-unmixed effectiveness is the published approximate correlation, flagged on the sheet. (Incropera 7th ed. Table 11.3.)
  A3. Specific heats are user records at the mean temperature of each stream. (Entered as reference records, which carry their source, edition and licence.)
  A4. arrangement (Flow arrangement) is taken as counterflow. (Plate exchanger treated as pure counterflow.)
  A5. mode (Mode) is taken as sizing (given duty). (Sizing a new exchanger.)
  A6. cp_h (Hot-side specific heat) is taken as 4190 J/(kg·K). (Water at about 70 °C.)
  A7. cp_c (Cold-side specific heat) is taken as 4180 J/(kg·K). (Water at about 50 °C.)
  A8. U_clean (Overall heat transfer coefficient (clean)) is taken as 3000 W/(m²·K). (Typical clean plate exchanger, water/water.)
  A9. R_f (Total fouling resistance) is taken as 0.0001 m²·K/W. (Treated closed circuits on both sides.)
  A10. dT_min (Minimum approach temperature difference) is taken as 3 K. (Practical approach for a plate exchanger.)

4. Working
  [12] C_h = m_h * cp_h
      = 1.2 kg/s × 4190 J/(kg·K)
      = 5028 W/K
        Hot-side capacity rate
  [13] C_c = m_c * cp_c
      = 1.5 kg/s × 4180 J/(kg·K)
      = 6270 W/K
        Cold-side capacity rate
  [14] C_min = min(C_h, C_c)
      = min(5028 W/K, 6270 W/K)
      = 5028 W/K
        Minimum capacity rate
  [15] C_r = min(C_h, C_c) / max(C_h, C_c)
      = min(5028 W/K, 6270 W/K) / max(5028 W/K, 6270 W/K)
      = 0.80191
        Capacity ratio
  [16] U_f = 1 / (1 / U_clean + R_f)
      = 1 / (1 / 3000 W/(m²·K) + 0.0001 m²·K/W)
      = 2307.7 W/(m²·K)
        Overall coefficient with fouling
  [18] eps = Q_req / (C_min·ΔT_max)
      = 120000 / (5028 × 40)
      = 0.59666
        Effectiveness required
  [19] NTU = NTU(ε, C_r) inverse relation    « Incropera 7th ed. Table 11.4
      = ε = 0.59666, C_r = 0.80191
      = 1.2973
        Transfer units required
  [20] A_req = NTU·C_min / U_f
      = 1.2973 × 5028 / 2307.7
      = 2.8267 m²
        Required heat transfer area
  [21] UA = NTU·C_min
      = 1.2973 × 5028
      = 6523 W/K
        Required UA
  [22] Phi = ε·C_min·(T_h,in − T_c,in)
      = 0.59666 × 5028 × 40
      = 120 kW
        Heat duty
  [23] T_h_out = T_h,in − Q/C_h
      = 80 °C − 120000 / 5028
      = 56.134 degC
        Hot-side outlet temperature
  [24] T_c_out = T_c,in + Q/C_c
      = 40 °C + 120000 / 6270
      = 59.139 degC
        Cold-side outlet temperature
  [25] dT_c = T_c,out − T_c,in
      = 59.139 °C − 40 °C
      = 19.139 K
        Cold-side temperature rise
  [26] T_c_m = (T_c,in + T_c,out)/2
      = (40 °C + 59.139 °C)/2
      = 49.569 degC
        Mean cold-side temperature
  [27] dT_app = min(T_h,in − T_c,out, T_h,out − T_c,in)
      = 16.134 K
      = 16.134 K
        Approach temperature difference
  [28] dT_lm = (ΔT_1 − ΔT_2)/ln(ΔT_1/ΔT_2), counterflow ends
      = ΔT_1 = 20.861 K, ΔT_2 = 16.134 K
      = 18.396 K
        Log-mean temperature difference
  [29] F_corr = 1 (pure counterflow, parallel flow or C_r = 0)    « Incropera 7th ed. §11.3
      = R = 1.247, P = 0.47847
      = 1
        LMTD correction factor
  [30] Q_lmtd = U_f·A·F·ΔT_lm
      = 2307.7 × 2.8267 × 1 × 18.396
      = 120 kW
        Duty by the LMTD method (cross-check)
  [31] u_app = dT_min / dT_app
      = 3 K / 16.134 K
      = 0.18595
        Approach utilisation (minimum / actual)
