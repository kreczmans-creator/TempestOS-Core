1. Scope and method
Calculates interstitial condensation in a layered wall by the Glaser method over twelve monthly climate steps. It gives the condensate on each interface, the annual balance, the peak against the limit and the surface temperature factor.
Layer resistance and diffusion thickness: R_j = d_j/λ_j; s_d,j = μ_j·d_j Total resistance, U-value and interface temperature: R_T = R_si + Σ R_j + R_se; U = 1/R_T; T_k = T_int − (T_int − T_e)·(R_si + Σ_(j≤k) R_j)/R_T Saturation pressure (Magnus): p_sat = 610.5·exp(17.269·T/(237.3 + T)) over water (T ≥ 0 °C); p_sat = 610.5·exp(21.875·T/(265.5 + T)) over ice (T < 0 °C) Vapour pressures: p_e = RH_e·p_sat(T_e); p_i = RH_i·p_sat(T_int), or p_e + f_Δp·Δv(T_e)·R_v·(T_i + T_e)/2 in the moisture-excess mode with R_v = 461.5 J/(kg·K) Vapour flux and condensate: g = δ0·Δp/Δs_d with δ0 = 2×10⁻¹⁰ kg/(m·s·Pa); g_c = g_in − g_out on the tangent-construction profile; Mc accumulates g_c·t over 12 months, a wet interface drying at g_c < 0 Refinement check: the same year with every layer split into 20 equal sub-layers of the same λ and μ; the annual balance passes only when it passes both ways Annual balance and peak: PASS when no condensate remains after 12 months; Mc_max = the most held on any interface, checked against Mc_limit Surface factor: f_Rsi = 1 − R_si,f/R_T,f; f_Rsi,min = the largest monthly (T_si,min − T_e)/(T_int − T_e) with p_sat(T_si,min) = p_i/φ_crit; PASS when f_Rsi ≥ f_Rsi,min

3. Assumptions
  A1. Vapour diffuses one-dimensionally through homogeneous layers in series, in steady state each month. (The Glaser method.)
  A2. Each month uses its mean climate, with no air movement, liquid transport, moisture storage, or solar or rain gain. (The Glaser method.)
  A3. Material properties λ and μ are constants of each layer. (The Glaser method.)
  A4. Condensate does not change the properties of a layer. (The Glaser method.)
  A5. Condensation is checked at the interfaces between layers. (The Glaser method.)
  A6. The saturation line is straight between interface values, which can miss condensate inside a layer. (Stated limit; whether ISO 13788 requires sub-division is to be checked against the owner's copy.)
  A7. The annual balance is also run with every layer split into 20 sub-layers and passes only when both runs pass. (Refinement check.)
  A8. The limit on the condensate per interface is checked on the interfaces as entered. (Stated limit.)
  A9. The Magnus saturation pressure is used over water at and above 0 °C and over ice below. (Derived.)
  A10. The water-vapour permeability of air is δ0 = 2×10⁻¹⁰ kg/(m·s·Pa). (Derived; closed-form physics, not data of a standard.)
  A11. A blank month length is the calendar days of the month, with the climate rows taken as January to December. (Calendar months, February 28 days; enter the days where the record starts in another month.)
  A12. A wet interface that dries within a month is dry from the next month. (Monthly steps.)
  A13. The rates of a month are those of the wet state at its start. (Monthly steps.)
  A14. The climate, humidity-class points, surface resistances, critical humidity and condensate limit are the user's own records. (No table from a standard is shipped.)
  A15. climate (Twelve monthly outside conditions (the user's climate record, in the order the year is run)) is taken as 12 row(s). (Invented illustrative maritime year; replace with the project's own monthly climate record (take it from your licensed source).)
  A16. humidity_mode (Internal humidity from) is taken as constant internal relative humidity. (Constant internal humidity; no humidity-class record is held for this project.)
  A17. breathable (The build-up is timber or breathable (hygroscopic layers, capillary transport)) is taken as no. (Mineral wool and brick: no timber or hygroscopic layers.)
  A18. dp_f (Safety factor on the vapour-pressure difference Δp = Δv·R_v·(T_i + T_e)/2 in the moisture-excess mode (1.10 by convention; a record, wording to be checked against the owner's copy of ISO 13788)) is taken as 1.1. (Moisture-excess mode only; this example holds a constant internal humidity, so the conventional 1.10 is kept unused.)
  A19. Month length: 12 row(s) of the climate gave no days, so the calendar days of the month are used with the rows taken as January to December (February 28 days). If the record starts in another month, enter the days of each row. (Calendar months.)
