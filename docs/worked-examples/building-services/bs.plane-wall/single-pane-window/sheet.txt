Title: Plane wall heat transfer (layers and films)
Author: TempestOS    Authored: —
Checker: —    Checked: —
Issued: —

1. Scope and method
Calculates the steady heat flow through a composite plane wall, between two fluids or between two known surface temperatures. It gives the overall U-value, the heat flux and every surface and interface temperature.
Resistance of each surface film: R = 1/(h·A). Resistance of each conducting layer: R_i = t_i/(k_i·A). Total resistance, hot side first: R_tot = Σ R. Heat flow through the wall: Phi = (T_1 − T_2)/R_tot. Heat flux and overall U-value: q = Phi/A; U_wall = 1/(R_tot·A). Surface and interface temperatures, stepping down from T_1 across each resistance in turn: T_next = T_previous − Phi·R.

2. Inputs
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  layers | Conducting layers, side 1 first | 1 row(s) |  | drawing: DRG-GL-801 rev A | any
  layers[1] |  | name = glass; t = 8 mm; k = 0.78 W/(m·K) |  |  | 
  A | Wall area normal to the heat flow | 1.2 | m² | drawing: DRG-GL-801 rev A | 0.001 … 10000 m²
  T_1 | Side 1 temperature (the fluid's, or the surface's when no film is given) | 20 | °C | spec: SPEC-BS-04 design conditions | -60 … 400 °C
  T_2 | Side 2 temperature (the fluid's, or the surface's when no film is given) | -10 | °C | spec: SPEC-BS-04 design conditions | -60 … 400 °C
  h_1 | Side 1 film coefficient (optional; blank = no film, so T_1 is the surface temperature) | 10 | W/(m²·K) | spec: SPEC-BS-04 design conditions | 2 … 10000 W/(m²·K)
  h_2 | Side 2 film coefficient (optional; blank = no film, so T_2 is the surface temperature) | 40 | W/(m²·K) | spec: SPEC-BS-04 design conditions | 2 … 10000 W/(m²·K)

3. Assumptions
  A1. Steady, one-dimensional conduction through plane layers of constant conductivity, with no internal heat generation. (Thermal-circuit idealisation: equal layer areas, no edge losses.)
  A2. Layers are in perfect thermal contact. (A contact resistance can be entered as a thin layer of equivalent thickness and conductivity.)
  A3. Each film coefficient is constant over its surface. (Thermal-circuit idealisation.)

4. Working
  [6] R_h1 = 1 / (h_1 · A)    « Incropera §3.1
      = 1 / (10 W/(m²·K) × 1.2 m²)
      = 0.083333 K/W
        Side 1 film resistance
  [7] R_1 = t_1 / (k_1 · A)    « Incropera §3.1
      = 8 mm / (0.78 W/(m·K) × 1.2 m²)
      = 0.008547 K/W
        Conduction resistance of glass
  [8] R_h2 = 1 / (h_2 · A)    « Incropera §3.1
      = 1 / (40 W/(m²·K) × 1.2 m²)
      = 0.020833 K/W
        Side 2 film resistance
  [9] R_tot = Σ R    « Çengel §3-3
      = 0.083333 + 0.008547 + 0.020833 K/W
      = 0.11271 K/W
        Total thermal resistance
  [10] Phi = (T_1 - T_2) / R_tot
      = (20 degC − (-10 degC)) / 0.11271 K/W
      = 0.26616 kW
        Heat flow from side 1 to side 2
  [11] q = Phi / A
      = 0.26616 kW / 1.2 m²
      = 221.8 W/m²
        Heat flux
  [12] U_wall = 1 / (R_tot * A)
      = 1 / (0.11271 K/W × 1.2 m²)
      = 7.3934 W/(m²·K)
        Overall heat transfer coefficient
  [13] T_s0 = T_1 − Phi · R_h1
      = 20 °C − 266.16 W × 0.083333 K/W
      = -2.1801 degC
        Side 1 surface
  [14] T_s1 = T_s0 − Phi · R_1
      = -2.1801 °C − 266.16 W × 0.008547 K/W
      = -4.455 degC
        Side 2 surface

5. Results
  Phi = 0.2662 kW    Heat flow
  q = 221.8 W/m²    Heat flux
  U_wall = 7.393 W/(m²·K)    Overall heat transfer coefficient
  R_tot = 0.1127 K/W    Total thermal resistance
  R_h1 = 0.08333 K/W    Side 1 film resistance
  R_1 = 0.008547 K/W    Layer 1 resistance
  R_h2 = 0.02083 K/W    Side 2 film resistance
  T_s0 = -2.180 °C    Side 1 surface temperature
  T_s1 = -4.455 °C    Side 2 surface temperature
