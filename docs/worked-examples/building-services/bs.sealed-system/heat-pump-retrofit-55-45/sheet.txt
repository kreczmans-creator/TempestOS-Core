1. SCOPE AND METHOD
A closed LTHW system below 100 °C: the catalogue output (at ΔT 50 K) each room's emitter needs at the design flow, return and room temperatures, checked against the selected emitters; and the minimum expansion vessel for the system volume, fill and maximum temperatures, static head and pressures.
Emitter temperature difference: ΔT = (t_f + t_r)/2 − t_i, or the log mean (t_f − t_r)/ln((t_f − t_i)/(t_r − t_i)) Output at design: Φ = Φ50·(ΔT/50)^n; required catalogue output Φ50 = Φ_req/(ΔT/50)^n System volume: V_s = Σ V_em + V_pipe + V_plant Expansion: e_x = ρ(t_fill)/ρ(t_max) − 1 (water density by Kell 1975), or a glycol record Pre-charge: p_i = ρ·g·h_static + margin (≥ static head) Vessel: V_e = e_x·V_s/[1 − (p_i + 1)/(p_f + 1)] (pressures in bar gauge, + 1 bar for absolute)
  Ref: BS EN 442-2:2014 cl. 5 — Radiators and convectors — test methods and rating (characteristic equation)
  Ref: CIBSE Guide B1 2016, §1.4 — Heating — sealed systems and expansion
  Ref: BS EN 12828:2012+A1:2014 cl. 4.6 — Heating systems in buildings — expansion vessels and pressure

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Closed LTHW system below 100 °C; emitter output follows Φ50·(ΔT/50)^n with the manufacturer's n. (Catalogue ratings at 75/65/20 °C.)
  A2. Water density from the Kell (1975) correlation, within about 0.01 % of IAPWS-95 at atmospheric pressure, 0–100 °C; glycol mixtures take e from a record. (Formula, not a table.)
  A3. Gauge pressures are made absolute by adding 1 bar, as the vessel relation is usually written. (Within 1.3 % of the standard atmosphere.)
  A4. n (Emitter exponent) is taken as 1.3. (Panel radiators, manufacturer exponent about 1.3.)
  A5. dT_method (Mean temperature difference) is taken as arithmetic mean (catalogue rating). (Catalogue ratings use the arithmetic mean.)
  A6. V_pipe (Pipework water content) is taken as 40 L. (15 mm and 22 mm copper, about 120 m.)
  A7. t_fill (Fill temperature) is taken as 10 degC. (Mains water at fill.)
  A8. p_margin (Pre-charge margin over static head) is taken as 0.3 bar. (Usual margin over static head.)
  A9. p_f (Final (maximum working) pressure, gauge) is taken as 2.5 bar. (0.5 bar below the safety valve.)
