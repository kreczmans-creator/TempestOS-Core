1. Scope and method
Calculates duct sizes by the equal-friction or the velocity method, rounded up to standard sizes. It reports the velocity, friction rate and losses of each section and the pressure drop along the index run.
Round duct diameter from the design target: D by Colebrook with the air properties at T (equal friction), or D = √(4Q/πv) (velocity), rounded up to the next standard size. Rectangular duct of equal friction and capacity: D_e = 1.30 (ab)^0.625/(a + b)^0.25. Flat-oval duct of equal friction and capacity: D_e = 1.55 A^0.625/P^0.25, with A = πb²/4 + b(a − b) and P = πb + 2(a − b). Velocity method, rectangular or flat-oval duct with side a fixed: b from the free area A = Q/v (rectangle b = A/a; flat oval the smaller root of (1 − π/4)b² − ab + A = 0), rounded up; D_e is kept for the friction only. Friction loss per metre, at the velocity v_e of the equivalent round duct: Δp/L = f ρ v_e²/(2D_e). Fitting losses, at the actual velocity: Δp = ΣC ρ v²/2. Index-run totals, summed over every section of the run: Δp_run = Σ(Δp/L · L + ΣC ρ v²/2).

3. Assumptions
  A1. Air is dry at the stated temperature and pressure. (Ideal gas, ρ = p/(287.055 T).)
  A2. The viscosity of air follows Sutherland's law. (Standard air properties.)
  A3. Rectangular and flat-oval ducts are sized through the equivalent diameter for equal friction and capacity. (Huebscher / Heyt–Diaz relations as given in ASHRAE Fundamentals Ch. 21.)
  A4. Fitting loss coefficients C are user-supplied (manufacturer or ASHRAE/CIBSE data, not embedded) and refer to the section's velocity pressure. (Non-negotiable: no standard tables embedded.)
  A5. method (Sizing method) is taken as velocity. (Velocity method for an extract system.)
  A6. eps_rough (Duct roughness) is taken as 0.15 mm. (Spiral-wound galvanised duct with joints.)
  A7. T_air (Air temperature) is taken as 25 °C. (Workshop air 25 °C.)
  A8. p_atm (Barometric pressure) is taken as 100.5 kPa. (Site 70 m AOD, ≈ 100.5 kPa.)
  A9. dp_target (Target friction rate (equal friction)) is taken as 1 Pa/m. (Equal-friction method only; the velocity method does not use it, so the template default is kept.)

4. Working
  [10] rho = p / (287.055 T)
      = 100500 Pa / (287.055 × 298.15 K)
      = 1.1743 kg/m³
        Air density
  [11] mu = Sutherland's law
      = T = 298.15 K
      = 18.371 uPa·s
        Air dynamic viscosity
  [12] D_s1 = hood branch: diameter
      = target 211.1 mm rounded up to 50 mm
      = 250 mm
        Diameter, hood branch
  [13] v_s1 = hood branch: Q / A
      = 0.35 / 0.049087
      = 7.1301 m/s
        Velocity, hood branch
  [14] Re_s1 = hood branch: ρ v_e D_e / μ (v_e = 4Q/πD_e²)
      = 1.1743 × 7.1301 × 0.25 / 0.000018371
      = 113940
        Reynolds number, hood branch
  [15] f_s1 = hood branch: friction factor
      = Colebrook–White
      = 0.02041
        Friction factor, hood branch
  [16] dpL_s1 = hood branch: f ρ v_e² / (2 D_e)
      = 0.02041 × 1.1743 × 7.1301² / (2 × 0.25)
      = 2.4369 Pa/m
        Friction rate, hood branch
  [17] dp_f_s1 = hood branch: (Δp/L) L
