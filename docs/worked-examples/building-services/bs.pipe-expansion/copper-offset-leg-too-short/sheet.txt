1. Scope and method
Calculates the thermal growth of a pipe run between anchors, the leg or loop that absorbs it, and the force and moment on the anchors. It is a screening calculation, not a piping stress analysis.
Pipe section: d_i = OD − 2·t; A_m = π/4·(OD² − d_i²); I = π/64·(OD⁴ − d_i⁴); Z = I/(OD/2) Temperature change and growth: ΔT = T_operate − T_install, or as entered; L_tot = L, or L + L2 for a Z-bend; ΔL = α·L_tot·|ΔT|, its sign giving the direction Fully restrained pipe: σ_r = E·α·|ΔT| (E at the installation temperature); F_r = E_r·α·|ΔT|·A_m (E_r = E_hot, else E) Guided cantilever, required leg: Δ = ΔL for an offset leg or Z-bend and ΔL/2 for a U-loop; L_req = √(3·E·OD·Δ/Sa); PASS when L_req ≤ the available leg H Force and moment of the available leg: F_leg = 12·E_r·I·Δ/H³ (along the run); M_leg = 6·E_r·I·Δ/H² (= F_leg·H/2); σ_leg = 3·E·OD·Δ/H² = Sa·(L_req/H)² Runs of an offset leg or Z-bend: the runs bend for the leg's growth Δ_leg = α·|ΔT|·H under one force F_run = 12·E_r·I·Δ_leg/(L³ + L2³) along the leg (L2 = 0 for an L-bend); run i takes L_i³/(L³ + L2³) of Δ_leg, with σ_i = 3·E·OD·Δ_i/L_i² and M_i = F_run·L_i/2, and passes when √(σ_i/Sa) ≤ 1 Force on the anchors: √(F_leg² + F_run²) from the two perpendicular components; the moment reported is the largest root moment of the legs and runs Screening and bellows: OD·Y/(L_dev − U)² ≤ K1 with Y = α·|ΔT|·U (offset leg or Z-bend L_dev = L_tot + H, U = √(L_tot² + H²); U-loop L_dev = L + 2·H, U = L); axial bellows anchor force = P·A_eff + k·ΔL, with no leg check and no screening

3. Assumptions
  A1. The result is a screen, not a piping stress analysis. (Stated scope.)
  A2. The result tells whether a simple leg or loop is plausible and what it puts on its anchors. (Stated scope.)
  A3. It does not replace a flexibility analysis of the real routing, including elbow flexibility, intensification, torsion, branches, supports and loads. (Stated scope.)
  A4. A guided cantilever is straight, uniform, fixed against rotation at both ends and loaded only by the movement it absorbs. (Beam theory.)
  A5. Its stiffness is 12·E·I/H³ and its end moment is F·H/2. (Beam theory.)
  A6. Elbows are taken as rigid and the leg as slender. (Beam theory.)
  A7. The beam model is not valid for legs shorter than about ten diameters. (Beam theory.)
  A8. No stress-intensification factor and no elbow flexibility are applied. (Stated limit.)
  A9. The stress at the elbow root of a real bend is higher than the beam stress M/Z. (Stated limit.)
  A10. A leg that passes here can still exceed Sa at the elbow, which is why the result is a screen. (Stated limit.)
  A11. A U-loop is two identical legs in series, each a guided cantilever taking half the growth. (Loop model.)
  A12. The cross leg of a U-loop is a rigid guide. (Loop model.)
  A13. The force along the line is the same in both legs of a U-loop. (Loop model.)
  A14. The moment M is the moment at the leg roots, the corners of the loop. (Loop model.)
  A15. It reaches an anchor only where the leg ends at the anchor, as in an offset leg. (Loop model.)
  A16. The growth of the loop's own legs, which the cross leg has to absorb, is not checked. (Loop model.)
  A17. The run of an offset leg bends for the leg's growth as a second guided cantilever, checked against Sa. (Offset-leg and Z-bend model.)
  A18. In a Z-bend the two runs are in series under one force. (Offset-leg and Z-bend model.)
  A19. Each run takes its share of the leg's growth in proportion to L_i³ and is checked as its own cantilever. (Offset-leg and Z-bend model.)
  A20. The leg of a Z-bend takes the growth of both runs. (Offset-leg and Z-bend model.)
  A21. The force on the anchors is the resultant of the two perpendicular forces along the run and along the leg. (Offset-leg and Z-bend model.)
  A22. anchor_moment is the largest root moment of the legs and runs, M = F·L/2 with each end fixed against rotation. (Stated limit.)
  A23. It is not the moment on a frame anchor, which depends on the whole frame. (Stated limit.)
  A24. A formal analysis is needed before an anchor is sized for moment. (Stated limit.)
  A25. α is the mean coefficient of expansion between the install and operating temperatures. (Material library.)
  A26. E is the modulus at the installation temperature, used for the stress check as the piping codes do. (The ASME process piping code, cl. 319.3.2 and 319.5 (numbers to be checked against the owner's copy).)
  A27. E_hot, when given, is used only for the reactions on the anchors. (Code convention to be checked.)
  A28. The material properties are taken from a library material applied at the right temperature. (Material library.)
  A29. Sa and K1 are the owner's records from the owner's own copy of the piping code or the design basis. (No table from a standard is shipped.)
  A30. No allowable stress, K1 or code constant ships. (No table from a standard is shipped.)
  A31. The sheet applies the relation to the numbers entered, whatever code or load case they come from. (No table from a standard is shipped.)
  A32. No weight, spring-hanger or cold-spring effect is included in the leg force. (Stated limit.)
  A33. The anchor force adds support friction μ·w·L only when μ and the weight per metre are entered. (Stated limit; a warning says so when they are not.)
  A34. The bellows anchor force is the pressure thrust P·A_eff plus the spring force k·ΔL at the governing pressure. (Stated limit.)
  A35. The governing pressure is the design or test pressure, whichever governs, because compression and extension ratings can differ. (Stated limit.)
  A36. Guide friction and any other bellows load come from the manufacturer's data. (Stated limit.)
  A37. temp_mode (Temperature change from) is taken as install and operating temperatures. (Install and operating temperatures are both known.)
  A38. E (Young's modulus of the pipe material at the installation (reference) temperature, for the stress check, in GPa (steel 210, copper 120, PE and PP about 1; not MPa)) is taken as 120 GPa. (W3 seed 'Copper (generic)', indicative; replace with the project's material record.)
  A39. alpha (Mean coefficient of thermal expansion between the install and operating temperatures) is taken as 17 um/(m·K). (W3 seed 'Copper (generic)' (17e-6 1/K), indicative; replace with the project's material record.)
  A40. T_install (Temperature at installation (when the run is anchored)) is taken as 10 °C. (Mean site temperature when the riser is anchored.)
  A41. Sa (Allowable displacement-stress range for the legs and runs (your piping code and its formula; optional for an axial bellows; no default)) is taken as 60 MPa. (Invented illustrative allowable range; take Sa from the project's piping code and its formula.)
  A42. K_scr (Screening limit K1 for OD·Y/(L − U)², in mm²/m² (or in²/ft², …) from your piping code (optional; blank = screening not evaluated, so the sheet does not pass; no default)) is taken as 100 mm²/m². (Invented illustrative screening limit; take K1 from the project's piping code.)
  A43. Layout: the offset leg of 2.5 m takes the whole movement of 24.48 mm as a guided cantilever fixed against rotation at both ends, and the run of 24 m bends for the leg's growth of 2.55 mm. Screening only — not a piping stress analysis. (Guided-cantilever model.)
  A44. The available leg (2.5 m) is shorter than the 2.8163 m required: the bending stress at its root is 76.143 MPa against Sa = 60 MPa. Lengthen the leg or loop, add a loop or bellows, or analyse the line formally. (Result of the check.)

4. Working
  [9] dT = T_operate - T_install
      = 70 degC − 10 degC
      = 60 K
        Temperature change, operating − install
  [11] E_r = E

      = 120 GPa
        Modulus for the reactions (E, no E_hot given)
  [12] D_i = OD - 2 * t_wall
      = 54 mm − 2 × 1.5 mm
      = 51 mm
        Inside diameter
  [13] A_m = pi / 4 * (OD^2 - D_i^2)
      = π / 4 × ((54 mm)^2 − (51 mm)^2)
      = 247.4 mm²
        Metal cross-section
  [14] I_x = pi / 64 * (OD^4 - D_i^4)
      = π / 64 × ((54 mm)^4 − (51 mm)^4)
      = 8.5307 cm⁴
        Second moment of area of the pipe section
  [15] Z_x = I_x / (OD / 2)
      = 8.5307 cm⁴ / (54 mm / 2)
      = 3.1595 cm³
        Section modulus
  [16] L_tot = L
      = 24 m
      = 24 m
        Length that grows between the anchors
  [17] dL = alpha * L_tot * abs(dT)
      = 17 um/(m·K) × 24 m × abs(60 K)
      = 24.48 mm
        Thermal growth between the anchors, as a magnitude
  [18] dL_signed = alpha * L_tot * dT
      = 17 um/(m·K) × 24 m × 60 K
      = 24.48 mm
        Thermal movement with its sign: positive lengthens, negative shortens
  [19] sigma_res = E * alpha * abs(dT)
      = 120 GPa × 17 um/(m·K) × abs(60 K)
      = 122.4 MPa
        Stress in the fully restrained pipe (E at the installation temperature)
  [20] F_res = E_r * alpha * abs(dT) * A_m
      = 120 GPa × 17 um/(m·K) × abs(60 K) × 247.4 mm²
      = 30.282 kN
        Force on the anchors of the fully restrained pipe
  [21] F_friction = support friction not entered
      = μ and w_pipe blank
      = 0 kN
        Friction of the sliding supports (not entered: none in the anchor force)
  [24] u_res = sigma_res / Sa
      = 122.4 MPa / 60 MPa
      = 2.04
        Fully restrained stress as a fraction of Sa (above 1: the movement has to be absorbed)
  [25] Delta = dL
      = 24.48 mm
      = 24.48 mm
        Movement the offset leg absorbs (all of it)
  [26] L_req = sqrt(3 * E * OD * Delta / Sa)
      = sqrt(3 × 120 GPa × 54 mm × 24.48 mm / 60 MPa)
      = 2.8163 m
        Leg length at which the guided-cantilever bending stress reaches Sa
  [27] u_leg = L_req / H
      = 2.8163 m / 2.5 m
      = 1.1265
        Required leg over available leg
  [28] F_leg = 12 * E_r * I_x * Delta / H^3
      = 12 × 120 GPa × 8.5307 cm⁴ × 24.48 mm / (2.5 m)^3
      = 0.19246 kN
        Force of the available leg on its anchors or guides
  [29] M_leg = 6 * E_r * I_x * Delta / H^2
      = 6 × 120 GPa × 8.5307 cm⁴ × 24.48 mm / (2.5 m)^2
      = 0.24057 kN·m
        Moment at the root of the available leg
  [30] sigma_leg = 3 * E * OD * Delta / H^2
      = 3 × 120 GPa × 54 mm × 24.48 mm / (2.5 m)^2
      = 76.143 MPa
        Bending stress at the root of the available leg (E at the installation temperature)
  [31] u_sigma = sigma_leg / Sa
      = 76.143 MPa / 60 MPa
      = 1.269
        Bending stress over Sa (= u_leg²)
  [32] Delta_run = alpha * abs(dT) * H
      = 17 um/(m·K) × abs(60 K) × 2.5 m
      = 2.55 mm
        Growth of the leg, which the run or runs have to absorb
  [33] S_run = L^3
      = (24 m)^3
      = 13824 m³
        Cube of the run (its flexibility)
  [34] F_run = 12 * E_r * I_x * Delta_run / S_run
      = 12 × 120 GPa × 8.5307 cm⁴ × 2.55 mm / 13824 m³
      = 0.00002266 kN
        Force of the run or runs, along the leg
  [35] sigma_run = 3 * E * OD * Delta_run * L / S_run
      = 3 × 120 GPa × 54 mm × 2.55 mm × 24 m / 13824 m³
      = 0.086062 MPa
        Bending stress at the root of the run
  [36] u_run = sqrt(sigma_run / Sa)
      = sqrt(0.086062 MPa / 60 MPa)
      = 0.037873
        Required run over available run (= √(σ/Sa))
  [37] L_req_run = L * u_run
      = 24 m × 0.037873
      = 0.90896 m
        Cantilever length for this run's share of the leg's growth
  [38] M_run = F_run * L / 2
      = 0.00002266 kN × 24 m / 2
      = 0.00027192 kN·m
        Moment at the root of the run
  [39] F_along_run = F_leg + F_friction
      = 0.19246 kN + 0 kN
      = 0.19246 kN
        Component of the anchor force along the run: the leg's force plus the support friction
  [40] F_along_leg = F_run
      = 0.00002266 kN
      = 0.00002266 kN
        Component of the anchor force along the leg: the runs' force
  [41] anchor_force = sqrt(F_along_run^2 + F_run^2)
      = sqrt((0.19246 kN)^2 + (0.00002266 kN)^2)
      = 0.19246 kN
        Force on the anchors: resultant of the two perpendicular components (support friction only if μ and w_pipe are entered)
  [42] anchor_moment = max(M_leg, M_run)
      = max(0.24057 kN·m, 0.00027192 kN·m)
      = 0.24057 kN·m
        Largest root moment of the leg and the run or runs (guided-cantilever model)
  [43] L_dev = L_tot + H
      = 24 m + 2.5 m
      = 26.5 m

  [44] U_a = sqrt(L_tot^2 + H^2)
      = sqrt((24 m)^2 + (2.5 m)^2)

  [45] Y_scr = alpha * abs(dT) * U_a

      = 24.612 mm

  [46] scr = OD * Y_scr / (L_dev - U_a)^2
      = 54 mm × 24.612 mm / (26.5 m − 24.13 m)^2
      = 236.59 mm²/m²

  [48] u_scr = scr / K_scr
      = 236.59 mm²/m² / 100 mm²/m²
      = 2.3659
        Screening ratio over the K1 record
  [49] pass = K1 given and every check passes
      = 3 check(s)
      = 0
