1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Screening only — not a piping stress analysis. Thermal growth of a straight run between anchors, the fully restrained stress and force, the leg an offset leg or a U-loop (two guided cantilevers) needs to absorb the growth within the owner's allowable displacement-stress range Sa and whether the available leg is long enough (an offset leg also loads the run with the leg's own growth), the force and moment on the anchors, the screening ratio D·Y/(L − U)² against the owner's K1 (PASS, or the line needs a formal stress analysis), and, for an axial bellows, the anchor force P·A_eff plus its spring force. No allowable stress, K1 or code table ships.
Pipe section: d_i = OD − 2·t; A_m = π/4·(OD² − d_i²); I = π/64·(OD⁴ − d_i⁴); Z = I/(OD/2) Temperature change: ΔT = T_operate − T_install, or as entered; the movement is taken for |ΔT|, the sign gives the direction Thermal growth between anchors: ΔL = α·L·|ΔT| Fully restrained pipe: σ_r = E·α·|ΔT| (E at the installation temperature); F_r = E_r·α·|ΔT|·A_m (E_r = E_hot, else E) Movement one leg must absorb: Δ = ΔL for an offset leg; Δ = ΔL/2 for a U-loop (two identical legs in series, the cross leg a rigid guide) Guided cantilever, required leg: L_req = √(3·E·OD·Δ/Sa), the leg at which the bending stress M/Z reaches Sa; PASS when L_req ≤ the available leg H Force and moment of the available leg: F = 12·E_r·I·Δ/H³; M = 6·E_r·I·Δ/H² (= F·H/2); σ_leg = M/Z = 3·E·OD·Δ/H² = Sa·(L_req/H)² Offset leg only, the run bends for the leg's growth: Δ_run = α·|ΔT|·H; L_req,run = √(3·E·OD·Δ_run/Sa), PASS when L_req,run ≤ L; F_run = 12·E_r·I·Δ_run/L³; M_run = 6·E_r·I·Δ_run/L²; the anchor force and moment are the larger of the leg's and the run's Screening: OD·Y/(L_dev − U)² ≤ K1 with Y = α·|ΔT|·U, the free growth between the anchors; offset leg L_dev = L + H, U = √(L² + H²); U-loop L_dev = L + 2·H, U = L (so Y = ΔL); PASS, or the line needs a formal stress analysis Axial bellows (no leg or loop, no leg check, no screening): anchor force = P·A_eff + k·ΔL; the movement is checked against the bellows' rated axial movement when given
  Ref: ASME B31.3 para. 319.4.1 (screening relation; number to be checked against the owner's copy) — Process piping — flexibility analysis, exemption from formal analysis
  Ref: ASME B31.3 para. 319.3.2 and 319.5 (reference and hot moduli; numbers to be checked against the owner's copy) — Process piping — displacement stress range and reactions
  Ref: EN 13480-3 cl. 12 (number to be checked against the owner's copy) — Metallic industrial piping — flexibility analysis

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Screening only — not a piping stress analysis. The result tells whether a simple leg or loop is plausible and what it puts on its anchors; it does not replace a flexibility analysis of the real routing (elbow flexibility, intensification, torsion, branches, restraint friction, supports, sustained and occasional loads). (Stated scope.)
  A2. A guided cantilever: the available leg is straight, uniform, fixed against rotation at both ends and loaded only by the movement it absorbs (stiffness 12·E·I/H³, end moment F·H/2). Elbows are taken as rigid and the leg as slender: the beam model is not valid for legs shorter than about ten diameters. (Beam theory.)
  A3. No stress-intensification factor and no elbow flexibility are applied: the stress at the elbow root of a real bend is higher than the beam stress M/Z, by the intensification factor of the code, so a leg that passes here can still exceed Sa at the elbow. This is the main reason the result is a screen and not a stress analysis. (Stated limit.)
  A4. A U-loop is two identical legs in series, each a guided cantilever taking half the growth, the cross leg a rigid guide; the force along the line is the same F in both legs. The moment M is the moment at the leg roots (the corners of the loop); it reaches an anchor only where the leg ends at the anchor (an offset leg). The growth of the loop's own legs, which the cross leg has to absorb, is not checked. (Loop model.)
  A5. An offset leg is anchored at both ends: the run L bends for the growth of the leg (α·|ΔT|·H) as a second guided cantilever and is checked against the same Sa; the anchor force and moment reported are the larger of the leg's and the run's. (Offset-leg model.)
  A6. The material properties are single values: α the mean coefficient between the install and operating temperatures; E the modulus at the installation (reference) temperature, used for the stress check, as the piping codes do (ASME B31.3 para. 319.3.2 and 319.5, numbers to be checked against the owner's copy); E_hot, when given, is used only for the reactions on the anchors. Take them from a W3 material applied at the right temperature. (Material library; code convention to be checked.)
  A7. Sa and K1 are the owner's records from the owner's own copy of the piping code (or the design basis), with their provenance; no allowable stress, K1 or code constant ships. The sheet applies the relation to the numbers entered and does not know which code, material or load case they belong to. (No table from a standard is shipped.)
  A8. No friction, weight, spring-hanger or cold-spring effect is included in the leg force. The axial bellows anchor force is the pressure thrust P·A_eff plus the spring force k·ΔL: guide friction and any other bellows load come from the manufacturer's data. (Stated limit.)
  A9. E (Young's modulus of the pipe material at the installation (reference) temperature, for the stress check) is taken as 210 GPa. (W3 seed 'Carbon steel (generic)', indicative; replace with the project's material record.)
  A10. alpha (Mean coefficient of thermal expansion between the install and operating temperatures) is taken as 12 um/(m*K). (W3 seed 'Carbon steel (generic)' (12e-6 1/K), indicative; replace with the project's material record.)
  A11. temp_mode (Temperature change from) is taken as install and operating temperatures. (Install and operating temperatures are both known.)
  A12. T_install (Temperature at installation (when the run is anchored)) is taken as 10 degC. (Mean site temperature when the main is anchored.)
  A13. Sa (Allowable displacement-stress range Sa for the leg (your piping code and its formula; no default; not needed for an axial bellows)) is taken as 100 MPa. (Invented illustrative allowable range; take Sa from the project's piping code and its formula.)
  A14. K_scr (Screening limit K1 for OD·Y/(L − U)², in mm²/m² (or in²/ft², …) from your piping code (no default; blank = screening not evaluated and the sheet does not pass)) is taken as 120 mm^2/m^2. (Invented illustrative screening limit; take K1 from the project's piping code.)
  A15. Layout: the U-loop is two legs of 4 m in series, each a guided cantilever taking half the movement (21 mm); the cross leg is a rigid guide and its width does not enter. Screening only — not a piping stress analysis. (Guided-cantilever model.)

4. WORKING
  [9] dT = T_operate - T_install
      = 80 degC − 10 degC
      = 70 K
        Temperature change, operating − install
  [11] E_r = E
      = 210 GPa
      = 210 GPa
        Modulus for the reactions (E, no E_hot given)
  [12] D_i = OD - 2 * t_wall
      = 88.9 mm − 2 × 3.2 mm
      = 82.5 mm
        Inside diameter
  [13] A_m = pi / 4 * (OD^2 - D_i^2)
      = π / 4 × ((88.9 mm)^2 − (82.5 mm)^2)
      = 861.55 mm²
        Metal cross-section
  [14] I_x = pi / 64 * (OD^4 - D_i^4)
      = π / 64 × ((88.9 mm)^4 − (82.5 mm)^4)
      = 79.206 cm⁴
        Second moment of area of the pipe section
  [15] Z_x = I_x / (OD / 2)
      = 79.206 cm⁴ / (88.9 mm / 2)
      = 17.819 cm³
        Section modulus
  [16] dL = alpha * L * abs(dT)
      = 12 um/(m·K) × 50 m × abs(70 K)
      = 42 mm
        Thermal growth between the anchors, as a magnitude
  [17] dL_signed = alpha * L * dT
      = 12 um/(m·K) × 50 m × 70 K
      = 42 mm
        Thermal movement with its sign: positive lengthens, negative shortens
  [18] sigma_res = E * alpha * abs(dT)
      = 210 GPa × 12 um/(m·K) × abs(70 K)
      = 176.4 MPa
        Stress in the fully restrained pipe (E at the installation temperature)
  [19] F_res = E_r * alpha * abs(dT) * A_m
      = 210 GPa × 12 um/(m·K) × abs(70 K) × 861.55 mm²
      = 151.98 kN
        Force on the anchors of the fully restrained pipe
  [22] u_res = sigma_res / Sa
      = 176.4 MPa / 100 MPa
      = 1.764
        Fully restrained stress as a fraction of Sa (above 1: the movement has to be absorbed)
  [23] Delta = dL / 2
      = 42 mm / 2
      = 21 mm
        Movement each leg of the U-loop absorbs (half of it)
  [24] L_req = sqrt(3 * E * OD * Delta / Sa)
      = sqrt(3 × 210 GPa × 88.9 mm × 21 mm / 100 MPa)
      = 3.4295 m
        Leg length at which the guided-cantilever bending stress reaches Sa
  [25] u_leg = L_req / H
      = 3.4295 m / 4 m
      = 0.85737
        Required leg over available leg
  [26] F_leg = 12 * E_r * I_x * Delta / H^3
      = 12 × 210 GPa × 79.206 cm⁴ × 21 mm / (4 m)^3
      = 654.93 N
        Force of the available leg on its anchors or guides
  [27] M_leg = 6 * E_r * I_x * Delta / H^2
      = 6 × 210 GPa × 79.206 cm⁴ × 21 mm / (4 m)^2
      = 1309.9 N·m
        Moment at the root of the available leg
  [28] sigma_leg = 3 * E * OD * Delta / H^2
      = 3 × 210 GPa × 88.9 mm × 21 mm / (4 m)^2
      = 73.509 MPa
        Bending stress at the root of the available leg (E at the installation temperature)
  [29] u_sigma = sigma_leg / Sa
      = 73.509 MPa / 100 MPa
      = 0.73509
        Bending stress over Sa (= u_leg²)
  [30] anchor_force = F_leg
      = 654.93 N
      = 654.93 N
        Force on the anchors (the force along the loop)
  [31] anchor_moment = M_leg
      = 1309.9 N·m
      = 1309.9 N·m
        Moment at the root of the loop's legs
  [32] L_dev = L + 2 * H
      = 50 m + 2 × 4 m
      = 58 m
        Developed length between the anchors, run plus two legs
  [33] U_a = L
      = 50 m
      = 50 m
        Straight distance between the anchors (the loop is in line)
  [34] Y_scr = alpha * abs(dT) * U_a
      = 12 um/(m·K) × abs(70 K) × 50 m
      = 42 mm
        Resultant displacement Y: the free growth between the anchors
  [35] scr = OD * Y_scr / (L_dev - U_a)^2
      = 88.9 mm × 42 mm / (58 m − 50 m)^2
      = 58.341 mm²/m²
        Screening ratio OD·Y/(L − U)²
  [37] u_scr = scr / K_scr
      = 58.341 mm²/m² / 120 mm²/m²
      = 0.48617
        Screening ratio over the K1 record
  [38] pass = K1 given and every check passes
      = 2 check(s)
      = 1
        1 when the leg, the run (offset leg) and the screening checks all pass; 0 when one fails or when no K1 was entered
