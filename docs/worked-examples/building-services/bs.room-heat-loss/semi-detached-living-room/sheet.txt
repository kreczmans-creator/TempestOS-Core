1. Scope and method
Calculates the U-values of built-up elements and the steady-state design heat loss of one room. It gives the fabric and ventilation loss, the room load for sizing emitters, the heat loss coefficient and an optional check against target U-values.
Element U-value: U = 1/(R_si + Σ d/λ + R_g + R_se), or the user's U override for ground floors and glazing Element fabric loss: Φ_i = A·U·f·(T_int − θ_adj), with θ_adj = θ_e where the element faces outside Thermal bridges: Φ_tb = y_tb·Σ(A·f)_ext·(T_int − θ_e) on the elements facing outside Fabric total: Φ_T = ΣΦ_i + Φ_tb Ventilation loss: q_v = n·V; Φ_V = 0.34·q_v·(T_int − θ_e), or (T_int − T_supply) through an MVHR supply, plus 0.34·n_inf·V·(T_int − θ_e) infiltration with MVHR Room load: Φ_HL = Φ_T + Φ_V (CIBSE DHDG method: × (1 + additional allowance)) Heat loss coefficient: HLC = Φ_HL/(T_int − θ_e); loss per floor area Φ_HL/A_floor U-value check: U against the target U of each element (the global target applies to built-up elements facing outside only; an element's own target applies wherever it faces)

3. Assumptions
  A1. Steady state: every temperature is constant, with no solar or internal gains, no thermal mass and no dynamic simulation. (Design heat loss, not an annual energy estimate.)
  A2. Layers are homogeneous and in series, with one-dimensional heat flow. (BS EN ISO 6946:2017 homogeneous-layer calculation.)
  A3. Bridged layers such as timber studs, mortar joints and fixings are not corrected for. (BS EN ISO 6946:2017 homogeneous-layer calculation.)
  A4. A thermal-bridge allowance y_tb is added instead of correcting bridged layers. (Stated.)
  A5. A blank Rsi is the conventional BS EN ISO 6946 value for the element's heat-flow direction. (Small conventional BS EN ISO 6946 set (three values), not a table of design data.)
  A6. The conventional Rsi is 0.13 m²K/W horizontal, 0.10 upward and 0.17 downward. (Small conventional BS EN ISO 6946 set (three values), not a table of design data.)
  A7. A blank Rse is 0.04 m²K/W for an element facing outside. (Small conventional BS EN ISO 6946 set, not a table of design data.)
  A8. Where an element faces another space, a blank Rse is the same as Rsi. (Small conventional BS EN ISO 6946 set, not a table of design data.)
  A9. The global target U applies to built-up elements facing outside only. (Scope of the target check: an internal partition or floor is checked only against a target entered on its own row.)
  A10. Ground-floor and glazing U-values are entered by the user as an override. (Stated limit.)
  A11. The calculator does not model ground heat transfer. (Stated limit.)
  A12. The air constant 0.34 Wh/(m³·K) is the conventional rounded ρ·c_p of air. (Derived: about 1.2 kg/m³ × 1005 J/(kg·K) ÷ 3600, not read from a table.)
  A13. Design temperatures, conductivities, surface resistances and the thermal-bridge allowance are the user's own records. (No table from a standard or a manufacturer is shipped.)
  A14. method (Calculation method (changes the citations; the DHDG option adds the allowance below)) is taken as BS EN 12831-1 elemental. (Elemental method; the design-team default.)
  A15. theta_e (External design temperature (location record)) is taken as -3 °C. (Illustrative location design temperature; take the project's own from its design-temperature record.)
  A16. y_tb (Thermal-bridge allowance on the area facing outside) is taken as 0.1 W/(m²·K). (Illustrative thermal-bridge allowance; replace with the project's bridging record.)
  A17. n (Air change rate) is taken as 0.8 1/h. (Natural ventilation allowance for a living room.)
  A18. Conventional BS EN ISO 6946 surface resistances (R_si 0.13 horizontal, 0.10 upward, 0.17 downward; R_se 0.04 outside, or the same as R_si where the element faces another space; m²K/W) are used where blank, for: Front wall, Gable wall, Party wall. (Small conventional set; enter your own where the construction needs other values.)

4. Working
  [8] R_1 = R_si + Σ d/λ + R_g + R_se    « BS EN ISO 6946
      = Front wall: 0.13 + (0.12202 + 3.3333 + 0.5 + 0.0325) + 0 + 0.04
      = 4.1579 m²·K/W
        Front wall: total thermal resistance
  [9] U_1 = 1/R    « BS EN ISO 6946
      = 1 / 4.1579
      = 0.24051 W/(m²·K)
        Front wall: U-value
  [10] Phi_1 = A·U·f·(T_int − θ_adj)    « BS EN 12831-1 cl. 6.3.2
      = 8.7 × 0.24051 × 1 × (21 − -3) °C
      = 0.050218 kW
        Front wall: fabric loss to θ_e = -3 °C
  [11] U_2 = U override (user-entered)
      = Window: 1.4 W/m²K
      = 1.4 W/(m²·K)
        Window: U-value as entered (ground floor or glazing)
  [12] Phi_2 = A·U·f·(T_int − θ_adj)    « BS EN 12831-1 cl. 6.3.2
      = 2.1 × 1.4 × 1 × (21 − -3) °C
      = 0.07056 kW
        Window: fabric loss to θ_e = -3 °C
  [13] R_3 = R_si + Σ d/λ + R_g + R_se    « BS EN ISO 6946
      = Gable wall: 0.13 + (0.12202 + 3.3333 + 0.5 + 0.0325) + 0 + 0.04
      = 4.1579 m²·K/W
        Gable wall: total thermal resistance
  [14] U_3 = 1/R    « BS EN ISO 6946
      = 1 / 4.1579
      = 0.24051 W/(m²·K)
        Gable wall: U-value
  [15] Phi_3 = A·U·f·(T_int − θ_adj)    « BS EN 12831-1 cl. 6.3.2
      = 8.64 × 0.24051 × 1 × (21 − -3) °C
      = 0.049872 kW
        Gable wall: fabric loss to θ_e = -3 °C
  [16] R_4 = R_si + Σ d/λ + R_g + R_se    « BS EN ISO 6946
      = Party wall: 0.13 + (0.0325 + 0.16667 + 0.16667 + 0.0325) + 0.18 + 0.13
      = 0.83833 m²·K/W
        Party wall: total thermal resistance
  [17] U_4 = 1/R    « BS EN ISO 6946
      = 1 / 0.83833
      = 1.1928 W/(m²·K)
        Party wall: U-value
  [18] Phi_4 = A·U·f·(T_int − θ_adj)    « BS EN 12831-1 cl. 6.3.2
      = 8.64 × 1.1928 × 1 × (21 − 18) °C
      = 0.030918 kW
        Party wall: fabric loss to θ_adj = 18 °C
  [19] U_5 = U override (user-entered)
      = Ground floor: 0.25 W/m²K
      = 0.25 W/(m²·K)
        Ground floor: U-value as entered (ground floor or glazing)
  [20] Phi_5 = A·U·f·(T_int − θ_adj)    « BS EN 12831-1 cl. 6.3.2
      = 16.2 × 0.25 × 1 × (21 − -3) °C
      = 0.0972 kW
        Ground floor: fabric loss to θ_e = -3 °C
  [21] A_fab = Σ A
      = 44.28 m²
      = 44.28 m²
        Total fabric area
  [22] A_ext = Σ A facing outside
      = 35.64 m²
      = 35.64 m²
        Fabric area facing outside
  [23] A_tb = Σ A·f facing outside    « BS EN 12831-1 cl. 6.3.2
      = 35.64 m²
      = 35.64 m²
        Fabric area facing outside weighted by the temperature factor (thermal-bridge allowance applies)
  [24] U_m = Σ A·U / Σ A
      = 21.467 / 44.28
      = 0.48479 W/(m²·K)
        Area-weighted mean U-value of the fabric (without the temperature factor f)
  [25] U_eff = Σ A·U·f / Σ A
      = 21.467 / 44.28
      = 0.48479 W/(m²·K)
        Heat-loss weighted mean U-value of the fabric (includes the temperature factor f)
  [26] dT = T_int - theta_e
      = 21 degC − (-3 degC)
      = 24 K
        Design temperature difference, inside to outside
  [27] Phi_el = Σ Φ_i    « BS EN 12831-1 cl. 6.3.2
      = 298.77 W
      = 0.29877 kW
        Fabric loss through the elements
  [28] Phi_tb = y_tb * A_tb * dT    « BS EN 12831-1 cl. 6.3.2
      = 0.1 W/(m²·K) × 35.64 m² × 24 K
      = 0.085536 kW
        Thermal-bridge allowance
  [29] Phi_T = Phi_el + Phi_tb    « BS EN 12831-1 cl. 6.3.2
      = 0.29877 kW + 0.085536 kW
      = 0.3843 kW
        Fabric heat loss
  [30] q_v = n * V
      = 0.8 1/h × 38.88 m³
      = 31.104 m³/h
        Ventilation air flow
  [31] dT_v = dT
      = 24 K
      = 24 K
        Ventilation temperature difference, inside to outside
  [32] Phi_V = 0.34 Wh/(m^3*K) * q_v * dT_v    « BS EN 12831-1 cl. 6.3.3
      = 0.34 Wh/(m^3*K) × 31.104 m³/h × 24 K
      = 0.25381 kW
        Ventilation heat loss (0.34 Wh/m³K = conventional ρ·c_p of air, derived)
  [33] Phi_HL = Phi_T + Phi_V    « BS EN 12831-1 cl. 6.3.2
      = 0.3843 kW + 0.25381 kW
      = 0.63811 kW
        Room design heat loss
  [34] HLC = Phi_HL / dT
      = 0.63811 kW / 24 K
      = 26.588 W/K
        Heat loss coefficient
  [36] Phi_A = Phi_HL / A_floor
      = 0.63811 kW / 16.2 m²
      = 39.39 W/m²
        Room design heat loss per floor area
  [37] U_target_1 = target U of the element
      = the global target U_target
      = 0.3 W/(m²·K)
        Front wall: target U-value
  [38] u_target_1 = U / U_target
      = 0.24051 / 0.3
      = 0.8017
        Front wall: utilisation of the target U-value
  [39] U_target_3 = target U of the element
      = the global target U_target
      = 0.3 W/(m²·K)
        Gable wall: target U-value
  [40] u_target_3 = U / U_target
      = 0.24051 / 0.3
      = 0.8017
        Gable wall: utilisation of the target U-value
  [41] U_target_4 = target U of the element
      = the target entered on the element's row
      = 1.2 W/(m²·K)
        Party wall: target U-value
  [42] u_target_4 = U / U_target
      = 1.1928 / 1.2
      = 0.99404
        Party wall: utilisation of the target U-value
