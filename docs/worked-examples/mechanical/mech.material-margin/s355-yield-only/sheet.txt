1. SCOPE AND METHOD
The factors of safety of a material against an applied (equivalent) stress on its yield and, when given, its ultimate strength, against a required factor; the strengths can be filled from a released material record so the margin cites its source.
n_y = f_y/σ; n_u = f_u/σ. The governing factor is the lower of n_y and n_u/k_u, where k_u (≥ 1) scales the ultimate margin to the yield basis; u = n_req/n_gov.
  Ref: Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design §1-10 — Design factor and factor of safety

2. INPUTS
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  sigma | Applied (equivalent) stress | 250 | MPa | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | 0.01 … 5000 MPa
  f_y | Yield strength | 355 | MPa | drawing: DRG-BR-702 rev A | 1 … 5000 MPa
  k_u | Ratio of the ultimate to the yield safety factor required | 1.5 |  | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 1 … 3
  n_req | Required factor of safety on yield | 1.5 |  | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 1 … 10

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Static loading; the applied stress is the equivalent stress at the critical point. (Fatigue and stability are separate checks.)

4. WORKING
  [5] n_y = f_y / sigma    « Shigley §1-10
      = 355 MPa / 250 MPa
      = 1.42
        Factor of safety on yield
  [6] n_gov = n_y
      = 1.42
      = 1.42
        Governing factor (yield only)
  [7] u = n_req / n_gov
      = 1.5 / 1.42
      = 1.0563
        Required over governing factor of safety

5. RESULTS
  n_y = 1.42    Factor on yield
  n_gov = 1.42    Governing factor
  u = 1.0563    Utilisation

6. CHECKS (governing first)
  FAIL  Factor of safety against the required factor: u = 1.0563 vs u ≤ 1  utilisation 1.06  « Shigley §1-10
