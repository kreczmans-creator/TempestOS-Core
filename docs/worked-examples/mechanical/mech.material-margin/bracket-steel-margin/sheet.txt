1. SCOPE AND METHOD
The factors of safety of a material against an applied (equivalent) stress on its yield and, when given, its ultimate strength, against a required factor; the strengths can be filled from a released material record so the margin cites its source.
Factor of safety against yield and against ultimate: n_y = f_y/σ; n_u = f_u/σ. Governing factor, the ultimate margin scaled to the yield basis by k_u ≥ 1: n_gov = min(n_y, n_u/k_u). Utilisation against the required factor: u = n_req/n_gov.
  Ref: Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design §1-10 — Design factor and factor of safety

2. INPUTS
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  sigma | Applied (equivalent) stress | 120 | MPa | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | 0.01 … 5000 MPa
  f_y | Yield strength | 300 | MPa | drawing: DRG-BR-701 rev A | 1 … 5000 MPa
  f_u | Ultimate tensile strength (optional) | 450 | MPa | drawing: DRG-BR-701 rev A | 1 … 5000 MPa
  k_u | Ratio of the ultimate to the yield safety factor required | 1.5 |  | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 1 … 3
  n_req | Required factor of safety on yield | 1.5 |  | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 1 … 10

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Static loading; the applied stress is the equivalent stress at the critical point. (Fatigue and stability are separate checks.)

4. WORKING
  [5] n_y = f_y / sigma    « Shigley §1-10
      = 300 MPa / 120 MPa
      = 2.5
        Factor of safety on yield
  [7] n_u = f_u / sigma
      = 450 MPa / 120 MPa
      = 3.75
        Factor of safety on ultimate strength
  [8] n_gov = min(n_y, n_u / k_u)
      = min(2.5, 3.75 / 1.5)
      = 2.5
        Governing factor (ultimate margin scaled to the yield basis)
  [9] u = n_req / n_gov
      = 1.5 / 2.5
      = 0.6
        Required over governing factor of safety

5. RESULTS
  n_u = 3.75    Factor on ultimate
  n_y = 2.5    Factor on yield
  n_gov = 2.5    Governing factor
  u = 0.6    Utilisation
