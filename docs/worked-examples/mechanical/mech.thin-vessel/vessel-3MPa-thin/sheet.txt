Title: Thin cylindrical shell under internal pressure
Author: TempestOS    Authored: —

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Minimum shell thickness of a thin cylindrical pressure vessel under internal pressure for circumferential and longitudinal stress, the governing required thickness with corrosion allowance and design factor, the hoop stress at the corroded thickness provided, and the provided thickness against the requirement.
t_c = P·R/(S·E − 0.6·P) (circumferential); t_l = P·R/(2·S·E + 0.4·P) (longitudinal). t_req = n·max(t_c, t_l) + c. σ_θ = P·(R + 0.6·t)/(E·t) at t = t_p − c. Valid while t_c ≤ R/2 and P ≤ 0.385·S·E.
  Ref: ASME BPVC Section VIII Division 1 UG-27(c)(1) — Thickness of shells under internal pressure, circumferential stress
  Ref: ASME BPVC Section VIII Division 1 UG-27(c)(2) — Thickness of shells under internal pressure, longitudinal stress

2. INPUTS
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  P | Internal design pressure | 3 | MPa | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | 0.01 … 100 MPa
  R | Inside radius (corroded) | 750 | mm | drawing: DRG-PV-602 rev A | 5 … 10000 mm
  S | Allowable stress at design temperature | 138 | MPa | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 10 … 1000 MPa
  E_j | Joint efficiency | 1 |  | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 0.45 … 1
  n | Design factor on the computed thickness (legacy; 1 for none) | 1 |  | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 1 … 5
  c | Corrosion allowance | 1.5 | mm | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 0 … 25 mm
  t_p | Shell thickness provided (nominal, before corrosion) | 15 | mm | drawing: DRG-PV-602 rev A | 0.5 … 500 mm

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Cylindrical shell away from heads, nozzles and supports; membrane stresses only. (UG-27 scope.)
  A2. The joint efficiency reduces the allowable stress uniformly. (Code-form simplification carried from the legacy module.)

4. WORKING
  [8] t_c = P * R / (S * E_j - 0.6 * P)    « ASME VIII-1 UG-27(c)(1)
      = 3 MPa × 750 mm / (138 MPa × 1 − 0.6 × 3 MPa)
      = 16.52 mm
        Thickness for circumferential stress
  [9] t_l = P * R / (2 * S * E_j + 0.4 * P)    « ASME VIII-1 UG-27(c)(2)
      = 3 MPa × 750 mm / (2 × 138 MPa × 1 + 0.4 × 3 MPa)
      = 8.1169 mm
        Thickness for longitudinal stress
  [10] t_req = n * max(t_c, t_l) + c
      = 1 × max(16.52 mm, 8.1169 mm) + 1.5 mm
      = 18.02 mm
        Required nominal thickness (design factor and corrosion allowance included)
  [11] t_corr = t_p - c
      = 15 mm − 1.5 mm
      = 13.5 mm
        Corroded thickness provided
  [12] sigma_h = P * (R + 0.6 * t_corr) / (E_j * t_corr)    « ASME VIII-1 UG-27(c)(1)
      = 3 MPa × (750 mm + 0.6 × 13.5 mm) / (1 × 13.5 mm)
      = 168.47 MPa
        Hoop stress at the corroded thickness provided
  [13] u = t_req / t_p
      = 18.02 mm / 15 mm
      = 1.2013
        Required over provided thickness

5. RESULTS
  t_c = 16.52 mm    Thickness, circumferential stress
  t_l = 8.1169 mm    Thickness, longitudinal stress
  t_req = 18.02 mm    Required nominal thickness
  sigma_h = 168.47 MPa    Hoop stress at the corroded thickness
  u = 1.2013    Thickness utilisation
