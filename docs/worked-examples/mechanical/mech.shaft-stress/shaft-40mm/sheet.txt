Title: Shaft under combined bending and torsion
Author: TempestOS    Authored: —
Checker: —    Checked: —
Issued: —

1. Scope and method
Calculates the bending and torsional stresses in a solid round shaft, with stress-concentration factors, and the factors of safety against yield. It covers static loading only, not fatigue or hollow shafts.
Bending stress, with its stress concentration: σ = K_t·32M/(πd³). Torsional shear stress, with its stress concentration: τ = K_ts·16T/(πd³). Maximum shear stress (Tresca): τ_max = √((σ/2)² + τ²). von Mises equivalent stress: σ' = √(σ² + 3τ²). Factors of safety against yield, the lower governing: n_T = (S_y/2)/τ_max; n_VM = S_y/σ'.

2. Inputs
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  d | Shaft diameter | 40 | mm | drawing: DRG-SH-401 rev A | 1 … 2000 mm
  S_y | Yield strength | 350 | MPa | drawing: DRG-SH-401 rev A | 50 … 3000 MPa
  M | Bending moment (magnitude) | 500 | N·m | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | 0 … 10000000 N·m
  T | Torque (magnitude) | 800 | N·m | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | 0 … 10000000 N·m
  K_t | Bending stress-concentration factor (1 for none) | 1 |  | drawing: DRG-SH-401 rev A | 1 … 5
  K_ts | Torsional stress-concentration factor (1 for none) | 1 |  | drawing: DRG-SH-401 rev A | 1 … 5
  n_req | Required factor of safety | 2 |  | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 1 … 10

3. Assumptions
  A1. The shaft is a solid round section, linear elastic, under static loading. (The stress formulas of the method assume this.)

4. Working
  [8] sigma = K_t * 32 * M / (pi * d^3)    « Shigley §7-4
      = 1 × 32 × 500 N·m / (π × (40 mm)^3)
      = 79.577 MPa
        Bending stress
  [9] tau = K_ts * 16 * T / (pi * d^3)    « Shigley §7-4
      = 1 × 16 × 800 N·m / (π × (40 mm)^3)
      = 63.662 MPa
        Torsional shear stress
  [10] tau_max = sqrt((sigma / 2)^2 + tau^2)    « Shigley §5-4
      = sqrt((79.577 MPa / 2)^2 + (63.662 MPa)^2)
      = 75.073 MPa
        Maximum shear stress (Tresca)
  [11] sigma_vm = sqrt(sigma^2 + 3 * tau^2)    « Shigley §5-5
      = sqrt((79.577 MPa)^2 + 3 × (63.662 MPa)^2)
      = 135.98 MPa
        von Mises stress
  [12] n_T = (S_y / 2) / tau_max
      = 350 MPa / 2 / 75.073 MPa
      = 2.3311
        Factor of safety, maximum shear stress
  [13] n_VM = S_y / sigma_vm
      = 350 MPa / 135.98 MPa
      = 2.5739
        Factor of safety, distortion energy
  [14] n_min = min(n_T, n_VM)
      = min(2.3311, 2.5739)
      = 2.3311
        Governing factor of safety
