Title: Shaft under combined bending and torsion

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Bending and torsional stresses in a solid round shaft with stress-concentration factors, the maximum shear (Tresca) and von Mises equivalent stresses, and the factors of safety against yield, against a required factor.
Bending stress, with its stress concentration: σ = K_t·32M/(πd³). Torsional shear stress, with its stress concentration: τ = K_ts·16T/(πd³). Maximum shear stress (Tresca): τ_max = √((σ/2)² + τ²). von Mises equivalent stress: σ' = √(σ² + 3τ²). Factors of safety against yield, the lower governing: n_T = (S_y/2)/τ_max; n_VM = S_y/σ'.
  Ref: Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design §5-4, §5-5 — Maximum-shear-stress and distortion-energy theories
  Ref: Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design §7-4 — Shaft design for stress

2. INPUTS
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  d | Shaft diameter | 25 | mm | drawing: DRG-SH-402 rev A | 1 … 2000 mm
  M | Bending moment (magnitude) | 120 | N*m | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | 0 … 10000000 N*m
  T | Torque (magnitude) | 60 | N*m | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | 0 … 10000000 N*m
  S_y | Yield strength | 250 | MPa | drawing: DRG-SH-402 rev A | 50 … 3000 MPa
  K_t | Bending stress-concentration factor (1 for none) | 1.6 |  | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 1 … 5
  K_ts | Torsional stress-concentration factor (1 for none) | 1.3 |  | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 1 … 5
  n_req | Required factor of safety | 1.5 |  | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 1 … 10

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Solid round section, linear elastic; static loading. (Fatigue is mech.fatigue-miner's job; hollow shafts are not covered.)

4. WORKING
  [8] sigma = K_t * 32 * M / (pi * d^3)    « Shigley §7-4
      = 1.6 × 32 × 120 N·m / (π × (25 mm)^3)
      = 125.16 MPa
        Bending stress
  [9] tau = K_ts * 16 * T / (pi * d^3)    « Shigley §7-4
      = 1.3 × 16 × 60 N·m / (π × (25 mm)^3)
      = 25.424 MPa
        Torsional shear stress
  [10] tau_max = sqrt((sigma / 2)^2 + tau^2)    « Shigley §5-4
      = sqrt((125.16 MPa / 2)^2 + (25.424 MPa)^2)
      = 67.549 MPa
        Maximum shear stress (Tresca)
  [11] sigma_vm = sqrt(sigma^2 + 3 * tau^2)    « Shigley §5-5
      = sqrt((125.16 MPa)^2 + 3 × (25.424 MPa)^2)
      = 132.68 MPa
        von Mises stress
  [12] n_T = (S_y / 2) / tau_max
      = 250 MPa / 2 / 67.549 MPa
      = 1.8505
        Factor of safety, maximum shear stress
  [13] n_VM = S_y / sigma_vm
      = 250 MPa / 132.68 MPa
      = 1.8842
        Factor of safety, distortion energy
  [14] n_min = min(n_T, n_VM)
      = min(1.8505, 1.8842)
      = 1.8505
        Governing factor of safety
  [15] u = n_req / n_min
      = 1.5 / 1.8505
      = 0.81059
        Required over governing factor of safety

5. RESULTS
  sigma = 125.16 MPa    Bending stress
  tau = 25.424 MPa    Torsional shear
  tau_max = 67.549 MPa    Maximum shear stress
  sigma_vm = 132.68 MPa    von Mises stress
  n_T = 1.8505    Factor of safety (Tresca)
  n_VM = 1.8842    Factor of safety (von Mises)
  n_min = 1.8505    Governing factor of safety
