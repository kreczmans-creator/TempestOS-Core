Title: Thermal expansion of a restrained bar
Author: TempestOS    Authored: —

1. Scope and method
A bar heated or cooled between two restraints: its free movement, the part closed by a gap before the restraint engages, the restraint force for a rigid or an elastic restraint, the axial stress that results and the movement that actually occurs, with the stress against an allowable.
Free expansion of the bar: δ_free = α·L·ΔT. Expansion actually restrained, once any gap g has closed: δ_e = sign(δ_free)·max(|δ_free| − g, 0). Axial stiffness of the bar: k_bar = E·A/L. Restraint force, from rigid supports or an elastic restraint k_s: F = δ_e·k_bar (rigid); F = δ_e/(1/k_bar + 1/k_s) (elastic). Thermal stress in the bar, compressive on heating: σ = −F/A. Net movement of the bar end: δ = δ_free − F/k_bar.

2. Inputs
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  L | Bar length | 2 | m | drawing: DRG-TX-502 rev A | 0.01 … 1000 m
  A | Cross-section area | 500 | mm² | drawing: DRG-TX-502 rev A | 1 … 1000000 mm²
  E | Modulus of elasticity | 200 | GPa | drawing: DRG-TX-502 rev A | 1 … 500 GPa
  alpha | Coefficient of thermal expansion | 0.000012 | 1/K | drawing: DRG-TX-502 rev A | 1e-7 … 0.0001 1/K
  dT | Temperature change (positive heating) | 60 | K | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | -300 … 1000 K
  g | Gap closed before the restraint engages | 0 | mm | drawing: DRG-TX-502 rev A | 0 … 1000 mm
  k_s | Restraint stiffness (leave empty for a rigid restraint) | 100 | kN/mm | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 0.001 … 10000000 kN/mm
  sigma_allow | Allowable stress | 150 | MPa | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 1 … 2000 MPa

3. Assumptions
  A1. The expansion coefficient is constant over the temperature change and the bar stays elastic. (Linear thermoelasticity.)
  A2. The bar is uniformly heated and does not buckle under the compressive force. (Check buckling separately (struct.column-buckling).)

4. Working
  [9] delta_free = alpha * L * dT    « Shigley §3-17
      = 0.000012 1/K × 2 m × 60 K
      = 1.44 mm
        Free thermal movement (positive on heating)
  [10] delta_e = delta_free - g
      = 1.44 mm − 0 mm
      = 1.44 mm
        Movement after the gap closes (engaged movement)
  [11] k_bar = E * A / L
      = 200 GPa × 500 mm² / 2 m
      = 50 kN/mm
        Axial stiffness of the bar
  [12] F = delta_e / (1 / k_bar + 1 / k_s)    « Roark Ch. 15
      = 1.44 mm / (1 / 50 kN/mm + 1 / 100 kN/mm)
      = 48000 N
        Restraint force (elastic restraint in series with the bar)
  [13] sigma = -F / A
      = −48000 N / 500 mm²
      = -96 MPa
        Axial stress (negative compressive)
  [14] delta = delta_free - F / k_bar
      = 1.44 mm − 48000 N / 50 kN/mm
      = 0.48 mm
        Actual movement
  [15] u = abs(sigma) / sigma_allow
      = abs(-96 MPa) / 150 MPa
      = 0.64
        Stress utilisation
