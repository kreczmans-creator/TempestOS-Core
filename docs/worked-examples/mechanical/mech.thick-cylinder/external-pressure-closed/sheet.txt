Title: Thick-walled cylinder (Lamé)

1. Scope and method
Calculates the radial, hoop and axial stresses at the bore and outer surface of a thick-walled cylinder under internal and external pressure. It also gives von Mises and Tresca stresses, the utilisation and a thin-wall estimate.
Lamé constants from the bore and outside pressures: A = (a² p_i − b² p_o)/(b² − a²); B = a² b² (p_i − p_o)/(b² − a²). Radial and hoop stress at radius r: σ_r = A − B/r²; σ_θ = A + B/r². Axial stress, closed or open ends: σ_z = A (closed); σ_z = 0 (open). Equivalent stresses from the three principal stresses: von Mises; Tresca = the largest principal difference.

2. Inputs
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  closed | Closed ends (axial stress carried by the wall) | yes |  | drawing: DRG-CYL-02 rev B | any
  a | Inner radius | 40 | mm | drawing: DRG-CYL-02 rev B | 0.1 … 5000 mm
  b | Outer radius | 60 | mm | drawing: DRG-CYL-02 rev B | 0.1 … 5000 mm
  E | Young's modulus (the elastic collapse pressure under external pressure only) | 207 | GPa | assumption: Generic steel Young modulus for the elastic collapse pressure; take it from the material record of the tube. | 1 … 500 GPa
  nu | Poisson's ratio (the elastic collapse pressure under external pressure only) | 0.3 |  | assumption: Generic steel Poisson ratio for the elastic collapse pressure; take it from the material record of the tube. | 0.1 … 0.49
  p_i | Internal pressure | 0 | MPa | assumption: Vented bore. | 0 … 1000 MPa
  p_o | External pressure | 20 | MPa | spec: SPEC-SUB-1 §2 external pressure | 0 … 1000 MPa
  sigma_allow | Allowable stress | 100 | MPa | spec: SPEC-SUB-1 §4 | 1 … 3000 MPa

3. Assumptions
  A1. The cylinder is long, elastic and isotropic, and its stresses vary with radius only. (Lamé solution, with end effects neglected.)
  A2. Closed ends carry the pressure end load as a uniform axial stress. (σ_z = A.)
  A3. E (Young's modulus (the elastic collapse pressure under external pressure only)) is taken as 207 GPa. (Generic steel Young modulus for the elastic collapse pressure; take it from the material record of the tube.)
  A4. nu (Poisson's ratio (the elastic collapse pressure under external pressure only)) is taken as 0.3. (Generic steel Poisson ratio for the elastic collapse pressure; take it from the material record of the tube.)
  A5. p_i (Internal pressure) is taken as 0 MPa. (Vented bore.)

4. Working
  [9] A = (a^2 * p_i - b^2 * p_o) / (b^2 - a^2)    « Roark Table 13.5
      = ((40 mm)^2 × 0 MPa − (60 mm)^2 × 20 MPa) / ((60 mm)^2 − (40 mm)^2)
      = -36 MPa
        Lamé constant A
  [10] B = a^2 * b^2 * (p_i - p_o) / (b^2 - a^2)    « Roark Table 13.5
      = (40 mm)^2 × (60 mm)^2 × (0 MPa − 20 MPa) / ((60 mm)^2 − (40 mm)^2)
      = -57600 MPa·mm²
        Lamé constant B
  [11] sigma_r_a = A - B / a^2
      = (-36 MPa) − (-57600 MPa·mm²) / (40 mm)^2
      = 0 MPa
        Radial stress at the bore
  [12] sigma_t_a = A + B / a^2
      = -36 MPa + (-57600 MPa·mm²) / (40 mm)^2
      = -72 MPa
        Hoop stress at the bore
  [13] sigma_r_b = A - B / b^2
      = (-36 MPa) − (-57600 MPa·mm²) / (60 mm)^2
      = -20 MPa
        Radial stress at the outer surface
  [14] sigma_t_b = A + B / b^2
      = -36 MPa + (-57600 MPa·mm²) / (60 mm)^2
      = -52 MPa
        Hoop stress at the outer surface
  [15] sigma_z = A
      = -36 MPa
      = -36 MPa
        Axial stress (closed ends)
  [16] sigma_vm_a = sqrt(((sigma_t_a - sigma_r_a)^2 + (sigma_r_a - sigma_z)^2 + (sigma_z - sigma_t_a)^2) / 2)
      = sqrt((((-72 MPa) − 0 MPa)^2 + (0 MPa − (-36 MPa))^2 + ((-36 MPa) − (-72 MPa))^2) / 2)
      = 62.354 MPa
        von Mises stress at the bore
  [17] sigma_vm_b = sqrt(((sigma_t_b - sigma_r_b)^2 + (sigma_r_b - sigma_z)^2 + (sigma_z - sigma_t_b)^2) / 2)
      = sqrt((((-52 MPa) − (-20 MPa))^2 + ((-20 MPa) − (-36 MPa))^2 + ((-36 MPa) − (-52 MPa))^2) / 2)
      = 27.713 MPa
        von Mises stress at the outer surface
  [18] sigma_tr_a = max(sigma_t_a, sigma_r_a, sigma_z) - min(sigma_t_a, sigma_r_a, sigma_z)
      = max(-72 MPa, 0 MPa, -36 MPa) − min(-72 MPa, 0 MPa, -36 MPa)
      = 72 MPa
        Tresca stress at the bore
  [19] sigma_tr_b = max(sigma_t_b, sigma_r_b, sigma_z) - min(sigma_t_b, sigma_r_b, sigma_z)
      = max(-52 MPa, -20 MPa, -36 MPa) − min(-52 MPa, -20 MPa, -36 MPa)
      = 32 MPa
        Tresca stress at the outer surface
  [20] sigma_vm_max = max(sigma_vm_a, sigma_vm_b)
      = max(62.354 MPa, 27.713 MPa)
      = 62.354 MPa
        Maximum von Mises stress
  [21] u = sigma_vm_max / sigma_allow
      = 62.354 MPa / 100 MPa
      = 0.62354
        Utilisation
  [22] sigma_thin = (p_i - p_o) * (a + b) / 2 / (b - a)
      = (0 MPa − 20 MPa) × (40 mm + 60 mm) / 2 / (60 mm − 40 mm)
      = -50 MPa
        Thin-wall hoop estimate at the mean radius
  [23] k_wall = b / a
      = 60 mm / 40 mm
      = 1.5
        Wall ratio b/a
