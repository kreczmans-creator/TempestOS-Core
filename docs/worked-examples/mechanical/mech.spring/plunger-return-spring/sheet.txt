1. Scope and method
A cylindrical helical spring of round wire, cold-coiled, with a linear characteristic: compression (rate, working points, solid length by end type, stress at the working points and at solid, buckling, Goodman fatigue, surge frequency), extension (the same with the initial tension and the hook stresses) or torsion (rate with the legs, curvature-corrected bending stress, wound diameter and body length).
Index and rate: C = D/d; k = G·d⁴/(8·D³·n) (compression, extension); k_φ = E·d⁴/(64·D·n_a), n_a = n + (l₁ + l₂)/(3π·D) (torsion) Spring stress: τ_k = k_c·8·F·D/(π·d³), k_c = (C + 0.5)/(C − 0.75) (Bergsträsser) or (4C − 1)/(4C − 4) + 0.615/C (Wahl); torsion σ = q·32·M/(π·d³) with q = (C + 0.07)/(C − 0.75) (EN 13906-3, the default) or K_i = (4C² − C − 1)/(4C·(C − 1)) (Shigley) Solid length (compression): n_t = n + e_n; EN 13906-1 form L_s = d_max·(n_t + e_s) with (e_n, e_s) = (0, 1) plain, (1, 0) plain and ground, (2, 1.5) squared (closed, unground), (2, 0) squared and ground, and the minimum coil clearance S_a = (0.0015·D²/d + 0.1·d)·n checked at the working length (L₂ ≥ L_s + S_a); Shigley form L_s = d·(n_t + e_s) with e_s = 1 for squared; pitch p = (L₀ − a·d)/(n + b) Extension spring hook stresses: F = F₀ + k·s; hook bending σ_A = F·[K_A·16·D/(π·d³) + 4/(π·d²)], K_A = (4C₁² − C₁ − 1)/(4C₁·(C₁ − 1)), C₁ = 2r₁/d; hook torsion τ_B = K_B·8·F·D/(π·d³), K_B = (4C₂ − 1)/(4C₂ − 4), C₂ = 2r₂/d Buckling (compression): λ_eff = ν·L₀/D; y_cr = L₀·C₁′·[1 − √(1 − C₂′/λ_eff²)], C₁′ = E/(2(E − G)), C₂′ = 2π²(E − G)/(2G + E); stable at any deflection when λ_eff² < C₂′ Fatigue (Goodman, as M4): 1/n_f = a/S_e + m/S_u with a = (x₂ − x₁)/2, m = (x₂ + x₁)/2 of the stresses at the two working points; S_u = f_su·R_m in shear, R_m in bending; R_m typed or R_m = A/d^m from a W3 material (A in MPa, d in mm) Surge frequency: f_n = (d/(2π·n·D²))·√(G/(2ρ)); warning when f_n/f_op < 13 Torsion body: D_w = D·n/(n + φ/2π); L_w = d·(n + 1 + φ/2π)

3. Assumptions
  A1. Linear characteristic: the rate is constant between the working points; no progressive or conical spring, no variable pitch. (Cold-coiled round wire up to 20 mm.)
  A2. Active coils n are as entered; end coils are accounted for only in the solid length (and the pitch) by the end type; the hook and leg flexibility is in the rate only for the torsion legs. (Shigley 10th ed. Ch. 10, geometry of the ends.)
  A3. Solid length: the EN 13906-1 form (default) uses the maximum wire diameter d_max, adds the 1.5 d of an unground squared end and checks the minimum coil clearance S_a at the working length L₂; plain ends have no EN row and keep the geometric form. The Shigley form (nominal d, no clearance) is selectable. (EN 13906-1 relations written in own notation, to be checked against the owner's copy; Shigley 10th ed. Table 10-1.)
  A4. Torsion bending stress: the EN 13906-3 factor q = (C + 0.07)/(C − 0.75) is the default; Shigley's K_i = (4C² − C − 1)/(4C(C − 1)) is selectable and about 1 % lower at C = 8. (EN 13906-3 relation written in own notation, to be checked against the owner's copy.)
  A5. R_m(d) and the endurance strength are the user's inputs from the user's own copy of the wire standard and design reference, with no default. The permissible stress fractions f_τ, f_σ and f_su do default (0.45, 0.75, 0.67), to generic textbook fractions that the user replaces with the licensed EN 13906 value. R_m(d) can instead be taken from the curve R_m = A/d^m of a W3 material (revision-pinned; A in MPa for d in mm, the diameter range of the fit is the user's to respect). No wire-strength table and no permissible-stress diagram is shipped. (No wire-strength table or permissible-stress diagram is shipped.)
  A6. The stress correction factor k_c is applied to the working and the solid stress alike (conservative for the static check). (Choose 'None (uncorrected)' for the uncorrected static stress; the fatigue stresses (Goodman) always carry the curvature factor, the Bergsträsser one when 'None' is chosen.)
  A7. Extension: hook fatigue and the hook shape beyond its two radii are not assessed; the initial tension is a force the user enters. (Shigley 10th ed. Ch. 10, extension springs.)
  A8. Surge is the first natural frequency of a spring with both ends fixed; a spring with one end free has half of it. (Shigley 10th ed. Ch. 10, critical frequency.)
  A9. G (Shear modulus) is taken as 79.3 GPa. (Generic spring steel shear modulus; take it from the W3 material of the wire certificate.)
  A10. E (Young's modulus) is taken as 206 GPa. (Generic spring steel Young's modulus; take it from the W3 material of the wire certificate.)
  A11. rho (Density) is taken as 7850 kg/m³. (Generic steel density.)
  A12. rm_source (Where R_m(d) comes from: typed (no default: the wire certificate or your wire standard) or the curve R_m = A/d^m of a W3 material) is taken as Typed R_m(d). (R_m(d) typed for this example; a W3 material with the curve R_m = A/d^m can be applied instead.)
  A13. Rm (Tensile strength of the wire at this diameter R_m(d), typed (no default)) is taken as 1650 MPa. (Example value for a 3 mm wire. Enter R_m(d) from your own copy of the wire standard or the wire certificate (record spring.wire-strength); the standard's table is not shipped.)
  A14. f_tau (Permissible shear stress as a fraction of R_m (generic textbook default; replace with the licensed EN 13906 value)) is taken as 0.45. (Static permissible shear stress fraction of R_m for the example; take it from your design reference (record spring.permissible).)
  A15. f_su (Ultimate shear strength as a fraction of R_m (Goodman, shear; generic textbook default, replace with the licensed EN 13906 value)) is taken as 0.67. (Ultimate shear strength fraction of R_m used by the Goodman relation (textbook steel value); confirm in your reference.)
  A16. S_e (Endurance strength for the stress kind (shear; bending in torsion) at N_c cycles, alternating (no default: from the wire maker or your fatigue reference)) is taken as 450 MPa. (Example alternating shear endurance strength at 10^7 cycles; take it from the wire maker or your fatigue reference (record spring.endurance).)

4. Working
  [7] C = D / d    « EN 13906-1 / EN 13906-2 / EN 13906-3
      = 24 mm / 3 mm
      = 8
        Spring index
  [15] k_c = (C + 0.5) / (C - 0.75)
      = (8 + 0.5) / (8 − 0.75)
      = 1.1724
        Shear stress correction factor (Bergsträsser)
  [16] k_c_fat = k_c
      = 1.1724
      = 1.1724
        Shear stress correction factor for the fatigue stresses (as the static stress)
  [17] tau_perm = f_tau * Rm
      = 0.45 × 1650 MPa
      = 742.5 MPa
        Permissible shear stress (fraction of R_m, user input)
  [20] k = G * d^4 / (8 * D^3 * n)    « EN 13906-1
      = 79.3 GPa × (3 mm)^4 / (8 × (24 mm)^3 × 9)
      = 6.4535 N/mm
        Spring rate
  [21] n_t = n + 2
      = 9 + 2
      = 11
        Total coils (derived: end coils added to the active coils)
  [22] d_max = d
      = 3 mm
      = 3 mm
        Maximum wire diameter (none entered: the nominal d)
  [23] L_s = d_max * (n_t + 0.0)    « EN 13906-1
      = 3 mm × (11 + 0.0)
      = 33 mm
        Solid length L_c (EN 13906-1 form: maximum wire diameter)
  [24] S_a = (0.0015 * D^2 / d + 0.1 * d) * n    « EN 13906-1
      = (0.0015 × (24 mm)^2 / 3 mm + 0.1 × 3 mm) × 9
      = 5.292 mm
        Minimum sum of the coil clearances S_a
  [25] L_n = L_s + S_a    « EN 13906-1
      = 33 mm + 5.292 mm
      = 38.292 mm
        Minimum spring length L_n = L_c + S_a
  [26] p = (L0 - 2 * d) / (n + 0)
      = (70 mm − 2 × 3 mm) / (9 + 0)
      = 7.1111 mm
        Pitch of the free spring (derived)
  [27] s_c = L0 - L_s
      = 70 mm − 33 mm
      = 37 mm
        Travel to solid
  [28] F_c = k * s_c
      = 6.4535 N/mm × 37 mm
      = 238.78 N
        Force at solid length
  [32] s_1 = F_1 / k
      = 60 N / 6.4535 N/mm
      = 9.2974 mm
        Deflection at working point 1
  [33] s_2 = F_2 / k
      = 180 N / 6.4535 N/mm
      = 27.892 mm
        Deflection at working point 2
  [34] tau_1 = k_c * 8 * F_1 * D / (pi * d^3)    « EN 13906-1
      = 1.1724 × 8 × 60 N × 24 mm / (π × (3 mm)^3)
      = 159.23 MPa
        Shear stress at working point 1
  [35] tau_2 = k_c * 8 * F_2 * D / (pi * d^3)    « EN 13906-1
      = 1.1724 × 8 × 180 N × 24 mm / (π × (3 mm)^3)
      = 477.68 MPa
        Shear stress at working point 2
  [36] tau_c = k_c * 8 * F_c * D / (pi * d^3)    « EN 13906-1
      = 1.1724 × 8 × 238.78 N × 24 mm / (π × (3 mm)^3)
      = 633.67 MPa
        Shear stress at solid length
  [37] u_2 = tau_2 / tau_perm
      = 477.68 MPa / 742.5 MPa
      = 0.64335
        Utilisation at working point 2
  [38] u_c = tau_c / tau_perm
      = 633.67 MPa / 742.5 MPa
      = 0.85343
        Utilisation at solid length
  [39] L_1 = L0 - s_1
      = 70 mm − 9.2974 mm
      = 60.703 mm
        Spring length at working point 1
  [40] L_2 = L0 - s_2
      = 70 mm − 27.892 mm
      = 42.108 mm
        Spring length at working point 2
  [41] u_s = s_2 / s_c
      = 27.892 mm / 37 mm
      = 0.75384
        Working deflection / travel to solid
  [42] s_n = L0 - L_n
      = 70 mm − 38.292 mm
      = 31.708 mm
        Travel to the minimum length L_n
  [43] u_n = s_2 / s_n
      = 27.892 mm / 31.708 mm
      = 0.87965
        Working deflection / travel to the minimum length (coil clearance at L₂)
  [44] lambda_eff = ν·L₀/D    « EN 13906-1
      = 0.5 × 70 / 24
      = 1.4583
        Effective slenderness
  [45] C1p = E/(2(E − G))    « Shigley 10th ed. Ch. 10, buckling
      = 206 / (2 × (206 − 79.3))
      = 0.81294
        Buckling constant C₁′
  [46] C2p = 2π²(E − G)/(2G + E)    « Shigley 10th ed. Ch. 10, buckling
      = 2π² × (206 − 79.3) / (2 × 79.3 + 206)
      = 6.8595
        Buckling constant C₂′
  [47] u_b = stable at any deflection (λ_eff² < C₂′)
      = 2.1267 < 6.8595
      = 0
        Deflection at point 2 against the buckling deflection (none: stable)
  [48] f_n = d / (2 * pi * n * D^2) * sqrt(G / (2 * rho))    « EN 13906-1 / EN 13906-2
      = 3 mm / (2 × π × 9 × (24 mm)^2) × sqrt(79.3 GPa / (2 × 7850 kg/m³))
      = 207 Hz
        Surge (natural) frequency, both ends fixed
  [50] r_f = f_n / f_op
      = 207 Hz / 8 Hz
      = 25.875
        Surge frequency / operating frequency
  [55] tau_a = k_c_fat / k_c * (tau_2 - tau_1) / 2
      = 1.1724 / 1.1724 × (477.68 MPa − 159.23 MPa) / 2
      = 159.23 MPa
        Alternating shear stress
  [56] tau_m = k_c_fat / k_c * (tau_2 + tau_1) / 2
      = 1.1724 / 1.1724 × (477.68 MPa + 159.23 MPa) / 2
      = 318.46 MPa
        Mean shear stress
  [57] S_su = f_su * Rm
      = 0.67 × 1650 MPa
      = 1105.5 MPa
        Ultimate shear strength (fraction of R_m, user input)
  [58] u_f = tau_a/S_e + tau_m/S_su    « Goodman, as M4 (Shigley 10th ed. Ch. 6)
      = 159.23 / 450 + 318.46 / 1105.5
      = 0.64191
        Goodman utilisation (1/n_f)
  [59] n_f = 1/u_f
      = 1 / 0.64191
      = 1.5579
        Goodman safety factor
  [60] u_fat = n_f_req * u_f
      = 1.2 × 0.64191
      = 0.77029
        Fatigue utilisation against the required factor
