Title: Rolling bearing basic rating life (simple form)

1. Scope and method
Calculates the equivalent dynamic load, the basic rating life L₁₀ and the life modified for reliability of a rolling bearing, against a required life. It takes the reliability factor as entered and has no a_ISO factor.
Equivalent dynamic load from the radial and axial loads: F_dyn = X·F_r + Y·F_a. Basic rating life in millions of revolutions (p = 3 for ball and 10/3 for roller bearings): L₁₀ = (C/F_dyn)^p. Rating life in hours, at speed n: L₁₀h = L₁₀·10⁶ revolutions at speed n. Life adjusted for the required reliability: L_nm = a₁·L₁₀; L_nmh = a₁·L₁₀h. Range of validity of the life equation: F_dyn ≤ 0.5·C.

2. Inputs
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  type | Bearing type (selects the life exponent) | ball |  | drawing: DRG-GB-211 rev A | any
  C | Basic dynamic load rating | 25 | kN | drawing: DRG-GB-211 rev A | 0.1 … 100000 kN
  F_r | Radial load | 3 | kN | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | 0 … 100000 kN
  F_a | Axial load | 1.5 | kN | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | 0 … 100000 kN
  n | Speed | 1000 | rpm | spec: LOAD-SCHEDULE-01 | 1 … 100000 rpm
  X | Radial load factor (bearing catalogue) | 0.56 |  | drawing: DRG-GB-211 rev A | 0 … 2
  Y | Axial load factor (bearing catalogue) | 1.5 |  | drawing: DRG-GB-211 rev A | 0 … 5
  a_1 | Life modification factor for reliability | 0.64 |  | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 0.05 … 1
  L_req | Required life (0 for no criterion) | 2000 | h | spec: SPEC-MECH-03 design criteria | 0 … 1000000 h

3. Assumptions
  A1. Load and speed are steady, and the bearing is correctly fitted, lubricated and free of contamination. (These are the conditions of the basic rating life.)
  A2. X and Y are the catalogue factors for this bearing and load ratio. (Supplied by the user.)

4. Working
  [10] p = 3    « ISO 281:2007 cl. 6
      = 3
        Life exponent (ball bearing)
  [11] F_dyn = X * F_r + Y * F_a    « ISO 281:2007 cl. 6
      = 0.56 × 3 kN + 1.5 × 1.5 kN
      = 3.93 kN
        Equivalent dynamic load
  [12] k_P = F_dyn / C
      = 3.93 kN / 25 kN
      = 0.1572
        Load ratio of the equivalent load to the rating
  [13] L_10 = (C / F_dyn)^p    « ISO 281:2007 cl. 6
      = (25 kN / 3.93 kN)^3
      = 257.42
        Basic rating life (millions of revolutions)
  [14] L_10h = L_10 * 1e6 * 2 * pi / n
      = 257.42 × 1e6 × 2 × π / 1000 rpm
      = 4290.3 h
        Basic rating life in hours (10⁶ revolutions of 2π rad at speed n)
  [15] L_nm = a_1 * L_10
      = 0.64 × 257.42
      = 164.75
        Modified rating life (millions of revolutions)
  [16] L_nmh = a_1 * L_10h
      = 0.64 × 4290.3 h
      = 2745.8 h
        Modified rating life in hours
  [17] u_life = L_req / L_nmh
      = 2000 h / 2745.8 h
      = 0.72838
        Required over modified life

5. Results
  L_nmh = 2746 h    Modified life in hours
  L_10h = 4290 h    L₁₀ in hours
  F_dyn = 3.930 kN    Equivalent dynamic load
  k_P = 0.1572    Load ratio
  L_10 = 257.42    L₁₀ (millions of revolutions)
  L_nm = 164.75    Modified life (millions of revolutions)
