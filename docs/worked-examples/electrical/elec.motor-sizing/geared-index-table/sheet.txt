1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Torque, speed and power a motor needs to move a load through a mechanism on a point-to-point cycle: the load reflected to the motor shaft, the acceleration from the motion profile, peak torque during acceleration, RMS torque over the cycle (thermal duty), peak speed, inertia ratio, and a check of a candidate motor's rated and peak torque and speed with a safety margin.
Motor-to-load ratio of a linear mechanism, in metres per motor radian: k = p/(2π i) (screw); D/(2 i) (belt); D_p/(2 i) (rack). Load inertia reflected to the motor through a linear mechanism: J_refl = m k²/η. Load torque at the motor through a linear mechanism: T_load = F k/η, with F = m g (sin θ + μ cos θ) + F_p. Reflected inertia and torque of a rotary load through a gearbox: J_refl = J_L/(i² η); T_load = T_L/(i η). Move profile, trapezoidal 1/3–1/3–1/3 (adjustable): v_pk = s/(t_move − t_acc); a = v_pk/t_acc. Move profile, S-curve (sine acceleration, same peak speed): a_pk = (π/2) a_trap. Torque to accelerate the total inertia, and the peak torque: T_acc = J_total α; T_pk = T_acc + T_load. RMS (thermal) torque over the whole cycle, dwell included: T_rms = √(Σ T_i² t_i / t_cycle).
  Ref: Hughes & Drury, Electric Motors and Drives Ch. on motor and drive selection — Load torque, inertia referred to the motor, duty cycles
  Ref: Manufacturer servo-sizing guides (Kollmorgen, Parker) — Reflected inertia, RMS torque, inertia ratio practice

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Rigid transmission; the mechanism efficiency applies to the driving direction and is also used in the deceleration phase (conservative for peak, slightly pessimistic for RMS). (Servo-sizing practice.)
  A2. The friction force is Coulomb friction on the moved mass; viscous and breakaway friction are not modelled unless included in F_p. (Load model.)
  A3. The S-curve profile has sine-shaped acceleration over the same acceleration time, so its peak acceleration is π/2 times the trapezoidal value. (Jerk-limited profile idealisation.)
  A4. mechanism (Mechanism) is taken as gearbox rotary. (Sizing convention.)
  A5. J_mech (Transmission inertia referred to the motor shaft (screw, pulleys, gearbox, coupling)) is taken as 0 kg*m^2. (Sizing convention.)
  A6. T_L (Constant load torque at the output (rotary)) is taken as 0 N*m. (Sizing convention.)
  A7. profile (Motion profile) is taken as trapezoidal. (Sizing convention.)
  A8. SF (Safety margin on torque) is taken as 1.25. (Sizing convention.)
  A9. J_ratio_max (Inertia ratio limit (load : motor), warning) is taken as 10. (Sizing convention.)

4. WORKING
  [20] k = 1 / i
      = 1 / 10
      = 0.1
        Output radians per motor radian
  [21] T_load = T_L / (i * eta)
      = 0 N·m / (10 × 0.85)
      = 0 N·m
        Load torque at the motor shaft
  [22] J_refl = J_L / (i^2 * eta)
      = 0.5 kg·m² / (10^2 × 0.85)
      = 0.0058824 kg·m²
        Load inertia reflected to the motor shaft
  [23] theta_m = s * 2 * pi * i
      = 0.25 × 2 × π × 10
      = 15.708 rad
        Motor angle for the move
  [24] omega_pk = theta_m / (t_move * (1 - f_acc))
      = 15.708 rad / (0.3 s × (1 − 0.33333))
      = 78.54 rad/s
        Peak motor speed (trapezoid)
  [25] a_trap = omega_pk / (f_acc * t_move)
      = 78.54 rad/s / (0.33333 × 0.3 s)
      = 785.4 rad/s²
        Trapezoidal angular acceleration at the motor
  [27] a_pk = a_trap
      = 785.4 rad/s²
      = 785.4 rad/s²
        Peak acceleration
  [28] alpha_pk = a_pk
      = 785.4 rad/s²
      = 785.4 rad/s²
        Peak motor angular acceleration
  [29] J_total = J_motor + J_mech + J_refl
      = 0.0005 kg·m² + 0 kg·m² + 0.0058824 kg·m²
      = 0.0063824 kg·m²
        Total inertia at the motor shaft
  [30] T_acc = J_total * alpha_pk
      = 0.0063824 kg·m² × 785.4 rad/s²
      = 5.0127 N·m
        Acceleration torque
  [31] T_pk = T_acc + T_load
      = 5.0127 N·m + 0 N·m
      = 5.0127 N·m
        Peak torque (accelerating against the load)
  [32] T_dec = -T_acc + T_load
      = −5.0127 N·m + 0 N·m
      = -5.0127 N·m
        Torque during deceleration
  [33] n_pk = omega_pk
      = 78.54 rad/s
      = 750 rpm
        Peak motor speed
  [34] P_pk = T_pk * omega_pk
      = 5.0127 N·m × 78.54 rad/s
      = 0.3937 kW
        Peak mechanical power
  [35] t_acc = f_acc * t_move
      = 0.33333 × 0.3 s
      = 0.1 s
        Acceleration time
  [36] t_const = t_move - 2 * t_acc
      = 0.3 s − 2 × 0.1 s
      = 0.1 s
        Constant-velocity time
  [37] t_cycle = t_move + t_dwell
      = 0.3 s + 0.7 s
      = 1 s
        Cycle time
  [38] T_rms = sqrt((T_pk^2 * t_acc + T_load^2 * t_const + T_dec^2 * t_acc) / t_cycle)
      = sqrt(((5.0127 N·m)^2 × 0.1 s + (0 N·m)^2 × 0.1 s + (-5.0127 N·m)^2 × 0.1 s) / 1 s)
      = 2.2417 N·m
        RMS torque over the cycle (thermal)
  [39] J_ratio = (J_refl + J_mech) / J_motor
      = (0.0058824 kg·m² + 0 kg·m²) / 0.0005 kg·m²
      = 11.765
        Inertia ratio (load : motor)
  [40] u_peak = SF * T_pk / T_peak
      = 1.25 × 5.0127 N·m / 9 N·m
      = 0.69621
        Peak torque utilisation with safety margin
  [41] u_rms = SF * T_rms / T_rated
      = 1.25 × 2.2417 N·m / 3 N·m
      = 0.93406
        Continuous torque utilisation with safety margin
  [42] u_speed = n_pk / n_max
      = 750 rpm / 3000 rpm
      = 0.25
        Speed utilisation
  [43] duty = T_rms / T_rated
      = 2.2417 N·m / 3 N·m
      = 0.74725
        Thermal duty (without margin)
