1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Torque, speed and power a motor needs to move a load through a mechanism on a point-to-point cycle: the load reflected to the motor shaft, the acceleration from the motion profile, peak torque during acceleration, RMS torque over the cycle (thermal duty), peak speed, inertia ratio, and a check of a candidate motor's rated and peak torque and speed with a safety margin. A rotary load that runs continuously (a pump, a fan) is sized instead from its steady torque and speed and a run-up time.
Linear mechanisms: k = metres per motor radian (screw p/(2π i); belt D/(2 i); rack D_p/(2 i)); J_refl = m k²/η; T_load = F k/η with F = m g (sin θ + μ cos θ) + F_p. Rotary: J_refl = J_L/(i² η), T_load = T_L/(i η). Trapezoidal 1/3–1/3–1/3 (adjustable): v_pk = s/(t_move − t_acc), a = v_pk/t_acc; S-curve: sine acceleration with a_pk = (π/2) a_trap and the same v_pk. T_acc = J_total α; T_pk = T_acc + T_load; T_rms = √(Σ T_i² t_i / t_cycle) including the dwell. RMS mechanical power (the thermal-equivalent continuous power a supply sees; regeneration counts as power): P_rms = ω_pk √{[t_acc·(2/3)(T_load² + T_acc²) + T_load² t_const]/t_cycle} (trapezoid), with t_acc·[(3/4) T_load² + (5/16) T_acc²] for the S-curve; the instantaneous peak P_pk is not a continuous demand. Continuous rotary duty: ω = 2π n_L i/60; T_load = T_L/(i η); T_rms = T_load; T_pk = J_total ω/t_start + T_load (full load during the run-up, conservative); n_pk = n_L i; P_cont = T_load ω; P_rms = P_cont; P_rated = T_rated·2π n_rated/60, the candidate motor's nameplate rated shaft power from its rated torque and rated speed n_rated (not the running speed), the shaft power its supply must be sized for; without n_rated there is no P_rated and the sheet warns.
  Ref: Hughes & Drury, Electric Motors and Drives Ch. on motor and drive selection — Load torque, inertia referred to the motor, duty cycles
  Ref: Manufacturer servo-sizing guides (Kollmorgen, Parker) — Reflected inertia, RMS torque, inertia ratio practice

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Rigid transmission; the mechanism efficiency applies to the driving direction and is also used in the deceleration phase (conservative for peak, slightly pessimistic for RMS). (Servo-sizing practice.)
  A2. The friction force is Coulomb friction on the moved mass; viscous and breakaway friction are not modelled unless included in F_p. (Load model.)
  A3. Continuous duty: the load torque is constant at the stated speed and the motor accelerates the whole inertia linearly to speed in t_start while carrying the full load torque (conservative); the move distance, move time, acceleration fraction, dwell and profile are ignored. (Run-up idealisation for pumps and fans.)
  A4. The S-curve profile has sine-shaped acceleration over the same acceleration time, so its peak acceleration is π/2 times the trapezoidal value. (Jerk-limited profile idealisation.)
  A5. mechanism (Mechanism) is taken as gearbox rotary. (Sizing convention.)
  A6. J_mech (Transmission inertia referred to the motor shaft (screw, pulleys, gearbox, coupling)) is taken as 0 kg*m^2. (Sizing convention.)
  A7. T_L (Constant load torque at the output (rotary)) is taken as 0 N*m. (Sizing convention.)
  A8. profile (Motion profile) is taken as trapezoidal. (Sizing convention.)
  A9. SF (Safety margin on torque) is taken as 1.25. (Sizing convention.)
  A10. J_ratio_max (Inertia ratio limit (load : motor), warning) is taken as 10. (Sizing convention.)

4. WORKING
  [21] k = 1 / i
      = 1 / 10
      = 0.1
        Output radians per motor radian
  [22] T_load = T_L / (i * eta)
      = 0 N·m / (10 × 0.85)
      = 0 N·m
        Load torque at the motor shaft
  [23] J_refl = J_L / (i^2 * eta)
      = 0.5 kg·m² / (10^2 × 0.85)
      = 0.0058824 kg·m²
        Load inertia reflected to the motor shaft
  [24] theta_m = s * 2 * pi * i
      = 0.25 × 2 × π × 10
      = 15.708 rad
        Motor angle for the move
  [25] omega_pk = theta_m / (t_move * (1 - f_acc))
      = 15.708 rad / (0.3 s × (1 − 0.33333))
      = 78.54 rad/s
        Peak motor speed (trapezoid)
  [26] a_trap = omega_pk / (f_acc * t_move)
      = 78.54 rad/s / (0.33333 × 0.3 s)
      = 785.4 rad/s²
        Trapezoidal angular acceleration at the motor
  [28] a_pk = a_trap
      = 785.4 rad/s²
      = 785.4 rad/s²
        Peak acceleration
  [29] alpha_pk = a_pk
      = 785.4 rad/s²
      = 785.4 rad/s²
        Peak motor angular acceleration
  [30] J_total = J_motor + J_mech + J_refl
      = 0.0005 kg·m² + 0 kg·m² + 0.0058824 kg·m²
      = 0.0063824 kg·m²
        Total inertia at the motor shaft
  [31] T_acc = J_total * alpha_pk
      = 0.0063824 kg·m² × 785.4 rad/s²
      = 5.0127 N·m
        Acceleration torque
  [32] T_pk = T_acc + T_load
      = 5.0127 N·m + 0 N·m
      = 5.0127 N·m
        Peak torque (accelerating against the load)
  [33] T_dec = -T_acc + T_load
      = −5.0127 N·m + 0 N·m
      = -5.0127 N·m
        Torque during deceleration
  [34] n_pk = omega_pk
      = 78.54 rad/s
      = 750 rpm
        Peak motor speed
  [35] P_pk = T_pk * omega_pk
      = 5.0127 N·m × 78.54 rad/s
      = 0.3937 kW
        Peak mechanical power
  [36] t_acc = f_acc * t_move
      = 0.33333 × 0.3 s
      = 0.1 s
        Acceleration time
  [37] t_const = t_move - 2 * t_acc
      = 0.3 s − 2 × 0.1 s
      = 0.1 s
        Constant-velocity time
  [38] t_cycle = t_move + t_dwell
      = 0.3 s + 0.7 s
      = 1 s
        Cycle time
  [39] P_rms = omega_pk * sqrt((t_acc * (2 / 3) * (T_load^2 + T_acc^2) + T_load^2 * t_const) / t_cycle)
      = 78.54 rad/s × sqrt((0.1 s × 2 / 3 × ((0 N·m)^2 + (5.0127 N·m)^2) + (0 N·m)^2 × 0.1 s) / 1 s)
      = 0.10165 kW
        RMS mechanical power over the cycle (thermal-equivalent continuous power)
  [40] T_rms = sqrt((T_pk^2 * t_acc + T_load^2 * t_const + T_dec^2 * t_acc) / t_cycle)
      = sqrt(((5.0127 N·m)^2 × 0.1 s + (0 N·m)^2 × 0.1 s + (-5.0127 N·m)^2 × 0.1 s) / 1 s)
      = 2.2417 N·m
        RMS torque over the cycle (thermal)
  [41] J_ratio = (J_refl + J_mech) / J_motor
      = (0.0058824 kg·m² + 0 kg·m²) / 0.0005 kg·m²
      = 11.765
        Inertia ratio (load : motor)
  [42] u_peak = SF * T_pk / T_peak
      = 1.25 × 5.0127 N·m / 9 N·m
      = 0.69621
        Peak torque utilisation with safety margin
  [43] u_rms = SF * T_rms / T_rated
      = 1.25 × 2.2417 N·m / 3 N·m
      = 0.93406
        Continuous torque utilisation with safety margin
  [44] u_speed = n_pk / n_max
      = 750 rpm / 3000 rpm
      = 0.25
        Speed utilisation
  [45] duty = T_rms / T_rated
      = 2.2417 N·m / 3 N·m
      = 0.74725
        Thermal duty (without margin)
