1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Torque, speed and power a motor needs to move a load through a mechanism on a point-to-point cycle: the load reflected to the motor shaft, the acceleration from the motion profile, peak torque during acceleration, RMS torque over the cycle (thermal duty), peak speed, inertia ratio, and a check of a candidate motor's rated and peak torque and speed with a safety margin. A rotary load that runs continuously (a pump, a fan) is sized instead from its steady torque and speed and a run-up time.
Motor-to-load ratio of a linear mechanism, in metres per motor radian: k = p/(2π i) (screw); D/(2 i) (belt); D_p/(2 i) (rack). Load inertia reflected to the motor through a linear mechanism: J_refl = m k²/η. Load torque at the motor through a linear mechanism: T_load = F k/η, with F = m g (sin θ + μ cos θ) + F_p. Reflected inertia and torque of a rotary load through a gearbox: J_refl = J_L/(i² η); T_load = T_L/(i η). Move profile, trapezoidal 1/3–1/3–1/3 (adjustable): v_pk = s/(t_move − t_acc); a = v_pk/t_acc. Move profile, S-curve (sine acceleration, same peak speed): a_pk = (π/2) a_trap. Torque to accelerate the total inertia, and the peak torque: T_acc = J_total α; T_pk = T_acc + T_load. Alternating torque amplitude of a point-to-point cycle about T_load, for a shaft's fatigue: T_alt = (T_pk − T_dec)/2 (0 in continuous duty). Torque behind the ratio on the output shaft of a rotary mechanism: T_out = T·i·η (mean, alternating and peak), n_out = n_pk/i. Peak axial thrust of a ball screw on its support bearings: F_ax_pk = F_load + m a_pk (0 for other mechanisms). RMS (thermal) torque over the whole cycle, dwell included: T_rms = √(Σ T_i² t_i / t_cycle). RMS mechanical power over the cycle, the thermal-equivalent continuous power a supply sees (regeneration counts as power; the instantaneous peak P_pk is not a continuous demand), trapezoidal profile: P_rms = ω_pk √{[t_acc·(2/3)(T_load² + T_acc²) + T_load² t_const]/t_cycle}; for the S-curve the acceleration term becomes t_acc·[(3/4) T_load² + (5/16) T_acc²]. Continuous rotary duty (a pump or fan), speed at the motor: ω = 2π n_L i/60; n_pk = n_L i. Continuous rotary duty, load torque and RMS torque: T_load = T_L/(i η); T_rms = T_load. Peak torque during the run-up, with full load throughout (conservative): T_pk = J_total ω/t_start + T_load. Continuous power: P_cont = T_load ω; P_rms = P_cont. Rated shaft power of the candidate motor, from its rated torque and its rated speed n_rated (not the running speed), the shaft power its supply must be sized for; without n_rated there is no P_rated and the sheet warns: P_rated = T_rated·2π n_rated/60.
  Ref: Hughes & Drury, Electric Motors and Drives Ch. on motor and drive selection — Load torque, inertia referred to the motor, duty cycles
  Ref: Manufacturer servo-sizing guides (Kollmorgen, Parker) — Reflected inertia, RMS torque, inertia ratio practice

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Rigid transmission; the mechanism efficiency applies to the driving direction and is also used in the deceleration phase (conservative for peak, slightly pessimistic for RMS). (Servo-sizing practice.)
  A2. The friction force is Coulomb friction on the moved mass; viscous and breakaway friction are not modelled unless included in F_p. (Load model.)
  A3. Continuous duty: the load torque is constant at the stated speed and the motor accelerates the whole inertia linearly to speed in t_start while carrying the full load torque (conservative); the move distance, move time, acceleration fraction, dwell and profile are ignored. (Run-up idealisation for pumps and fans.)
  A4. The S-curve profile has sine-shaped acceleration over the same acceleration time, so its peak acceleration is π/2 times the trapezoidal value. (Jerk-limited profile idealisation.)
  A5. mechanism (Mechanism) is taken as lead / ball screw. (Sizing convention.)
  A6. i (Gear ratio i (motor : output, 1 = none)) is taken as 1. (Sizing convention.)
  A7. mu (Friction coefficient (guide / bearing)) is taken as 0.1. (Linear guides with seals: μ = 0.1.)
  A8. theta (Incline from horizontal (+ moving uphill)) is taken as 0 deg. (Sizing convention.)
  A9. F_p (Process force opposing the move (linear)) is taken as 0 N. (Sizing convention.)
  A10. profile (Motion profile) is taken as trapezoidal. (Sizing convention.)
  A11. SF (Safety margin on torque) is taken as 1.25. (Sizing convention.)
  A12. J_ratio_max (Inertia ratio limit (load : motor), warning) is taken as 10. (Sizing convention.)

4. WORKING
  [22] k = p_screw / (2 * pi * i)
      = 10 mm / (2 × π × 1)
      = 1.5915 mm
        Travel per motor radian
  [23] F_load = m * 9.80665 m/s^2 * (sin(theta) + mu * cos(theta)) + F_p
      = 50 kg × 9.80665 m/s^2 × (sin(0 deg) + 0.1 × cos(0 deg)) + 0 N
      = 49.033 N
        Load force along the motion (gravity, friction, process)
  [24] T_load = F_load * k / eta
      = 49.033 N × 1.5915 mm / 0.9
      = 0.08671 N·m
        Load torque at the motor shaft
  [25] J_refl = m * k^2 / eta
      = 50 kg × (1.5915 mm)^2 / 0.9
      = 0.00014072 kg·m²
        Load inertia reflected to the motor shaft
  [26] v_pk = s * 1 m / (t_move * (1 - f_acc))
      = 0.5 × 1 m / (0.5 s × (1 − 0.33333))
      = 1.5 m/s
        Peak linear velocity (trapezoid)
  [27] a_trap = v_pk / (f_acc * t_move)
      = 1.5 m/s / (0.33333 × 0.5 s)
      = 9 m/s²
        Trapezoidal acceleration
  [28] omega_pk = v_pk / k
      = 1.5 m/s / 1.5915 mm
      = 942.48 rad/s
        Peak motor speed
  [30] a_pk = a_trap
      = 9 m/s²
      = 9 m/s²
        Peak acceleration
  [31] alpha_pk = a_pk / k
      = 9 m/s² / 1.5915 mm
      = 5654.9 rad/s²
        Peak motor angular acceleration
  [32] J_total = J_motor + J_mech + J_refl
      = 0.0002 kg·m² + 0.0001 kg·m² + 0.00014072 kg·m²
      = 0.00044072 kg·m²
        Total inertia at the motor shaft
  [33] T_acc = J_total * alpha_pk
      = 0.00044072 kg·m² × 5654.9 rad/s²
      = 2.4922 N·m
        Acceleration torque
  [34] T_pk = T_acc + T_load
      = 2.4922 N·m + 0.08671 N·m
      = 2.5789 N·m
        Peak torque (accelerating against the load)
  [35] T_dec = -T_acc + T_load
      = −2.4922 N·m + 0.08671 N·m
      = -2.4055 N·m
        Torque during deceleration
  [36] T_alt = (T_pk - T_dec) / 2
      = (2.5789 N·m − (-2.4055 N·m)) / 2
      = 2.4922 N·m
        Alternating torque amplitude of the cycle about T_load
  [37] F_ax_pk = F_load + m * a_pk
      = 49.033 N + 50 kg × 9 m/s²
      = 499.03 N
        Peak axial thrust on the screw's support bearings (load force plus the moved mass accelerated)
  [38] n_pk = omega_pk
      = 942.48 rad/s
      = 9000 rpm
        Peak motor speed
  [39] P_pk = T_pk * omega_pk
      = 2.5789 N·m × 942.48 rad/s
      = 2.4306 kW
        Peak mechanical power
  [40] t_acc = f_acc * t_move
      = 0.33333 × 0.5 s
      = 0.16667 s
        Acceleration time
  [41] t_const = t_move - 2 * t_acc
      = 0.5 s − 2 × 0.16667 s
      = 0.16667 s
        Constant-velocity time
  [42] t_cycle = t_move + t_dwell
      = 0.5 s + 0.5 s
      = 1 s
        Cycle time
  [43] P_rms = omega_pk * sqrt((t_acc * (2 / 3) * (T_load^2 + T_acc^2) + T_load^2 * t_const) / t_cycle)
      = 942.48 rad/s × sqrt((0.16667 s × 2 / 3 × ((0.08671 N·m)^2 + (2.4922 N·m)^2) + (0.08671 N·m)^2 × 0.16667 s) / 1 s)
      = 0.78414 kW
        RMS mechanical power over the cycle (thermal-equivalent continuous power)
  [44] T_rms = sqrt((T_pk^2 * t_acc + T_load^2 * t_const + T_dec^2 * t_acc) / t_cycle)
      = sqrt(((2.5789 N·m)^2 × 0.16667 s + (0.08671 N·m)^2 × 0.16667 s + (-2.4055 N·m)^2 × 0.16667 s) / 1 s)
      = 1.4402 N·m
        RMS torque over the cycle (thermal)
  [45] J_ratio = (J_refl + J_mech) / J_motor
      = (0.00014072 kg·m² + 0.0001 kg·m²) / 0.0002 kg·m²
      = 1.2036
        Inertia ratio (load : motor)
  [46] u_peak = SF * T_pk / T_peak
      = 1.25 × 2.5789 N·m / 6 N·m
      = 0.53728
        Peak torque utilisation with safety margin
  [47] u_rms = SF * T_rms / T_rated
      = 1.25 × 1.4402 N·m / 2 N·m
      = 0.90012
        Continuous torque utilisation with safety margin
  [48] u_speed = n_pk / n_max
      = 9000 rpm / 10000 rpm
      = 0.9
        Speed utilisation
  [49] duty = T_rms / T_rated
      = 1.4402 N·m / 2 N·m
      = 0.7201
        Thermal duty (without margin)
