1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Torque, speed and power a motor needs to move a load through a mechanism on a point-to-point cycle: the load reflected to the motor shaft, the acceleration from the motion profile, peak torque during acceleration, RMS torque over the cycle (thermal duty), peak speed, inertia ratio, and a check of a candidate motor's rated and peak torque and speed with a safety margin.
Linear mechanisms: k = metres per motor radian (screw p/(2π i); belt D/(2 i); rack D_p/(2 i)); J_refl = m k²/η; T_load = F k/η with F = m g (sin θ + μ cos θ) + F_p. Rotary: J_refl = J_L/(i² η), T_load = T_L/(i η). Trapezoidal 1/3–1/3–1/3 (adjustable): v_pk = s/(t_move − t_acc), a = v_pk/t_acc; S-curve: sine acceleration with a_pk = (π/2) a_trap and the same v_pk. T_acc = J_total α; T_pk = T_acc + T_load; T_rms = √(Σ T_i² t_i / t_cycle) including the dwell.
  Ref: Hughes & Drury, Electric Motors and Drives Ch. on motor and drive selection — Load torque, inertia referred to the motor, duty cycles
  Ref: Manufacturer servo-sizing guides (Kollmorgen, Parker) — Reflected inertia, RMS torque, inertia ratio practice

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Rigid transmission; the mechanism efficiency applies to the driving direction and is also used in the deceleration phase (conservative for peak, slightly pessimistic for RMS). (Servo-sizing practice.)
  A2. The friction force is Coulomb friction on the moved mass; viscous and breakaway friction are not modelled unless included in F_p. (Load model.)
  A3. The S-curve profile has sine-shaped acceleration over the same acceleration time, so its peak acceleration is π/2 times the trapezoidal value. (Jerk-limited profile idealisation.)
  A4. mechanism (Mechanism) is taken as lead / ball screw. (Sizing convention.)
  A5. i (Gear ratio i (motor : output, 1 = none)) is taken as 1. (Sizing convention.)
  A6. mu (Friction coefficient (guide / bearing)) is taken as 0.1. (Linear guides with seals: μ = 0.1.)
  A7. theta (Incline from horizontal (+ moving uphill)) is taken as 0 deg. (Sizing convention.)
  A8. F_p (Process force opposing the move (linear)) is taken as 0 N. (Sizing convention.)
  A9. profile (Motion profile) is taken as trapezoidal. (Sizing convention.)
  A10. SF (Safety margin on torque) is taken as 1.25. (Sizing convention.)
  A11. J_ratio_max (Inertia ratio limit (load : motor), warning) is taken as 10. (Sizing convention.)

4. WORKING
  [21] k = p_screw / (2 * pi * i)
      = 10 mm / (2 × π × 1)
      = 1.5915 mm
        Travel per motor radian
  [22] F_load = m * 9.80665 m/s^2 * (sin(theta) + mu * cos(theta)) + F_p
      = 50 kg × 9.80665 m/s^2 × (sin(0 deg) + 0.1 × cos(0 deg)) + 0 N
      = 49.033 N
        Load force along the motion (gravity, friction, process)
  [23] T_load = F_load * k / eta
      = 49.033 N × 1.5915 mm / 0.9
      = 0.08671 N·m
        Load torque at the motor shaft
  [24] J_refl = m * k^2 / eta
      = 50 kg × (1.5915 mm)^2 / 0.9
      = 0.00014072 kg·m²
        Load inertia reflected to the motor shaft
  [25] v_pk = s * 1 m / (t_move * (1 - f_acc))
      = 0.5 × 1 m / (0.5 s × (1 − 0.33333))
      = 1.5 m/s
        Peak linear velocity (trapezoid)
  [26] a_trap = v_pk / (f_acc * t_move)
      = 1.5 m/s / (0.33333 × 0.5 s)
      = 9 m/s²
        Trapezoidal acceleration
  [27] omega_pk = v_pk / k
      = 1.5 m/s / 1.5915 mm
      = 942.48 rad/s
        Peak motor speed
  [29] a_pk = a_trap
      = 9 m/s²
      = 9 m/s²
        Peak acceleration
  [30] alpha_pk = a_pk / k
      = 9 m/s² / 1.5915 mm
      = 5654.9 rad/s²
        Peak motor angular acceleration
  [31] J_total = J_motor + J_mech + J_refl
      = 0.0002 kg·m² + 0.0001 kg·m² + 0.00014072 kg·m²
      = 0.00044072 kg·m²
        Total inertia at the motor shaft
  [32] T_acc = J_total * alpha_pk
      = 0.00044072 kg·m² × 5654.9 rad/s²
      = 2.4922 N·m
        Acceleration torque
  [33] T_pk = T_acc + T_load
      = 2.4922 N·m + 0.08671 N·m
      = 2.5789 N·m
        Peak torque (accelerating against the load)
  [34] T_dec = -T_acc + T_load
      = −2.4922 N·m + 0.08671 N·m
      = -2.4055 N·m
        Torque during deceleration
  [35] n_pk = omega_pk
      = 942.48 rad/s
      = 9000 rpm
        Peak motor speed
  [36] P_pk = T_pk * omega_pk
      = 2.5789 N·m × 942.48 rad/s
      = 2.4306 kW
        Peak mechanical power
  [37] t_acc = f_acc * t_move
      = 0.33333 × 0.5 s
      = 0.16667 s
        Acceleration time
  [38] t_const = t_move - 2 * t_acc
      = 0.5 s − 2 × 0.16667 s
      = 0.16667 s
        Constant-velocity time
  [39] t_cycle = t_move + t_dwell
      = 0.5 s + 0.5 s
      = 1 s
        Cycle time
  [40] T_rms = sqrt((T_pk^2 * t_acc + T_load^2 * t_const + T_dec^2 * t_acc) / t_cycle)
      = sqrt(((2.5789 N·m)^2 × 0.16667 s + (0.08671 N·m)^2 × 0.16667 s + (-2.4055 N·m)^2 × 0.16667 s) / 1 s)
      = 1.4402 N·m
        RMS torque over the cycle (thermal)
  [41] J_ratio = (J_refl + J_mech) / J_motor
      = (0.00014072 kg·m² + 0.0001 kg·m²) / 0.0002 kg·m²
      = 1.2036
        Inertia ratio (load : motor)
  [42] u_peak = SF * T_pk / T_peak
      = 1.25 × 2.5789 N·m / 6 N·m
      = 0.53728
        Peak torque utilisation with safety margin
  [43] u_rms = SF * T_rms / T_rated
      = 1.25 × 1.4402 N·m / 2 N·m
      = 0.90012
        Continuous torque utilisation with safety margin
  [44] u_speed = n_pk / n_max
      = 9000 rpm / 10000 rpm
      = 0.9
        Speed utilisation
  [45] duty = T_rms / T_rated
      = 1.4402 N·m / 2 N·m
      = 0.7201
        Thermal duty (without margin)
