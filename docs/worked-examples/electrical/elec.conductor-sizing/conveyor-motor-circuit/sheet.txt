1. Scope and method
Calculates the design current, the smallest preferred device rating and the smallest conductor whose tabulated capacity carries that rating. It checks I_b ≤ I_n ≤ I_z and I₂ ≤ 1.45 I_z, using your own capacity and rating records.
Design current: I_b = P/(√3·U·cosφ·η) three-phase, P/(U·cosφ·η) single-phase, P/U DC; or the linked I_b Protective device rating: I_n = smallest preferred rating in the ratings record with I_n ≥ I_b Device and installation factors: C_f = 0.725 for a semi-enclosed fuse (1.45/2, derived), else 1; C_c = the entered factor for a cable buried direct or in ducts, 1 when none applies Required tabulated capacity: I_t,req = I_n/(C_a·C_g·C_i·C_f·C_c); optional derived C_a = √[(θ_p − θ_a)/(θ_p − θ_ref)] (derived approximation — compare with the licensed table) Conductor cross-section: CSA = smallest cross-section in the ampacity record with I_t ≥ I_t,req; AWG gauge n: d = 0.127·92^((36 − n)/39) mm, area = π d²/4 (derived) Carrying capacity: I_z = I_t·C_a·C_g·C_i Device and conductor coordination: I_b ≤ I_n ≤ I_z and I₂ ≤ 1.45·I_z, I₂ = 1.45·I_n inherent for MCB types B, C, D; I₂ = 2·I_n by default for a semi-enclosed fuse; an entered I₂ (datasheet) is always checked; in the BS 7671 installation context a gG fuse to BS 88 (and an MCCB or MPCB entered without I₂, taken as I₂ ≤ 1.45·I_n) has the I₂ condition deemed satisfied when I_n ≤ I_z; in the machine-wiring context (IEC 60269-1 gG fuses have I₂ = 1.6·I_n, not 1.45·I_n) I₂ must be entered Device code: 1 = MCB type B, 2 = type C, 3 = type D, 4 = fuse, MCCB or MPCB (carried to fault-protection so the fault check uses the device chosen here)

3. Assumptions
  A1. Every tabulated value is entered from the user's own licensed copy or a manufacturer datasheet. (No table from a standard or a manufacturer is shipped.)
  A2. No table from a standard or a manufacturer ships with the calculator. (No table from a standard or a manufacturer is shipped.)
  A3. The capacities in the table are for the installation method stated. (Records carry their source and edition.)
  A4. The method key is descriptive and is not interpreted. (Records carry their source and edition.)
  A5. A gG fuse is deemed to meet I₂ ≤ 1.45·I_z when I_n ≤ I_z only under BS 7671. (BS 7671 reg. 433.1.201; IEC 60269-1.)
  A6. An IEC 60269-1 gG fuse has I₂ = 1.6·I_n, so in the machine-wiring context I₂ is entered from the datasheet. (BS 7671 reg. 433.1.201; IEC 60269-1.)
  A7. The ambient, grouping, insulation, fuse and installation factors are applied as a product. (The count is carried to the sheet and warns when C_g = 1 with more than one circuit.)
  A8. C_g is not looked up from the number of grouped circuits. (The count is carried to the sheet and warns when C_g = 1 with more than one circuit.)
  A9. The optional derived C_a is an approximation for the temperature difference only. (Derived, not tabulated.)
  A10. The derived C_a is labelled as an approximation, so compare it with the licensed table. (Derived, not tabulated.)
  A11. C_f = 0.725 applies to a semi-enclosed fuse only: 1.45 divided by an assumed fusing factor of 2. (Derived.)
  A12. C_c is the factor for a cable buried direct or in ducts, entered from the user's licensed table. (BS 7671 App. 4 names the factor; its value is licensed and is not shipped.)
  A13. AWG areas follow the geometry d = 0.127·92^((36 − n)/39) mm. (Derived.)
  A14. No AWG capacity is supplied. (Derived.)
  A15. DC circuits use I_b = P/U. (Method.)
  A16. The power factor and the efficiency are not applied to a DC circuit. (Method.)
  A17. T_ref (Reference ambient temperature for the derived C_a) is taken as 30 °C. (Reference ambient of the licensed table; only used when C_a is derived.)
  A18. derive_Ca (Derive C_a from the temperatures instead of the entered factor) is taken as no. (The entered C_a is used; the derived approximation is not applied.)
  A19. Machine wiring context (BS EN 60204-1): the conductor is sized for the protective device rating; the capacities and factors are the user's records for the installation method stated. (The context changes citations, and whether a gG fuse, MCCB or MPCB without I₂ may be deemed to meet I₂ ≤ 1.45·I_z (installation) or must have I₂ entered (machine wiring); no other number differs.)
  A20. No factor for a cable buried direct or in ducts is applied (C_c = 1). (Enter C_c from your licensed table for a cable buried direct or in ducts.)

4. Working
  [14] I_b = P / (sqrt(3) * U * cos_phi * eta)
      = 4 kW / (sqrt(3) × 400 V × 0.82 × 0.85)
      = 8.2834 A
        Design current (three-phase)
  [15] I_n = smallest preferred rating with I_n ≥ I_b    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 7.2
      = smallest rating ≥ 8.2834 A in the ratings record
      = 10 A
        Rated current of the protective device (ratings record)
  [16] device_code = 1 = MCB type B, 2 = type C, 3 = type D, 4 = fuse, MCCB or MPCB    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 7.2
      = MCB type C
      = 2
        Protective device type code (carried to E5 so the fault check uses this device)
  [17] C_f = 1 (not a semi-enclosed fuse)    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 12.4
      = MCB type C
      = 1
        Semi-enclosed fuse factor
  [22] T_op = 70 degC
      = 70 degC
        Conductor operating temperature for the insulation
  [25] I_t_req = I_n / (C_a * C_g * C_i * C_f * C_c)    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 12.4
      = 10 A / (0.91 × 0.77 × 1 × 1 × 1)
      = 14.271 A
        Required tabulated capacity
  [26] I_t = I_t of the selected row of the ampacity record    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 12.4
      = I_t,req = 14.271 A
      = 17.7 A
        Tabulated current-carrying capacity of the selected conductor
  [27] CSA = smallest cross-section in the ampacity record with I_t ≥ I_t,req    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 12.4
      = I_t,req = 14.271 A
      = 2.5 mm²
        Selected conductor cross-section
  [28] I_z = I_t * C_a * C_g * C_i    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 12.4
      = 17.7 A × 0.91 × 0.77 × 1
      = 12.402 A
        Current-carrying capacity in the installed conditions
  [29] u_Ib = I_b / I_n    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 7.2; BS 7671 reg. 433.1.1
      = 8.2834 A / 10 A
      = 0.82834
        Design current utilisation of the device
  [30] u_In = I_n / I_z    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 7.2; BS 7671 reg. 433.1.1
      = 10 A / 12.402 A
      = 0.8063
        Device rating utilisation of the conductor
  [31] I2 = 1.45 * I_n    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 7.2; BS 7671 reg. 433.1.1
      = 1.45 × 10 A
      = 14.5 A
        Conventional operating current (inherent 1.45 I_n)
  [32] I2_lim = 1.45 * I_z    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 7.2; BS 7671 reg. 433.1.1
      = 1.45 × 12.402 A
      = 17.983 A
        Largest conventional operating current the conductor allows, 1.45 I_z
  [33] u_I2 = I2 / I2_lim    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 7.2; BS 7671 reg. 433.1.1
      = 14.5 A / 17.983 A
      = 0.8063
        I₂ utilisation of 1.45 I_z
