1. Scope and method
The minimum width of a printed-circuit trace for a current and an allowed temperature rise, from the legacy IPC-2221 curve-fit, and the resistance, voltage drop and I²R loss of the trace as drawn: legacy IPC-2221 curve-fit, conservative for multilayer boards. External or internal layer; copper weight in oz/ft²; resistance at the trace's operating temperature (ambient plus rise). The IPC-2152 method (charts, plane and board-thickness factors) is not implemented.
Trace cross-section: I = k·ΔT^0.44·A^0.725, so A = [I/(k·ΔT^0.44)]^(1/0.725) with A in mil², k = 0.048 external, 0.024 internal Copper thickness: t = w_Cu·t_oz, t_oz = 1.378 mil per oz/ft² (35 µm, derived) Minimum width: w_min = A/t; utilisation u_w = w_min/w_act Resistivity at the operating temperature: ρ_T = ρ_20·[1 + α·(T_amb + ΔT − 20)] Trace resistance: R = ρ_T·L/(w_act·t); voltage drop ΔV = I·R; loss P = I²·R

3. Assumptions
  A1. The curve-fit is the legacy IPC-2221 relation, drawn for a trace on an outer or inner layer with no plane or convective help, 1 to 3 oz copper, up to 35 A, rises of 10 to 100 K and widths to 10 mm; it is conservative for multilayer boards and is a screening of width, not a thermal analysis of the board. (IPC-2221 curve-fit (legacy); IPC-2152 is not implemented.)
  A2. Copper thickness is the nominal 1.378 mil (35 µm) per oz/ft² of finished copper; plating build-up on outer layers and etch-back are not modelled. (Derived: 35 µm / 25.4 µm per mil.)
  A3. Resistance uses the trace as drawn at the temperature ambient + rise, from the resistivity at 20 °C and a linear temperature coefficient; vias, pads, bends and skin effect are ignored. (Derived constants for annealed copper.)
  A4. Steady direct current (or RMS current at low frequency); the rise is the allowed rise in the worst case. (Scope of the curve-fit.)

4. Working

  [15] I_n = I / (1 A)

  [16] dT_n = dT / (1 K)

  [17] A_req = (I_n / (k * dT_n^n_dT))^(1 / n_A) * (1 mil^2)    « IPC-2221 §6 (legacy)
      = (5 / (0.048 × 20^0.44))^(1 / 0.725) × 1 mil^2
      = 98.511 mil²
        Minimum conductor cross-section
  [18] t_Cu = Cu_oz * t_oz
      = 2 × 1.378 mil
      = 70.002 um
        Copper thickness
  [19] w_min = A_req / t_Cu
      = 98.511 mil² / 70.002 um
      = 0.9079 mm
        Minimum trace width
  [20] u_w = w_min / w_act
      = 0.9079 mm / 2.5 mm
      = 0.36316
        Width utilisation (minimum / as drawn)
  [21] A_act = w_act * t_Cu
      = 2.5 mm × 70.002 um
      = 0.17501 mm²
        Cross-section of the trace as drawn
  [22] T_trace = T_amb + dT
      = 25 degC + 20 K
      = 45 degC
        Trace operating temperature
  [23] rho_T = rho_20 * (1 + alpha * (T_trace - 20 degC))
      = 0.01724 ohm·mm²/m × (1 + 0.00393 1/K × (45 degC − 20 degC))
      = 0.018934 ohm·mm²/m
        Copper resistivity at the operating temperature
  [24] trace_R = rho_T * L / A_act
      = 0.018934 ohm·mm²/m × 100 mm / 0.17501 mm²
      = 10.819 mohm
        Trace resistance at the operating temperature
  [25] vdrop = I * trace_R
      = 5 A × 10.819 mohm
      = 54.095 mV
        Voltage drop along the trace
  [26] trace_Ploss = I^2 * trace_R
      = (5 A)^2 × 10.819 mohm
      = 270.47 mW
        Power loss in the trace (I²R)
