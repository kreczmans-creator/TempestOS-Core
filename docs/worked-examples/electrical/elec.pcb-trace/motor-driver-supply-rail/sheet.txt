1. Scope and method
Calculates the minimum width of a printed-circuit trace for a current and an allowed temperature rise by the legacy IPC-2221 curve-fit, conservative for multilayer boards. It also gives the resistance, voltage drop and I²R loss of the trace as drawn.
Trace cross-section: I = k·ΔT^0.44·A^0.725, so A = [I/(k·ΔT^0.44)]^(1/0.725) with A in mil², k = 0.048 external, 0.024 internal Copper thickness: t = w_Cu·t_oz, t_oz = 1.378 mil per oz/ft² (35 µm, derived) Minimum width: w_min = A/t; utilisation u_w = w_min/w_act Resistivity at the operating temperature: ρ_T = ρ_20·[1 + α·(T_amb + ΔT − 20)] Trace resistance: R = ρ_T·L/(w_act·t); voltage drop ΔV = I·R; loss P = I²·R

3. Assumptions
  A1. The curve-fit is the legacy IPC-2221 relation for a trace with no plane or convective help. (IPC-2221 curve-fit (legacy); IPC-2152 is not implemented.)
  A2. The curve-fit was drawn for 1 to 3 oz copper and currents up to 35 A. (IPC-2221 curve-fit (legacy).)
  A3. The curve-fit was drawn for rises of 10 to 100 K and widths up to 10 mm. (IPC-2221 curve-fit (legacy).)
  A4. The result screens the trace width and is not a thermal analysis of the board. (Scope of the curve-fit.)
  A5. The curve-fit is conservative for multilayer boards. (IPC-2221 curve-fit (legacy).)
  A6. Copper thickness is the nominal 1.378 mil (35 µm) per oz/ft² of finished copper. (Derived: 35 µm / 25.4 µm per mil.)
  A7. Plating build-up on outer layers and etch-back are not modelled. (Scope of the sheet.)
  A8. The resistance uses the trace as drawn at the temperature ambient plus rise. (Derived constants for annealed copper.)
  A9. The resistivity at 20 °C is corrected with a linear temperature coefficient. (Derived constants for annealed copper.)
  A10. Vias, pads, bends and skin effect are ignored. (Scope of the sheet.)
  A11. The current is steady direct current, or RMS current at low frequency. (Scope of the curve-fit.)
  A12. The rise is the allowed rise in the worst case. (Scope of the curve-fit.)

4. Working
  [14] k = k_ext    « IPC-2221 cl. 6 (legacy)
      = 0.048
      = 0.048
        Curve-fit constant k (external layer)
  [15] I_n = I / (1 A)
      = 5 A / 1 A
      = 5
        Current as a number of amperes
  [16] dT_n = dT / (1 K)
      = 20 K / 1 K
      = 20
        Temperature rise as a number of kelvin
  [17] A_req = (I_n / (k * dT_n^n_dT))^(1 / n_A) * (1 mil^2)    « IPC-2221 cl. 6 (legacy)
      = (5 / (0.048 × 20^0.44))^(1 / 0.725) × 1 mil^2
      = 98.511 mil²
        Minimum conductor cross-section
  [18] t_Cu = Cu_oz * t_oz
      = 2 × 1.378 mil
      = 70.002 um
        Copper thickness
  [19] w_min = A_req / t_Cu
      = 98.511 mil² / 70.002 um
      = 0.9079 mm
        Minimum trace width
  [20] u_w = w_min / w_act
      = 0.9079 mm / 2.5 mm
      = 0.36316
        Width utilisation (minimum / as drawn)
  [21] A_act = w_act * t_Cu
      = 2.5 mm × 70.002 um
      = 0.17501 mm²
        Cross-section of the trace as drawn
  [22] T_trace = T_amb + dT
      = 25 degC + 20 K
      = 45 degC
        Trace operating temperature
  [23] rho_T = rho_20 * (1 + alpha * (T_trace - 20 degC))
      = 0.01724 ohm·mm²/m × (1 + 0.00393 1/K × (45 degC − 20 degC))
      = 0.018934 ohm·mm²/m
        Copper resistivity at the operating temperature
  [24] trace_R = rho_T * L / A_act
      = 0.018934 ohm·mm²/m × 100 mm / 0.17501 mm²
      = 10.819 mohm
        Trace resistance at the operating temperature
  [25] vdrop = I * trace_R
      = 5 A × 10.819 mohm
      = 54.095 mV
        Voltage drop along the trace
  [26] trace_Ploss = I^2 * trace_R
      = (5 A)^2 × 10.819 mohm
      = 270.47 mW
        Power loss in the trace (I²R)
