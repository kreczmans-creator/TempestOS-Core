1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Total heat load inside a control enclosure, the effective surface that can dissipate it for the mounting condition, the internal temperature rise with natural convection and radiation through a stated overall heat-transfer coefficient, the forced-ventilation airflow for a target rise (density corrected for altitude), the fan duty point against a fan curve and the system resistance, optional solar gain, and the cooling-unit capacity needed when the limit cannot be met.
P = Σ P_i n_i d_i (+ solar α I A_sun). A_eff = Σ f_face × A_face. Natural: ΔT = P/(h A_eff). Forced: V = P/(ρ c_p ΔT_target) with ρ = p(z)/(287.055 T) and c_p = 1006 J/kg·K; fan duty where p_fan(V) = p_sys (V/V_ref)² (bisection). Cooling unit: P_cool = P − h A_eff (T_max − T_amb) when positive.
  Ref: IEC TR 60890 — A method of temperature-rise verification of low-voltage switchgear and controlgear assemblies by calculation
  Ref: Manufacturer enclosure climate-control guides (Rittal, nVent Hoffman) — Effective surface, heat-transfer coefficient and fan-sizing practice (cited, not copied)
  Ref: BS EN 61439-1 10.10 — Verification of temperature rise

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Heat leaves the enclosure through its exposed faces at one overall coefficient h (natural convection plus radiation to surroundings at the ambient temperature); covered faces dissipate nothing. (IEC TR 60890-style effective-surface approach; h is a reference value the user may edit.)
  A2. Forced ventilation removes the full heat load with air entering at ambient and leaving at the target rise; natural dissipation is neglected for the airflow (conservative). (Manufacturer fan-sizing practice.)
  A3. The fan curve is linear between the entered points; the system resistance scales with airflow squared. (Duty-point idealisation.)
  A4. A_sun (Sunlit area) is taken as 0.48 m^2. (One 600 × 800 face in the sun.)
  A5. alpha_sun (Surface absorptivity) is taken as 0.5. (Light-grey paint.)
  A6. f_top (Top exposure (1 free, 0 covered)) is taken as 1. (Free-standing kiosk, bottom on a plinth.)
  A7. f_front (Front exposure) is taken as 1. (Free-standing kiosk, bottom on a plinth.)
  A8. f_rear (Rear exposure (0 = wall-mounted)) is taken as 1. (Free-standing.)
  A9. f_left (Left side exposure) is taken as 1. (Free-standing kiosk, bottom on a plinth.)
  A10. f_right (Right side exposure) is taken as 1. (Free-standing kiosk, bottom on a plinth.)
  A11. f_bottom (Bottom exposure (0 = on the floor)) is taken as 0. (Free-standing kiosk, bottom on a plinth.)
  A12. altitude (Site altitude (air density)) is taken as 0 m. (Free-standing kiosk, bottom on a plinth.)

4. WORKING
  [17] P_loads = Σ P_i × n_i × d_i
      = 2 item(s)
      = 300 W
        Internal heat load
  [18] P_solar = alpha_sun * I_sun * A_sun
      = 0.5 × 800 W/m² × 0.48 m²
      = 192 W
        Solar gain
  [19] P_total = P_loads + P_solar
      = 300 W + 192 W
      = 492 W
        Total heat to remove
  [20] A_eff = f_top * W * D + f_bottom * W * D + f_front * W * H + f_rear * W * H + f_left * H * D + f_right * H * D    « IEC TR 60890 approach
      = 1 × 0.6 m × 0.3 m + 0 × 0.6 m × 0.3 m + 1 × 0.6 m × 0.8 m + 1 × 0.6 m × 0.8 m + 1 × 0.8 m × 0.3 m + 1 × 0.8 m × 0.3 m
      = 1.62 m²
        Effective dissipating surface
  [21] dT_nat = P_total / (h * A_eff)
      = 492 W / (5.5 W/(m²·K) × 1.62 m²)
      = 55.219 K
        Temperature rise with natural dissipation
  [22] T_int = T_amb + dT_nat
      = 40 degC + 55.219 K
      = 95.219 degC
        Internal temperature with natural dissipation
  [23] dT_allow = T_max - T_amb
      = 45 degC − 40 degC
      = 5 K
        Allowed rise
  [24] u_nat = dT_nat / dT_allow
      = 55.219 K / 5 K
      = 11.044
        Natural dissipation utilisation
  [25] P_nat_max = h * A_eff * dT_allow
      = 5.5 W/(m²·K) × 1.62 m² × 5 K
      = 44.55 W
        Heat the enclosure dissipates naturally at the limit
  [26] P_cool = max(0 W, P_total - P_nat_max)
      = max(0 W, 492 W − 44.55 W)
      = 447.45 W
        Cooling-unit capacity needed if ventilation is not used
  [27] p_site = 101325 (1 − 2.25577e-5 z)^5.2559
      = z = 0 m
      = 101.32 kPa
        Site pressure
  [28] rho_air = p_site / (287.055 J/(kg*K) * T_amb)
      = 101.32 kPa / (287.055 J/(kg*K) × 40 degC)
      = 1.1272 kg/m³
        Air density at ambient and altitude
  [29] V_req = P_total / (rho_air * 1006 J/(kg*K) * dT_allow)
      = 492 W / (1.1272 kg/m³ × 1006 J/(kg*K) × 5 K)
      = 312.39 m³/h
        Airflow for forced ventilation at the allowed rise (natural dissipation neglected)
  [30] V_req_net = max(0 m^3/h, (P_total - P_nat_max) / (rho_air * 1006 J/(kg*K) * dT_allow))
      = max(0 m^3/h, (492 W − 44.55 W) / (1.1272 kg/m³ × 1006 J/(kg*K) × 5 K))
      = 284.11 m³/h
        Airflow crediting natural dissipation
