1. Scope and method
The voltage dip when a three-phase induction motor starts, at the motor terminals and at the point of common coupling, from the supply impedance (a fault level or a transformer), the cable and the motor's locked-rotor impedance for a direct-on-line or star-delta start (a passive motor impedance) or a soft-start or drive start (a regulated current), by the complex potential divider; the dip is checked against a user limit (default 10 %). Also the capacitor kvar to raise the running power factor to a target, the capacitor current and the parallel-resonance order, with a warning when harmonic loads are present. Steady-state phasor model: no motor run-up dynamics, no flicker assessment, no starting-torque adequacy check.
Rated current: I_n = P/(√3·U·cos φ·η), or entered Starting current at rated voltage: DOL I_LR·I_n; star-delta I_LR·I_n/3; soft starter min(k_soft·I_n, I_LR·I_n); drive k_vfd·I_n Motor starting impedance (DOL, star-delta, and a soft starter that does not reach its limit): |Z_m| = (U/√3)/I_start, for a soft starter (U/√3)/(I_LR·I_n), at the starting power factor, Z_m = |Z_m|·(cos φ_st + j sin φ_st) Regulated current (a drive; a soft starter when its fully conducting current E/|Z_s + Z_c + Z_LR| exceeds k_soft·I_n): |I| = I_start held, lagging V_m by φ; with R + jX = Z_s + Z_c, a = |I|(R cos φ + X sin φ), b = |I|(X cos φ − R sin φ): |V_m| = √(E² − b²) − a, V_pcc = V_m + I·Z_c, and Z_m = V_m/I is the equivalent impedance Source impedance: transformer |Z_T| = u_k·U²/S_n, plus an optional upstream network |Z_Q| = U²/S_kQ in series; fault level |Z_s| = c·U²/S_k or c·U/(√3·I_k); each element Z = |Z|·(1 + j·X/R)/√(1 + (X/R)²) Supply cable impedance: R = ρ_T·L/(A·n) with ρ_T = ρ_20·[1 + α(T_c − 20)], or R′·L/n; X = X′·L/n (as E3) Phasor divider for a passive motor (phase voltage E = U/√3 at 0°): I = E/(Z_s + Z_c + Z_m); V_pcc = E·(Z_c + Z_m)/(Z_s + Z_c + Z_m); V_m = E·Z_m/(Z_s + Z_c + Z_m) Voltage dip: 100·(1 − |V|/E) at the motor terminals and at the point of common coupling; checked against the limits Cable drop along V_m: dV_cab = √3·Re[I·Z_c·V̄_m/|V_m|] = √3·I·(R cos φ_st + X sin φ_st), the E3 three-phase form at the dipped current Power-factor correction: P_in = P/η (or √3·U·I_n·cos φ when I_n is entered), Q_c = P_in·(tan φ₁ − tan φ₂) with φ₁ the motor's (a drive's displacement factor with a drive), I_c = Q_c/(√3·U), I after = P_in/(√3·U·cos φ₂) Parallel resonance: S_sc = U²/|Z_s|, parallel resonance order h ≈ √(S_sc/Q_c) (|Z_s| used for X_s, so h reads 1 to 1.4 % low)

3. Assumptions
  A1. The supply is a stiff source behind the stated impedance (voltage E = U/√3 before the start). A direct-on-line or star-delta start, and a soft starter that does not reach its current limit at the dipped voltage (full conduction), draw current through a passive winding: a constant impedance equal to the locked-rotor impedance at rated voltage and the starting power factor, so the current falls with the dipped voltage. A soft starter that reaches its limit and a drive regulate the current: they hold |I| = I_start whatever the voltage, so the dip is deeper than the passive model gives; the current phasor lags the voltage at the starter or drive input by the stated power factor. (Standard steady-state starting-dip screening model; the regulated case is solved in closed form.)
  A2. The dip is the steady-state phasor value at the instant of locked rotor; the current falls as the motor accelerates, so the dip is the worst case. No other load is running on the supply and no run-up dynamics, flicker (Pst) or tap-changer action is assessed. (Stated limitation.)
  A3. A star-delta start draws one third of the direct-on-line current at rated voltage (and one third of the starting torque, which is not checked here); the current spike at the star-to-delta changeover is not modelled. (Star-delta connection ratio.)
  A4. The cable resistance follows E3: from the resistivity, area and temperature, or from R′ per kilometre; X′ per kilometre is a user or record value. No cable table is embedded. (User or record values.)
  A5. The capacitor kvar corrects the running power factor of the motor input power P/η at rated load; the capacitor sits at the point of common coupling, and the resonance order uses the short-circuit apparent power U²/|Z_s| there (|Z_s| stands in for X_s, so h reads 1 to 1.4 % low). Capacitor switching, detuning and self-excitation on disconnection are not assessed. (Standard power-factor-correction relation.)
  A6. Source: E5 'Z_s' and 'I_f' are earth-fault-loop quantities (line to earth through the protective conductor), not the positive-sequence source impedance a motor start sees, so they are not linked; the fault level links E5 I_k3. E5 computes I_k3 with c = 1.1 and U_n = √3·U_0 (398.4 V at 230 V), so the I_k basis defaults c to 1.1 and U should be √3·U_0 for an exact match with E5's Z_T. E6 declares no impedance port, so the transformer rating and impedance are entered. (Linking decision.)
  A7. The source impedance should be taken at the minimum fault level (the deepest dip); a transformer alone is an infinite bus behind it, which understates the dip by a few per cent and overstates the resonance order unless the upstream network fault level S_kQ is entered. (Stated limitation.)
  A8. With a drive, the power-factor correction starts from the drive's displacement factor, not the motor's, the drive is itself a harmonic load, and the 'torque factor' and the voltage at the starter or drive input (not the motor terminals) are reported only for what they are. (Regulated-current starters.)
  A9. XR_Q (Upstream network X/R ratio) is taken as 10. (Network X/R taken as 10, mostly reactive.)
  A10. T_c (Conductor temperature for the resistance) is taken as 70 °C. (Cable warm from the running load before a restart.)
  A11. harmonics (Harmonic loads present on the supply) is taken as no. (No drives or other harmonic loads on the supply.)

4. Working
  [10] I_rated = P / (sqrt(3) * U * cos_phi * eta)
      = 160 kW / (sqrt(3) × 400 V × 0.88 × 0.95)
      = 276.24 A
        Motor rated current from the rated output
  [13] I_start = I_LR_r * I_rated / 3
      = 7 × 276.24 A / 3
      = 644.57 A
        Starting current at rated voltage (star connection: one third of direct on line)
  [14] S_start = sqrt(3) * U * I_start
      = sqrt(3) × 400 V × 644.57 A
      = 446.57 kVA
        Starting apparent power
  [20] Z_T = u_k * U^2 / S_n
      = 4 % × (400 V)^2 / 500 kVA
      = 0.0128 ohm
        Transformer impedance magnitude
  [21] R_T = Z_T / sqrt(1 + XR^2)
      = 0.0128 ohm / sqrt(1 + 4.2^2)
      = 0.0029647 ohm
        Transformer resistance
  [22] X_T = R_T * XR
      = 0.0029647 ohm × 4.2
      = 0.012452 ohm
        Transformer reactance
  [23] Z_Q = U^2 / S_kQ
      = (400 V)^2 / 15 MVA
      = 0.010667 ohm
        Upstream network impedance magnitude (referred to the secondary)
  [24] R_Q = Z_Q / sqrt(1 + XR_Q^2)
      = 0.010667 ohm / sqrt(1 + 10^2)
      = 0.0010614 ohm
        Upstream network resistance
  [25] X_Q = R_Q * XR_Q
      = 0.0010614 ohm × 10
      = 0.010614 ohm
        Upstream network reactance
  [26] R_src = R_T + R_Q
      = 0.0029647 ohm + 0.0010614 ohm
      = 0.0040261 ohm
        Source resistance (transformer plus upstream network)
  [27] X_src = X_T + X_Q
      = 0.012452 ohm + 0.010614 ohm
      = 0.023066 ohm
        Source reactance (transformer plus upstream network)
  [28] Z_src = hypot(R_src, X_src)
      = hypot(0.0040261 ohm, 0.023066 ohm)
      = 0.023414 ohm
        Source impedance magnitude
  [36] rho_T = rho_20 * (1 + alpha * (T_c - 20 degC))
      = 0.01724 ohm·mm²/m × (1 + 0.00393 1/K × (70 degC − 20 degC))
      = 0.020628 ohm·mm²/m
        Resistivity at the operating temperature
  [37] R_cab = rho_T * L_cab / (A_c * n_par)
      = 0.020628 ohm·mm²/m × 60 m / (95 mm² × 1)
      = 0.013028 ohm
        Cable resistance (parallel paths combined)
  [38] X_cab = X_km * L_cab / n_par
      = 0.08 ohm/km × 60 m / 1
      = 0.0048 ohm
        Cable reactance
  [39] Z_m = U / (sqrt(3) * I_start)
      = 400 V / (sqrt(3) × 644.57 A)
      = 0.35829 ohm
        Motor starting impedance magnitude
  [40] R_m = Z_m * cos_phi_st
      = 0.35829 ohm × 0.3
      = 0.10749 ohm
        Motor starting resistance
  [41] X_m = Z_m * sqrt(1 - cos_phi_st^2)
      = 0.35829 ohm × sqrt(1 − 0.3^2)
      = 0.34178 ohm
        Motor starting reactance
  [42] E_ph = E = U/√3 (reference phasor, angle 0°)
      = 400 V / √3
      = 230.94 V
        Source phase voltage before the start
  [43] Z_s_ph = Z_s = R_s + jX_s
      = R_s = 0.0040261 Ω, X_s = 0.023066 Ω
      = "0.0040261 + j0.023066 Ω  [0.023414 Ω ∠ 80.099°]"
        Source impedance phasor
  [44] Z_c_ph = Z_c = R_c + jX_c
      = R_c = 0.013028 Ω, X_c = 0.0048 Ω
      = "0.013028 + j0.0048 Ω  [0.013884 Ω ∠ 20.226°]"
        Cable impedance phasor
  [45] Z_m_ph = Z_m = |Z_m|·(cos φ_st + j sin φ_st)
      = 0.35829 Ω · (0.3 + j0.95394)
      = "0.10749 + j0.34178 Ω  [0.35829 Ω ∠ 72.542°]"
        Motor starting impedance phasor
  [46] Z_t_ph = Z_t = Z_s + Z_c + Z_m
      = (0.0040261 + j0.023066) + (0.013028 + j0.0048) + (0.10749 + j0.34178)
      = "0.12454 + j0.36965 Ω  [0.39006 Ω ∠ 71.381°]"
        Total series impedance phasor
  [47] I_ph = I = E/Z_t
      = 230.94 V / (0.39006 Ω ∠ 71.381°)
      = "189.03 − j561.07 A  [592.06 A ∠ −71.381°]"
        Starting current phasor at the dipped voltage
  [48] V_m_ph = V_m = E·Z_m/Z_t
      = 230.94 V · (0.35829 Ω ∠ 72.542°) / (0.39006 Ω ∠ 71.381°)
      = "212.08 + j4.301 V  [212.13 V ∠ 1.1618°]"
        Motor terminal voltage phasor (phase)
  [49] V_pcc_ph = V_pcc = E·(Z_c + Z_m)/Z_t
      = 230.94 V · (0.36694 Ω ∠ 70.826°) / (0.39006 Ω ∠ 71.381°)
      = "217.24 − j2.1012 V  [217.25 V ∠ −0.55417°]"
        Point of common coupling voltage phasor (phase)
  [50] I_dip = |I|
      = 230.94 V / 0.39006 Ω
      = 592.06 A
        Starting current at the dipped voltage
  [51] V_m = |V_m|·√3
      = 212.13 V · √3
      = 367.41 V
        Voltage at the motor terminals while starting (line)
  [52] V_pcc = |V_pcc|·√3
      = 217.25 V · √3
      = 376.28 V
        Point of common coupling voltage while starting (line)
  [53] start_dip = 100·(1 − |V_m|/E)
      = 100 · (1 − 212.13 / 230.94)
      = 8.147 %
        Voltage dip at the motor terminals
  [54] dip_pcc = 100·(1 − |V_pcc|/E)
      = 100 · (1 − 217.25 / 230.94)
      = 5.9289 %
        Voltage dip at the point of common coupling
  [55] dV_cab = √3·Re[I·Z_c·V̄_m/|V_m|]
      = √3 · Re[(592.06 A ∠ −71.381°) · (0.013884 Ω ∠ 20.226°) · e^(−j1.1618°)]
      = 8.7035 V
        Cable drop along the starting voltage: √3·I·(R cos φ + X sin φ), the E3 three-phase form at this current
  [56] k_T = (|V_m|/E)²
      = (212.13 / 230.94)²
      = 0.8437
        Torque factor at the dipped voltage relative to rated voltage (torque varies as V²)
  [57] u_dip = start_dip / dip_limit
      = 8.147 % / 10 %
      = 0.8147
        Dip at the motor terminals against the limit
  [58] P_in = P / eta
      = 160 kW / 0.95
      = 168.42 kW
        Motor input power at rated load
  [59] tan_1 = sqrt(1 - cos_phi^2) / cos_phi
      = sqrt(1 − 0.88^2) / 0.88
      = 0.53974
        tan φ₁ at the running power factor
  [60] tan_2 = sqrt(1 - cos_phi2^2) / cos_phi2
      = sqrt(1 − 0.95^2) / 0.95
      = 0.32868
        tan φ₂ at the target power factor
  [61] Q_c = max(0 kW, P_in * (tan_1 - tan_2))
      = max(0 kW, 168.42 kW × (0.53974 − 0.32868))
      = 35.547 kvar
        Capacitor kvar to reach the target power factor
  [62] I_cap = Q_c / (sqrt(3) * U)
      = 35.547 kvar / (sqrt(3) × 400 V)
      = 51.307 A
        Capacitor line current at rated voltage
  [63] I_run1 = P_in / (sqrt(3) * U * cos_phi)
      = 168.42 kW / (sqrt(3) × 400 V × 0.88)
      = 276.24 A
        Running line current before correction
  [64] I_run2 = P_in / (sqrt(3) * U * cos_phi2)
      = 168.42 kW / (sqrt(3) × 400 V × 0.95)
      = 255.89 A
        Running line current after correction (when the target is reached)
  [65] S_sc = U^2 / Z_src
      = (400 V)^2 / 0.023414 ohm
      = 6.8334 MVA
        Short-circuit apparent power at the point of supply
  [66] h_res = sqrt(S_sc / Q_c)
      = sqrt(6.8334 MVA / 35.547 kvar)
      = 13.865
        Parallel resonance order of the capacitor bank with the supply
  [68] u_dip_pcc = dip_pcc / dip_limit_pcc
      = 5.9289 % / 7 %
      = 0.84699
        Point-of-common-coupling dip against the limit
