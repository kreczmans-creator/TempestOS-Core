1. Scope and method
Calculates the fill of a conduit, trunking or tray, the bend radius and the pull of the cables through a route. The space factor, bend multiple, friction coefficient and pulling limits are your own records.
Cable area: A_cab = Σ n_i·π·d_i²/4 (n_i cables of overall diameter d_i) Containment fill: A_int = π·D²/4 (conduit, internal diameter D) or W·H (trunking or tray, internal width × depth); fill = A_cab/A_int; u_fill = fill/f_max, f_max the space factor from your record Bend radius: R_min = n_bend·d_max, d_max the largest cable diameter, n_bend from your record; each bend u_b = R_min/R Cable weight: w = Σ n_i·m_i (kg/m); force per metre w·g·w_c, g = 9.80665 m/s², w_c your weight-correction factor (1 = full weight) Straight length: T₂ = T₁ + w·g·w_c·μ·L Tension at a bend: T₂ = T₁·cosh(μ_e·θ) + √(T₁² + (w·g·w_c·R)²)·sinh(μ_e·θ), μ_e = w_c·μ, θ in radians (horizontal bend, cable on the containment floor, the default); capstan option, no weight term: T₂ = T₁·e^(μ_e·θ) Sidewall pressure and direction: SWP = T₂/R; the route is run in the listed order and in reverse, and the direction that passes every tension and sidewall pressure check is recommended (else the one of lower maximum tension) Pulling limits: T_cab = k_n·N·min(T_max,i) (load shared equally), u_T = T/T_cab; u_grip = T/T_grip, T_grip your grip or pulling-eye limit; u_P = SWP_max/P_lim

3. Assumptions
  A1. Cables are circular and counted by overall diameter. (Fill by geometry; the factor is a user record.)
  A2. There is no packing or cable and conduit factor method. (Fill by geometry; the factor is a user record.)
  A3. The jam-ratio warning is the only jamming check. (Fill by geometry; the factor is a user record.)
  A4. The space factor is yours, from your own record. (Fill by geometry; the factor is a user record.)
  A5. Every cable is pulled together as one bundle on one pulling head with the load shared equally. (Equal sharing; unequal sharing is not modelled.)
  A6. Each cable carries T/N, so the weakest cable governs. (Equal sharing; unequal sharing is not modelled.)
  A7. The cable limit is N times the lowest maximum pulling tension, derated by your factor above 3 cables. (Equal sharing; unequal sharing is not modelled.)
  A8. The grip or pulling-eye limit is a separate limit you enter. (Equal sharing; unequal sharing is not modelled.)
  A9. Straight lengths are horizontal and the cable weight bears on the containment over the full length. (Equivalent friction w_c·μ at the bends; blank w_c means 1.)
  A10. Bends are taken in the plane of the route. (Equivalent friction w_c·μ at the bends; blank w_c means 1.)
  A11. Inclines, vertical runs and pulley or roller reductions are not modelled, so fold them into μ. (Equivalent friction w_c·μ at the bends; blank w_c means 1.)
  A12. A cradled or triangular weight correction is entered as w_c. (Equivalent friction w_c·μ at the bends; blank w_c means 1.)
  A13. The factor w_c scales the weight on straight lengths and the friction in bends. (Equivalent friction w_c·μ at the bends; blank w_c means 1.)
  A14. The contact force of a cradled or triangular arrangement is w_c times that of a flat one. (Equivalent friction w_c·μ at the bends; blank w_c means 1.)
  A15. On a straight length T₂ = T₁ + w·g·w_c·μ·L. (Equivalent friction w_c·μ at the bends; blank w_c means 1.)
  A16. At a bend μ_e = w_c·μ in the cosh/sinh or capstan form. (Equivalent friction w_c·μ at the bends; blank w_c means 1.)
  A17. A bend in a vertical plane has the weight along the cable rather than on the floor. (Equivalent friction w_c·μ at the bends; blank w_c means 1.)
  A18. For that bend, take the capstan option and fold the rise into the route. (Equivalent friction w_c·μ at the bends; blank w_c means 1.)
  A19. The coefficient of friction is one constant for the whole route. (A user record with its source.)
  A20. Static and running friction are not distinguished. (A user record with its source.)
  A21. Sidewall pressure is the tension leaving the bend over the bend radius, for the bundle as a whole. (SWP = T₂/R.)
  A22. Enter the cable maker's per-cable limit unchanged. (SWP = T₂/R.)
  A23. The bundle T/R against it is conservative for a triangular arrangement and roughly equal for a cradled one. (SWP = T₂/R.)
  A24. Do not multiply the limit by the number of cables. (SWP = T₂/R.)
  A25. The bend radius uses the individual cable diameter. (Cable data sheet.)
  A26. For a triplexed or bundled assembly, enter the assembly diameter from the cable maker as the overall diameter. (Cable data sheet.)
  A27. The tension at the start of the pull is the same in both directions. (Weight term for a horizontal bend; capstan equation as the option.)
  A28. With the horizontal-bend (weight) model a heavy cable gains tension at a bend even from T₁ = 0. (Weight term for a horizontal bend; capstan equation as the option.)
  A29. With the capstan option a bend at the very start of a pull with no start tension adds nothing. (Weight term for a horizontal bend; capstan equation as the option.)
  A30. Enter the pulling rope or drum tension when it matters. (Weight term for a horizontal bend; capstan equation as the option.)

4. Working
  [1] A_c1 = n·π·d²/4
      = 2 × π × (14 mm)² / 4
      = 307.88 mm²
        Servo power: cross-section of 2 cable(s)
  [2] A_c2 = n·π·d²/4
      = 4 × π × (9 mm)² / 4
      = 254.47 mm²
        Encoder and control: cross-section of 4 cable(s)
  [3] A_cab = Σ n_i·π·d_i²/4
      = A_c1 + A_c2
      = 562.35 mm²
        Total cable cross-section
  [4] OD_max = max d_i
      = 14 mm
      = 14 mm
        Largest cable overall diameter
  [5] w = Σ n_i·m_i
      = 2 × 0.35 kg/m + 4 × 0.12 kg/m
      = 1.18 kg/m
        Mass per metre of the bundle
  [8] A_int = pi * D_int^2 / 4
      = π × (40 mm)^2 / 4
      = 1256.6 mm²
        Internal cross-section of the conduit
  [10] fill = A_cab / A_int
      = 562.35 mm² / 1256.6 mm²
      = 44.75 %
        Fill: cable cross-section over internal cross-section
  [11] u_fill = A_cab / (A_int * f_max)
      = 562.35 mm² / (1256.6 mm² × 0.5)
      = 0.895
        Fill utilisation against the space factor
  [13] R_min = n_bend * OD_max
      = 10 × 14 mm
      = 140 mm
        Minimum bend radius for the largest cable
  [14] u_b2 = R_min/R
      = 140 mm / 500 mm
      = 0.28
        Segment 2: bend radius utilisation
  [15] u_b4 = R_min/R
      = 140 mm / 400 mm
      = 0.35
        Segment 4: bend radius utilisation
  [16] u_b6 = R_min/R
      = 140 mm / 600 mm
      = 0.23333
        Segment 6: bend radius utilisation
  [17] u_bend = max R_min/R
      = 3 bend(s)
      = 0.35
        Bend radius utilisation (tightest bend)
  [18] R_bend = min R of the bends
      = 3 bend(s)
      = 400 mm
        Smallest bend radius of the route
  [21] wg = w * 9.80665 m/s^2
      = 1.18 kg/m × 9.80665 m/s^2
      = 11.572 N/m
        Weight per metre of the bundle on the containment
  [23] Ta_1 = T₁ + w·g·w_c·μ·L
      = 150 N + 11.572 N/m × 0.3 × 6 m
      = 170.83 N
        Segment 1 (as listed): tension after the straight length
  [24] Ta_2 = T₁·cosh(μ_e·θ) + √(T₁² + (w·g·w_c·R)²)·sinh(μ_e·θ)
      = 170.83 N × cosh(0.47124) + √(170.83² + (11.572 N/m × 0.5 m)²) × sinh(0.47124)
      = 273.71 N
        Segment 2 (as listed): tension after the bend
  [25] Pa_2 = T₂/R
      = 273.71 N / 0.5 m
      = 547.43 N/m
        Segment 2 (as listed): sidewall pressure
  [26] Ta_3 = T₁ + w·g·w_c·μ·L
      = 273.71 N + 11.572 N/m × 0.3 × 14 m
      = 322.31 N
        Segment 3 (as listed): tension after the straight length
  [27] Ta_4 = T₁·cosh(μ_e·θ) + √(T₁² + (w·g·w_c·R)²)·sinh(μ_e·θ)
      = 322.31 N × cosh(0.47124) + √(322.31² + (11.572 N/m × 0.4 m)²) × sinh(0.47124)
      = 516.36 N
        Segment 4 (as listed): tension after the bend
  [28] Pa_4 = T₂/R
      = 516.36 N / 0.4 m
      = 1290.9 N/m
        Segment 4 (as listed): sidewall pressure
  [29] Ta_5 = T₁ + w·g·w_c·μ·L
      = 516.36 N + 11.572 N/m × 0.3 × 9 m
      = 547.6 N
        Segment 5 (as listed): tension after the straight length
  [30] Ta_6 = T₁·cosh(μ_e·θ) + √(T₁² + (w·g·w_c·R)²)·sinh(μ_e·θ)
      = 547.6 N × cosh(0.23562) + √(547.6² + (11.572 N/m × 0.6 m)²) × sinh(0.23562)
      = 693.1 N
        Segment 6 (as listed): tension after the bend
  [31] Pa_6 = T₂/R
      = 693.1 N / 0.6 m
      = 1155.2 N/m
        Segment 6 (as listed): sidewall pressure
  [32] Tb_6 = T₁·cosh(μ_e·θ) + √(T₁² + (w·g·w_c·R)²)·sinh(μ_e·θ)
      = 150 N × cosh(0.23562) + √(150² + (11.572 N/m × 0.6 m)²) × sinh(0.23562)
      = 189.89 N
        Segment 6 (reversed): tension after the bend
  [33] Pb_6 = T₂/R
      = 189.89 N / 0.6 m
      = 316.49 N/m
        Segment 6 (reversed): sidewall pressure
  [34] Tb_5 = T₁ + w·g·w_c·μ·L
      = 189.89 N + 11.572 N/m × 0.3 × 9 m
      = 221.14 N
        Segment 5 (reversed): tension after the straight length
  [35] Tb_4 = T₁·cosh(μ_e·θ) + √(T₁² + (w·g·w_c·R)²)·sinh(μ_e·θ)
      = 221.14 N × cosh(0.47124) + √(221.14² + (11.572 N/m × 0.4 m)²) × sinh(0.47124)
      = 354.28 N
        Segment 4 (reversed): tension after the bend
  [36] Pb_4 = T₂/R
      = 354.28 N / 0.4 m
      = 885.7 N/m
        Segment 4 (reversed): sidewall pressure
  [37] Tb_3 = T₁ + w·g·w_c·μ·L
      = 354.28 N + 11.572 N/m × 0.3 × 14 m
      = 402.88 N
        Segment 3 (reversed): tension after the straight length
  [38] Tb_2 = T₁·cosh(μ_e·θ) + √(T₁² + (w·g·w_c·R)²)·sinh(μ_e·θ)
      = 402.88 N × cosh(0.47124) + √(402.88² + (11.572 N/m × 0.5 m)²) × sinh(0.47124)
      = 645.43 N
        Segment 2 (reversed): tension after the bend
  [39] Pb_2 = T₂/R
      = 645.43 N / 0.5 m
      = 1290.9 N/m
        Segment 2 (reversed): sidewall pressure
  [40] Tb_1 = T₁ + w·g·w_c·μ·L
      = 645.43 N + 11.572 N/m × 0.3 × 6 m
      = 666.26 N
        Segment 1 (reversed): tension after the straight length
  [41] T_max_a = T at the end of the route, as listed
      = 693.1 N
      = 693.1 N
        Maximum tension, pulling in the listed order
  [42] T_max_b = T at the end of the route, reversed
      = 666.26 N
      = 666.26 N
        Maximum tension, pulling in the reverse order
  [43] P_max_a = max SWP, as listed
      = 1290.9 N/m
      = 1290.9 N/m
        Maximum sidewall pressure, pulling in the listed order
  [44] P_max_b = max SWP, reversed
      = 1290.9 N/m
      = 1290.9 N/m
        Maximum sidewall pressure, pulling in the reverse order
  [45] T_cab = N·min(T_max,i)
      = 6 × 200 N
      = 1200 N
        Pulling tension limit of the bundle from the cables (equal load share, weakest cable)
  [47] max_tension = min(T_max,a ; T_max,b)
      = 693.1 N ; 666.26 N
      = 666.26 N
        Maximum pulling tension, recommended direction
  [48] P_max = max SWP in the recommended direction
      = 1290.9 N/m
      = 1290.9 N/m
        Maximum sidewall pressure, recommended direction
  [49] u_Tc1 = (T/N)/(k_n·T_max,i)
      = 111.04 N / (1 × 600 N)
      = 0.18507
        Servo power: share of the pull against its own limit
  [50] u_Tc2 = (T/N)/(k_n·T_max,i)
      = 111.04 N / (1 × 200 N)
      = 0.55521
        Encoder and control: share of the pull against its own limit
  [51] u_T = max_tension / T_cab
      = 666.26 N / 1200 N
      = 0.55521
        Tension utilisation against the cable limit (the weakest cable)
  [52] u_P = P_max / P_lim
      = 1290.9 N/m / 3000 N/m
      = 0.43028
        Sidewall pressure utilisation against the limit
  [53] f_lim = f_max
      = 0.5
      = 50 %
        Space factor limit as a percentage
