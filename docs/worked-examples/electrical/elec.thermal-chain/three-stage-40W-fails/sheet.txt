1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Steady-state source (junction) temperature from a series chain of thermal resistances to ambient (junction to case, case to heat sink, heat sink to ambient), with each stage's temperature rise, the margin against the permitted maximum and the largest total resistance the limit allows: the heat sink budget.
Temperature rise across each stage: ΔT_i = P·R_i. Total resistance of the chain and the total rise: R_tot = Σ R_i; ΔT_tot = P·R_tot. Source (hottest node) temperature, each node below it being the one above less that stage's rise: T_1 = T_a + ΔT_tot. Margin against the permitted maximum: margin = T_max − T_1. Largest total resistance the limit allows, the heat sink budget: R_max = (T_max − T_a)/P. Utilisation of the permitted rise: u = ΔT_tot/(T_max − T_a).
  Ref: JEDEC JESD51-1 — Integrated circuit thermal measurement method: electrical test method (junction-to-reference resistances)
  Ref: Çengel & Ghajar, Heat and Mass Transfer §15-2 — Thermal resistance networks in the cooling of electronic equipment
  Ref: Incropera & DeWitt, Fundamentals of Heat and Mass Transfer §3.1 — Thermal resistance in series

2. INPUTS
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  P | Steady power dissipated (the heat flow through every stage) | 40 | W | spec: DS-PWR-902 device and heat sink datasheets rev A | 0 … 10000 W
  T_a | Ambient the last stage rejects to | 40 | degC | spec: SPEC-EL-05 thermal limits | -40 … 85 degC
  stages | Thermal resistances in series, hottest first | 3 row(s) |  | spec: DS-PWR-902 device and heat sink datasheets rev A | any
  stages[1] |  | name = junction to case; R = 0.5 K/W |  |  | 
  stages[2] |  | name = case to sink; R = 0.2 K/W |  |  | 
  stages[3] |  | name = sink to ambient; R = 1.8 K/W |  |  | 
  T_max | Maximum permitted temperature of the hottest node (junction or case) | 125 | degC | spec: SPEC-EL-05 thermal limits | 40 … 200 degC

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Steady state: the whole dissipated power flows through the one chain, and each resistance is constant. (No thermal capacitance (nothing about warm-up or a transient overload); no parallel path through the board.)
  A2. The stated ambient is the temperature the last stage actually rejects to. (Air preheated by neighbouring parts can be well above room temperature.)

4. WORKING
  [4] R_tot = Σ R_i    « Incropera §3.1
      = 0.5 + 0.2 + 1.8 K/W
      = 2.5 K/W
        Total thermal resistance
  [5] dT_tot = P * R_tot
      = 40 W × 2.5 K/W
      = 100 K
        Total rise from ambient to the source
  [6] T_1 = T_a + dT_tot    « Çengel §15-2
      = 40 degC + 100 K
      = 140 degC
        Source temperature (hot side of junction to case)
  [7] dT_1 = P · R_1
      = 40 W × 0.5 K/W
      = 20 K
        Rise across junction to case
  [8] dT_2 = P · R_2
      = 40 W × 0.2 K/W
      = 8 K
        Rise across case to sink
  [9] T_2 = T_1 − dT_1
      = 140 °C − 20 K
      = 120 degC
        Hot side of case to sink
  [10] dT_3 = P · R_3
      = 40 W × 1.8 K/W
      = 72 K
        Rise across sink to ambient
  [11] T_3 = T_2 − dT_2
      = 120 °C − 8 K
      = 112 degC
        Hot side of sink to ambient
  [12] dT_m = T_max - T_1
      = 125 degC − 140 degC
      = -15 K
        Thermal margin (negative when over the limit)
  [13] u = dT_tot / (T_max - T_a)
      = 100 K / (125 degC − 40 degC)
      = 1.1765
        Utilisation: rise over permitted rise
  [14] R_max = (T_max - T_a) / P
      = (125 degC − 40 degC) / 40 W
      = 2.125 K/W
        Largest total resistance the limit allows (heat sink budget)
