1. SCOPE AND METHOD
The demand of a set of loads per supply (three-phase AC, single-phase AC, DC) and the minimum DC power supply, transformer and battery for it, each with a margin; the heat the loads dissipate for the enclosure calculation.
Demand per load: P_i·d_i·k_i; apparent power P_i·d_i·k_i/cosφ_i (arithmetic sum, conservative) Bus currents: I_3φ = S_3φ/(√3·U), I_1φ = S_1φ/U, I_DC = P_DC/U_DC Heat dissipated: Φ = Σ(1 − η_i)·P_i·d_i·k_i PSU: P_PSU ≥ margin·P_DC/f_derate (f_derate from the manufacturer's curve at θ and altitude) Transformer: S_T ≥ margin·S; starting peak S_peak = S + max(m_i·P_i/cosφ_i − S_i) ≤ k_T·S_T (the starting load undiversified) Battery: C = I_b·t/(DoD·η_b·k_temp·k_age), I_b = Σ backed P_i·d_i·k_i/(U_b·η_conv); Peukert: I_b·H·(t/H)^(1/k_p) in place of I_b·t
  Ref: Linden's Handbook of Batteries 5th ed., Ch. 3–4 — Battery sizing and the Peukert relation
  Ref: BS EN 60204-1:2018 cl. 4.3 — Electrical supply (supply voltage tolerance; cited by number only)

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Each load's P is its electrical input; DC loads count at power factor 1 in the transformer demand (the PSU's own loss is a load row if wanted). (Load budget convention.)
  A2. Apparent powers are added arithmetically, which is conservative when power factors differ. (Upper bound on S.)
  A3. PSU derating, transformer overload and battery factors are the manufacturer's values, entered as records. (§A3 reference records.)
  A4. margin (Sizing margin) is taken as 1.25. (25 % growth margin.)
  A5. f_derate (PSU derating factor at the ambient and altitude) is taken as 1. (PSU rated to 60 °C without derating (datasheet).)
  A6. k_T (Transformer short-time overload allowed for inrush) is taken as 3. (Short-time overload allowed by the supply authority.)
  A7. eta_conv (Conversion efficiency battery → loads) is taken as 1. (DC UPS module, loads on the battery bus.)
  A8. DoD (Allowed depth of discharge) is taken as 0.8. (VRLA, 80 % depth of discharge.)
  A9. eta_b (Battery discharge efficiency) is taken as 0.95. (Discharge efficiency.)
  A10. k_temp (Temperature capacity factor) is taken as 1. (Battery kept at 20 °C.)
  A11. k_age (Ageing (end-of-life) factor) is taken as 0.8. (End-of-life capacity 80 %.)

  [30] S_T_req = margin * S_dem

  [31] S_peak = S_dem + max(m_i·P_i/cosφ_i − S_i)
      = 12.979 + 70.349 (Duty pump starting)
      = 83.328 kVA

  [32] I_b = Σ backed P_i·d_i·k_i/(U_b·η_conv)
      = 120 / (24 × 1)
      = 5 A
        Battery discharge current
  [33] Ah = I_b * t_auto
      = 5 A × 2 h
      = 10 A·h
        Discharge capacity
  [34] C_req = Ah / (DoD * eta_b * k_temp * k_age)
      = 10 A·h / (0.8 × 0.95 × 1 × 0.8)
      = 16.447 A·h
        Minimum battery capacity
  [36] u_PSU = P_PSU_req / P_PSU
      = 150 W / 240 W
      = 0.625
        PSU utilisation
  [38] u_T = S_T_req / S_T

      = 0.64897
        Transformer utilisation
  [39] u_inrush = S_peak / (k_T * S_T)
      = 83.328 kVA / (3 × 25 kVA)
      = 1.111
        Inrush utilisation
  [41] u_batt = C_req / C_batt
      = 16.447 A·h / 17 A·h
      = 0.96749
        Battery utilisation
