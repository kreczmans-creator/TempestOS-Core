1. Scope and method
Calculates the demand of a set of loads per supply and the minimum DC power supply, transformer and battery for it. It also gives the heat the loads dissipate for the enclosure calculation.
Demand per load: P_i·d_i·k_i; apparent power P_i·d_i·k_i/cosφ_i (arithmetic sum, conservative); this duty-weighted demand sizes the transformer and the enclosure heat Running load of each load while it is on: P_i·k_i, apparent P_i·k_i/cosφ_i (the duty d is the share of time on, so it is left out: a cable, a bus and a power supply carry the load while it is on) Bus currents: I_3φ = S_3φ,run/(√3·U), I_1φ = S_1φ,run/U, I_DC = P_DC,run/U_DC, from the running loads; when a bus is chosen for the DC power supply its input P_DC,run/η_PSU (power factor 1) is added to that bus Heat dissipated inside the enclosure: Φ = Σ h_i·P_i·d_i·k_i + P_DC·(1/η_PSU − 1), h_i = the share of the input power released inside (blank: the loss 1 − η_i; electronics 1; a machine outside the enclosure 0); the second term is the power supply's own loss, present when an AC bus is chosen for it Power supply sizing: P_PSU ≥ SF·P_DC,run/f_derate (the running DC load, duty not applied; f_derate from the manufacturer's curve at T_amb and altitude) Transformer sizing: S_T ≥ SF·S for the running load; starting peak S_peak = S + max(m_i·P_i/cosφ_i − S_i) ≤ k_T·S_T (the starting load undiversified), so S_T ≥ S_peak/k_T for the start; the rating to buy is the larger of the two Battery sizing: C = I_b·t/(DoD·η_b·k_temp·k_age), I_b = Σ backed P_i·d_i·k_i/(U_b·η_conv); Peukert: I_b·H·(t/H)^(1/k_p) in place of I_b·t, never below I_b·t (the exponent is not valid beyond the rated hour rate H)

3. Assumptions
  A1. Each load's P is its electrical input. (Load budget convention.)
  A2. DC loads count at power factor 1 in the transformer demand. (Load budget convention.)
  A3. With a PSU bus chosen, DC loads count at P/η_PSU, which already includes the supply's own loss. (Load budget convention.)
  A4. The sheet adds that loss to Φ itself, as P_DC·(1/η_PSU − 1), whenever psu_bus and η_PSU are set. (Load budget convention.)
  A5. Do not add the power supply as a load row, which would count its loss twice. (Load budget convention.)
  A6. The heat released inside the enclosure is h·P·d·k per load. (Stated convention for the heat a load releases inside the panel.)
  A7. The default h = 1 − η is the loss of a drive or supply. (Stated convention for the heat a load releases inside the panel; change h for a load that differs.)
  A8. Electronics release all their input (h = 1) and a machine outside the panel releases none (h = 0). (Stated convention for the heat a load releases inside the panel; change h for a load that differs.)
  A9. Apparent powers are added arithmetically, which is conservative when power factors differ. (Upper bound on S.)
  A10. PSU derating, transformer overload and battery factors are the manufacturer's values, entered as records. (Entered as reference records, which carry their source, edition and licence.)
  A11. P_add (Additional three-phase load (linked from the motor-sizing RMS power)) is taken as 0 kW. (No further load linked into this panel: every load is in the table.)
  A12. cosphi_add (Power factor of the additional load) is taken as 0.85. (Not used while P_add is 0; template default kept.)
  A13. eta_add (Efficiency of the additional load) is taken as 0.9. (Not used while P_add is 0; template default kept.)
  A14. inrush_add (Starting multiple of the additional load) is taken as 1. (Not used while P_add is 0; template default kept.)
  A15. heat_add (Share of the additional load's input power released inside the enclosure (a linked machine is outside it: 0)) is taken as 0. (Not used while P_add is 0; template default kept.)
  A16. eta_psu (DC power supply efficiency (power factor taken as 1)) is taken as 0.9. (24 V supply efficiency 90 %, power factor taken as 1.)
  A17. SF (Sizing safety factor) is taken as 1.25. (25 % growth margin.)
  A18. f_derate (PSU derating factor at the ambient and altitude) is taken as 1. (PSU rated to 60 °C without derating (datasheet).)
  A19. k_T (Transformer short-time overload allowed for inrush) is taken as 3. (Short-time overload allowed by the supply authority.)
  A20. eta_conv (Conversion efficiency battery → loads) is taken as 1. (DC UPS module, loads on the battery bus.)
  A21. DoD (Allowed depth of discharge) is taken as 0.8. (VRLA, 80 % depth of discharge.)
  A22. eta_b (Battery discharge efficiency) is taken as 0.95. (Discharge efficiency.)
  A23. k_temp (Temperature capacity factor) is taken as 1. (Battery kept at 20 °C.)
  A24. k_age (Ageing (end-of-life) factor) is taken as 0.8. (End-of-life capacity 80 %.)
