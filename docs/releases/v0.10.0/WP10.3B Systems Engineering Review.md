## 2. "Integrate Directly, Never Duplicate Registration" — Independently Verified

Re-read every constructor/method of `RibbonView` directly: it holds a
reference to the real `ICommandRegistry`/`IWorkspaceManager`/
`IWorkspace`, calls `.Items`/`.CanRename`/`.CanDelete`/`.CanRevise`/
`.DeleteObjectAsync`/`.InvokeAsync` directly, and registers **zero**
new descriptors, handlers, or commands of its own — confirmed by the
complete absence of `RegisterDescriptor`/`RegisterHandler` anywhere in
`RibbonView.cs` (direct `grep`, zero matches).

## 3. `ADR-0070` Compliance — Independently Verified

Re-read `CommandPaletteOverlay.cs` directly: unmodified in its own
core behaviour (still the global, `Ctrl+K`-reachable, `Items`-driven
overlay); the one change (`CommandUnavailable`) is additive, fixing a
defect, not altering the "view over the registry, disabled not hidden"
decision itself. The Ribbon does not replace or bypass the Palette —
both remain independently reachable, exactly as `ADR-0070`'s own
Alternatives Considered anticipated ("a menu-equivalent... remains
part of the specification").
