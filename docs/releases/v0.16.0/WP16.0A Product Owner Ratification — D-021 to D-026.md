rows for `D-021` through `D-026`.** It exists because those rows record an
approval date, and an approval date with no retrievable instruction behind
it is not evidence.

Recorded because the Product Owner instruction requires it explicitly, and
because a register that shows only the ratified state loses the fact that
the whole of v0.16.0 was built on decisions that were **not yet approved**.

| Date | State | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-09-04 | **Proposed** — reserved by number, approval date deliberately blank | `WP16.0A v0.16.0 Scope Decision.md`; PR #6, whose Product Approval section was left blank by its author |

own report, PR #6's body, and every Work Package report that said
"Proposed" continue to say it, correctly, for the period they describe.

## 2. The six decisions as ratified

### `D-021` — Ratified

Definition 2 (`WP11.0B Architecture Roadmap.md` §1) governs v1.0. The
2026-08-28 project-centric decision (`TD-84`) becomes the v1.x roadmap.
The `Product Roadmap.md` Phase 5.5 formulation is retained as the
downstream expression of this decision.

### `D-022` — Ratified

The Companion mobile application is formally deferred to v1.1. The
Companion branch (`claude/tempestos-companion-mobile-ubznt3`) is
**retained**, and is neither merged nor deleted. `TD-82` is **Deferred by
decision**. `FCR-0092` remains Companion-scoped.

### `D-023` — Ratified

No third-party plugin support is included in v1.0. `src/Plugins/` remains
empty by design. `TD-49`, `TD-50`, `TD-56`, `TD-61` and `TD-64` retain
their "before enablement" grading and are **not** v1.0 blockers.

### `D-024` — Ratified

REST remains loopback-only. The REST listener is disabled by default and
is enabled explicitly through the established configuration mechanism.
`TD-13`, `TD-14`, `TD-16` and `FCR-0003`/`FCR-0004` remain declared debt
for v1.0.

### `D-025` — Ratified, with the support boundary stated exactly

The ratified support boundary, in the Product Owner's own words:

> Windows supported and CI-verified; macOS supported by design but not
> CI-verified; Linux launches successfully under Avalonia 11.3.20, with
> advisory smoke evidence and the determinism track record, but Linux is
> **not** CI-verified in the same sense as Windows.

Three constraints attach to this ratification and bind every downstream
document:

1. **Do not record Linux as "supported" without qualification.**
2. **Do not claim Linux is CI-verified.**
3. **Retain the distinction** between successful Linux launch evidence and
   formal CI verification.

The evidence this boundary rests on: `WP 16.5B` verified an
exception-free Linux startup to a running session under `Xvfb` — a virtual
framebuffer, not a real X11 or Wayland desktop — and `TD-116` is Resolved.
The `linux-launch-smoke` CI job runs that launch on every push but is
**advisory and not part of `CI Gate`**. It has since accumulated a track
record it lacked when the spike report was written: five for five in the
`WP 16.4A` determinism matrix, asserting the strengthened
`Host -> Running.` marker rather than a bare exit code. That is a real
track record and it is still not `CI Gate`.

### `D-026` — Ratified

`WP 15.2A` is carried into v0.16.0. `docs/releases/v0.15.1/` remains
deleted. No `v0.15.1` release exists. **The prior irreversible folder
disposition is accepted and ratified** — the deletion had already occurred
before ratification, was disclosed as such at the gate, and the Product
Owner ratified it in that knowledge.

## 3. What this ratification does not do

- It does **not** authorise the merge of `feature/v0.16.0` to `main`.
- It does **not** authorise the `v0.16.0` tag or its push.
- It does **not** record a Product Approval verdict for the release. The
  verdict field in `Release Register.md` stays blank.
- It creates **no** decision beyond the six above. `D-027` and higher do
  not exist.
