# WP 14.1A Completion Report — Brand Alignment to the Tempest Engineering Design System

Per Engineering Governance §4 (nine mandatory sections).

## 1. Summary

Replaced the Companion's provisional `WP 14.0A` branding with the
authoritative Tempest Engineering Design System supplied by the Product
Owner: instrument-dark-first navy themes, the indigo/cyan/violet triad
with machine-state green/amber/red, the supplied mark and TEMPEST OS
lockup carried as verbatim geometry and PNG artwork, the pack's font
builds, squared corners, top-edge card status rules, UPPERCASE Chakra
labels, mono machine data with UTC `Z` timestamps and `INFO WARN ERR OK`
log levels, no emoji or hand-drawn glyphs. Conformance is now
test-guarded. Two real layout/idiom defects found by the visual review
were fixed structurally.

## 4. Architectural Decisions & Reasoning

No new ADR — no decision changed. `ADR-0113`'s visual-identity paragraph
was corrected in place (the `ADR-0111`/`WP 13.10B` precedent): the
decision (the Companion realises the brand concretely) stands; its
concrete values were provisional and are now the pack's. The pack's
"never rebuild the logo in code" rule is honoured as never *redrawing*:
`TempestMarkGeometry` carries the supplied SVG coordinates unchanged and
documents itself as a transcription. The desktop's divergence became
`FCR-0092` rather than silent side-effect work.

## 7. Assumptions

- The supplied pack is the authoritative brand; where it and the
  `WP 14.0A` brief conflict, the pack wins everywhere (confirmed by the
  Product Owner's own commissioning words for this Work Package).
- The pack's fonts are the brand's canonical builds; the previously
  embedded Google-Fonts builds of the same OFL families were replaced.
- "Tempest OS" (spaced, per the pack's lockups and readme) is the brand
  spelling in user-facing prose; the platform's code/document identity
  "TempestOS" is unchanged.

## 8. Observations

- `WP 14.0A`'s visual review process (headless Skia frame captures)
  caught both genuine defects this Work Package fixed: the Avalonia
  `Foreground = null` local-value trap (fixed in `WP 14.0A`) and, here,
  a `ScrollViewer` width-constraint gap that let one unbreakable mono
  line push its card past the viewport — fixed structurally in
  `CompanionPage`, so no page can regress it.
- The pack's web component kit and site export are deliberately not
  imported — brand assets, fonts, and rules only.
