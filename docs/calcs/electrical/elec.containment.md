# E8 — Containment fill, bend radius and cable pulling (`elec.containment@1.0.0`)

**Scope.** The fill of a conduit, trunking or tray by the cables in it against a space factor the user enters; the bend radius of a pull route against a multiple of the largest cable diameter the user enters; and the pull of the cables through a route of straight lengths and bends, worked in both directions, with the direction of lower maximum tension recommended. No IET or other cable and conduit factor table, no space-factor value and no bend or friction value ships (Addendum B §B5): the factor method is not offered, and every limit is a record the user enters from their own references and the cable maker's data. Standards are cited by clause number and title only.

**Method.** Fill is geometric: `A_cab = Σ nᵢ·π·dᵢ²/4`, `A_int = π·D²/4` (conduit) or `W·H` (trunking or tray, internal width × depth), `fill = A_cab/A_int`, utilisation `u_fill = fill/f_max`. Bend radius: `R_min = n_bend·d_max` with `d_max` the largest cable diameter; each bend `u_b = R_min/R`, the check is the tightest bend. Weight `w = Σ nᵢ·mᵢ` (kg/m), force per metre `w·g`, `g = 9.80665 m/s²`. Pulling, in pull order from a start tension `T₁`: straight length `T₂ = T₁ + w·g·μ·L` (w in kg/m times g gives N/m); bend `T₂ = T₁·e^(μθ)` (capstan equation, θ in radians); sidewall pressure at a bend `SWP = T₂/R` (N/m). The route is run as listed (direction a) and reversed (direction b), with the same start tension; tension never falls along a pull, so the maximum is the end tension. The recommended direction is the one with the lower maximum tension (equal tension: the lower SWP; equal both: either). The tension limit is `T_lim = Σ nᵢ·T_max,ᵢ` over the cable table, or an override; `u_T = max_tension/T_lim`, `u_P = P_max/P_lim`.

**Inputs.** `cables` table (name, `od` mm, `count` whole number, `mass` kg/m each, optional `T_max` N each); `cont_type` (conduit or trunking/tray); `D_c` or `W_c` and `H_c` (mm); `f_max` space factor (record `containment.space-factor`, no default, required); `n_bend` (record `cable.bend-radius`, optional); `route` table (kind Straight or Bend; `L` m for a straight, `theta` degrees and `R` mm for a bend); `T_1` start tension (default 0 N); `mu` (record `containment.friction`, required when there is a route); `T_lim` override and `P_lim` sidewall pressure limit (record `cable.pulling-limit`, both optional). Hard limits: diameters 0.1 to 300 mm, count 1 to 1000, mass 0 to 1000 kg/m, bend angle up to 720° per row, μ 0 to 2, space factor 0.01 to 1, 40 cable rows and 50 route rows. A tension above 1e12 N is refused, not shown.

**Outputs.** `A_cab`, `A_int`, `fill` (%), `u_fill`, `OD_max`, `w`; `R_min`, `u_bend`; `T_max_a`, `T_max_b`, `P_max_a`, `P_max_b`, `max_tension`, `P_max` (recommended direction), `T_lim`, `u_T`, `u_P`, and the text `direction` ("Reverse: pull from segment 5 to segment 1", "As listed: …", or "Either direction"). One working step per segment and direction (`Ta_i`, `Tb_i`, `Pa_i`, `Pb_i`). **Ports.** `fill` (`cont.fill`) and `max_tension` (`cont.max_tension`); links E4 and E3 (the conductor sized in E4 and the run checked in E3 are the cables entered here; their diameters and masses come from the cable data sheet, and no port binds into the cable table). **Checks.** Fill against the space factor; bend radius against the cable minimum; pulling tension against the limit; sidewall pressure against the limit (each only when its limit is given). **Warnings.** No `n_bend` with bends in the route; no tension limit (a cable without `T_max` and no override); no `P_lim` with bends; the recommended direction exceeds `P_lim` but the other direction does not.

**Limitations.** Circular cables counted by overall diameter, no packing or jamming rules, no cable or conduit factors. Straight lengths are horizontal and the cable weight bears on the containment over the full length; inclines, vertical runs, cradled or triangular weight corrections, rollers and pulleys are not modelled (fold them into μ). One μ for the whole route; static and running friction not distinguished. Bends use the capstan equation only (no weight term). SWP is the whole bundle's tension over the bend radius, so the limit must be entered in the same terms. The tension limit is the sum of the cable limits (equal load sharing on one pulling head) unless overridden.

**Test cases.** All Confirmed and analytic, recomputed in python3: `ka-fill-3x10-in-25` (3 × 10 mm in a 25 mm conduit: 48 % exactly; with a five-segment route at μ 0.35: 154.919528 N as listed, 138.420988 N reversed, SWP 461.4812 / 284.2706 N/m, T_lim 1200 N), `ka-pull-tray-two-bends` (mixed bundle in 100 × 50 trunking from 100 N: 1074.235304 N as listed, 941.126090 N reversed), `ka-capstan-180` (T₂ = 200·e^(0.35π) = 600.567352 N, SWP 1501.41838 N/m), `ka-trunking-fill` (12 × 8 mm in 100 × 50: 12.0637 %), `ka-bend-too-tight` (R_min 120 mm against a 100 mm bend: u_b = 1.2). **Not pinned — owner check:** a cable maker's published pulling-guide example (straight plus bend tension and sidewall pressure); none is stated here because its inputs and answer cannot be quoted with confidence, so no Unconfirmed or Secondary case is declared. The clause number BS 7671 reg. 522.8 is cited from memory and should be checked against the owner's copy.
