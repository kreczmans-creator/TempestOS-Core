# E9 — Motor start voltage dip and PF correction (`elec.motor-start@1.2.0`)

**Scope.** The voltage dip when a three-phase induction motor starts, at the motor terminals (the starter or drive input for a soft starter in current limit or a drive) and at the point of common coupling (PCC), from the supply impedance (a fault level S_k or I_k with an X/R ratio, or a transformer S_n, u_k, X/R with an optional upstream network S_kQ in series), the cable (R + jX, as E3) and the motor, for a direct-on-line, star-delta, soft-start or drive start. The dip is checked against a user limit (default 10 % at the motor, editable) and an optional PCC limit. Also the capacitor kvar to raise the running power factor to a target, the capacitor current, the parallel-resonance order and a warning when harmonic loads are present. Steady-state phasor model of the locked-rotor instant: no run-up dynamics, flicker (Pst) assessment, tap-changer action, other running load or starting-torque adequacy check. IEC 60909-0 cl. 5.3 and IEC 61000-3-11 are cited by number and title only; no limit is embedded.

**Method.**
- Rated current `I_rated = P/(√3·U·cos φ·η)` or the entered `I_n`. Starting current at rated voltage: DOL `I_LR·I_rated`; star-delta `I_LR·I_rated/3`; soft starter `min(k_soft·I_rated, I_LR·I_rated)`; drive `k_vfd·I_rated` at the drive's displacement power factor.
- Source: transformer `|Z_T| = u_k·U²/S_n`, plus an optional upstream network `|Z_Q| = U²/S_kQ` (X/R `XR_Q`) added as phasors; fault level `c·U²/S_k` or `c·U/(√3·I_k)` (c blank defaults to 1.0 for S_k and 1.1 for I_k, the factor E5 computes `I_k3` with); each element `Z = |Z|(1 + j·X/R)/√(1 + (X/R)²)`; `Z_src = |Z_s|`.
- Cable: `R = ρ_T·L/(A·n)`, `ρ_T = ρ_20[1 + α(T_c − 20)]` (or `R′·L/n`), `X = X′·L/n`.
- **Passive start** (DOL, star-delta, and a soft starter that does not reach its limit): the motor is a winding of constant impedance, `|Z_m| = (U/√3)/I_start` (for a soft starter `(U/√3)/(I_LR·I_n)`), `Z_m = |Z_m|(cos φ_st + j sin φ_st)`; the current falls with the dipped voltage. Phasors (E = U/√3 at 0°, `System.Numerics.Complex`, every phasor printed on the sheet with magnitude and angle): `Z_t = Z_s + Z_c + Z_m`, `I = E/Z_t`, `V_m = E·Z_m/Z_t`, `V_pcc = E·(Z_c + Z_m)/Z_t`.
- **Regulated start** (a drive; a soft starter only when it reaches its limit): a soft starter cannot pass more than the locked-rotor current at the dipped voltage, so the passive locked-rotor divider is solved first, `|I_pas| = E/|Z_s + Z_c + Z_LR|`; if `|I_pas| ≤ k_soft·I_n` the sheet reports "Full conduction, limit not reached" (`start_mode`) and the passive result stands, otherwise the current is held, `|I| = I_start`, lagging `V_m` by φ (the starting power factor, or the drive's displacement factor). With `R + jX = Z_s + Z_c`, `a = |I|(R cos φ + X sin φ)`, `b = |I|(X cos φ − R sin φ)`: `|V_m| = √(E² − b²) − a`, `V_pcc = V_m + I·Z_c`, and `Z_m = V_m/I` is the equivalent impedance. A drive whose current the supply cannot hold blocks the sheet (`current-limit`, naming `k_vfd` to lower); a soft starter never asks for more than the passive current, so it does not block. A passive impedance would let the current fall and understate this dip (about 9 % of the dip on the soft-start case, which can turn a fail into a pass near the limit).
- Dip `= 100·(1 − |V|/E)`. `dV_cab = √3·Re[I·Z_c·V̄_m/|V_m|]` is the cable drop along the starting voltage; it equals E3's `√3·I·(R cos φ + X sin φ)` at the dipped current `I_dip` and the starting power factor (proved in a test by running E3). `k_T = (|V_m|/E)²` is the torque factor of a passive start (not given for a regulated start, where torque follows current).
- PF correction: `P_in = P/η` (or `√3·U·I_n·cos φ` when `I_n` is entered, with a warning if the two differ by more than 5 %), `Q_c = P_in(tan φ₁ − tan φ₂)` where φ₁ is the motor's (a drive's displacement factor with a drive; zero, with a warning, when the target is not above it), `I_c = Q_c/(√3·U)`, running current before and after. Resonance: `S_sc = U²/|Z_s|`, `h ≈ √(S_sc/Q_c)`; `|Z_s|` stands in for `X_s`, so h reads 1 to 1.4 % low.

**Inputs.** Supply: `U` 50–1000 V, `source` (fault level / transformer), `fault_basis`, `S_k` MVA (use the minimum fault level), `I_k` kA, optional `c_src` 0.95–1.1, `S_n` kVA, `u_k` %, optional `S_kQ` MVA and `XR_Q`, `XR` 0–100. Motor: `P` kW (nameplate), `eta`, `cos_phi`, optional `I_n`, `I_LR_r` 1–15, `cos_phi_st` 0.05–1. Starter: `starter`, `k_soft`, `k_vfd`, `cos_phi_vfd`. Cable: `cable_data`, `L_cab` m, `n_par`, `A_c`, `rho_20`, `alpha`, `T_c`, `R_km`, `X_km`. Limits: `dip_limit`, optional `dip_limit_pcc`. Correction: `cos_phi2` 0.5–1, `harmonics` flag. Every numeric input has a hard range (a value outside it blocks the sheet). Record kinds: `cable.impedance`, `conductor.material`, `motor.start`, `starter.limits`, `transformer.data`; per-km R and X are your own datasheet or licensed-table values, never embedded.

**Outputs.** `Z_src`, `Z_m`, `I_rated`, `I_start`, `S_start`, `I_dip`, `V_m`, `V_pcc`, `start_dip`, `dip_pcc`, `dV_cab`, `k_T`, `u_dip` (`u_dip_pcc`), `P_in`, `kvar`, `I_cap`, `I_run1`, `I_run2`, `S_sc`, `h_res`. **Ports.** `start_dip` (`motor.start_dip`, %), `kvar` (`pfc.kvar`, kvar), `I_start` (A, for the starting-current coordination check; it is not a design current), `I_rated` (A, the running design current → E4 `I_b`). **Checks.** dip ≤ limit at the motor (or starter or drive input); PCC dip ≤ limit when given. **Warnings.** target power factor not above the motor's or drive's (`pfc`); capacitors with a drive or soft starter (`pfc-starter`); harmonic loads flagged, with the resonance order, and a drive without the flag (`harmonics`); entered `I_n` and P/η disagree by more than 5 % (`rated`); linked `I_k` with c other than 1.1 (`c_src`); unsourced tabulated values.

**Linking.** `I_k` takes E5 `I_k3` (kA), and since 1.2.0 `c_src` takes E5 `c_k3` (the factor `c_max` that `I_k3` was computed with), so the source impedance stays E5's own `Z_T` whatever `c_max` is; the `c_src` warning is raised only when `c_src` is typed, not when it is linked. The linked `I_k3` is the fault level at the transformer terminals: the dip is a lower bound unless a supply cable or upstream network (`S_kQ`) is added. E5 computes it with c = 1.1 and `U_n = √3·U_0` (398.4 V at 230 V): the blank `c_src` already defaults to 1.1 on the I_k basis, a typed `c_src` other than 1.1 with a linked `I_k` warns (the fault level's voltage factor must match `c_src`; linking `c_src` from E5 `c_k3` removes the mismatch), and setting `U` to `√3·U_0` makes the source impedance equal E5's `Z_T` exactly (tested). E5 `Z_s` and `I_f` are earth-fault-loop quantities (line to earth through the protective conductor), not the positive-sequence source impedance a motor start sees, so they are not linked. E6 declares no impedance port (`P_dem`, `S_dem`, `I_3ph` only), so `S_n` and `u_k` are entered. `P` is the motor nameplate rating, entered: E1 `P_cont` is a load requirement and only a lower bound, and `P_pk` would inflate `I_n` and the dip. `A_c` and `L_cab` take E4 `CSA` and `L`; `I_rated` feeds E4 `I_b`.

**Limitations.** The source impedance should be the one at the minimum fault level (the deepest dip); a transformer alone is an infinite bus unless the upstream `S_kQ` is entered, which understates the dip by a few per cent and overstates the resonance order. A passive start is a constant impedance at the locked-rotor instant (the current falls as the motor accelerates, so the dip is the worst case); a regulated start holds the current. Low-voltage (≤ 1 kV) three-phase systems with a balanced source behind the stated impedance. The current spike at the star-to-delta changeover is not modelled. The capacitor sits at the PCC; switching transients, detuning, self-excitation and the capacitor voltage rating are not assessed; with a drive, the correction starts from the drive's displacement factor and the drive is itself a harmonic load. The dip limit and the network operator's PCC limit are yours to enter.

**Test cases.** All analytic and recomputed in python3 with complex arithmetic (Confirmed): `ka-pump-dol-transformer` (75 kW DOL on 500 kVA, 4 %: dip 7.7757 % motor, 4.6746 % PCC, 18.892 kvar, h 25.72), `ka-hand-phasor` (all three impedances at 53.13°: dip exactly 20 % and 10 %, |I| 461.88 A, `dV_cab` 40 V, cross-checked against E3's 40 V in a test), `ka-star-delta-fault-current` (I_k route with c = 1.1), `ka-soft-start-full-conduction` (a 5.5 × I_n limit on a weak 2 MVA supply is not reached, so the passive DOL dip of 25.976 % stands), `ka-soft-start-current-limit` and `ka-vfd-current-limit` (current held; solved in python by fixed-point iteration of the phasors and checked against the closed form: 9.1223 % and 2.4783 % at the starter or drive input), `ka-transformer-upstream-network` (315 kVA behind 20 MVA: 9.5523 % and 7.4333 %), `ka-pfc-resonance`. **Not pinned — owner check:** the Schneider Electric *Electrical Installation Guide* power-factor-correction example (the kvar-per-kW factor table and its worked example); no inputs and answer are quoted here because they could not be stated with confidence. The relation it uses, `Q_c = P(tan φ₁ − tan φ₂)`, is covered by the analytic cases above.
