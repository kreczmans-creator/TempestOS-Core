# E9 — Motor start voltage dip and PF correction (`elec.motor-start@1.0.0`)

**Scope.** The voltage dip when a three-phase induction motor starts, at the motor terminals and at the point of common coupling (PCC), from the supply impedance (a fault level S_k or I_k with an X/R ratio, or a transformer S_n, u_k, X/R), the cable (R + jX, as E3) and the motor's locked-rotor impedance, for a direct-on-line, star-delta, soft-start or drive start. The dip is checked against a user limit (default 10 % at the motor, editable) and an optional PCC limit. Also the capacitor kvar to raise the running power factor to a target, the capacitor current, the parallel-resonance order and a warning when harmonic loads are present. Steady-state phasor model of the locked-rotor instant: no run-up dynamics, flicker (Pst) assessment, tap-changer action, other running load or starting-torque adequacy check. IEC 60909-0 cl. 5.3 and IEC 61000-3-11 are cited by number and title only; no limit is embedded.

**Method.**
- Rated current `I_rated = P/(√3·U·cos φ·η)` or the entered `I_n`. Starting current at rated voltage: DOL `I_LR·I_rated`; star-delta `I_LR·I_rated/3`; soft starter `min(k_soft·I_rated, I_LR·I_rated)`; drive `k_vfd·I_rated` at the drive's displacement power factor.
- Motor: `|Z_m| = (U/√3)/I_start`, `Z_m = |Z_m|(cos φ_st + j sin φ_st)`.
- Source: transformer `|Z_s| = u_k·U²/S_n`; fault level `c·U²/S_k` or `c·U/(√3·I_k)`; `Z_s = |Z_s|(1 + j·X/R)/√(1 + (X/R)²)`.
- Cable: `R = ρ_T·L/(A·n)`, `ρ_T = ρ_20[1 + α(T_c − 20)]` (or `R′·L/n`), `X = X′·L/n`.
- Phasors (E = U/√3 at 0°, `System.Numerics.Complex`; every phasor is printed on the sheet with its magnitude and angle): `Z_t = Z_s + Z_c + Z_m`, `I = E/Z_t`, `V_m = E·Z_m/Z_t`, `V_pcc = E·(Z_c + Z_m)/Z_t`. Dip `= 100·(1 − |V|/E)`.
- `dV_cab = √3·Re[I·Z_c·V̄_m/|V_m|]` is the cable drop along the motor voltage; it equals E3's `√3·I·(R cos φ + X sin φ)` at the dipped current `I_dip` and the starting power factor (proved in a test by running E3). `k_T = (|V_m|/E)²` is the torque factor at the dipped voltage.
- PF correction: `P_in = P/η`, `Q_c = P_in(tan φ₁ − tan φ₂)` (zero, with a warning, when the target is not above the motor's own), `I_c = Q_c/(√3·U)`, running current before and after. Resonance: `S_sc = U²/|Z_s|`, `h ≈ √(S_sc/Q_c)`.

**Inputs.** Supply: `U` 50–1000 V, `source` (fault level / transformer), `fault_basis`, `S_k` MVA, `I_k` kA, `c_src` 0.95–1.1, `S_n` kVA, `u_k` %, `XR` 0–100. Motor: `P` kW, `eta`, `cos_phi`, optional `I_n`, `I_LR_r` 1–15, `cos_phi_st` 0.05–1. Starter: `starter`, `k_soft`, `k_vfd`, `cos_phi_vfd`. Cable: `cable_data` (resistivity and area / R and X per km), `L_cab` m, `n_par`, `A_c`, `rho_20`, `alpha`, `T_c`, `R_km`, `X_km`. Limits: `dip_limit`, optional `dip_limit_pcc`. Correction: `cos_phi2` 0.5–1, `harmonics` flag. Every numeric input has a hard range (a value outside it blocks the sheet). Record kinds: `cable.impedance` (R_km, X_km), `conductor.material`, `motor.start`, `starter.limits`, `transformer.data`; per-km R and X are your own datasheet or licensed-table values, never embedded.

**Outputs.** `Z_src`, `Z_m`, `I_rated`, `I_start`, `S_start`, `I_dip`, `V_m`, `V_pcc`, `start_dip`, `dip_pcc`, `dV_cab`, `k_T`, `u_dip` (`u_dip_pcc`), `P_in`, `kvar`, `I_cap`, `I_run1`, `I_run2`, `S_sc`, `h_res`. **Ports.** `start_dip` (`motor.start_dip`, %), `kvar` (`pfc.kvar`, kvar), `I_start` (A, → E4, E5). **Checks.** motor-terminal dip ≤ limit; PCC dip ≤ limit when given. **Warnings.** target power factor not above the motor's (`pfc`); capacitors with a drive or soft starter (`pfc-starter`); harmonic loads flagged, with the resonance order (`harmonics`); unsourced tabulated values.

**Linking.** `I_k` takes E5 `I_k3` (kA); set `c_src` to 1.1 to match E5's voltage factor, and the source impedance then equals E5's `Z_T` (tested). E5 `Z_s` and `I_f` are earth-fault-loop quantities (line to earth through the protective conductor), not the positive-sequence source impedance a motor start sees, so they are not linked. E6 declares no impedance port (`P_dem`, `S_dem`, `I_3ph` only), so `S_n` and `u_k` are entered. `P` takes E1 `P_cont` or `P_pk` (W, converted); `A_c` and `L_cab` take E4 `CSA` and `L`; `I_start` feeds E4 and E5.

**Limitations.** Constant-impedance motor at the locked-rotor instant (conservative: the current falls as the motor accelerates); a soft starter or drive regulates current, so its dip is slightly over-stated. Low-voltage (≤ 1 kV) three-phase systems with a balanced source behind the stated impedance. The capacitor sits at the PCC; switching transients, detuning, self-excitation and the capacitor voltage rating are not assessed. The dip limit and the network operator's PCC limit are yours to enter.

**Test cases.** All analytic and recomputed in python3 with complex arithmetic (Confirmed): `ka-pump-dol-transformer` (75 kW DOL on 500 kVA, 4 %: dip 7.7757 % motor, 4.6746 % PCC, 18.892 kvar, h 25.72), `ka-hand-phasor` (all three impedances at 53.13°: dip exactly 20 % and 10 %, |I| 461.88 A, `dV_cab` 40 V, cross-checked against E3's 40 V in a test), `ka-star-delta-fault-current` (I_k route with c = 1.1), `ka-soft-start-current-limit`, `ka-vfd-current-limit`, `ka-pfc-resonance`. **Not pinned — owner check:** the Schneider Electric *Electrical Installation Guide* power-factor-correction example (the kvar-per-kW factor table and its worked example); no inputs and answer are quoted here because they could not be stated with confidence. The relation it uses, `Q_c = P(tan φ₁ − tan φ₂)`, is covered by the analytic cases above.
