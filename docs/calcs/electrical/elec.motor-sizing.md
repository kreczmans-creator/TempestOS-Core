# E1 — Motor and actuator sizing (`elec.motor-sizing@1.4.0`)

**Scope.** Linear (screw, belt/pulley, rack/pinion) or rotary (direct, gearbox) loads: friction, gravity on an incline, process force, load inertia; reflected inertia and load torque at the motor; trapezoidal or S-curve (sine-acceleration) point-to-point move with dwell; peak and RMS torque, peak speed and power, thermal duty, inertia ratio; candidate-motor checks (peak, continuous, speed) with a safety margin. Since 1.1.0 a rotary load that runs continuously (a pump or fan) can be sized in `duty_mode` = continuous from its steady torque and speed and a run-up time.

**Method.** k = travel per motor radian; J_refl = m k²/η or J_L/(i² η); T_load = F k/η or T_L/(i η); trapezoid v_pk = s/(t_move − t_acc), a = v_pk/t_acc; S-curve a_pk = (π/2) a_trap (D-22); T_acc = J_total α; T_pk = T_acc + T_load; T_rms = √(Σ T_i² t_i / t_cycle) with the dwell at zero torque. **RMS power (1.2.0)**, the thermal-equivalent continuous power a supply sees (regeneration counts as power; the instantaneous peak `P_pk` is not a continuous demand): `P_rms = ω_pk √{[t_acc·(2/3)(T_load² + T_acc²) + T_load² t_const]/t_cycle}` for the trapezoid, with `t_acc·[(3/4) T_load² + (5/16) T_acc²]` for the sine acceleration, from integrating (T·ω)² over the phases; in continuous duty `P_rms = P_cont`. It is the port the motor-circuit pack links to E6 (G-B-11).

**Continuous duty (1.1.0, rotary mechanisms only).** `ω = 2π n_L i/60`; `T_load = T_L/(i η)`; `T_rms = T_load`; `T_pk = J_total ω/t_start + T_load` (the whole inertia is accelerated linearly to speed in `t_start` while carrying the full load: conservative); `n_pk = n_L i`; `P_cont = T_load ω`; **`P_rated = T_rated·ω_rated` (1.3.0, rated speed 1.4.0)**, the candidate motor's nameplate rated shaft power from its rated torque `T_rated` and its rated speed `n_rated` (`ω_rated = 2π n_rated/60`; an optional input in the `motor.rating` record with the motor's other ratings): the power a supply cable or drive for the motor must be sized for, as distinct from `P_cont`, the power the load takes. The running speed is not a substitute (a 4-pole motor runs near, not at, its rated speed): without `n_rated` there is no `P_rated` and the sheet warns (`rated-speed`); a pack that binds `P_rated` downstream (the cable step) is refused without it, with the reason naming `n_rated`, and `n_rated` entered on a point-to-point sheet is ignored with the warning `rated-speed-ignored`. The hydraulic power unit pack links `P_rated` to the cable step. The move distance, move time, acceleration fraction, dwell and profile are ignored, with the notice `continuous-ignored`. `n_L`, `t_start` and `J_L` are required (block `continuous-input`, message starting with the symbol), and a linear mechanism is refused. The input is named `duty_mode`, because `duty` is already the thermal-duty output. Point-to-point is the default and its numbers are unchanged.

**Inputs.** `mechanism`, `i`, `eta`, `p_screw`, `D_pulley`, `J_mech`, `m`, `mu`, `theta`, `F_p`, `J_L`, `T_L`, `duty_mode`, `n_L`, `t_start`, `profile`, `s`, `t_move`, `f_acc`, `t_dwell`, `J_motor`, `T_rated`, `T_peak`, `n_rated` (optional), `n_max`, `SF`, `J_ratio_max`. **Outputs.** `k`, `T_load`, `J_refl`, `J_total`, `v_pk`/`theta_m`, `a_pk`, `alpha_pk`, `T_acc`, `T_pk`, `T_dec`, `T_rms`, `n_pk`, `P_pk`, `P_rms`, `P_cont` and `P_rated` (continuous duty), `J_ratio`, `duty`, `u_peak`, `u_rms`, `u_speed`. **Checks.** peak torque, RMS torque, speed (with SF); warnings for inertia ratio, overhauling, triangular profile, and a direct-coupled rotary load left at an overall efficiency below 0.95 (`eta`, G-B-18c).

**Test cases.** Ball-screw slide and geared index table, both analytic (Confirmed; P_rms 784.142 W and 101.652 W, the first checked by numerical integration); continuous pump duty through a 4:1 gearbox at 700 rpm with a 2 s run-up (analytic, Confirmed): ω = 293.2153 rad/s, T_load = 0.833333 N·m, T_pk = 1.008448 N·m, T_rms = 0.833333 N·m, P_cont = 244.3461 W, u_peak = 0.210093, u_rms = 0.520833, u_speed = 0.8. Output ports are declared (they were derived before).
