# E4 — Conductor sizing and overload coordination (`elec.conductor-sizing@1.0.0`)

**Scope.** One circuit: the design current, the smallest preferred device rating that carries it, the smallest conductor whose tabulated capacity (the user's own record) carries that rating once the correction factors are applied, and the overload coordination checks. The context choice (BS EN 60204-1 machine wiring, the default, or BS 7671 installation) changes the clause citations and the scope note only, never a number. **Independent check demanded** (DA-12); every numeric input outside its range is a hard FAIL (hard limit = stated range). Ships with E5 (`elec.fault-protection`).

**Method.**

- `I_b = P/(√3·U·cosφ·η)` three-phase, `P/(U·cosφ·η)` single-phase, with `P` the **rated output (shaft) power** and η converting it to input; `P/U` DC, where `P` is the electrical power drawn (power factor and efficiency not applied); or the linked `I_b_in`, which replaces the computed value.
- `I_n` = smallest rating in the `ratings` record with `I_n ≥ I_b`.
- `C_c = 0.725` for a semi-enclosed fuse only (1.45 divided by an assumed fusing factor of 2, derived), else 1.
- `I_t,req = I_n/(C_a·C_g·C_i·C_c)`. `C_a` is entered, or with `derive_Ca` derived as `√[(θ_p − θ_a)/(θ_p − θ_ref)]`, labelled "derived approximation — compare with the licensed table" (θ_p 70 °C for PVC, 90 °C for XLPE, the entered `theta_p` for "user"; θ_ref default 30 °C).
- `CSA` = smallest cross-section in the `ampacity` record with `I_t ≥ I_t,req` (row order does not matter). With the AWG system the row carries the gauge n and the area is the derived geometry `d = 0.127·92^((36 − n)/39) mm`, `A = π d²/4`; no AWG capacity is supplied.
- `I_z = I_t·C_a·C_g·C_i` (the factors actually applied; C_c is not part of it).
- Checks (BS 7671 reg. 433.1.1; BS EN 60204-1 cl. 12.4 and 7.2 in the machine context): `I_b ≤ I_n`, `I_n ≤ I_z`, `I₂ ≤ 1.45·I_z`. For MCB types B, C, D `I₂ = 1.45·I_n` is inherent. For a gG fuse no `I₂` is shown: the condition is deemed satisfied when `I_n ≤ I_z` (BS 7671 reg. 433.1.201, cited by number; a pass/fail condition on the sheet). For a semi-enclosed fuse `I₂ = 2·I_n` by default (reg. 433.1.202), or the `I2` input. For an MCCB or MPCB `I₂` comes from the `I2` input (without it a warning is raised and the check is skipped). The two regulation numbers 433.1.201 and 433.1.202 are cited as given to me and are to be checked against the owner's copy.

**Inputs.** `context`, `supply`, `P` 0.01–500 kW (rated output power), `U` 12–1000 V, `cos_phi` 0.5–1, `eta` 0.5–1, optional `I_b_in` (A); `L` 0.1–500 m, `theta_a` −10 to 70 °C, `insulation` (PVC 70 °C / XLPE 90 °C / user), optional `theta_p` 50–250 °C, optional `method` (descriptive key such as B2, C, E), `N_g` 1–20 grouped circuits, `system` (metric / AWG); `device` (MCB type B, C, D, gG fuse, semi-enclosed fuse, MCCB, MPCB), optional `I2` (MCCB, MPCB; semi-enclosed fuse default 2·`I_n`); `C_a`, `C_g`, `C_i` 0.1–1.5 (default 1; record kind `cable.correction-factors`), `derive_Ca`, `theta_ref`; tables `ratings` (`I_n`; record kind `device.ratings`) and `ampacity` (`CSA` mm² or `AWG` gauge, and `I_t` A; record kind `cable.ampacity`). **No ampacity, rating or correction-factor value ships**: the tables are empty until the user enters them from their own licensed copy. `N_g` is carried to the sheet; `C_g` is not looked up from it, and `C_g = 1` with more than one circuit raises a warning.

**Outputs.** `I_b`, `I_n`, `C_c`, `C_a`, `I_t_req`, `CSA`, `I_z`, `L`, `u_Ib = I_b/I_n`, `u_In = I_n/I_z`, `I2` and `u_I2 = I₂/(1.45 I_z)` (MCB, semi-enclosed fuse, or an entered `I2`), and `gauge` (AWG system). **Ports.** `I_b` (A), `I_n` (A), `CSA` (mm²), `I_z` (A), `L` (m) → E3 (CSA, length), E5 (CSA, device), E2. **Checks.** `I_b ≤ I_n`, `I_n ≤ I_z`, `I₂ ≤ 1.45 I_z`, each PASS/FAIL with its utilisation. **Blocks** (each starts with the symbol and a colon): `ampacity:` empty table (enter your own licensed capacities, record kind `cable.ampacity`) or a non-integer AWG gauge ("AWG gauge must be a whole number"); `ratings:` empty table; `I_n:` no rating of at least `I_b`; `CSA:` no conductor carries `I_t,req`; `theta_p:`, `theta_a:` or `C_a:` when a derived factor cannot be formed.

**Limitations.** Single circuit, one installation method per run; the factors are applied as a product and are the user's; the derived `C_a` is a temperature-difference approximation only; the I²t/short-circuit and voltage-drop checks are E5 and E3; E5 accepts a line conductor of 0.5 mm² and above, so a selected CSA below 0.5 mm² (AWG 21 and finer) cannot be linked to E5; no cable ampacity, rating factor or standard table is embedded (§A5).

**Test cases.** All analytic and Confirmed, recomputed in Python. The capacities (1.5→12.3, 2.5→17.7, 4→24.1, 6→31.9, 10→44.6 A; AWG 14→11.2, 12→16.9, 10→26.3, 8→37.4 A) and the factors (C_a 0.91, C_g 0.77) are synthetic values invented for these tests, not taken from any table; the ratings 6, 10, 16, 20, 25, 32, 40, 50, 63 A are the typical preferred series. `ka-three-phase-mcb-c` (θ_a 35 °C, two circuits; I_b 14.1507 A, I_n 16 A, I_t,req 22.8343 A, 4 mm², I_z 16.8869 A), `ka-single-phase` (I_b 15.2555 A, 2.5 mm², I_z 17.7 A), `ka-dc` (I_b 25 A, I_t,req 27.4725 A, 6 mm², I_z 29.029 A), `ka-semi-enclosed` (C_c 0.725, I_t,req 27.5862 A, 6 mm², I₂ 40 A), `ka-derived-ca` (C_a = √(30/40) = 0.866025), `ka-awg-10` (AWG 10 = 5.2612 mm²). Not pinned — owner check: the Schneider *Electrical Installation Guide* chapter G worked examples, the IET *Electrical Installation Design Guide* examples and the Kollmorgen current-carrying-capacity note; no value from them is in source, tests or docs. The worked example `conveyor-motor-circuit` (4 kW rated output, type C MCB, machine panel) uses synthetic values entered with record-style provenance.
