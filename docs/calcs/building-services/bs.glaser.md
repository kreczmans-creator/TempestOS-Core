# B7 — Glaser condensation (`bs.glaser@1.2.0`)

**Scope.** Interstitial condensation in a layered wall by the Glaser method over twelve monthly steps, and the surface condensation check, in the form ISO 13788 describes: the temperature and saturation pressure at every layer interface, the vapour-pressure line with its tangent construction at the condensation planes, the condensate on each interface month by month carried forward from the first month of condensation (drying when the weather turns), the annual balance, the peak accumulation against the owner's limit, and the surface temperature factor f_Rsi against f_Rsi,min at the critical surface humidity. A Glaser diagram (saturation line, vapour-pressure line, temperature) is returned as series and as vectors for the chosen month.

**Method.** `R_j = d_j/λ_j`, `s_d,j = μ_j·d_j`, `R_T = R_si + ΣR_j + R_se`; node temperature `θ_k = θ_i − (θ_i − θ_e)(R_si + Σ_(j≤k)R_j)/R_T` (the same series-resistance arithmetic as B4, and the same `layers` table shape plus a `mu` column). Saturation pressure by the Magnus forms `610.5·exp(17.269θ/(237.3 + θ))` over water (θ ≥ 0 °C) and `610.5·exp(21.875θ/(265.5 + θ))` over ice; `p_sat(20 °C) = 2336.95 Pa`. Outside pressure `p_e = RH_e·p_sat(θ_e)`; inside pressure `p_i = RH_i·p_sat(θ_i)`, or `p_e + f_Δp·Δv(θ_e)·R_v·(T_i + T_e)/2` with Δv linear between two user points and constant beyond them, the mean of the inside and the month's outside temperature (in K) and a safety factor `f_Δp` (`dp_f`, a record, default 1.10, applied to the pressure difference in both the interstitial and the surface check; R_v = 461.5 J/(kg·K), derived R/M_w; limited to saturation with a warning). The sheet states that the clause wording (mean temperature and margin) must be checked against the owner's copy of ISO 13788. Vapour flux through a layer `g = δ0·Δp/Δs_d`, `δ0 = 2×10⁻¹⁰ kg/(m·s·Pa)`.

*Tangent construction.* The profile is the line from `p_i` to `p_e` bent down onto the saturation line at the interfaces it would rise above, and held on it at every wet interface (one carrying condensate). From a contact point the next contact is the point with the smallest slope to it among the saturation values of the interfaces ahead and the end pressure; that leaves every saturation point in between on or above the line, and gives the convex kinks at which moisture condenses. Net moisture arriving at a contact interface `g_c = g_in − g_out = δ0(slope_after − slope_before)` ≥ 0 at a free contact, either sign at a wet one.

*Monthly accumulation.* Months run in the order of the climate record. A month condenses if the dry profile touches saturation anywhere. Accumulation starts in the first month that condenses after one that does not (row 1 if every month condenses) and runs for twelve months carrying `Mc_k` forward: `Mc_k ← max(0, Mc_k + g_c·t)` where the interface is a contact or wet. A wet interface is held at saturation and dries at the month's rate until nothing is left; one that dries inside a month is taken as dry from the next (the month's rates are those of its starting state). `t` is the row's `days`; where blank, the **calendar days of the month with the rows taken as January to December** (February 28), stated on the sheet (enter the days if the record starts in another month). Annual balance PASS when nothing remains after the twelve months (`Mc_end`); `Mc_max` is the most held on any one interface in any month, checked against the user's `Mc_limit`.

*Surface factor.* `f_Rsi = 1 − R_si,f/R_T,f` (R_si,f = `Rsi_f`, default **0.25 m²K/W, the conventional value for an opaque element** and a recorded input; 0.13 is for glazing; the sheet warns below 0.25); for each month with the outside air at least 1 K colder than the inside, `θ_si,min` solves `p_sat(θ_si,min) = p_i/φ_crit` (the Magnus form inverted) and `f_Rsi,min = max_month (θ_si,min − θ_e)/(θ_i − θ_e)`, floored at 0. PASS when `f_Rsi ≥ f_Rsi,min` (utilisation `f_Rsi,min/f_Rsi`).

**Inputs.**
- `element` (text, optional) picks the rows of the `layers` table whose `element` cell matches (blank = all rows), so a B4 layers table can be pasted in whole; `layers` (≤ 30 rows, inside first): `element`, `name`, `d` 0.01–2000 mm (soft 1–500), `lambda` 0.005–10 W/(m·K), `mu` ≥ 1 (hard), record `material.conductivity`.
- `U_ref` (optional, W/(m²·K), 0.01–10): the U-value of the same wall from the room heat-loss calculation (B4 `U_1`, linked in the envelope-moisture chain). The sheet reports `U_wall = 1/R_T` and `dU_ref = |U_wall − U_ref|/U_ref` and warns (`u-ref-mismatch`, not a check) when it exceeds 2 %, i.e. when the two calculations describe different constructions. Added in 1.2.0: the heat-loss step offers no per-layer output to link, so the layers are typed in both and this value is what ties them.
- `Rsi` (default 0.13), `Rse` (0.04) m²K/W, `Rsi_f` (default 0.25, for the surface check; all three hard-limited above zero, since R_si = 0 would make f_Rsi = 1 and always pass), record `surface.resistance`.
- `theta_int` 0–40 °C (default 20); `humidity_mode` constant RH (`RH_int` 1–100 %, default 50) or moisture excess from two points `dv_a`/`theta_a`, `dv_b`/`theta_b` (record `climate.humidity-class`, which also holds `dp_f`, the safety factor on Δp, 1–1.5, default 1.10).
- `climate` (exactly 12 rows): `month` (label), `theta_e` −60…60 °C, `RH_e` 0–100 %, `days` (optional; blank = calendar days, rows January to December) — the user's own monthly climate, record `climate.monthly`. **No climate data of ISO 13788 (or of any source) ships.**
- `RH_crit` (default 80 %, 30–100), `Mc_limit` (optional, g/m²; blank = Mc_max is reported with no limit check), record `criteria.condensation`; `breathable` (flag), and an optional `timber` flag column on the layers table; `diagram_month` (1–12, blank = the month of the peak, or the coldest if nothing condenses).

The layer λ is a column of a table, so it cannot carry a W3 `MaterialProperty` (that binds one scalar input to a library property); it is a `material.conductivity` record like B4's. Nothing from a standard (material μ values, humidity-class table, limits, climate) is embedded.

**Outputs.** Per layer `R_j`, `sd_j`; `R_T`, `U_wall`, `dU_ref` (with `U_ref`), `S_d`, `psat_i`; vectors `p_ext_m`, `p_int_m` (Pa by month); `n_start` (row the accumulation starts in; 0 = no condensation); `Mc_1…Mc_12` (condensate on the wall at the end of each month, g/m², in the climate's row order, with the month's change in the working); `Mc_if_k` (peak on each interface); `Mc_max`, `Mc_tot` (peak of the whole wall), `Mc_end`; for the diagram month `n_diag` and the vectors `sd_if`, `x_if`, `theta_if`, `psat_if`, `p_if` with series "Saturation pressure", "Vapour pressure" (against s_d) and "Temperature" (against depth); `R_Tf`, `fRsi`, `fRsi_min`, `n_fcrit`, `u_f`, `u_Mc`, `pass`. **Ports.** `Mc_max` (g/m²), `fRsi`, `pass` (1 when every check passes, else 0). **Checks.** Annual moisture balance; peak condensate against `Mc_limit` (when given); f_Rsi against f_Rsi,min. **Warnings.** `en15026` when the build-up is flagged timber or breathable, or when any layer is flagged `timber` (the warning names the layers; use an EN 15026 hygrothermal simulation); `rsi-f-low` when `Rsi_f` is below 0.25; a single layer has no interface; the humidity class exceeds saturation; no month cold enough for a surface requirement; rows of another element ignored.

**Limitations.** Steady state in each month with monthly mean climate: no air movement, liquid transport, moisture storage, rain or solar gain; λ and μ constant (no moisture dependence); condensation only at the interfaces between layers (split a thick layer where the plane could lie inside it; the saturation line is joined straight between interface values, which slightly understates the risk for convex p_sat). A single constant internal temperature; the humidity class is two user points (linear, constant beyond), not a table. The safety margin on the internal humidity is the Δp factor of the moisture-excess mode only; in the constant-humidity mode none is applied (add it to `RH_int`). Condensation is found only at the interfaces between layers; splitting a layer into sub-layers made no difference in any test case or worked example, so this is a stated limit, not a defect. Not for timber, breathable or hygroscopic build-ups (EN 15026); not an assessment of the risk of mould growth or of the surface moisture content.

**Test cases (analytic, Confirmed; recomputed in python3, the profile also against an independent obstacle-problem solve).** `ka-seasonal-drying` (100 mm insulation λ 0.04 μ 2 on 200 mm brick λ 0.8 μ 10, R_T 2.92 m²K/W, S_d 2.2 m, in an invented maritime year with calendar-day months: accumulation from November, peak 3083.081 g/m² in March, dry by June, Mc_end 0, balance PASS, peak against a 3000 g/m² limit u = 1.0277); `ka-psat-20` (p_sat(20 °C) = 2336.951 Pa, i.e. the brief's 2337 Pa to its rounding, and a plaster and brick wall at 40 % internal humidity that never condenses and passes the surface check); `ka-single-interface-all-year` (constant 0 °C / 90 %: 38.7385 g/m² a day, 1200.895 g/m² in January, 14139.573 g/m² after the 365 days, balance FAIL); `ka-two-planes-tangent` (five layers, contacts at interfaces 2 and 4: 5143.147 and 772.008 g/m² after twelve months); `ka-surface-factor` (R_si,f 0.25: f_Rsi 0.575372 < f_Rsi,min 0.590256, FAIL); `ka-u-ref-cross-check` (the plaster and brick wall: U_wall = 1/0.46875 = 2.133333 W/m²K, 3.0303 % from a U_ref of 2.2: warns); `ka-humidity-class` (Δv 5 g/m³ at ≤ 0 °C to 0 at 20 °C with the 1.10 margin on Δp: start in October, peak 5111.052 g/m², 91.744 g/m² left, balance FAIL, f_Rsi,min 0.670474) and `ka-humidity-class-no-margin` (f_Δp = 1: peak 4249.892 g/m², PASS balance, f_Rsi,min 0.630284). The climate is invented and every λ, μ and thickness is an illustrative input, not design data. **A published Glaser worked example (an ISO 13788 annex example, a textbook example, or a Condensa/WUFI run) is not pinned**: no example's inputs and answer could be stated from memory with confidence, so none is entered; it needs the owner's copy (see "not pinned" in the build report). Worked examples: `internal-insulation-no-vcl` (peak 3022.6 g/m² against a 500 g/m² limit, FAIL), `internal-insulation-with-vcl` (no condensation, PASS).

**Citations to check.** ISO 13788 clause numbers (cl. 5 surface temperature factor, cl. 6 interstitial condensation) are cited from memory for the structure of the standard and are to be checked against the owner's copy; no text of the standard is reproduced. The δ0, Magnus coefficients and R_v are closed-form physics; the calendar-day month with rows taken as January to December, the 1 K threshold for the surface requirement and the "first month of condensation after a dry month" start rule are this product's reading and are stated on the sheet.
