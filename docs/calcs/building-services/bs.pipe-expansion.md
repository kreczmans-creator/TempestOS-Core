# B8 — Pipe expansion, loops and anchor forces (`bs.pipe-expansion@1.2.0`)

**Screening only — not a piping stress analysis.** The sheet says so in its scope and in its first assumption.

**Scope.** Thermal growth of a straight run between anchors; the fully restrained stress and force; the leg an offset leg or a U-loop needs to absorb the growth within the owner's allowable displacement-stress range `Sa`, and whether the available leg (and, for an offset leg or Z-bend, each run) is long enough; the force on the anchors (the resultant of its two perpendicular components) and the largest root moment of the guided-cantilever model; the screening ratio `D·Y/(L − U)²` against the owner's `K1` (PASS, or "needs formal stress analysis"); and an axial bellows layout whose anchor force is `P·A_eff` plus its spring force. Building Services, Thermal. Cites ASME B31.3 para. 319.4.1, 319.3.2 and 319.5 and EN 13480-3 cl. 12 by number only (to be checked against the owner's copy); no text of either is reproduced.

**Method.** `d_i = OD − 2t`, `A_m = π/4(OD² − d_i²)`, `I = π/64(OD⁴ − d_i⁴)`, `Z = I/(OD/2)`. `ΔT = T_operate − T_install` (or entered; negative for a cold line). `ΔL = α·L·|ΔT|` (magnitude; `dL_signed = α·L·ΔT` and the text `direction` give the sign: growth presses on the anchors, contraction pulls). Fully restrained: `σ_r = E·α·|ΔT|`, `F_r = E_r·α·|ΔT|·A_m`, `u_res = σ_r/Sa` (information: above 1 the movement has to be absorbed).

*Moduli.* `E` is the modulus at the **installation (reference) temperature**, the one used for the stress check (`L_req`, `σ_leg`, `σ_r`), as the piping codes do (ASME B31.3 para. 319.3.2 and 319.5, to be checked against the owner's copy); a hot modulus would lower the stress and understate the need. The optional `E_hot` (the modulus at the operating temperature) is used **only for the reactions** (`F_res`, `F_leg`, `M_leg`, `F_run`, `M_run`, hence the ports); `E_r = E_hot`, else `E`.

*Guided cantilever.* Each leg is fixed against rotation at both ends and loaded by the movement alone: required leg `L_req = √(3·E·OD·Δ/Sa)`, the leg at which the root bending stress `M/Z` reaches `Sa`; on the available leg `H`: `F = 12·E_r·I·Δ/H³`, `M = 6·E_r·I·Δ/H²` (= `F·H/2`), `σ_leg = 3·E·OD·Δ/H² = Sa·(L_req/H)²`. PASS when `L_req ≤ H`.

*Loop model.* Layout **offset leg** (an L- or Z-bend anchored at both ends): the leg takes the whole movement, `Δ = ΔL` with `ΔL = α·L_tot·|ΔT|`, and **the run or runs bend for the growth of the leg**, `Δ_leg = α·|ΔT|·H`. An **L-bend** has one run `L`: `σ_run = 3·E·OD·Δ_leg/L²`, `u_run = √(σ_run/Sa)` (so the run needed is `L_req,run = L·u_run = √(3·E·OD·Δ_leg/Sa)`, PASS when `u_run ≤ 1`), `F_run = 12·E_r·I·Δ_leg/L³`, `M_run = F_run·L/2`. A **Z-bend** adds the optional second run `L2` on the other side of the leg (offset-leg layout only; `L_tot = L + L2` is the length that grows and the leg absorbs the growth of both): the two runs act **in series under one force** `F_run = 12·E_r·I·Δ_leg/(L³ + L2³)`, run `i` taking the share `L_i³/(L³ + L2³)` of `Δ_leg`, root stress `σ_i = 3·E·OD·Δ_i/L_i²` and moment `F_run·L_i/2`, **and each run is checked as its own cantilever** (`u_run`, `u_run2`); the stiffer (shorter) run carries the lower stress. Layout **U-loop**: two identical legs in series, each a guided cantilever taking half the growth, `Δ = ΔL/2`; the cross leg is a rigid guide and its width does not enter; the force along the line is the same `F` in both legs; `M` is the moment at the leg roots (the loop corners). A U-loop needs `L_req(offset)/√2` and loads the anchors with half the force and moment of an offset leg of the same length. The growth of the loop's own legs, which the cross leg has to take, is not checked.

*Forces on the anchors.* The leg's force `F_leg` acts along the run and the runs' force `F_run` acts along the leg, so the two components are perpendicular: `anchor_force = √(F_leg² + F_run²)`, with the components also reported as `F_along_run` and `F_along_leg` (for a square L-bend the resultant is √2 times either). A U-loop has only `F_along_run`. **`anchor_moment` is the largest root moment of the legs and runs in the guided-cantilever simplification (`M = F·L/2`, each end fixed against rotation); it is not the moment on a frame anchor**, which depends on the whole frame (the other legs' stiffness, the elbow rotations, the anchor's own fixity); a formal analysis is needed before an anchor is sized for moment.

*Screening.* `OD·Y/(L_dev − U)² ≤ K1` where `Y` is the resultant displacement, **the free growth between the anchors `Y = α·|ΔT|·U`** (not `ΔL` of the run alone), `L_dev` the developed length and `U` the straight distance between the anchors: offset leg or Z-bend `L_dev = L_tot + H`, `U = √(L_tot² + H²)`; U-loop `L_dev = L + 2H`, `U = L` (so `Y = ΔL`). For a square 10 m × 10 m L-bend the ratio is √2 times what the run's growth alone would give. PASS, or "needs formal stress analysis" (a failure is not an overstress; it means the exemption does not apply). `K1` is a record in whatever consistent unit the code states it (`mm²/m²`, `in²/ft²`, …); the ratio is dimensionless and the engine converts. **Without a `K1` the ratio is shown, not judged; `screen` says "not evaluated … (treat as: needs formal stress analysis)" and `pass` is 0.**

*Axial bellows.* Third layout: the bellows absorbs the movement, so **no leg check, no `Sa` and no screening**. `anchor_force = P_b·A_eff + k_b·ΔL` (`P_b` is the **governing** gauge pressure, ≥ 0, the design or the test pressure that governs the anchor sizing; **a bellows' compression and extension ratings can differ**, so enter the governing one; manufacturer's effective area, optional spring rate), `anchor_moment = 0`; when the rated axial movement `dL_rated` is given, `u_bellows = ΔL/dL_rated` is checked; `pass` is 1 only when a rating was given and met. Guide friction, lateral and angular movement and the bellows' other limits are the manufacturer's. Bellows inputs given with a leg or loop layout are not used and raise the `bellows-unused` warning (prefixed with the first such input); `K1` given with the bellows layout raises the separate `k1-unused` warning.

**Inputs.** `E` (GPa, 0.01–1000 hard) and `alpha` (µm/(m·K), 0.1–1000 hard) carry W3 `MaterialProperty` `E` and `alpha` and are listed in the `material.properties` record kind: apply the material at the installation temperature; `alpha` must be the mean coefficient between the install and operating temperatures. `E_hot` optional, typed (not mapped to W3). `OD`, `t_wall` (mm; wall must be under half the diameter, else block; record `pipe.section`) — **plain inputs: B1 (`bs.pipe-pump`) declares no OD or wall port** (its ports are flow, head, pressure, power and speed), so there is nothing to bind today; the two quantity inputs accept a link as soon as an upstream calculation declares them. `L` (anchor spacing, 0.1–10 000 m; the first run of an offset leg or Z-bend), `L2` (optional second run of a Z-bend, offset-leg layout only; ignored with `l2-unused` otherwise). `temp_mode`, `T_install`, `T_operate` (degC) or `dT_in` (K, signed, −300 … 1000 hard); zero change blocks. `layout` (offset leg, U-loop, axial bellows). **Records with no default:** `H` (available leg, 0.01–1000 m; required for a leg or loop), `Sa` (record `pipe.allowable-range`; required for a leg or loop — the sheet blocks "Sa: enter …"), `K_scr` (`K1`, record `pipe.screening-k1`; optional). Bellows: `P_b` (bar gauge, ≥ 0; a vacuum bellows is outside this sheet), `A_eff` (cm²), `k_b` (N/mm), `dL_rated` (mm), record `bellows.data`.

**Outputs.** The section, `dT`, `dL`, `dL_signed`, `direction`, `sigma_res`, `F_res` are produced before `Sa` is asked for, so they are on the sheet even while it is blocked on `Sa` or `H`. Then `u_res`, `Delta`, `L_req`, `u_leg`, `F_leg`, `M_leg`, `sigma_leg`, `u_sigma`; offset leg only `L_tot`, `Delta_run`, `S_run`, `F_run`, `sigma_run`, `L_req_run`, `u_run`, `M_run` (and `sigma_run2`, `L_req_run2`, `u_run2`, `M_run2` for a Z-bend); `F_along_run`, `F_along_leg`, `anchor_force`, `anchor_moment`, `L_dev`, `U_a`, `Y_scr`, `scr`, `u_scr` (with `K1`), `screen` (text), `pass`; axial bellows `F_thrust`, `F_spring`, `u_bellows`. **Ports** `exp.anchor_force` → `anchor_force` (N; the resultant of the force along the run and the force along the leg, or thrust plus spring for a bellows), `exp.anchor_moment` → `anchor_moment` (N·m; the largest root moment of the guided-cantilever model, not a frame anchor moment; zero for a bellows), `exp.dL` → `dL` (mm, a magnitude; `dL_signed` carries the sign). **Checks.** Available leg against required leg; each run against its required length (offset leg: the run; Z-bend: the first and the second run); screening `u_scr ≤ 1` (named "Screening only … not met: the line needs a formal stress analysis") when `K1` is given; movement against rating (bellows). `pass` = 1 only when `K1` is given and every check passes (leg layouts). **Warnings.** `short-leg` (the available leg, or for an offset leg a run, under ten pipe diameters: the beam model does not hold; the message starts with the input concerned, `H`, `L` or `L2`), `bellows-unused`, `screening-k1` (no `K1`).

**Limitations.** Screening only. Guided-cantilever beam model: straight uniform slender leg, rigid elbows. **No stress-intensification factor or elbow flexibility is applied, so the stress at the elbow root can be higher than the beam stress** (stated as its own sheet assumption). No pressure or ovalisation effects, torsion or out-of-plane legs, multi-plane routing, branches or tees, support friction, weight, spring hangers or cold spring, sustained or occasional loads, or fatigue cycle count beyond what `Sa` contains. A Z-bend has two parallel runs in one plane only; a three-dimensional or multi-leg route needs a formal analysis. `E` and `α` are single values. **No allowable stress, K1 or code constant is shipped** (§B5): `Sa` and `K1` are the owner's records from the owner's own copy of the code, with provenance; the invented `Sa` and `K1` in the test cases and worked examples are illustrative inputs, not code values.

**Test cases (analytic, Confirmed; recomputed in python3).**
- `ka-offset-leg-steel-50m` — steel 88.9 × 3.2 mm, E 210 GPa, α 12 µm/(m·K), 10 → 80 °C, 50 m: **ΔL = 42 mm**, σ_r = 176.4 MPa, F_r = 151.98 kN, A_m = 861.55 mm²; invented Sa 100 MPa, 6 m offset leg: L_req = 4.85005 m (u 0.80834), F = 388.11 N, M = 1164.33 N·m, σ_leg 65.34 MPa; the run needs 1.68010 m (u 0.0336) for the leg's 5.04 mm and pushes 0.080 N along the leg, so the anchors take the resultant 388.10882 N; Y = 42.301 mm (U 50.3587 m, L_dev 56 m), ratio 118.168 against an invented K1 of 100 mm²/m², so "needs formal stress analysis".
- `ka-u-loop-too-short` — the same line with a U-loop of two 3 m legs: Δ = 21 mm, L_req = 3.42950 m (u 1.14317, FAIL), F = 1552.44 N, M = 2328.65 N·m, ratio 103.717 (Y = 42 mm), K1 120 met.
- `ka-cold-copper-loop` — 28 × 1.2 mm copper, E 120 GPa, α 16.6, 30 m, ΔT = −40 K entered: ΔL = 19.92 mm (`dL_signed` −19.92), σ_r = 79.68 MPa, F_r = 8.0504 kN; U-loop legs 1.5 m, invented Sa 60 MPa: L_req = 1.29355 m (u 0.86237), F = 38.62 N, M = 28.97 N·m, ratio 61.97 (K1 150 met), pass.
- `ka-axial-bellows` — the 50 m steel line on a bellows at 6 bar, A_eff 80 cm², 120 N/mm, rated 50 mm: thrust 4800 N, spring 5040 N, anchors 9840 N, u 0.84, pass.

- `ka-z-bend-steel` — the same steel with runs L = 30 m and L2 = 20 m either side of the 6 m leg: ΔL = 42 mm, leg as in the first case, F_run = 0.287422 N, run stresses 241.95 and 161.30 kPa (u 0.049188 and 0.040162, moments 4.3113 and 2.8742 N·m), anchors 388.10892 N.

**Worked examples.** `lthw-main-expansion-loop` (U-loop of two 4 m legs on a 50 m steel LTHW main: 3.43 m required, passes, 655 N on the anchors, screen passes), `copper-offset-leg-too-short` (24 m copper riser, a 2.5 m offset leg against 2.82 m required fails both the leg check and the screen; the run is not the problem) and `copper-axial-bellows` (the same riser on a bellows: 2.4 kN thrust plus 1.96 kN spring, 4.36 kN on the anchors, within the rated movement). E and α are the W3 seed values (`Carbon steel (generic)`, `Copper (generic)`), labelled as assumed and indicative.

**Version 1.1.0 (review fixes).** Screening `Y` is the resultant `α·|ΔT|·U` (was `ΔL`: unconservative for an offset leg); the run of an offset leg is checked for the leg's growth; the axial bellows layout; `E` is the reference-temperature modulus with an optional `E_hot` for the reactions; `pass` is 0 without a `K1`; the section, `ΔL` and `F_r` no longer wait for `Sa`; `dL_signed` and `direction`; `dT_in` limit −300 K; the short-leg warning is about the available leg only; the elbow intensification factor is its own assumption.

**Version 1.2.0 (second review).** `anchor_force` is the resultant `√(F_leg² + F_run²)` of the perpendicular components (also reported separately); `anchor_moment` is stated as the guided-cantilever simplification and not a frame anchor moment; the optional second run `L2` makes the offset-leg layout a Z-bend, each run checked as its own cantilever (replacing the earlier advice to enter a combined spacing); the method text is reworded; `k1-unused` is its own warning and the warnings are prefixed with the input they are about; `P_b` is the governing pressure and the compression/extension rating note is added.
