# B8 — Pipe expansion, loops and anchor forces (`bs.pipe-expansion@1.0.0`)

**Screening only — not a piping stress analysis.** The sheet says so in its scope and in its first assumption.

**Scope.** Thermal growth of a straight run between anchors; the fully restrained stress and force; the leg an offset leg or a U-loop needs to absorb the growth within the owner's allowable displacement-stress range `Sa`, and whether the available leg is long enough; the force and moment that leg puts on its anchors; the screening ratio `D·Y/(L − U)²` against the owner's `K1` (PASS, or "needs formal stress analysis"); and the pressure thrust `P·A_eff` of a bellows. Building Services, Thermal. Cites ASME B31.3 para. 319.4.1 and EN 13480-3 cl. 12 by number only (to be checked against the owner's copy); no text of either is reproduced.

**Method.** `d_i = OD − 2t`, `A_m = π/4(OD² − d_i²)`, `I = π/64(OD⁴ − d_i⁴)`, `Z = I/(OD/2)`. `ΔT = T_operate − T_install` (or entered; negative for a cold line; the movement is taken for `|ΔT|`). `ΔL = α·L·|ΔT|`. Fully restrained: `σ_r = E·α·|ΔT|`, `F_r = σ_r·A_m`, `u_res = σ_r/Sa` (information: above 1 the movement has to be absorbed). Each leg is a **guided cantilever** (fixed against rotation at both ends, loaded by the movement alone): required leg `L_req = √(3·E·OD·Δ/Sa)`, the leg at which the root bending stress `M/Z` reaches `Sa`; on the available leg `H`: `F = 12·E·I·Δ/H³`, `M = 6·E·I·Δ/H²` (= `F·H/2`), `σ_leg = M/Z = 3·E·OD·Δ/H² = Sa·(L_req/H)²`. PASS when `L_req ≤ H`.

*Loop model.* Layout **offset leg** (an L- or Z-bend): the leg takes the whole movement, `Δ = ΔL`. Layout **U-loop**: two identical legs in series, each a guided cantilever taking half the growth, `Δ = ΔL/2`; the cross leg is a rigid guide and its width does not enter. The force along the line is the same `F` in both legs; `M` is the moment at the leg roots (the loop corners) and reaches an anchor only where the leg ends at the anchor (an offset leg). Consequence of the model: a U-loop of legs `H` needs `L_req(offset)/√2`, and loads the anchors with half the force and moment of an offset leg of the same length.

*Screening.* `OD·Y/(L_dev − U)² ≤ K1` with `Y = ΔL` (the resultant displacement of the single direction of movement), `L_dev` the developed length and `U` the straight distance between the anchors: offset leg `L_dev = L + H`, `U = √(L² + H²)`; U-loop `L_dev = L + 2H`, `U = L`. PASS, or "needs formal stress analysis" (a failure is not an overstress; it means the exemption does not apply). `K1` is a record in whatever consistent unit the code states it (`mm²/m²`, `in²/ft²`, …); the ratio is dimensionless and the engine converts. Without a `K1` the ratio is shown, not judged, and the result text says "not evaluated … (treat as: needs formal stress analysis)". **Bellows:** `F_p = P_b·A_eff` (gauge pressure, manufacturer's effective area); the spring force of the bellows and guide friction are not included.

**Inputs.** `E` (GPa, 0.01–1000 hard) and `alpha` (µm/(m·K), 0.1–1000 hard) carry W3 `MaterialProperty` `E` and `alpha` and are listed in the `material.properties` record kind: apply the material **at the operating temperature**; `alpha` must be the mean coefficient between the install and operating temperatures (the library's temperature tables give the value at one temperature). `OD`, `t_wall` (mm; wall must be under half the diameter, else block; record `pipe.section`) — **plain inputs: B1 (`bs.pipe-pump`) declares no OD or wall port** (its ports are flow, head, pressure, power and speed), so there is nothing to bind today; the two quantity inputs accept a link as soon as an upstream calculation declares them. `L` (anchor spacing, 0.1–10 000 m). `temp_mode` (install and operating temperatures, or temperature change entered), `T_install`, `T_operate` (degC) or `dT_in` (K, signed); zero change blocks. `layout` (offset leg or U-loop), `H` (available leg, each leg of a U-loop, 0.01–1000 m). **Records with no default:** `Sa` (record `pipe.allowable-range`; required — the sheet blocks "Sa: enter …" without it), `K_scr` (`K1`, record `pipe.screening-k1`; optional). `P_b` (bar gauge), `A_eff` (cm², record `bellows.effective-area`), optional, both or neither.

**Outputs.** `D_i`, `A_m`, `I_x`, `Z_x`, `dT`, `dL`, `sigma_res`, `F_res`, `u_res`, `Delta`, `L_req`, `u_leg`, `anchor_force`, `anchor_moment`, `sigma_leg`, `u_sigma`, `L_dev`, `U_a`, `scr`, `u_scr` (with `K1`), `screen` (text: `PASS`, `needs formal stress analysis` or `not evaluated …`), `F_thrust` (with a bellows), `pass`. **Ports** `exp.anchor_force` → `anchor_force` (N), `exp.anchor_moment` → `anchor_moment` (N·m), `exp.dL` → `dL` (mm). **Checks.** Available leg against required leg (`u_leg ≤ 1`); screening (`u_scr ≤ 1`, named "Screening only … not met: the line needs a formal stress analysis", when `K1` is given). **Warnings.** `short-leg` (required or available leg under ten pipe diameters: the beam model does not hold), `bellows-incomplete`, `screening-k1` (no `K1`).

**Limitations.** Screening only. Guided-cantilever beam model: straight uniform slender leg, rigid elbows (no flexibility or stress-intensification factors, no pressure or ovalisation effects), no torsion or out-of-plane legs, no multi-plane routing, branches or tees, no support friction, weight, spring hangers or cold spring, no sustained or occasional loads, no fatigue cycle count beyond what `Sa` already contains. One direction of movement `Y = ΔL`; an L-bend whose leg also moves, or a Z-bend with two growing runs, must be entered as its combined spacing or analysed formally. `E` and `α` are single values. The bellows result is the pressure thrust alone. **No allowable stress, K1 or code constant is shipped** (§B5): `Sa` and `K1` are the owner's records from the owner's own copy of the code, with provenance; the invented `Sa` and `K1` in the test cases and worked examples are illustrative inputs, not code values.

**Test cases (analytic, Confirmed; recomputed in python3).**
- `ka-offset-leg-steel-50m` — steel 88.9 × 3.2 mm, E 210 GPa, α 12 µm/(m·K), 10 → 80 °C, 50 m: **ΔL = 42 mm**, σ_r = 176.4 MPa, F_r = 151.98 kN, A_m = 861.55 mm²; invented Sa 100 MPa, 6 m offset leg: L_req = 4.85005 m (u 0.80834), F = 388.11 N, M = 1164.33 N·m, σ_leg 65.34 MPa; invented K1 100 mm²/m²: ratio 117.33 (L_dev 56 m, U 50.3587 m) so "needs formal stress analysis".
- `ka-u-loop-too-short` — the same line with a U-loop of two 3 m legs: Δ = 21 mm, L_req = 3.42950 m (u 1.14317, FAIL), F = 1552.44 N, M = 2328.65 N·m, ratio 103.717 (L_dev 56 m, U 50 m), K1 120 met.
- `ka-cold-copper-bellows` — 28 × 1.2 mm copper, E 120 GPa, α 16.6, 30 m, ΔT = −40 K entered: ΔL = 19.92 mm, σ_r = 79.68 MPa, F_r = 8.0504 kN; U-loop legs 1.5 m, invented Sa 60 MPa: L_req = 1.29355 m (u 0.86237), F = 38.62 N, M = 28.97 N·m, ratio 61.97 (K1 150 met); bellows 10 bar × 50 cm² = 5000 N.
