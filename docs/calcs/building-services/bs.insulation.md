# B9 — Insulation thickness, ISO 12241 formula route (`bs.insulation@1.0.0`)

**Scope.** The thickness of insulation on a pipe (cylinder) or a flat surface that meets, separately and together, a heat-flow limit, a limit on the surface temperature of a hot surface (personnel protection) and an anti-condensation requirement for a cold one (surface above the dew point plus a margin); the governing thickness is the largest. For a chosen thickness it gives the heat flow, the surface temperature, the mean insulation temperature and PASS/FAIL per criterion. Steady state, one homogeneous layer. **No thickness table of any standard (not BS 5422) is used or shipped.**

**Method.** Pipe, per metre: `R = R_oth + ln(D_e/D_i)/(2πλ) + 1/(h_se·π·D_e)`, `D_e = D_i + 2t`; flat, per square metre: `R = R_oth + t/λ + 1/h_se`. `q = (θ_i − θ_a)/R` (positive from the process to the ambient; the sheet reports the magnitude and says "heat loss" or "heat gain"); `θ_se = θ_a + q/(h_se·π·D_e)` (pipe) or `θ_a + q/h_se` (flat). `R_oth` is the optional inner film `1/(h_i·π·D_p)` and pipe wall `ln(D_i/D_p)/(2πλ_w)` (flat: `1/h_i + t_w/λ_w`), both off unless entered; with neither, the pipe's outer wall is at the process temperature.

*Surface coefficient.* Either `h_se` as given (default mode; a user record `surface.coefficient`) or `h_se = h_c + h_r` solved together with `θ_se`, on which it depends (`q = (θ_i − θ_se)/R_tot = A·h_se(θ_se)·(θ_se − θ_a)`, one root between θ_a and θ_i, by bisection). Radiation `h_r = ε·σ·(T_s² + T_a²)(T_s + T_a)` to surroundings at the ambient air temperature (σ = 5.670374419×10⁻⁸). Free convection to air, Holman's simplified relations at atmospheric pressure, the larger of the laminar and turbulent forms: horizontal cylinder `1.32(ΔT/D_e)^0.25` and `1.24·ΔT^(1/3)`; vertical surface `1.42(ΔT/L)^0.25` and `1.31·ΔT^(1/3)`. With wind `v > 0`: forced convection by Churchill–Bernstein for a cylinder in cross-flow (Incropera 7th ed. §7.4) or the laminar/mixed flat-plate relations along the plate (§7.2), air `k = 0.0241(T/273.15)^0.81`, `ν = 1.33×10⁻⁵(T/273.15)^1.7` at the film temperature and Pr = 0.71 (derived fits, within about 3 % over 250–400 K), blended with free convection as `(h_nat³ + h_forced³)^(1/3)` (§9.6). The correlation is **indicative and is not the standard's own**; the standard's coefficients are not reproduced. Prefer a recorded `h_se` when the surface is known.

*Criteria and solution.* Heat-flow limit `|q| ≤ q_max` (per metre for a pipe, `q_max_m`; per square metre flat, `q_max_A`); hot-surface limit `θ_se ≤ θ_se,max` (hot only, must be above the ambient); anti-condensation `θ_se ≥ θ_dew + margin` (cold only), `θ_dew` from the Magnus relation over water (ice below 0 °C) at θ_a and RH — the **same closed form B7 (`GlaserModel.SaturationPressure` / `DewPoint`) uses**, so the two calculators agree on the dew point. Each criterion is solved on the thickness: the thickness is scanned from 0 to 1 m on a 400-point grid (finer near 0) for the last point where the criterion is broken, and the bracket after it is bisected (the project's `RootFinding.Bisect`; MathNet is not referenced by the assembly). The result is the least thickness beyond which the criterion holds for **every thicker layer**, which is what matters when thin insulation on a small pipe raises the heat flow; it is 0 when the bare surface complies everywhere. Governing `t = max` over the selected criteria. A criterion that no thickness up to 1 m meets blocks (`unattainable`).

*Critical diameter.* `ln(D_e/D_i)/(2πλ) + 1/(h_se·π·D_e)` is least at `D_e = 2λ/h_se` (`D_crit`, reported). A pipe smaller than `D_crit`, or a chosen thickness whose heat flow exceeds the bare pipe's, warns (`critical-radius`) with the numbers.

*Conductivity.* λ is a scalar input mapped to the W3 material property `lambda` (record kind `material.properties`); the sheet reports the mean insulation temperature `(θ_i + θ_se)/2` to take λ at, and an optional `theta_lambda` (the temperature λ applies at) warns (`lambda-temperature`) when it is more than 20 K from that mean.

**Inputs.** `geometry` (pipe / flat); `D_i` 5–3000 mm (pipe; soft 10–1000); `theta_i` −200…650 °C, `theta_a` −60…70 °C (differ by at least 0.01 K); `lambda` 0.005–2 W/(m·K) (soft 0.015–0.2; W3 `lambda`); `theta_lambda` (optional); `h_mode` (given / correlation); `h_se` 0.5–200 W/(m²K) (record `surface.coefficient`); `eps` 0.01–1 (record `surface.emissivity`), `v_wind` 0–60 m/s, `L_surf` 0.01–100 m (correlation; flat height or flow length); `h_i`, `t_w`, `lambda_w` (optional; wall needs both `t_w` and `lambda_w`, thinner than the pipe radius); the criteria, each applying only when entered (record `criteria.insulation`): `q_max_m`, `q_max_A`, `theta_se_max`, `dew_margin` (with `RH_a` 1–100 %); `t_chosen` 0–1000 mm (optional: blank reports at the required thickness and makes no checks).

**Outputs.** `theta_dew`; per selected criterion `t_q`, `t_se`, `t_dew` (mm) and, with a chosen thickness, its utilisation `u_q`, `u_se`, `u_dew`; `thickness` (governing required, mm); `governing` (text); `q_per_m` (W/m, pipe) or `q_area` (W/m², flat) and `theta_se` at the evaluated thickness (chosen, else required); `theta_m`; `R`, `R_oth`; `D_crit` (pipe); `h_c`, `h_r`, `h_se` (correlation); series of heat flow and surface temperature against thickness. **Ports.** `thickness` (mm), `q_per_m` (W/m, pipe only), `theta_se` (°C). **Checks (with `t_chosen`).** one utilisation check per selected criterion. **Warnings.** `criterion-na` (a hot-surface limit on a cold pipe, or a dew margin on a hot one, is ignored), `critical-radius`, `lambda-temperature`. **Blocks.** `temperatures`, `criteria` (none applies), `unattainable`, `surface-limit`, `dew` (dew point + margin not below ambient), `wall`.

**Limitations.** Steady state, one layer of constant λ (no temperature or moisture dependence), no supports, joints or thermal bridges, no air gaps, no sheet-metal jacket resistance, no heat gain by radiation from sun or neighbours, no wind-dependent λ. Condensation is judged on the outer surface only (no vapour-barrier design: use B7 for the wall). Flat surfaces are treated as vertical. The correlation is indicative (Holman's air relations and Churchill–Bernstein with fitted air properties); the ISO 12241 surface coefficients are not implemented. ISO 12241 clause numbers are cited by title only and are to be checked against the owner's copy.
