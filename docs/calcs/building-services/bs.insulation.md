# B9 — Insulation thickness, ISO 12241 formula route (`bs.insulation@1.1.0`)

**Scope.** The thickness of insulation on a pipe (cylinder) or a flat surface that meets, separately and together, a heat-flow limit, a limit on the surface temperature of a hot surface (personnel protection) and an anti-condensation requirement for a cold one (surface above the dew point plus a margin); the governing thickness is the largest. For a chosen thickness it gives the heat flow, the surface temperature, the mean insulation temperature and PASS/FAIL per criterion. Steady state, one homogeneous layer. **No thickness table of any standard (not BS 5422) is used or shipped.**

**Method.** Pipe, per metre: `R = R_oth + ln(D_e/D_i)/(2πλ) + 1/(h_se·π·D_e)`, `D_e = D_i + 2t`; flat, per square metre: `R = R_oth + t/λ + 1/h_se`. `q = (θ_i − θ_a)/R` (positive from the process to the ambient; the sheet reports the magnitude and says "heat loss" or "heat gain"); `θ_se = θ_a + q/(h_se·π·D_e)` (pipe) or `θ_a + q/h_se` (flat). `R_oth` is the optional inner film `1/(h_i·π·D_p)` and pipe wall `ln(D_i/D_p)/(2πλ_w)` (flat: `1/h_i + t_w/λ_w`), both off unless entered; with neither, the pipe's outer wall is at the process temperature.

*Surface coefficient.* Either `h_se` as given (default mode; a user record `surface.coefficient`) or `h_se = h_c + h_r` solved together with `θ_se`, on which it depends (`q = (θ_i − θ_se)/R_tot = A·h_se(θ_se)·(θ_se − θ_a)`, one root between θ_a and θ_i, by bisection). Radiation `h_r = ε·σ·(T_s² + T_a²)(T_s + T_a)` to surroundings at the ambient air temperature (σ = 5.670374419×10⁻⁸). Free convection to air, Holman's simplified relations at atmospheric pressure, the larger of the laminar and turbulent forms: horizontal cylinder `1.32(ΔT/D_e)^0.25` and `1.24·ΔT^(1/3)`; vertical surface `1.42(ΔT/L)^0.25` and `1.31·ΔT^(1/3)`. With wind `v > 0`: forced convection by Churchill–Bernstein for a cylinder in cross-flow (Incropera 7th ed. §7.4) or the laminar/mixed flat-plate relations along the plate (§7.2), air `k = 0.0241(T/273.15)^0.81`, `ν = 1.33×10⁻⁵(T/273.15)^1.7` at the film temperature and Pr = 0.71 (derived fits, conductivity within about 3 % and viscosity within about 4 % over 250–400 K), blended with free convection as `(h_nat³ + h_forced³)^(1/3)` (mixed convection, Incropera §9.9; §9.6 is free convection; section numbers to be checked against the owner's copy; the free-convection constants are Holman's). The correlation is **indicative and is not the standard's own**; the standard's coefficients are not reproduced. Prefer a recorded `h_se` when the surface is known.

*Criteria and solution.* Heat-flow limit `|q| ≤ q_max` (per metre for a pipe, `q_max_m`; per square metre flat, `q_max_A`); hot-surface limit `θ_se ≤ θ_se,max` (hot only, must be above the ambient); anti-condensation `θ_se ≥ θ_dew + margin` (cold only), `θ_dew` from the Magnus relation over water (ice below 0 °C) at θ_a and RH — the **same closed form B7 (`GlaserModel.SaturationPressure` / `DewPoint`) uses**, so the two calculators agree on the dew point. An optional `theta_dew` input (for example linked from B2) replaces the Magnus value, and then `RH_a` is not used. Below 0 °C the Magnus form is over ice, so RH is then relative to ice (meteorological RH is usually relative to water): enter the dew point for sub-zero air. A cold sheet with no `dew_margin` warns (`no-condensation-check`) that condensation is not checked; 0 K is a valid margin. Each criterion is solved on the thickness: the thickness is scanned from 0 to 1 m on a 400-point grid (finer near 0) for the last point where the criterion is broken, and the bracket after it is bisected (the project's `RootFinding.Bisect`; MathNet is not referenced by the assembly). The result is the least thickness beyond which the criterion holds for **every thicker layer**, which is what matters when thin insulation on a small pipe raises the heat flow; it is 0 when the bare surface complies everywhere. Governing `t = max` over the selected criteria. A criterion that no thickness up to 1 m meets shows its required thickness as "> 1000 mm" with a warning (`unattainable`) when a thickness is chosen, and the chosen thickness is checked against it (FAIL); with no thickness chosen there is nothing to evaluate and the sheet blocks. When the bare surface meets a heat-flow limit but a window of thin layers breaks it (a pipe below the critical diameter), the sheet says so (`bare-complies`) with the window's start and end. The heat-flow criterion is called a heat-loss limit on a hot pipe and a heat-gain limit on a cold one.

*Critical diameter.* `ln(D_e/D_i)/(2πλ) + 1/(h_se·π·D_e)` is least at `D_e = 2λ/h_se` (`D_crit`, reported). `D_crit` uses h_se at the bare surface (in correlation mode the h at an insulated surface is lower, so `D_crit` is slightly understated). A pipe smaller than `D_crit`, or a thickness whose heat flow exceeds the bare pipe's, warns (`critical-radius`) with the numbers.

*Conductivity.* λ is a scalar input mapped to the W3 material property `lambda` (record kind `material.properties`); the sheet reports the mean insulation temperature to take λ at, `(θ_face + θ_se)/2` with `θ_face = θ_i − q·R_oth` the insulation's inner face (just `θ_i` with no film or wall), and an optional `theta_lambda` (the temperature λ applies at) warns (`lambda-temperature`) when it is more than 20 K from that mean.

**Inputs.** `geometry` (pipe / flat); `D_i` 5–3000 mm (pipe; soft 10–1000); `theta_i` −200…650 °C, `theta_a` −60…70 °C (differ by at least 0.01 K); `lambda` 0.005–2 W/(m·K) (soft 0.015–0.2; W3 `lambda`); `theta_lambda` (optional); `h_mode` (given / correlation); `h_se` 0.5–200 W/(m²K) (record `surface.coefficient`; the default of 10 is a generic value, not a standard's, which the user replaces; against condensation a high h_se is the unfavourable case, so on a cold surface with a dew-point margin the sheet solves the correlation in still air at the emissivity on the sheet (`eps`, 0.9 by default, read for this judgement only while h_se is given) and warns `h-se-condensation` when it asks for more than the fixed value does, naming the thickness at bright cladding (ε = 0.1) as a note); `eps` 0.01–1 (record `surface.emissivity`), `v_wind` 0–60 m/s, `L_surf` 0.01–100 m (correlation; flat height or flow length); `h_i`, `t_w`, `lambda_w` (optional; wall needs both `t_w` and `lambda_w`, thinner than the pipe radius); the criteria, each applying only when entered (record `criteria.insulation`): `q_max_m` (opens on a generic 25 W/m, which the user replaces with the project limit), `q_max_A`, `theta_se_max`, `dew_margin` (with `RH_a` 1–100 %, or `theta_dew` −100…70 °C entered); `t_chosen` 0–1000 mm (optional: blank reports at the required thickness and makes no checks; entered, nothing blocks). No criterion has a default except the pipe heat-flow limit `q_max_m` (25 W/m on a new sheet).

**Outputs.** `theta_dew`; per selected criterion `t_q`, `t_se`, `t_dew` (mm) and, with a chosen thickness, its utilisation `u_q`, `u_se`, `u_dew`; `thickness` (governing required, mm; the text "> 1000 mm" when a criterion is unattainable, which breaks a downstream link to `thickness` (a link needs a number: choose a thickness only after the criteria are attainable); absent when no criterion applies); `governing` (text); `q_per_m` (W/m, pipe) or `q_area` (W/m², flat) and `theta_se` at the evaluated thickness (chosen, else required); `theta_m`; `R`, `R_oth`; `D_crit` (pipe); `h_c`, `h_r`, `h_se` (correlation); series of heat flow and surface temperature against thickness. **Ports.** `thickness` (mm), `q_per_m` (W/m, pipe only), `q_area` (W/m², flat only), `theta_se` (°C). **Checks (with `t_chosen`).** one utilisation check per selected criterion. **Warnings.** `criterion-na` (a hot-surface limit on a cold pipe, or a dew margin or an entered `theta_dew` on a hot one, is ignored), `no-condensation-check`, `no-criteria` (a thickness is chosen but no criterion applies: the heat flow and surface temperature are reported), `unattainable`, `bare-complies`, `critical-radius`, `lambda-temperature`. **Blocks.** `temperatures`, `criteria` (none applies and no thickness is chosen), `unattainable` (no thickness chosen), `surface-limit`, `dew` (dew point + margin not below ambient), `wall`.

**Limitations.** Steady state, one layer of constant λ (no temperature or moisture dependence), no supports, joints or thermal bridges, no air gaps, no sheet-metal jacket resistance, no heat gain by radiation from sun or neighbours, no wind-dependent λ. Condensation is judged on the outer surface only (no vapour-barrier design: use B7 for the wall). Pipes are treated as horizontal and flat surfaces as vertical for free convection. The correlation is indicative (Holman's air relations and Churchill–Bernstein with fitted air properties); the ISO 12241 surface coefficients are not implemented. ISO 12241 and ISO 13788 clause numbers are cited by title only and are to be checked against the owner's copy. The unused-mode inputs (`eps` in given-h mode, `q_max_m` on a flat sheet) still raise the generic "tabulated value not from a record" warning, as the Glaser sheet does.
