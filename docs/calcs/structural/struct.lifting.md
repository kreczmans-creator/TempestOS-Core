# S8 — Lifting lugs and spreader/lifting beams (`struct.lifting@1.1.0`) — GATED, OFF BY DEFAULT

**Status.** Behind the `lifting` feature flag (`ADR-0173`): composed into the build but withheld from the catalogue, the Worksheets tree, the Ctrl+K entries, every service path and every pack until the installation's `appsettings.json`, in the folder beside the executable, says `{ "Calc": { "Features": { "Lifting": true } } }` (no other source is read). The older lifting-lug and pin-joint module (`calc.lifting-lug-pin-joint`, Engineering Calculators) is withheld by the same flag. Issue needs a competent-person attestation (`AttestAsync`, by a signed-in person other than the authors) and a second, independent check. **The flag stays off until the owner has compared the equations below with BTH-1 and EN 13155+A1 (owner test pack, S8 review checklist, §B10 item 6).**

**This sheet is a design calculation, not a thorough examination.** It does not replace the thorough examination and inspection of lifting equipment by a competent person (LOLER reg 9), the marking, test and certification the regulations and the standards require (LOLER reg 7), the planning of the lifting operation, the weld, bolt and beam checks on the linked sheets, or the rating of the pin, shackle and sling (PUWER reg 4, reg 5). The sheet prints this statement in its scope, as the output `sheet_notice`, and in the marking and certification checklist (`checklist`).

**Signatures are not authenticated by TempestOS.** The names of the author, the checkers and the competent person on the sheet are the sign-in names of this installation (recorded beside the typed name and statement); TempestOS does not verify that anyone is who they say, that the attester is competent, or that the second checker is independent in fact. That is the owner's and the company's to establish. The sheet prints the notice in its scope, in an assumption and as the output `sheet_notice`.

**Scope.** A pin-loaded lifting lug (a plate with a bored hole, loaded by a pin) and the lug loads of a spreader or lifting beam: the sling force on each lug from the rated load, the sling angle and the out-of-plane angle; tension beyond the hole, single-plane fracture beyond the hole, shear-out on two planes, pin bearing and the dishing ratio; capacity and utilisation by the BTH-1 allowable-stress approach (clause 3-3.3, cited by number only), by the EN 13155 proof route (2× the rated load without permanent set, 3× without release, at most 16,000 cycles) or by both; the governing mode; a proof-load value; and a marking and certification checklist as text.

**Method.** Formulas in the author's notation (the template method text carries them). Angles in degrees at the interface; N, mm, MPa inside. The geometry and capacity relations follow the failure modes of the published course example the sheet is checked against (below); only their clause numbers are cited, never their text.

- *Loads.* `n_eff = min(n_lugs, 3)`; `F_v = WLL/n_eff` (vertical load on each lug); the sling leaves the lug at `θ` in the lug plane and `β` out of it, so the sling force is `F_s = F_v/(cos θ · cos β)`, `F_ip = F_s cos β` (= `F_v/cos θ`), `F_op = F_s sin β`, and the horizontal component (the axial force in a spreader beam, for S7) is `N_beam = F_v tan θ`; out-of-plane moment `M_op = F_op·h` when the lever arm `h` is given.
- *Tilt of a horizontal beam (EN 13155).* With the `beam` switch the automatic 6° tilt is applied and the larger sling force governs:
  - *Pitch* (about a horizontal axis across the beam): the slings leave at `θ + 6°` and `θ − 6°`. The slings meet at the hook, so their horizontal components are equal and opposite and the vertical components carry the load; the sling at `θ − 6°` carries `F_s,pitch = 2 F_v sin(θ + 6°)/sin 2θ` (in the lug plane; divided by `cos β` for the resultant). If `θ ≤ 6°` the tilted beam can put the whole load on one vertical sling and `F_s,pitch = WLL/cos β`. A single lug has no other lug to take load off it, so the pitch case is the nominal one.
  - *Roll* (about the beam's own axis): the out-of-plane angle grows by 6°: `F_s,roll = F_v/(cos θ · cos(β + 6°))`.
  - `F_s` is the maximum of the nominal, pitch and roll cases; `F_ip`, `F_op` and `N_beam` are the envelopes over the cases.
- *Geometry.* In the sheet's names `b_e` is the distance from the hole edge to the plate edge on each side across the load line and `a` the end distance along it (default `R − D_h/2`). `side = min(b_e, R − D_h/2)`; effective width `b_e,eff = min(side, 4t, 0.6·(f_u/f_y)·√(D_h/side)·side)`; `A_t = 2·t·b_e,eff` (tension beyond the hole); `A_fr = [1.13·a + 0.92·side/(1 + side/D_h)]·t` (single-plane fracture beyond the hole); `φ = 55·D_p/D_h` (degrees); `A_v = 2t[a + (D_p/2)(1 − cos φ)]` (two shear planes); `A_b = D_p·t` (bearing).
- *BTH-1 mode (3-3.3).* One allowable stress `F_a = min(f_y/n_y, f_u/n_u)` for every section; pin-clearance factor `C_r = 1 − 0.275√(1 − D_p²/D_h²)`, applied as stated for `D_p/D_h < 1` with no cut-off (conservative; see the owner questions); design load `D = N_d·k_svc·F_s`; `P_t = C_r F_a A_t`, `P_sp = C_r F_a A_fr`, `P_v = F_a A_v/√3` (√3 is the distortion-energy shear ratio, derived), `P_b = k_b F_a A_b`; `u = D/P` for each; dishing `D_h/t ≤ dish_max`. Capacity `cap_bth = WLL/max(u)`.
- *EN 13155 mode.* The four sections with `Y = f_y·A` (shear `f_y/√3·A_v`) and `U = f_u·A` (shear `f_u/√3·A_v`), the fracture area taken without `C_r`: `P_EN = min(Y/2, U/3)` (no permanent set at 2× the rated load, no release at 3×); `u = F_s/P_EN`; the governing route is named ("2×WLL elastic" or "3×WLL no release"). Capacity `cap_en = WLL/max(u)`. The notch-stress route of EN 13001-3-1 (cited) is not implemented; this is a nominal-section proof.
- *Both.* Every check of both modes is listed; the capacity is `WLL/lug_util` with `lug_util` the larger utilisation, i.e. the smaller of the two capacities.
- *Spreader beam.* With the `beam` switch and `A_beam`: axial utilisation `N_d k_svc N_beam/(A F_a)` (BTH-1) and/or `max(2N_beam/(A f_y), 3N_beam/(A f_u))` (EN 13155), larger of the two for both modes; beam buckling is S7's (link `N_beam`).
- *Governing.* `util = max(lug_util, u_beam, u_dish)` (the port `spreader.util`); `capacity = WLL/lug_util` in kN (`lug.capacity`); `governing_mode` text, e.g. "BTH-1: bearing" or "EN 13155 (2×WLL elastic): double-plane shear"; a failed dishing limit is named in it. The capacity does not depend on `WLL` (it is the rated load the lug would carry); utilisation is proportional to it.
- *Proof load.* `proof_load = k_proof·WLL`.

**Inputs.** Mode (`mode`: BTH-1 / EN 13155 / both); `WLL` 0.5–10,000 kN; `n_lugs` 1–4; `theta` 0–60°; `beta` 0–30°; `beam`; `n_cycles` 1–**16,000**; `t` 5–150, `Dh` 10–500, `Dp` 8–500, `R` 15–600, `be` 5–400, optional `a` 5–600 and `h` 1–3000 mm; `f_y` 100–1100, `f_u` 150–1400 MPa (W3 material properties, record kind `material.properties`); the **user-record factors with no default**: `design_category`, `service_class` (labels), `Nd` 1–5, `k_svc` 1–3 (blank: 1.0), `n_y` 1–**4**, `n_u` 1–**6**, `k_b` 0.5–3, `dish_max` 1–100 (blank: no dishing check), `k_proof` 1–3 (blank: no proof load); optional `A_beam` 1–50,000 cm²; the attestation inputs `attest_name`, `attest_qualification`, `attest_statement`. **Every numeric input has a hard limit equal to its stated range: outside it the sheet does not run.** Blocks: pin larger than hole, `R` not above the hole radius, `f_u < f_y`, a missing BTH-1 factor (`Nd`, `n_y`, `n_u`, `k_b`) in BTH-1 or both mode.

**Outputs.** `capacity`, `governing_mode`, `util`, `lug_util`, `cap_bth`, `u_bth`, `cap_en`, `u_en`, `u_beam`; `F_s`, `F_s_pitch`, `F_s_roll` (with the beam switch), `F_ip`, `F_op`, `N_beam`, `M_op`; `Cr`, `phi`, `side`, `be_eff`, `a_eff`, `A_t`, `A_fr`, `A_v`, `A_b`; BTH-1 `Fa`, `D_bth`, `P_t`, `P_sp`, `P_v`, `P_b`, `u_t`, `u_sp`, `u_v`, `u_b`, `dish`, `u_dish`; EN 13155 `P_en_t|sp|v|b`, `u_en_t|sp|v|b`; `proof_load`; `sheet_notice`; `checklist`. (1.1.0 replaced `A_net`, `Fy_a`, `Fu_a`, `theta_d` and `beta_d`.) **Ports.** `capacity` (`lug.capacity`), `governing_mode` (`lug.governing_mode`), `util` (`spreader.util`), and for the linked checks `F_s`, `F_ip`, `F_op` (weld group M6, S4, bolt group M3) and `N_beam` (member check S7, section S1). The links are declared and documented; **no pack wires them**: chain 6 (lifting beam, S8 → S7 → S4/M6 → S1) is the integrator's, run with the flag on in the test host only.

**Governance.**

- *Attestation.* A template that requires one is attested with `AttestAsync` (the Desktop form's **Attest** button), which records the competent person under the **signed-in principal**, not under typed text: the attester must be a known signed-in person who is none of the calculation's authors (the creator, anyone who set or changed an input, or any member of its pack), compared by sign-in id and by the display name the principal directory gives it (on Windows the sign-in id is a SID, which no typed name resembles). The name, qualification and statement typed (statement at least 20 characters) are display text: they are written into the three attestation inputs, with the attester as their source, and stored with the principal and the time. A later change to any input voids the attestation. A first checker must be someone other than the attester, and an attestation is not required to *reject* a calculation.
- *Second check.* Issue demands a **second Checked signature** (`CountersignAsync` / `CountersignPackAsync`, the form's **Countersign** button) by a signed-in person who is none of the authors, not the attester and not the first checker (by sign-in or typed name); the statement cannot be empty (at least 20 characters). A rejection is recorded, refuses Issue, and lets the checked calculation be revised. The issued sheet header reads "checker; second check: name"; the attestation prints in the inputs table; the issued snapshot records that the lifting feature was enabled and where.
- *Evidence.* The calculation's Evidence record is managed by its worksheet (`ADR-0173`): the generic Evidence Check, Issue and Revise acts refuse it, and its editor offers "Open worksheet" instead.

**Limitations.** Nominal-stress screening of four sections on a plate loaded through a pin; no stress concentration, no fatigue (cycles limited to 16,000 by a hard limit), no buckling of the lug, no weld, bolt or beam check (linked sheets), no pin, shackle or sling rating; equal sharing between the lugs with a fourth not relied on; one pin diameter and a round-ended or straight-ended lug (through `a`); the horizontal-beam tilt is the 6° pitch and roll of EN 13155 applied one at a time, not both together. The design factor, service-class factor, allowable-stress divisors, bearing multiple, dishing limit and proof-load factor are the user's records; none is shipped, none has a default (ASME BTH-1 and EN 13155 text and tables are never shipped). **Signatures are not authenticated by TempestOS** (above).

**Owner questions (§B10 item 6, to be answered before the flag is enabled).** These are choices the sheet makes that the standards, not the author, must settle:

1. *Signatures.* TempestOS does not authenticate signatures or competence (above). Is the company's own procedure for who may attest, check and countersign, and for how that is evidenced, adequate for the sheet to be issued from?
2. *Four lugs.* The sheet takes `n_eff = min(n_lugs, 3)`: with four lugs three are assumed to share the load, a fourth is not relied on. Is that the rule BTH-1 and the company's lifting procedure intend for a statically indeterminate four-lug set, or is a different share required?
3. *Dishing.* The dishing ratio is taken as `D_h/t` against a user-record limit `dish_max`. Is that the definition the code uses (the code may define it on the pin or hole and the plate thickness differently)?
4. *`C_r` cut-off.* `C_r` is applied as `1 − 0.275√(1 − (D_p/D_h)²)` for every `D_p/D_h < 1`, with no cut-off. The course example and the code may take `C_r = 1` for a close-fitting pin (a ratio at or above a stated limit); omitting the cut-off is conservative. Is the cut-off to be added, and at what ratio?
5. *Tilt.* The 6° tilt is applied as a pitch case and a roll case taken one at a time, and the larger sling force governs. Does EN 13155 require both at once, or a different angle?
6. *Allowable stress.* One value `min(f_y/n_y, f_u/n_u)` is used for every BTH-1 section, as in the course example; does BTH-1 take separate allowables for yield-governed and fracture-governed sections?

**Not pinned (owner check).** The BTH-1 clause numbers other than 3-3.3 and the EN 13155 clause numbers (the sheet says "clause to be confirmed"); the LOLER and PUWER regulation numbers in the checklist (reg 7, 9; reg 4, 5) are from memory of the Regulations and to be confirmed.
