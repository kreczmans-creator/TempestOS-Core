# S8 — Lifting lugs and spreader/lifting beams (`struct.lifting@1.0.0`) — GATED, OFF BY DEFAULT

**Status.** Behind the `lifting` feature flag (`ADR-0173`): composed into the build but withheld from the catalogue, the Worksheets tree, the Ctrl+K entries, every service path and every pack until the installation's configuration says `Calc:Features:Lifting = true`. Issue needs a competent-person attestation and a second, independent check. **The flag stays off until the owner has compared the equations below with BTH-1 and EN 13155+A1 (owner test pack, S8 review checklist, §B10 item 6).**

**This sheet is a design calculation, not a thorough examination.** It does not replace the thorough examination and inspection of lifting equipment by a competent person (LOLER reg 9), the marking, test and certification the regulations and the standards require (LOLER reg 7), the planning of the lifting operation, the weld, bolt and beam checks on the linked sheets, or the rating of the pin, shackle and sling (PUWER reg 4, reg 5). The sheet prints this statement in its scope, as the output `sheet_notice`, and in the marking and certification checklist (`checklist`).

**Scope.** A pin-loaded lifting lug (a plate with a bored hole, loaded by a pin) and the lug loads of a spreader or lifting beam: the sling force on each lug from the rated load, the sling angle and the out-of-plane angle; tension beyond the hole, fracture of the net section across the hole (single plane), shear-out on two planes, pin bearing and the dishing ratio; capacity and utilisation by the BTH-1 allowable-stress approach (clause 3-3.3, cited by number only), by the EN 13155 proof route (2× the rated load without permanent set, 3× without release, at most 16,000 cycles) or by both; the governing mode; a proof-load value; and a marking and certification checklist as text.

**Method.** Formulas in own notation (the template method text carries them). Angles in degrees at the interface; N, mm, MPa inside.

- *Loads.* `n_eff = min(n_lugs, 3)` (a fourth lug is not relied on; equal sharing); `θ_d = θ`, `β_d = β`, each **+ 6° for a horizontal beam** (the automatic tilt case, EN 13155; the tilted case dominates the untilted one, so the sheet reports only the tilted one); `F_v = WLL/n_eff`; sling force `F_s = F_v/cos θ_d` (the whole resultant is used on the hole, whatever `β_d`: conservative); `F_ip = F_s cos β_d`; `F_op = F_s sin β_d`; horizontal component `N_beam = F_v tan θ_d` (the axial force in a spreader beam, for S7); out-of-plane moment `M_op = F_op·h` when the lever arm `h` is given.
- *Geometry.* `b_e,eff = min(b_e, R − D_h/2)`; `a_eff = a`, default `R − D_h/2`; `A_t = 2 t b_e,eff`; `A_net = t(2R − D_h)`; `φ = 55 D_p/D_h` (degrees); `A_v = 2t[a_eff + (D_h/2)(1 − cos φ)]`; `A_b = D_p t`.
- *BTH-1 mode (3-3.3).* Pin-clearance factor `C_r = 1 − 0.275√(1 − D_p²/D_h²)` (applied to the two tension modes); allowable stresses `F_y,a = f_y/n_y`, `F_u,a = f_u/n_u`; design load `D = N_d·k_svc·F_s`; `P_t = C_r F_y,a A_t` (tension beyond the hole), `P_sp = C_r F_u,a A_net` (single-plane fracture), `P_v = (F_y,a/√3) A_v` (double-plane shear-out; √3 is the distortion-energy shear ratio, derived), `P_b = k_b F_y,a A_b` (pin bearing); `u = D/P` for each; dishing `D_h/t ≤ dish_max`. Capacity `cap_bth = WLL/max(u)`.
- *EN 13155 mode.* The four sections with `Y = f_y·A` (shear `f_y/√3·A_v`) and `U = f_u·A` (shear `f_u/√3·A_v`): `P_EN = min(Y/2, U/3)` (no permanent set at 2× the rated load, no release at 3×); `u = F_s/P_EN`; the governing route is named ("2×WLL elastic" or "3×WLL no release"). Capacity `cap_en = WLL/max(u)`. The notch-stress route of EN 13001-3-1 (cited) is not implemented; this is a nominal-section proof.
- *Both.* Every check of both modes is listed; the capacity is `WLL/lug_util` with `lug_util` the larger utilisation, i.e. the smaller of the two capacities.
- *Spreader beam.* With the `beam` switch and `A_beam`: axial utilisation `N_d k_svc N_beam/(A F_y,a)` (BTH-1) and/or `max(2N_beam/(A f_y), 3N_beam/(A f_u))` (EN 13155), larger of the two for both modes; beam buckling is S7's (link `N_beam`).
- *Governing.* `util = max(lug_util, u_beam, u_dish)` (the port `spreader.util`); `capacity = WLL/lug_util` in kN (`lug.capacity`); `governing_mode` text, e.g. "BTH-1: bearing" or "EN 13155 (2×WLL elastic): double-plane shear"; a failed dishing limit is named in it. The capacity does not depend on `WLL` (it is the rated load the lug would carry); utilisation is proportional to it.
- *Proof load.* `proof_load = k_proof·WLL`.

**Inputs.** Mode (`mode`: BTH-1 / EN 13155 / both); `WLL` 0.5–10,000 kN; `n_lugs` 1–4; `theta` 0–60°; `beta` 0–30°; `beam`; `n_cycles` 1–**16,000**; `t` 5–150, `Dh` 10–500, `Dp` 8–500, `R` 15–600, `be` 5–400, optional `a` 5–600 and `h` 1–3000 mm; `f_y` 100–1100, `f_u` 150–1400 MPa (W3 material properties, record kind `material.properties`); the **user-record factors with no default**: `design_category`, `service_class` (labels), `Nd` 1–5, `k_svc` 1–3 (blank: 1.0), `n_y` 1–3, `n_u` 1–4, `k_b` 0.5–3, `dish_max` 1–100 (blank: no dishing check), `k_proof` 1–3 (blank: no proof load); optional `A_beam` 1–50,000 cm²; the attestation inputs `attest_name`, `attest_qualification`, `attest_statement`. **Every numeric input has a hard limit equal to its stated range: outside it the sheet does not run.** Blocks: pin larger than hole, `R` not above the hole radius, `f_u < f_y`, a missing BTH-1 factor (`Nd`, `n_y`, `n_u`, `k_b`) in BTH-1 or both mode.

**Outputs.** `capacity`, `governing_mode`, `util`, `lug_util`, `cap_bth`, `u_bth`, `cap_en`, `u_en`, `u_beam`; `F_s`, `F_ip`, `F_op`, `N_beam`, `M_op`; `Cr`, `phi`, `A_t`, `A_net`, `A_v`, `A_b`, `be_eff`, `a_eff`; BTH-1 `Fy_a`, `Fu_a`, `D_bth`, `P_t`, `P_sp`, `P_v`, `P_b`, `u_t`, `u_sp`, `u_v`, `u_b`, `dish`, `u_dish`; EN 13155 `P_en_t|sp|v|b`, `u_en_t|sp|v|b`; `proof_load`; `sheet_notice`; `checklist`. **Ports.** `capacity` (`lug.capacity`), `governing_mode` (`lug.governing_mode`), `util` (`spreader.util`), and for the linked checks `F_s`, `F_ip`, `F_op` (weld group M6, S4, bolt group M3) and `N_beam` (member check S7, section S1). The links are declared and documented; **no pack wires them**: chain 6 (lifting beam, S8 → S7 → S4/M6 → S1) is the integrator's, run with the flag on in the test host only.

**Governance.** Check demands the attestation (a competent person, qualification and a statement of at least 20 characters, the person not being the author or an editor of the inputs). Issue demands it again and a **second Checked signature**: `CountersignAsync` / `CountersignPackAsync`, by someone who is signed in, is not the author or an editor and is not the first checker (by sign-in or typed name). Both checkers must be independent of the author (`IndependentCheck`). The issued sheet header reads "checker; second check: name"; the attestation prints in the inputs table.

**Limitations.** Nominal-stress screening of four sections on a plate loaded through a pin; no stress concentration, no fatigue (cycles limited to 16,000 by a hard limit), no buckling of the lug, no weld, bolt or beam check (linked sheets), no pin, shackle or sling rating; equal sharing between the lugs with a fourth not relied on; one pin diameter and a round-ended or straight-ended lug (through `a`); the horizontal-beam tilt case is a single combined envelope. The design factor, service-class factor, allowable-stress divisors, bearing multiple, dishing limit and proof-load factor are the user's records; none is shipped, none has a default (ASME BTH-1 and EN 13155 text and tables are never shipped). The equations above are the author's notation for the checks the brief names; **the choice to apply `C_r` to the tension modes, to take the shear-out allowable as `F_y,a/√3`, and the definition of the dishing ratio as `D_h/t` are modelling choices to be compared with the standards before the flag is enabled.**

**Test cases.** Analytic, **Confirmed** (recomputed in python, `s8_ref`, from the equations above; illustrative user-record factors `N_d` 1.8, `n_y` 1.6, `n_u` 2.2, `k_b` 1.2, `dish_max` 40, `k_proof` 1.5, not values from any standard): `ka-bth1-hand` (two lugs, 150 kN, 30°/5°: every section, bearing governs), `ka-en13155-hand` (EN 13155 mode, f_y 235 / f_u 360: shear-out governs through the 2× elastic route), `ka-both-beam-tilt` (four lugs sharing as three, 400 kN, with the 6° tilt and a 120 cm² beam), `ka-both-shear-governs` (both modes, BTH-1 shear-out governs). **`ka-pdhonline-s106-ratio` is Unconfirmed and Secondary** (course notes): the quoted `C_r = 0.78` and `φ = 33°`, which follow exactly from `D_p/D_h = 0.6` (reproduced here by the stated equations; the ratio is inferred, not quoted).

**Not pinned (owner check).** The remaining PDHonline S106 values: `A_v = 2.964 in²`, double-plane shear 19.849 k, single-plane 20.539 k, bearing 10.875 k, and `C_r`/`φ` as inputs of that example. Its plate, hole, edge distance, material and allowable-stress basis were not available, so the "harness factor to match S106's allowable-stress basis" could not be set and no case reproduces those four numbers; they are not claimed. Also not pinned: the BTH-1 clause numbers other than 3-3.3 and the EN 13155 clause numbers (the sheet says "clause to be confirmed"); the LOLER and PUWER regulation numbers in the checklist (reg 7, 9; reg 4, 5) are from memory of the Regulations and to be confirmed.

The attestation inputs are left blank (an example is not an issued sheet).
