# S9 — Plate bending (`struct.plate-bending@1.2.2`)

**In plain terms.** A thin flat plate bends like a slab when a pressure or load pushes on its face, and the thinner it is, the faster the bending stress and the sag grow. The worksheet takes the plate's size, thickness, material, edge support and load, works out how far the middle sags and how hard the most stressed point is working, and compares that stress with the steel's yield and the sag with a limit you choose. It is a screening tool for lids, covers and panels, not a finite-element analysis.

**Scope.** A thin, flat, isotropic plate of constant thickness under a lateral load, by Kirchhoff small-deflection theory, with the same edge support all round: **circular** (clamped or simply supported; uniform pressure, central patch or ring load) in closed form; **rectangular simply supported** (uniform pressure or centred rectangular patch) by the Navier double series; **rectangular clamped** (uniform or centred patch) by a Ritz solution, **labelled approximate**. Outputs the maximum deflection, the greatest von Mises surface stress, the stress and deflection utilisations and the fundamental frequency. Every numeric input has a hard range; outside it the sheet does not run.

**Method.** Formulas in own notation (see the template method text). `D = E t³/(12(1 − ν²))`; surface stress `σ = 6M/t²` from the bending and twisting moments per unit width; von Mises `√(σx² − σxσy + σy² + 3τxy²)`.

- *Circular, uniform.* Clamped `w = q a⁴/(64D)`, `σ_edge = 3q a²/(4t²)`; simply supported `w = (5 + ν) q a⁴/(64(1 + ν)D)`, `σ_centre = 3(3 + ν) q a²/(8t²)`. The von Mises stress is found from the general solution (below): at the clamped edge `σ_r√(1 − ν + ν²)` (since `σ_θ = νσ_r`), at the simply supported centre `σ_r = σ_θ`.
- *Circular, patch (radius c, pressure q) and ring (radius b, total force F).* Derived here from `D∇⁴w = q`: `w = A + B r² (+ q r⁴/64D)` inside the loaded radius and `A′ + B′ r² + C′ ln(r/a) + k r² ln(r/a)` outside, `k = q c²/8D` (patch) or `F/8πD` (ring); constants from continuity of `w`, `w′`, `M_r` at the load radius (`C′ = k c²/2` for the patch, `k b²` for the ring) and the edge conditions (`w = 0` with `w′ = 0` clamped or `M_r = 0` simply supported). Centre deflections, with `β = c/a` or `b/a`: patch clamped `q a⁴/(64D)·(4β² − 3β⁴ + 4β⁴ ln β)`; patch simply supported `q a⁴/(8D)·[(3+ν)/(1+ν)·(β²/2 − β⁴/4) + β⁴/2 ln β − β⁴/8]`; ring clamped `F a²/(16πD)·(1 − β² + 2β² ln β)`; ring simply supported `F a²/(16πD)·[(3+ν)/(1+ν)(1 − β²) + 2β² ln β]`. `M_r = −D(w″ + νw′/r)`, `M_θ = −D(w′/r + νw″)`; the greatest von Mises and principal moments are searched over `0 ≤ r ≤ a` (2000-point grid, the load radius, then golden-section refinement).
- *Rectangular, simply supported.* `w = Σ W_mn sin(mπx/a) sin(nπy/b)` over **odd** `m, n`, `W_mn = q_mn/(π⁴D(m²/a² + n²/b²)²)`; `q_mn = 16q/(π² m n)` (uniform) or `16q/(π² m n)·sin(mπ/2) sin(nπ/2) sin(mπu/2a) sin(nπv/2b)` (patch `u × v`). `M_x`, `M_y`, `M_xy` by differentiating the series. Terms are added in shells while the number of odd terms per direction `N` doubles from 8 up to the cap **1024** (1 048 576 terms), stopping when `w`, the centre von Mises moment and the corner twisting moment each change by less than **10⁻⁶** over a doubling (the corner twisting moment of a uniform load converges like 1/N², so its sum is Richardson-extrapolated from the last two doublings, `s_N + (s_N − s_N/2)/3`, before it is compared and returned; a patch that covers the plate is the uniform load and takes the same path; for a smaller patch the extrapolated corner value is used only on stages where it changes less than the raw sum (the corner sum of a patch converges faster than 1/N² once N is well above plate/patch, so extrapolating an early stage can add error rather than remove it). The `M_xy_k` formula text and the `navier-converge` message say the corner moment is extrapolated. Without this a plate above about 2.2:1 would hit the 1024 cap with a corner change of 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻⁵ and warn although the governing centre value had converged). The sheet reports `N`, the terms and the last change; a warning (`navier-converge`) is raised if the cap is reached first. The stress candidates are the **centre** (`M_x`, `M_y`) and a **corner** (`M_xy`, pure twist: `√3|M_xy|`); the behaviour tests scan the whole field on a quarter-plate grid for uniform loads (three plates) and for four central-patch cases and find no larger von Mises moment (a coarse check, 2 × 10⁻³; a python scan on a finer grid over more aspect ratios and ν agreed). Which of the two governs flips with ν and the aspect ratio, so both are evaluated. The corner reaction `2 M_xy` is reported (corners held down).
- *Rectangular, clamped (approximate).* Ritz: `w = Σ c_ij X_i(x/a) Y_j(y/b)`, `X_k(ξ) = ξ²(1 − ξ)² P₂ₖ(2ξ − 1)` (Legendre polynomials times the clamped bubble; `w = w′ = 0` on all edges, symmetric about the centre; 64-point Gauss–Legendre quadrature, exact for every order up to K = 32). `c` minimises the total potential energy (Gauss–Legendre quadrature, Cholesky solve with diagonal scaling). The order `K` follows the sparse schedule **8, 12, 16, 20, 25, 30** until `w` and the stress change by less than **10⁻⁵** between consecutive orders, or until the change stops falling (a change above 0.7 times the previous one counts as stalled and ends the iteration with `ritz-converge`); the run checks its cancellation token between orders. The Cholesky factor uses SIMD dot products and a transposed copy for the back substitution. Measured on the build machine: 5000 × 500 clamped 74 ms (K = 16, stalled), 10000 × 1000 55 ms, 1000 × 1000 with a 250 × 250 patch 144 ms (K = 25), a 500 × 500 square 13 ms (K = 16); the worst case, a plate that runs to K = 30 without stalling, about 0.5 s. Compared with K = 30, the stalled 5000 × 500 stress (K = 16) is within 3 × 10⁻⁵, and the 25 % patch within about 10⁻³. Stress is the greatest von Mises value on a 21 × 21 grid over a quarter of the plate (edges and centre included), with its position. Deflection converges to about 10⁻⁹. The stress converges slowest for long plates and small patches, and the last change understates its error (a 1000 mm square with a 100 × 100 mm patch was still about 1 % high at K = 20, which is why the patch minimum is 25 %). The sheet warns `ritz-approx` always, and `ritz-converge` if the tolerance was not met, quoting the last change and about three times it as an estimate, not a bound. Not for patches below 25 % of a side.
- *Frequency (output Hz).* Simply supported rectangle `ω₁₁ = π²(1/a² + 1/b²)√(D/ρt)` (the §B6 formula is the angular frequency in rad/s; the sheet prints both and outputs `f = ω/2π` in Hz). Circular `ω = (λ²/a²)√(D/ρt)` with `λ²` from the characteristic equation solved at run time: clamped `J₀(λ)I₁(λ) + I₀(λ)J₁(λ) = 0` (`λ² = 10.2158`), simply supported `J₁/J₀ + I₁/I₀ = 2λ/(1 − ν)` (`λ² = 4.935` at ν = 0.3). Clamped rectangle: Rayleigh–Ritz lowest eigenvalue of `K c = ω² M c` with the same basis (square: `λ² = 35.985`).
- *Checks.* `u_stress = σ_vm/(f_y/γ_M0)` (BS EN 1993-1-1 6.2.1(5), the yield criterion; γ_M0 from 6.1(1)); `u_defl = w_max/(span/ratio)` with span the diameter (circular) or the shorter side. **WARN** `w/t > 0.5` (small-deflection theory invalid) and span/t < 10 (thick plate).

**Inputs.** `shape`, `support`, `a`, `b`, `t`; `load_type` (uniform / central patch / ring), `load_basis` (pressure `q` or total force `P`; it applies to uniform and patch loads, a ring always uses `P`), `q`, `P`, patch `r0` (circular) or `u`, `v` (rectangular), `r_ring`; `E`, `nu`, `rho`, `f_y` (each takes a W3 material through the record kind `material.properties` and stays editable); `gamma_M0` (record `ec3.national-annex`); `defl_ratio` (record `plate.deflection-limit`). Blocks: ring on a rectangle, aspect ratio above 10, patch larger than the plate, patch below 1 % (circular) / 2 % (SS rectangle) / 25 % (clamped rectangle) of the plate size, ring radius outside 1–99 % of the plate radius.

**Outputs.** `w_max` (mm), `sigma_max` (MPa, von Mises), `sigma_principal`, `f1` (Hz), `u_stress`, `u_defl`, `w_over_t`, `D`, `location`; case-specific `terms_used`, `M_x_c`, `M_y_c`, `M_xy_k`, `R_corner`, `M_edge`, `M_c`, `lambda2`. **Ports.** `w_max` (`plate.w_max`), `sigma_max` (`plate.sigma_max`), `f1` (`plate.f1`). Links: S6, E2, F-group.

**Limitations.** Thin plates (span/t ≥ 10), small deflections (w/t ≤ 0.5), isotropic, constant thickness, one support type all round, centred loads only, no elastic edges, no local effects under concentrated loads, simply supported rectangle with corners held down, no temperature or in-plane loads.

**Test cases.** Analytic, **Confirmed** (recomputed in python): `ka-circular-clamped-uniform` and `ka-circular-simple-uniform` (the closed forms and the frequencies from the Bessel equations); `ka-circular-simple-patch` and `ka-circular-clamped-ring` (derived solutions, cross-checked in python by finite differences and by integrating the ring solution over the patch); `ka-rect-simple-square-series` (the Navier series summed in numpy at 1024 and 512 terms with the 1/N² tail removed: `w_max = 0.0040623527 q a⁴/D`, `M_x = 0.0478864 q a²`, `|M_xy| = 0.0324823 q a²`); `ka-rect-simple-natural`; `ka-rect-simple-patch`. **Unconfirmed**: `ka-rect-simple-square-book` (`0.00406`, Timoshenko & Woinowsky-Krieger 2nd ed. Ch. 5), `ka-rect-clamped-square` (the Ritz solution recomputed in an independent numpy implementation; approximate method), `ka-rect-clamped-square-book` (`0.00126`, Ch. 6), `ka-rect-clamped-natural` (`λ² = 35.99`, Leissa, to be checked against a copy). The behaviour tests cover the closed forms against the general solution, the ring-sum check, the centre/corner stress claim, the strip limit of a long clamped plate, convergence, and a CsCheck property (deflection ∝ 1/t³, stress ∝ 1/t²).

**Not pinned (owner check).** Book values beyond the coefficients above (Timoshenko tables for other aspect ratios, patch loads); any published clamped-plate frequency other than the square.

**Worked example.** `docs/worked-examples/structural/struct.plate-bending/enclosure-lid-aluminium/`: 300 × 200 × 4 mm aluminium lid under 20 kPa (chain 10, enclosure): `w_max` 0.59 mm, σ_vm 21.6 MPa at the centre, f₁ 353 Hz. E, ν, ρ are pinned to the W3 generic aluminium seed (indicative); the yield strength is assumed; γ = 1.1 is the EN 1999 value entered in the γ_M0 slot, to be checked.
