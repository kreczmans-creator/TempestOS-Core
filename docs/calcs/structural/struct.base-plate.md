# S6 — Column base plate, EN 1993-1-8 (`struct.base-plate@1.0.0`)

**Scope.** An unstiffened rectangular base plate under an I/H, RHS or CHS column on grouted concrete: bearing resistance and the plate thickness it needs, the tension T-stub and holding-down bolts and the compression flange T-stub for a major-axis moment, and shear by friction plus bolts. **Independent check demanded** (the author cannot check it, DA-12); every input outside its range is a hard FAIL (plate 100–1500 mm, t_p 10–100 mm, f_y 235–460 MPa, f_ck 20–50 MPa, α 1–3, and so on). The concrete and anchor design is **not** done here: the sheet outputs the holding-down bolt tension per bolt for Hilti PROFIS, and the PROFIS report is filed as Evidence and referenced by `profis_ref`.

**Method.**
- Bearing (cl. 6.2.5): `f_cd = α_cc f_ck/γ_c`; `f_jd = β_j α f_cd` with β_j = 2/3 fixed and shown; `c = t_p √[f_y/(3 f_jd γ_M0)]`. `α` is the user's concentration factor √(A_c1/A_c0) (EN 1992-1-1 cl. 6.7, ≤ 3); a value above 1 warns until `alpha_basis` states the foundation geometry.
- Effective area `A_eff` round the column footprint. I/H: `2(b + 2c)(t_f + 2c) + (h − 2t_f − 2c)(t_w + 2c)`; when `h − 2t_f − 2c < 0` the strips overlap and the full rectangle `(b + 2c)(h + 2c)` is used, and the sheet says so. RHS: `(b + 2c)(h + 2c) − (b − 2t − 2c)(h − 2t − 2c)`, inner terms ≥ 0. CHS: `π[(D/2 + c)² − (D/2 − t − c)²]`, inner term ≥ 0. **Where c would pass the plate edge it is cut back to the edge** (outstand `min(c, (B − b)/2)` across the width, `min(c, (L − h)/2)` along the length, `min(c, (min(B, L) − D)/2)` for CHS), so no area outside the plate is counted; the whole is also capped at B·L. This is never larger than the uncut area clipped to B·L, and equal to it when nothing is cut. The sheet states when the cut-back is used.
- `N_j,Rd = f_jd A_eff`; `u_N = max(N_Ed, 0)/N_j,Rd`. Required thickness `t_req`: the smallest t_p in 10–100 mm (bisection) with `f_jd A_eff(c(t_p)) ≥ N_c,Ed`; shown as 10 mm when it is carried at the minimum, "> 100 mm" when 100 mm is not enough, "— (no compression)" when N_Ed ≤ 0.
- Moment (cl. 6.2.8.3, 6.2.6.11, simplified): compression resultant at distance `z_C` from the column axis (blank: `z/2`, symmetric), tension row at `z_T = z − z_C`: `C_Ed = (M_Ed + N_Ed z_T)/z`, `T_Ed = max(C_Ed − N_Ed, 0) = max((M_Ed − N_Ed z_C)/z, 0)`; symmetric this is `M_Ed/z ± N_Ed/2`. `F_t,Ed = T_Ed/n_t` is the port to PROFIS. Compression side (only when M_Ed > 0, since pure axial load is covered by `N_j,Rd`): `C_Rd = f_jd A_flange`, A_flange = `(b + 2c)(t_f + 2c)` (I/H), `(b + 2c)(t + 2c)` (RHS), half of A_eff (CHS), capped at half of A_eff.
- Plate tension T-stub (cl. 6.2.6.11, 6.2.4, Table 6.2): `M_pl,Rd = 0.25 l_eff t_p² f_y/γ_M0`; mode 1 `4 M_pl,Rd/m`; mode 2 `(2 M_pl,Rd + n n_t F_t,Rd)/(m + n)`, `n = min(e_min, 1.25 m)`; mode 3 `n_t F_t,Rd`; `T_Rd` = the minimum, with the governing mode named. With `prying` off (bolts long) the single plate mode is `2 M_pl,Rd/m` (mode 1-2 without prying) and mode 3. `u_T = T_Ed/T_Rd`, `u_bt = F_t,Ed/F_t,Rd`.
- Shear (cl. 6.2.2): `V_Rd = C_f,d max(N_Ed, 0) + n_b F_vb,Rd`; `u_V = V_Ed/V_Rd`. A column in net tension has no friction.
- `u_max` is the largest utilisation. The anchorage condition "Anchorage designed externally (PROFIS report filed as Evidence)" passes when `profis_ref` is given; otherwise there is a warning (rule `anchorage`), not a failure.

**Inputs.** `column` (I/H, RHS, CHS); `h`, `b` 50–1200 mm, `t_f`, `t_w`, `t` 3–100 mm, `D` 50–1200 mm (only those of the chosen section are needed); `B`, `L` 100–1500 mm, `t_p` 10–100 mm; `f_y` 235–460 MPa (record `steel.strengths`); `f_ck` 20–50 MPa; `alpha_cc`, `gamma_c` (record `ec2.national-annex`; defaults 0.85, 1.5); `gamma_M0`, `C_fd` (record `ec3.national-annex`; defaults 1.0, 0.2); `alpha` 1–3 and optional `alpha_basis`; `N_Ed` −5000…10000 kN (compression positive), `M_Ed` 0–2000 kN·m, `V_Ed` 0–2000 kN; `n_t`, `n_b` (whole numbers, n_b ≥ 2 n_t), `z`, optional `z_C`, `m`, `l_eff` (from the cl. 6.2.6.5 patterns), `e_min`, `prying`, `F_t_Rd` and `F_vb_Rd` per bolt (from S4 by link); optional `profis_ref`.

**Outputs.** `f_cd`, `beta_j`, `f_jd`, `c`, `A_eff`, `N_j_Rd`, `u_N`, `t_req`, `z_T`, `C_Ed`, `T_Ed`, `F_t_Ed`, `A_flange`, `C_Rd`, `u_C`, `M_pl_Rd`, `T_Rd_1`, `n_pry`, `T_Rd_2`, `T_Rd_3`, `T_Rd`, `T_mode`, `u_T`, `u_bt`, `F_f_Rd`, `V_Rd`, `u_V`, `u_max`. **Ports.** `F_t_Ed` (kN per bolt → Hilti PROFIS), `N_j_Rd`, `u_max`. Links in: S1 section, S2/S5 actions, S4 bolt resistances. **Checks.** concrete bearing; compression flange T-stub (M_Ed > 0); tension T-stub; bolt tension; shear; anchorage condition.

**Limitations.** Static loading; uniaxial (major-axis) moment; unstiffened plate; the simplified T-stub model (resultants at the compression flange centre and the bolt row; the web gets no credit in the moment check; one `l_eff` for modes 1 and 2; prying is the user's `prying` switch, not a computed L_b ≤ L_b*); β_j = 2/3 assumes the grout conditions of cl. 6.2.5 are met (not checked); the CHS compression side is half the annulus (approximate); bolts share shear equally; **anchorage, pull-out, breakout and edge distances are designed externally in PROFIS**; no shear key, no weld design, no fatigue.

**Test cases.** `ka-ih-moment-bolts` (N 300, M 120, V 80, z 300: C_Ed 550, T_Ed 250, F_t,Ed 125 kN; C_Rd 601.84; modes 628.57 / 296.35 / 282 → 282 kN; V_Rd 436 kN; u_max 0.9139), `ka-ih-axial` (UC-like 260×256×17×10 on 400×400×20 S275, C25/30: f_jd 14.167 MPa, c 50.875 mm, A_eff 98 849.99 mm², N_j,Rd 1400.37 kN, t_req 17.075 mm), `ka-rhs-axial` (RHS 200×200×10: A_eff 84 929.55 mm², N_j,Rd 1203.17 kN), `ka-chs-axial` (CHS 220×10: A_eff 73 724.93 mm², N_j,Rd 1044.44 kN), `ka-ih-overlap-clip` (overlap and plate-edge cut-back: A_eff 106 049.64 mm²) — all analytic, Confirmed, recomputed independently in python. **Not pinned — owner check:** the SCI P358 column-base worked examples and Wald, Sokol, Steenhuis & Jaspart, "Component method for steel column bases", *Heron* 53(1/2), 2008, are not available to the author; no published inputs or answers are stated here. The worked example `portal-column-base-uc254` is **Unconfirmed** (UC 254×254×89 dimensions and the `l_eff` estimate to be checked).
