# S7 — EN 1993-1-1 lateral-torsional buckling and N+M interaction (`struct.ltb-interaction@1.0.0`)

**Scope.** A uniform, doubly-symmetric I-section member of class 1, 2 or 3 on fork supports (free to rotate about the minor axis and to warp at the ends, restrained against twist): the elastic critical moment, lateral-torsional buckling resistance (6.3.2.2 general case or 6.3.2.3 rolled and welded equivalent sections), flexural buckling about both axes (6.3.1) and the interaction of axial compression with bending about both axes (6.3.3, Annex B or Annex A). **Every numeric input has a hard range** (soft = hard): an input outside it blocks the sheet. Class 4 blocks.

**Limitations (also on the sheet).** Doubly-symmetric uniform members, fork supports; no mono-symmetric or tapered members, no cantilevers, no intermediate restraint, no cross-section classification or class 4 effective properties, no shear or cross-section (6.2.9) check; one moment diagram about y-y (linear, or `C_1`, `k_c`, `C_m` entered for others). `k` and `k_w` below 1 model partial end restraint; the ψ formula for `C_1` is for end moments with `k = 1`.

**Editions.** `edition` = `2005+A1:2014` (default, in force until the 2022 edition is adopted) or `2022`. **The 2022 choice changes the label only**: the arithmetic implemented is that of 2005+A1:2014 (the differences between the editions are not implemented because they cannot be stated with certainty), clause numbers shown are the 2005 ones, and a warning says so.

**Method.** Formulas in own notation (see the template method text). `M_cr` by the general (NCCI SN003) expression with `C_1`, `C_2`, `k`, `k_w`, `z_g` (for a doubly-symmetric section; `z_g` positive for a load on the compression-flange side of the shear centre, which destabilises; `z_g` negative raises `M_cr`). `C_1 = 1.88 − 1.40ψ + 0.52ψ² ≤ 2.70` for end moments (derived curve fit, labelled) unless `C_1` is entered; no table of `C_1` or `C_2` is shipped. `λ̄_LT = √(W_y f_y/M_cr)`, `W_y = W_pl,y` (class 1–2) or `W_el,y` (class 3). `χ_LT` by 6.3.2.2 (`Φ_LT = 0.5[1 + α_LT(λ̄_LT − 0.2) + λ̄_LT²]`, `χ_LT = 1/(Φ_LT + √(Φ_LT² − λ̄_LT²)) ≤ 1`) or 6.3.2.3 (`λ̄_LT,0`, `β`, `χ_LT ≤ 1/λ̄_LT²`); curve `auto` selects from the section type and h/b (Table 6.4 for 6.3.2.2, Table 6.5 for 6.3.2.3) or the curve is chosen. Optional `f` modification `χ_LT,mod = χ_LT/f ≤ 1`, `f = 1 − 0.5(1 − k_c)[1 − 2(λ̄_LT − 0.8)²] ≤ 1` (6.3.2.3 only), `k_c` entered or `1/(1.33 − 0.33ψ)`. `M_b,Rd = χ_LT W_y f_y/γ_M1`. Flexural buckling: `N_cr = π²EI/L_cr²`, `λ̄`, `χ` by the curve chosen for each axis, `N_b,Rd = χ A f_y/γ_M1`. Interaction 6.61 and 6.62 with `k_yy, k_yz, k_zy, k_zz` from **Annex B** (Table B.1, or Table B.2 when the member is susceptible to torsional deformation; plastic for class 1–2, elastic for class 3; `C_m = 0.6 + 0.4ψ ≥ 0.4` for a linear diagram or entered) or from **Annex A** (μ, w, n_pl, a_LT, `C_ij`, `C_my`, `C_mz`, `C_mLT`; `N_cr,T` with `l_T = k_w L`; N must stay below the elastic buckling forces; the LTB terms `b_LT … e_LT` are zero when `λ̄_0 ≤ 0.2√C_1⁴√((1 − N/N_cr,z)(1 − N/N_cr,T))`). The Annex A and Annex B expressions are implemented from the published formulae; **their coefficients are to be checked against the owner's copy of the standard** (the Annex A sheet carries a warning to that effect).

**Inputs.** Method `edition`, `ltb_method`, `annex`, `torsion`; material `f_y` 235–460 MPa, `E` 190–220 GPa, `G` 70–90 GPa (each takes a W3 material through the record kind `material.properties` and stays editable), `gamma_M1` 1.0–1.25 (record `ec3.national-annex`); section `section_class`, `section_type`, `h`, `b`, `A`, `I_y`, `I_z`, `I_t`, `I_w`, `W_pl_y`, `W_el_y`, `W_pl_z`, `W_el_z` (typed, or linked from an S1 sheet: S1's `I_xx`, `I_yy`, `J`, `I_w`, `W_pl_x`, `W_el_x`, `W_pl_y`, `W_el_y` map to `I_y`, `I_z`, `I_t`, `I_w`, `W_pl_y`, `W_el_y`, `W_pl_z`, `W_el_z`; no section tables are shipped); restraint `L` 0.1–100 m, `k` and `k_w` 0.5–1.0, `z_g` ±1000 mm, optional `C_1` 1–4, `C_2` 0–1.5, `ltb_curve`, `use_f`, optional `k_c` (record `ec3.mcr-factors`); factor set `alpha_a0 … alpha_d`, `lambda_LT0`, `beta_LT` (record `ec3.ltb-factors`, recommended values as defaults, labelled); actions `moment_mode` (`ψ and M_y,Ed` or `end moments` with signed `M_y_A`, `M_y_B`), `N_Ed` (tension positive, compression negative, as S2/S3; a positive value is outside the range), `M_y_Ed` (link from S2 `M_abs_e1` or S5), `psi_y`, `M_z_Ed`, `psi_z`, optional `C_m_y`, `C_m_z`, `C_m_LT`; buckling `L_cr_y`, `L_cr_z`, `curve_y`, `curve_z`.

**Outputs.** `C_1`, `Mcr`, `lambda_LT`, `alpha_LT`, `Phi_LT`, `chi_LT`, `Mb_Rd`, `N_Rk`, `N_cr_y`, `N_cr_z`, `lambda_y`, `lambda_z`, `chi_y`, `chi_z`, `N_b_y_Rd`, `N_b_z_Rd`, `N_b_Rd`, `k_yy`, `k_yz`, `k_zy`, `k_zz`, `C_my`, `C_mz`, `C_mLT`, `u_LTB`, `u_buckling`, `u_61`, `u_62`, `u_interaction`, `u_member`, `governing`. **Ports.** `Mb_Rd` (the brief's `ltb.Mb_Rd`), `u_interaction` (`member.util_interaction`), `u_member`, `Mcr`, `chi_LT`, `lambda_LT`, `N_b_Rd`, `u_LTB`. **Checks.** LTB; flexural buckling; equations 6.61 and 6.62 (governing first). Links: S1 (section), S2 and S5 (moment, axial force), S3, S8 (spreader beam).

**Test cases.** Analytic, **Confirmed**, recomputed in python (independent reimplementation): `ka-mcr-uniform-6322` (welded I 500×200×16/10 from plate properties, 6 m, uniform moment: M_cr 398.306 kN·m, λ̄_LT 1.36691, χ_LT 0.315963 curve d, M_b,Rd 235.142 kN·m; the classical closed form is also asserted in the behaviour tests), `ka-6323-f-modified` (ψ = 0: C_1 1.88, M_cr 748.816, χ_LT 0.561679, f 0.885561, χ_LT,mod 0.634264, M_b,Rd 472.024), `ka-mcr-k-zg-c2` (k 0.8, C_1 1.35, C_2 0.45, z_g +250: M_cr 440.833), `ka-flexural-buckling` (χ_y 0.931891, χ_z 0.603545, N_b,Rd 2373.98 kN). **Unconfirmed** (analytic from the Annex B/A expressions as implemented; the coefficient tables are typed from the published expressions): `ka-annex-b-class2` (Table B.2, k_yy 0.848513, k_zz 1.600473, 6.62 = 1.24793), `ka-annex-b-class3-b1` (Table B.1 elastic), `ka-annex-a-class2`.
