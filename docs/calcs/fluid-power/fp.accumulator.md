# F2 — Accumulator sizing, gas law (`fp.accumulator@1.0.0`)

**Scope.** The pre-charge gas volume V0 of a hydraulic accumulator that delivers (or absorbs) a liquid volume ΔV between the lowest working pressure p1 and the highest p2, the pre-charge pressure at the charging temperature, the next size from the user's size list, and the bar·litre product against the PSSR Schedule 1 250 bar·L exception. Three duties: energy storage, shock and pulsation (screening only; the pressure wave is not modelled) and emergency stroke (ΔV is the stroke volume, to be linked from the cylinder circuit F1 later). **Not a pressure-vessel code check**: no PD 5500, EN 13445 or ASME VIII calculation, no PED / UK PER or PSSR compliance statement (ADR-0170, Addendum B §B5). Every input outside its valid range is a hard FAIL (hard limit = valid range).

**Method.** Absolute pressures `pa = p + p_atm` (Gauge entry) or as entered (Absolute entry), through the shared `GasLaw` kernel (`Tempest.Calc.Engine.Physics.GasLaw.ChargeVolume`, also behind B6): `V0 = ΔV / [(pa0/pa1)^(1/n) − (pa0/pa2)^(1/n)]`, n = 1 (isothermal) … 1.4 (adiabatic), times the real-gas factor `f_rg` (default 1). Pre-charge at the charging temperature `p0(T_c) = pa0 · (T_c + 273.15)/(T_max + 273.15)` (the addendum writes 273; the 0.15 K changes the result by under 0.01 %); `p0_set` is that pressure on the entered basis (gauge or absolute). Rules (records): `u_r0 = pa0/(r_0·pa1)` with r_0 = 0.9 and `u_rp = (pa2/pa0)/r_p` with r_p = 4, both PASS/FAIL checks. Next size: the smallest listed size not below V0. `bar·L = PS · V`, PS = pa2 − p_atm (gauge), V the selected size (else V0); the `pssr` output states whether it is below 250 bar·L.

**Inputs.** `mode`, `dV` 0.001–1000 L, `p_basis` (Gauge / Absolute), `p_0`, `p_1`, `p_2` 0–700 bar, `p_atm` 0.5–1.1 bar, `n` 1–1.4, `f_rg` 0.8–1.5, `T_max` −40…150 °C, `T_c` −40…100 °C; records `accumulator.rules` (`r_0` 0.1–1, `r_p` 1–20) and `accumulator.sizes` (optional table `sizes`, column `V`, 0.01–5000 L). Blocks: `p_0` zero or above `p_1`, or `p_2` not above `p_1` (absolute).

**Outputs.** `V0` (L), `V_sel` and `u_V` (when a size list is entered and a size is large enough), `p0` (absolute, at the charging temperature), `p0_set`, `u_r0`, `u_rp`, `bar_litres`, `pssr` (text). **Ports.** `V0`, `p0`, `bar_litres` (`acc.V0`, `acc.p0`, `acc.bar_litres`). **Checks.** pre-charge within the p0/p1 rule; pressure ratio within the p2/p0 rule; a listed size covers V0 (only when a list is entered and none is large enough). **Warnings.** `realgas` above 200 bar absolute (ideal-gas volume, or your factor in force); `shock` for the screening duty; `exponent` for n below 1.3 in a shock or emergency duty; `size`; constants not taken from a record.

**Limitations.** Ideal gas unless the user supplies `f_rg` (no nitrogen real-gas table ships); single gas pre-charge, no gas-side heat-transfer model (n is the user's), no bladder or piston friction, no oil compressibility or leakage; the shock duty is a gas-volume screening only. The size list and the pre-charge rules are the manufacturer's data and are not shipped. The 250 bar·L indication assumes PS is the highest working pressure and V the nominal volume; applicability of PSSR (relevant fluid, definitions) is for the competent person.

**Test cases.** Analytic, recomputed in python3 (Confirmed): `ka-parker-numbers-absolute` (the Parker HY10-4004/UK numbers p0 90, p1 100, p2 210 bar, ΔV 14 L, isothermal, **read as absolute bar**: V0 = 14/(0.9 − 0.428571) = 29.69697 L, p0 81.64475 bar at 20 °C from 50 °C, bar·L 6206.27); `ka-parker-numbers-gauge` (the same numbers as gauge bar: 29.80704 L, and the p0 ≤ 0.9·p1 rule fails by 0.1 %); `ka-adiabatic` (n = 1.4: 36.69279 L); `ka-charge-temperature` (80 °C to 20 °C: 74.70905 bar abs); `ka-size-and-real-gas` (f_rg 1.05: 31.18182 L, next size 32 L, 6687.576 bar·L); `ka-below-pssr` (1.25 L, 123.73 bar·L). The quoted "about 29.7 to 29.8 L" fits both readings (29.697 as absolute, 29.807 as gauge + 1.01325 bar); the absolute reading puts p0 exactly on the 0.9·p1 limit, which is why it is the one used for the worked example. **Unconfirmed + Secondary:** `ka-parker-hy10-4004` (V0 about 29.7 L and the catalogue's 32 L selection, as the addendum quotes the Parker example), to be checked against the owner's copy of the catalogue, including whether its pressures are gauge or absolute. **Not pinned (owner check):** the NFPA "How to Size an Accumulator" temperature example (V2 = 1347.77 cm³): its inputs are not stated in the addendum.
