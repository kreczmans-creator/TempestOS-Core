# F3 — O-ring and seal gland (`fp.oring-gland@1.1.0`)

**Scope.** Squeeze, installed stretch, gland fill and extrusion gap of a radial piston seal, a radial rod seal or a face seal, from the ring and groove dimensions with their tolerances, at the cold and shrunk end and the hot and swollen end of the temperature and swell range. Static or dynamic duty selects the squeeze band. The worst case and the capability indices run through the tolerance-stack engine of M1 (`Tempest.Calc.Engine.Analysis.ToleranceStack.ToleranceStackEngine`); the seeded Monte Carlo is the engine's `MonteCarlo` run on the exact non-linear model, and reports the fraction of samples outside each limit; a capability index per criterion comes from the linearised stack. A face seal is entered with the pressure outside or inside. Measured groove or ring distributions (mean, standard deviation, distribution) can replace the toleranced dimensions from an optional table, so the scan import W2 can feed it. **No data is shipped**: the ring size, the squeeze, fill and stretch bands and the permitted extrusion gap are inputs or user records; there is no AS568 or ISO 3601 size table, no gland table and no extrusion chart in source, seeds, fixtures, tests or this document (Addendum B §B5). Fluid Power, ADR-0170.

**Method.** Lengths in millimetres inside the model, ratios as fractions. Case factors: ring `f = (1 + α(T − 20 °C))·(1 + s)^(1/3)` (linear thermal expansion times the cube root of the volume swell), groove metal `g = 1 + α_m(T − 20 °C)`; cold end `(T_min, s_min)`, hot end `(T_max, s_max)`, installed `f = g = 1`. Stretch `X = g·D_ref/(f·d) − 1` where `D_ref` is the diameter the ring ID sits on: the groove root `D_g` (piston, face) or the rod `D_s` (rod seal). Effective section `W_eff = f·W·(1 − r(X))/√(1 − c)`, where `c = 1 − g·D_g/(f·(d + 2W))` (not below zero) is the compression of the ring outside diameter where that is held in the groove — a rod seal in its housing groove, an internal-pressure face seal on the groove outer wall — and zero for every other seal (a constant-volume thickening, M4). Depth `h` is `(D_s − D_g)/2` (piston), `(D_g − D_s)/2` (rod) or the entered `h_f` (face); gap is `D_s − D_c`, `D_c − D_s` or the entered `e_f`. Squeeze `q = 1 − g·h/W_eff`. Groove area `A_g = (g·b)(g·h) − 2(1 − π/4)(g·r_g)²`. Fill is a volume ratio: the maximum (hot end) is `(π/4)·(f·W_max)²·(f/g)/A_g,min`, i.e. `(π/4)·W²·(1 + αΔT)³(1 + s)/(A_g,nom·g³)` — a ring held circumferentially by the groove takes the whole thermal and swell volume change in its section (M2; the isotropic `f²` it replaces read 2 to 5 points low, on the unsafe side); where the outside diameter is held the same volume change is the compression, `(π/4)·(f·W)²/(1 − c)`; the minimum (cold end) is `(π/4)·W_eff²/A_g,max`. A rod seal and an internal-pressure face seal also report the outside-diameter compression `(d + 2W − D_g)/(d + 2W)`, checked against its own limit `od_hi`.
The stretch is also reported and checked at the cold end (`X_c`, against `x_hi`), where the ring is shortest against the groove.
The cross-section reduction `r(X)` is a user-selectable relation: **None**; **Incompressible rubber (derived)**, `r = 1 − 1/√(1 + X)` (constant volume, default); **Parker ORD 5700 section 3.5 quadratic (as cited)**, `Y = 0.56 + 0.59·X − 0.0046·X²` with X and Y in per cent, applied for X above zero and used for about **3 to 25 % stretch only** (M3): above 25 % the 25 % value is held, below 3 % the reduction is blended linearly to zero at no stretch so that it does not jump from nothing to 0.56 % at the first trace of stretch, and the sheet warns (`stretch`) when a stretch lies outside the range — implemented as Addendum B quotes it, with the section reference only, and **not confirmed** against the handbook (what Y measures, and the exact range it covers, are an owner check; the sub-3 % blend is this sheet's, not the handbook's); **Linear in stretch**, `r = ν·X` for the Poisson ratio ν (a W3 material property).
**Worst case** (engine): each range is the worst-case band of a linear stack (depth, ring section scaled by `f`, groove width, the two ring and seat diameters, the gap); the stretch ends are `D_ref,max/d_min − 1` and `D_ref,min/d_max − 1`. Minimum squeeze `= 1 − h_max,cold/W_eff,min` with `W_eff,min = f_c·W_min·(1 − r(X_c,max))`; maximum squeeze `= 1 − h_min,hot/W_eff,max`. Maximum fill uses the hot end on the unstretched section `f_h·W_max` over the smallest area; minimum fill the cold end on `W_eff,min` over the largest area. The ends of one dimension are taken independently, so where the groove root diameter sets both the depth and the stretch of a piston seal the worst case is slightly conservative; the sheet prints the exact non-linear corner as a check and warns (`worst-case`) if it is more than 0.2 points below the stack value.
**Monte Carlo** (engine): the engine's seeded `MonteCarlo.Run` evaluates the exact criterion (`ORingGlandModel.Evaluate`) on every sample, each dimension that varies drawn in dimension order (W, d, D_s, D_g, D_c, h_f, b, e_f) from its own distribution; the fraction below the lower and above the upper limit is reported (L1; the linearised model under-predicted the minimum-squeeze tail by about 9 % relative). The capability index C_pk stays on the criterion linearised about the mean dimensions with a secant slope across each band, `a_i = (F(mid_i + half_i) − F(mid_i − half_i))/(2·half_i)`, through `ToleranceStackEngine.Analyse`. Dimensions are normal, uniform or triangular about their mid-limit (`σ = half/k`, `half/√3`, `half/√6`); a measured row sets the mean, σ and distribution, with half-band `k·σ`, `σ√3` or `σ√6`; a measured row whose `dim` is not a gland dimension blocks (L6).
**Extrusion gap.** The largest gap of a radial seal is taken over the installed, cold and hot temperatures with each part expanding by its own metal — the sealing-surface part (bore of a piston seal, rod of a rod seal) by `alpha_s` (blank: the groove metal's `alpha_m`), the groove-metal part by `alpha_m` — plus the diametral growth of the bore under pressure `dD_p` that the user enters (0 if not known; L3). A face seal's axial gap is as dimensioned.
**Extrusion limit.** The permitted gap is the smaller of the entered `gap_allow` and the record `ext` read at the ring hardness `H_A` and the pressure `p_op`: points `(H, p, gap)` are grouped by hardness into curves of gap against pressure; linear in pressure within a curve and in hardness between curves. Pressure above a curve's last point, or hardness below the softest listed curve, is outside the record: the nearest listed value is shown, the check **fails** and the sheet warns (`extrusion-range`). Hardness above the hardest curve uses that curve (conservative). With neither a record nor a value the extrusion is not verified: the sheet warns (`extrusion`) and the check "Extrusion verified against a permitted gap" **fails** while the operating pressure is above zero (L2).

**Inputs.** `type` (Piston / Rod / Face), `duty` (Static / Dynamic), `face_p` (face seals: External, ring ID seated on the groove inner wall and stretched, the default; Internal, ring OD seated on the groove outer wall and compressed, with `D_g` the outer wall; M5); ring `d_r`, `d_r_tol`, `W`, `W_tol` (record `oring.ring`; mm, ±); gland `D_s`, `D_g`, `D_c`, `h_f`, `e_f`, `b`, each with `_u` and `_l` signed deviations (D_s, D_g, D_c for radial seals, h_f and e_f for face seals, D_g for both), and the bottom corner radius `r_g`; environment `T_min`, `T_max` (°C), `alpha` (ring, 1/K, material property `alpha`), `alpha_m` (groove metal, 1/K; not a W3 material property because the library applies one material record to a sheet, so it is entered from the metal's data sheet — L5), `alpha_s` (optional, the sealing-surface part), `s_min`, `s_max` (volume change, %); `red` and `nu` (material property `nu`); bands `q_lo_s`, `q_hi_s`, `q_lo_d`, `q_hi_d`, `fill_lo`, `fill_hi`, `x_lo`, `x_hi`, `od_hi` (record `oring.criteria`; defaults 15–30 % static, 10–25 % dynamic, 60–85 % fill, 0–6 % stretch, OD compression up to 6 %, a placeholder equal to the stretch limit, M4); `H_A`, `p_op`, `dD_p`, `gap_allow` and the table `ext` (record `oring.extrusion`; columns `H`, `p`, `gap`); `n_mc` (1 000–100 000), `seed`, `k_sigma`, `dist`; the optional table `measured` (columns `dim` ∈ W, d_r, D_s, D_g, D_c, h_f, b, e_f, `mean`, `sigma`, `dist`). Every numeric input has a hard limit wider than its usual range. Blocks name the input: a lower deviation above the upper, a size that is zero or negative across its band, a gland with no depth, a negative gap, temperature, swell or band limits out of order, a groove area that is not positive.

**Outputs.** Squeeze `squeeze_nom` (mean dimensions, installed), `squeeze_min`, `squeeze_max`; fill `fill_min`, `fill_max`; stretch `stretch_nom`, `stretch_min`, `stretch`, `X_c` (cold end), `u_stretch` (against `x_hi`), `red_nom`; rod seals and internal-pressure face seals `od_comp_nom`, `od_comp`, `c_c`, `c_h`; gap `gap_min`, `gap_max`, `gap_allow`, `u_gap`; Monte Carlo `p_sq_min`, `p_sq_max`, `p_fill_max`, `p_fill_min`, `p_stretch`, `p_od` (rod, internal face), `p_gap` and `cpk_*` for each. A contribution Pareto of the minimum-squeeze spread. **Ports.** `squeeze_min`, `squeeze_max`, `fill_max`, `stretch` (`seal.squeeze_min`, `seal.squeeze_max`, `seal.fill_max`, `seal.stretch`), all in %. **Checks (PASS/FAIL).** Squeeze minimum and maximum within the band; fill maximum and minimum within the band; installed stretch not above its limit, also at the cold end, and not below its lower limit (not for an internal-pressure face seal); ring OD compression not above `od_hi` (rod, internal face); extrusion gap within the permitted gap, or the failing "Extrusion verified" check when no limit is entered. **Warnings.** `measured`, `extrusion`, `extrusion-range`, `expansion` (ring expands less than the groove metal: the cold and hot ends are not the bounds), `stretch`, `contact` (minimum squeeze not above zero), `face` (a dynamic face seal), `worst-case`, tabulated values not from a record.

**Limitations.** A plain rectangular groove with a bottom corner radius and a circular ring: edge breaks, groove wall angles, surface finish, groove eccentricity, pressure, friction, compression set, stress relaxation and gland fill under swell beyond the volume swell entered are not modelled. Squeeze and fill are bounded at the cold and hot ends only (the model is monotonic in temperature and swell when the ring expands more than the groove metal). The worst case is a stack of independent ends and the capability index is first-order in the tolerances; the Monte Carlo is exact in the model. The reduction relation applies to a ring stretched uniformly; the quadratic of Parker ORD 5700 section 3.5 is as quoted in Addendum B and used for about 3 to 25 % only. The gap does not model the bore's dilation by itself (it is entered) or a groove-side metal different from the mating part. The extrusion limit is the permitted gap from the user's record: no handbook chart is shipped. A face seal is entered with its depth and gap directly and has no coupling between the seat diameter and the depth; the internal-pressure face seal takes its ring OD seat from `D_g`. Not a seal-maker's design review: the compound, the surface finish, the lead-in chamfer and the dynamic duty (friction, wear, spiral failure) are for the seal maker.

**Test cases.** Analytic, recomputed in python3 (Confirmed): `ka-thermal-swell` (the defaults: squeeze 16.956 % to 26.957 %, fill 63.951 % to 84.643 % on the volume basis, cold-end stretch 4.4072 %, gap 0.106076 mm at 80 °C), `ka-worst-case-hand` (no reduction, no thermal change: squeeze 19.17647 % to 25 %, fill 67.5132 % to 78.6440 %, stretch 2.0833 % to 3.8306 %, gap 0.106 mm, all by hand arithmetic), `ka-rod-seal` (OD thickening: squeeze_max 26.820 %, fill_max 84.566 %), `ka-face-seal`, `ka-face-seal-internal` (pressure inside: OD compression 1.477 % at most), `ka-gap-over-temperature` (aluminium bore round a steel piston, 0.02 mm dilation: gap 0.163661 mm), `ka-incompressible-reduction` (r = 1 − 1/√1.04 = 1.94193 % at 4 % stretch), `ka-linear-reduction` (ν·X = 1.96 %), `ka-extrusion-record` (interpolation in an illustrative record invented for the test: 0.1575 mm at 80 Shore A and 150 bar), and two seeded Monte Carlo cases, `ka-monte-carlo-seeded` (20 000 samples, seed 7, normal tolerances, Parker quadratic) and `ka-monte-carlo-measured` (10 000 samples, seed 11, triangular tolerances with a measured groove and ring section), whose fractions are checked against a python3 re-implementation of the engine's xoshiro256** sampler on the exact non-linear criteria, and whose capability indices against the linearised stacks (the counts agree exactly; the nearest sample to any limit is more than 3e-7 away, far above rounding). **Unconfirmed + Secondary:** see *Definition of done* below.

**Definition of done (Addendum B §A3, §B4).** Every case above that is analytic is Confirmed; no published known-answer case is shipped, and none is invented. **Unconfirmed / Secondary items to list in the owner test pack:** `ka-parker-quadratic` (Unconfirmed, Secondary: Y at 4 % stretch is 2.8464 %, the arithmetic of the relation as Addendum B quotes it from Parker ORD 5700 section 3.5; the 3 to 25 % range, the sub-3 % blend and what Y measures are to be checked against the owner's copy of the handbook) and `ka-stretch-rule` (Unconfirmed, Secondary: the installation rule of up to 6 % piston-groove stretch, quoted from Parker PTD 5705; 5.8 % reads 0.967 of the limit; to be checked against the owner's copy, including the edition). **Not pinned (owner check):** the Parker ORD 5700 design examples of sections 3.5 to 3.7 — their inputs and answers are not available, so no published example is pinned.
