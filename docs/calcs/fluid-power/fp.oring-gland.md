# F3 — O-ring and seal gland (`fp.oring-gland@1.0.0`)

**Scope.** Squeeze, installed stretch, gland fill and extrusion gap of a radial piston seal, a radial rod seal or a face seal, from the ring and groove dimensions with their tolerances, at the cold and shrunk end and the hot and swollen end of the temperature and swell range. Static or dynamic duty selects the squeeze band. The worst case and a seeded Monte Carlo both run through the tolerance-stack engine of M1 (`Tempest.Calc.Engine.Analysis.ToleranceStack.ToleranceStackEngine`); the Monte Carlo reports the fraction of samples outside each limit and a capability index per criterion. Measured groove or ring distributions (mean, standard deviation, distribution) can replace the toleranced dimensions from an optional table, so the scan import W2 can feed it. **No data is shipped**: the ring size, the squeeze, fill and stretch bands and the permitted extrusion gap are inputs or user records; there is no AS568 or ISO 3601 size table, no gland table and no extrusion chart in source, seeds, fixtures, tests or this document (Addendum B §B5). Fluid Power, ADR-0170.

**Method.** Lengths in millimetres inside the model, ratios as fractions. Case factors: ring `f = (1 + α(T − 20 °C))·(1 + s)^(1/3)` (linear thermal expansion times the cube root of the volume swell), groove metal `g = 1 + α_m(T − 20 °C)`; cold end `(T_min, s_min)`, hot end `(T_max, s_max)`, installed `f = g = 1`. Stretch `X = g·D_ref/(f·d) − 1` where `D_ref` is the diameter the ring ID sits on: the groove root `D_g` (piston, face) or the rod `D_s` (rod seal). Effective section `W_eff = f·W·(1 − r(X))`. Depth `h` is `(D_s − D_g)/2` (piston), `(D_g − D_s)/2` (rod) or the entered `h_f` (face); gap is `D_s − D_c`, `D_c − D_s` or the entered `e_f`. Squeeze `q = 1 − g·h/W_eff`. Groove area `A_g = (g·b)(g·h) − 2(1 − π/4)(g·r_g)²`; fill `= (π/4)·W²/A_g`. A rod seal also reports the outside-diameter compression `(d + 2W − D_g)/(d + 2W)`.
The cross-section reduction `r(X)` is a user-selectable relation: **None**; **Incompressible rubber (derived)**, `r = 1 − 1/√(1 + X)` (constant volume, default); **Parker ORD 5700 section 3.5 quadratic (as cited)**, `Y = 0.56 + 0.59·X − 0.0046·X²` with X and Y in per cent, applied for X above zero and held at its vertex (64 % stretch) — implemented as Addendum B quotes it, with the section reference only, and **not confirmed** against the handbook (what Y measures, and the range it covers, are an owner check); **Linear in stretch**, `r = ν·X` for the Poisson ratio ν (a W3 material property).
**Worst case** (engine): each range is the worst-case band of a linear stack (depth, ring section scaled by `f`, groove width, the two ring and seat diameters, the gap); the stretch ends are `D_ref,max/d_min − 1` and `D_ref,min/d_max − 1`. Minimum squeeze `= 1 − h_max,cold/W_eff,min` with `W_eff,min = f_c·W_min·(1 − r(X_c,max))`; maximum squeeze `= 1 − h_min,hot/W_eff,max`. Maximum fill uses the hot end on the unstretched section `f_h·W_max` over the smallest area; minimum fill the cold end on `W_eff,min` over the largest area. The ends of one dimension are taken independently, so where the groove root diameter sets both the depth and the stretch of a piston seal the worst case is slightly conservative; the sheet prints the exact non-linear corner as a check and warns (`worst-case`) if it is more than 0.2 points below the stack value.
**Monte Carlo** (engine): each criterion is linearised about the mean dimensions with a secant slope across each band, `a_i = (F(mid_i + half_i) − F(mid_i − half_i))/(2·half_i)`; the linear model (one contributor per dimension plus a zero-width constant) goes to `ToleranceStackEngine.Analyse` (σ, C_pk) and `.Simulate` (seeded; the fraction below the lower and above the upper limit). Dimensions are normal, uniform or triangular about their mid-limit (`σ = half/k`, `half/√3`, `half/√6`); a measured row sets the mean, σ and distribution, with half-band `k·σ`, `σ√3` or `σ√6`. Against a full non-linear Monte Carlo of 300 000 samples the linearised fractions differ by about 0.2 percentage points at the 1–7 % level (Parker quadratic, normal tolerances).
**Extrusion.** The permitted gap is the smaller of the entered `gap_allow` and the record `ext` read at the ring hardness `H_A` and the pressure `p_op`: points `(H, p, gap)` are grouped by hardness into curves of gap against pressure; linear in pressure within a curve and in hardness between curves. Pressure above a curve's last point, or hardness below the softest listed curve, is outside the record: the nearest listed value is shown, the check **fails** and the sheet warns (`extrusion-range`). Hardness above the hardest curve uses that curve (conservative). With neither a record nor a value the check is not made and the sheet warns (`extrusion`).

**Inputs.** `type` (Piston / Rod / Face), `duty` (Static / Dynamic); ring `d_r`, `d_r_tol`, `W`, `W_tol` (record `oring.ring`; mm, ±); gland `D_s`, `D_g`, `D_c`, `h_f`, `e_f`, `b`, each with `_u` and `_l` signed deviations (D_s, D_g, D_c for radial seals, h_f and e_f for face seals, D_g for both), and the bottom corner radius `r_g`; environment `T_min`, `T_max` (°C), `alpha` (ring, 1/K, material property `alpha`), `alpha_m` (groove metal, 1/K), `s_min`, `s_max` (volume change, %); `red` and `nu` (material property `nu`); bands `q_lo_s`, `q_hi_s`, `q_lo_d`, `q_hi_d`, `fill_lo`, `fill_hi`, `x_lo`, `x_hi` (record `oring.criteria`; defaults 15–30 % static, 10–25 % dynamic, 60–85 % fill, 0–6 % stretch); `H_A`, `p_op`, `gap_allow` and the table `ext` (record `oring.extrusion`; columns `H`, `p`, `gap`); `n_mc` (1 000–100 000), `seed`, `k_sigma`, `dist`; the optional table `measured` (columns `dim` ∈ W, d_r, D_s, D_g, D_c, h_f, b, e_f, `mean`, `sigma`, `dist`). Every numeric input has a hard limit wider than its usual range. Blocks name the input: a lower deviation above the upper, a size that is zero or negative across its band, a gland with no depth, a negative gap, temperature, swell or band limits out of order, a groove area that is not positive.

**Outputs.** Squeeze `squeeze_nom` (mean dimensions, installed), `squeeze_min`, `squeeze_max`; fill `fill_min`, `fill_max`; stretch `stretch_nom`, `stretch_min`, `stretch`, `u_stretch` (against `x_hi`), `red_nom`; rod seals `od_comp_nom`, `od_comp`; gap `gap_min`, `gap_max`, `gap_allow`, `u_gap`; Monte Carlo `p_sq_min`, `p_sq_max`, `p_fill_max`, `p_fill_min`, `p_stretch`, `p_od` (rod), `p_gap` and `cpk_*` for each. A contribution Pareto of the minimum-squeeze spread. **Ports.** `squeeze_min`, `squeeze_max`, `fill_max`, `stretch` (`seal.squeeze_min`, `seal.squeeze_max`, `seal.fill_max`, `seal.stretch`), all in %. **Checks (PASS/FAIL).** Squeeze minimum and maximum within the band; fill maximum and minimum within the band; installed stretch not above and not below its limits; ring OD compression not above the stretch limit (rod); extrusion gap within the permitted gap (when a limit is known). **Warnings.** `measured`, `extrusion`, `extrusion-range`, `expansion` (ring expands less than the groove metal: the cold and hot ends are not the bounds), `stretch`, `contact` (minimum squeeze not above zero), `face` (a dynamic face seal), `worst-case`, tabulated values not from a record.

**Limitations.** A plain rectangular groove with a bottom corner radius and a circular ring: edge breaks, groove wall angles, surface finish, groove eccentricity, pressure, friction, compression set, stress relaxation and gland fill under swell beyond the volume swell entered are not modelled. Squeeze and fill are bounded at the cold and hot ends only (the model is monotonic in temperature and swell when the ring expands more than the groove metal). The Monte Carlo is first-order in the tolerances. The reduction relation applies to a ring stretched uniformly; the quadratic of Parker ORD 5700 section 3.5 is as quoted in Addendum B. The extrusion limit is the permitted gap from the user's record: no handbook chart is shipped. A face seal is entered with its depth and gap directly and has no coupling between the seat diameter and the depth. Not a seal-maker's design review: the compound, the surface finish, the lead-in chamfer and the dynamic duty (friction, wear, spiral failure) are for the seal maker.

**Test cases.** Analytic, recomputed in python3 (Confirmed): `ka-thermal-swell` (the defaults: squeeze 16.956 % to 26.957 %, fill 63.951 % to 82.594 %), `ka-worst-case-hand` (no reduction, no thermal change: squeeze 19.17647 % to 25 %, fill 67.5132 % to 78.6440 %, stretch 2.0833 % to 3.8306 %, gap 0.106 mm, all by hand arithmetic), `ka-rod-seal`, `ka-face-seal`, `ka-incompressible-reduction` (r = 1 − 1/√1.04 = 1.94193 % at 4 % stretch), `ka-linear-reduction` (ν·X = 1.96 %), `ka-extrusion-record` (interpolation in an illustrative record invented for the test: 0.1575 mm at 80 Shore A and 150 bar), and two seeded Monte Carlo cases, `ka-monte-carlo-seeded` (20 000 samples, seed 7, normal tolerances, Parker quadratic) and `ka-monte-carlo-measured` (10 000 samples, seed 11, triangular tolerances with a measured groove and ring section), whose fractions and capability indices are checked against a python3 re-implementation of the engine's xoshiro256** sampler on the same linearised stacks (the counts agree exactly; the nearest sample to any limit is more than 1e-7 away, far above rounding). **Unconfirmed + Secondary:** `ka-parker-quadratic` (Y at 4 % stretch is 2.8464 %, the arithmetic of the relation as Addendum B quotes it from Parker ORD 5700 section 3.5) and `ka-stretch-rule` (the installation rule of up to 6 % piston-groove stretch, quoted from Parker PTD 5705; 5.8 % reads 0.967 of the limit); both to be checked against the owner's copy of the handbook, including the edition. **Not pinned (owner check):** the Parker ORD 5700 design examples of sections 3.5 to 3.7 — their inputs and answers are not available, so no published example is pinned.
