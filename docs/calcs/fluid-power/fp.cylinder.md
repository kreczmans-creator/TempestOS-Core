# F1 — Cylinder and actuator circuit (`fp.cylinder@1.0.0`)

**Scope.** A double-acting hydraulic cylinder in its circuit: bore, rod and annulus areas; force and speed on the extend and the retract stroke with mechanical and volumetric efficiency and an optional back pressure, or with the regenerative extend connection; swept volume per stroke and cycle time; the pump delivery, shaft power, torque and speed; a hydraulic motor's torque; the velocity in the suction, pressure and return lines against the user's limits, with the smallest listed hose or pipe bore that meets them; a sharp orifice's flow for flow-control screening; and a screening check of the rod against buckling. **Screening only; ISO/TS 13725 method not implemented**: the buckling result carries that label on the sheet (scope, assumptions, the `buckling_note` output and the check name), and none of the standard's text, factors or tables is shipped (Addendum B §B5). Not a cylinder design to a standard. Every numeric input outside its valid range is a hard FAIL (hard limit = valid range).

**Method.** Areas `A_b = πD²/4`, `A_r = πd_r²/4`, `A_a = A_b − A_r`, ratio `φ = A_b/A_a`. Force `F_ext = (p·A_b − p_back·A_a)·η_m`, `F_ret = (p·A_a − p_back·A_b)·η_m`; regenerative extend `F_ext = p·A_r·η_m` (the standard values are kept as `F_std`, `v_std` for comparison). Speed `v_ext = Q·η_vc/A_b` (regenerative: `A_r`), `v_ret = Q·η_vc/A_a`; `t = stroke/v`, `t_cycle = t_ext + t_ret + t_dwell`. Swept volume `ΔV_ext = A_b·stroke` (regenerative: `A_r·stroke`), `ΔV_ret = A_a·stroke`, `dV` the larger. Pump `Q_p = V_g·n·η_vp`, `P = p·Q_p/η_tp` (the pump delivery, so a pump that delivers more than the circuit takes is taken to pass the excess over the relief valve at `p`, the conservative case for the drive; with no pump entered the circuit flow `Q` is used), `T_shaft = P/ω`, displacement needed `V_g,req = Q/(n·η_vp)`. Motor `T = Δp·V_g/(2π)·η_hm` (the displacement is entered per revolution, `cm^3/rev`, so the unit carries the 1/2π). Lines `v = Q_line/A_line`, `Q_line = Q` or the pump delivery if larger (suction and pressure lines; regenerative pressure line `Q_line·A_b/A_r`), return line `Q_line·A_b/A_a` (cap-side discharge on the retract stroke); smallest bore `d = √(4Q/(π·v_max))` and the recommended bore is the smallest listed size not below it. Orifice `Q = C_d·A_o·√(2Δp/ρ)`, bore for the circuit flow `d_o = √(4Q/(π·C_d·√(2Δp/ρ)))`. Rod buckling (screening): `L_eff = K_m·(stroke + L_add)`, solid round rod `r = d_r/4`, `I = πd_r⁴/64`, `λ = L_eff/r`, `λ_c = π√(2E/f_y)`; Euler `F_cr = π²E·I/L_eff²` for `λ > λ_c`, Johnson `F_cr = A_r·f_y·[1 − f_y·λ²/(4π²E)]` for `λ ≤ λ_c` (the two meet, with equal slope, at `λ_c`); `F_allow = F_cr/S_f`, utilisation `u_buck = F_push/F_allow`.

**Inputs.** `mode` (Standard / Regenerative extend); `p` 1–700 bar (gauge, at the cylinder and the pump), `p_back` 0–700 bar, `Q` 0.1–2000 L/min, `rho` 700–1300 kg/m³ (fluid, not a W3 material); loads `F_push`, `F_pull` 0–15000 kN; cylinder `D` 10–500 mm, `d_r` 4–450 mm (blocks if not below `D`), `stroke` 1–5000 mm, `eta_m`, `eta_vc` 0.5–1, `t_dwell` 0–3600 s; rod buckling `K_m` 0.5–2.5, `L_add` 0–5000 mm, `E` 50–250 GPa and `f_y` 100–1500 MPa (both W3 material properties, `MaterialProperty` set), `S_f` 1–20; pump (optional, entered together) `V_g` 0.1–1000 cm³/rev, `n_p` 50–6000 rpm, `eta_vp`, `eta_tp` 0.5–1 (blocks if `eta_tp > eta_vp`); motor (optional, entered together) `V_gm` 0.1–2000 cm³/rev, `dp_m` 1–700 bar, `eta_hm` 0.5–1; lines `d_suc`, `d_prs`, `d_ret` 3–200 mm, limits `v_suc_max`, `v_prs_max`, `v_ret_max` 0.1–20 m/s, optional table `hoses` (column `ID`, 3–200 mm); orifice `C_d` 0.2–1, `dp_o` 0.1–700 bar (blocks above `p`), `d_o` 0.1–100 mm (needs `dp_o`). **Records.** `material.properties` (`E`, `f_y`), `cylinder.mounting` (`K_m`), `cylinder.line-limits` (`v_suc_max`, `v_prs_max`, `v_ret_max`), `cylinder.hose-sizes` (`hoses`). The sheet's defaults for the three velocity limits (1.2, 5, 3 m/s) are placeholders, not values from a standard or a catalogue; no mounting table, hose table or velocity table ships. Blocks also if the back pressure takes all the force on a stroke.

**Outputs.** `A_b`, `A_r`, `A_a`, `phi`, `F_ext`, `F_ret` (`F_std` and `v_std` in the regenerative connection), `v_ext`, `v_ret`, `t_ext`, `t_ret`, `t_cycle`, `dV_ext`, `dV_ret`, `dV`, `u_push`, `u_pull`, `Q`, `p`; pump `Q_p`, `Vg_req`, `u_pump`, `P_pump`, `T_shaft`, `n_shaft`; motor `T_motor`; lines `Q_line`, `Q_prs`, `Q_rtn`, `v_suc`, `v_prs`, `v_rtn`, `u_suc`, `u_prs`, `u_rtn`, `d_min_*`, `d_rec_*`; orifice `d_o_req`, `Q_o`; rod `L_eff`, `lam`, `lam_c`, `F_cr`, `F_allow`, `u_buck`, `buckling_note` (text). **Ports.** `Q` (`hyd.flow`), `p` (`hyd.pressure`), `P_pump` (`hyd.pump_power`), `u_buck` (`hyd.buckling_util`), `F_ext` (`hyd.cylinder_force`), `dV` (swept volume of the larger stroke, to F2's emergency-stroke ΔV), `T_shaft` and `n_shaft` (pump shaft torque and speed, to E1 as B1 does; present when a pump is entered). **Checks.** extend force covers the push load; retract force covers the pull load; rod buckling (screening); suction, pressure and return line velocity within the limit; pump delivery covers the circuit flow (pump entered). **Warnings.** `size` when no listed size is large enough for a line.

**Limitations.** Steady motion only: no acceleration, cushioning, compressibility or pressure losses (line losses come from the pipe calculation, B1; `p` is the pressure at the cylinder port and the pump outlet). Mechanical and volumetric efficiencies are the user's. The pump is a fixed-displacement delivery at a stated speed; no pump curve, no relief-valve or load-sensing model. The rod is a solid round column with the mounting's effective-length factor; the tube, piston and rod-end stiffness, the side load and the stop-tube are not modelled, and the utilisation uses the push load entered, not the cylinder's own thrust. **ISO/TS 13725 is not implemented.** The orifice is the sharp-edged turbulent relation with the user's `C_d` (no viscosity, no cavitation).

**Test cases.** Analytic, recomputed in python3 (all Confirmed): `ka-circuit-standard` (100/70 at 160 bar, 36 L/min, 28 cm³/rev pump at 1450 rpm, motor, orifice, hose list, Johnson rod: `F_ext` 119.3805 kN, `F_ret` 60.88407 kN, `v_ext` 74.8665 mm/s, `t_cycle` 10.0846 s, `P_pump` 11.6879 kW, `T_shaft` 76.9731 N·m, `T_motor` 36.6693 N·m, return line 2.5678 m/s, `d_o_req` 5.0694 mm, `F_cr` 1787.81 kN, `u_buck` 0.15662); `ka-regenerative` (`F_ext` 58.4965 kN, `v_ext` 152.789 mm/s, `Q_prs` 78.7143 L/min); `ka-euler-slender` (40 mm rod over 2100 mm: λ = 210, Euler `F_cr` 59.0596 kN, `u_buck` 0.88893); `ka-back-pressure-fixed-free` (10 bar back pressure, K = 2: `F_ext` 115.5753 kN, `F_ret` 53.4228 kN, Johnson `F_cr` 1681.70 kN); `ka-orifice` (ρ 800, Δp 10 bar, C_d 0.6: jet 30 m/s, a 4 mm orifice passes 22.6195 L/min and the bore for that flow is 4 mm); `ka-lossless` (η = 1: `F = p·A`, `v = Q/A`, `t_ext` 4 s). **Unconfirmed / Secondary:** none shipped. **Not pinned (owner check):** the Esposito *Fluid Power with Applications* 7th ed. cylinder force/speed and pump power examples (Ch. 5–6) and the Parker industrial cylinder catalogue rod-sizing procedure: their inputs and answers cannot be stated from a source available to the build, so no case is added; check the sheet against the owner's copy of the book and the catalogue (the catalogue procedure would also be Secondary).
