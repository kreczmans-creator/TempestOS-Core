# F1 — Cylinder and actuator circuit (`fp.cylinder@1.4.0`)

**In plain terms.** A hydraulic cylinder turns fluid pressure into force and fluid flow into speed; because the rod takes up part of one side, the cylinder pushes harder than it pulls and moves faster in than out. The worksheet works out the areas, the force and speed on each stroke, the pump flow, shaft power and torque needed, the line velocities and an orifice, with the regenerative connection as an option.

**Scope.** A double-acting hydraulic cylinder in its circuit: bore, rod and annulus areas; force and speed on the extend and the retract stroke with mechanical and volumetric efficiency and an optional back pressure, or with the regenerative extend connection; swept volume per stroke and cycle time; the pump delivery, shaft power, torque and speed; a hydraulic motor's torque; the velocity in the suction, pressure and return lines against the user's limits, with the smallest listed hose or pipe bore that meets them; a sharp orifice's flow for flow-control screening; and a screening check of the rod against buckling. **Screening only; ISO/TS 13725 method not implemented**: the buckling result carries that label on the sheet (scope, assumptions, the `buckling_note` output and the check name), and none of the standard's text, factors or tables is shipped (Addendum B §B5). Not a cylinder design to a standard. Every numeric input outside its valid range is a hard FAIL (hard limit = valid range).

**Method.** Areas `A_b = πD²/4`, `A_r = πd_r²/4`, `A_a = A_b − A_r`, ratio `φ = A_b/A_a`. Force `F_ext = (p·A_b − p_back·A_a)·η_m`, `F_ret = (p·A_a − p_back·A_b)·η_m`; regenerative extend `F_ext = p·A_r·η_m` (the standard values are kept as `F_std`, `v_std` for comparison). Speed `v_ext = Q·η_vc/A_b` (regenerative: `A_r`), `v_ret = Q·η_vc/A_a`; `t = stroke/v`, `t_cycle = t_ext + t_ret + t_dwell`. Required speed (since 1.3.0, optional): when `v_req` is entered the flow is `Q = v_req·A_b/η_vc` (regenerative extend: `A_r`) and replaces the entered `Q`, which should then be blank (a `Q` other than the sheet's opening 36 L/min is ignored with the warning `v-req-q`; a `Q` still at the opening value is not warned about); the derived flow must lie within the 0.1 to 2000 L/min of `Q`, else the sheet blocks (`flow`). Neither a flow nor a speed blocks. Swept volume `ΔV_ext = A_b·stroke` (regenerative: `A_r·stroke`), `ΔV_ret = A_a·stroke`, `dV` the larger. Pump `Q_p = V_g·n·η_vp`, `P = p·Q_p/η_tp` (the pump delivery, so a pump that delivers more than the circuit takes is taken to pass the excess over the relief valve at `p`, the conservative case for the drive; with no pump entered the circuit flow `Q` is used), `T_shaft = P/ω`, displacement needed `V_g,req = Q/(n·η_vp)`. Motor `T = Δp·V_g/(2π)·η_hm` (the displacement is entered per revolution, `cm^3/rev`, so the unit carries the 1/2π). Lines `v = Q/A_line`: the pressure and return flow base is `Q_line = max(Q, Q_p)` (no volumetric efficiency, while the speeds use `Q·η_vc`: the line flows are the conservative side of the speeds); the suction line carries the geometric delivery, `Q_suc = max(Q, V_g·n)`; the line to the cylinder cap port is judged at the larger flow of the two strokes, `Q_prs = Q_line·A_b/A_a` (the cap-side discharge on the retract stroke, the same flow as the return line `Q_rtn`; in the regenerative extend the larger of that and `Q_line·A_b/A_r`); smallest bore `d = √(4Q/(π·v_max))` and the recommended bore is the smallest listed size not below it. Orifice `Q = C_d·A_o·√(2Δp/ρ)`, bore for the circuit flow `d_o = √(4Q/(π·C_d·√(2Δp/ρ)))`. Rod buckling (screening): the buckling length is the pin-to-pin length of the cylinder at full stroke, `L_b = L_closed + stroke` (the closed pin-to-pin length plus the stroke; since 1.4.0 the cylinder body is part of it, as a cylinder buckles over its whole length and not over the rod alone; with `L_closed` blank it is estimated as `L_closed = stroke + L_add`, so `L_b = 2·stroke + L_add`, and the sheet warns `length`), `L_eff = K_m·L_b`, solid round rod `r = d_r/4`, `I = πd_r⁴/64`, `λ = L_eff/r`, `λ_c = π√(2E/f_y)`; Euler `F_cr = π²E·I/L_eff²` for `λ > λ_c`, Johnson `F_cr = A_r·f_y·[1 − f_y·λ²/(4π²E)]` for `λ ≤ λ_c` (the two meet, with equal slope, at `λ_c`); `F_allow = F_cr/S_f`, the rod load is `F_buck = max(F_push, F_stall)` with the stall thrust `F_stall = p·A_b`: when the cylinder stalls at the end of its stroke, meets an obstruction or runs up to its relief setting the rod carries the full pressure force on the bore, whatever push load was entered. At stall there is no flow, so no back pressure and no friction loss: `F_stall` has no `η_m` and no `p_back` (L-a), and the regenerative extend is judged at the same standard-connection `F_stall`, because the standard connection can always be selected and its thrust is the larger (L-b). No pressure-limiting device is credited; utilisation `u_buck = F_buck/F_allow`.

**Inputs.** `mode` (Standard / Regenerative extend); `p` 1–700 bar (gauge, at the cylinder and the pump), `p_back` 0–700 bar, `Q` 0.1–2000 L/min (blank when `v_req` gives it), optional `v_req` 0.1–10000 mm/s (the required extend speed; M9 `output_motion` links here as a kinematic proxy: a cylinder-actuated linkage runs at about constant cylinder speed, not at constant crank speed), `rho` 700–1300 kg/m³ (fluid, not a W3 material); loads `F_push`, `F_pull` 0–15000 kN; cylinder `D` 10–500 mm, `d_r` 4–450 mm (blocks if not below `D`), `stroke` 1–5000 mm, `eta_m`, `eta_vc` 0.5–1, `t_dwell` 0–3600 s; rod buckling `K_m` 0.5–2.5, optional `L_closed` 1–20000 mm (the closed pin-to-pin length; blank = estimated), `L_add` 0–5000 mm (the length of the closed cylinder beyond its stroke, used only when `L_closed` is blank), `E` 50–250 GPa and `f_y` 100–1500 MPa (both W3 material properties, `MaterialProperty` set), `S_f` 1–20; pump (optional, entered together) `V_g` 0.1–1000 cm³/rev, `n_p` 50–6000 rpm, `eta_vp`, `eta_tp` 0.5–1 (blocks if `eta_tp > eta_vp`); motor (optional, entered together) `V_gm` 0.1–2000 cm³/rev, `dp_m` 1–700 bar, `eta_hm` 0.5–1; lines `d_suc`, `d_prs`, `d_ret` 3–200 mm, limits `v_suc_max`, `v_prs_max`, `v_ret_max` 0.1–20 m/s, optional table `hoses` (column `ID`, 3–200 mm); orifice `C_d` 0.2–1, `dp_o` 0.1–700 bar (blocks above `p`), `d_o` 0.1–100 mm (needs `dp_o`). **Records.** `material.properties` (`E`, `f_y`), `cylinder.mounting` (`K_m`; the default 1 is pinned-pinned and a fixed-free mounting is K = 2, so enter the real mounting), `cylinder.buckling-safety` (`S_f`; the default 3.5 is a placeholder), `cylinder.line-limits` (`v_suc_max`, `v_prs_max`, `v_ret_max`), `cylinder.hose-sizes` (`hoses`). The sheet's defaults for the three velocity limits (1.2, 5, 3 m/s; the suction figure is at the top of the usual band, so tighten it from your design reference), for `K_m` (1) and for `S_f` (3.5) are placeholders, not values from a standard or a catalogue; no mounting table, hose table or velocity table ships. Blocks also if the back pressure takes all the force on a stroke.

**Outputs.** `A_b`, `A_r`, `A_a`, `phi`, `F_ext`, `F_ret` (`F_std` and `v_std` in the regenerative connection), `v_ext`, `v_ret`, `t_ext`, `t_ret`, `t_cycle`, `dV_ext`, `dV_ret`, `dV`, `u_push`, `u_pull`, `p_push` (the pressure the push load needs at the cylinder, `(F_push/η_m + p_back·A_a)/A_b`; regenerative `F_push/(η_m·A_r)`; gauge), `Q`, `p`; pump `Q_p`, `Vg_req`, `u_pump`, `P_pump`, `T_shaft`, `n_shaft`; motor `T_motor`; lines `Q_line`, `Q_suc`, `Q_prs`, `Q_rtn`, `v_suc`, `v_prs`, `v_rtn`, `u_suc`, `u_prs`, `u_rtn`, `d_min_*`, `d_rec_*`; orifice `d_o_req`, `Q_o`; rod `L_b`, `L_eff`, `lam`, `lam_c`, `F_cr`, `F_allow`, `F_stall`, `F_buck`, `u_buck`, `buckling_note` (text). **Ports.** `Q` (`hyd.flow`), `p` (`hyd.pressure`), `P_pump` (`hyd.pump_power`), `u_buck` (`hyd.buckling_util`), `F_ext` (`hyd.cylinder_force`), `dV` (swept volume of the larger stroke, to F2's emergency-stroke ΔV), `p_push` (since 1.4.0, bar gauge, to F2's `p_load`: its lowest working pressure must carry the push load), `T_shaft` and `n_shaft` (pump shaft torque and speed, to E1 as B1 does; present when a pump is entered), and since 1.2.0 `Q_prs` (`L/min`, the flow in the line to the cap port, the larger of the two strokes, to the pressure line B1), `rho` (`kg/m^3`, the fluid density) and `d_prs` (`mm`, the line bore), so a pressure-line calculation takes the line F1 judges, its density and its bore by link, and goes stale when the bore is revised. **Checks.** extend force covers the push load; retract force covers the pull load; rod buckling (screening); suction, pressure (cap-port line, larger stroke flow) and return line velocity within the limit; pump delivery covers the circuit flow (pump entered). **Warnings.** `size` when no listed size is large enough for a line; `length` when no closed length is entered and the buckling length is estimated (since 1.4.0).

**Limitations.** Steady motion only: no acceleration, cushioning, compressibility or pressure losses (line losses come from the pipe calculation, B1; `p` is the pressure at the cylinder port and the pump outlet). Mechanical and volumetric efficiencies are the user's. The pump is a fixed-displacement delivery at a stated speed; no pump curve, no relief-valve or load-sensing model. The rod is a solid round column with the mounting's effective-length factor; the tube, piston and rod-end stiffness, the side load and the stop-tube are not modelled, and the rod is judged at the larger of the push load entered and the stall thrust `p·A_b` (a reduced load is not credited for a pressure-limiting device). **ISO/TS 13725 is not implemented.** The orifice is the sharp-edged turbulent relation with the user's `C_d` (no viscosity, no cavitation).

**Test cases.** Analytic, recomputed in python3 (all Confirmed): `ka-circuit-standard` (100/70 at 160 bar, 36 L/min, 28 cm³/rev pump at 1450 rpm, motor, orifice, hose list, Johnson rod: `F_ext` 119.3805 kN, `F_ret` 60.88407 kN, `v_ext` 74.8665 mm/s, `t_cycle` 10.0846 s, `P_pump` 11.6879 kW, `T_shaft` 76.9731 N·m, `T_motor` 36.6693 N·m, return line 2.5678 m/s, suction at the geometric delivery 0.53847 m/s, cap-port line at the retract flow 75.627 L/min 6.2690 m/s (utilisation 1.254, a FAIL on the 16 mm line; the next listed size is 20 mm), `d_o_req` 5.0694 mm, `L_eff` 1100 mm (2 × 500 + 100), `F_cr` 1465.71 kN, `u_buck` 0.30008 at the 125.66 kN stall thrust); `ka-regenerative` (`F_ext` 58.4965 kN, `v_ext` 152.789 mm/s, `Q_prs` 78.7143 L/min); `ka-euler-slender` (40 mm rod over a 4100 mm pin-to-pin length: λ = 410, Euler `F_cr` 15.4939 kN; the 15 kN push load alone reads 3.3884, but a stalled cylinder at 160 bar carries `p·A_b` = 80.4248 kN (76.4035 kN with `η_m`, which does not relieve a stalled rod), above `F_cr` even without a safety factor, so `u_buck` = 80.4248·3.5/15.4939 = 18.1676, a FAIL); `ka-back-pressure-fixed-free` (10 bar back pressure, K = 2: `F_ext` 115.5753 kN, `F_ret` 53.4228 kN, `L_eff` 1400 mm, Johnson `F_cr` 1181.50 kN, `u_buck` 0.37226 at the 125.66 kN stall thrust (no back pressure at stall)); `ka-orifice` (ρ 800, Δp 10 bar, C_d 0.6: jet 30 m/s, a 4 mm orifice passes 22.6195 L/min and the bore for that flow is 4 mm); `ka-lossless` (η = 1: `F = p·A`, `v = Q/A`, `t_ext` 4 s). `ka-required-speed` (since 1.3.0: the lossless cylinder driven by `v_req` = 100 mm/s with `Q` blank: `Q` = 47.1239 L/min exactly, `v_ext` 100 mm/s, `t_ext` 4 s). **Unconfirmed / Secondary:** none shipped. See *Definition of done* below.

**Definition of done (Addendum B §A3, §B4).** Every case above is analytic and Confirmed; no published known-answer case is shipped, and none is invented. **Unconfirmed / Secondary items to list in the owner test pack:** none (this calculator has none). **Not pinned (owner check):** the Esposito *Fluid Power with Applications* 7th ed. cylinder force/speed and pump power examples (Ch. 5–6) and the Parker industrial cylinder catalogue rod-sizing procedure: their inputs and answers cannot be stated from a source available to the build, so no case is added; check the sheet against the owner's copy of the book and the catalogue (the catalogue procedure would also be Secondary).
