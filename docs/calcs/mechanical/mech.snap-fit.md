# M8 — Snap-fits: cantilever, annular, torsion (`mech.snap-fit@1.2.0`)

**Scope.** Plastic snap-fit joints of five forms, chosen by `mode`: a cantilever lug of **constant section**, of **tapered thickness** or of **tapered width**, an **annular** (closed ring) snap, and a **torsion bar** with a lever. For each: the strain at the critical section against the permissible strain of the plastic (one-time or repeated joining; FAIL above 1), the deflection force from beam theory with the secant modulus, the **assembly force** over the entry face and the **separation force** over the return face with Coulomb friction, the **self-locking** flag, the permissible deflection, and an always-shown root-fillet advisory. The design guide (Covestro, *Snap-Fit Joints for Plastics: A Design Guide*, Section B) is cited by section and title only; none of its text, charts or tables is reproduced. No plastics data are shipped (Addendum B §B5): `Es`, the permissible strains, `ν`, `μ`, `K_ann` and `K_F` have no default. **Attribution:** the sheet cites the guide only for the relations the spec quotes (the constant-section strain and force, `γ_perm ≈ ε_perm·(1 + ν)`, `γ = φ·r/l`, the joining forces and the self-locking condition). The tapered-beam factors, the annular force and the torsion torque and force are derived here and cited as derived.

**Method.** Euler–Bernoulli beam theory. Position `t = x/l` from the root; tip load `P`; moment `M = P·l·(1 − t)`; surface strain `ε(t) = M·(h/2)/(E·I)`; tip deflection `y = ∫ M·m/(E·I) dx` with the unit-load moment `m = l·(1 − t)`.
- *Constant section.* `ε = 6·P·l/(E·b·h²)` at the root, so `P = (b·h²/6)·(Es·ε/l)` (the spec form) and `y = 4·P·l³/(E·b·h³) = (2/3)·ε·l²/h`: the geometry factor is **`K_y = 2/3`**; the design guides print the rounded 0.67 (0.5 % higher). The sheet uses the derived 2/3.
- *Tapered thickness* `h(t) = h·(1 − a·t)`, `a = 1 − r_h`, `r_h = h_tip/h` (default 0.5, "h tip = h/2"), width constant. Strain shape `φ(t) = (1 − t)/(1 − a·t)²`, `ε_max = (6·P·l/(E·b·h²))·φ_max`, so `P = b·h²·Es·ε/(6·l·φ_max)`. Deflection `y = 12·P·l³·J/(E·b·h³)` with `J = ∫₀¹ (1 − t)²/(1 − a·t)³ dt = [2r − r²/2 − ln r − 3/2]/a³` (`r = r_h`). Eliminating `P`: **`K_y = 2·J/φ_max`**, `y = K_y·ε_max·l²/h`. The strain is largest at the root when `a ≤ 1/2` (`r_h ≥ 1/2`, `φ_max = 1`); for a steeper taper `φ'(0) > 0` and the peak is inside the beam at `t* = (2a − 1)/a`, `φ_max = (1 − t*)/(1 − a·t*)²` (sheet outputs `t_crit`, `phi_max`). `r_h = 0.5`: `J = 0.545177`, **`K_y = 1.09035`**.
- *Tapered width* `b(t) = b·(1 − a·t)`, `a = 1 − r_b` (default 0.5), thickness constant. `φ(t) = (1 − t)/(1 − a·t)`, largest at the root for every `a`, so `P = b·h²·Es·ε/(6·l)` (as the constant section); `J = ∫₀¹ (1 − t)²/(1 − a·t) dt = [1/2 − 2r + 3r²/2 − r²·ln r]/a³`, **`K_y = 2·J`**. `r_b = 0.5`: `K_y = 0.77259`; `r_b = 0.25`: `K_y = 0.85520`.
- Both tapers join the constant section at a ratio of 1 (`K_y = 2/3`, tested). Below a taper of 0.02 the closed forms lose digits to cancellation, so `J` is integrated numerically there (Simpson, 2000 intervals; the switch is seamless, tested).
- *Strain from the deflection.* `ε = y·h/(K_y·l²)`; permissible deflection `y_perm = K_y·ε_perm·l²/h`.
- *Annular (derived, a free thin closed ring; not a relation of the design guide).* The **nominal** hoop strain of the mean diameter is `ε_nom = ΔD/D` (`ΔD` = interference on the diameter, `D` = mean diameter). The strain checked is the **larger of** `K_ann·ε_nom` and the inner-surface strain `ΔD/(D − h)`, with `K_ann` the user's strain factor (record `snapfit.annular-factor`, **no default**, required in this mode only; 1 for a plain free ring). The force comes from the nominal strain only: hoop force `Es·ε_nom·h·b` (`h` wall, `b` the **effective axial length of a free ring**, not the lip of a longer tube), radial contact force summed round the ring `P = K_F·2π·Es·ε_nom·h·b`, with `K_F` the user's optional force factor (record `snapfit.annular-force-factor`, **no default**, blank is 1). A tube behind the ring stiffens the joint and raises the force; only `K_F` can represent that, and `K_ann` does not touch the force. The strain on the inner (contact) surface is `ΔD/(D − h)`, slightly above `ΔD/D` (output `eps_inner`); with `K_ann` below `D/(D − h)` (for example 1) it is the one checked. A wall above a tenth of the mean diameter (`h/D > 0.1`) warns (`thick-ring`) that the thin-ring model has run out. `Es(ε)` (typed or from the W3 points) is the secant modulus **at the nominal hoop strain `ε_nom = ΔD/D`**, the strain that drives the force. `ΔD_perm = ε_perm·min(D/K_ann, D − h)`. Cross-check: the work of the radial force over the radial growth `ΔD/2` equals the hoop strain energy `½·Es·ε_nom²·π·D·h·b` (tested).
- *Torsion bar* (solid round, radius `r`; lever `a`; lever-end deflection `y`; `bar_support`): `φ = y/a` (small angle; WARN above 20°). **Held at one end** (default; `l` = the free length): `l_eff = l`. **Held at both ends with the lever in the middle** (`l` = the whole length between the supports): two halves twist in parallel, `l_eff = l/2`, `n = 2`. Then `γ = φ·r/l_eff` (both ends: `2φr/l`), equivalent tensile strain `ε = γ/(1 + ν)`, `γ_perm = ε_perm·(1 + ν)`, utilisation `γ/γ_perm = ε/ε_perm`; `G = Es/(2(1 + ν))`, `T = n·G·(π r⁴/2)·φ/l_eff`, `P = T/a` (both ends: `P = 4·G·J·φ/(l·a)`); `y_perm = γ_perm·l_eff·a/r`. For the same total length the bar held at both ends has twice the strain and four times the force of one held at one end. Cross-check: the strain energy `G·γ²·π·r²·l/4` equals `P·y/2` for either support (tested). The torque, force and two-ended case are derived; `γ = φ·r/l` and `γ_perm` are the brief's design-guide relations, to be checked.
- *Assembly and separation.* `W = P·(μ + tan α)/(1 − μ·tan α)` over the entry face (angle `α` to the joining direction), `W′ = P·(μ + tan α′)/(1 − μ·tan α′)` over the return face (`α′`). **Self-locking** when `α′ ≥ arctan(1/μ)` (`π/2` when `μ = 0`): the separation force is infinite, the sheet prints `self-locking (infinite)` instead of a number and warns (rule `self-locking`; right for a permanent joint, wrong for a serviceable one); `alpha_lock = arctan(1/μ)` is always reported. The retention port has no number in that case, so a link to `retention_force` is refused by the linking service ("has no numeric output") and a pinned link goes stale ("no longer gives"); tested in the Library suite. Assembly **jams** when `μ·tan α ≥ 1`: no number, WARN `assembly-jam` and a failed check.
- *Secant modulus.* Typed (`Es`, no default), or interpolated linearly from the **W3 secant-modulus points `Es(ε)`** (`Es_pts`, `MaterialProperty` `Es_curve`, ≤ 50 rows, in any order, a point at zero strain allowed) at the design strain; outside the points the end value is held and the sheet warns (`es-range`), with no extrapolation. A usable row has a strain of zero or more and a positive modulus (`es-curve` blocks otherwise).
- *Temperature.* `T_use` is recorded on the sheet with the note that `Es` and the permissible strains must be the values at that temperature. The W3 points and strain limits are taken as entered; the sheet cannot check their temperature.
- *Root fillet.* An always-present advisory (`fillet_advisory`): the strain is nominal; a sharp corner at the root concentrates it; fillet generously and apply your own concentration factor if the radius is small. When a steep thickness taper puts the strain peak inside the beam, the advisory names where (`t_crit·l` from the root). No concentration chart is shipped.
- *Friction.* `μ` is a typed input (record `snapfit.friction`). A W3 material can hold friction coefficients by pair (`mu_pairs`), but the sheet does not read them: a change to a W3 friction pair does **not** make the sheet stale. Read `μ` from the pair and type it, with its source.

**Inputs.** `mode`; `l`, `h`, `b` (cantilever root dimensions; for the annular snap `h` = wall and `b` = axial length), `h_ratio`, `b_ratio`, `y`; annular `D_ring`, `dD`, `K_ann` (required, no default), `K_F` (optional); torsion `r_bar`, `a_arm`, `bar_support` (one end / both ends; default one end) (and `l`, `y`); `alpha`, `alpha_r`, `mu` (record `snapfit.friction`, **no default**); `Es_source`, `Es` (no default; record `snapfit.secant-modulus`), `Es_pts` (W3 points); `loading` (one-time / repeated) selecting `eps_perm_once` or `eps_perm_repeated` (both `MaterialProperty`, **no default**); `nu` (`MaterialProperty`, torsion only, no default); `T_use` (degC, recorded). Material record kind `material.properties` lists `Es_pts`, `eps_perm_once`, `eps_perm_repeated`, `nu`: applying a W3 plastic fills them, each citing the material, its revision and the page of the value, and the sheet goes stale when a later revision is saved. A blank input a form needs blocks with its name and what to enter (rule `missing`). Hard limits: the lengths and sections are positive (`y` and the diameter interference `dD` may be zero), `α` 0–89°, `α′` 0–90°, `μ` 0–2, `ε_perm` 10⁻⁶–1.

**Outputs.** `eps` (%), `eps_perm` (%), `strain_util` (FAIL > 1; check "Strain at the critical section against the permissible strain"), `Es`, `P`, `y_perm`; cantilever `l_h` (WARN < 5, rule `slender`), `K_y`, `phi_max` (and `t_crit` when the peak is inside); annular `eps_nom`, `eps_inner`, `K_F`; torsion `l_eff`, `phi`, `gamma`, `gamma_perm`, `G_sec`, `T_bar`; `assembly_force`, `alpha_lock`, `retention_force` (or the self-locking text), `self_locking`, `fillet_advisory`; check "Assembly: the entry face does not jam".

**Ports.** `assembly_force` N (`snap.assembly_force`), `retention_force` N (`snap.retention_force`; a text, with no number, when the return angle locks, so a link to it is refused then), `strain_util` (`snap.strain_util`). **Links (manual for now).** No port of M1 is wired to this sheet: the user types the M1 result into `y` (or `dD`, `l`, `h`) by hand. The undercut `y` (and the interference `ΔD`, the lug length and thickness) is the natural result of the M1 tolerance stack of the mating parts, so the M1 worst-case or Monte-Carlo extreme of the undercut is the `y` of this sheet; running it at the maximum interference gives the worst strain and assembly force, and at the minimum the worst retention. **Monte Carlo:** this sheet is deterministic. A Monte-Carlo run of the M1 stack engine (distributions on `y`, `l`, `h`, `μ`) can be run on its own and its extremes typed in; there is no automatic hook yet, and W2 scan distributions of a measured lug would feed the same inputs the same way.

**Limitations.** Linear beam theory with a constant secant modulus over the beam at the design strain (the strain varies along a tapered beam; `Es` is read at the peak); nominal strain without the root-fillet concentration; no creep, stress relaxation, moisture or impact-speed effect; rigid mating part; rectangular cantilever (no trapezoidal or U-shaped section, no hollow); free thin closed annular ring (the effective length `b`, `K_ann` and `K_F` stand for anything else); round solid torsion bar, held at one end or at both ends with a central lever; small twist; Coulomb friction with one coefficient for both faces. Beam theory under-predicts stiffness when `l/h < 5` (warned).

**Test cases.** All analytic and **Confirmed**, recomputed in python3 (`m8_geom.py`, `m8_kat.py`; the geometry factors also by numerical double integration of the curvature, agreeing to 10⁻⁹): `ka-constant` (K_y = 2/3, ε = 1.33333 %, P = 8.53333 N, W = 9.055114 N, arctan(1/μ) = 73.3008°, W′ = 36.096414 N), `ka-taper-thickness` (K_y = 1.0903549, P = 5.217465 N), `ka-taper-thickness-deep` (r_h = 0.3: t* = 0.571429, φ_max = 1.190476, K_y = 1.2684382), `ka-taper-width` (r_b = 0.25: K_y = 0.8551983), `ka-annular` (ε_nom = 1.33333 %, ε = 1.6 %, inner surface 1.403509 %, P = 1507.964 N from the nominal strain, W = 1600.171 N), `ka-annular-inner-surface` (K_ann = 1, h = 3 mm: the inner surface 1.481481 % governs, u = 0.37037, ΔD_perm = 1.08 mm, P = 3015.929 N), `ka-annular-force-factor` (K_F = 1.25: P = 1884.956 N, strain unchanged), `ka-torsion` (one end: γ = 0.015, γ_perm = 0.0414, T = 0.136591 N·m, P = 13.659098 N), `ka-torsion-both-ends` (γ = 0.03, T = 0.546364 N·m, P = 54.636394 N, y_perm = 2.07 mm; also by strain energy), `ka-curve` (Es(2 %) = 2100 MPa from three points). The worked example `enclosure-lid-hook` (tapered thickness) is in `docs/worked-examples/mechanical/mech.snap-fit/`. **Not pinned — owner check:** (1) the Covestro *Snap-Fit Joints for Plastics: A Design Guide* Section B worked example (inputs and answer not available to the build); (2) the Covestro joining-techniques guide Fig. 25 relations (the figure's exact factors are not stated here). The derived factors `K_y = 1.09` (tip thickness h/2) and `0.86` (tip width b/4) agree with the values the build remembers the design guides printing for tapers, but that is memory, not a pinned source: check both, and the guide's printed 0.67 against the derived 2/3, against the owner's copy. The annular and torsion relations are written from first principles and from the brief's `γ_perm ≈ ε_perm·(1 + ν)`; compare them with the guide's before relying on them. **For the owner:** the sheet uses the derived `K_y = 2/3` for the constant section, 0.5 % below the 0.67 the guides print; say if you want the rounded value instead.

**Changes in 1.2.0:** the annular check takes the larger of `K_ann·ε_nom` and the inner-surface strain, `ΔD_perm` follows, `thick-ring` warns at `h/D > 0.1`; `Es` is documented as read at `ε_nom` for the annular snap; the Es interpolation is cited as "W3 material points (linear interpolation)" and `φ = y/a` as derived. `ka-annular` and the other existing values are unchanged (there `K_ann·ε_nom` governs).

**Changes in 1.1.0** (Opus review): the torsion bar can be held at both ends; the annular force no longer scales with `K_ann` (new optional `K_F`; `K_ann` has no default and is required in the annular mode only; inner-surface strain shown); derived relations are no longer cited to the guide; a W3 point at zero strain is accepted; the fillet advisory names an inside strain peak; `self_locking` reads `No` / `Yes: …`. `ka-annular` moved (P, W, W′: the force now uses the nominal strain); every other existing value is unchanged.
