# M7 — Helical springs: compression, extension, torsion (`mech.spring@1.2.0`)

**Scope.** Cylindrical helical springs of round wire, cold-coiled (d up to 20 mm), linear characteristic. Compression is the first mode: rate, working points (given as loads or as deflections), shear stress with a correction factor at the two working points and at solid length, solid length (EN 13906-1 form or Shigley form) with the minimum coil clearance at the working length, pitch by end type, buckling, Goodman fatigue, surge frequency. Extension adds the initial tension and the two hook stresses. Torsion gives the rate with the legs, the curvature-corrected bending stress, the wound diameter and the body length. EN 13906-1/-2/-3 are cited by part and title only; their text, wire-strength tables and permissible-stress diagrams are not reproduced or shipped (Addendum B §B5).

**Method.**
- Index and rate: `C = D/d`; `k = G d⁴/(8 D³ n)` (compression, extension); torsion `k_φ = E d⁴/(64 D n_a)`, `n_a = n + (l₁ + l₂)/(3π D)`.
- Shear stress: `τ = k_c·8 F D/(π d³)`, `k_c` by choice: Bergsträsser `(C + 0.5)/(C − 0.75)` (the default; this is the form the spec brief calls the Wahl option, and the one printed as K_B in Shigley), Wahl `(4C − 1)/(4C − 4) + 0.615/C`, or none (uncorrected). The same factor is applied to the stress at solid, which is conservative for the static check.
- Solid length by end type, two selectable forms (`solid_method`). **EN 13906-1 (the default):** `n_t = n + e_n`, `L_s = d_max (n_t + e_s)` with the maximum wire diameter `d_max` (input `d_max`, blank = the nominal `d`, no tolerance added) and `(e_n, e_s)` = (0, 1) plain, (1, 0) plain and ground, (2, 1.5) squared (closed, unground), (2, 0) squared and ground; plain ends have no row in the standard and keep the geometric form. The minimum sum of the coil clearances `S_a = (0.0015 D²/d + 0.1 d) n` gives the minimum length `L_n = L_s + S_a`; the sheet checks `L₂ ≥ L_n` (utilisation `s₂/(L₀ − L_n)`, check "Minimum coil clearance at L₂") and refuses a free length not above `L_n` (rule `solid`). **Shigley Table 10-1:** `L_s = d (n_t + e_s)` with nominal `d` and `e_s` = 1 for squared, no clearance. The two forms differ for squared unground ends (0.5 d) and by the wire tolerance. Pitch `p = (L₀ − a d)/(n + b)` with `(a, b)` = (1, 0), (0, 1), (3, 0), (2, 0). Travel to solid `s_c = L₀ − L_s`, `F_c = k s_c`; the stress at solid and the working deflection against the travel (`u_s = s₂/s_c`) are checked in both forms. The EN relations are written in own notation from the published expressions and are **to be checked against the owner's copy** of EN 13906-1.
- Extension: `F = F₀ + k s`; body length `d (n + 1)`; hook bending `σ_A = F [K_A·16 D/(π d³) + 4/(π d²)]`, `K_A = (4C₁² − C₁ − 1)/(4 C₁ (C₁ − 1))`, `C₁ = 2 r₁/d`; side-bend torsion `τ_B = K_B·8 F D/(π d³)`, `K_B = (4C₂ − 1)/(4C₂ − 4)`, `C₂ = 2 r₂/d`.
- Torsion: curvature factor by choice (`tors_factor`): **EN 13906-3 `q = (C + 0.07)/(C − 0.75)` (the default)** or Shigley's `K_i = (4C² − C − 1)/(4C (C − 1))` (about 1 % lower at C = 8); `σ = q·32 M/(π d³)` (or `K_i`); wound to angle φ the wire length is constant, so `D_w = D n/(n + φ/2π)` and the closed body is `d (n + 1 + φ/2π)` long.
- Buckling (compression): `λ_eff = ν L₀/D`; `y_cr = L₀ C₁′ [1 − √(1 − C₂′/λ_eff²)]`, `C₁′ = E/(2(E − G))`, `C₂′ = 2π²(E − G)/(2G + E)`; when `λ_eff² < C₂′` the spring is stable at any deflection. PASS when `s₂ < y_cr`, otherwise WARN (rule `buckling`).
- Fatigue (Goodman, the same relation as M4 `mech.shaft`: `1/n_f = a/S_e + m/S_u`; shared function `FatigueRelations.GoodmanUtilisation`, M4 itself unchanged): `a`, `m` from the stresses at working points 1 and 2; `S_u = f_su·R_m` in shear (compression, extension body), `R_m` in bending (torsion). Checked as `n_f,req·(1/n_f) ≤ 1`. `S_e` has no default: a blank one blocks the sheet ("S_e: enter …"), so the fatigue check is always made; warning `fatigue-cycles` when `N_c < 10⁴`.
- Wire strength `R_m(d)` (`rm_source`): typed (`Rm`, **no default**; blank blocks with what to enter, rule `wire-strength`) or taken from the curve of a W3 material, `R_m = A/d^m` with `A` = `Rm_A` (MPa, for `d` in mm) and `m` = `Rm_m` (both `MaterialProperty`, in the record kind `material.properties`, pinned by revision: a new revision makes the sheet stale). The curve value must lie in 50 … 5000 MPa. The diameter range over which the fit holds is the material record's to state and the user's to respect; the sheet does not know it.
- Surge: `f_n = (d/(2π n D²))√(G/(2ρ))`, both ends fixed (the same as `½√(k/m)` with the wire mass). With an operating frequency `f_op`, `r_f = f_n/f_op`, WARN (rule `surge`) when `r_f < 13`.

**Inputs.** `mode`, `corr`, `d` 0.1–20 mm (hard 0.05–50), `D`, `n` (≥ 2 usual; torsion: body turns), `ends`, `L0`; hooks `r_h1`, `r_h2`; torsion legs `l_1`, `l_2`; working points by `work` (loads `F_1`, `F_2` / torques `M_1`, `M_2`, or deflections `s_1`, `s_2` / angles `phi_1`, `phi_2`) and `F_0` (extension). Material `G`, `E`, `rho`, `Rm_A`, `Rm_m` (W3 `MaterialProperty`; record kind `material.properties`). Solid length: `solid_method`, `d_max`; torsion factor: `tors_factor`. User inputs with records: `rm_source` and `Rm` = R_m(d) (`spring.wire-strength`, no default), `f_tau`, `f_sigma`, `f_su` (`spring.permissible`; they default to the generic textbook fractions 0.45, 0.75 and 0.67, which the user replaces with the licensed EN 13906 value), `nu_seat` = ν, the end-support coefficient (`spring.seating`; 0.5 both ends fixed and parallel, 0.7 one fixed one pinned, 1 both pinned, 2 one end free), `S_e` (`spring.endurance`, no default), `N_c`, `n_f_req`, `f_op`. Inputs a mode does not use are optional; one the chosen mode needs and that is blank blocks with a message naming it (`missing`). **No strength is invented:** `Rm` and `S_e` have no default (a new sheet opens blocked and says what to enter). The remaining defaults on a new sheet are example values; none is a published figure.

**Expected working travel (1.2.0).** Optional input `s_work` (mm, 0.1–1000): the working travel the mechanism expects of the spring (M9 `lift` links here). In compression and extension the sheet compares it with its own `s_trav = s₂ − s₁` (an output) and warns `travel-mismatch` when they differ by more than 1 %: the rate the mechanism assumed is then not this spring's. In torsion mode the travel check does not apply (a torsion spring works through an angle): a typed or linked `s_work` is not used and the sheet warns `s-work-unused`. Blank, nothing changes.

**Outputs.** Compression: `C`, `k`, `k_c`, `n_t`, `L_s`, `p`, `F_1`, `F_2`, `s_1`, `s_2`, `L_1`, `L_2`, `s_c`, `F_c`, `tau_1`, `tau_2`, `tau_c`, `u_2`, `u_c`, `u_s`, with the EN form `d_max`, `S_a`, `L_n`, `s_n`, `u_n`; `y_cr`, `u_b`, `buckling` (PASS/WARN), `f_n`, `r_f`, `u_f`, `n_f`; `Rm` in every mode. Extension: the rate and working points, `tau_0`, `tau_1`, `tau_2`, `u_2`, `K_A`, `K_B`, `sigma_A`, `tau_B`, `u_A`, `u_B`, `f_n`, `r_f`, `u_f`, `n_f`. Torsion: `n_a`, `k_phi`, `q` (EN) or `K_i` (Shigley), `phi_1`, `phi_2`, `M_1`, `M_2`, `sigma_1`, `sigma_2`, `u_2`, `D_w`, `L_w`, `u_f`, `n_f`. **Checks.** compression: static stress at solid length, travel to solid (a utilisation, `s₂/s_c`), minimum coil clearance at L₂ (EN form), Goodman; extension: body stress, hook bending, hook torsion, Goodman; torsion: bending stress, Goodman.

**Ports.** `k` N/mm (`spring.rate`), `F_1`, `F_2` N (`spring.force_at`: the forces at the two working points of the sheet, i.e. at the entered or derived deflections `s₁`, `s₂`; it is not a force at an arbitrary deflection, which M9 gets from the rate `k`), `L_s` mm (`spring.solid_length`: solid length in compression, closed body length in extension and torsion), `f_n` Hz (`spring.surge_freq`). The torsion rate is `k_phi` (N·m/rad, a different dimension) and is not a port. Links onward: M9 (follower force and rate), M4, M1.

**Limitations.** Linear characteristic; cold-coiled round wire up to 20 mm; no conical or variable-pitch springs, no rectangular wire; the wire-diameter tolerance enters only through the optional `d_max` of the EN solid-length form; the spring ranges are soft by design (index outside 4 … 20 and `n` below 2 only warn, `d` above 20 mm is a soft limit with a wider hard one), because Addendum B §B4 requires a hard FAIL outside the valid range for S7, S8, F1 and F2 only; the Goodman function is the shared `FatigueRelations.GoodmanUtilisation` (M4 itself is unchanged), not a call into the M4 sheet; extension hook fatigue and hook shape beyond the two radii are not assessed; surge for both ends fixed only; R_m(d), permissible stress fractions and endurance strength are the user's (no EN 10270 table, no EN 13906 permissible-stress diagram); the fatigue check is a Goodman line on the user's endurance strength, not a diagram.

**Test cases.** Analytic and Confirmed, recomputed in python3: `ka-compression` (k = 4.8828125 N/mm exactly, L_s 20 mm, s₂ 8.192 mm, τ₂ 238.842 MPa, τ_c 728.888 MPa, f_n 350.845 Hz, 1/n_f 0.347693), `ka-wahl-slender` (Wahl k_c 1.144833, y_cr 15.7950 mm, s₂/y_cr 0.759734, f_n 179.633 Hz), `ka-extension` (k = 1.953125 N/mm exactly, σ_A 693.006 MPa, τ_B 381.972 MPa, f_n 140.338 Hz), `ka-torsion` (Shigley K_i chosen explicitly: k_φ 0.503096 N·m/rad, σ₂ 210.596 MPa, D_w 15.8745 mm), `ka-rm-curve` (R_m = 1800/2^0.1 = 1679.459 MPa from A and m; the stresses and utilisations follow). **Unconfirmed** (the EN 13906 relations are typed from the published expressions): `ka-torsion-en` (q = 1.113103, σ₂ 212.587 MPa), `ka-squared-en` (squared unground ends with d_max 2.04 mm: L_s 23.46 mm, S_a 3.136 mm, L_n 26.596 mm, u_n 0.445). **Unconfirmed + Secondary** (solutions manual): `ka-shigley-10-3`, Shigley 10th ed. problem 10-3, d 2.5 mm, D 28.5 mm, n 13, plain and ground ends, G 81 GPa, R_m 1935.9 MPa: the sheet's method reproduces k = 1.3142 N/mm, K_B = 1.1174, L_s = 35 mm, F_s = π d³·0.45 R_m/(8 K_B D) = 167.85 N (published 167.86) and L₀ = L_s + F_s/k = 162.72 mm; the published L₀ is entered, so F_c = 167.856 N and u_c = 1.0000 at solid length. Working points, E, ρ and S_e of that case are placeholders, not part of the problem. **Not pinned (owner check):** the Shigley solutions-manual values for problems 10-5 (k 28 lbf/in, τ_s 48.56 kpsi) and 10-6 (k 3.333 N/mm, τ_s 178.2 MPa): the problem statements are not available to the build, so their inputs cannot be stated; add them as Unconfirmed + Secondary cases once the owner has the problem text.
