# M9 — Mechanism kinematics: four-bar, slider-crank, cam (`mech.mechanism@1.2.0`)

**Scope.** A single-degree-of-freedom planar mechanism driven at constant input speed: a **four-bar linkage** or an **offset slider-crank**, and a **cam** with a translating follower (last bullets of Method). For the two linkages: the closed-form vector-loop position on both circuits (open and crossed), velocity and acceleration over a sweep of the input angle, Grashof class, transmission angle against an editable minimum, toggle or dead-centre positions, the loop-closure residual, and the input torque by virtual work. Rigid links, planar, no clearance, no friction, one degree of freedom. Standards are not involved; Norton (*Design of Machinery*) and Uicker, Pennock and Shigley (*Theory of Machines and Mechanisms*) are cited by chapter for the methods.

**Method.**
- Four-bar: ground `d` from O₂ (origin) to O₄ = (d, 0), crank `a`, coupler `b`, rocker `c` (inputs `L1 … L4`). Loop: `a·e^{jθ₂} + b·e^{jθ₃} = d + c·e^{jθ₄}`. With `ℓ = |A − O₄|` and `cos γ = (c² + ℓ² − b²)/(2cℓ)` (law of cosines, the Freudenstein result), `θ₄ = φ − γ` (**open**) or `φ + γ` (**crossed**, the mirror image about the line O₄A), `φ` the direction O₄→A; `θ₃` from `B − A`. The sweep stays on the chosen branch.
- Velocity and acceleration (constant ω₂): the differentiated loop is a 2×2 system with determinant `b·c·sin(θ₃ − θ₄)`; `ω₃ = ω₂·a·sin(θ₄ − θ₂)/(b·sin(θ₃ − θ₄))`, `ω₄ = ω₂·a·sin(θ₃ − θ₂)/(c·sin(θ₃ − θ₄))`, and α from the second derivative. Where `|sin(θ₃ − θ₄)| < 10⁻⁹` (a toggle) the system is singular and the row is left out of the table and counted (warning `reach`).
- Grashof: `s + l < p + q` Grashof (crank-rocker if the input link is the shortest, double-crank if the ground link is, double-rocker if the coupler is, "rocker-crank" if the output link is; in the last two the input link cannot turn fully), `=` the change point (parallelogram, deltoid: a collinear singular position every cycle), `>` non-Grashof (triple rocker). The class text is output `grashof`; `S_plus_L`, `P_plus_Q` the two sums.
- Transmission angle `cos μ = (b² + c² − ℓ²)/(2bc)`, folded into 0 … 90°; the check is `μ_req/μ_min ≤ 1` (default `μ_req` 40°, editable). Toggle crank angles (coupler and rocker collinear) are where `ℓ = b + c` or `|b − c|`: `cos θ₂ = (d² + a² − ℓ²)/(2ad)`, output `toggle_angles`.
- Slider-crank: crank `r` (`L2`), rod `l` (`L3`), slide line at height `e` (`e_off`): `x = r·cosθ + σ·√(l² − (r·sinθ − e)²)`, `σ = +1` open (slider on the +x side), `−1` crossed. `dx/dθ = −r·sinθ − r·cosθ·(r·sinθ − e)/(x − r·cosθ)`, rod angle `θ₃ = atan2(e − r·sinθ, x − r·cosθ)`, `dθ₃/dθ = −r·cosθ/(x − r·cosθ)`, second derivatives analytically; `v = ω·dx/dθ`, `a = ω²·d²x/dθ²`. Transmission angle `μ = 90° − |rod angle to the slide line| = arccos(|r·sinθ − e|/l)`. Dead centres (crank and rod collinear, v = 0): outer `θ = asin(e/(l + r))`, inner `θ = π + asin(e/(l − r))` (open; `π − …` and `−…` on the crossed circuit); stroke between them `√((l + r)² − e²) − √((l − r)² − e²)` (output `stroke_closed`; `stroke` is the swept extent). The crank turns fully only if `l ≥ r + |e|` (output `slider_class`).
- Input torque by virtual work, `T_in·ω_in = Σ F·v`: four-bar `T_in = (T_L + J·α₄)·ω₄/ω₂` with `T_L` the external torque on the output link (constant in direction, or `sign(ω₄)·T_L` when the load opposes the motion) and `J` its moment of inertia about the pivot; slider `T_in = (m·a + F_L)·dx/dθ` with the slider mass `m` and external force `F_L`. The crank and the coupler or rod are massless (add their mass to the output element for a first allowance).
- **Cam.** The cam turns at constant speed `ω` (`n_in`); the segment table (`segs`, at most 24 rows) lists rise, dwell and return with a motion law, the angle β and the lift h, and must add to 360° and close (rises equal returns, never below the base circle). In a segment with `x = (θ − θ₀)/β`: rise `s = s₀ + h·f(x)`, return `s = s₀ − h·f(x)`. Laws: cycloidal `f = x − sin(2πx)/(2π)`; 3-4-5 `10x³ − 15x⁴ + 6x⁵`; 4-5-6-7 `35x⁴ − 84x⁵ + 70x⁶ − 20x⁷`; modified trapezoid from the five acceleration pieces `A·sin 4πx`, `A`, `A·cos 4π(x − 3/8)`, `−A`, `−A·cos 4π(x − 7/8)` with `A = 8π/(π + 2)`. Peak coefficients `C_v = max f′`, `C_a = max |f″|`, `C_j = max |f‴|`: cycloidal 2, 2π, 4π²; modified trapezoid 2, 8π/(π + 2) = 4.8881, 32π²/(π + 2) = 61.43; 3-4-5 15/8, 10/√3 = 5.7735, 60; 4-5-6-7 35/16, 7.5132, 52.5; so `v_pk = C_v·h·ω/β`, `a_pk = C_a·h·ω²/β²`, `j_pk = C_j·h·ω³/β³`. SVAJ: `v = ω·s′`, `a = ω²·s″`, `j = ω³·s‴` (′ = d/dθ).
- Roller follower (offset `e_off`, prime radius `R_p = R_b + R_r`): pressure angle `φ = arctan[(s′ − e)/(√(R_p² − e²) + s)]` (for `e = 0` the `arctan[s′/(R_p + s)]` of the in-line follower), checked against `phi_max` (default 30°, editable; check `φ_max/φ_allowed ≤ 1`); `Rb_min_phi` is the base circle that holds φ at its limit (`R_p,req = √(s₀,req² + e²)`, `s₀,req = max(|s′ − e|/tan φ_max − s)`). The pitch curve is `P(θ) = Rot(−θ)·(e, √(R_p² − e²) + s)`; its radius of curvature `ρ = |P′|³/(P′ × P″)` where convex (for `e = 0` Norton's `[(R_p + s)² + s′²]^{3/2}/[(R_p + s)² + 2s′² − (R_p + s)s″]`); the profile is **undercut** where `ρ_min ≤ R_r` (check "No undercut" FAILs; `rho_min` reads "none" when the pitch curve is nowhere convex). Flat-faced in-line follower: no pressure angle, the profile radius `R_b + s + s″` must stay positive (cusp check) and the face must reach `w_face = max |s′|` either side of the axis.
- Follower force `F = m·a + k·s + F_pre + F_ext` (mass `m_f`, spring rate `k_sp` N/mm and preload `F_pre` N at the base circle, constant external load `F_ext`; `k_sp` and `F_pre` take the dimensions of the M7 `k` and `F_1` ports so they can be linked); the follower leaves the cam where `F < 0` (check "Follower stays on the cam: F_min ≥ 0", warning `contact`). Input torque by virtual work `T = F·s′` (from `T·ω = F·v`).
- **Assembly limits and unbounded peaks (1.1.0).** A four-bar cannot be assembled beyond its toggles (`ℓ = b + c` or `|b − c|`, the angles of output `toggle_angles`); a slider-crank with `l < r + |e|` not beyond `sin θ = (e ± l)/r` (output `limit_angles`). Driven at constant speed into one of these the speed, acceleration, torque and force are unbounded, so the sheet **cuts the sweep** to the first assemblable stretch (the closed-form limit angles bound it, and each is marked when it lies in the requested sweep), warns (rule `limit`, naming the angles and how to set `theta_start` and `theta_range` inside the stretch), reports `mu_min = 0`, and **withholds every peak, RMS and mean quantity and all ports** instead of printing a discretisation artefact. A sweep that stays inside the stretch reports them normally. A change-point linkage (parallelogram, deltoid) is continued through its singular position **on the same branch by continuity** (γ changes sign there), the table shows the continued motion, and the peaks are withheld with a warning. Singular rows at an exact limit are left out of the table.
- Forces and drive outputs (1.1.0): the four-bar's tangential pin force `P = (T_L + J·α₄)/c` is `follower_force`; the coupler (a two-force member) carries `P/sin μ`, which is also the crank-pin force: `F_pin_pk`. Slider: the slide-direction force `F_x = m·a + F_L` is `follower_force`, the rod carries `F_x/cos θ₃` (`F_pin_pk`) and the guide reacts with `F_x·tan θ₃` (`F_guide_pk`). Cam: `follower_force` is the axial force, `F_pin_pk` the roller contact normal force `F/cos φ`. `T_rms_in` is the RMS input torque, `P_mean = ω·mean(T_in)` the mean input power (Simpson's rule; zero for a conservative load over a cycle), `n_in` the input speed, for E1's thermal sizing. For M7: `lift` and `F_spring_max = k·lift + F_pre` (the spring force at full lift, without inertia).
- **Self-consistency.** The loop residual `|a·e^{jθ₂} + b·e^{jθ₃} − c·e^{jθ₄} − d|` (slider: `|r·e^{jθ} + l·e^{jθ₃} − (x + j·e)|`) is computed at every table row and every scan angle, reported as `res_max` (mm), and asserted below `10⁻⁹` mm by a check ("Loop-closure residual below 1e-9 mm at every step") and a warning if it fails.
- **Peaks** are those of the continuous motion over the swept range: a dense scan (about 4 points per degree of sweep, at least 720) refined by golden section between the neighbours of the best sample, so they do not depend on the table's step count `N` (`steps`, 2 … 3600, N + 1 rows). A sweep that passes a toggle or leaves the assembly range uses only the rows that exist.

**Inputs.** `mode` (Four-bar / Slider-crank / Cam), `circuit`, links `L1 … L4`, `e_off`, sweep `theta_start`, `theta_range`, `steps`, `n_in` (constant input speed), `mu_req`, loads `load_dir`, `T_load`, `J_out` (four-bar), `m_slider`, `F_load` (slider); cam `follower` (roller / flat-faced), `Rb`, `R_r`, `phi_max`, the segment table `segs` (columns `kind`, `law`, `beta` in deg, `h` in mm), `m_f`, `k_sp`, `F_pre`, `F_ext`. Inputs a mechanism does not use are optional; one it needs and that is blank blocks with a message naming it (`missing`).

**Outputs.** Tables of the sweep (vectors, also drawn as series): four-bar `theta2_sw`, `theta3_sw`, `theta4_sw`, `omega3_sw`, `omega4_sw`, `alpha3_sw`, `alpha4_sw`, `mu_sw`, `T_in_sw`; slider `theta_sw`, `x_sw`, `v_sw`, `a_sw`, `theta3_sw`, `omega3_sw`, `mu_sw`, `T_in_sw`. Values at the first input angle (`*_0`, and `theta4_alt` / `x_alt` on the other circuit). Peaks `omega_out_pk`, `alpha_out_pk`, `omega3_pk`, `alpha3_pk`, `swing`, `mu_min`, `x_max`, `x_min`, `stroke`, `res_max`. **Ports** (the symbol after the dot of `mech.*`): `input_torque` (N·m, peak), `output_motion` (m/s: the **peak linear speed** of the output element: the slider, the cam follower, or for a four-bar the output pin at the rocker length `c`; the rocker's angular velocity is `omega_out_pk`), `peak_accel` (m/s²: same element, for a four-bar the magnitude of the pin's acceleration), `follower_force` (N: the slide-direction force on the slider, the cam follower's axial force, or the tangential force at the rocker pin `(T_L + J·α₄)/c`), and, added in 1.1.0 (extra ports beyond the four of the spec, at the integrator's decision): `F_pin_pk` (N: coupler / connecting-rod / roller contact-normal force, for M4 bearing loads and M5), `T_rms_in` (N·m), `n_in` (rpm), `f_in` (Hz, 1.2.0: `n_in/60`; rpm is an angular velocity, so `n_in` cannot be linked to a frequency input such as M7 `f_op`) and `P_mean` (W) for E1 (peak `input_torque` suits E1's peak check; as a constant load it would make the RMS sizing very conservative), `lift` (mm) and `F_spring_max` (N, cam) for M7 (linking `follower_force` to M7 while `k_sp` comes from M7 would be a cycle). **M9 → F1 (`output_motion`, `peak_accel`, `follower_force`) is a kinematic proxy**: a cylinder-actuated linkage runs at about constant cylinder speed, not constant crank speed, and a four-bar pin speed is a cylinder speed only if the cylinder acts perpendicular to the rocker at that radius. Cam: tables `theta_sw`, `s_sw`, `v_sw`, `a_sw`, `j_sw`, `phi_sw`, `kappa_sw` (roller: pitch-curve curvature, positive convex; flat-faced: `rho_sw`, the signed profile radius), `F_sw`, `T_in_sw`, the segment table (`seg_start`, `seg_beta`, `seg_s_end`, `seg_v_pk`, `seg_a_pk`, `seg_j_pk`, `seg_law`), peaks `lift`, `v_pk`, `a_pk`, `j_pk`, `F_max`, `F_min`, `phi_max_pk`, `Rb_min_phi`, `rho_min` (signed for the flat-faced follower; the cusp check FAILs at or below zero), `w_face`, `F_pin_pk`, `F_spring_max`, `T_rms_in`, `P_mean`. Linkage extras: `res_vel_max`, `toggle_angles` / `limit_angles`, `limit_positions` (crank and coupler collinear: the rocker's dead centres), `F_guide_pk`. Checks: transmission angle (`μ_req = 0` always passes), loop-closure and velocity-loop residuals (linkages); pressure angle, undercut or cusp, contact (cam).

**Cam offset sign.** The cam turns counter-clockwise (θ increasing) with the follower axis at +x offset `e` from the cam centre; positive `e` reduces the pressure angle where `ds/dθ > e` (the rise) and negative `e` raises it. `T_load` and `F_load` positive resist positive `ω₄` / `+x` motion; with "Opposes motion" the sign is applied against the actual direction of motion.

**Limitations.** Rigid, planar, no clearance, no friction, single degree of freedom, constant input speed; the cam follower translates (no swing arm or oscillating follower), the flat-faced follower is in line, the spring is linear and its surge is not modelled (M7 reports it); link inertia and gravity are not modelled beyond the output element; the open/crossed labels name the two branches of the closed form and the sweep stays on one of them; a change-point linkage (parallelogram, deltoid) is continued through its change points on the same branch, with the circuit named at a reference angle independent of the sweep (the middle of the first interval between change points in 0 … 2π, π/2 when there are none), so `theta_start` does not change which motion `Open` means and the label at a crank angle is the same on every turn (the parity at the start of the sweep is that of the start angle wrapped into 0 … 2π; only the true change points, θ₂ = 0 or π with all four links collinear, flip the branch, the other toggles are assembly limits); a linkage with one change point per turn (a kite, for example 40/40/100/100) repeats every 720°, and a sweep of more than one turn warns; the peak of a quantity that jumps at a sign change of the velocity (an opposing load) is the limit value at the jump.

**Test cases.** Analytic and Confirmed, recomputed in python3 (Freudenstein quadratic as an independent route to the position; the differentiated loop solved as a real 2×2 system and cross-checked by central finite differences; peaks by a 20001-point scan polished by golden section): `ka-fourbar-crank-rocker-open` (120/40/110/90 mm at θ₂ = 90°: θ₃ 25.617841°, θ₄ 103.371151°, ω₄ 5.1533032 rad/s, α₄ 26.749883 rad/s², μ_min 45.815615°, peak T_in 1.5134299 N·m), `ka-fourbar-parallelogram` (closed form: θ₄ = θ₂, θ₃ = 0, ω₄ = ω₂, T_in = T_L, μ_min = 5° on a 5° … 175° sweep), `ka-fourbar-crank-rocker-crossed`, `ka-slider-inline` (closed form: x(90°) = √(l² − r²), v = −rω, a = ω²r²/√(l² − r²), stroke 2r, peak a = rω²(1 + r/l)), `ka-slider-offset` (stroke √((l + r)² − e²) − √((l − r)² − e²) = 100.335104 mm), `ka-slider-crossed`; cams: `ka-cam-cycloidal` (closed form: v_pk = 2hω/β = 1.6 m/s, a_pk = 2πhω²/β² = 201.06193 m/s², j_pk = 4π²hω³/β³ = 50532.375 m/s³), `ka-cam-modified-trapezoid` (C_a = 8π/(π + 2)), `ka-cam-345` (C_v = 15/8, C_a = 10/√3, C_j = 60), `ka-cam-4567` (C_v = 35/16, C_a = f″((5 − √5)/10) = 7.513188, C_j = 52.5), `ka-cam-dwell-only` (ρ_min = R_b + R_r = 48 mm exactly), `ka-cam-flat-faced` (w_face = 2h/β = 19.0986 mm), and the pressure angle, curvature radius and minimum base circle of the offset and in-line roller cams (`ka-cam-offset-roller`, `ka-cam-inline-roller`: pitch curve differentiated numerically by a 4th-order finite-difference stencil, independently of the closed form in the calculator).
