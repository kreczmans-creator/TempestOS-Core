# M9 — Mechanism kinematics: four-bar, slider-crank (`mech.mechanism@1.0.0`)

**Scope.** A single-degree-of-freedom planar mechanism driven at constant input speed: a **four-bar linkage** or an **offset slider-crank** (this page; the cam mode is added below once built). For each: the closed-form vector-loop position on both circuits (open and crossed), velocity and acceleration over a sweep of the input angle, Grashof class, transmission angle against an editable minimum, toggle or dead-centre positions, the loop-closure residual, and the input torque by virtual work. Rigid links, planar, no clearance, no friction, one degree of freedom. Standards are not involved; Norton (*Design of Machinery*) and Uicker, Pennock and Shigley (*Theory of Machines and Mechanisms*) are cited by chapter for the methods.

**Method.**
- Four-bar: ground `d` from O₂ (origin) to O₄ = (d, 0), crank `a`, coupler `b`, rocker `c` (inputs `L1 … L4`). Loop: `a·e^{jθ₂} + b·e^{jθ₃} = d + c·e^{jθ₄}`. With `ℓ = |A − O₄|` and `cos γ = (c² + ℓ² − b²)/(2cℓ)` (law of cosines, the Freudenstein result), `θ₄ = φ − γ` (**open**) or `φ + γ` (**crossed**, the mirror image about the line O₄A), `φ` the direction O₄→A; `θ₃` from `B − A`. The sweep stays on the chosen branch.
- Velocity and acceleration (constant ω₂): the differentiated loop is a 2×2 system with determinant `b·c·sin(θ₃ − θ₄)`; `ω₃ = ω₂·a·sin(θ₄ − θ₂)/(b·sin(θ₃ − θ₄))`, `ω₄ = ω₂·a·sin(θ₃ − θ₂)/(c·sin(θ₃ − θ₄))`, and α from the second derivative. Where `|sin(θ₃ − θ₄)| < 10⁻⁹` (a toggle) the system is singular and the row is left out of the table and counted (warning `reach`).
- Grashof: `s + l < p + q` Grashof (crank-rocker if the input link is the shortest, double-crank if the ground link is, double-rocker if the coupler is, "rocker-crank" if the output link is; in the last two the input link cannot turn fully), `=` the change point (parallelogram, deltoid: a collinear singular position every cycle), `>` non-Grashof (triple rocker). The class text is output `grashof`; `S_plus_L`, `P_plus_Q` the two sums.
- Transmission angle `cos μ = (b² + c² − ℓ²)/(2bc)`, folded into 0 … 90°; the check is `μ_req/μ_min ≤ 1` (default `μ_req` 40°, editable). Toggle crank angles (coupler and rocker collinear) are where `ℓ = b + c` or `|b − c|`: `cos θ₂ = (d² + a² − ℓ²)/(2ad)`, output `toggle_angles`.
- Slider-crank: crank `r` (`L2`), rod `l` (`L3`), slide line at height `e` (`e_off`): `x = r·cosθ + σ·√(l² − (r·sinθ − e)²)`, `σ = +1` open (slider on the +x side), `−1` crossed. `dx/dθ = −r·sinθ − r·cosθ·(r·sinθ − e)/(x − r·cosθ)`, rod angle `θ₃ = atan2(e − r·sinθ, x − r·cosθ)`, `dθ₃/dθ = −r·cosθ/(x − r·cosθ)`, second derivatives analytically; `v = ω·dx/dθ`, `a = ω²·d²x/dθ²`. Transmission angle `μ = 90° − |rod angle to the slide line| = arccos(|r·sinθ − e|/l)`. Dead centres (crank and rod collinear, v = 0): outer `θ = asin(e/(l + r))`, inner `θ = π + asin(e/(l − r))` (open; `π − …` and `−…` on the crossed circuit); stroke between them `√((l + r)² − e²) − √((l − r)² − e²)` (output `stroke_closed`; `stroke` is the swept extent). The crank turns fully only if `l ≥ r + |e|` (output `slider_class`).
- Input torque by virtual work, `T_in·ω_in = Σ F·v`: four-bar `T_in = (T_L + J·α₄)·ω₄/ω₂` with `T_L` the external torque on the output link (constant in direction, or `sign(ω₄)·T_L` when the load opposes the motion) and `J` its moment of inertia about the pivot; slider `T_in = (m·a + F_L)·dx/dθ` with the slider mass `m` and external force `F_L`. The crank and the coupler or rod are massless (add their mass to the output element for a first allowance).
- **Self-consistency.** The loop residual `|a·e^{jθ₂} + b·e^{jθ₃} − c·e^{jθ₄} − d|` (slider: `|r·e^{jθ} + l·e^{jθ₃} − (x + j·e)|`) is computed at every table row and every scan angle, reported as `res_max` (mm), and asserted below `10⁻⁹` mm by a check ("Loop-closure residual below 1e-9 mm at every step") and a warning if it fails.
- **Peaks** are those of the continuous motion over the swept range: a dense scan (about 4 points per degree of sweep, at least 720) refined by golden section between the neighbours of the best sample, so they do not depend on the table's step count `N` (`steps`, 2 … 3600, N + 1 rows). A sweep that passes a toggle or leaves the assembly range uses only the rows that exist.

**Inputs.** `mode` (Four-bar / Slider-crank / Cam), `circuit`, links `L1 … L4`, `e_off`, sweep `theta_start`, `theta_range`, `steps`, `n_in` (constant input speed), `mu_req`, loads `load_dir`, `T_load`, `J_out` (four-bar), `m_slider`, `F_load` (slider). Inputs a mechanism does not use are optional; one it needs and that is blank blocks with a message naming it (`missing`).

**Outputs.** Tables of the sweep (vectors, also drawn as series): four-bar `theta2_sw`, `theta3_sw`, `theta4_sw`, `omega3_sw`, `omega4_sw`, `alpha3_sw`, `alpha4_sw`, `mu_sw`, `T_in_sw`; slider `theta_sw`, `x_sw`, `v_sw`, `a_sw`, `theta3_sw`, `omega3_sw`, `mu_sw`, `T_in_sw`. Values at the first input angle (`*_0`, and `theta4_alt` / `x_alt` on the other circuit). Peaks `omega_out_pk`, `alpha_out_pk`, `omega3_pk`, `alpha3_pk`, `swing`, `mu_min`, `x_max`, `x_min`, `stroke`, `res_max`. **Ports** (the symbol after the dot of `mech.*`): `input_torque` (N·m, peak), `output_motion` (m/s: the **peak linear speed** of the output element: the slider, the cam follower, or for a four-bar the output pin at the rocker length `c`; the rocker's angular velocity is `omega_out_pk`), `peak_accel` (m/s²: same element, for a four-bar the magnitude of the pin's acceleration), `follower_force` (N: the rod force on the slider, the cam follower force, or the tangential force at the rocker pin `(T_L + J·α₄)/c`). Checks: transmission angle, loop-closure residual.

**Limitations.** Rigid, planar, no clearance, no friction, single degree of freedom, constant input speed; link inertia and gravity are not modelled beyond the output element; the open/crossed labels name the two branches of the closed form, and the sweep stays on one of them (a mechanism that can change branch at a change point is not followed through it); the peak of a quantity that jumps at a sign change of the velocity (an opposing load) is the limit value at the jump.

**Test cases.** Analytic and Confirmed, recomputed in python3 (Freudenstein quadratic as an independent route to the position; the differentiated loop solved as a real 2×2 system and cross-checked by central finite differences; peaks by a 20001-point scan polished by golden section): `ka-fourbar-crank-rocker-open` (120/40/110/90 mm at θ₂ = 90°: θ₃ 25.617841°, θ₄ 103.371151°, ω₄ 5.1533032 rad/s, α₄ 26.749883 rad/s², μ_min 45.815615°, peak T_in 1.5134299 N·m), `ka-fourbar-parallelogram` (closed form: θ₄ = θ₂, θ₃ = 0, ω₄ = ω₂, T_in = T_L, μ_min = 5° on a 5° … 175° sweep), `ka-fourbar-crank-rocker-crossed`, `ka-slider-inline` (closed form: x(90°) = √(l² − r²), v = −rω, a = ω²r²/√(l² − r²), stroke 2r, peak a = rω²(1 + r/l)), `ka-slider-offset` (stroke √((l + r)² − e²) − √((l − r)² − e²) = 100.335104 mm), `ka-slider-crossed`. **Not pinned (owner check):** Norton, *Design of Machinery* 6th ed., Example 4-1, and the Uicker, Pennock and Shigley 5th ed. four-bar example: the book examples' link lengths and θ₃/θ₄ are not available to the build, so no published number is entered; add them as Unconfirmed cases from the owner's copy.
