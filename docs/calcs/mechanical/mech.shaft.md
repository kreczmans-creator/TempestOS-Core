# M4 — Shaft: static, fatigue, keys, critical speed (`mech.shaft@1.5.1`)

**In plain terms.** A shaft carries gears, pulleys and bearings, so it bends and twists, and every turn the bending stress reverses, which is why shafts fail by fatigue at shoulders and keyways. The worksheet takes the stepped shaft, its loads and torque, finds the bending moment, slope and deflection along it, the fatigue and yield safety factors at the sections you mark, the keys, and the speed at which the shaft would whirl.

**Scope.** Stepped (optionally hollow) shaft on two bearings with radial loads in two planes and a torque with a steady (mean) part and an optional alternating part: M, slope and deflection along it; fatigue and yield factors at user-marked sections; slopes at the bearings; deflection at the loads; first lateral critical speed; parallel keys.

**Method.** The shaft is an S2 beam (`Tempest.Calc.Structural.Frame`, pin at A, roller at B) solved once per plane; M, slope and deflection combine as vectors. `K_f = 1 + q(K_t − 1)`; `σ_a = K_f 32 M d/(π(d⁴ − d_i⁴))`, `τ_m = K_fs 16 T d/(π(d⁴ − d_i⁴))` on the smaller side of a shoulder. `S_e = k_a k_b k_c k_d k_e k_f S_e1`, `S_e1` (the rotating-beam limit S_e′) `= 0.5 S_ut` (≤ 1400 MPa, else 700); `k_a = a S_ut^b` (ground, machined, hot-rolled, forged; 10th ed. Table 6-2 coefficients, overridable by a `shaft.marin` record — the 11th edition revised them), `k_b = 1.24 d^−0.107` (≤ 51 mm) or `1.51 d^−0.157` (to 254 mm, held above), `k_c = 1`, `k_e = 1 − 0.08 z_R` (normal quantile). DE-Goodman with the torque split into a steady part `T_m` (input `T`) and an alternating amplitude `T_a` (input `T_a`, 1.2.0; blank = 0), bending fully reversed: `1/n_f = √(σ_a² + 3τ_a²)/S_e + √3 τ_m/S_ut` with `τ_a = K_fs 16 T_a d/(π(d⁴ − d_i⁴))` (with `T_a` = 0 this is the earlier `σ_a/S_e + √3 τ_m/S_ut`; with `T_m` = 0 the torque is fully alternating); `n_y = S_y/√(σ_a² + 3τ_pk²)`, where `τ_pk` is built from the larger of `T_m + T_a` and the optional peak torque `T_pk` (blank: `T_m + T_a`), so the first-cycle yield check sees the peak torque (Shigley 10th ed. §6-9, §7-4). **The key is checked on the same peak torque** (1.2.0; it used `T` alone): `τ = 2T_k/(d w L)`, `σ = 4T_k/(d h L)`, `L_req` with `T_k` = the larger of `T_m + T_a` and `T_pk`. Rayleigh `ω = √(g Σm y/Σm y²)` with shaft self-mass and carried masses; Dunkerley `1/ω² = Σ m δ_ii` (§7-6); **the ratio `r_c` and the warning use the Dunkerley lower bound** (the Rayleigh value is an upper bound), warning when `n_c,D/n < 2`. When nothing deflects (ρ = 0 and every carried mass on a bearing, or no mass at all) no critical speed is found and a warning (`critical-speed-skipped`) says why. Keys `τ = 2T_k/(d w L)`, `σ = 4T_k/(d h L)`, `L_req = max(2T_k n_target/(d w 0.577 S_y), 4T_k n_target/(d h S_y))` at the target factor (§7-7). A key given only some of `x_key`, `w`, `h`, `L_key` warns (rule `key`) instead of dropping the check.

**Inputs.** `segments` (x_end 1–3000 mm, d 5–300 mm, bore), `x_A`, `x_B`; `loads` (x, F_y, F_z kN, mass kg); `T` 0–50 kN·m (steady or mean torque) between `x_T1` and `x_T2` (blank: the shaft length), `w_masses` (add the weights of the carried masses and the shaft, off by default), optional `T_a` (alternating torque amplitude about it, 1.2.0), optional `T_pk` (peak torque for the yield check and the key); `n` 0–30 000 rpm; `sections` (x, K_t, K_ts, q, q_s); `S_ut` 300–2000 MPa, `S_y`, `E`, `rho` (record `shaft.material`); `finish`, `R` 50–99.9 %, `k_d`, `k_f`; `n_target` 1.2–4; `theta_max`, `y_max` (record `shaft.limits`); optional key `x_key`, `w`, `h`, `L_key`, `S_y_key`, `S_y_hub`.

**Outputs.** `R_A`, `R_B`, `d_A`, `d_B`, `M_max`, `theta_A`, `theta_B`, `y_load`, `S_e`, `n_f_min`, `n_y_min`, per-section `X1…`, `n_c`, `n_c_D`, `r_c`, key `tau_key`, `sigma_key`, `L_key_req`. **Ports.** `R_A`, `R_B` → M5; `d_A`, `d_B` → M2; `n_f_min`, `n_c`. Links in: E1 torque and speed (the load torque `T_load` as `T`, the torque swing `T_alt` as `T_a`, the peak `T_pk` as `T_pk`; **not** the RMS torque `T_rms`, a thermal figure: before 1.2.0 the drive-train and mechanism-review packs bound it to `T` as a steady torque and the shaft saw no alternating torque).

**Port names (C-2).** Each port is listed under its public name, `<area>.<quantity>`; the output symbol stays as its symbol and as an alias, so a link, a pack binding or an inputs file written before the rename still resolves. `R_A` = `shaft.reaction_a`; `R_B` = `shaft.reaction_b`; `d_A` = `shaft.seat_diameter_a`; `d_B` = `shaft.seat_diameter_b`; `n_f_min` = `shaft.safety_fatigue_min`; `n_c` = `shaft.speed_critical`.

**Limitations.** Linear elastic; two simple supports (the bearings must be at least 1/200 of the shaft length apart, else the sheet blocks in its own words); no torsional vibration; first lateral mode only; K_t from records or fit equations, never a digitised chart.

**Changes in 1.3.1 (mech.shaft-02, from the C-1 verification).** Each section line prints its yield safety factor again, `n_y = …`, as the governing side's value (the side with the lower fatigue factor), and names the other side's value when that is lower and sets `n_y_min`; 1.3.0 had dropped it when the shoulder was checked on both sides. Output text only; no number changes. The test of -01 now pins the sheet's own Rayleigh value of 7246.6 rpm at 0.1 % (the finite-element value 7189.7 rpm is 0.8 % below it, as an upper bound should be; a continuous-beam quadrature of the same signed quotient gives 7263.8 rpm, python3).

**Changes in 1.3.0 (mech.shaft-01 to -05).** -01: the Rayleigh quotient uses the signed static deflection, so an overhung mass that lifts the span no longer inflates the numerator (bearings at 100 and 500 mm, 40 mm shaft of 700 mm, 5 kg at the tip: `n_c` 7975.0 rpm before, 7246.6 rpm now against the consistent-mass eigenvalue 7189.7 rpm; Dunkerley 7006.7 rpm stays below). -02: a shoulder is checked on both sides, each on its own diameter and bore, and the lower fatigue safety factor governs (Ø50 with a Ø46 bore beside Ø40 solid, 2 kN at the shoulder: `n_f` 5.3065 before, 2.8700 now). -03: `x_T2` has no 400 mm default; blank is the shaft length (L 800 mm, T 1 kN·m, section at 600 mm: `n_f` 5.3065 with no torque there, 2.3914 now), and a torque with a section or key outside `x_T1 … x_T2` warns (`torque-span`). -04: the carried-mass column and the shaft's own weight add no weight unless the new switch `w_masses` is on, which loads the vertical plane with m·g at the nodes (20 kg at mid-span with 2 kN: `M_max` 200 N·m, 219.61 N·m with the switch); a carried mass with the switch off warns (`mass-weight`) and the label and assumption say so. -05: the key bearing uses the lowest yield strength of the key, the shaft and the new optional `S_y_hub` (shaft 250 MPa, key 400 MPa, T 1.2 kN·m, Ø50, 14 × 9 key, n_target 2: `L_key_req` 53.33 mm before, 85.33 mm now; a 200 MPa hub gives 106.67 mm); without a hub strength the key check warns (`key-hub`).

**Changes in 1.5.1 (merge of the C-1 line's 1.3.1 and the C-2 line's 1.5.0).** The C-1 line had 1.3.1 (the section lines print `n_y` again) and the C-2 line 1.5.0 (the critical-speed check, the title and the public port names); the merged template is 1.5.1, above both. No number changes from the merge.

**Changes in 1.5.0 (C-2 fix, M1; shipped as part of 1.5.1).** `critical-speed`: the first critical speed (Dunkerley) must be at least twice the operating speed; below that the new check "Critical speed against the operating speed" fails (input `n_c_req = 2·n`, capacity `n_c_D`), where it only warned before. Every other value is unchanged.

**Test cases.** `ka-simply-supported` (slope 2.430 mrad, deflection 0.3240 mm, n_c 5305.2 rpm — analytic, Confirmed), `ka-key` (τ 36.99, σ 84.56 MPa — analytic, Confirmed), `ka-shigley-7-1` (S_e 201.50 MPa, n_f 1.621, n_y 4.489 with the example's data converted to SI exactly — **Unconfirmed** until compared with the book's printed answers). Mott 6th ed. shaft examples are not pinned (owner copy needed). 1.2.0: alternating torque T_m 50 + T_a 150 N·m on the Example 7-1 shaft with a 28.48 N·m bending moment (n_f 2.1325 against 6.9987 for the 50 N·m taken alone, n_y 4.9555), fully alternating 124.28 N·m (n_f 1.5999 against 1.6210 as a steady torque), and the key on T_pk, all from the formulas above in python3.
