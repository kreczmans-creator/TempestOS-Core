## Addendum A — twelve more calculators, linking, packs and records

Written against `release/v0.26.0` at `510f337c`. Addendum A forbids a tag, a release or a merge to `main` (§A0, DA-01). ### What was built

- Twelve calculators: M4 shaft, M5 bearing life, M6 weld group, S4 bolts and welds (EN 1993-1-8), S5 load combinations, S6 column base plate, B4 room heat loss, B5 heat exchanger, B6 sealed system, E4 conductor sizing, E5 fault protection, E6 load budget. With the 16 earlier templates that makes 28 templates and 116 known-answer cases, 13 of them Unconfirmed (listed in test-pack section 4).
- Result linking (L1): outputs link to inputs, including single cells of a table. A link is pinned to a revision, checks dimensions, refuses cycles and chains over 20, and marks everything downstream stale. A stale calculation cannot be checked or issued; "Update linked inputs" or "Update to revision n" mends it.
- Calculation packs: five built-in pack templates (drive train, motor circuit, wet heating, enclosure cooling, steel support) with example inputs, checked once and issued as one bundle. Five pack worked examples under `docs/worked-examples/packs/`.
- Desktop: a Saved calculations list (with a STALE mark and an Open button) and a Calculation packs panel (create from a template, open a member, check, reopen, issue).
- Reference records with a licence lock. A licensed record's values never leave in an export; issued sheets withhold a licensed table's rows; a withheld value cannot be re-imported until the record is applied again. No record ships with the product.
- Independent check on E4, E5 and S6: the creator and anyone who set or changed an input cannot check.
- Reviews: Security, Red, Green, Black, Blue (two rounds), Purple, Yellow, White, Grey and Orange each have an Addendum A section in `docs/reviews/`. No Critical or High finding is open. DA-17 to DA-24 record the decisions.
- Tests on the final code (run on Linux): Engine 384, Sheets 111, Library 168 passed and 1 skipped (D-32), Calculators 869. The Desktop and Core suites were not re-run for this section.

### Decisions and limits that need your call
