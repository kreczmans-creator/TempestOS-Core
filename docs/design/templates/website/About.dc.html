About

Tempest Engineering is the independent design practice of Steven Kreczman.

I've spent ten years designing mechanical hardware, most of it in environments where getting it wrong is expensive: defence electronics, MoD equipment support, and industrial hydraulics.

Background

Northrop Grumman UK — Mission Systems

2022–2026

Principal Mechanical Engineer and team lead. Design of electronic equipment enclosures and mounting hardware for defence programmes, alongside responsibility for the technical output of a small engineering team.

KBR Government Solutions

Equipment support engineering on MoD contracts — sustaining and modifying in-service hardware, where the constraint is usually what already exists rather than what you'd choose to design.

Physical Digital

Metrology fixture design and scan-to-CAD reverse engineering. Building accurate models from physical parts, and the fixtures needed to measure them repeatably.

HUSCO International

Hydraulic valve design. Tight tolerances, high pressures, and volume manufacture.

How I work

Three things I'd want a client to know

I'd rather ask than assume

Most design problems that go wrong are requirements problems. I'll push on the brief early, which occasionally means an awkward first conversation and almost always means a cheaper project.

Drawings are a deliverable, not an afterthought

A model that can't be manufactured from its drawing isn't finished. GD&T applied properly, tolerances that reflect function rather than habit.

I'll tell you if it isn't a fit

Some packages need a bigger team, specialist analysis, or a capability I don't have. Saying so early costs me a job and saves you a quarter.
