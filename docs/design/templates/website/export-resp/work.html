Most of my work is covered by NDA or is classified. What follows is representative of the type and standard of work, not of any specific programme.

Demonstration pieces
Render — manifold block, three-quarter view

Hydraulic manifold block

Own design. Cross-drilled porting, cavity selection to standard, and a drawing pack with full GD&T applied to the mounting face and port positions. Shown alongside the section view and the release drawing.

Render — bracket + full drawing sheet

Machined bracket, drawing pack

A deliberately ordinary part, drawn properly. Datum scheme, position and profile tolerances derived from the assembly stack, and a title block to BS8888. This is what a drawing from me looks like.

Screenshot — FEA mesh and results, side by side

Structural FEA study

Static analysis of the bracket above, showing mesh, boundary conditions and results. The point isn't the colours — it's that the assumptions are stated and the result is defensible at a design review.

Render or scan overlay — scan-to-CAD

Scan-to-CAD reconstruction

Point cloud through to a fully parametric solid model, with deviation analysis showing how closely the model matches the physical part.
