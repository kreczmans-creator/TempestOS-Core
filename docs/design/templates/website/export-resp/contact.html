The quickest way to start is an email with a short description of what you're building and roughly when you need it. If there's enough to go on I'll come back with an initial view; if not, I'll ask the two or three questions that matter.

I aim to reply within one working day.

Before you write, it helps to know
→What the part or assembly does, and what it sits inside
→Whether there's existing CAD, drawings, or a physical sample
→Which CAD format you work in
→Your target date, and whether it's firm

None of this is essential. An email that just says "can you do this?" is fine too.
