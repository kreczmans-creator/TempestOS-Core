Capabilities

Software, standards and the kind of work I take on. If you're checking whether I can work in your toolchain, the answer is probably here.

Design & development

Concept through detailed design and release. Sheet metal, machined components, castings, enclosures and multi-part assemblies. Design for manufacture is part of the process rather than a review gate at the end — I'd rather resolve a tooling constraint at concept than redraw at release.

PTC Creo · Siemens NX · SolidWorks
Analysis

Structural FEA and CFD, used to inform design decisions and to substantiate them at design review. Static, modal, thermal and flow work, sized to the question being asked — a hand calculation that answers it is a better deliverable than an analysis that impresses.

Drawings & data packs

Fully dimensioned, GD&T-toleranced drawings to BS8888 or ASME Y14.5, issued to your title block, numbering and revision scheme. Tolerances derived from function and assembly stack, not applied by habit. Native and neutral CAD (STEP, Parasolid) supplied as required.

Reverse engineering & metrology

Scan-to-CAD reconstruction where no model exists — legacy hardware, obsolete parts, or geometry that was never captured. Inspection fixture design for repeatable measurement.

Hydraulics

Valve and manifold design, circuit work, and component selection. CETOP Level 3, Mobile Hydraulics.

Defence & secure programmes

UK SC cleared, with experience of MoD equipment support contracts and defence electronics programmes. Comfortable working within controlled environments and to customer configuration management processes.

What I don't do

Electronics and PCB design, software, and certification sign-off. If a package needs those, I'll say so and where possible point you somewhere sensible.
