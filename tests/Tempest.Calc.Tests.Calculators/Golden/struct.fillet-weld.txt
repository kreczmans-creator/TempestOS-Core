Title: Fillet weld (EN 1993-1-8)

1. Scope and method
Calculates the utilisation of a fillet weld loaded parallel, transverse and normal to its axis, with the required throat and leg. The simplified method compares the resultant over the throat with the design shear strength.
Resultant force on the weld: F = √(F_∥² + F_⊥² + F_n²). Design shear strength of the weld, simplified method: f_vw,d = f_u/(√3·β_w·γ_M2). Long-joint reduction of a lap joint longer than 150·a (4.11(3)): β_Lw = min(1, 1.2 − 0.2·L/(150·a)); 1 for a shorter lap or another joint type. Shear stress on the throat: τ_w = F/(a·L). Weld resistance and utilisation: F_w,Rd = β_Lw·f_vw,d·a·L; u = F/F_w,Rd. Throat needed (not less than 3 mm, with the β_Lw of the throat provided) and the matching leg: a_req = max(F/(β_Lw·f_vw,d·L), 3 mm); leg = √2·a.

2. Inputs
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  joint | Joint type: a lap joint is reduced beyond 150 throats, and Other is for a weld whose stress follows the base metal | Lap joint |  | manual | any
  L | Effective weld length | 200 | mm | manual | 30 … 10000 mm
  a | Throat thickness | 5 | mm | manual | 3 … 50 mm
  f_u | Ultimate tensile strength of the weaker part joined | 490 | MPa | manual | 300 … 1000 MPa
  F_par | Force parallel to the weld axis | 150 | kN | manual | -100000 … 100000 kN
  F_tr | Force transverse to the weld axis, in the plane of the parts | 0 | kN | manual | -100000 … 100000 kN
  F_n | Force normal to the plane of the parts | 0 | kN | manual | -100000 … 100000 kN
  beta_w | Correlation factor (0.8 S235, 0.85 S275, 0.9 S355, 1.0 S420 and S460) | 0.9 |  | manual | 0.8 … 1
  gamma_M2 | Partial factor for the weld resistance | 1.25 |  | manual | 1 … 2

3. Assumptions
  A1. The resultant force is shared uniformly along the effective length. (Simplified method.)
  A2. Equal-leg fillet: leg = √2 × throat. (Geometry.)
  A3. A lap joint longer than 150·a is reduced by β_Lw = 1.2 − 0.2·L/(150·a), not above 1. (BS EN 1993-1-8 4.11(3).)
  A4. The weld length L is the length of the lap in the direction of the force. (BS EN 1993-1-8 4.11(3).)

4. Working
  [10] beta_Lw = no long-joint reduction    « BS EN 1993-1-8 4.11
      = lap not longer than 150·a
      = 1
        Long-joint reduction factor (1: not reduced)
  [11] F = sqrt(F_par^2 + F_tr^2 + F_n^2)
      = sqrt((150 kN)^2 + (0 kN)^2 + (0 kN)^2)
      = 150 kN
        Resultant force on the weld
  [12] f_vw_d = f_u / (sqrt(3) * beta_w * gamma_M2)    « BS EN 1993-1-8 4.5.3.3
      = 490 MPa / (sqrt(3) × 0.9 × 1.25)
      = 251.47 MPa
        Design shear strength of the weld
  [13] tau_w = F / (a * L)
      = 150 kN / (5 mm × 200 mm)
      = 150 MPa
        Stress on the throat
  [14] F_w_Rd = beta_Lw * f_vw_d * a * L    « BS EN 1993-1-8 4.5.3.3
      = 1 × 251.47 MPa × 5 mm × 200 mm
      = 251.47 kN
        Design resistance of the weld, reduced by the long-joint factor when it applies
  [15] u = F / F_w_Rd
      = 150 kN / 251.47 kN
      = 0.5965
        Utilisation
  [16] a_calc = F / (beta_Lw * f_vw_d * L)
      = 150 kN / (1 × 251.47 MPa × 200 mm)
      = 2.9825 mm
        Throat needed for the load (with the long-joint factor of the throat provided)
  [17] a_req = max(a_calc, 3 mm)    « BS EN 1993-1-8 4.5.2(2)
      = max(2.9825 mm, 3 mm)
      = 3 mm
        Required throat (not less than 3 mm)
  [18] s_leg = sqrt(2) * a
      = sqrt(2) × 5 mm
      = 7.0711 mm
        Leg length of the throat provided (equal legs)

5. Results
  F_w_Rd = 251.47 kN    Weld resistance
  u = 0.60    Utilisation
  f_vw_d = 251.5 MPa    Design shear strength
  tau_w = 150 MPa    Throat stress
  a_req = 3 mm    Required throat
  s_leg = 7.071 mm    Leg length provided
  F = 150 kN    Resultant force (governing)
  beta_Lw = 1    Long-joint factor
  a_calc = 2.982 mm    Throat needed
