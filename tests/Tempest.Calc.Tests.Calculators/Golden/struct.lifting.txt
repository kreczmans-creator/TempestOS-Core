Title: Lifting lugs and spreader beams

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
This is a design calculation, not a thorough examination. Pin-loaded lifting lugs and the lug loads of spreader or lifting beams, checked by the BTH-1 allowable-stress approach (clause 3-3.3), by the EN 13155 proof route (2× the rated load without permanent set, 3× without release) or both: sling force from the rated load, sling angle and out-of-plane angle (with the automatic 6° tilt cases for a horizontal beam); tension beyond the hole, single-plane fracture beyond the hole, shear-out on two planes, pin bearing and dishing; capacity and utilisation per mode, the governing mode, the proof load and a marking and certification checklist. Scope notes (LOLER and PUWER): this sheet does not replace the thorough examination and inspection of lifting equipment by a competent person (LOLER reg 9), the marking, test and certification that the regulations and the standards require, the planning of the lifting operation, the weld, bolt and beam checks of the linked sheets, or the pin, shackle and sling ratings. It is available only while the lifting feature is enabled; its issue needs a competent-person attestation and a second independent check. Signatures are not authenticated by TempestOS: the signed-in names of the author, the checkers and the competent person are as entered into this installation.
Sling force: F_v = WLL/n_eff (n_eff = n_lugs, at most 3); F_s = F_v/(cos θ·cos β); F_ip = F_s·cos β; F_op = F_s·sin β; N_beam = F_v·tan θ; M_op = F_op·h Horizontal beam, automatic 6° tilt: pitch (θ ± 6°, load redistributed, the sling at θ − 6° takes 2·F_v·sin(θ + 6°)/sin 2θ); roll (β + 6°); the larger F_s governs, F_ip, F_op and N_beam are the envelopes Geometry: side = min(b_e, R − D_h/2); b_e,eff = min(side, 4t, 0.6·(f_u/f_y)·√(D_h/side)·side); a_eff = a (default R − D_h/2); A_t = 2·t·b_e,eff; A_fr = [1.13·a_eff + 0.92·side/(1 + side/D_h)]·t; φ = 55·D_p/D_h (degrees); A_v = 2·t·[a_eff + (D_p/2)(1 − cos φ)]; A_b = D_p·t Pin clearance: C_r = 1 − 0.275·√(1 − D_p²/D_h²) BTH-1 (3-3.3): F_a = min(f_y/n_y, f_u/n_u); design load D = N_d·k_svc·F_s; P_t = C_r·F_a·A_t; P_sp = C_r·F_a·A_fr; P_v = F_a·A_v/√3; P_b = k_b·F_a·A_b; u = D/P for each; dishing D_h/t ≤ dish_max EN 13155 (2× elastic, 3× no release): P_EN = min(Y/2, U/3) for each section, Y with f_y and U with f_u (shear with f/√3; fracture area without C_r); u = F_s/P_EN Capacity: WLL_cap = WLL/u_lug for the governing section of each mode (smaller of the modes used); beam: u_beam from N_beam on A_beam; proof load = k_proof·WLL
  Ref: ASME BTH-1 3-3.3 — Pin-connected plates (design of lifting lugs)
  Ref: BS EN 13155 design requirements (clause to be confirmed) — Non-fixed load lifting attachments: 2× elastic, 3× no release
  Ref: BS EN 13001-3-1 5 — Proof of static strength: the calculation route cited, not implemented
  Ref: LOLER 1998 reg 7, reg 9 — Marking of lifting equipment; thorough examination and inspection
  Ref: PUWER 1998 reg 4, reg 5 — Suitability and maintenance of work equipment

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. A design calculation, not a thorough examination: it checks the lug plate at the pin hole against the user's chosen criteria and nothing else. (LOLER reg 9 and PUWER duties are the owner's and the competent person's.)
  A2. Nominal-stress screening of four sections (tension beyond the hole, single-plane fracture beyond the hole, shear-out, bearing) on a plate loaded through a pin; no stress-concentration factor, no fatigue (the cycle count is limited to 16,000), no weld, bolt or beam check, which are made on the linked sheets. (The notch-stress route of EN 13001-3-1 is cited, not implemented.)
  A3. The load on the hole is the resultant sling force F_s whatever the out-of-plane angle, which is conservative; the out-of-plane component and its moment are outputs for the weld and base checks. (F_op = F_s·sin β.)
  A4. Equal sharing between the lugs, with a fourth lug not relied on (three share). The share of a statically indeterminate set is not computed. (Conservative convention; replace with the actual share if it is known.)
  A5. A horizontal beam adds the automatic 6° tilt as two cases: pitch (sling angles θ ± 6° with the load redistributed between the lugs by statics, the slings meeting at the hook) and roll (out-of-plane angle + 6°), the larger sling force governing. With θ ≤ 6° the pitch case puts the whole load on one lug; with more than two lugs the two-lug redistribution is applied to each lug's share. (Addendum B §B6 S8; EN 13155.)
  A6. The allowable stress is the one lesser value min(f_y/n_y, f_u/n_u) for every BTH-1 section, as in the published worked example the sheet is checked against; the pin-clearance factor C_r is applied as stated from D_p/D_h < 1 with no cut-off. (Conservative; owner checklist.)
  A7. Signatures are not authenticated by TempestOS: the platform asks for no password, so the author, the two checkers and the competent person are as entered into this installation; any person can switch to another name. (ADR-0173; the feature stays off until this is addressed.)
  A8. The design factor, service-class factor, allowable-stress divisors, bearing multiple, dishing limit and proof-load factor are the user's records from their own licensed copy of the code; none is shipped and none has a default. (ASME BTH-1 and EN 13155 text and tables are not shipped.)
  A9. The equations are written out in the spec document in the author's notation (the single-plane fracture and effective-width relations and the 1.13 and 0.92 constants follow the published course example); the owner compares them with BTH-1 (2023 or later) and EN 13155+A1 before the feature is ever enabled. (Owner test pack, S8 review checklist.)

4. WORKING
  [19] n_eff = min(n_lugs, 3)    « spec
      = min(2, 3)
      = 2
        Lugs sharing the load (a fourth is not relied on)
  [20] F_v = WLL/n_eff    « spec
      = 150 kN / 2
      = 75 kN
        Vertical load on each lug
  [21] F_s_nom = F_v/(cos θ·cos β)    « spec
      = 75 kN / (cos 30° × cos 5°)
      = 86.933 kN
        Sling force on each lug, untilted
  [22] F_s = F_s,nom    « spec
      = no tilt case
      = 86.933 kN
        Sling force on each lug (resultant), governing case
  [23] F_ip = F_s·cos β    « spec
      = 86.933 kN × cos β
      = 86.603 kN
        In-plane component of the sling force (envelope)
  [24] F_op = F_s·sin β    « spec
      = 86.933 kN × sin β
      = 7.5767 kN
        Out-of-plane component of the sling force (envelope)
  [25] N_beam = F_v·tan θ    « spec
      = 75 kN × tan(30°)
      = 43.301 kN
        Horizontal sling component per lug (axial force in a spreader beam, envelope)
  [27] M_op = F_op·h    « spec
      = 7.5767 kN × 150 mm
      = 1.1365 kN·m
        Out-of-plane moment at the weld or base
  [28] side = min(b_e, R − D_h/2)    « spec
      = min(35, 65 − 50/2) mm
      = 35 mm
        Edge distance on each side of the hole
  [29] a_eff = a, or R − D_h/2    « spec
      = 65 − 50/2 mm
      = 40 mm
        End distance along the load line
  [30] phi = 55·D_p/D_h (degrees)    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 55 × 45 / 50
      = 49.5 deg
        Angle of the shear planes
  [31] be_eff = min(b_e,eff, 4t, 0.6·(f_u/f_y)·√(D_h/b_e,eff)·b_e,eff)    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = min(35, 80, 33.231) mm
      = 33.231 mm
        Effective width beyond the hole in tension
  [32] A_t = 2·t·b_e,eff    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 2 × 20 × 33.231 mm
      = 1329.2 mm²
        Area beyond the hole in tension
  [33] A_fr = (1.13·a + 0.92·b_e/(1 + b_e/D_h))·t    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = (1.13 × 40 + 0.92 × 35 / (1 + 35/50)) × 20 mm
      = 1282.8 mm²
        Single-plane fracture area beyond the hole (before C_r)
  [34] A_v = 2·t·[a + (D_p/2)(1 − cos φ)]    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 2 × 20 × [40 + (45/2) × (1 − cos 49.5°)] mm
      = 1915.5 mm²
        Shear area of the two shear-out planes
  [35] A_b = D_p·t    « spec
      = 45 × 20 mm
      = 900 mm²
        Projected bearing area of the pin
  [36] Cr = 1 − 0.275·√(1 − D_p²/D_h²)    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 1 − 0.275 × √(1 − (45/50)²)
      = 0.88013
        Pin-clearance factor
  [37] Fa = min(f_y/n_y, f_u/n_u)    « spec
      = min(355 / 1.6, 470 / 2.2) MPa
      = 213.64 MPa
        Allowable stress
  [38] D_bth = N_d·k_svc·F_s    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 1.8 × 1 × 86.933 kN
      = 156.48 kN
        Design load on each lug
  [39] P_t = C_r·F_a·A_t    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 0.88013 × 213.64 MPa × 1329.2 mm²
      = 249.93 kN
        Tension beyond the hole
  [40] P_sp = C_r·F_a·A_fr    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 0.88013 × 213.64 MPa × 1282.8 mm²
      = 241.21 kN
        Single-plane fracture beyond the hole
  [41] P_v = F_a·A_v/√3    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 213.64 MPa × 1915.5 mm² / √3
      = 236.26 kN

  [42] P_b = k_b·F_a·A_b    « BTH-1 3-3.3

        Pin bearing
  [43] u_t = D/P    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 156.48 kN / 249.93 kN

        Utilisation, tension beyond the hole
  [44] u_sp = D/P    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 156.48 kN / 241.21 kN

        Utilisation, single-plane fracture
  [45] u_v = D/P    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 156.48 kN / 236.26 kN

  [46] u_b = D/P    « BTH-1 3-3.3

  [48] cap_bth = WLL/u_bth    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 150 kN / 0.6782

        Rated load the lug carries by the BTH-1 approach
