Title: Lifting lugs and spreader beams

1. Scope and method
Calculates the capacity and utilisation of a pin-loaded lifting lug and of a spreader beam by the BTH-1 approach, the EN 13155 proof route or both. It is a design calculation, not a thorough examination under LOLER or PUWER.
Sling force: F_v = WLL/n_eff (n_eff = n_lugs, at most 3); F_s = F_v/(cos θ·cos β); F_ip = F_s·cos β; F_op = F_s·sin β; N_beam = F_v·tan θ; M_op = F_op·h Horizontal beam, automatic 6° tilt: pitch (θ ± 6°, load redistributed, the sling at θ − 6° takes 2·F_v·sin(θ + 6°)/sin 2θ, and the force along the tilted beam is F_v·sin(θ + 6°)/cos θ); roll (β + 6°); the larger F_s governs, F_ip, F_op and N_beam are the envelopes Lug section geometry: side = min(b_e, r_lug − D_h/2); b_e,eff = min(side, 4t_p, 0.6·(f_u/f_y)·√(D_h/side)·side); a_eff = a (default r_lug − D_h/2); A_t = 2·t_p·b_e,eff; A_fr = [1.13·a_eff + 0.92·side/(1 + side/D_h)]·t_p; φ = 55·D_p/D_h (degrees); A_v = 2·t_p·[a_eff + (D_p/2)(1 − cos φ)]; A_b = D_p·t_p Pin clearance: C_r = 1 − 0.275·√(1 − D_p²/D_h²) BTH-1 (3-3.3): F_a = min(f_y/n_y, f_u/n_u); design load D = N_d·k_svc·F_s; P_t = C_r·F_a·A_t; P_sp = C_r·F_a·A_fr; P_v = F_a·A_v/√3; P_b = k_b·F_a·A_b; u = D/P for each; dishing D_h/t_p ≤ dish_max EN 13155 (2× elastic, 3× no release): P_EN = min(Y/2, U/3) for each section, Y with f_y and U with f_u (shear with f/√3; fracture area without C_r); u = F_s/P_EN Lug capacity and proof load: WLL_cap = WLL/u_lug for the governing section of each mode (smaller of the modes used); beam: u_beam from N_beam on A_beam; proof load = k_proof·WLL

3. Assumptions
  A1. This is a design calculation, not a thorough examination. (It checks the lug plate at the pin hole against the user's chosen criteria and nothing else. The owner and the competent person keep the LOLER reg 9 and PUWER duties.)
  A2. The sheet screens four sections by nominal stress: tension beyond the hole, single-plane fracture, shear-out and bearing. (There is no stress-concentration factor, no fatigue (at most 16,000 cycles) and no weld, bolt or beam check. The notch-stress route of EN 13001-3-1 is cited, not implemented.)
  A3. The load on the hole is the resultant sling force F_s whatever the out-of-plane angle, which is conservative. (The out-of-plane component and its moment are outputs for the weld and base checks: F_op = F_s·sin β.)
  A4. The lugs share the load equally, and a fourth lug is not relied on. (This is a conservative convention. The share of a statically indeterminate set is not computed, so replace the sharing with the actual share if it is known.)
  A5. A horizontal beam adds an automatic 6° tilt as a pitch case and a roll case. (Pitch: the sling angles are θ ± 6° with the load redistributed between the lugs by statics, and the slings meet at the hook. Roll: the out-of-plane angle grows by 6°. N_beam is the force along the beam axis, and in the pitch case the more loaded lug sends F_v·sin(θ + 6°)/cos θ along it. With θ ≤ 6° the pitch case puts the whole load on one lug. With more than two lugs the two-lug redistribution is applied to each lug's share. Owner's specification (6° tilt of a horizontal beam); EN 13155.)
  A6. The allowable stress is the one lesser value min(f_y/n_y, f_u/n_u) for every BTH-1 section. (This follows the published worked example the sheet is checked against. The pin-clearance factor C_r is applied as stated from D_p/D_h < 1 with no cut-off. Conservative; owner checklist.)
  A7. TempestOS does not authenticate signatures, so any person can switch to another name. (The platform asks for no password, so the author, the two checkers and the competent person are as entered into this installation. ADR-0173; the feature stays off until this is addressed.)
  A8. The design factor, service-class factor, divisors, bearing multiple, dishing limit and proof-load factor are the user's records. (They come from the user's own licensed copy of the code. None is shipped and none has a default, and the text and tables of ASME BTH-1 and EN 13155 are not shipped.)
  A9. The owner compares the equations in the spec document with BTH-1 and EN 13155 before the feature is enabled. (The single-plane fracture and effective-width relations and the constants 1.13 and 0.92 follow the published course example. The review checklist of the owner test pack lists the comparison.)

4. Working
  [19] n_eff = min(n_lugs, 3)    « spec
      = min(2, 3)
      = 2
        Lugs sharing the load (a fourth is not relied on)
  [20] F_v = WLL/n_eff    « spec
      = 150 kN / 2
      = 75 kN
        Vertical load on each lug
  [21] F_s_nom = F_v/(cos θ·cos β)    « spec
      = 75 kN / (cos 30° × cos 5°)
      = 86.933 kN
        Sling force on each lug, untilted
  [22] F_s = F_s,nom    « spec
      = no tilt case
      = 86.933 kN
        Sling force on each lug (resultant), governing case
  [23] F_ip = F_s·cos β    « spec
      = 86.933 kN × cos β
      = 86.603 kN
        In-plane component of the sling force (envelope)
  [24] F_op = F_s·sin β    « spec
      = 86.933 kN × sin β
      = 7.5767 kN
        Out-of-plane component of the sling force (envelope)
  [25] N_beam = F_v·tan θ    « spec
      = 75 kN × tan(30°)
      = 43.301 kN
        Axial force along the spreader beam at the more loaded lug (envelope over the cases)
  [27] M_op = F_op·h    « spec
      = 7.5767 kN × 150 mm
      = 1.1365 kN·m
        Out-of-plane moment at the weld or base
  [28] side = min(b_e, r_lug − D_h/2)    « spec
      = min(35, 65 − 50/2) mm
      = 35 mm
        Edge distance on each side of the hole
  [29] a_eff = a, or r_lug − D_h/2    « spec
      = 65 − 50/2 mm
      = 40 mm
        End distance along the load line
  [30] phi = 55·D_p/D_h (degrees)    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 55 × 45 / 50
      = 49.5 deg
        Angle of the shear planes
  [31] be_eff = min(b_e,eff, 4t_p, 0.6·(f_u/f_y)·√(D_h/b_e,eff)·b_e,eff)    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = min(35, 80, 33.231) mm
      = 33.231 mm
        Effective width beyond the hole in tension
  [32] A_t = 2·t_p·b_e,eff    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 2 × 20 × 33.231 mm
      = 1329.2 mm²
        Area beyond the hole in tension
  [33] A_fr = (1.13·a + 0.92·b_e/(1 + b_e/D_h))·t_p    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = (1.13 × 40 + 0.92 × 35 / (1 + 35/50)) × 20 mm
      = 1282.8 mm²
        Single-plane fracture area beyond the hole (before C_r)
  [34] A_v = 2·t_p·[a + (D_p/2)(1 − cos φ)]    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 2 × 20 × [40 + (45/2) × (1 − cos 49.5°)] mm
      = 1915.5 mm²
        Shear area of the two shear-out planes
  [35] A_b = D_p·t_p    « spec
      = 45 × 20 mm
      = 900 mm²
        Projected bearing area of the pin
  [36] Cr = 1 − 0.275·√(1 − D_p²/D_h²)    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 1 − 0.275 × √(1 − (45/50)²)
      = 0.88013
        Pin-clearance factor
  [37] Fa = min(f_y/n_y, f_u/n_u)    « spec
      = min(355 / 1.6, 470 / 2.2) MPa
      = 213.64 MPa
        Allowable stress
  [38] D_bth = N_d·k_svc·F_s    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 1.8 × 1 × 86.933 kN
      = 156.48 kN
        Design load on each lug
  [39] P_t = C_r·F_a·A_t    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 0.88013 × 213.64 MPa × 1329.2 mm²
      = 249.93 kN
        Tension beyond the hole
  [40] P_sp = C_r·F_a·A_fr    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 0.88013 × 213.64 MPa × 1282.8 mm²
      = 241.21 kN
        Single-plane fracture beyond the hole
  [41] P_v = F_a·A_v/√3    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 213.64 MPa × 1915.5 mm² / √3
      = 236.26 kN
        Double-plane shear-out
  [42] P_b = k_b·F_a·A_b    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 1.2 × 213.64 MPa × 900 mm²
      = 230.73 kN
        Pin bearing
  [43] u_t = D/P    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 156.48 kN / 249.93 kN
      = 0.62609
        Utilisation, tension beyond the hole
  [44] u_sp = D/P    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 156.48 kN / 241.21 kN
      = 0.64874
        Utilisation, single-plane fracture
  [45] u_v = D/P    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 156.48 kN / 236.26 kN
      = 0.66231
        Utilisation, double-plane shear
  [46] u_b = D/P    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 156.48 kN / 230.73 kN
      = 0.6782
        Utilisation, bearing
  [47] u_bth = max(u_t, u_sp, u_v, u_b)    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = governing BTH-1 section
      = 0.6782
        BTH-1 utilisation
  [48] cap_bth = WLL/u_bth    « BTH-1 3-3.3
      = 150 kN / 0.6782
      = 221.17 kN
        Rated load the lug carries by the BTH-1 approach
  [50] dish = D_h/t_p    « spec
      = 50 / 20
      = 2.5
        Dishing ratio
  [51] u_dish = (D_h/t)/dish_max    « spec
      = 2.5 / 40
      = 0.0625
        Dishing-ratio utilisation
  [52] lug_util = max over the modes used    « spec
      = governing lug section
      = 0.6782
        Governing lug utilisation
  [53] capacity = WLL/lug_util    « spec
      = 150 kN / 0.6782
      = 221.17 kN
        Rated load the lug carries in the governing mode
  [54] util = max(lug_util, u_beam, u_dish)    « spec
      = max(0.6782, 0, 0.0625)
      = 0.6782
        Governing utilisation of the lug and the spreader
  [56] proof_load = k_proof·WLL    « spec
      = 1.5 × 150 kN
      = 225 kN
        Proof-load value
  [57] u_limit = limit
      = the utilisation limit
      = 1
        Utilisation limit
