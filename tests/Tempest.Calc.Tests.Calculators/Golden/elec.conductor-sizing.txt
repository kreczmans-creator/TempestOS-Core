Title: Conductor sizing and overload coordination

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
One circuit: the design current, the smallest preferred device rating that carries it, the smallest conductor whose tabulated current-carrying capacity (your own record) carries that rating once the ambient, grouping, installation and semi-enclosed-fuse factors are applied, and the overload coordination checks I_b ≤ I_n ≤ I_z and I₂ ≤ 1.45 I_z. The context (machine wiring or installation) changes the clause citations and the scope note only, never a number.
Design current: I_b = P/(√3·U·cosφ·η) three-phase, P/(U·cosφ·η) single-phase, P/U DC; or the linked I_b Device: I_n = smallest preferred rating in the ratings record with I_n ≥ I_b Fuse factor: C_c = 0.725 for a semi-enclosed fuse (1.45/2, derived), else 1 Required tabulated capacity: I_t,req = I_n/(C_a·C_g·C_i·C_c); optional derived C_a = √[(θ_p − θ_a)/(θ_p − θ_ref)] (derived approximation — compare with the licensed table) Conductor: CSA = smallest cross-section in the ampacity record with I_t ≥ I_t,req; AWG gauge n: d = 0.127·92^((36 − n)/39) mm, area = π d²/4 (derived) Carrying capacity: I_z = I_t·C_a·C_g·C_i Coordination: I_b ≤ I_n ≤ I_z and I₂ ≤ 1.45·I_z, I₂ = 1.45·I_n inherent for MCB types B, C, D and gG fuses, otherwise the device's I₂ from its datasheet
  Ref: BS 7671:2018 (as amended) reg. 433.1.1 — Co-ordination of conductors and overload protective devices
  Ref: BS 7671:2018 (as amended) reg. 523.1 — Current-carrying capacity of a cable
  Ref: BS EN 60204-1:2018 cl. 12.4 — Current-carrying capacity of conductors and cables in normal service
  Ref: BS EN 60204-1:2018 cl. 7.2 — Overcurrent protection
  Ref: BS EN 60898-1 cl. 3 — Circuit-breakers for overcurrent protection: conventional operating current

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Every tabulated value (the capacities, the device ratings, the correction factors) is entered from the user's own licensed copy or a manufacturer datasheet; none ships with the calculator. (§A5.)
  A2. The capacities in the table are for the installation method stated; the method key is descriptive and is not interpreted. (Records carry their source and edition.)
  A3. The ambient, grouping and insulation factors are applied as a product; C_g is not looked up from the number of grouped circuits. (The count is carried to the sheet and warns when C_g = 1 with more than one circuit.)
  A4. The optional derived C_a is an approximation for the temperature difference only and is labelled as such; compare it with the licensed table. (Derived, not tabulated.)
  A5. C_c = 0.725 applies to a semi-enclosed fuse only: 1.45 divided by an assumed fusing factor of 2. (Derived.)
  A6. AWG areas follow the geometry d = 0.127·92^((36 − n)/39) mm; no AWG capacity is supplied. (Derived.)
  A7. DC circuits use I_b = P/U; the power factor and efficiency are not applied. (Method.)
  A8. Machine wiring context (BS EN 60204-1): the conductor is sized for the protective device rating; the capacities and factors are the user's records for the installation method stated. (The context changes citations only; the numbers are the same in both contexts.)

4. WORKING
  [13] I_b = P / (sqrt(3) * U * cos_phi * eta)
      = 7.5 kW / (sqrt(3) × 400 V × 0.85 × 0.9)
      = 14.151 A
        Design current (three-phase)
  [14] I_n = smallest preferred rating with I_n ≥ I_b    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 7.2
      = I_b = 14.151 A
      = 16 A
        Rated current of the protective device (ratings record)
  [15] C_c = 1 (not a semi-enclosed fuse)    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 12.4
      = MCB type C
      = 1
        Semi-enclosed fuse factor
  [20] I_t_req = I_n / (C_a * C_g * C_i * C_c)    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 12.4
      = 16 A / (0.94 × 0.8 × 1 × 1)
      = 21.277 A
        Required tabulated capacity
  [21] I_t = I_t of the selected row of the ampacity record    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 12.4
      = I_t,req = 21.277 A
      = 28 A
        Tabulated current-carrying capacity of the selected conductor
  [22] CSA = smallest cross-section in the ampacity record with I_t ≥ I_t,req    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 12.4
      = I_t,req = 21.277 A
      = 4 mm²
        Selected conductor cross-section
  [23] I_z = I_t * C_a * C_g * C_i    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 12.4
      = 28 A × 0.94 × 0.8 × 1
      = 21.056 A
        Current-carrying capacity in the installed conditions
  [24] u_Ib = I_b / I_n    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 7.2; BS 7671 reg. 433.1.1
      = 14.151 A / 16 A
      = 0.88442
        Design current utilisation of the device
  [25] u_In = I_n / I_z    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 7.2; BS 7671 reg. 433.1.1
      = 16 A / 21.056 A
      = 0.75988
        Device rating utilisation of the conductor
  [26] I2 = 1.45 * I_n    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 7.2; BS 7671 reg. 433.1.1
      = 1.45 × 16 A
      = 23.2 A
        Conventional operating current (inherent 1.45 I_n)
  [27] u_I2 = I2 / (1.45 * I_z)    « BS EN 60204-1 cl. 7.2; BS 7671 reg. 433.1.1
      = 23.2 A / (1.45 × 21.056 A)
      = 0.75988
        I₂ utilisation of 1.45 I_z
