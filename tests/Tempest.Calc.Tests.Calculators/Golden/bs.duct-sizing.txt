Title: Duct sizing

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Sizes each duct section by the equal-friction method (a target friction rate) or the velocity method (a target velocity), rounds to standard sizes, and reports velocity, friction rate, friction and fitting losses per section and the total pressure drop along the index run, with warnings against user velocity and pressure-drop limits.
Round duct diameter from the design target: D by Colebrook with the air properties at T (equal friction), or D = √(4Q/πv) (velocity), rounded up to the next standard size. Rectangular duct of equal friction and capacity: D_e = 1.30 (ab)^0.625/(a + b)^0.25. Flat-oval duct of equal friction and capacity: D_e = 1.55 A^0.625/P^0.25, with A = πb²/4 + b(a − b) and P = πb + 2(a − b). Velocity method, rectangular or flat-oval duct with side a fixed: b from the free area A = Q/v (rectangle b = A/a; flat oval the smaller root of (1 − π/4)b² − ab + A = 0), rounded up; D_e is kept for the friction only. Friction loss per metre, at the velocity v_e of the equivalent round duct: Δp/L = f ρ v_e²/(2D_e). Fitting losses, at the actual velocity: Δp = ΣC ρ v²/2. Index-run totals, summed over every section of the run: Δp_run = Σ(Δp/L · L + ΣC ρ v²/2).
  Ref: ASHRAE Handbook — Fundamentals Ch. 21 — Duct design (friction chart basis, equivalent diameters, loss coefficients)
  Ref: CIBSE Guide C Section 4 — Flow of fluids in pipes and ducts
  Ref: BS EN 1505 — Ventilation for buildings: sheet metal air ducts and fittings with rectangular cross-section, dimensions
  Ref: BS EN 1506 — Ventilation for buildings: sheet metal air ducts and fittings with circular cross-section, dimensions

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Air is dry at the stated temperature and pressure (ρ = p/(287.055 T)); viscosity by Sutherland's law. (Standard air properties.)
  A2. Rectangular and flat-oval ducts are sized through the equivalent diameter for equal friction and capacity. (Huebscher / Heyt–Diaz relations as given in ASHRAE Fundamentals Ch. 21.)
  A3. Fitting loss coefficients C are user-supplied (manufacturer or ASHRAE/CIBSE data, not embedded) and refer to the section's velocity pressure. (Non-negotiable: no standard tables embedded.)
