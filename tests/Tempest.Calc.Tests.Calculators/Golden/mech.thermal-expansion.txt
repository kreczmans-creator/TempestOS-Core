Title: Thermal expansion of a restrained bar

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
A bar heated or cooled between two restraints: its free movement, the part closed by a gap before the restraint engages, the restraint force for a rigid or an elastic restraint, the axial stress that results and the movement that actually occurs, with the stress against an allowable.
Free expansion of the bar: δ_free = α·L·ΔT. Expansion actually restrained, once any gap g has closed: δ_e = sign(δ_free)·max(|δ_free| − g, 0). Axial stiffness of the bar: k_bar = E·A/L. Restraint force, from rigid supports or an elastic restraint k_s: F = δ_e·k_bar (rigid); F = δ_e/(1/k_bar + 1/k_s) (elastic). Thermal stress in the bar, compressive on heating: σ = −F/A. Net movement of the bar end: δ = δ_free − F/k_bar.
  Ref: Roark's Formulas for Stress and Strain Ch. 15 — Thermal stresses
  Ref: Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design §3-17 — Thermal stresses and strains

2. INPUTS
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  L | Bar length | 2 | m | manual | 0.01 … 1000 m
  A | Cross-section area | 500 | mm^2 | manual | 1 … 1000000 mm^2
  E | Modulus of elasticity | 200 | GPa | manual | 1 … 500 GPa
  alpha | Coefficient of thermal expansion | 0.000012 | 1/K | manual | 1e-7 … 0.0001 1/K
  dT | Temperature change (positive heating) | 60 | K | manual | -300 … 1000 K
  g | Gap closed before the restraint engages | 0 | mm | manual | 0 … 1000 mm
  sigma_allow | Allowable stress | 150 | MPa | manual | 1 … 2000 MPa

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. The expansion coefficient is constant over the temperature change and the bar stays elastic. (Linear thermoelasticity.)
  A2. The bar is uniformly heated and does not buckle under the compressive force. (Check buckling separately (struct.column-buckling).)

4. WORKING
  [8] delta_free = alpha * L * dT    « Shigley §3-17
      = 0.000012 1/K × 2 m × 60 K
      = 1.44 mm
        Free thermal movement (positive on heating)
  [9] delta_e = delta_free - g
      = 1.44 mm − 0 mm
      = 1.44 mm
        Movement after the gap closes (engaged movement)
  [10] k_bar = E * A / L
      = 200 GPa × 500 mm² / 2 m
      = 50 kN/mm
        Axial stiffness of the bar
  [11] F = delta_e * k_bar    « Roark Ch. 15
      = 1.44 mm × 50 kN/mm
      = 72000 N
        Restraint force (rigid restraint)
  [12] sigma = -F / A
      = −72000 N / 500 mm²
      = -144 MPa
        Axial stress (negative compressive)
  [13] delta = delta_free - F / k_bar
      = 1.44 mm − 72000 N / 50 kN/mm
      = 0 mm
        Actual movement
  [14] u = abs(sigma) / sigma_allow
      = abs(-144 MPa) / 150 MPa
      = 0.96
        Stress utilisation

5. RESULTS
  delta_free = 1.44 mm    Free movement
  delta_e = 1.44 mm    Engaged movement
  k_bar = 50 kN/mm    Bar stiffness
  F = 72000 N    Restraint force
  sigma = -144 MPa    Axial stress
  delta = 0 mm    Actual movement
  u = 0.96    Stress utilisation
