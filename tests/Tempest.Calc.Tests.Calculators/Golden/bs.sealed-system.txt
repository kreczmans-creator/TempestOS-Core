Title: Sealed heating system

1. Scope and method
Calculates the catalogue output each room's emitter needs at the design flow, return and room temperatures, and checks it against the selected emitters. It also gives the minimum expansion vessel for the system volume, temperatures, static head and pressures.
Emitter temperature difference: ΔT = (T_flow + T_return)/2 − T_room, or the log mean (T_flow − T_return)/ln((T_flow − T_room)/(T_return − T_room)) Output at design: Φ = Φ50·(ΔT/50)^n; required catalogue output Φ50 = Φ_req/(ΔT/50)^n System volume: V_s = Σ V_em + V_pipe + V_plant Water expansion: e_x = ρ(T_fill)/ρ(T_max) − 1 (water density by Kell 1975), or a glycol record Vessel pre-charge: p_i = ρ·g·h_static + p_margin (at least the static head) Vessel size: V_e = e_x·V_s/[1 − (p_i + 1)/(p_f + 1)] (pressures in bar gauge, + 1 bar for absolute); the expansion volume only, sized for the higher of T_max and T_flow; a water reserve and a safety-valve allowance are not included

3. Assumptions
  A1. The system is a closed LTHW system below 100 °C. (Catalogue ratings at 75/65/20 °C.)
  A2. Emitter output follows Φ50·(ΔT/50)^n with the manufacturer's n. (Catalogue ratings at 75/65/20 °C.)
  A3. Water density comes from the Kell (1975) correlation, within about 0.01 % of IAPWS-95 at atmospheric pressure. (Formula, not a table.)
  A4. Glycol mixtures take the expansion coefficient e from a record. (Formula, not a table.)
  A5. Gauge pressures are made absolute by adding 1 bar, as the vessel relation is usually written. (Within 1.3 % of the standard atmosphere.)
  A6. V_e is the expansion volume by the CIBSE heating guide's relation only. (Stated omission.)
  A7. V_e does not include a water reserve for system losses. (Stated omission; add it when the vessel is selected, from the heating-system standard in use.)
  A8. V_e does not include a margin between the final pressure and the safety valve setting beyond p_f below p_prv. (Stated omission; add it when the vessel is selected, from the heating-system standard in use.)

4. Working
  [14] dT = (T_flow + T_return) / 2 - T_room    « BS EN 442-2 cl. 5
      = (75 degC + 65 degC) / 2 − 20 degC
      = 50 K
        Mean water-to-room temperature difference
  [15] dT_fr = T_flow - T_return
      = 75 degC − 65 degC
      = 10 K
        Flow-to-return temperature difference (what the water gives up across the emitters)
  [16] T_m = (T_flow + T_return) / 2
      = (75 degC + 65 degC) / 2
      = 70 degC
        Mean water temperature, (flow + return)/2
  [17] f_dT = (dT / 50 K)^n
      = (50 K / 50 K)^1.3
      = 1
        Output correction from ΔT 50 K
  [18] R1 = Φ_req/f_dT
      = 1500 / 1
      = 1.5 kW
        Lounge: catalogue output needed; selected 1600 W gives 1600 W (utilisation 0.938)
  [19] Phi_tot = Σ Φ_req
      = 1500 W
      = 1.5 kW
        Total heat required by the rooms
  [20] V_em = Σ V_em
      = 8 L
      = 8 L
        Emitter water content
  [21] V_s = V_em + V_pipe + V_plant
      = 8 L + 20 L + 10 L
      = 38 L
        System water volume
  [22] rho_fill = ρ(T_fill), Kell 1975
      = T = 10 °C
      = 999.7 kg/m³
        Water density at fill
  [23] rho_max = ρ(T_max), Kell 1975
      = T = 85 °C
      = 968.62 kg/m³
        Water density at maximum temperature
  [24] e_x = rho_fill / rho_max - 1
      = 999.7 kg/m³ / 968.62 kg/m³ − 1
      = 0.032086
        Expansion coefficient of water
  [25] p_st = rho_fill * 9.80665 m/s^2 * h_static
      = 999.7 kg/m³ × 9.80665 m/s^2 × 5 m
      = 49.019 kPa
        Static head at the vessel
  [26] p_i = p_st + p_margin
      = 49.019 kPa + 30 kPa
      = 79.019 kPa
        Pre-charge (initial) pressure, gauge
  [27] V_e = e_x * V_s / (1 - (p_i + 1 bar) / (p_f + 1 bar))    « CIBSE Guide B1 §1.4
      = 0.032086 × 38 L / (1 − (79.019 kPa + 1 bar) / (250 kPa + 1 bar))
      = 2.4959 L
        Minimum expansion vessel volume
  [28] Phi_req_gov = Φ_req of the governing room
      = the room with the highest Φ_req/(Φ50·f_dT)
      = 1.5 kW
        Heat required by the governing room
  [29] Phi_out_gov = Φ50·f_dT of the governing room
      = its selected emitter at the design temperatures
      = 1.6 kW
        Output of the selected emitter of the governing room at the design temperatures
  [30] u_emit = max over rooms Φ_req/(Φ50·f_dT)
      = worst room
      = 0.9375
        Emitter utilisation (governing room)
