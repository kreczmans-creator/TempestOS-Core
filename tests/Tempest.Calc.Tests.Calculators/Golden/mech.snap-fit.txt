Title: Snap-fits

1. Scope and method
Calculates the strain, deflection force and joining forces of a plastic snap-fit: a cantilever lug, an annular ring or a torsion bar. It checks the strain against the permissible strain and whether the joint is self-locking.
Cantilever geometry (Euler–Bernoulli): y = K_y·ε·l²/h with K_y = 2/3 (constant section; the design guides print the rounded 0.67); a linear taper of the thickness to r_h = h_tip/h gives K_y = 2·J/φ_max with J = [2r_h − r_h²/2 − ln r_h − 3/2]/(1 − r_h)³ and φ_max = 1 for r_h ≥ 1/2 (otherwise the strain peaks inside the beam at t = (2a − 1)/a, a = 1 − r_h, with φ_max = (1 − t)/(1 − a·t)²); a linear taper of the width to r_b gives K_y = 2·J with J = [1/2 − 2r_b + 3r_b²/2 − r_b²·ln r_b]/(1 − r_b)³ Strain and force of the cantilever: ε = y·h/(K_y·l²); F_defl = b·h²·Es·ε/(6·l·φ_max) = (b·h²/6)·(Es·ε/l) at the root of a constant section; Es is the secant modulus at ε (typed, or interpolated from the W3 points Es(ε)) Annular (derived, free thin ring): nominal hoop strain ε_nom = ΔD/D (the mean diameter D widened by the diametral interference ΔD); the strain checked is the larger of ε = K_ann·ε_nom (K_ann the user's strain factor) and the inner-surface strain ΔD/(D − h); the radial contact force summed round the ring is F_defl = K_F·2π·Es·ε_nom·h·b (hoop force Es·ε_nom·h·b over the radius, K_F the user's optional force factor, 1 if none), b being the effective length of a free ring Torsion bar of radius r with a lever of length a deflected y: φ = y/a; the bar is held at one end (free length l_eff = l) or at both ends with the lever in the middle (l_eff = l/2, two halves in parallel); γ = φ·r/l_eff, equivalent tensile strain ε = γ/(1 + ν), γ_perm = ε_perm·(1 + ν); G = Es/(2(1 + ν)), T = n·G·(π r⁴/2)·φ/l_eff with n = 1 or 2, F_defl = T/a (both ends: F_defl = 4·G·J·φ/(l·a)) Joining forces: W = F_defl·(μ + tan α)/(1 − μ·tan α) on the entry face (angle α to the joining direction) and W′ = F_defl·(μ + tan α′)/(1 − μ·tan α′) on the return face (α′); the joint is self-locking, with an infinite separation force, when α′ ≥ arctan(1/μ); assembly jams when μ·tan α ≥ 1 Utilisation and permissible deflection: u = ε/ε_perm (FAIL above 1); y_perm = K_y·ε_perm·l²/h (annular ΔD_perm = ε_perm·min(D/K_ann, D − h); torsion y_perm = γ_perm·l_eff·a/r)

3. Assumptions
  A1. The cantilever follows Euler–Bernoulli beam theory, with a rigid root, a linear taper and small deflection. (Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (derived); the guide's closed forms for the constant section are to be checked against the owner's copy.)
  A2. The secant modulus is constant over the whole beam, at the design strain. (One modulus stands for the stress–strain curve.)
  A3. The strain is the nominal surface strain at the critical section, with no local concentration from the root fillet. (The geometry factors are derived by integration, and the 0.67 of the design guides is the constant-section 2/3 rounded.)
  A4. Es and the permissible strain are the plastic's values for the temperature, loading and moisture of use. (No plastics data are shipped, so they come from the plastic maker's data or a material record.)
  A5. The annular snap is a free, thin, closed ring of effective axial length b. (Derived from ring geometry, not a relation of the design guide, so compare it with the owner's copy of the guide.)
  A6. The nominal hoop strain is the diametral interference over the mean diameter, ΔD/D. (Derived from ring geometry.)
  A7. The strain checked is the larger of K_ann times the nominal strain and the inner-surface strain ΔD/(D − h). (K_ann is the user's strain factor (record snapfit.annular-factor, no default), and the inner-surface strain is slightly above the mean-diameter value.)
  A8. The contact force is the hoop force Es·ε_nom·h·b taken round the ring, times the optional force factor K_F. (K_F is the user's record (blank is 1), and a tube behind the ring stiffens the joint, which only K_F can represent.)
  A9. The torsion bar is solid and round, loaded at a lever end, with a small angle φ = y/a. (γ = φ·r/l and γ_perm ≈ ε_perm·(1 + ν) are the brief's design-guide relations, to be checked against the owner's copy.)
  A10. The bar is held at one end, or at both ends with the lever in the middle. (Held at both ends it has twice the strain and four times the stiffness of one end for the same total length, and the two-ended case is derived.)
  A11. The permissible shear strain is ε_perm·(1 + ν), and the equivalent tensile strain is γ/(1 + ν). (G = Es/(2(1 + ν)) uses the secant modulus at that strain.)
  A12. The mating part is rigid, with Coulomb friction μ on the inclined face (α for assembly, α′ for separation). (Friction on an inclined plane, with the friction coefficient the user's record.)
  A13. The deflection force is taken at the design deflection, with no creep, stress relaxation or impact effect in service. (A static joint model.)
  A14. Es and the permissible strain are the plastic's values at 23 °C, the temperature of use. The sheet cannot check them, or the moisture state of the part. (Recorded temperature of use.)

4. Working
  [10] l_h = l / h
      = 15 mm / 2 mm
      = 7.5
        Slenderness of the beam l/h
  [11] phi_max = 1    « Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (derived)
      = —
      = 1
        Largest value of the strain shape along the beam (the root)
  [12] K_y = 2/3    « Euler–Bernoulli beam theory (derived)
      = constant section
      = 0.66667
        Deflection factor: y = K_y·ε·l²/h (derived; the design guides print the rounded 0.67)
  [13] eps_max = y * h / (K_y * l^2)    « Covestro, Snap-Fit Joints for Plastics: A Design Guide
      = 1 mm × 2 mm / (0.66667 × (15 mm)^2)
      = 1.3333 %
        Maximum surface strain at the critical section (nominal)
  [16] eps_perm = eps_perm_once    « Covestro, Snap-Fit Joints for Plastics: A Design Guide
      = 0.04
      = 4 %
        Permissible strain (one-time joining, as entered)
  [19] F_defl = b * h^2 * Es * eps_max / (6 * l * phi_max)    « Covestro, Snap-Fit Joints for Plastics: A Design Guide
      = 6 mm × (2 mm)^2 × 2400 MPa × 1.3333 % / (6 × 15 mm × 1)
      = 8.5333 N
        Deflection force at the tip: F_defl = (b·h²/6)·(Es·ε/l) at the root of a constant section
  [20] y_perm = K_y * eps_perm * l^2 / h    « Covestro, Snap-Fit Joints for Plastics: A Design Guide
      = 0.66667 × 4 % × (15 mm)^2 / 2 mm
      = 3 mm
        Permissible tip deflection
  [21] strain_util = eps_max / eps_perm
      = 1.3333 % / 4 %
      = 0.33333
        Strain / permissible strain
  [22] tan_a = tan(alpha)
      = tan(30 deg)
      = 0.57735
        tan α of the entry face
  [23] mu_tan_a = mu * tan_a
      = 0.3 × 0.57735
      = 0.17321
        μ·tan α of the entry face
  [24] assembly_force = F_defl * (mu + tan_a) / (1 - mu_tan_a)    « Covestro, Snap-Fit Joints for Plastics: A Design Guide
      = 8.5333 N × (0.3 + 0.57735) / (1 − 0.17321)
      = 9.0551 N
        Assembly force W = F_defl·(μ + tan α)/(1 − μ·tan α)
  [25] alpha_lock = arctan(1/μ)    « Covestro, Snap-Fit Joints for Plastics: A Design Guide
      = arctan(1/0.3)
      = 73.301 deg
        Return angle above which the joint is self-locking (π/2 when μ = 0)
  [26] tan_ar = tan(alpha_r)
      = tan(60 deg)
      = 1.7321
        tan α′ of the return face
  [27] retention_force = F_defl * (mu + tan_ar) / (1 - mu * tan_ar)    « Covestro, Snap-Fit Joints for Plastics: A Design Guide
      = 8.5333 N × (0.3 + 1.7321) / (1 − 0.3 × 1.7321)
      = 36.096 N
        Separation force W′ = F_defl·(μ + tan α′)/(1 − μ·tan α′)
