Title: Beam formulas (single span)

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Closed-form reactions, maximum moment and its position, maximum shear and maximum deflection for the standard single-span beam cases, as a quick check. Left end at x = 0; loads downward; sagging moments positive; deflection downward positive.
Formulas from Roark Table 8.1, in this sheet's own notation, for the chosen support and load case. Simply supported beam, point load at a (b = L − a), reaction and largest moment: R_A = Pb/L; M_max = Pab/L. Simply supported beam, point load at a, largest deflection when a ≥ b: δ_max = Pb(L² − b²)^(3/2)/(9√3 L EI) at x = √((L² − b²)/3). Propped cantilever, uniform load, reaction and moments: R_B = 3wL/8; M_A = −wL²/8; sagging M_max = 9wL²/128 at x = 5L/8.
  Ref: Roark's Formulas for Stress and Strain Ch. 8, Table 8.1 — Shear, moment, slope and deflection formulas for elastic straight beams

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Prismatic, linear elastic, Euler–Bernoulli beam; small deflections. (Roark Table 8.1 conditions.)
  A2. A cantilever is fixed at the left end (x = 0) and free at the right. (Case convention.)
