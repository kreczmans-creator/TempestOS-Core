Title: Beam formulas (single span)

1. Scope and method
Calculates reactions, maximum moment and shear, and maximum deflection of a single-span beam for the standard support and load cases. Left end at x = 0; loads downward; sagging moments and downward deflections positive.
Formula source: Roark Table 8.1, in this sheet's own notation, for the chosen support and load case. Simply supported beam, point load at a (b = L − a), reaction and largest moment: R_A = F b / L; M_max = F a b / L. Simply supported beam, point load at a, largest deflection when a ≥ b: δ_max = F b (L² − b²)^(3/2) / (9√3 L EI) at x = √((L² − b²)/3). Propped cantilever, uniform load, reaction and moments: R_B = 3wL/8; M_A = −wL²/8; sagging M_max = 9wL²/128 at x = 5L/8.

2. Inputs
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  case | Case | simply supported, UDL |  | manual | any
  L | Span | 6 | m | manual | 0.1 … 100 m
  I | Second moment of area | 8000 | cm⁴ | manual | 1 … 100000000 cm⁴
  E | Young's modulus | 210 | GPa | manual | 1 … 500 GPa
  w | Distributed load, downward (optional; blank = not used by the point-load and couple cases) | 10 | kN/m | manual | 0 … 10000 kN/m
  F | Point load, downward (optional; blank = not used by the distributed-load and couple cases) | 50 | kN | manual | 0 … 100000 kN
  M_0 | Applied couple (optional; blank = not used by the other cases) | 20 | kN·m | manual | 0 … 1000000 kN·m
  a | Load position from the left end (optional; blank = not used by the distributed-load cases) | 3 | m | manual | 0 … 100 m

3. Assumptions
  A1. The beam is prismatic, linear elastic and Euler–Bernoulli, with small deflections. (Roark Table 8.1 conditions.)
  A2. A cantilever is fixed at the left end (x = 0) and free at the right. (Case convention.)

4. Working
  [5] EI = E * I
      = 210 GPa × 8000 cm⁴
      = 16800 kN·m²
        Flexural rigidity
  [7] R_A = w * L / 2    « Roark 8.1 2e
      = 10 kN/m × 6 m / 2
      = 30 kN
        Left reaction
  [8] R_B = w * L / 2
      = 10 kN/m × 6 m / 2
      = 30 kN
        Right reaction
  [9] M_max = w * L^2 / 8
      = 10 kN/m × (6 m)^2 / 8
      = 45 kN·m
        Maximum (sagging) moment
  [10] x_M = L / 2
      = 6 m / 2
      = 3 m
        Position of the maximum moment
  [11] M_min = 0 kN*m
      = 0 kN·m
        Minimum moment
  [12] V_max = w * L / 2
      = 10 kN/m × 6 m / 2
      = 30 kN
        Maximum shear
  [13] delta_max = 5 * w * L^4 / (384 * EI)
      = 5 × 10 kN/m × (6 m)^4 / (384 × 16800 kN·m²)
      = 10.045 mm
        Maximum deflection
  [14] x_delta = L / 2
      = 6 m / 2
      = 3 m
        Position of the maximum deflection
