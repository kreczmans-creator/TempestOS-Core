Title: O-ring and seal gland

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Squeeze, stretch, gland fill and extrusion gap of a radial piston, radial rod or face O-ring seal, from the ring and groove dimensions with their tolerances, at the cold and shrunk and at the hot and swollen end of the temperature and swell range. The ring's cross-section shrinks with stretch by a relation you choose (none, constant-volume rubber, the quadratic cited from Parker ORD 5700 section 3.5, or linear in stretch). Worst case and a seeded Monte Carlo both run through the tolerance-stack engine of M1; the Monte Carlo reports the fraction outside each limit. Measured groove distributions (from the scan import) can replace the toleranced dimensions. Ring sizes, the squeeze and fill bands and the extrusion limit are your own records: no O-ring size table, gland table or extrusion chart is shipped.
Stretch: X = (g·D_ref)/(f·d) − 1, D_ref the diameter the ring ID sits on (groove root for a piston or face seal, rod for a rod seal); f = (1 + α(T − 20 °C))(1 + swell)^(1/3) the ring and g = 1 + α_m(T − 20 °C) the groove metal Cross-section: W_eff = f·W·(1 − r(X)), r(X) the chosen relation (none; 1 − 1/√(1+X); Y = 0.56 + 0.59X − 0.0046X² per cent as cited; ν·X) Depth and gap: piston h = (D_s − D_g)/2, gap = D_s − D_c; rod h = (D_g − D_s)/2, gap = D_c − D_s; face h and gap entered directly (all times g) Squeeze: q = 1 − g·h/W_eff, minimum at the cold end from the largest depth over the smallest W_eff, maximum at the hot end Gland fill: (π/4)·W²/(g·b·g·h − 2(1 − π/4)(g·r_g)²), maximum at the hot end on the unstretched section, minimum at the cold end on W_eff Worst case: each range from the engine's worst-case stack of the dimension (Σ|coefficient|·half-band), combined at the corner that is worst for the quantity Monte Carlo: each criterion linearised about the mean dimensions (secant slope across each band), sampled by the engine's seeded Monte Carlo; the fraction below the lower and above the upper limit Extrusion: the largest gap against the permitted gap from your record (interpolated in hardness and pressure) or the value entered
  Ref: Parker O-Ring Handbook ORD 5700 Section 3.5 — Cross-section reduction with stretch (relation quoted in Addendum B; not confirmed)
  Ref: Parker PTD 5705 — O-ring installation guidance (stretch rule quoted in Addendum B; not confirmed)
  Ref: ISO 3601-1 — O-rings: inside diameters, cross-sections, tolerances and size codes
  Ref: ISO 3601-2 — O-rings: housing dimensions for general applications

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. The gland is a plain rectangular groove with a bottom corner radius; the ring is a circle of the stated cross-section. Edge breaks, groove wall angles and surface finish are not modelled. (Closed-form gland geometry; no gland table.)
  A2. Squeeze is the compressed fraction of the effective cross-section W_eff, which is the nominal section after thermal expansion, swell and the cross-section reduction of the stretch. The reduction relation is chosen by the user; the quadratic of Parker ORD 5700 section 3.5 is implemented as quoted in Addendum B and is not confirmed against the handbook. (The relation is an input choice; the default is the constant-volume rubber relation, derived.)
  A3. The cold end is the lowest temperature with the least swell and sets the minimum squeeze and fill; the hot end is the highest temperature with the most swell and sets the maximum. This assumes the ring expands more than the groove metal (alpha above alpha_m). (Bounding cases of a monotonic model.)
  A4. The worst case combines each dimension at its extreme independently. Where one dimension appears twice (the groove root diameter sets both the depth and the stretch of a piston seal) the worst case is slightly conservative. (Independent ends of linear stacks.)
  A5. The Monte Carlo samples each dimension from its own distribution about its mean and evaluates each criterion on its first-order (secant) linearisation. It counts the fraction outside each limit at the cold or hot end; it does not model pressure, friction or compression set. (Linear model on the engine's seeded sampler.)
  A6. The extrusion gap is the largest diametral clearance of a radial seal (the axial gap of a face seal) at installation temperature. The permitted gap is the user's record; the sheet never states a handbook value. (Extrusion limits are licensed data and are not shipped.)
  A7. Static duty: the squeeze band for static seals from the oring.criteria record is used. (The bands are the user's record.)
