Title: Tolerance stack-up (1D)

1. Scope and method
Calculates the worst-case, root-sum-square and modified RSS limits of a linear tolerance loop, with the predicted spread and capability. It adds a contribution Pareto and a Monte Carlo prediction against a stated requirement.
Worst case, every contributor at its limit: T_wc = Σ|half-bands|. Root sum of squares, the statistical stack: T_rss = √Σ half². Modified RSS, a factor on the RSS (Bender-style): T_mrss = factor × RSS. Predicted standard deviation of the stack, from each contributor's distribution: σ = √Σ σ_i². Process capability, taken from the nearer limit: C_pk = min(USL − μ, μ − LSL)/(3σ). Monte Carlo check, every contributor sampled from a fixed seed and summed with its sign: stack = Σ s_i x_i.

3. Assumptions
  A1. Every contributor acts along the one axis of the stack. (A linear stack: no angular or datum-shift effects beyond what the user enters as a contributor.)
  A2. Contributors vary independently about their mid-limit and any stated mean shift, each with its stated distribution. (The worst case is a guarantee; every statistical figure is a prediction about a population.)
  A3. A position or profile contributor contributes ± half its tolerance zone, plus half the stated MMC bonus. (Diametral zones are bilateral about the true position.)
