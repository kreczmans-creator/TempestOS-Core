Title: Lifting lug and pin joint

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
A pinned lug between two cheek plates under a load along the lug: tension on the net section through the hole, bearing of the pin on the hole, shear tear-out beyond the hole, and the pin in double shear and in bending, each against an allowable stress; the largest utilisation governs.
Tension across the net section beside the hole: σ_t = P/((W − d_h)·t). Bearing of the pin on the lug: σ_br = P/(d_p·t). Tear-out shear beyond the hole, on two planes: τ_to = P/(2·a·t). Pin shear, in double shear: τ_p = P/(2·π·d_p²/4). Pin bending, from the lever arm across the lug, the gap and the shackle: M = (P/2)·(t/4 + g + t_s/2); σ_b = 32·M/(π·d_p³). Utilisation of each mode, its stress over the allowable from the material and design category in force (the sheet adds no design factor of its own): u = σ/σ_allow.
  Ref: ASME BTH-1 Ch. 3 — Pinned connections
  Ref: Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design Ch. 3 — Shear, bearing and bending stresses

2. INPUTS
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  P | Load on the lug (along the lug) | 50 | kN | manual | 0.01 … 100000 kN
  t | Lug thickness | 20 | mm | manual | 1 … 500 mm
  W | Lug width at the hole | 100 | mm | manual | 5 … 2000 mm
  d_h | Hole diameter | 32 | mm | manual | 2 … 1000 mm
  a | Edge distance, hole edge to lug end (load direction) | 40 | mm | manual | 1 … 2000 mm
  d_p | Pin diameter | 30 | mm | manual | 2 … 1000 mm
  t_s | Cheek plate thickness (each) | 12 | mm | manual | 1 … 500 mm
  g | Clearance, lug face to cheek plate (each side) | 2 | mm | manual | 0 … 100 mm
  f_t | Allowable tensile stress, lug | 150 | MPa | manual | 1 … 2000 MPa
  f_br | Allowable bearing stress, lug | 200 | MPa | manual | 1 … 3000 MPa
  f_v | Allowable shear stress, lug | 90 | MPa | manual | 1 … 2000 MPa
  f_b_pin | Allowable bending stress, pin | 250 | MPa | manual | 1 … 3000 MPa
  f_v_pin | Allowable shear stress, pin | 150 | MPa | manual | 1 … 2000 MPa

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. The load acts along the lug's axis through the hole centre. (No out-of-plane or side load.)
  A2. The pin bears uniformly over the lug thickness and over each cheek plate; each cheek plate reacts half the load. (Pin-bending idealisation for a lug between two cheek plates.)
  A3. A close-fitting pin (d_p ≥ 0.9·d_h). (Uniform-bearing idealisation.)

4. WORKING
  [14] k_fit = d_p / d_h
      = 30 mm / 32 mm
      = 0.9375
        Pin-to-hole ratio
  [15] A_net = (W - d_h) * t
      = (100 mm − 32 mm) × 20 mm
      = 1360 mm²
        Net section through the hole
  [16] sigma_t = P / A_net    « ASME BTH-1 Ch. 3
      = 50 kN / 1360 mm²
      = 36.765 MPa
        Net-section tension
  [17] A_br = d_p * t
      = 30 mm × 20 mm
      = 600 mm²
        Bearing area
  [18] sigma_br = P / A_br
      = 50 kN / 600 mm²
      = 83.333 MPa
        Bearing of the pin on the hole
  [19] A_to = 2 * a * t
      = 2 × 40 mm × 20 mm
      = 1600 mm²
        Tear-out area (two planes beyond the hole)
  [20] tau_to = P / A_to
      = 50 kN / 1600 mm²
      = 31.25 MPa
        Tear-out shear
  [21] A_p = 2 * pi * d_p^2 / 4
      = 2 × π × (30 mm)^2 / 4
      = 1413.7 mm²
        Pin area in double shear
  [22] tau_p = P / A_p
      = 50 kN / 1413.7 mm²
      = 35.368 MPa
        Pin shear
  [23] M_p = P / 2 * (t / 4 + g + t_s / 2)
      = 50 kN / 2 × (20 mm / 4 + 2 mm + 12 mm / 2)
      = 325 N·m
        Pin bending moment (uniform bearing on lug and cheek plates)
  [24] Z_p = pi * d_p^3 / 32
      = π × (30 mm)^3 / 32
      = 2650.7 mm³
        Pin section modulus
  [25] sigma_b = M_p / Z_p
      = 325 N·m / 2650.7 mm³
      = 122.61 MPa
        Pin bending stress
  [26] u_t = sigma_t / f_t
      = 36.765 MPa / 150 MPa
      = 0.2451
        Net-section utilisation
  [27] u_br = sigma_br / f_br
      = 83.333 MPa / 200 MPa
      = 0.41667
        Bearing utilisation
  [28] u_to = tau_to / f_v
      = 31.25 MPa / 90 MPa
      = 0.34722
        Tear-out utilisation
  [29] u_p = tau_p / f_v_pin
      = 35.368 MPa / 150 MPa
      = 0.23579
        Pin shear utilisation
  [30] u_b = sigma_b / f_b_pin
      = 122.61 MPa / 250 MPa
      = 0.49043
        Pin bending utilisation
