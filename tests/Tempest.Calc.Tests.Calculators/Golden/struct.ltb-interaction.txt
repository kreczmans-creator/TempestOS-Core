Title: EN 1993-1-1 LTB and N+M interaction

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Lateral-torsional buckling (6.3.2), flexural buckling (6.3.1) and the interaction of axial compression with bending (6.3.3) for a uniform doubly-symmetric I-section member on fork supports, class 1 to 3: M_cr, λ̄_LT, χ_LT and M_b,Rd; χ_y, χ_z and N_b,Rd; the Annex B (or Annex A) interaction factors and both interaction ratios, with the governing check.
Critical moment (NCCI SN003 form): M_cr = C₁·π²E·I_z/(kL)²·[√((k/k_w)²·I_w/I_z + (kL)²·G·I_t/(π²E·I_z) + (C₂z_g)²) − C₂z_g]; C₁ = 1.88 − 1.40ψ + 0.52ψ² ≤ 2.70 (end moments, k = 1) unless entered LTB slenderness: λ̄_LT = √(W_y·f_y/M_cr), W_y = W_pl,y (class 1, 2) or W_el,y (class 3) Lateral-torsional buckling, general case (6.3.2.2): Φ_LT = 0.5[1 + α_LT(λ̄_LT − 0.2) + λ̄_LT²]; χ_LT = 1/(Φ_LT + √(Φ_LT² − λ̄_LT²)) ≤ 1 Lateral-torsional buckling, rolled sections (6.3.2.3): Φ_LT = 0.5[1 + α_LT(λ̄_LT − λ̄_LT,0) + β·λ̄_LT²]; χ_LT = 1/(Φ_LT + √(Φ_LT² − β·λ̄_LT²)) ≤ 1, ≤ 1/λ̄_LT²; optional χ_LT,mod = χ_LT/f ≤ 1, ≤ 1/λ̄_LT² (eq. 6.58), f = 1 − 0.5(1 − k_c)[1 − 2(λ̄_LT − 0.8)²] ≤ 1 Buckling resistance in bending: M_b,Rd = χ_LT·W_y·f_y/γ_M1 Flexural buckling: N_cr = π²EI/L_cr², λ̄ = √(A·f_y/N_cr), Φ = 0.5[1 + α(λ̄ − 0.2) + λ̄²], χ = 1/(Φ + √(Φ² − λ̄²)) ≤ 1, N_b,Rd = χ·A·f_y/γ_M1; the curve of each axis from Table 6.2 (section type, h/b, t_f; f_y ≤ 420 MPa) or chosen Interaction (6.3.3(4)): N_Ed/(χ_y·N_Rk/γ_M1) + k_yy·M_y,Ed/(χ_LT·M_y,Rk/γ_M1) + k_yz·M_z,Ed/(M_z,Rk/γ_M1) ≤ 1; N_Ed/(χ_z·N_Rk/γ_M1) + k_zy·M_y,Ed/(χ_LT·M_y,Rk/γ_M1) + k_zz·M_z,Ed/(M_z,Rk/γ_M1) ≤ 1 Factors k_ij: Annex B from the slenderness, N_Ed/(χ·N_Rk/γ_M1) and C_m (C_m = 0.6 + 0.4ψ ≥ 0.4 for a linear diagram, or entered); Annex A from the μ, w, n_pl, a_LT and C_ij expressions (class 1, 2: plastic column of Table A.1; class 3: elastic column, without the 0.6√(w_i/w_j) factors of k_yz, k_zy)
  Ref: EN 1993-1-1:2005+A1:2014 6.3.1.2, 6.3.1.3 — Buckling curves; slenderness for flexural buckling

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Uniform doubly-symmetric I-section member on fork supports (free to warp and to rotate about the minor axis at the ends, restrained against twist); no mono-symmetric or tapered members, no cantilevers, no continuous-restraint effects. (Scope of S7; k and k_w below 1 model partial end restraint of the minor-axis rotation and of warping.)
  A2. Class 1, 2 or 3 as stated by the user; class 4 effective properties are outside this sheet. (6.2.2.5 is not implemented.)
  A3. C₁ from the ψ formula applies to end-moment diagrams with k = 1; for transverse loads, k ≠ 1 or other diagrams the user enters C₁, C₂ (and k_c, C_m) from the source in use. With C₁ entered the sheet blocks until k_c (when the f modification is used) and C_my (Annex B) are entered too, unless the diagram is given as end moments: ψ is then a real, entered moment shape. (No table of C₁ or C₂ values is shipped (reference records only).)
  A4. The imperfection factors, β and λ̄_LT,0 default to the recommended values and are a user-verifiable factor set; the National Annex in use may differ. (Record kind ec3.ltb-factors.)
  A5. The 2022 edition choice changes the label printed on the sheet only, by decision: the arithmetic implemented is that of EN 1993-1-1:2005+A1:2014, the differences between the editions are not implemented (they cannot be stated with certainty), and the clause numbers in the 2022 edition differ and must be checked by the user. (The sheet says so (warning `edition`).)
  A6. The flexural buckling curves selected automatically are those of Table 6.2 for S 235 to S 420 (rolled I: a, b for h/b > 1.2 and t_f ≤ 40 mm, b, c above 40 mm or h/b ≤ 1.2 up to 100 mm, d, d beyond; welded I: b, c up to 40 mm, c, d above); for f_y above 420 MPa or a combination the table does not cover the curves are chosen by hand. (Table 6.2 is a small selection rule and is not reproduced beyond it.)
  A7. The Annex A and Annex B expressions are implemented as published; their coefficients are to be checked against the owner's copy of the standard. (Interaction factors for diagrams other than linear are entered, not derived.)
