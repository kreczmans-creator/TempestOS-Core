Title: EN 1993-1-1 LTB and N+M interaction

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Lateral-torsional buckling (6.3.2), flexural buckling (6.3.1) and the interaction of axial compression with bending (6.3.3) for a uniform doubly-symmetric I-section member on fork supports, class 1 to 3: M_cr, λ̄_LT, χ_LT and M_b,Rd; χ_y, χ_z and N_b,Rd; the Annex B (or Annex A) interaction factors and both interaction ratios, with the governing check.
Critical moment (NCCI SN003 form): M_cr = C₁·π²E·I_z/(kL)²·[√((k/k_w)²·I_w/I_z + (kL)²·G·I_t/(π²E·I_z) + (C₂z_g)²) − C₂z_g]; C₁ = 1.88 − 1.40ψ + 0.52ψ² ≤ 2.70 (end moments, k = 1) unless entered LTB slenderness: λ̄_LT = √(W_y·f_y/M_cr), W_y = W_pl,y (class 1, 2) or W_el,y (class 3) 6.3.2.2: Φ_LT = 0.5[1 + α_LT(λ̄_LT − 0.2) + λ̄_LT²]; χ_LT = 1/(Φ_LT + √(Φ_LT² − λ̄_LT²)) ≤ 1 6.3.2.3: Φ_LT = 0.5[1 + α_LT(λ̄_LT − λ̄_LT,0) + β·λ̄_LT²]; χ_LT = 1/(Φ_LT + √(Φ_LT² − β·λ̄_LT²)) ≤ 1, ≤ 1/λ̄_LT²; optional χ_LT,mod = χ_LT/f ≤ 1, f = 1 − 0.5(1 − k_c)[1 − 2(λ̄_LT − 0.8)²] ≤ 1 Buckling resistance in bending: M_b,Rd = χ_LT·W_y·f_y/γ_M1 Flexural buckling: N_cr = π²EI/L_cr², λ̄ = √(A·f_y/N_cr), Φ = 0.5[1 + α(λ̄ − 0.2) + λ̄²], χ = 1/(Φ + √(Φ² − λ̄²)) ≤ 1, N_b,Rd = χ·A·f_y/γ_M1 Interaction (6.3.3(4)): N_Ed/(χ_y·N_Rk/γ_M1) + k_yy·M_y,Ed/(χ_LT·M_y,Rk/γ_M1) + k_yz·M_z,Ed/(M_z,Rk/γ_M1) ≤ 1; N_Ed/(χ_z·N_Rk/γ_M1) + k_zy·M_y,Ed/(χ_LT·M_y,Rk/γ_M1) + k_zz·M_z,Ed/(M_z,Rk/γ_M1) ≤ 1 Factors k_ij: Annex B from the slenderness, N_Ed/(χ·N_Rk/γ_M1) and C_m (C_m = 0.6 + 0.4ψ ≥ 0.4 for a linear diagram, or entered); Annex A from the μ, w, n_pl, a_LT and C_ij expressions
  Ref: EN 1993-1-1:2005+A1:2014 6.3.1.2, 6.3.1.3 — Buckling curves; slenderness for flexural buckling
  Ref: EN 1993-1-1:2005+A1:2014 6.3.2.2 — Lateral-torsional buckling curves: general case
  Ref: EN 1993-1-1:2005+A1:2014 6.3.2.3 — Lateral-torsional buckling curves for rolled sections and equivalent welded sections

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Uniform doubly-symmetric I-section member on fork supports (free to warp and to rotate about the minor axis at the ends, restrained against twist); no mono-symmetric or tapered members, no cantilevers, no continuous-restraint effects. (Scope of S7; k and k_w below 1 model partial end restraint of the minor-axis rotation and of warping.)
  A2. Class 1, 2 or 3 as stated by the user; class 4 effective properties are outside this sheet. (6.2.2.5 is not implemented.)
  A3. C₁ from the ψ formula applies to end-moment diagrams with k = 1; for transverse loads, k ≠ 1 or other diagrams the user enters C₁, C₂ (and k_c, C_m) from the source in use. (No table of C₁ or C₂ values is shipped (reference records only).)
  A4. The imperfection factors, β and λ̄_LT,0 default to the recommended values and are a user-verifiable factor set; the National Annex in use may differ. (Record kind ec3.ltb-factors.)
  A5. The 2022 edition choice changes the label printed on the sheet only. The arithmetic implemented is that of EN 1993-1-1:2005+A1:2014; clause numbers in the 2022 edition differ and must be checked by the user. (The differences between the editions are not implemented.)
  A6. The Annex A and Annex B expressions are implemented as published; their coefficients are to be checked against the owner's copy of the standard. (Interaction factors for diagrams other than linear are entered, not derived.)
