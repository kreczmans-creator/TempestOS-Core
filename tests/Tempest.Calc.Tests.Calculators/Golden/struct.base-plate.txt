Title: Column base plate (EN 1993-1-8)

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
An unstiffened rectangular base plate under an I/H, RHS or CHS column on grouted concrete: bearing resistance, the force regime and holding-down bolt tension for a major-axis moment, the tension T-stub of the plate and the compression T-stub, shear by friction and anchor bolts, and the plate thickness that satisfies them together. The bolt tension and shear per anchor go to Hilti PROFIS for the anchorage, which is done outside this sheet.
Bearing (cl. 6.2.5): f_cd = α_cc·f_ck/γ_c; f_jd = β_j·α·f_cd, β_j = 2/3; c = t_p·√[f_y/(3·f_jd·γ_M0)] Axial (cl. 6.2.8.2): N_j,Rd = f_jd·A_eff, A_eff the bearing strips of width c round the column footprint (full rectangle when they overlap); u_N = N_c,Ed/N_j,Rd Moment (cl. 6.2.8.3, Table 6.7): z_C from the section ((h − t_f)/2, (h − t)/2, half-annulus centroid) or entered, x_b the bolt row from the axis, z = z_C + x_b; C_Ed = (M_Ed + N_Ed·x_b)/z and T_Ed = C_Ed − N_Ed when both are ≥ 0; with N_Ed > 0 and T_Ed < 0 both sides are in compression, C = N_Ed/2 ± M_Ed/(2z_C); with N_Ed < 0 and C_Ed < 0 both rows are in tension, T = −N_Ed/2 ± M_Ed/(2x_b); F_t,Ed = T_Ed/n_t at the more loaded row Plate T-stub (cl. 6.2.6.11, 6.2.4, Table 6.2): M_pl,Rd = 0.25·l_eff·t_p²·f_y/γ_M0; without prying min(2·M_pl,Rd/m, n_t·F_t,Rd); with prying the modes 4·M_pl,Rd/m, (2·M_pl,Rd + n·ΣF_t,Rd)/(m + n) with n = min(e_min, 1.25m), and n_t·F_t,Rd; l_eff is the total for the n_t bolts Compression T-stub (cl. 6.2.6.9, 6.2.6.10): C_Rd = f_jd·A_flange, checked when M_Ed > 0 and the resultant is in compression Shear (cl. 6.2.2): F_2,vb,Rd = α_bc·f_ub·A_s/γ_M2, α_bc = 0.44 − 0.0003·f_yb; F_vb,Rd = min(F_1,vb,Rd, F_2,vb,Rd); V_Rd = C_f,d·N_c,Ed + n_b·F_vb,Rd; V_b,Ed = max(V_Ed − C_f,d·N_c,Ed, 0)/n_b Required thickness: the smallest t_p in 10–100 mm with u_N, u_C and u_T all ≤ 1, z_C, C_Ed and T_Ed re-evaluated at every trial t_p
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.2 — Design resistance of column bases in shear
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.4, Table 6.2 — Equivalent T-stub in tension
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.5 — Equivalent T-stub in compression
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.6.9 — Concrete in compression including grout
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.6.10 — Base plate in bending under compression
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.6.11 — Base plate in bending under tension
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.6.12 — Anchor bolts in tension
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.8.2 — Column bases subject to axial forces
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 6.2.8.3, Table 6.7 — Column bases subject to axial force and bending moment

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. N_Ed is positive in compression here, the opposite of S3 (tension positive): a link from S2 must take the reaction R1_y (up positive, compression into the base), never the member force N_e1. (Sign convention.)
  A2. Static loading; a major-axis moment only (uniaxial); an unstiffened rectangular plate on grout of adequate strength and thickness for β_j = 2/3 (checked when the grout strength and thickness are entered). (Scope of S6.)
  A3. The simplified T-stub model: one compression resultant at the centre of the compression T-stub (flange centre, or the half-annulus centroid of a CHS) and one tension resultant per bolt row; the force regime follows EN 1993-1-8 Table 6.7. The web is not given credit in the moment check. (EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.8.3.)
  A4. The effective length l_eff is the total for the n_t bolts of a row, read from the cl. 6.2.6.5 patterns; it is used for modes 1 and 2 alike (l_eff,1 ≤ l_eff,2, so this is conservative for mode 2). (One input.)
  A5. Concrete, anchor tension, pull-out, breakout, edge and the tension–shear interaction of the anchors are designed in Hilti PROFIS from F_t,Ed and V_b,Ed per anchor; the report is filed as Evidence and referenced. With prying switched on the prying force Q is not in F_t,Ed and PROFIS must add it. (External anchorage design.)
  A6. The friction resistance counts only the compression, C_f,d·max(N_Ed, 0); a column in net tension has none. Anchor bolts share shear equally, with their holes assumed no larger than the standard clearance (oversized or slotted holes are outside this sheet). (EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.2.)
  A7. The bearing outstand c is cut back where it would pass the plate edge; the web and the column interior keep the full c. The CHS compression side is half of the annular bearing area, with its centroid as lever arm (an approximation). (Conservative against an uncut c.)
  A8. The compression T-stub is checked against the flange area alone as soon as M_Ed > 0, while at M_Ed = 0 the whole footprint (web included) resists, so the compression check jumps at M_Ed = 0⁺ (conservative). (Limitation.)
  A9. Partial factors and the friction coefficient come from the records in use (γ_c, α_cc: EN 1992-1-1 NA; γ_M2: EN 1993-1-8 NA; γ_M0: EN 1993-1-1 NA; C_f,d: the grout used); the values shown default to the EN recommended and UK values. (A small set of factors only; no table from a standard is shipped.)
  A10. β_j = 2/3 assumes the grout is at least 0.2·f_ck and no thicker than 0.2 × the smaller plate dimension; enter the grout strength and thickness to check it. (EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5.)

4. WORKING
  [31] f_cd = alpha_cc * f_ck / gamma_c    « EN 1992-1-1 cl. 3.1.6
      = 0.85 × 25 MPa / 1.5
      = 14.167 MPa
        Concrete design compressive strength
  [32] beta_j = 2/3, fixed    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5
      = grout conditions of cl. 6.2.5 assumed met
      = 0.66667
        Joint coefficient β_j
  [33] f_jd = beta_j * alpha * f_cd    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5
      = 0.66667 × 1.5 × 14.167 MPa
      = 14.167 MPa
        Design bearing strength of the joint
  [34] c = t_p * sqrt(f_y / (3 * f_jd * gamma_M0))    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5
      = 20 mm × sqrt(275 MPa / (3 × 14.167 MPa × 1))
      = 50.875 mm
        Additional bearing width round the column footprint
  [35] A_eff = 2(b + 2c)(t_f + 2c) + (h − 2t_f − 2c)(t_w + 2c)    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5
      = 2 × 357.75 × 118.75 + 124.25 × 111.75 mm²
      = 98850 mm²
        Effective bearing area
  [36] N_j_Rd = f_jd * A_eff    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.8.2, 6.2.5
      = 14.167 MPa × 98850 mm²
      = 1400.4 kN
        Axial bearing resistance of the joint
  [37] N_c_Ed = max(N_Ed, 0)
      = N_Ed = 300 kN
      = 300 kN
        Design compression in the column
  [38] u_N = N_c_Ed / N_j_Rd
      = 300 kN / 1400.4 kN
      = 0.21423
        Axial bearing utilisation
  [39] t_req_N = smallest t_p in 10–100 mm with f_jd·A_eff(c(t_p)) ≥ N_c,Ed (bisection)    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5
      = N_c,Ed = 300 kN; carried at the 10 mm minimum
      = 10 mm
        Plate thickness required for axial bearing alone
  [40] z_C = (h − t_f)/2    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.8.3
      = (260 − 17)/2 mm
      = 121.5 mm
        Compression resultant distance from the column axis
  [41] z = z_C + x_b    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.8.3
      = 121.5 + 165
      = 286.5 mm
        Lever arm between the compression resultant and the bolt row
  [42] C_Ed = (M_Ed + N_Ed·x_b)/z    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.8.3, Table 6.7
      = (100 kN·m + 300 kN × 165 mm)/286.5 mm
      = 521.82 kN
        Compression resultant under the compression flange
  [43] T_Ed = C_Ed − N_Ed    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.8.3, Table 6.7
      = 521.82 − 300
      = 221.82 kN
        Tension resultant at the bolt row
  [44] C_Ed_2 = none
      = other side
      = 0 kN
        Compression resultant under the other flange
  [45] T_Ed_2 = none
      = other row
      = 0 kN
        Tension resultant at the other bolt row
  [46] F_t_Ed = T_Ed / n_t    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.6.12
      = 221.82 kN / 2
      = 110.91 kN
        Holding-down bolt tension per bolt at the more loaded row (→ PROFIS)
  [47] A_flange = (b + 2c)(t_f + 2c), at most A_eff/2    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.6.9
      = min(357.75 × 118.75, A_eff/2) mm²
      = 42483 mm²
        Bearing area of the compression flange
  [48] C_Rd = f_jd * A_flange    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.6.9, 6.2.6.10
      = 14.167 MPa × 42483 mm²
      = 601.84 kN
        Bearing resistance of the compression flange
  [49] u_C = C_Ed / C_Rd
      = 521.82 kN / 601.84 kN
      = 0.86704
        Compression flange utilisation
  [50] M_pl_Rd = 0.25 * l_eff * t_p^2 * f_y / gamma_M0    « EN 1993-1-8 Table 6.2
      = 0.25 × 200 mm × (20 mm)^2 × 275 MPa / 1
      = 5.5 kN·m
        Plastic moment resistance of the plate over l_eff
  [51] T_Rd_1 = 2 * M_pl_Rd / m    « EN 1993-1-8 Table 6.2
      = 2 × 5.5 kN·m / 28 mm
      = 392.86 kN
        Mode 1-2 without prying forces
  [52] T_Rd_3 = n_t * F_t_Rd    « EN 1993-1-8 Table 6.2
      = 2 × 141 kN
      = 282 kN
        Mode 3: bolt failure
  [53] T_Rd = min(T_Rd_1, T_Rd_3)    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.4
      = min(392.86 kN, 282 kN)
      = 282 kN
        Tension T-stub resistance (no prying)
  [54] u_T = T_Ed / T_Rd
      = 221.82 kN / 282 kN
      = 0.78658
        Plate bending and bolts in tension, T-stub utilisation
  [55] u_bt = F_t_Ed / F_t_Rd
      = 110.91 kN / 141 kN
      = 0.78658
        Holding-down bolt tension utilisation
  [56] t_req = smallest t_p in 10–100 mm with u_N, u_C, u_T ≤ 1, with z_C, C_Ed and T_Ed recomputed at every trial t_p (scan and bisection)    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.5, 6.2.6.9, 6.2.6.11
      = N_c,Ed = 300 kN, C_Ed = 521.82 kN, T_Ed = 221.82 kN
      = 17.609 mm
        Plate thickness required
  [57] alpha_bc = 0.44 − 0.0003·f_yb    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.2
      = 0.44 − 0.0003 × 640
      = 0.248
        Anchor bolt shear factor α_bc
  [58] F_2_vb_Rd = alpha_bc * f_ub * A_s / gamma_M2    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.2
      = 0.248 × 800 MPa × 244.79 mm² / 1.25
      = 38.853 kN
        Anchor bolt shear resistance F_2,vb,Rd
  [59] F_vb_Rd = min(F_1_vb_Rd, F_2_vb_Rd)    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.2
      = min(198.8 kN, 38.853 kN)
      = 38.853 kN
        Anchor bolt shear resistance per bolt (bearing or shear)
  [60] F_f_Rd = C_fd * N_c_Ed    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.2
      = 0.2 × 300 kN
      = 60 kN
        Friction resistance, compression only
  [61] V_Rd = F_f_Rd + n_b * F_vb_Rd    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.2
      = 60 kN + 4 × 38.853 kN
      = 215.41 kN
        Shear resistance of the base
  [62] u_V = V_Ed / V_Rd
      = 80 kN / 215.41 kN
      = 0.37138
        Shear utilisation
  [63] V_b_Ed = max(V_Ed − F_f,Rd, 0)/n_b    « EN 1993-1-8 cl. 6.2.2
      = max(80 − 60, 0)/4
      = 5 kN
        Shear per anchor bolt after friction (→ PROFIS)
  [64] u_max = max(u_N, u_C, u_T, u_bt, u_V)
      = u_N = 0.21423, u_C = 0.86704, u_T = 0.78658, u_bt = 0.78658, u_V = 0.37138
      = 0.86704
        Governing utilisation

7. APPLICABILITY
  WARNING record: Tabulated values not taken from a reference record: alpha_cc, gamma_c (EN 1992-1-1 National Annex factors); gamma_M2 (EN 1993-1-8 National Annex factor); gamma_M0 (EN 1993-1-1 National Annex factor); C_fd (Friction coefficient plate to grout); f_y (Plate strength). Enter each as one so its source, edition and licence travel with it.
  WARNING f_y_thickness: f_y: one yield strength (275 MPa) is used at every plate thickness, but EN 10025-2 lowers the yield strength of a grade as the thickness passes 16 mm and again above 40 mm. t_p = 20 mm and t_req = 17.609 mm pass the first step: enter the yield strength for the plate thickness in use (steel.strengths record), so M_pl,Rd and the T-stub are not overstated.
  WARNING alpha_basis: alpha_basis: α above 1 needs the foundation geometry it rests on (A_c1/A_c0, α ≤ 3, EN 1992-1-1 cl. 6.7) stated.
  WARNING anchorage-result: u_profis: the anchorage is not assessed on this sheet. The concrete failure modes of the anchors (cone, pull-out, edge, shear, interaction), which usually govern, are designed in PROFIS; u_max covers the steel parts only, whether or not a report is attached. Enter the report's governing utilisation to include it.
