Title: Thin cylindrical shell under internal pressure

1. Scope and method
Calculates the minimum thickness of a thin cylindrical shell under internal pressure, for circumferential and longitudinal stress. It adds corrosion allowance and a design factor, and compares the thickness provided with the requirement.
Thickness needed for the circumferential stress: t_c = p_design·R_in/(S·E_j − 0.6·p_design). Thickness needed for the longitudinal stress: t_l = p_design·R_in/(2·S·E_j + 0.4·p_design). Required thickness, with the design factor and corrosion allowance: t_req = n_design·max(t_c, t_l) + c. Hoop stress in the plate provided, after corrosion: σ_θ = p_design·(R_in + 0.6·t)/(E_j·t), at t = t_p − c. Range of validity of the thin-wall formulae: t_c ≤ R_in/2 and p_design ≤ 0.385·S·E_j.

2. Inputs
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  R_in | Inside radius (corroded) | 500 | mm | manual | 5 … 10000 mm
  t_p | Shell thickness provided (nominal, before corrosion) | 12 | mm | manual | 0.5 … 500 mm
  S | Allowable stress at design temperature | 138 | MPa | manual | 10 … 1000 MPa
  p_design | Internal design pressure | 2 | MPa | manual | 0.01 … 100 MPa
  c | Corrosion allowance | 3 | mm | manual | 0 … 25 mm
  E_j | Joint efficiency | 0.85 |  | manual | 0.45 … 1
  n_design | Design factor on the computed thickness (legacy; 1 for none) | 1 |  | manual | 1 … 5

3. Assumptions
  A1. The shell is cylindrical, away from heads, nozzles and supports, and carries membrane stresses only. (The scope of the UG-27 formulae.)
  A2. The joint efficiency reduces the allowable stress uniformly. (Code-form simplification carried from the legacy module.)

4. Working
  [8] t_c = p_design * R_in / (S * E_j - 0.6 * p_design)    « ASME VIII-1 UG-27(c)(1)
      = 2 MPa × 500 mm / (138 MPa × 0.85 − 0.6 × 2 MPa)
      = 8.6133 mm
        Thickness for circumferential stress
  [9] t_l = p_design * R_in / (2 * S * E_j + 0.4 * p_design)    « ASME VIII-1 UG-27(c)(2)
      = 2 MPa × 500 mm / (2 × 138 MPa × 0.85 + 0.4 × 2 MPa)
      = 4.2481 mm
        Thickness for longitudinal stress
  [10] t_req = n_design * max(t_c, t_l) + c
      = 1 × max(8.6133 mm, 4.2481 mm) + 3 mm
      = 11.613 mm
        Required nominal thickness (design factor and corrosion allowance included)
  [11] t_corr = t_p - c
      = 12 mm − 3 mm
      = 9 mm
        Corroded thickness provided
  [12] sigma_h = p_design * (R_in + 0.6 * t_corr) / (E_j * t_corr)    « ASME VIII-1 UG-27(c)(1)
      = 2 MPa × (500 mm + 0.6 × 9 mm) / (0.85 × 9 mm)
      = 132.13 MPa
        Hoop stress at the corroded thickness provided
  [13] u = t_req / t_p
      = 11.613 mm / 12 mm
      = 0.96777
        Required over provided thickness

5. Results
  t_req = 11.61 mm    Required nominal thickness (governing)
  sigma_h = 132.1 MPa    Hoop stress at the corroded thickness
  t_c = 8.613 mm    Thickness, circumferential stress
  t_l = 4.248 mm    Thickness, longitudinal stress
  u = 0.97    Thickness utilisation
