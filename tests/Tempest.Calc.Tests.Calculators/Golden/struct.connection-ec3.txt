Title: Bolts and fillet welds (EN 1993-1-8)

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
The design resistances of one bolt in a plate (shear, bearing, tension, punching, combined shear and tension), an optional block-tearing check of the bolt group, the spacing limits, and a fillet weld by the simplified and the directional method, each against its design force.
Bolt strengths: f_ub = class × 100 MPa; A_s = π/4·((d₂ + d₃)/2)², d₂ = d − 0.6495P, d₃ = d − 1.2269P Shear (cl. 3.6, Table 3.4): F_v,Rd = α_v·f_ub·A·n_s/γ_M2; α_v = 0.6 (4.6, 5.6, 8.8 or shank in the plane), 0.5 (4.8, 5.8, 6.8, 10.9 with threads in the plane) Bearing: F_b,Rd = k₁·α_b·f_u·d·t/γ_M2; α_b = min(α_d, f_ub/f_u, 1), α_d = e₁/3d₀ (end) or p₁/3d₀ − 1/4 (inner); k₁ = min(2.8e₂/d₀ − 1.7, 1.4p₂/d₀ − 1.7, 2.5) Tension: F_t,Rd = k₂·f_ub·A_s/γ_M2, k₂ = 0.9; punching B_p,Rd = 0.6π·d_m·t·f_u/γ_M2, d_m = (s + e)/2 from the across-flats s and the across-corners e of the smaller of head and nut, or d_m = s when e is not entered (conservative); combined F_v/F_v,Rd + F_t/(1.4F_t,Rd) ≤ 1 Block tearing (cl. 3.10.2): V_eff,1,Rd = f_u·A_nt/γ_M2 + f_y·A_nv/(√3·γ_M0) Weld, simplified (cl. 4.5.3.3): f_vw,d = f_u/(√3·β_w·γ_M2), F_w,Rd = f_vw,d·a; directional (cl. 4.5.3.2): √(σ⊥² + 3(τ⊥² + τ∥²)) ≤ f_u/(β_w·γ_M2), σ⊥ ≤ 0.9f_u/γ_M2
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 3.5, Table 3.3 — Positioning of holes for bolts
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 3.6.1, Table 3.4 — Design resistance of individual fasteners
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 3.10.2 — Block tearing
  Ref: EN 1993-1-8:2005 cl. 4.5.3.2, 4.5.3.3, Table 4.1 — Fillet welds: directional and simplified methods; correlation factor
  Ref: ISO 898-1:2013 cl. 9.1 — Nominal stress area (formula)

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. Static loading; category A (bearing type) shear and category D (non-preloaded) tension; no slip-resistant joints or moment end plates. (Scope of S4.)
  A2. Bearing is taken at the worst of an end bolt and an inner bolt, and at an edge bolt across the load (k₁ with e₂ and p₂). (Conservative for every bolt in the group.)
  A3. Spacing minima (Table 3.3) and γ_M factors are entered from a National Annex record; the values shown default to EN 1993-1-8 recommended. (A small set of factors only; no table from a standard is shipped.)
  A4. Group forces come from M3 and weld forces per length from M6 by link. (Group forces and weld forces are linked from the bolt-group and weld-group calculations.)
