Title: Heat exchanger (LMTD and ε-NTU)

1. Scope and method
Calculates a single-phase heat exchanger with constant properties: the duty and outlet temperatures for a given area, or the area for a given duty. It covers counterflow, parallel flow, shell and tube, crossflow and one fluid at constant temperature.
Capacity rates: C_h = ṁ_h·c_p,h, C_c = ṁ_c·c_p,c, C_r = C_min/C_max; with one fluid at constant temperature (C_r = 0) that fluid's capacity rate is infinite, C_min is the other stream's and the constant stream leaves at its inlet temperature Overall coefficient with fouling: U_f = 1/(1/U_clean + R_f) Transfer units: NTU = U_f·A/C_min; effectiveness ε(NTU, C_r) for the arrangement Heat exchanger duty: Phi = ε·C_min·(T_h,in − T_c,in); outlets from the energy balance Area for a required duty: ε = Q_req/(C_min·ΔT_max), NTU from the inverse relation, A = NTU·C_min/U_f Cross-check with LMTD: Phi = U_f·A·F_corr·ΔT_lm, F_corr from R and P (one shell pass) or back-calculated

2. Inputs
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  arrangement | Flow arrangement | counterflow |  | manual | any
  mode | Mode | rating (given area) |  | manual | any
  const_side | Which side is at constant temperature (used only by the C_r = 0 arrangement) | hot side at constant temperature (e.g. condensing vapour) |  | manual | any
  m_h | Hot-side mass flow | 1 | kg/s | manual | 0.01 … 50 kg/s
  cp_h | Hot-side specific heat | 4180 | J/(kg·K) | manual | 500 … 10000 J/(kg·K)
  T_h_in | Hot-side inlet temperature | 80 | °C | manual | -20 … 180 °C
  m_c | Cold-side mass flow | 1 | kg/s | manual | 0.01 … 50 kg/s
  cp_c | Cold-side specific heat | 4180 | J/(kg·K) | manual | 500 … 10000 J/(kg·K)
  T_c_in | Cold-side inlet temperature | 20 | °C | manual | -20 … 150 °C
  U_clean | Overall heat transfer coefficient (clean) | 1000 | W/(m²·K) | manual | 20 … 8000 W/(m²·K)
  R_f | Total fouling resistance | 0 | m²·K/W | manual | 0 … 0.01 m²·K/W
  A | Heat transfer area (rating) | 4.18 | m² | manual | 0.05 … 500 m²
  dT_min | Minimum approach temperature difference | 2 | K | manual | 0 … 50 K
  Q_req | Required duty | 120 | kW | manual | 0.001 … 100000 kW

3. Assumptions
  A1. Single phase on both sides (except the C_r = 0 arrangement), constant properties, no heat loss to surroundings. (Incropera 7th ed. §11.3–11.4.)
  A2. The crossflow both-unmixed effectiveness is the published approximate correlation, flagged on the sheet. (Incropera 7th ed. Table 11.3.)
  A3. Specific heats are user records at the mean temperature of each stream. (Entered as reference records, which carry their source, edition and licence.)

4. Working
  [12] C_h = m_h * cp_h
      = 1 kg/s × 4180 J/(kg·K)
      = 4180 W/K
        Hot-side capacity rate
  [13] C_c = m_c * cp_c
      = 1 kg/s × 4180 J/(kg·K)
      = 4180 W/K
        Cold-side capacity rate
  [14] C_min = min(C_h, C_c)
      = min(4180 W/K, 4180 W/K)
      = 4180 W/K
        Minimum capacity rate
  [15] C_r = min(C_h, C_c) / max(C_h, C_c)
      = min(4180 W/K, 4180 W/K) / max(4180 W/K, 4180 W/K)
      = 1
        Capacity ratio
  [16] U_f = 1 / (1 / U_clean + R_f)
      = 1 / (1 / 1000 W/(m²·K) + 0 m²·K/W)
      = 1000 W/(m²·K)
        Overall coefficient with fouling
  [18] NTU = U_f·A / C_min
      = 1000 × 4.18 / 4180
      = 1
        Number of transfer units
  [19] eps = ε = (1 − exp(−NTU(1 − C_r)))/(1 − C_r·exp(−NTU(1 − C_r))); NTU/(1 + NTU) at C_r = 1    « Incropera 7th ed. Table 11.3
      = NTU = 1, C_r = 1
      = 0.5
        Effectiveness
  [20] UA = U_f·A
      = 1000 × 4.18
      = 4180 W/K
        UA
  [21] Phi = ε·C_min·(T_h,in − T_c,in)
      = 0.5 × 4180 × 60
      = 125.4 kW
        Heat duty
  [22] T_h_out = T_h,in − Q/C_h
      = 80 °C − 125400 / 4180
      = 50 degC
        Hot-side outlet temperature
  [23] T_c_out = T_c,in + Q/C_c
      = 20 °C + 125400 / 4180
      = 50 degC
        Cold-side outlet temperature
  [24] dT_c = T_c,out − T_c,in
      = 50 °C − 20 °C
      = 30 K
        Cold-side temperature rise
  [25] T_c_m = (T_c,in + T_c,out)/2
      = (20 °C + 50 °C)/2
      = 35 degC
        Mean cold-side temperature
  [26] dT_app = min(T_h,in − T_c,out, T_h,out − T_c,in)
      = 30 K
      = 30 K
        Approach temperature difference
  [27] dT_lm = (ΔT_1 − ΔT_2)/ln(ΔT_1/ΔT_2), counterflow ends
      = ΔT_1 = 30 K, ΔT_2 = 30 K
      = 30 K
        Log-mean temperature difference
  [28] F_corr = 1 (pure counterflow, parallel flow or C_r = 0)    « Incropera 7th ed. §11.3
      = R = 1, P = 0.5
      = 1
        LMTD correction factor
  [29] Q_lmtd = U_f·A·F·ΔT_lm
      = 1000 × 4.18 × 1 × 30
      = 125.4 kW
        Duty by the LMTD method (cross-check)
  [30] u_app = dT_min / dT_app
      = 2 K / 30 K
      = 0.066667
        Approach utilisation (minimum / actual)
  [32] u_duty = Q_req / Phi
      = 120 kW / 125.4 kW
      = 0.95694
        Duty utilisation (required / achieved)
