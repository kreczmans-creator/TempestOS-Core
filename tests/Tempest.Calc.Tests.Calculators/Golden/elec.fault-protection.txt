Calc ID: elec.fault-protection    Revision: A
Title: Fault protection (BS 7671)

1. Scope and method
Calculates automatic disconnection of supply in a TN system for one circuit: loop impedance, disconnection time and the adiabatic conductor check. It also gives the prospective three-phase fault at the origin against the breaking capacity.
Loop impedance: Z_s = Z_e + (R_1 + R_2)·[1 + α(θ − 20)], R = k_R·ρ_20·L/S (k_R = 1.125 takes the pure-copper ρ_20 to the cable standard's maximum conductor resistance; k enters with the pure ρ_20) Fault current: I_f = C_min·U_0/Z_s, C_min = 0.95 Maximum loop impedance: Z_s,max = C_min·U_0/I_a; I_a = 5, 10 or 20·I_n for MCB types B, C, D, or read from the time–current points at t_req (log-log) Adiabatic conductor check: S_min = √(I²t)/k, k = √[Q_c(β + 20)/ρ_20 · ln(1 + (θ_f − θ_i)/(β + θ_i))] (derived); I²t = I_f²·t_d from 0.1 s; below 0.1 s the device's let-through I²t at the maximum prospective fault bounds both conductors at the origin (reg. 434.5.2, 543.1.3), and without it the sheet fails 'Conductors against let-through at the origin' Protective device check: the device chosen here must be the one carried from the cable step (device_code 1 = MCB B, 2 = C, 3 = D, 4 = fuse or MCCB), else the sheet is blocked Fault level (simplified): Z_T = u_k·U_n²/S_r, I_k3 = c·U_n/(√3·Z_T), U_n = √3·U_0

3. Assumptions
  A1. The system is TN with a single earth fault of negligible impedance. (Scope of the check.)
  A2. There is no motor contribution, no arc fault and no IT system. (Scope of the check.)
  A3. The line and protective conductors are the same material at the same temperature. (Conductor data from a material record.)
  A4. The resistance is ρ₂₀·L/S corrected by α. (Conductor data from a material record.)
  A5. The factor k is derived from the material constants, not read from a licensed table. (Derived constants, not read from a table.)
  A6. Fuse and MCCB characteristics are points the user reads off the manufacturer's curve. (No time–current table ships.)
  A7. The points are interpolated log-log, with no extrapolation. (No time–current table ships.)
  A8. The optional field-test value 0.8 × Z_s,max is a rule of thumb for measurements at ambient temperature. (Labelled on the sheet.)
  A9. The line conductor check (BS 7671 clause 434.5.2) needs the device's let-through energy at the maximum prospective fault. (Stated limitation: the end-of-line earth fault is the minimum fault, not the maximum.)
  A10. The end-of-line earth fault is the minimum fault and does not bound that energy. (Stated limitation: the end-of-line earth fault is the minimum fault, not the maximum.)
  A11. The line conductor check is made only when I2t_let is entered or the disconnection time is 0.1 s or more. (Stated limitation: the end-of-line earth fault is the minimum fault, not the maximum.)

4. Working
  [19] R_12 = rho_20 * k_R * L * (1 / S_line + 1 / S_cpc) * (1 + alpha * (T_op - 20 degC))
      = 0.017241 ohm·mm²/m × 1 × 20 m × (1 / 2.5 mm² + 1 / 1.5 mm²) × (1 + 0.00393 1/K × (70 degC − 20 degC))
      = 0.44008 ohm
        R₁ + R₂ at the conductor temperature
  [20] Z_s = Z_e + R_12    « BS 7671 reg. 411.4.4
      = 0.35 ohm + 0.44008 ohm
      = 0.79008 ohm
        Earth fault loop impedance
  [21] I_f = C_min * U_0 / Z_s
      = 0.95 × 230 V / 0.79008 ohm
      = 276.55 A
        Earth fault current
  [22] I_a = 5 * I_n    « BS EN 60898-1
      = 5 × 32 A
      = 160 A
        Instantaneous tripping current (5 I_n)
  [23] Z_s_max = C_min * U_0 / I_a    « BS 7671 reg. 411.4.4
      = 0.95 × 230 V / 160 A
      = 1.3656 ohm
        Maximum earth fault loop impedance
  [24] u_Zs = Z_s / Z_s_max
      = 0.79008 ohm / 1.3656 ohm
      = 0.57855
        Loop impedance utilisation
  [25] Z_test = 0.8 * Z_s_max
      = 0.8 × 1.3656 ohm
      = 1.0925 ohm
        Field-test maximum at ambient (0.8 × Z_s,max, rule of thumb)
  [26] t_d = instantaneous region (I_f ≥ I_a): t ≤ 0.1 s
      = I_f = 276.55 A
      = 0.1 s
        Disconnection time
  [27] I2t = I_f²·0.1 s (no let-through entered)
      = 276.55² × 0.1
      = 7648.2 A²·s
        Fault energy at the end-of-line fault, clearing time taken as 0.1 s
  [28] k = √[Q_c(β + 20)/ρ₂₀ · ln(1 + (θ_f − θ_i)/(β + θ_i))] (derived)    « BS 7671 reg. 543.1.3
      = √[0.00345 × 254.5 / 0.000017241 × ln(1 + 90/304.5)]
      = 114.84
        Adiabatic factor k, A·√s/mm² (derived, compare with the licensed table)
  [29] S_min = √(I²t)/k    « BS 7671 reg. 543.1.3, 434.5.2
      = √7648.2 / 114.84
      = 0.76155 mm²
        Minimum conductor cross-section (adiabatic)
  [30] u_cpc = S_min / S_cpc
      = 0.76155 mm² / 1.5 mm²
      = 0.5077
        Protective conductor utilisation

6. Checks (governing first)
  FAIL  Conductors against let-through at the origin: I2t_let not entered vs I²t the device lets through at the maximum prospective fault  « BS 7671 reg. 434.5.2, 543.1.3
  PASS  Loop impedance against the maximum: 0.7901 Ω vs 1.3656 Ω  utilisation 0.58  « BS 7671 reg. 411.4.4
  PASS  Adiabatic minimum against the protective conductor: 0.7616 mm² vs 1.500 mm²  utilisation 0.51  « BS 7671 reg. 543.1.3
  PASS  Disconnection time against the required time: 0.1000 s vs 0.4000 s  utilisation 0.25  « BS 7671 reg. 411.3.2.2

7. Applicability
  WARNING  records: tabulated values not taken from a reference record are rho_20, k_R, alpha, Q_c, beta, theta_i, theta_f (Conductor material constants). Enter each as one so its source, edition and licence travel with it.  [record]
  WARNING  k_R: the factor 1 leaves R₁ + R₂ near the pure resistivity, so Z_s is understated. Use 1.125 unless the conductor resistance is measured or taken from the maker's data.  [resistance]
  WARNING  I2t_let: disconnection is below 0.1 s and no let-through I²t is entered. The check 'Conductors against let-through at the origin' fails until I2t_let is entered.  [let-through]
