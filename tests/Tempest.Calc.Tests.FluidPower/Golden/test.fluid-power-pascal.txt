Title: Cylinder force from pressure (harness fixture)

1. Scope and method
The force a hydraulic cylinder develops from a gauge pressure acting on a piston area, losses ignored.
Force: F = p·A (Pascal's law, gauge pressure on the full bore area; no seal friction, no back pressure).

2. Inputs
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  p | Gauge pressure | 100 | bar | manual | 1 … 400 bar
  A | Piston area | 1000 | mm² | manual | 1 … 1000000 mm²

3. Assumptions
  A1. No seal friction, back pressure or acceleration: the static force. (Pascal's law on a confined incompressible fluid.)

4. Working
  [3] F = p * A
      = 100 bar × 1000 mm²
      = 10 kN
        Cylinder force

5. Results
  F = 10.00 kN    Cylinder force

  None.
