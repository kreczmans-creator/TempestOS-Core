Title: Cylinder and actuator circuit

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
A double-acting hydraulic cylinder in its circuit: the bore, rod and annulus areas; the force and speed on the extend and the retract stroke with mechanical and volumetric efficiency and an optional back pressure, or with the regenerative extend connection; the swept volume and the cycle time; the pump delivery, shaft power, torque and speed; a hydraulic motor's torque; the velocity in the suction, pressure and return lines against your own limits and the smallest listed hose or pipe bore that meets them; a sharp orifice's flow for flow-control screening; and a screening check of the rod against buckling (Euler above the transition slenderness, Johnson parabola below it). screening only; ISO/TS 13725 method not implemented. Not a cylinder design to a standard: the limits, the hose sizes, the mounting factor and the material properties are your records.
Areas: A_b = π·D²/4, A_r = π·d_r²/4, A_a = A_b − A_r, area ratio φ = A_b/A_a Force: F_ext = (p·A_b − p_back·A_a)·η_m, F_ret = (p·A_a − p_back·A_b)·η_m; regenerative extend F_ext = p·A_r·η_m Speed and time: v_ext = Q·η_vc/A_b (regenerative: A_r), v_ret = Q·η_vc/A_a; t = stroke/v; t_cycle = t_ext + t_ret + t_dwell Swept volume: ΔV_ext = A_b·stroke (regenerative: A_r·stroke), ΔV_ret = A_a·stroke; ΔV = the larger Pump: Q_p = V_g·n·η_vp; P = p·Q_p/η_tp; T = P/ω; displacement needed V_g,req = Q/(n·η_vp) Hydraulic motor: T = Δp·V_g/(2π)·η_hm (V_g per revolution) Lines: v = Q_line/A_line with Q_suction = Q_pressure = Q_line (regenerative pressure line: Q_line·A_b/A_r) and Q_return = Q_line·A_b/A_a; smallest bore d = √(4Q/(π·v_max)), recommended = the smallest listed size not below it Orifice: Q = C_d·A_o·√(2Δp/ρ); bore for the circuit flow d_o = √(4Q/(π·C_d·√(2Δp/ρ))) Rod buckling (screening): L_eff = K·(stroke + L_add), r = d_r/4, λ = L_eff/r, λ_c = π·√(2E/f_y); Euler F_cr = π²·E·I/L_eff² for λ > λ_c, Johnson F_cr = A_r·f_y·[1 − f_y·λ²/(4π²E)] for λ ≤ λ_c; utilisation u = F_push·S_f/F_cr
  Ref: ISO/TS 13725 — Cylinder buckling load: method not implemented, screening only
  Ref: Esposito, Fluid Power with Applications Cylinder and pump chapters — Force, speed, flow and power relations, closed form

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. The supply pressure p is the gauge pressure at the cylinder port and, for the pump, at the pump outlet; line losses are not subtracted (the line losses of a pipe run come from the pipe calculation). The force is the pressure on the area less the back pressure on the other side, times the mechanical efficiency. (F = p·A·η_m, closed form.)
  A2. The speed is the flow into the cylinder times the volumetric efficiency over the area, in steady motion: no acceleration, cushioning or compressibility. The cycle time is the two strokes plus the dwell. (v = Q·η_vc/A.)
  A3. The pump power is the supply pressure times the pump's delivery over the total efficiency: when the pump delivers more than the circuit takes, the excess is taken to go over the relief valve at the supply pressure, which is the conservative case for the drive. The shaft torque is the power over the angular speed. (P = p·Q_p/η_tp.)
  A4. The line velocities use the pump delivery when it is larger than the circuit flow. The return line carries the discharge of the cap side on the retract stroke, Q·A_b/A_a. In the regenerative extend the cap-side (pressure) line carries Q·A_b/A_r. The velocity limits are your records; the sheet's defaults are placeholders, not values from a standard. (Continuity.)
  A5. The orifice relation is the sharp-edged turbulent one, Q = C_d·A·√(2Δp/ρ), with your discharge coefficient; it ignores viscosity and cavitation and is a screening of a flow-control orifice, not a valve selection. (Orifice equation, closed form.)
  A6. The rod is treated as a solid round column of its own diameter with the effective-length factor of the mounting; the tube, the piston and the rod-end stiffness are not modelled, and the load is the push load you enter. screening only; ISO/TS 13725 method not implemented. (Euler and Johnson column relations, closed form.)
  A7. Standard connection: the extend force is the pressure on the bore area less the back pressure on the annulus area; the retract force the pressure on the annulus less the back pressure on the bore. (Continuity and force balance.)

      = π × (100 mm)^2 / 4
      = 78.54 cm²
        Bore (cap-side) area
  [36] A_r = pi * d_r^2 / 4
      = π × (70 mm)^2 / 4

  [37] A_a = A_b - A_r
      = 78.54 cm² − 38.485 cm²
      = 40.055 cm²

  [38] phi = A_b / A_a
      = 78.54 cm² / 40.055 cm²

  [39] F_ext = (p * A_b - p_back * A_a) * eta_m

  [40] v_ext = Q * eta_vc / A_b

      = 3.927 L
