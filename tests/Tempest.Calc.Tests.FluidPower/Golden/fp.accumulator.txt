Title: Accumulator sizing (gas law)

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
The pre-charge gas volume V0 of a hydraulic accumulator that delivers, or absorbs, a liquid volume ΔV between the lowest working pressure p1 and the highest p2, from p·Vⁿ = const with absolute pressures and an exponent n between 1 (isothermal) and 1.4 (adiabatic); the pre-charge pressure at the charging temperature; the next size from your own size list; and the bar·litre product against the PSSR Schedule 1 250 bar·L exception. Three modes: energy storage, shock and pulsation (screening only) and emergency stroke (ΔV is the stroke volume, linkable from the cylinder circuit). Not a pressure-vessel code check.
Absolute pressures: p_a = p_gauge + p_atm (or as entered, in Absolute mode) Gas volume: V0 = ΔV / [(p0/p1)^(1/n) − (p0/p2)^(1/n)], n = 1 isothermal … 1.4 adiabatic, then × the real-gas factor f_rg (user input) Pre-charge rules (records): p0 ≤ r_0·p1 (default 0.9, bladder type) and p2/p0 ≤ r_p (default 4) Pre-charge at the charging temperature: p0(T_c) = p0(T_max)·(T_c + 273.15)/(T_max + 273.15) Next size: the smallest listed size not below V0 Bar·litres: PS·V, PS = p2 as a gauge pressure, V the selected size (else V0); compared with 250 bar·L (PSSR Schedule 1, indication only)
  Ref: Pressure Systems Safety Regulations 2000 (SI 2000/128) Schedule 1 — 250 bar·litre exception, indication only
  Ref: Pressure Equipment Directive 2014/68/EU and UK PER 2016 — Pressure equipment; not checked here
  Ref: Esposito, Fluid Power with Applications Ch. on accumulators — Gas-charged accumulator sizing, p·Vⁿ = const

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. All gas-law pressures are absolute. A gauge entry is converted with the atmospheric pressure entered here, so the same numbers read as gauge or as absolute give different volumes. (p·Vⁿ = const on a fixed mass of gas.)
  A2. The exponent n is the user's: 1 for a slow discharge with heat exchange (isothermal), towards 1.4 for a fast one (adiabatic). The larger n gives the larger volume for a given ΔV. (Gas-law exponents, closed form.)
  A3. The gas is ideal unless a real-gas factor is entered; above 200 bar the sheet warns that nitrogen departs from the ideal-gas law and a factor from a gas-property source should be entered. (Real-gas correction as a user factor.)
  A4. The pre-charge rules (p0 ≤ 0.9·p1 and p2/p0 ≤ 4 by default) are records for the accumulator type and make and are checked as PASS/FAIL; the right values come from the manufacturer. (Rules are records, not built-in limits.)
  A5. The bar·litre product uses the highest working pressure as a gauge pressure and the selected size (else the calculated volume). It indicates whether the 250 bar·L exception of PSSR Schedule 1 can apply; it is not a compliance statement, and PED / UK PER, PSSR examination and the vessel's own design code stay with the manufacturer and the competent person. (Addendum B §B5 regulatory gating: no pressure-vessel code check.)
  A6. Energy storage duty: ΔV is the liquid volume the accumulator delivers between p_2 and p_1 over a cycle; n = 1 for a slow discharge, towards 1.4 for a fast one. (Gas-law sizing for a stored-energy duty.)
