Title: O-ring and seal gland

1. SCOPE AND METHOD
Squeeze, stretch, gland fill and extrusion gap of a radial piston, radial rod or face O-ring seal, from the ring and groove dimensions with their tolerances, at the cold and shrunk and at the hot and swollen end of the temperature and swell range. The ring's cross-section shrinks with stretch by a relation you choose (none, constant-volume rubber, the quadratic cited from Parker ORD 5700 section 3.5, or linear in stretch). The worst case and the capability indices run through the tolerance-stack engine of M1, and the seeded Monte Carlo of the engine runs the exact non-linear model and reports the fraction outside each limit. A face seal is entered with the pressure on its outside or its inside. Measured groove distributions (from the scan import) can replace the toleranced dimensions. Ring sizes, the squeeze and fill bands and the extrusion limit are your own records: no O-ring size table, gland table or extrusion chart is shipped.
Stretch: X = (g·D_ref)/(f·d) − 1, D_ref the diameter the ring ID sits on (groove root for a piston or face seal, rod for a rod seal); f = (1 + α(T − 20 °C))(1 + swell)^(1/3) the ring and g = 1 + α_m(T − 20 °C) the groove metal Cross-section: W_eff = f·W·(1 − r(X))/√(1 − c), r(X) the chosen relation (none; 1 − 1/√(1+X); Y = 0.56 + 0.59X − 0.0046X² per cent as cited, used for about 3 to 25 % stretch only; ν·X) and c the compression of the ring outside diameter where it is held (rod seal in the housing groove; internal-pressure face seal on the groove outer wall), c = 1 − g·D_g/(f·(d + 2W)) Depth and gap: piston h = (D_s − D_g)/2, gap = D_s − D_c; rod h = (D_g − D_s)/2, gap = D_c − D_s; face h and gap entered directly (all times g) Squeeze: q = 1 − g·h/W_eff, minimum at the cold end from the largest depth over the smallest W_eff, maximum at the hot end Gland fill: ring area over the groove area g·b·g·h − 2(1 − π/4)(g·r_g)²; maximum at the hot end on a volume basis, (π/4)·(f·W)²·(f/g) with f³ = (1 + αΔT)³(1 + s) (an outside diameter held in the groove: (π/4)·(f·W)²/(1 − c)), minimum at the cold end (π/4)·W_eff² Worst case: each range from the engine's worst-case stack of the dimension (Σ|coefficient|·half-band), combined at the corner that is worst for the quantity Monte Carlo: the engine's seeded Monte Carlo on the exact criterion, each dimension from its own distribution; the fraction below the lower and above the upper limit. The capability index C_pk uses the criterion linearised about the mean dimensions (secant slope across each band) Extrusion: the largest gap over the installed, cold and hot temperatures (the sealing-surface part and the groove metal each with its own expansion) plus the bore dilation under pressure, against the permitted gap from your record (interpolated in hardness and pressure) or the value entered; with neither the check fails (not verified)
  Ref: Parker O-Ring Handbook ORD 5700 Section 3.5 — Cross-section reduction with stretch (relation quoted in Addendum B; not confirmed)
  Ref: Parker PTD 5705 — O-ring installation guidance (stretch rule quoted in Addendum B; not confirmed)
  Ref: ISO 3601-1 — O-rings: inside diameters, cross-sections, tolerances and size codes
  Ref: ISO 3601-2 — O-rings: housing dimensions for general applications

3. ASSUMPTIONS
  A1. The gland is a plain rectangular groove with a bottom corner radius; the ring is a circle of the stated cross-section. Edge breaks, groove wall angles and surface finish are not modelled. (Closed-form gland geometry; no gland table.)
  A2. Squeeze is the compressed fraction of the effective cross-section W_eff, which is the nominal section after thermal expansion, swell and the cross-section reduction of the stretch. The reduction relation is chosen by the user; the quadratic of Parker ORD 5700 section 3.5 is implemented as quoted in Addendum B, used for about 3 to 25 % stretch only, and is not confirmed against the handbook. Where the ring outside diameter is held (a rod seal in its housing groove, an internal-pressure face seal), the compression c thickens the section to W/√(1 − c). (The relation is an input choice; the default is the constant-volume rubber relation, derived.)
  A3. The cold end is the lowest temperature with the least swell and sets the minimum squeeze and fill; the hot end is the highest temperature with the most swell and sets the maximum. This assumes the ring expands more than the groove metal (alpha above alpha_m). (Bounding cases of a monotonic model.)
  A4. The worst case combines each dimension at its extreme independently. Where one dimension appears twice (the groove root diameter sets both the depth and the stretch of a piston seal) the worst case is slightly conservative. (Independent ends of linear stacks.)
  A5. The Monte Carlo samples each dimension from its own distribution about its mean and evaluates each criterion exactly (the non-linear model) on the engine's seeded sampler. It counts the fraction outside each limit at the cold or hot end; it does not model pressure, friction or compression set. The capability index C_pk uses the first-order (secant) linearisation. (Exact model on the engine's seeded sampler.)
  A6. The gland fill at the hot end is on a volume basis: a ring held circumferentially by the groove takes the whole volume change of thermal expansion and swell in its section, (1 + αΔT)³(1 + s), while the groove circumference grows with the groove metal. This is higher than scaling the section area isotropically. (Volume conservation of the held ring.)
  A7. The extrusion gap is the largest diametral clearance of a radial seal (the axial gap of a face seal) over the installed, cold and hot temperatures, the sealing-surface part and the groove metal each with its own expansion coefficient, plus the diametral growth of the bore under pressure that the user enters (0 if none). The permitted gap is the user's record; the sheet never states a handbook value. With no permitted gap entered the extrusion check fails (not verified) while the operating pressure is above zero. (Extrusion limits are licensed data and are not shipped.)
  A8. Static duty: the squeeze band for static seals from the oring.criteria record is used. (The bands are the user's record.)
