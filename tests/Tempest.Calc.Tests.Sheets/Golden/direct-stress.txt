Calc ID: test.direct-stress    Revision: A
Title: Direct stress check

1. Scope and method
Axial stress in a prismatic member under a tensile or compressive load, compared with an allowable stress.
σ = F / A; utilisation = σ / σ_allow.

2. Inputs
  Symbol | Description | Value | Unit | Source | Valid range
  F | Axial load | 12 | kN | drawing: DRG-001 rev B | 0 … 10000 kN
  A | Cross-section area | 200 | mm² | drawing: DRG-001 rev B | ≥ 1 mm²
  sigma_allow | Allowable stress | 160 | MPa | spec: SPEC-7 §4 | ≥ 1 MPa
  grade | Steel grade | S275 |  | assumption: Grade not stated on the drawing. | any
  slender | Member is slender | no |  | manual | any

3. Assumptions
  A1. The load acts through the centroid. (No bending from eccentricity.)
  A2. grade (Steel grade) is taken as S275. (Grade not stated on the drawing.)

4. Working
  [6] sigma = F / A    « EN 1993-1-1 §6.2.3
      = 12 kN / 200 mm²
      = 60 MPa
        Direct stress
  [7] u = sigma / sigma_allow
      = 60 MPa / 160 MPa
      = 0.375
        Utilisation
  [8] note_grade = grade
      = S275
      = "S275"
        Steel grade as chosen

5. Results
  sigma = 60.0 MPa    Direct stress
  u = 0.38    Utilisation (governing)

6. Checks (governing first)
  PASS  Direct stress: u = 0.38 vs u ≤ 1  utilisation 0.38  « EN 1993-1-1 §6.2.3
