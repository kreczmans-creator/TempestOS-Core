# v0.58.0 — Engineering Polymers Depth Pass

## Scope

Deepen the Engineering Polymers foundation from family-level taxonomy to
representative grade/variant records.

## Families covered

ABS, PA, POM, PC, PBT, PPS, PEI, PPA, LCP and PEEK.

## Grade/variant coverage

Representative unfilled and reinforced/modified variants are now explicitly
identified where they are central to engineering selection.

## Critical engineering controls

Each representative record carries:

- material identity;
- material class;
- product-form context;
- common uses;
- selection drivers;
- watchouts;
- relative cost;
- general availability;
- required quantitative property fields;
- conditioning requirement;
- reinforcement-orientation requirement where applicable;
- evidence status.

## Quantitative-data boundary

No unsupported numerical values are invented in this pass. The records establish
the exact fields and provenance conditions that must be satisfied before values
can be promoted to design-use data.

## Next

Populate authoritative grade-specific quantitative properties, starting with
mechanical and thermal data, then electrical/environmental data and finally
creep/fatigue and standards mappings.
