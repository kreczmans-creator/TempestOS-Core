# v0.69.0 — Engineering Metals: Joints, Pressure, Welding & Product Form

This release continues the metals design-engineering foundation from v0.68.0.

## Fasteners and joints

The library now treats preload, stiffness, external-load transfer, separation,
slip, fatigue, corrosion and assembly scatter as a joint-level problem.

## Pressure components

Pressure-boundary screening is introduced, including membrane-stress
relationships, cyclic service, discontinuities, corrosion and inspection.
Thin-wall equations are explicitly restricted to preliminary screening; final
design belongs to the governing pressure-vessel code.

## Welded structures

Weld design is treated separately from parent-metal design. Load path, weld
detail, HAZ behaviour, residual stress, defects, distortion, fatigue and NDT
are included.

## Product form

Wrought, cast and forged forms are distinguished because processing can alter
grain flow, texture, anisotropy, defects, residual stress and fatigue
performance.

## Corrosion-fatigue

The interaction between corrosion and cyclic loading is now explicitly
represented, including pit-driven initiation and environment-sensitive crack
growth. Fretting-fatigue is also included.

## Standards

Major design/test standards and codes are mapped to the relevant domains.
Actual project applicability and edition remain controlled engineering
decisions.

## Boundary

The library provides structured engineering reasoning and screening workflows.
It does not create generic design allowables or replace qualified standards,
codes, material certificates or application-specific verification.
