# v0.39.0 — Aluminium Mechanical Deepening

The aluminium foundation has been deepened from alloy-level screening records
to explicit temper/product-form records.

## Added

- 15 temper/product-form mechanical records
- yield strength
- ultimate tensile strength
- elongation
- hardness
- elastic modulus
- Poisson's ratio
- derived shear modulus
- fatigue-data framework
- fracture-data framework
- controlled evidence-gap register

## Engineering significance

Temper and product form are now first-class dimensions in the aluminium
mechanical layer. This prevents a generic alloy designation from being treated
as though it has one universal strength value.

Fatigue and fracture have been modelled as separate engineering evidence
domains. No generic endurance limit or tensile-strength substitution is used.

## Status

The records added here remain **unverified engineering-reference estimates**.
They are suitable only for screening and library development until linked to
authoritative specifications/test data.

No record is approved for design use.
