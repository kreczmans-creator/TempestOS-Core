# v0.66.0 — Engineering Metals Foundation

The next major material domain has begun with a fundamentals-first foundation
for engineering metals.

## Initial coverage

Six major families are represented:
- carbon steels;
- alloy steels;
- stainless steels;
- aluminium alloys;
- titanium alloys;
- copper alloys.

## Material identity

The material identity model distinguishes:
- grade/designation;
- temper or condition;
- product form;
- heat treatment;
- surface/coating state.

This prevents family-level descriptions from being mistaken for qualified
grade data.

## Engineering behaviour

The foundation covers:
- elastic and strength properties;
- hardness;
- toughness;
- fatigue;
- contact/bearing behaviour;
- thermal properties;
- environmental degradation;
- corrosion;
- processing and heat-treatment effects;
- principal failure modes.

## Standards

Common ISO/ASTM methods are mapped to tensile, fatigue, fracture, hardness,
impact, corrosion and metallography domains.

## Evidence boundary

This is a knowledge foundation, not a design-allowable database. Exact grade,
condition, product form, process state, test context and provenance are required
before numerical data are promoted for controlled engineering use.
