# Engineering Knowledge Foundation v0.21.0

## 316L source-backed tranche

This release adds the first source-bound 316L observations from Outokumpu's Supra range datasheet.

At 20 °C, the source reports values according to EN 10088-2 and distinguishes product form:
- cold rolled coil/sheet: Rp0.2 240 MPa, Rm 530–680 MPa, A 40%;
- hot rolled coil/sheet: Rp0.2 220 MPa, Rm 530–680 MPa, A 40%;
- quarto plate: Rp0.2 220 MPa, Rm 520–670 MPa, A 45%.

These are retained as source-specific observations. They remain `sourced` and `screening_only` until review and verification.
