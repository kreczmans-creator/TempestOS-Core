# v0.41.0 — Aluminium Quantitative Reference Layer

This release adds the first quantitative screening layer for aluminium shear,
bearing, fatigue, fracture and temperature-dependent strength.

## Important evidence boundary

The numerical values are explicitly classified as **unverified screening
references**. They are included to make the library useful for early material
selection and engineering reasoning, but they are not design allowables and
must not be represented as specification-certified values.

## Coverage

- 14 priority temper/product-form shear/bearing records
- 5 family-level fatigue screening records
- 5 family-level fracture toughness screening ranges
- 4 family-level temperature strength-retention curves

## Remaining evidence work

The next stage is authoritative source linkage and record-level provenance,
followed by the formal aluminium fundamentals audit. Product form, thickness,
orientation, temper, environment and test method must be preserved wherever
they affect the engineering property.
