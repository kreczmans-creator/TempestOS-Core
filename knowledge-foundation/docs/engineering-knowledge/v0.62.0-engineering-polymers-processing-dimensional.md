# v0.62.0 — Processing, Reinforcement & Dimensional Stability

The Engineering Polymers library now captures the manufacturing-state effects
that are fundamental to engineering polymer components.

## Processing

Covered:
- injection moulding
- extrusion
- thermoforming where applicable
- machining
- compression moulding
- additive manufacturing where applicable
- thermal history
- cooling
- residual stress
- flow orientation
- process-induced defects

## Reinforcement

The library distinguishes matrix-family behaviour from filled/reinforced
grades. Glass fibre, carbon fibre, mineral and tribological fillers are treated
as material-state variables rather than simple modifiers.

## Dimensional stability

Covered:
- shrinkage
- post-shrinkage
- warpage
- CTE
- moisture expansion
- dimensional drift
- anisotropy
- annealing/thermal history

## Joining

A first-pass joining screen now covers fasteners, snap-fits, welding,
solvent/adhesive bonding and insert moulding, with creep, clamp-load retention,
CTE mismatch and assembly tolerance identified as fundamental controls.

## Evidence boundary

All new records remain screening-level. Exact grade/process/geometry and
authoritative source data are required before design promotion.

## Next

Populate the polymer application/design rules layer: gears, bearings,
bushings, seals, snap-fits, threaded/bolted joints, press fits and structural
parts, including the principal failure modes and selection constraints.
