# Engineering Knowledge Foundation v1.8.0

## First steel verification tranche

This release moves from a generic sourcing queue to a concrete first verification tranche covering eight representative steels:

- S235JR
- S355JR
- C45
- 42CrMo4
- 304
- 316L
- 17-4 PH
- H13

The tranche intentionally spans structural, non-alloy Q&T, alloy Q&T, austenitic stainless, precipitation-hardening stainless and tool-steel families.

### Status discipline

The records are **not marked verified merely because a standard has been identified**.

For each observation the ledger requires:
- exact source locator;
- material identity;
- delivery/heat-treatment condition;
- product form;
- thickness/section where relevant;
- temperature;
- test method where applicable;
- unit/semantic validation;
- conflict resolution.

This is the point at which the database becomes auditable by another engineer rather than simply plausible.

### Research principle

Normative standards should establish required/guaranteed values where applicable. Secondary databases and handbooks may provide engineering-property context, but their provenance and applicability remain explicit.
