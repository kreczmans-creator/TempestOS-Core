# v0.44.0 — Tungsten Heavy Alloys and Cemented Carbides

The tungsten library is now deepened beyond monolithic tungsten.

## Heavy alloys

Initial grade-family records cover nominal 90W, 93W and 95W systems. The
records capture tungsten fraction, binder-system context, density, elastic
properties, strength, elongation, hardness, thermal/electrical behaviour,
manufacturing, machinability, fracture behaviour, cost, availability and uses.

## Cemented carbide

Initial WC-Co records cover approximately 6%, 10% and 15% cobalt binder
systems. Binder fraction is explicitly modelled because it materially changes
hardness, stiffness and fracture toughness.

## Ontology decision

Cemented carbide remains separate from metallic tungsten and tungsten heavy
alloys. The library therefore distinguishes:

1. tungsten metal
2. tungsten alloys
3. tungsten heavy alloys
4. tungsten-copper composites
5. cemented tungsten carbide

## Evidence boundary

All quantitative records remain unverified engineering references and are
screening-only. They are not design allowables.

## Next
