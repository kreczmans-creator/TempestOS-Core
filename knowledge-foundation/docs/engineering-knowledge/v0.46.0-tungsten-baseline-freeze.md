# v0.46.0 — Tungsten Fundamentals Audit and Baseline Freeze

## Result

The tungsten **fundamentals foundation is frozen**.

The audit confirms coverage across designation/composition, density,
mechanical, thermal, electrical, environmental, manufacturing, joining,
standards, cost, availability, uses, high-temperature behaviour, creep,
recrystallisation, fracture, provenance, heavy-alloy and carbide distinctions.

## Evidence boundary

The freeze is a **library foundation freeze**, not a design-data
qualification. Quantitative values remain screening/reference values until
they are linked to authoritative sources with sufficient applicability
context.

## Colour board

- RED: quantitative design qualification is blocked by missing authoritative
  source linkage.
- AMBER: grade-level high-temperature and carbide evidence remains open.
- YELLOW: exact standard-edition mapping remains open.
- GREEN: material ontology, fundamentals coverage and system separation are
  accepted.
