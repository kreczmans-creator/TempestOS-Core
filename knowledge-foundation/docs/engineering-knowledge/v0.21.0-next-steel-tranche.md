# Engineering Knowledge Foundation v0.21.0

## Steel evidence tranche

This release continues the source-backed steel work with 304, 17-4 PH and H13.

### 304
Outokumpu source data is bound to EN 10088-2 and the cold-rolled coil/sheet product form. The sourced values are Rp0.2 230 MPa, Rm 540–750 MPa and A 45% at 20 °C.

### 17-4 PH
The AK Steel source provides H900-condition mechanical values: 1170 MPa yield strength and 1310 MPa tensile strength at 20 °C in the cited product context.

### H13
ISO 4957 is now bound as the normative designation framework for H13 / X40CrMoV5-1. Property observations remain queued until a suitable grade/condition/product-form source is bound.
