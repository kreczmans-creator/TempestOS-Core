# v0.67.0 — Engineering Metals: Process, Joining & Application Fundamentals

This release deepens the metals foundation established in v0.66.0.

## Heat treatment and microstructure

The library now links heat-treatment routes and microstructural state to
engineering properties. Material identity therefore includes the relevant
condition rather than only the alloy family.

## Corrosion and surface engineering

Principal corrosion mechanisms and surface-control strategies are recorded.
Coatings, conversion treatments, passivation, anodising, surface preparation,
galvanic isolation and inspection are treated as part of the engineering
material system.

## Joining

Common joining processes are mapped by material family. The library explicitly
requires consideration of heat affected zones, residual stress, distortion,
metallurgical transformations and joint-specific inspection/qualification.

## Manufacturing

Process capability is linked to grain/texture, porosity, inclusions, residual
stress, heat-treatment variation, surface integrity and traceability.

## Application rules

Screening rules now cover shafts/axles, fasteners, brackets/housings,
springs/flexures and sliding components.

## Calculations

Generic screening patterns cover shaft stress, thermal fits, beam deflection
and bolt preload. They expose assumptions and do not create unsupported
allowables.

## Fundamentals boundary

The metals library is now materially stronger as a design-engineering
foundation, while exact grade/condition qualification remains a controlled
evidence activity.
