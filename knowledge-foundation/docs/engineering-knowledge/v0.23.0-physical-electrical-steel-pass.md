# Engineering Knowledge Foundation v0.23.0

## Physical and electrical steel properties

This pass adds source-backed physical/electrical property records for 304, 316L and 17-4 PH.

### 304
Outokumpu Core 304/4301, Table 7, according to EN 10088-1:
- density: 7.9 kg/dm3
- modulus of elasticity: 200 GPa at 20 °C
- CTE 20–100 °C: 16.0 × 10^-6/K
- thermal conductivity: 15 W/m/K at 20 °C
- thermal capacity: 500 J/kg/K at 20 °C
- electrical resistivity: 0.73 Ω·mm²/m at 20 °C
- non-magnetizable

### 316L
Outokumpu Supra 316L/4404, Table 7, according to EN 10088-1:
- density: 8.0 kg/dm3
- modulus of elasticity: 200 GPa at 20 °C
- CTE 20–100 °C: 16.0 × 10^-6/K
- thermal conductivity: 15 W/m/K at 20 °C
- thermal capacity: 500 J/kg/K at 20 °C
- electrical resistivity: 0.75 Ω·mm²/m at 20 °C
- non-magnetizable

### 17-4 PH
AK Steel/ARMCO data records condition-dependent density and electrical resistivity, together with temperature-banded thermal expansion and H900 thermal conductivity. These remain condition-specific and are not collapsed to one generic 17-4 PH value.

### H13
A conventional wrought H13 source is now bound to BÖHLER W302 ISODISC. The source explicitly maps 1.2344 / X40CrMoV5-1 / T20813 / H13 and states that the presented data refer exclusively to long products. Numerical mechanical-property import remains gated on the detailed datasheet.

## Trust boundary

All new observations are `sourced` and `reviewed`, but none are `verified` or `approved_for_design`.
