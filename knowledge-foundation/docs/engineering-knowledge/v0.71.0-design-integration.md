# v0.71.0 — Design Integration, GD&T, Mechanisms, Bearings, Gears & Verification

This release extends the engineering foundation from isolated mechanics into
the design/manufacturing/interface layer.

## Manufacturing/design integration

DFM is now treated as a design input rather than a late production check.
Machining, casting and forging constraints are represented with process-specific
failure modes and inspection considerations.

## GD&T and tolerance engineering

The library introduces functional datum selection, form/orientation/location
control, profile, tolerance stacks and drawing/inspection quality checks.

## Mechanisms and motion

Four-bar mechanisms, cam-followers, linkage load paths and backlash/clearance
are covered as reusable engineering reasoning patterns.

## Bearings

Bearing selection now includes load spectrum, speed, temperature, lubrication,
contamination, fits, internal clearance/preload and failure diagnosis.

## Gears

Gear-train fundamentals are connected to tooth bending, contact stress,
lubrication, scuffing, backlash and thermal effects.

## Systems verification

The verification foundation now explicitly connects requirements through
analysis, component verification, subassembly verification, system verification
and validation, with objective evidence and acceptance criteria.

## Boundary

The library remains a structured engineering knowledge foundation. Final
component selection, manufacturing qualification, gear/bearing ratings, GD&T
interpretation and system acceptance remain governed by the applicable
standards, supplier data, configuration and project-specific evidence.
